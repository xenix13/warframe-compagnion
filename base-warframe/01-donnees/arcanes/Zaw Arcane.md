# Zaw Arcanes (valeurs au rang maximum)

- **Exodia Brave** (Rare) : On Heavy Attack Kill: +5 Energy Rate for 4s. Stacks up to 3x.
- **Exodia Contagion** (Legendary) : After a Bullet Jump or Double Jump: Melee while Aim Gliding launches a projectile that explodes on impact
- **Exodia Epidemic** (Legendary) : After a Bullet Jump or Double Jump: Slam emits a shockwave forwards that suspends enemies in the air for a short period
- **Exodia Force** (Rare) : On Status Effect: 50% chance to deal 200% Weapon Damage to enemies within 6m.
- **Exodia Hunt** (Rare) : On Ground Slam: 50% chance to pull enemies within 12m into melee range.
- **Exodia Might** (Rare) : On Finisher Kill: 50% chance for +30% Life Steal for 8s
- **Exodia Triumph** (Uncommon) : +50% Additional Combo Count Chance
- **Exodia Valor** (Uncommon) : +200% Additional Combo Count Chance on Lifted enemies
