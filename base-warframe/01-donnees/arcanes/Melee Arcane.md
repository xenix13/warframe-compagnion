# Melee Arcanes (valeurs au rang maximum)

- **Melee Afflictions** (Rare) : Enemies affected by Status Effects gain 6 additional stacks when they're knocked down or flung by melee attacks.
- **Melee Animosity** (Rare) : On Melee Hit: Gain 42% Critical Chance on your next Heavy Attack, up to 420%
- **Melee Assimilation** (Rare) : On Shield Break: +150% Melee Damage on Heavy Attack and Heavy Attack kills restore +30% of max Shields for 20s.
- **Melee Careen** (Rare) : x2.50 Melee Damage against Frozen enemies. On Roll: Freeze enemies in a 5.5m radius with a 2s Cooldown.
- **Melee Crescendo** (Legendary) : On Finisher Kill: Gain 6 Initial Combo for the rest of your mission
- **Melee Doughty** (Rare) : Gain 1.0x Critical Damage for every 10% Puncture Status chance on your Melee Weapon.
- **Melee Duplicate** (Legendary) : On Base Critical Hits: 100% chance for your attack to strike a second time
- **Melee Exposure** (Rare) : On Ability Cast: Gain 60% Corrosive Damage on Melee strikes for 25s. Stacks up to 240%.
- **Melee Fortification** (Common) : On Melee Kill: +210 Armor for 10s
- **Melee Influence** (Rare) : On Melee Electricity Status: 20% chance for elemental Melee Status Effects to apply to enemies within 20m for 18s. Cannot refresh while active.
- **Melee Retaliation** (Common) : Gain 30% Melee Damage for every 200 current Shields, up to 420%. Bonus halved for Overshields.
- **Melee Vortex** (Rare) : Kill an enemy affected by Magnetic Status for a 45% chance to pull in enemies within 18m radius
