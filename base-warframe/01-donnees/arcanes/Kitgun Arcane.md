# Kitgun Arcanes (valeurs au rang maximum)

- **Pax Bolt** (Rare) : On Headshot Kill: +30% Ability Efficiency and +30% Ability Strength on the next Ability used within 4s.
- **Pax Charge** (Rare) : Battery Recharge Delay is reduced by +50%
- **Pax Seeker** (Rare) : On Headshot Kill: 4 projectiles burst from the target and seek the heads of nearby enemies.
- **Pax Soar** (Rare) : +50% Accuracy while Airborne ; -50% Weapon Recoil while Airborne ; +5s Aim Glide/Wall Latch Duration
- **Residual Boils** (Rare) : On Kitgun Kill: 20% chance to spawn volatile hives for 12s that explode when enemies approach for 80 Heat Damage in 10m. Standing in the area applies Heat Damage type to Theorem Arcanes.
- **Residual Malodor** (Rare) : On Kitgun Kill: 20% chance to create a frigid mist for 12s, dealing 40 Cold Damage/s. Standing in the area applies Cold Damage to Theorem Arcanes.
- **Residual Shock** (Rare) : On Kitgun Kill: 20% chance to spawn an electrified spike for 12s, dealing 200 Electricity Damage to enemies within 10m. Standing in the area applies Electricity Damage to Theorem Arcanes.
- **Residual Viremia** (Rare) : On Kitgun Kill: 20% chance to create pool of toxic blood for 12s, dealing 40 Toxin Damage/s. Standing in the area applies the Toxin Damage to Theorem Arcanes.
