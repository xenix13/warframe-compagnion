# Primary Arcanes (valeurs au rang maximum)

- **Fractalized Reset** (Rare) : On Ability Cast: +240% Reload Speed for 5s
- **Primary Blight** (Rare) : On Toxin Status Effect: +3.6% Critical Damage and +1.8% Multishot for 12s. Stacks up to 40x.
- **Primary Bulwark** (Rare) : Gain +1% damage for each unit of armor past 1,000, up to a max of +500%.
- **Primary Compression** (Rare) : On aim: x0.2 explosion radius, +100% damage and +5.5% ammo efficiency for every 1m radius lost.
- **Primary Crux** (Rare) : On Weak Point Hit: Gain +30% Status Chance and +6% Ammo Efficiency for 10s. Stacks up to 10x.
- **Primary Deadhead** (Rare) : On Weak Point Kill: +120% Damage for 24s. Stacks up to 3x. +30% to Headshot Multiplier -50% Weapon Recoil ; +30% to Headshot Multiplier ; -50% Weapon Recoil
- **Primary Debilitate** (Rare) : If an enemy has 10 stacks of a combined Status Effect, inflicting the same Status Effect again has a 100% chance to inflict one of the base Status Effects it is composed of.
- **Primary Dexterity** (Rare) : On Melee Kill: +60% Damage for 20s. Stacks up to 6x. +7.5s Combo Duration ; +7.5s Combo Duration
- **Primary Exhilarate** (Rare) : On Weapon Impact Status Effect: +1.2 Energy Regen/s for 10s. Stacks up to 3x.
- **Primary Frostbite** (Rare) : On Cold Status Effect: +3% Critical Damage and +2.25% Multishot for 12s. Stacks up to 40x.
- **Primary Merciless** (Rare) : On Kill: +30% Damage for 4s. Stacks up to 12x. +30% Reload Speed ; +30% Reload Speed
- **Primary Obstruct** (Rare) : On Weapon Magnetic Status Effect: Enemy weapons jam within 15m of target. Cooldown 10s.
- **Primary Overcharge** (Rare) : While at or above 90% Energy: Gain 35% of Max Energy as Multishot, capped at 350%.
- **Primary Plated Round** (Rare) : On Reload: Deal increased damage per round loaded based on max magazine size. Lasts for 10s.
