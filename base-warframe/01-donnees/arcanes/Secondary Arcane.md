- **Akimbo Slip Shot** (Rare) : While sliding or aim gliding: Gain 65% ammo efficiency with Dual Pistols.
- **Cascadia Accuracy** (Rare) : On Roll: +300% Critical Chance on Weak Point Hits for 4s
- **Cascadia Empowered** (Rare) : On Status Effect: Deals +750 Damage matching the Damage Type of the Status Effect
- **Cascadia Flare** (Rare) : On Heat Status Effect: +12% Damage for 10s. Stacks up to 480%.
- **Cascadia Overcharge** (Rare) : While Overshields Active: +300% Critical Chance
- **Conjunction Voltage** (Rare) : On Electricity Status Effect: +1.5% Reload Speed and +3% Multishot for 12s. Stacks up to 40x.
- **Secondary Cryogenic** (Rare) : On Puncture: Apply 3 Cold stacks on targets within 15m.
- **Secondary Deadhead** (Rare) : On Weak Point Kill: +120% Damage for 24s. Stacks up to 3x. +30% to Headshot Multiplier -50% Weapon Recoil ; +30% to Headshot Multiplier ; -50% Weapon Recoil
- **Secondary Dexterity** (Rare) : On Melee Kill: +60% Damage for 20s. Stacks up to 6x. +7.5s Combo Duration ; +7.5s Combo Duration
- **Secondary Encumber** (Rare) : On Status Effect: +24% chance to trigger a second random Status Effect.
- **Secondary Enervate** (Rare) : On Hit: Increase Critical Chance by 10%. Resets after 6 Big Critical Hit.
- **Secondary Fortifier** (Rare) : Gain 1 Overguard for every 100 Damage dealt to an enemy's Overguard. Deals x8 Extra Damage to Overguard. ; Deals x8 Extra Damage to Overguard.
- **Secondary Irradiate** (Rare) : On hitting enemies afflicted by 10 stacks of Radiation: Deal 180% of the hit damage to enemies within 7m.
- **Secondary Kinship** (Rare) : While Buffing Ally Warframes: +20% Critical Chance per buff
- **Secondary Merciless** (Rare) : On Kill: +30% Damage for 4s. Stacks up to 12x. +30% Reload Speed ; +30% Reload Speed
- **Secondary Outburst** (Rare) : On swapping to Secondary Weapon, consume all Combo Multipliers to increase Secondary Weapon Critical Chance and Critical Damage by 20% per Combo consumed for 30s.
- **Secondary Shiver** (Legendary) : Enemies take +45% damage per Cold Status
- **Secondary Surge** (Rare) : On Ability Cast: Next shot gains a Damage Multiplier for every 200 current Energy, up to x8.
