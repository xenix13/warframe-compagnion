# Compagnons

## Sentinels
- **Carrier** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Assault Mode' and 'Ammo Case' as default Precepts, Carrier is a seeker Sentinel. Carrier also comes with a shotgun weapon.
- **Carrier Prime** : santé 650, bouclier 300, armure 150. This ornate sentinel is an excellent example of late Orokin craftsmanship and styling.
- **Dethcube** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Assault Mode' and 'Vaporize' as default precepts, Dethcube acts exactly as advertised, as a badass cube of 'deth'. Comes loaded with Deth Machine Rifle weapon.
- **Dethcube Prime** : santé 600, bouclier 300, armure 150. With 'Assault Mode' and 'Vaporize' as default precepts, Dethcube Prime is the apex of lethal support. Comes loaded with Deth Machine Rifle Prime weapon.
- **Diriga** : santé 700, bouclier 150, armure 80. Diriga shocks nearby enemies, and picks off distant threats, utilizing the Vulklok sniper rifle and the 'Calculated Shot', 'Arc Coil' and 'Electro Pulse' precepts.
- **Djinn** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Thumper' and 'Fatal Attraction' as default Precepts, Djinn is a combat Sentinel. Djinn also comes with a poison dart weapon.
- **Helios** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Investigator' as its unique Precept and 'Deconstructor' as its weapon, the versatile Helios Sentinel acts as both a lethal guardian and an automatic codex scanner. Scans require an equipped Codex Scanner and an available charge.
- **Helios Prime** : santé 700, bouclier 300, armure 100. This knowledge hungry protector defends its master with Deconstructor Prime.
- **Nautilus** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Auto Omni' and 'Cordon' as default Precepts, this Sentinel is the ideal companion for long voyages aboard a Railjack.
- **Nautilus Prime** : santé 560, bouclier 350, armure 175. This brave deckhand defends his crew with Verglas Prime.
- **Oxylus** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Scan Aquatic Lifeforms' and 'Scan Matter' as default Precepts, Oxylus was designed to assist in Search-and-Rescue operations through the harsh landscape around The Orb Vallis. Oxylus also comes with a Multron rifle.
- **Prisma Shade** : santé 700, bouclier 300, armure 80. A rare jewel of the void. How could anything so beautiful be crafted for stealth?
- **Shade** : santé 600, bouclier 130, armure 80. With 'Revenge' and 'Ghost' as default Precepts, Shade is well suited for stealth gameplay. Shade also comes with a burst laser pistol.
- **Shade Prime** : santé 700, bouclier 300, armure 100. Shade Prime specializes in stealth and comes locked and loaded with the Burst Laser Prime. Together, you can dominate the shadows.
- **Taxon** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Assault Mode' and 'Molecular Conversion' as default Precepts, Taxon protects its owner with shield restoration. Equipped with the Artax ice-beam.
- **Wyrm** : santé 560, bouclier 250, armure 80. With 'Assault Mode' and 'Crowd Dispersion' as default Precepts, Wyrm is a highly offensive Sentinel. Wyrm also comes with a laser rifle.
- **Wyrm Prime** : santé 450, bouclier 600, armure 150. Both bodyguard and status symbol, the Wyrm Prime Sentinel was the last line of defense for high ranking Tenno in the Orokin Era.
