# Nekros (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 235 · Armure 105 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Polarités vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

There is a reaper whose name is Nekros. He provides deathly crowd control. Erstwhile enemies become allies when Nekros calls up recruits from the dead.

## Passif
Restore |HEALTH| Health with every enemy death within |RADIUS|m.

## Capacités
1. **Soul Punch** : A blow so powerful, it turns the enemy's very soul into a deadly projectile, damaging all in its path. Enemies that survive the blow are Marked for Harvest and become one of Nekros' Shadows of the Dead if killed while marked.
2. **Terrify** : Cast fear into the hearts of nearby enemies, causing them to run away in terror.
3. **Desecrate** : Forces fallen enemies around you to drop additional loot.
4. **Shadows Of The Dead** : Summon shadow versions of vanquished enemies to fight alongside you for a short period.

## Augments disponibles
- **Creeping Terrify** (Nekros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Terrify Augment: Affected enemies have 60% reduced Movement Speed.
- **Despoil** (Nekros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Desecrate Augment: No longer consumes Energy, but consumes 10 Health per corpse instead.
- **Discharge Strike** (Nekros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Soul Punch Augment: Depletes up to 25 Energy from the target.
- **Shield Of Shadows** (Nekros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Shadows of the Dead Augment: Each Shadow within 50m take 6% of the Damage done to Nekros in his stead.
- **Soul Survivor** (Nekros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Soul Punch Augment: Use on a downed ally to revive them with 30% Health.
