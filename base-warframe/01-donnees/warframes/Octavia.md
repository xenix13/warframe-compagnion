# Octavia (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 180 · Armure 160 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.05
Aura naramon · Polarités naramon, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Octavia turns bass, beat, and melody into a symphony of devastation. The music of her Mandachord gives her high survivability and supports allies. Her composition means the enemy’s destruction.

## Passif
Replenish |ENERGY| energy over |DURATION|s for Octavia and allies within |RANGE|m when abilities are activated.

## Capacités
1. **Mallet** : Rhythmically beats damage into nearby enemies and draws their fire. Damage inflicted on the Mallet increases its lethality.
2. **Resonator** : Launches a rollerball that charms foes to follow it. Combines with the Mallet to create a roving ball of sonic destruction.
3. **Metronome** : Grants buffs to those who consistently perform actions in time to Octavia's music. Timed jumps offer the Vivace speed buff. Crouching on the beat grants cloaking with the Nocturne buff. Firing rhythmically bestows Opera multishot buff. Timed melee swings give the Forte damage buff.
4. **Amp** : Draws power from the decibel level of sound in the area and uses it to amplify a damage buff for Octavia and her allies. It also doubles the damage and range of nearby Mallets.

## Augments disponibles
- **Conductor** (Octavia, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Resonator Augment: Reactivate the ability to command Resonator to move to your aim point at 150% Speed.
- **Partitioned Mallet** (Octavia, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Mallet Augment: Create an additional Mallet with 20% reduced range.
