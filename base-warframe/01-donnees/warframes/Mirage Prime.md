# Mirage Prime (Warframe)

Santé 200 · Bouclier 310 · Armure 185 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.2
Aura naramon · Polarités vazarin, vazarin, naramon, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Dazzle the opposition with this golden master of illusion and mayhem. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
Sliding lasts |DURATION|% longer and acrobatic maneuvers are |SPEED|% faster.

## Capacités
1. **Hall Of Mirrors** : Mirage creates an entourage of doppelgangers to confuse and distract the enemy.
2. **Sleight Of Hand** : Booby trap nearby objects while conjuring an irresistible jewel that bursts with radial blind when touched in darkness, or a radial explosion in light. Conjure multiple smaller jewels with the help of Hall of Mirrors.
3. **Eclipse** : (TAP) Temporarily reduce the damage Mirage takes from enemies. (HOLD) Temporarily increase Mirage's weapon damage.
4. **Prism** : Fires an energy prism that shoots lasers in all directions. Activating again detonates the prism, blinding nearby foes.

## Augments disponibles
- **Explosive Legerdemain** (Mirage, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Sleight of Hand Augment: Ammo and Orbs pickups are turned into proximity mines that deal 1000 Damage with a 100% Status Chance.
- **Hall Of Malevolence** (Mirage, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Hall of Mirrors Augment: The damage of your doppelgangers is increased by 5% every time you kill an enemy.
- **Prism Guard** (Mirage, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Prism Augment: Prism follows above Mirage. Duration changed to 4s.
- **Total Eclipse** (Mirage, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Eclipse Augment: While active, allies within 15m benefit from Eclipse.
