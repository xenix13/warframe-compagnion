# Ash (Warframe)

Santé 455 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 105 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1.15
Aura madurai · Polarités madurai, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Behold the patron saint of the Orokin school of political assassination. Ash specializes in stealth. The edge of his blade is sooner felt than seen.

## Passif
Slash Status Effects inflicted on enemies do |DAMAGE|% increased damage and last |DURATION|% longer.

## Capacités
1. **Shuriken** : Launches a spinning blade of pain, dealing high damage and impaling enemies to walls.
2. **Smoke Screen** : Drops a smoke bomb that stuns enemies and obscures their vision, rendering Ash invisible for a short time.
3. **Teleport** : Teleport to a target and perform a Finisher. If the finisher kills the target, Ash regains a portion of the energy spent.
4. **Blade Storm** : Project fierce Shadow Clones of Ash upon groups of distant enemies. Join the fray using Teleport. The Shadow Clones can be upgraded with mods.

## Augments disponibles
- **Rising Storm** (Ash, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Blade Storm Augment: Blade Storm attacks increase your Ability Combo Counter by 4. Passive: +10s Combo Duration
- **Seeking Shuriken** (Ash, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Shuriken Augment: Hits expose weaknesses on enemies, reducing their Armor by 70% for 8s.
- **Smoke Shadow** (Ash, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Smoke Screen Augment: Conceals allies within 15m and grants 150% Critical Chance while invisible.
- **Tear Gas** (Ash, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Smoke Screen Augment: Blinds enemies within 4m for 3s.
- **Teleport Rush** (Ash, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Teleport Augment: Using Teleport increases Parkour Velocity by 30% for 12s. Executing a target with Teleport extends Smoke Screen's duration by 5s. Teleport Mercy Kills fully refresh the duration.

Introduite : Vanilla (2012-10-25)
