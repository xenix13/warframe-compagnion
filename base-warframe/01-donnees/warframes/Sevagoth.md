# Sevagoth (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 235 · Armure 160 · Énergie 140 · Vitesse de sprint 0.95
Polarités vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

No longer adrift in the Void, Sevagoth has returned. He and his Shadow deal heavy damage and have high survivability. They are sustained by the reaping of wayward souls.

## Passif
On death, become Sevagoth's Shadow and fight to resurrect him by collecting the souls needed to rebuild his tombstone.

## Capacités
1. **Reap** : Sevagoth's Shadow flies outward ravaging enemies in his path. Survivors are damaged by Death's Harvest over time. The souls of the dead fill the Death Well.
2. **Sow** : Plant a death seed in nearby targets to drain their lifeforce. Reap what has been sown to detonate afflicted enemies, dealing a percentage of their health as radial damage. The souls of the dead fill the Death Well.
3. **Gloom** : Summon a radial pulse wave that ensnares and slows enemies, siphoning their lifeforce for the Death Well. Allies within the wave steal health with each attack.
4. **Exalted Shadow** : When the Death Well fills, Sevagoth's Shadow form is ready to be released. Tear the enemy asunder with a collection of melee-focused abilities.

## Augments disponibles
- **Dark Propagation** (Sevagoth, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Sow Augment: Enemies killed while inflicted with Sow spread its effect in a 15m radius. Shadow's Death Harvest also inflicts Sow.
- **Shadow Haze** (Sevagoth, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Reap Augment: Increase Critical Chance by 50% on enemies inflicted with Death Harvest. A new Shadow emerges from those enemies when they perish. Limit of 3 Shadows.
