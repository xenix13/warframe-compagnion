# Citrine Prime (Warframe)

Santé 400 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 325 · Énergie 150 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura naramon · Polarités vazarin, madurai, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

The crystalline citadel ascends. Citrine Prime captivates with refined radiance, her brilliant gems set within the finest gold filigree.

## Passif
Citrine grants nearby allies |BASE| health regeneration per second. Pick up a Health Orb to increase regeneration by |HPS|, up to a maximum of |MAX|.

## Capacités
1. **Fractured Blast** : Slash and stagger enemies with a crystal blast that inflicts Slash Status. Enemies afflicted with this Status Effect have an increased chance of dropping Health and Energy Orbs.
2. **Preserving Shell** : Citrine guards herself and nearby allies with a crystalline shell that gradually decays. Kills and assists increase the defensive power of the shell.
3. **Prismatic Gem** : Deploy a gem that shoots prismatic beams. The gem targets enemies that are taking weapon damage from Citrine and her allies. Its beams inflict Heat, Cold, Toxin, and Electricity Status Effects. Status Chance and Status Duration increase for nearby allies.
4. **Crystallize** : Citrine summons crystal fractals. The fractals rush forward, seeking enemies. Enemies touched by the fractals are paralyzed by crystalline growths. Hit the growths to deal Critical Damage.

## Augments disponibles
- **Prismatic Companion** (Citrine, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Prismatic Gem Augment: The gem now attaches to your free-moving companion. Passive : +50% Prismatic Gem Duration.
- **Recrystalize** (Citrine, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Crystallize Augment: Enemies killed by crystalline growths stagger and spread the crystals to enemies within 16m.
