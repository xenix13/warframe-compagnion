# Uriel (Warframe)

Santé 566 · Bouclier 566 · Armure 105 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura madurai · Polarités naramon, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

To fight as the Heretic of Xata is to have demons at your back. Uriel incinerates foes with searing brimstone, and commands three fiendish summons to do his devil’s work.

## Passif
Uriel commands, empowers, and protects three unique demons. CATENACH: Snares and slows enemies chaining them together. Damage dealt to any target in the chain is inflicted on all snared enemies. (Unlocks with Infernalis) GULPHAGOR : Latches onto foes dealing damage over time. If killed, a circle of pain is spawned. (Unlocks with Remedium) VYTHELAS: Inscribes demonic runes upon fallen foes that add Fire Rate and Heat Damage when collected. (Unlocks with Demonium)

## Capacités
1. **Infernalis** : Manifest an aura of flames inflicting Heat Damage and Status Effect on nearby enemies. Tap Dodge while airborne to take flight as a flaming meteor of destruction.
2. **Remedium** : Uriel heals himself and his demons. If his demons are dead, they are resurrected.
3. **Demonium** : Uriel rips out the souls of his demons, draining their health, and sends them in search of new victims. Souls explode on contact with an enemy, rendering it vulnerable to Damage.
4. **Brimstone** : Uriel and his demons create a growing ring of flaming brimstone. Charge the ability by utilizing demon abilities. Damage inflicted increases with each consecutive hit.

## Augments disponibles
- **Infernum** (Uriel, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Infernalis Augment: Release Fireballs for 1500Heat Damage with larger volleys when grabbing Vythelas' Runes during flight. Hits from Fireballs slowly charge Brimstone.
