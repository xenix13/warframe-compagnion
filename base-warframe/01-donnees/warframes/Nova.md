# Nova (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 180 · Armure 105 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.2
Aura naramon · Polarités madurai, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Harness antimatter. Nova uses antiparticles to provide crowd control and amplify damage output. She obliterates enemies by destabilizing matter itself.

## Passif
Enemies killed while slowed down have |CHANCE|% to drop health orbs. Enemies killed while sped up have |CHANCE|% to drop energy orbs.

## Capacités
1. **Null Star** : Creates antimatter particles that orbit Nova and attack nearby targets. Each active particle reduces damage to Nova's Health and Shields.
2. **Antimatter Drop** : Launch a large particle of charged antimatter that will detonate on contact. Direct the particle by aiming, shoot it to charge it further. Deals Blast Damage with a guaranteed Status Effect.
3. **Wormhole** : Creates a wormhole allowing instantaneous travel. Hold the ability to visualize placement.
4. **Molecular Prime** : Primes all enemies in an expanding radius with volatile antimatter. Press the ability to slow down enemy movement, hold the ability to speed them up.

## Augments disponibles
- **Antimatter Absorb** (Nova, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Antimatter Drop Augment: Absorbs enemy bullets within 5m, increasing the power of the explosion from Nova’s particle of antimatter.
- **Antimatter Mine** (Nova, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Antimatter Drop Augment: Creates a fully charged stationary orb that explodes after 6s or within enemy proximity.
- **Escape Velocity** (Nova, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Worm Hole Augment: Allies that travel through wormhole gain a 50% Speed bonus for 7s.
- **Molecular Fission** (Nova, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Molecular Prime Augment: Enemies hit by Null Stars are primed. When killed, primed enemies will restore a Null Star charge and have a 100% chance of restoring two.
- **Neutron Star** (Nova, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Null Star Augment: Particles deal 240 Heat Damage with guaranteed Status effect in 8m. On recast, remaining particles seek out enemies with doubled search radius.
