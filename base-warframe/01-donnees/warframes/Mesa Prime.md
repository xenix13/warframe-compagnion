# Mesa Prime (Warframe)

Santé 400 · Bouclier 180 · Armure 135 · Énergie 140 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura madurai · Polarités naramon, naramon, vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Cast a long shadow with this lethal enforcer. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
Shoot dual-wielded sidearms |SPEED|% faster and reload single-handed sidearms |RELOAD|% more rapidly. Gain |HEALTH| Bonus Health when not using Melee Weapons.

## Capacités
1. **Ballistic Battery** : When activated, this power stores damage caused by guns. When triggered again, that damage is channelled through the next gunshot.
2. **Shooting Gallery** : Gives an ally Extra Damage while jamming the guns of nearby enemies. This power shifts between team members.
3. **Shatter Shield** : Envelops Mesa in a barrier of energy, reflecting back incoming bullet damage.
4. **Peacemaker** : With intense focus, Mesa draws her Regulator pistols, shooting down her foes in rapid succession.

## Augments disponibles
- **Ballistic Bullseye** (Mesa, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Ballistic Battery Augment: The shot gains a +50% Final Critical Chance bonus, based on the amount charged.
- **Mesa's Waltz** (Mesa, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Peacemaker Augment: Mesa can move at 50% Speed while using Peacemaker.
- **Muzzle Flash** (Mesa, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Shooting Gallery Augment: After 6 kill assists by a player with Shooting Gallery, Mesa's next shot will blind enemies within 12m for 6s.
- **Staggering Shield** (Mesa, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Shatter Shield Augment: Reflected bullets have a chance to stagger enemies. The base 50% chance increases with power strength.
