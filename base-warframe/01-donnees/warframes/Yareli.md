# Yareli (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 105 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités madurai, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

The waverider can unleash a flood of destruction on the battlefield. Yareli’s aqueous attacks deal high damage. Surf’s up. Enemies down.

## Passif
Yareli gains +|CHANCE|% Critical Chance for Secondary weapons when she has been moving for at least |TIME|s.

## Capacités
1. **Sea Snares** : Form five water globules that seek out enemies and expand on contact, simultaneously damaging and immobilizing their victims. Enemies hit by the globules take increased damage from all sources.
2. **Merulina** : Summon Merulina, a rideable creature of the waves, and the inspiration for K-Driving. Merulina protects Yareli by absorbing a large portion of incoming damage.
3. **Aquablades** : Tear through foes with a trio of orbiting aquatic blades.
4. **Riptide** : Drag enemies into a crushing maelstrom and then blow them away in a watery burst. Each enemy trapped in the vortex increases the burst's damage.

## Augments disponibles
- **Loyal Merulina** (Yareli, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Merulina Augment: Yareli no longer rides Merulina. Instead, Merulina follows her and casts Sea Snare on nearby enemies. 2s cooldown.
- **Merulina Guardian** (Yareli, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Merulina Augment: Enemies eliminated during Sea Snares heal 20% of Merulina's health. Upon healing, gain +200% Reload Speed and Fire Rate on Secondary weapons for 20s.
- **Surging Blades** (Yareli, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Aquablades Augment: Activate Aquablades when the ability is in use or cooldown to throw one blade. Blades gain 10% damage when any Aquablade hits an enemy. No cost to throw with Merulina.
