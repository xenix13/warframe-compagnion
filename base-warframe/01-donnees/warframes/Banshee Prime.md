# Banshee Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 135 · Énergie 215 · Vitesse de sprint 1.15
Aura madurai · Polarités madurai, madurai, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Banshee Prime assails her foes by manipulating sonic forces to deadly effect. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
Banshee pierces the ears of enemies within a |RADIUS|m radius when casting Abilities, applying max Puncture Status Effect. Additionally, Banshee's weapon noise is hushed.

## Capacités
1. **Sonic Boom** : Banshee emits a sonic shockwave that pushes targets in range with enough force to incapacitate attackers and reduce their Armor.
2. **Sonar** : Using acoustic location, Banshee's Sonar power finds and tracks enemies, and exposes critical weak points to everyone in your squad.
3. **Silence** : Using Silence surrounds Banshee in an aura that stuns enemies, limits their perception and tactical response to gunfire and Warframe attacks, and renders them unable to activate their abilities.
4. **Sound Quake** : Channeling all of her acoustic energy into the environment, Banshee uses ultrasonic reverberations to violently shake the ground in a targeted area.

## Augments disponibles
- **Gaseous Quake** (Banshee, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Sound Quake Augment: Channel Gas Damage and Status Effect leaks with a 2.75x Damage Multiplier that increases every second on enemies in your sight.
- **Resonance** (Banshee, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Sonar Augment: Killing an enemy by shooting their Sonar's weak point will trigger another Sonar for 100% of its duration.
- **Savage Silence** (Banshee, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Silence Augment: Silence staggers enemies and Finisher damage is increased by 300%
- **Sonic Siphon** (Banshee, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Sonic Boom Augment: Gain Armor for each enemy hit by Sonic Boom for 20s.
