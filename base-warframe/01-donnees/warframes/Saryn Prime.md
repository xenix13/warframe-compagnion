# Saryn Prime (Warframe)

Santé 365 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 315 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura vazarin · Polarités naramon, vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

A golden blossom conceals deadly nectar. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
Status Effects inflicted upon enemies last |DURATION|% longer.

## Capacités
1. **Spores** : Inflict a target with a pox of Corrosive spores. Spread spores to nearby enemies by destroying them or killing their host. The longer the Spore spreads, its damage will increase.
2. **Molt** : Shedding her skin like a snake, Saryn leaves a decoy behind to draw fire from enemies.
3. **Toxic Lash** : While active, attacks deal additional Toxin Damage; this effect is doubled for Melee Strikes. Instantly burst spores when attacking afflicted enemies.
4. **Miasma** : Release a poisonous miasma that deals Viral Damage to enemies in range. Foes afflicted by spores are more susceptible to the mist.

## Augments disponibles
- **Contagion Cloud** (Saryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Toxic Lash Augment: Create 5m toxic clouds, dealing 300 Toxin Damage/s for 12s with every kill. Damage is twice as strong for Melee kills.
- **Regenerative Molt** (Saryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Molt Augment: After casting Molt, Saryn regenerates 50 Health/s for 10s.
- **Revealing Spores** (Saryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Spores Augment: Infected enemies within 40m appear on the minimap. +40 Enemy Radar.
- **Venom Dose** (Saryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Spores Augment: Hold to cast will grant all allies within 15m an additional 100% Corrosive Damage to their attacks for 40s.
