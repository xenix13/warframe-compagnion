# Narin (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 550 · Armure 165 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura naramon · Polarités madurai, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Narin descends in swirling eddies, her blade keen and deadly as an icy wind. She wields ice to defend as deftly as her swift rapier strikes.

## Passif
Each stack of Cold Status Effect on enemies increase chance to spawn Sangodae pickup by |CHANCE|%, granting Extra Cold Damage on Primary and Secondary weapons for |DURATION|s. Narin also gains Cold Damage for her abilities.

## Capacités
1. **Neote** : Lunge with Narin's ice rapier Neote, inflicting Cold Damage and Cold and Puncture Status Effect on foes directly ahead of her. Gain Ice for each enemy hit.
2. **Naraemagi** : Recover Shields by absorbing Cold Status Effect from nearby enemies. Gain Overguard if at full Shields. Gain Ice for each enemy hit.
3. **Hakchum** : Tap to leap into the air, tap again to land at target location. Applies Cold Damage Vulnerability and Status Effect in a large radius upon landing. Gain Ice for each enemy hit.
4. **Nurinarim** : Requires full Ice to cast. An ancestral spirit descends upon Narin and she begins sword dancing for continuous nearby damage. Press Attack to launch shattering icy slashes that deplete Armor and Shields. Hitting Frozen enemies causes them to explode, dealing massive radial Cold Damage. Attacks on Marked Targets cost no Ice and do more damage.

Introduite : Update 44.0 (2026-09-23)
