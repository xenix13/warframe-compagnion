# Equinox (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 135 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.15
Aura naramon · Polarités vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Equinox rises from the edge of day and night. Her Day form offers offensive support while her Night form offers defensive support. She thrives between realms.

## Passif
|PERCENT|% of Health Orbs are converted into Energy, and |PERCENT|% of Energy Orbs are converted into Health.

## Capacités
1. **Metamorphosis** : Switch forms, temporarily gaining bonus Shields and Armor in Night-Form, or bonus Damage and Speed in Day-Form.
2. **Rest & Rage** : In Night-Form, targets are put to sleep. In Day-Form, targets become more vulnerable to damage.
3. **Pacify & Provoke** : In Night-Form, reduces damage inflicted by nearby enemies. In Day-Form, increases Ability Strength of nearby allies.
4. **Mend & Maim** : In Night-Form, allies' Shields are replenished with each nearby enemy killed. In Day-Form, enemies are inflicted with Slash Status Effect. Deactivate while in Night-Form to restore Health and Shields for Equinox and allies. Deactivate while in Day-Form to cast a wave of slashing force.

## Augments disponibles
- **Calm & Frenzy** (Equinox, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Rest & Rage Augment: Killing an affected enemy causes the effect to spread to enemies within 5m for 100% of the remaining duration.
- **Duality** (Equinox, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Metamorphosis Augment: Equinox's other half breaks free for 10s, dealing 300% Damage.
- **Energy Transfer** (Equinox, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Mend & Maim Augment: 100% of charge is conserved when switching between forms.
- **Peaceful Provocation** (Equinox, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Pacify & Provoke Augment: Pacify converts damage done to allies into an aura that slows enemies by 40%. Provoke converts damage done to enemies into 15% extra Ability Strength.
- **Push & Pull** (Equinox, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Metamorphosis Augment: Switching to Day-form staggers enemies within 6m and knocks them down when switching to Night-form.
