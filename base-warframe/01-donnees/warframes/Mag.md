# Mag (Warframe)

Santé 180 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 105 · Énergie 140 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités naramon, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Take down your enemies with magnetic force. Mag alters magnetic fields to provide crowd control and strip enemy defenses. Few can resist her attraction or her repulsion.

## Passif
Nearby items gravitate toward Mag for easy collection.

## Capacités
1. **Pull** : Mag stuns enemies as she manifests a magnetic vortex. The vortex pulls in Polarize Shards and stunned enemies to place them directly in front of her.
2. **Magnetize** : (TAP) Enclose a target in a magnetic field that ensnares nearby enemies and deals damage over time. The field pulls Polarize Shards that orbited Mag and shrapnel into the deadly mix. (HOLD) Mag surrounds herself in a magnetic field that absorbs ranged attacks and reflects damage back in a destructive cone.
3. **Polarize** : Emit an energy pulse that depletes enemy shields and armor as it restores ally shields. Debris left over from the pulse becomes Polarize Shards. Nearby Polarize Shards orbit Mag and cut enemies, inflicting Slash Status Effect.
4. **Crush** : Magnetize the bones of nearby enemies, causing them to collapse upon themselves.

## Augments disponibles
- **Counter Pulse** (Mag, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Polarize Augment: Enemy weapons are jammed and robotics are disabled for 4s when hit by Polarize.
- **Fracturing Crush** (Mag, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Crush Augment: Crush gains +50% casting speed. The armor of surviving enemies decreases by 75% and they are unable to move for 7s.
- **Greedy Pull** (Mag, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Pull Augment: Adds 100% chance to pull pickups towards Mag.
- **Magnetized Discharge** (Mag, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Magnetize Augment: Recast on the target to detonate. Enemies hit have a 50% chance to be disarmed. Passive: +45% Magnetize Range.
- **Sapping Reach** (Mag, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Pull Augment: Steals up to 25 Energy from the target.
- **Shield Overload** (Mag, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Polarize Augment: Increases the time for the target's Shields to regenerate by 9s.
