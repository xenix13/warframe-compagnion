# Hildryn (Warframe)

Santé 180 · Bouclier 1280 · Armure 315 · Énergie 0 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura naramon · Exilus vazarin · Polarités vazarin, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Strength is Hildryn's virtue. Her unique high-damage abilities are powered by her Shields, which can be recharged with Energy. Hildryn proves that a strong offense is a good defense.

## Passif
Hildryn's full Shield Gate is high. After her Shields are depleted, she is invulnerable to damage up to 3.5s.

## Capacités
1. **Balefire** : Charge and launch devastating bolts of electricity.
2. **Pillage** : Pillage a percentage of Shields and Armor of nearby enemies to replenish Hildryn's own Shields and Overshields. Also removes Status Effects from Hildryn and her allies.
3. **Haven** : Create a shield aura around allies. Enemies that approach Hildryn will take Radiation Damage.
4. **Aegis Storm** : Take the skies and rain Balefire rockets down on the enemy. Nearby enemies are blasted into the air where they will create an Energy Orb every few seconds. When shields run out the enemies are smashed into the ground.

## Augments disponibles
- **Aegis Gale** (Hildryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Aegis Storm Augment: Balefire has an Alternate Fire during Aegis Storm. The blast deals 15% of Hildryn's Max Shield as additional damage.
- **Balefire Surge** (Hildryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Balefire Augment: Fully charged direct hits restore 250 Shield to Hildryn. Impact with Nullifier Shields will destroy them and restore 750 Shield.
- **Blazing Pillage** (Hildryn, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Pillage Augment: Enemies affected by Haven will be set ablaze for 200 Heat Damage and restore 50 additional Shields to Hildryn.
