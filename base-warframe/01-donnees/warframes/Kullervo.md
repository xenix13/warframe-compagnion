# Kullervo (Warframe)

Santé 1005 · Bouclier 0 · Armure 550 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura naramon · Polarités madurai, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Kullervo answers for his sins and the sins of others. His daggers and melee prowess deal high damage. Seize the power of treachery to slake his thirst for revenge.

## Passif
Kullervo boasts +|EFFICIENCY|% Heavy Attack Efficiency and +|WINDUP|% Heavy Attack Wind Up Speed on all melee weapons.

## Capacités
1. **Wrathful Advance** : (TAP) Kullervo charges a Heavy Attack, then teleports to his target and strikes. His rage-filled focus temporarily increases his Melee Critical Chance. (HOLD) Teleport to any marked location.
2. **Recompense** : Kullervo surrounds himself with daggers. Each dagger that strikes an enemy restores his health, but each dagger that misses an enemy strikes Kullervo, dealing a bit of damage. When Kullervo has max Health, he receives Overguard from struck enemies.
3. **Collective Curse** : Kullervo sends forth a curse that binds enemies. When a cursed enemy takes damage from him, every other cursed enemy also suffers a portion of that damage.
4. **Storm Of Ukko** : The Void answers Kullervo's invocation with a storm of daggers that rain down upon his enemies.

## Augments disponibles
- **Volatile Recompense** (Kullervo, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Recompense Augment: Daggers whirl in a ring around Kullervo for 25s, slashing nearby foes and dealing Blast Damage and Status Effect before returning to the ring.
- **Wrath Of Ukko** (Kullervo, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Storm of Ukko Augment: Wrathful Advance moves the storm of daggers to the teleportation location and increases its duration by 6s.
