# Protea Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 185 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 1.2
Aura aura · Polarités madurai, naramon, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Behold the child of two fathers. Protea Prime once fulfilled Parvos Granum's vision of a time-wielding guardian. Now she upholds the Tenno ideal of defending the defenseless.

## Passif
Every |CASTS|th Power Cast is granted +|STRENGTH|% Ability Strength.

## Capacités
1. **Grenade Fan** : Throw out grenades in an arc. (TAP) SHRAPNEL VORTEX Creates a slashing, staggering swirl of shrapnel. (HOLD) SHIELD SATELLITES Protea reconfigures Grenades to work as overcharging shield generators, protecting her, her allies and companions. When Shields break, a Satellite is destroyed to double minimum Shield Gate invincibility time.
2. **Blaze Artillery** : Deploys an artillery unit to blast plasma charges at enemies it faces. Each enemy hit increases the power of subsequent plasma attacks.
3. **Dispensary** : Deploys a device that generates 3 pickups after a short delay: empowered health orb, universal ammo pack and energy orb.
4. **Temporal Anchor** : Drops a Temporal Anchor which, after a short duration, Protea rewinds to trigger a temporal implosion. Implosion damage increases based on damage dealt between anchor drop and rewind. Everything lost or expended in that time is returned. Dying while Anchor is active rewinds Protea to the anchor, saving her. Protea is knocked down and the anchor vanishes.

## Augments disponibles
- **Repair Dispensary** (Protea, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Dispensary Augment: Dispensary also creates pick ups that heal companions for 20% of their maximum health and reduce their incapacitation timer by 12 sec.
- **Temporal Artillery** (Protea, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Blaze Artillery Augment : When Temporal Anchor is activated, one existing Artillery unit will attach to Protea, halting its duration countdown. Max combo count is 20x.
- **Temporal Erosion** (Protea, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Temporal Anchor Augment: While Temporal Anchor is active, each strike of Grenade Fan and Blaze Artillery strips 10% Armor from enemies.
