# Hydroid (Warframe)

Santé 365 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 240 · Énergie 140 · Vitesse de sprint 1.05
Aura naramon · Polarités madurai, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Hydroid summons the power of the ocean depths. He provides crowd control with aqueous attacks. His enemies will meet a watery demise.

## Passif
Enemies damaged by Hydroid are more vulnerable to Corrosive Status, with initial Status reducing Armor by |PCT|%.

## Capacités
1. **Tempest Barrage** : Summon a tempest to rain down upon a target area.
2. **Tidal Surge** : Crash through enemies in a ferocious wall of water.
3. **Plunder** : Plunder Armor from nearby enemies and increase Corrosive Damage on your Abilities and weapons. Enemies affected by Corrosive Status offer a greater increase.
4. **Tentacle Swarm** : Summon a creature from the depths. Its watery tentacles emerge from nearby surfaces to wreak havoc.

## Augments disponibles
- **Pilfering Swarm** (Hydroid, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Tentacle Swarm Augment: Enemies held by tentacles have a 100% chance at additional drops.
- **Rousing Plunder** (Hydroid, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Plunder Augment: Plunder gains 50% max Corrosive Damage and Armor, and heals allies within Affinity range for 50 Health.
- **Tidal Impunity** (Hydroid, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Tidal Surge Augment: Clears Status Effects and grants 12s of Status Immunity for yourself and allies that come in contact with it. Reduces Tidal Surge's Energy cost to 15.
- **Viral Tempest** (Hydroid, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Tempest Barrage Augment: Each projectile has a 100% chance of inflicting a Viral Status Effect.

Introduite : Update 13.0 (2014-04-09)
