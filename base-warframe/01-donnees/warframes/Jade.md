# Jade (Warframe)

Santé 365 · Bouclier 450 · Armure 135 · Énergie 150 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura ['aura', 'vazarin'] · Polarités naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Become enraptured by the song of devastation. Through her, the Jade Light can be a force of destruction or a source of support.

## Passif
Jade's profound understanding of the relationship between life and death grants her two Aura Mod Slots. Some of her Abilities apply Judgments, increasing enemy damage vulnerability by |CHANCE|% for |DURATION|s.

## Capacités
1. **Light's Judgment** : Create a well of light that heals allies and hurts enemies. Those who enter the well will be highlighted by Judgments.
2. **Symphony Of Mercy** : Cycle through three songs that strengthen allies. Power of The Seven increases Ability Strength. Deathbringer increases Weapon Damage. Spirit of Resilience increases Shield Effectiveness. Extend the duration of each song by killing enemies surrounded by Judgments.
3. **Ophanim Eyes** : Jade summons an accusatory gaze that slows nearby enemies and dissolves their Shields and Armor. When the gaze falls upon allies, they can be revived from a distance.
4. **Glory On High** : Soar with destructive power. Use alternate-fire to detonate Judgments, causing an explosion of Jade Light. Enemies inside the Light's Judgment radius empower the explosion.

## Augments disponibles
- **Jade's Judgment** (Jade, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Light's Judgment Augment: Enemies damaged by the well have a 5% chance per hit to spawn a Jade Light above them, damaging them for 3s.
