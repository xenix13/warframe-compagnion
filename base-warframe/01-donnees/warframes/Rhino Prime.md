# Rhino Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 290 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités vazarin, vazarin, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Takes Rhino's ground-shaking abilities to the next level with altered mod polarities that offer greater customization.

## Passif
Emit a shockwave dealing |DAMAGE| damage after landing from a great height.

## Capacités
1. **Rhino Charge** : Rhino charges towards a target, clobbering any in his path and goring his victim.
2. **Iron Skin** : Rhino hardens his skin, insulating himself from all damage and gaining Overguard.
3. **Roar** : Grants all nearby Warframes increased damage for a short duration.
4. **Rhino Stomp** : Rhino stomps with force sufficient to disrupt time, tumbling enemies around him in stasis.

## Augments disponibles
- **Iron Shrapnel** (Rhino, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Iron Skin Augment: Recasting Iron Skin will cause it to detonate, dealing 100% of its remaining Health as Puncture Damage, and knocking down enemies.
- **Ironclad Charge** (Rhino, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Rhino Charge Augment: Each enemy hit increases Rhino's Armor Rating by 50% for 10s.
- **Piercing Roar** (Rhino, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Roar Augment: Roar gains +40% range. Enemies within 25m are knocked down and suffer 5 stacks of Puncture Status.
- **Reinforcing Stomp** (Rhino, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Rhino Stomp Augment: Iron Skin Health is replenished by 4% for each enemy affected.

Introduite : Update 12.4 (2014-03-05)
