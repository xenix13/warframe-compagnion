# Styanax Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 925 · Armure 265 · Énergie 215 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura naramon · Polarités vazarin, naramon, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Shield of the innocent. Spear of justice. Styanax Prime holds the line in defiance of tyranny.

## Passif
Styanax's critical chance increases with his shields and doubles for spearguns.

## Capacités
1. **Axios Javelin** : Throw an Axios Javelin. When the javelin impales an enemy against a wall, surrounding enemies are pulled into the area and suffer a burst of damage.
2. **Tharros Strike** : Summon Tharros, the shield of Styanax. Swing Tharros to repel enemies and reduce their shields and armor. Styanax regenerates health for every enemy struck.
3. **Rally Point** : Draw enemy attention to Styanax. His resolve uplifts him and nearby allies, regenerating the squad's energy over time. Styanax and his allies also regenerate shields for every kill assist they contribute.
4. **Final Stand** : Exude might and valor. Rise into the air and throw a barrage of Axios Javelins. The javelins deal damage to nearby enemies wherever they land. Direct hits to enemies deal greater damage.

## Augments disponibles
- **Axios Javelineers** (Styanax, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Axios Javelin Augment: A pair of Styanax Specters spawn to throw javelins, creating vortexes on impact. Impale an enemy to increase vortex duration by 5s.
- **Intrepid Stand** (Styanax, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Final Stand Augment: Each javelin that damages an enemy grants 60 Overguard to Styanax and 30 Overguard to his allies within Affinity Range.
- **Tharros Lethality** (Styanax, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Tharros Strike Augment: Increases Critical Damage by 100% on enemies you hit. Critical Damage bonus is doubled for Spearguns.
