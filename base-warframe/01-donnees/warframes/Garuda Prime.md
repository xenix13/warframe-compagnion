# Garuda Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 420 · Énergie 220 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités naramon, vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

White, gold and deepest scarlet, the queen of gore in full regalia.

## Passif
Garuda's damage temporarily increases with each enemy she kills, to a maximum of |DAMAGE|%. Slashes with her talons if no melee weapon is equipped.

## Capacités
1. **Dread Mirror** : Rip the life force from an enemy and use it as a shield that captures damage, this kills significantly weakened enemies instantly. Charge to channel the captured damage into an explosive projectile.
2. **Blood Altar** : Impale an enemy on an altar of talons and siphon health for Garuda and her allies.
3. **Bloodletting** : Garuda sacrifices her health to generate energy and clear Status Effects.
4. **Seeking Talons** : Charge to expand the targeting area, release to send Garuda's talons careening toward each target in area. Surviving enemies are prone to Slash Status.

## Augments disponibles
- **Blending Talons** (Garuda, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Seeking Talons Augment: Tap to perform a 9m AoE around Garuda. Garuda's Talons gain 100% additional Combo Count Chance when hitting targets affected by Slash Status.
- **Blood Forge** (Garuda, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Bloodletting Augment: Garuda's equipped weapon is reloaded up to 100%.
- **Dread Ward** (Garuda, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Dread Mirror Augment: Become unkillable for 8s when Dread Mirror kills a target by ripping its life force.
