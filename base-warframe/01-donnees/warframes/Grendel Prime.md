# Grendel Prime (Warframe)

Santé 1095 · Bouclier 95 · Armure 400 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 0.95
Aura naramon · Polarités naramon, vazarin, vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

Primal. Insatiable. Grendel Prime devours all who oppose him.

## Passif
Each enemy consumed grants |ARMOUR| bonus armor.

## Capacités
1. **Feast** : Swallow enemies whole and store them in Grendel's gut. Hold to vomit out stored enemies, covering them in toxic bile.
2. **Nourish** : Regenerate health as Grendel absorbs nourishment from enemies in his gut. While he digests, enemies that attack or are attacked by Grendel suffer Viral Damage and energy sources provide more energy. These buffs extend to squad mates.
3. **Pulverize** : Grendel curls into a ball. He heals over time as he rolls, knocking over anyone in his path. Jumping slams Grendel into the ground and generates a damaging shockwave.
4. **Regurgitate** : Violently puke out a bile soaked enemy from Grendel's gut, turning the consumed into a toxic projectile that does bonus damage based on the enemy's Max Health. Nearby enemies are slowed and have their armor dissolved by the bile.

## Augments disponibles
- **Catapult** (Grendel, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Pulverize Augment: to launch Grendel in your aim direction. Costs 5. Crouch to slow down.
- **Gastro** (Grendel, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Regurgitate Augment: Regurgitated enemies bounce up to 3 times, creating gas clouds that last 8s and inflict nearby enemies with Gas Status Effect.
- **Gastroparesis** (Grendel, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Passive Augment: Each enemy consumed adds 15% Extra Gas Damage to Primary and Secondary Weapons.
- **Gourmand** (Grendel, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Feast Augment: Instead of Energy, consumes 200 Health on cast. Glutton grants an additional 150 Armor per enemy.
- **Hearty Nourishment** (Grendel, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Nourish Augment: Clear Status Effects and gain 5s of Status Immunity for each victim in Grendel's stomach.
