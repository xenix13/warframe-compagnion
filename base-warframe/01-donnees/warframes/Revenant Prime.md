# Revenant Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 825 · Armure 135 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura naramon · Polarités naramon, madurai, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

The lost one rises. Reify the essence of the Eidolon as you wield Revenant Prime’s power.

## Passif
Shield depletion smashes enemies within |RADIUS|m with a |DAMAGE| Damage knockdown shockwave.

## Capacités
1. **Enthrall** : Convert a target into a zealous thrall. Thralls turn on their allies and enthrall through damage. On death, they disintegrate into a damaging pillar of energy. The thrall horde remains under Revenants spell until this ability runs out.
2. **Mesmer Skin** : Become enveloped in Sentient energy, redirecting damage and stunning all those who dare attack. Stunned enemies can be Enthralled at no energy cost.
3. **Reave** : Dash through enemies as a wall of sentient energy, leeching shields and health from any encountered, enhanced for thralls.
4. **Danse Macabre** : Erupt with a multitude of Eidolon energy beams and sweep a circle of death around Revenant. The beams will modify their Damage Type to target select defenses, while incoming damage is redirected back into the beams. Hold fire to boost Status Effects and Damage, at the cost of increased energy consumption. Thralls killed by this ability leave overshield pickups.

## Augments disponibles
- **Blinding Reave** (Revenant, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Reave Augment: Reave gains +40% range and enemies are blinded by its fog for 10s.
- **Mesmer Shield** (Revenant, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Mesmer Skin Augment: Revenant receives +50% Mesmer Skin Strength and allies within Affinity Range are granted a maximum of 5 charges.
- **Thrall Pact** (Revenant, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Enthrall Augment: Gain +25% Primary Weapon Damage for each active Thrall.
