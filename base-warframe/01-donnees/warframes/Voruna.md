# Voruna (Warframe)

Santé 455 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 200 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 0.95
Aura vazarin · Polarités madurai, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Four beasts, one heart. Voruna's loyal wolf pack makes for a deadly combination of strength and stealth. Beware the monstrous howl that heralds fangs of vengeance.

## Passif
Voruna never fights alone. In mission, hold Abilities to call upon each wolf's unique Passive power.

## Capacités
1. **Shroud Of Dynar** : (TAP) Dynar shrouds Voruna with invisibility and accelerates her speed. Invisibility ends when Voruna attacks. For a short time after invisibility ends, melee attacks have increased Critical Damage, Status Chance, Critical Chance and inflict Slash Status Effects. Melee kill an enemy during this time to extend the melee buff once per cast. (HOLD) Dynar guards Voruna, increasing her Parkour Velocity.
2. **Fangs Of Raksh** : (TAP) Raksh's fangs tear into your enemies. 5 random Status Effects are applied at 10 Stacks each on an enemy. On target's death, spread the Status Effects to other nearby enemies. (HOLD) Raksh guards Voruna with Status Effect resistance.
3. **Lycath's Hunt** : (TAP) Lycath hunts to sustain the pack. Enemies killed by melee attacks drop health orbs and enemies killed by headshots drop energy orbs. Increase the duration of Lycath's hunt by killing enemies affected by 5 or more Status Effects. (HOLD) Lycath guards Voruna with increased Heavy Attack efficiency.
4. **Ulfrun's Descent** : (TAP) Voruna drops to all fours and prepares 5 brutal charges that lock onto enemies. Ulfrun, the most powerful wolf, leads the attack as Voruna dashes toward her target. The pack deals increased damage to targets and nearby enemies that are inflicted by Status Effects. Lethal attacks double the damage of Voruna's remaining charges. (HOLD) Ulfrun guards Voruna. If Voruna falls during this time, Ulfrun dies in her place.

## Augments disponibles
- **Prey Of Dynar** (Voruna, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Shroud of Dynar Augment: Increase an enemy’s Damage Vulnerability by 50%. Using Fang of Raksh on that enemy increases its spread radius by 150%.
- **Ulfrun's Endurance** (Voruna, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Ulfrun's Descent Augment: During Ulfrun’s attack, enemies that die from Slash Status within 20m restore Voruna’s charges.
