# Helminth (Warframe)

Santé 0 · Bouclier 0 · Armure 0 · Énergie 0 · Vitesse de sprint ?

## Capacités
1. **Energized Munitions** : Increase the efficiency of your ammo consumption.
2. **Master's Summons** : Heal your companion and call it to your side.
3. **Voracious Metastasis** : Consume Energy to heal yourself and grant matching Energy to each ally.
4. **Perspicacity** : Automatically succeed at your next hack attempt.
5. **Sickening Pulse** : Emit a pulse that adds stacks to Status Effects already afflicting enemies. The damage of Slash, Heat, and Toxin effects, excluding those created by Sickening Pulse itself, are added together to create a new instance of the Effect with a refreshed timer
6. **Hideous Resistance** : Grant yourself immunity to Status effects.
7. **Parasitic Armor** : Sacrifice Shields to reinforce Armor for a period of time.
8. **Rebuild Shields** : Instantly restore your shields.
9. **Infested Mobility** : Increase your parkour velocity.
10. **Expedite Suffering** : Condense all Slash and Toxin Status Effects into a single damage instance.
11. **Empower** : Increase the strength of your next ability use.
12. **Marked For Death** : Stun an enemy; a portion of the next damage you deal to it will be dealt to all enemies around it.
13. **Golden Instinct** : Send out a short-lived Void Spark that is drawn toward the nearest Medallion, Ayatan Sculpture, rare crate, unscanned Kuria or unscanned Fragment.
