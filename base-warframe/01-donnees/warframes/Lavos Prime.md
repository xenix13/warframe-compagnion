# Lavos Prime (Warframe)

Santé 600 · Bouclier 310 · Armure 575 · Énergie 0 · Vitesse de sprint 1.15
Aura naramon · Polarités vazarin, naramon, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Some Warframes were Prime from the start. Others ascended. Lavos, the master of transmutation, bowed to no one as he forged his own path to valor.

## Passif
Energy and Universal Orbs give Lavos status immunity for |DURATION|s. Hold any ability to imbue the next cast with additional Elemental Damage and Status.

## Capacités
1. **Ophidian Bite** : Lash out with a toxic serpentine strike, consuming the target to heal Lavos. Hold to imbue all abilities with Toxin.
2. **Vial Rush** : Dash forward, crashing through enemies and leaving an icy trail of broken vials. Hold to imbue the next ability cast with Cold.
3. **Transmutation Probe** : Launch a probe that converts Health and Energy Orbs into Universal Orbs that provide both, and ammo pickups into Universal Ammo Pickups. The probe shocks enemies in close proximity. Each electrocuted foe reduces other ability cooldowns by 1.5s. Hold to imbue the next ability cast with Electricity.
4. **Catalyze** : Catalyst Probes erupt from Lavos and douse combatants in a fiery gel. Damage is doubled for each element afflicting an enemy. Hold to imbue the next ability cast with Heat.

## Augments disponibles
- **Lingering Transmutation** (Lavos, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Transmutation Probe Augment: Probe returns to cast position after reaching max range, and remains nearby for 15s. Recall Probe by recasting. Recast again to end.
- **Swift Bite** (Lavos, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Ophidian Bite Augment: Reduce Ability Cooldowns by 4s when at least 4 enemies are hit. Ophidian Bite is granted 30% additional Ability Range.
- **Valence Formation** (Lavos, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Passive Augment: Casting an Ability imbued with extra Elemental Damage applies that Element as a 200% bonus to your weapons with guaranteed Status for 20s.
