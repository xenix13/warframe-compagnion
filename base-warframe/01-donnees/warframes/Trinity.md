# Trinity (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 105 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura vazarin · Polarités vazarin, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Trinity embodies redemption and health. Her extraordinary healing powers support allies. Where others destroy, she restores.

## Passif
Allies in Affinity Range gain |BUFF|% of Trinity's Max Energy as Health.

## Capacités
1. **Well Of Life** : Create a well of life on an enemy who will absorb Status Effect damage intended for nearby allies. Allies gain additional Health when they attack the target. If allies die, enemies in the well of life die in their stead.
2. **Energy Vampire** : Allies will gain energy over time when enemies are marked with Energy Vampire.
3. **Link** : Any damage taken while Link is active will be channeled to a nearby enemy.
4. **Blessing** : Restore the health and shields of allies within Affinity Range while reducing the damage they take from enemies.

## Augments disponibles
- **Abating Link** (Trinity, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Link Augment: Reduces Armor Rating by 60% on enemies targeted by Link.
- **Champion's Blessing** (Trinity, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Blessing Augment: Gain Primary and Secondary Critical Chance for 30s for each percent you heal on allies up to 350%.
- **Pool Of Life** (Trinity, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Well of Life Augment: On death, marked enemies will drop 4 Health Orbs with a 100% chance of dropping an Energy Orb.
- **Vampire Leech** (Trinity, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Energy Vampire Augment: Excess Energy replenishes Shields by 225%.
