# Khora (Warframe)

Santé 365 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 290 · Énergie 140 · Vitesse de sprint 1.05
Aura vazarin · Exilus naramon · Polarités madurai, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Khora is red in whip and claw. She works together with her Kavat companion, Venari, to deal high damage and provide crowd control. Two bodies, one will.

## Passif
The ferocious kavat, Venari, fights by Khora's side and provides her with a |SPEED|% speed boost while active. If killed, Venari will reappear after |RESPAWN|s.

## Capacités
1. **Whipclaw** : Send enemies reeling with a deafening whipcrack. Stats are boosted by the equipped mods on Whipclaw.
2. **Ensnare** : Bind a hapless target in living metal, entangling others who stray too close. Whipclaw will refresh the trap allowing it to capture more enemies.
3. **Venari** : Command Venari to focus on a target. Hold to cycle between Attack, Protect, and Heal postures. If Venari is killed, use this ability to revive her instantly.
4. **Strangledome** : Weave a dome of living chain that ensnares and strangles any enemy within, and any foolish enough to approach. Foes outside the trap will try to hasten their comrade's deaths by shooting them. Crack Whipclaw on the dome to further damage any trapped enemies.

## Augments disponibles
- **Accumulating Whipclaw** (Khora, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Whipclaw Augment: Hitting 3 enemies will grant a 35% stacking Damage Bonus to subsequent Whipclaws. Bonus will decay after 10s.
- **Pilfering Strangledome** (Khora, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Strangledome Augment: Enemies held in Strangledome have a 65% chance of dropping additional loot.
- **Venari Bodyguard** (Khora, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Venari Augment: Venari dies in Khora's place. Recovery timer increased to 150s. Killing enemies decreases the timer by 4s.
