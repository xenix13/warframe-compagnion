# Ember Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 365 · Armure 160 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura madurai · Polarités madurai, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Ember Prime offers the same potential for wanton destruction as Ember but provides unique mod polarities, allowing for greater customization.

## Passif
Receive |STRENGTH|% Ability Strength for every enemy within Affinity Range affected by Heat.

## Capacités
1. **Fireball** : Release a fiery projectile that ignites enemies on contact.
2. **Immolation** : Protect Ember with flame armor that burns stronger over time, consuming energy once its meter is at full strength. Cast again to extinguish the flame.
3. **Fire Blast** : Slam the ground to create a wave of incinerating plasma that knocks back enemies and strips their armor.
4. **Inferno** : Command a flaming comet to crash down in front of Ember, engulfing enemies with a fire that can spread through their ranks.

## Augments disponibles
- **Exothermic** (Ember, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Inferno Augment: Enemies killed while under the effect of Inferno have a 15% chance to drop an energy orb.
- **Fireball Frenzy** (Ember, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Fireball Augment: Hold to cast will grant all allies within 15m an additional 100% Heat Damage to their attacks for 40s.
- **Healing Flame** (Ember, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Fire Blast Augment: Each enemy hit heals by 25 to 50 depending on current Immolation level. With Ember, over healing grants you Overguard.
- **Immolated Radiance** (Ember, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Immolation Augment: Allies within Affinity range will receive 50% of Immolation's Damage Reduction.
- **Purifying Flames** (Ember, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Fire Blast Augment: Allies hit by the expanding ring of fire are cured of Status Effects and granted 4s of Status Immunity.

Introduite : Update 11.0 (2013-11-20)
