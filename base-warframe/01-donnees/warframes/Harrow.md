# Harrow (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 185 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura naramon · Polarités vazarin, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Achieve redemption through vengeance. The monastic Harrow increases damage to support allies. Defy enemies and ascend.

## Passif
Overshield capacity doubled. Start missions at maximum energy.

## Capacités
1. **Condemn** : Cast a wave of energy that chains them where they stand. Each enemy held reinforces Harrow's shields.
2. **Penance** : Sacrifice Shields to boost Reload Speed, and Fire Rate while converting damage inflicted on enemies into health for Harrow and nearby allies.
3. **Thurible** : Channel Harrow's energy into the Thurible to generate a buff. Once finished, kill enemies to bestow nearby allies with bursts of energy. The more energy channeled the greater the reward for each kill. Weak Point kills produce extra energy.
4. **Covenant** : Protect nearby allies with an energy force that absorbs all damage and converts it to a Critical Chance bonus for all those under the Covenant. Weak Point hits are amplified even further.

## Augments disponibles
- **Lasting Covenant** (Harrow, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Covenant Augment: Weak Point kills increase Critical Chance bonus duration by 3s.
- **Tribunal** (Harrow, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Condemn Augment: Other players will trigger 100% of the effects of Penance and Thurible when attacking chained enemies.
- **Warding Thurible** (Harrow, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Thurible Augment: Allies in range take 50% less Damage while channeling Thurible and grant 1.0 additional Energy charge whenever damaged.
