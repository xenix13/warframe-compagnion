# Nidus (Warframe)

Santé 675 · Bouclier 0 · Armure 350 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités madurai, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Nidus draws strength from the Infestation. It bolsters him with survivability as he deals high damage and provides crowd control. Plague your enemies.

## Passif
If Nidus is killed with at least |STACKS| stacks of Mutation, those |STACKS| stacks are consumed; this grants |DURATION|s of invulnerability and restores Health to |HEAL|%.

## Capacités
1. **Virulence** : Rupture the ground with a damaging fungal growth that steals energy from each enemy it strikes. For every five enemies hit, the Infestation mutates, multiplying its destructive force.
2. **Larva** : Spawn an Infested pod that erupts with tendrils, latches onto nearby enemies and pulls them in. Enemies killed while held have a chance of generating a Mutation stack.
3. **Parasitic Link** : Bind to a target with parasitic link. When cast on an ally, both the host and Nidus have their Ability Strength and Weapon Damage increased. Linked enemies take some of the damage inflicted on Nidus.
4. **Ravenous** : Gluttonous maggots swarm nearby enemies, feasting until they are hit with Virulence and burst with Infestation. The maggots benefit from Mutation and each enemy hit adds to the Mutation stack.

## Augments disponibles
- **Abundant Mutation** (Nidus, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Passive Augment: Nidus gains an additional 300 max stacks of Mutation. Undying has a 30s cooldown.
- **Insatiable** (Nidus, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Ravenous Augment: Nidus has a chance for additional Mutation stacks while in the infestation. The base 60% chance increases with power strength, and multiple stacks are possible above 100%.
- **Larva Burst** (Nidus, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Larva Augment: Reactivate Larva to detonate and deal 600 Toxin Damage in a 8m radius. This damage and Status Effect stacks for every enemy grabbed by the Larva.
- **Parasitic Vitality** (Nidus, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Parasitic Link Augment: Nidus and any ally he's bound to gain 4% Max Health per Mutation during Parasitic Link.
- **Teeming Virulence** (Nidus, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Virulence Augment: Hitting 4 enemies with Virulence grants 200% Primary Weapon Critical Chance for 15s.
