# Baruuk (Warframe)

Santé 180 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 185 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 1.2
Aura naramon · Exilus naramon · Polarités vazarin, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Beware the fury of the truly patient. Baruuk boasts survivability and crowd control. Pushed beyond his restraint, he unleashes the storm within.

## Passif
Each projectile dodged, each enemy lulled or disarmed, erodes Baruuk's restraint and fuels the storm within. As Baruuk's restraint is diminished he becomes up to |PERCENT|% more resistant to damage.

## Capacités
1. **Elude** : Dodge all incoming projectiles, but only while not attacking. Use again to deactivate this ability.
2. **Lull** : A calming wave slows enemies until they fall into a slumber. Enemies woken by damage will be confused and disoriented. Short-term amnesia means all waking enemies forget anything that happened before the lull.
3. **Desolate Hands** : Summon a bevy of orbiting daggers to seek out enemy guns, destroying them with a small explosion. Combine with Elude to double the range.
4. **Serene Storm** : With his Restraint eroded, Baruuk commands the Desert Wind to deliver powerful radial strikes with his fists and feet. Each moment commanding the storm restores his Restraint.

## Augments disponibles
- **Elusive Retribution** (Baruuk, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Elude Augment: Every attack Baruuk Eludes increases his Melee Attack Speed and Melee Critical Damage by 5% up to 6 stacks. Stacks last 6s.
- **Endless Lullaby** (Baruuk, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Lull Augment: Performing a finisher on or killing a sleeping enemy will retrigger Lull for 100% of the remaining duration. Passive: +50% Lull Duration.
- **Reactive Storm** (Baruuk, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Serene Storm Augment: Desert Wind is granted +250% Status Chance and changes its damage type to match enemy weaknesses.
