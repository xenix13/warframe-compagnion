# Nyx Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 365 · Armure 135 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1.12
Aura naramon · Polarités madurai, naramon, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Infiltrate the minds of your enemies with Nyx Prime. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
Nyx gains +|CRITICAL|% Critical Chance on Primary and Secondary weapons per Confused enemy within Affinity Range, up to +|MAX|%.

## Capacités
1. **Mind Control** : Nyx seizes control of a target's mind, compelling them to fight for the Tenno cause. Controlled enemies have increased Radiation Status Chance. When Nyx shoots them, they also have an increased Damage Multiplier.
2. **Psychic Bolts** : Nyx unleashes a volley of psychic bolts that track and strike nearby enemies with telekinetic precision. When enemies are slain, additional bolts scatter to new targets. Striking foes weakens their defenses and transfers a portion to Nyx.
3. **Chaos** : Nyx releases a devastating psychic pulse, disorienting enemies in a wide radius and forcing them to turn on each other. Confused foes lash out at random factions.
4. **Absorb** : Nyx draws in and contains the damage dealt to her and the damage confused enemies deal to each other then converts it into a devastating radial blast. Following the blast, she enters a brief invulnerable state and gains a Weapon Damage boost proportional to the absorbed damage.

## Augments disponibles
- **Assimilate** (Nyx, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Absorb Augment: Nyx can use weapons during Absorb and its duration is infinite, but it consumes 6.5 Energy/s and its area of effect is halved.
- **Chaos Sphere** (Nyx, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Chaos Augment: Enemies entering the Effect Range will be inflicted with Chaos. Effect Range lasts for 50% of the ability duration and shrinks over time.
- **Mind Freak** (Nyx, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Mind Control Augment: Controlled target inflicts +1000% Damage and gains +25% Movement and Attack Speed.
- **Pacifying Bolts** (Nyx, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Psychic Bolts Augment: Throw an additional 3 force bolts with guaranteed Radiation Status Effect. Enemies struck are confused for 10s.
- **Singularity** (Nyx, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Absorb Augment: Create a ring every 3s that drags in enemies at 15m/s.
