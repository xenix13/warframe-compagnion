# Inaros Prime (Warframe)

Santé 2215 · Bouclier 0 · Armure 240 · Énergie 140 · Vitesse de sprint 1.05
Aura naramon · Exilus naramon · Polarités madurai, vazarin, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Rise, eternal, to cast the fear of Inaros into them. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
When Inaros takes lethal damage, he entombs himself in a sarcophagus and incarnates as sand to attack enemies, draining their lifeforce to revive himself.

## Capacités
1. **Desiccation** : Blast enemies with a wave of cursed sand that blinds them and steals their health.
2. **Sandstorm** : Become a sandstorm. Inaros devours enemies pulled into his whirlwind, healing himself.
3. **Scarab Shell** : Activate to form Armor by draining Inaros' Health. Activate again to stop the formation early. The protective layer reduces incoming Damage and absorbs Status Effects.
4. **Scarab Swarm** : Summon a Scarab Swarm to attack enemies with guaranteed Corrosive Status. Damage scales with Inaros' Health. Enemies killed while immersed in the swarm summon a Swarm Kavat that fights alongside Inaros and guides the scarabs to other enemies.

## Augments disponibles
- **Desiccation's Curse** (Inaros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Desiccation Augment: Killing a blinded enemy with a Finisher has a 100% chance to summon a Swarm Kavat that will spread Scarab Swarm. Maximum Swarm Kavats +2.
- **Elemental Sandstorm** (Inaros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Sandstorm Augment: Sandstorm gains 50% Ability Range and has a 100% chance of inflicting Status Effects based on the Damage Types on the equipped Melee Weapon.
- **Negation Armor** (Inaros, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Scarab Shell Augment: When Inaros takes fatal damage, he consumes Scarab Shell to heal with a brief invulnerability that ends with a deadly Slash Status Effect. Cooldown 30s.
