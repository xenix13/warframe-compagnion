# Titania (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 270 · Armure 105 · Énergie 175 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités madurai, vazarin
Maîtrise requise 0

Titania’s fairy-like appearance belies her formidable regal power. The enchantress deals high damage and provides crowd control. She forces opponents to pay homage or perish.

## Passif
Titania generates Health for herself and nearby allies every time she casts an Ability.

## Capacités
1. **Spellbind** : Enemies fumble their weapons as they are whisked into the air. Nearby allies become immune to Status Effects. Hold the ability to cast the immunity onto Titania.
2. **Tribute** : Cycle through and extract one of the four Buffs when cast on an enemy. Thorns reduces incoming damage. Dust degrades enemy accuracy. Full Moon increases companion damage. Entangle slows enemies within range.
3. **Lantern** : Create a swarm of razorflies that transform an enemy into an irresistible floating beacon, attracting witless comrades before exploding on command.
4. **Razorwing** : Shrink down and take flight, while razorflies attack nearby enemies and amplify the damage they take.

## Augments disponibles
- **Beguiling Lantern** (Titania, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Lantern Augment: Attracted enemies take 100% more Weapon Damage.
- **Ironclad Flight** (Titania, zenurik, Rare, coût 7 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Disable vacuum in Razorwing. Reduced Damage by 40% while Airborne.
- **Razorwing Blitz** (Titania, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Razorwing Augment: Flight Speed increased by 25% and Fire Rate increased by 25% for 8s when using abilities. Stacks up to 4x.
- **Spellbound Harvest** (Titania, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Spellbind Augment: Hitting at least 4 enemies with Spellbind grants Titania 50. The next cast has +40% Ability Range.
