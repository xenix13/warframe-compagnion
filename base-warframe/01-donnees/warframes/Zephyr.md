# Zephyr (Warframe)

Santé 455 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 105 · Énergie 100 · Vitesse de sprint 1.1
Aura vazarin · Polarités madurai, naramon
Maîtrise requise 0

Command the power of the wind. Zephyr’s cyclonic abilities protect her, deal high damage, and provide crowd control. She swoops in from above and leaves a path of destruction.

## Passif
Zephyr moves faster and falls slower while airborne. Also gain |CRIT|% Critical Hit chance with weapons while airborne.

## Capacités
1. **Tail Wind** : Hold while airborne to hover Zephyr with reduced movement. From the air, tap to dash forward, or aim down to dive bomb enemies below.
2. **Airburst** : Launch a burst of massively dense air. Hold to send enemies flying, tap to pull them toward the burst. Damage increases per enemy hit.
3. **Turbulence** : Creates a wind shield around Zephyr, redirecting all incoming projectiles.
4. **Tornado** : Create deadly tornadoes that seek out and engulf enemies. Tornadoes deal the elemental Damage Type they absorb the most. Shoot engulfed enemies to inflict extra damage. Hold for stationary tornadoes or tap for wandering ones.

## Augments disponibles
- **Airburst Rounds** (Zephyr, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Airburst Augment: Each enemy hit by Airburst increases secondary damage by 40% for 14s.
- **Anchored Glide** (Zephyr, zenurik, Rare, coût 7 au rang max, Exilus, Augment) : Disable Zephyr's reduced airborne gravity. Increase Ability Strength by 15%.
- **Funnel Clouds** (Zephyr, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Tornado Augment: Creates 8 additional tornadoes. All tornadoes are 50% their original size and won't pick up enemies.
- **Jet Stream** (Zephyr, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Turbulence Augment: Turbulence increases Movement Speed by 40% and Projectile Speed by 100% for Zephyr and her allies.
- **Target Fixation** (Zephyr, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Tail Wind Augment: Casting Tail Wind resets Zephyr’s bullet jump. Each enemy hit increases Tail Wind Damage by 100%. Damage resets upon being on the ground for 2s.
