# Volt Prime (Warframe)

Santé 270 · Bouclier 455 · Armure 135 · Énergie 200 · Vitesse de sprint 1
Aura madurai · Polarités naramon, madurai, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

A glorious warrior from the past, Volt Prime features the same abilities as Volt but with unique mod polarities for greater customization.

## Passif
Grounded movement generates an electrical charge building up |DAMAGE| Damage per meter that is unleashed with the next attack.

## Capacités
1. **Shock** : Launch a voltaic projectile that stuns and damages its target. A chain of electricity extends from the target to shock nearby enemies.
2. **Speed** : Embody an electric current. Volt and his allies receive a brief movement speed boost and a reload speed buff.
3. **Electric Shield** : Volt deploys an electric shield that blocks enemy fire and adds Electricity Damage to projectiles. HOLD to equip the shield for mobile cover.
4. **Discharge** : Volt discharges the electricity that courses through him. The shockwave paralyzes and damages nearby enemies. Enemies on the edge of the shockwave are stunned.

## Augments disponibles
- **Capacitance** (Volt, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Discharge Augment: Converts 3% of Damage dealt into Shields split between Volt and Squadmates.
- **Kinetic Collision** (Volt, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Speed Augment: Running into an opponent increases the duration of Speed by 4s.
- **Recharge Barrier** (Volt, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Electric Shield Augment: Allies that pass through have 35% Shields restored.
- **Shock Trooper** (Volt, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Shock Augment: Hold to cast will grant all allies within 15m an additional 100% Electricity Damage to their attacks for 40s.
- **Shocking Speed** (Volt, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Speed Augment: While moving under the effects of Speed, enemies within 3m will take 300 Electricity Damage with guaranteed Status effect.
- **Transistor Shield** (Volt, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Electric Shield Augment: Allies can pick up Electric Shield. 300% of damage absorbed will be added to Volt's Static Discharge.
