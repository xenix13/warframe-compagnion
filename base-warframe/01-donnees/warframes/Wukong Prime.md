# Wukong Prime (Warframe)

Santé 455 · Bouclier 345 · Armure 290 · Énergie 145 · Vitesse de sprint 1.05
Aura madurai · Polarités naramon, vazarin, madurai
Maîtrise requise 0

The trickster-warrior ascends to his ultimate incarnation.

## Passif
After taking fatal damage Wukong automatically uses one of his mastered survival techniques. These techniques can only be invoked three times per mission.

## Capacités
1. **Celestial Twin** : Shedding part of himself, Wukong creates a twin to fight by his side. Attack at range and the twin will melee, pull a blade and the twin will lay down covering fire. Use again to command the twin to attack a target with increased damage.
2. **Cloud Walker** : Evaporate into a cloud of mist and float through the battlefield, dazing any enemies encountered, while healing Wukong and his twin.
3. **Defy** : Wukong and his twin become invulnerable and defy enemies to attack. All damage is captured, stored, and dealt back in a single furious strike of Wukong's staff. Bonus Armor is then granted relative to the damage captured.
4. **Primal Fury** : Summon the iron staff and unleash fury.

## Augments disponibles
- **Celestial Stomp** (Wukong, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Celestial Twin Augment: Hold to command the twin to perform a slam attack suspending enemies in the air within 20m for 25 Energy.
- **Enveloping Cloud** (Wukong, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Cloud Walker Augment: Allies within 4m of the cloud become invisible to enemies for 14s.
- **Primal Rage** (Wukong, zenurik, Rare, coût 9 au rang max, Augment) : Primal Fury Augment: Killing an enemy increases Critical Chance by 15%. The increase decays by 1%/s
