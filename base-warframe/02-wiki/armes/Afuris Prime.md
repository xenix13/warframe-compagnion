Afuris Prime is the Primed counterpart of the Afuris akimbo pistols, acting as a direct upgrade. Afuris Prime was released alongside Baruuk Prime and Cobra & Crane Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average sustained DPS (317.47)
- Very high magazine size (80)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Above average accuracy (2.00° to 5.00°)
- Above average reload speed (1.20 s)
- High ammo max (400)
- Very high fire rate (12.50 attacks/sec)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Low total damage (26)
- Low damage per shot (30.16)
