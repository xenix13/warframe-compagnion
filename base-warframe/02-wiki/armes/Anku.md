Reap havoc with this full sized Tenno-crafted scythe.

The Anku is a Puncture-focused Tenno scythe whose design is aesthetically derived from the Paris.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- stance polarity matches Stalking Fan.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Ether Reaper, Reaper Prime, and Tenet Grigori for the fastest attack speed of all scythes.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all scythes.
- Lowest status chance of all scythes.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (170)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (220.32)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (340)
- Below average damage × attack speed (440.64)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Below average total damage (510)
- Below average damage × attack speed (660.96)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Anku Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Anku with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Anku Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Ueymag, and 300 Ariette Scale.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
