Perforate Techrot, and anyone else who stands in your way, with this Scaldra machine gun. Alternative fire pummels targets with Efervon laced buckshot.

EFV-5 Jupiter is a Scaldra assault rifle used by Scaldra Jaegers. Inflicts Corrosive damage with a highly accurate fully automatic primary fire while its Alternate Fire charges its underslung shotgun to fire.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Alternate Fire charges for 1 second to release close-ranged buckshot.
- Innate multishot of 11 pellets.
- Consumes 15 ammo per shot.
- Projectiles have a guaranteed Corrosive proc.

- Innate two polarities.
- Can use the EFV-5 Jupiter-exclusive Overpressured Rounds mod.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Auto (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average burst DPS (608.28)
- Above average sustained DPS (467.45)
- High magazine size (65)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Very high accuracy (0.50° to 1.00°)
- High ammo max (540)
- Above average fire rate (7.83 attacks/sec)
- Above average status chance (25.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Buckshot (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high average number of procs per shot (11.63)
- High maximum falloff distance (10.0 m)
- Very high burst DPS (1719.70)
- Very high sustained DPS (1058.28)
- Very high fire rate (7.83 attacks/sec)
- Very high multishot (11)
- Very high average number of crits per shot (2.31)
- Very high accuracy (24.00° to 42.00°)
- High magazine size (65)
- Very high total damage (1,397)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- High ammo max (540)
- Very high damage per shot (1719.70)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Alternate Fire:
- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 0.79% from 5m to 10m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Cannot equip Terminal Velocity.

- Auto (wiki attack index 1)

- Buckshot (wiki attack index 2)
