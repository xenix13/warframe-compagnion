Purgator 1 is a Scaldra slug launcher used by Scaldra Dedicants. Shoots highly damaging single-target rounds that causes enemies to release a Gas cloud upon their death, but carries extremely low reserve ammo and slow reload speed.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Enemies directly killed by Purgator 1 leave a Gas status effect behind.
- Innate 1.1 meter punch through and Infinite Body Punch Through

Advantages over other Primary Launcher weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (31.00%)
- Very high burst DPS (1643.61)
- Very high sustained DPS (1133.52)
- Very high magazine size (9)
- Very high total damage (781)
- Very high damage per shot (1095.74)

Disadvantages over other Primary Launcher weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low ammo pickup (2)
- Low reload speed (2.70 s)
- Low ammo max (20)
- Low fire rate (1.50 attacks/sec)
- Below average status chance (19.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Enemies directly killed by the Purgator 1 leave behind 1 Gas status effect stack.
- Kills must be directly from the projectile itself, not from any Damage over Time Status Effects. DoT kills will generate clouds that deal no damage.

- The Purgator 1's gas cloud deals damage each tick equal to half of the killing shot's damage, multiplied by any faction damage, status damage, or unique Gas damage bonuses.
- Gas damage is affected by damage bonuses, critical hits, faction damage, headshots and status damage mods.
- Gas damage is affected by critical damage and the critical tier of the shot that spawned the gas cloud.
- Faction damage mods apply both to the killing blow, and to when the gas cloud applies its damage, effectively acting as a multiplier of 1.69x on Bane mods, or 2.40x for Primed Bane mods.
- Damage is affected by the 3.0x weakpoint multiplier and by bonuses to weakpoint damage, such as Primary Acuity.
- Multishot does not affect gas cloud damage.
- Status chance has no effect regarding the gas cloud.
- Elemental mods and abilities that add elemental damage to weapons do not affect gas cloud damage, not even mods that combine into Gas.
- However, if the killing blow also procs Gas on the target as a result of mods, the two Gas status effects will combine their damage.

- Armor, damage resistance/vulnerability, and elemental resistance/vulnerability to the Purgator 1's modded elemental damage do not affect the value of the gas.

- Gas clouds apply damage in the same tick as the kill occurs. This damage applies instantaneously, and will apply faster than Purgator 1's projectile can travel. This means that collateral targets may be hit with gas before the projectile.

## Tips

- Purgator 1's slow fire rate and innate Impact can benefit the most from Internal Bleeding.
- The weapon can be used to make gas traps that further enemies can rush into for free, passive damage. Boosting status duration with Hunter Track, Continuous Misery and/or Prismatic Gem can greatly extend the duration of the lingering cloud.
- Abilities such as the Noise Arrow from Quiver can lure enemies into the clouds while cloaked.
- Abilities that speed enemies up such as Molecular Prime can make enemies rush faster into these traps and Molecular Prime specifically also doubles the damage enemies take from the clouds.

- To maximize the gas clouds as your primary source of damage, prioritize critical hits, weakpoint hits, status damage and faction damage, as well as general damage bonuses.
- Crit builds are a viable way to increase gas cloud damage, since the critical tier of the hit that spawned the gas cloud affects the gas cloud's damage over time.
- Weakpoint hits typically increase the damage of the hit by 3.0x, and this bonus is applied to the gas cloud.
- Primary Acuity causes the weakpoint multiplier to more than double to 8.25x, and massively increases the crit chance.
- Primary Deadhead synergizes with this playstyle, as does Galvanized Scope and Bladed Rounds.

- Faction damage mods (such as Primed Bane of The Murmur) will apply first to the primary fire and then again to the gas clouds, a primed Bane mod will increase the damage of the gas clouds by 2.40x.
- Rifle Elementalist nearly doubles the effect of gas clouds, and extra punch-through improves the odds of a collateral projectile kill.
- Semi-Rifle Cannonade offers a moderate-sized boost to damage and punch-through with effectively no downside, since the low ammo max on the weapon already makes high rate-of-fire builds impractical.
- Multishot and elemental damage can be safely ignored.

- Mesa's Ballistic Battery will be charged by and boost the Gas cloud damage of the Purgator 1.
- Banshee's Sonar can be very effective as the gas clouds can hit Sonar's weakpoints if they happen to attach to the body/midsection of enemies.
- This is further improved by utilizing Resonance as weakpoint kills by the gas clouds will further spread the ability effect.
