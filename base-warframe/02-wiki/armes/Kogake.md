The Kogake is a Tenno sparring melee weapon.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000. It is also a weapon crafting ingredient for the Hirudo.

- Kogake → Hirudo

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Brutal Tide and Vicious Approach (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Grants a 5% Movement Speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Obex for the lowest base damage of all sparring weapons.
- Tied with Kogake Prime for the second slowest attack speed of all sparring weapons, after Korrudo.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (120)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (132.04)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low total damage (240)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low damage × attack speed (264.09)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low total damage (360)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low damage × attack speed (396.14)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Kogake (Normal Attack), compared to Kogake Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (120.00 vs. 242.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 84 vs. 169.4)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 18 vs. 36.3)
- Lower Slash damage ( 18 vs. 36.3)

- Lower total damage (120 vs. 242)
- Higher base critical chance (20.00% vs. 16.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 1.80x)
- Lower base status chance (10.00% vs. 38.00%)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (2 vs. 10)

## Notes

- With the Kogake equipped, Tenno will utilize kicks in their melee attacks.
- Ground Finisher will hit the downed enemy six times, each hit dealing 17 Impact, 20.6 Puncture, and 0.6 Slashing (total 38.2, grand total 229.2). This is also able to hit multiple enemies. The damage of this is increased by Pressure Point, Spoiled Strike, and Finishing Touch.
- Wall attacks with the Kogake deal Magnetic damage.
