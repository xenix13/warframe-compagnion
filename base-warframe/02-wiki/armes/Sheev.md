Built for close combat, the Sheev's plasma blade slices through flesh and armor with equal ease.

The Sheev is a razor-like dagger of Grineer design that serves as a backup in the event that a Grineer is disarmed by any means. It offers high Slash damage and status, and the tip of the blade glows with charged plasma that allows Heat procs on Slam Attacks, but has the slowest attack speed of any dagger.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Can use the dagger-exclusive Covert Lethality mod.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Stinging Thorn and Pointed Wind stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High total damage (270)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High total damage (540)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High status chance (25.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High total damage (810)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High status chance (25.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Karyst Prime for the slowest attack speed of all single daggers; additionally tied with Pathocyst and Plasma Sword for the slowest attack speed of all melees.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low attack speed (0.667x animation speed)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Low damage × attack speed (205.84)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Below average damage × attack speed (411.68)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Very low attack speed (0.667x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Below average damage × attack speed (617.52)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Very low attack speed (0.667x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- The Slam Radial Damage has a small chance to inflict the Heat status effect, but is unaffected by mods.
- When equipped without a Stance mod, the Sheev adds a 4th lunging stab on its normal attack combo that propels the Warframe a good distance forward, which is useful for catching up to moving enemies. It also has a 100% chance of inflicting Slash bleed proc. This effect is lost if one uses the Pointed Wind stance, which replaces the lunge with a sweeping slash.
- If the lunge lands behind an unalerted enemy, a stealth attack will be performed instead.

## Tips

- This weapon's high status chance make it ideal for status builds using mods like Virulent Scourge and Vicious Frost.
