These Wraith gauntlets have been augmented for power.

The Furax Wraith is the Wraith variant of the Furax, featuring both improved critical statistics and increased initial Combo Count.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Has an initial Combo Count of 20 (2.0x heavy attack multiplier).
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Seismic Palm and Gaia's Tragedy stances.
- Can use the Furax-exclusive Amalgam Furax Body Count mod.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.70x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.70x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (139)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)

## Notes

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Furax Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Furax with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Furax Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 300 Ariette Scales.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
- On the Furax Wraith, it is possible to start at a 6x combo multiplier by raising Initial Combo to 100 or above.
- Furax Wraith's passive (+20 initial combo), combined with the Corrupt Charge melee mod (+30), the Ready Steel aura mod (+24), and a Riven Mod with at least +26 Initial Combo will start the weapon at 6x multiplier, thereby making an Incarnon Transformation available at all times.

## Tips

- It is recommended to use a heavy crit build with the Furax Wraith due to its innate Initial Combo, and high crit chance and damage.
- The Incarnon form of Furax Wraith has high potential as a Heavy Slam build, due to the innate 90% efficiency of Heavy Slams while in Incarnon form, which enables non-stop Heavy Slam attacks without needing to slow down to rebuild combo.
- In order to carry out a fast tempo of heavy slam attacks, Parkour Velocity would be required. Increases to the Slam Damage stat would be effective at increasing DPS. This combination of requirements makes Nira's Anguish a prime choice, since it offers both.

- The innate Heat of the fields generated by Heavy Slam while in Incarnon form will combine with modded elements.
- The Kinetic Killer Incarnon perk does not necessarily apply to attacks initiated from a slide (including slide attacks and sliding heavy attacks), but it does apply to any attacks that make contact while the player is sliding.
- This means that potentially any attack can benefit from the increased base crit multiplier with a well-timed slide, including Heavy Attacks and Heavy Slams (although the input timing window for a slide during a Heavy Slam attack is much narrower).

- Before Update 26.0 (aka Melee 3.0), the short reach of Fists weapons and lack of Stance mods meant that using Furax Wraith required extreme close contact with the enemy. Wielders of these fists were vulnerable to staggers, knockdowns, and knockbacks, sometimes struggling to land hits even when right against the enemy.
- In Melee 3.0, Furax Wraith's melee attack range was increased from 0.5m to 1.25m, which significantly alleviated the problem. Reach ( Primed) and Incarnon Evolution Moonrise Velocity can further extend the reach of these fists.

- Evasive rolling maneuvers can allow the user to dart in and out of enemy melee range, while delivering high-powered attacks.
- The execution blow has a small AOE which can hit units overlapping the executed unit.
