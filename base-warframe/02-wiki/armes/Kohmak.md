The Kohmak, similar to the larger Kohm, is a hand-shotgun that doubles its volley with every successive shot. It can be researched from the Chem Lab in the Clan Dojo.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000; it is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Twin Kohmak (x2).

- Kohmak + Kohmak → Twin Kohmak

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Secondary Shotgun Sidearm weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- High overall status chance.
- Second largest magazine size of all shotgun secondaries, behind Twin Kohmak.
- Innate 1.5 meters punch through.
- Innate multishot that increases with auto-spool, each consecutive shot adds an additional pellet, up to a maximum of 5 pellets.
- Single Pellet (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high magazine size (40)
- Above average accuracy (5.00° to 20.00°)
- Very high status chance (69.00%)
- High crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Fully Spooled (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high fire rate (5.00 attacks/sec)
- High accuracy (5.00° to 20.00°)
- Very high magazine size (40)
- Very high ammo pickup (30)
- Very high ammo max (210)

Disadvantages over other Secondary Shotgun Sidearm weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Twin Kohmak for lowest base damage of all shotgun secondaries.
- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 66.67% from 12m to 24m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Requires a spool-up of 4 and 5 rounds before reaching optimal fire rate and multishot respectively. Fire rate starts at 20% of the listed value, and increases by 32% per shot.
- Consumes 1.67 ammo per shot at full spool-up.
- Accuracy decreases during spool-up.
- Single Pellet (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average active falloff slope (14.4m/%)
- Below average crit chance (11.00%)
- Very low burst DPS (33.3)
- Very low sustained DPS (31.71)
- Very low total damage (30)
- Very low reload speed (2.00 s)
- Very low damage per shot (33.3)
- Very low fire rate (1.00 attacks/sec)

- Fully Spooled (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low average number of procs per shot (0.69)
- Low crit chance (11.00%)
- Low maximum falloff distance (24.0 m)
- Low burst DPS (832.5)
- Very low multishot (5)
- Very low crit multiplier (2.00x)
- Low active falloff slope (14.4m/%)
- Low average number of crits per shot (0.55)
- Very low total damage (150)
- Very low reload speed (2.00 s)
- Very low damage per shot (166.5)

Comparisons:

- Kohmak (Fully Spooled), compared to Twin Kohmak (Fully Spooled):
- Equal base damage
- Lower burst DPS (832.5 vs. 1110.55)
- Lower sustained DPS (580.22 vs. 846.32)
- Lower fire rate (5.00 attacks/sec vs. 6.67 attacks/sec)
- Smaller magazine (40 vs. 80)
- Fewer shots per magazine (23 vs. 47)

- Tighter first-shot spread (5.00° vs. 10.00°)
- Tighter maximum spread (20.00° vs. 25.00°)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (5 vs. 10)

## Notes

- The Kohmak fires an increasing number of pellets the longer it is fired, until it reaches its maximum pellet count of five pellets per shot. It will also consume 1.6667(5/3) ammo per shot at maximum spool.
- With its base magazine size and fired from rest to full spool, the Kohmak can effectively fire 25 shots, with one round left in the magazine. The number of shots per magazine can be increased by firing the Kohmak slowly to prevent spool-up, allowing it to fire its maximum of 40 shots, but at the cost of effective damage.
