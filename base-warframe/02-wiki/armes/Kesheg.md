Slice through mobs of unruly enemies with this fierce halberd; the signature weapon of the Kuva Guardians.

The Kesheg is the signature polearm of the Grineer Kuva Guardians.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Shimmering Blight and Argent Scourge (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all polearms behind Pupacyst.
- Tied with Vaykor Sydon for the highest critical multiplier of all polearms.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (241)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (482)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (515.93)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High total damage (723)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (773.90)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Guandao and Pupacyst for the slowest attack speed of all polearms.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Below average status chance (23.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
