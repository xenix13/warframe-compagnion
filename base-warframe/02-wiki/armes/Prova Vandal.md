A customized version of the vicious Prova, with metallic finish and Lotus decal.

The Prova Vandal is the Vandal version of the Prova melee weapon, offering greater damage and status chance.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals both Impact and Electricity damage.
- Normal slam attacks have a forced Electricity proc.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sundering Weave, Cyclone Kraken and Rending Wind (Conclave only) stance.
- Can use the Prova-exclusive Static Discharge mod.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (32.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (14.00%)
- Below average total damage (198)
- Below average damage × attack speed (225.72)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (14.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (14.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Prova Vandal (Normal Attack), compared to Prova (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (198.00 vs. 128.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 80 vs. 52)
- Higher Electricity damage ( 118 vs. 76)

- Higher total damage (198 vs. 128)
- Higher base status chance (32.00% vs. 16.00%)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (8 vs. 3)
