The choice weapon of Mirage, these fast-firing dual pistols deliver a deadly performance.

The Akzani are Mirage's signature rapid firing dual pistols that rely on deep magazines to fuel its high projectile count per second. While they deal low damage per bullet, they sport a larger ammo reserve than other similar pistols which can help relieve ammo inefficiency.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon primarily deals Puncture damage.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high magazine size (100)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- High ammo max (400)
- Very high fire rate (20.00 attacks/sec)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all secondaries.
- Second lowest base damage per bullet of all secondaries, after Twin Grakatas.

- Very inefficient ammo economy.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (14.00%)
- Below average burst DPS (273.6)
- Below average sustained DPS (195.42)
- Very low total damage (12)
- Below average accuracy (4.00° to 8.00°)
- Low reload speed (2.00 s)
- Below average status chance (14.00%)
- Very low damage per shot (13.68)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Currently, Akzani lacks a Signature Weapon bonus with Mirage/ Mirage Prime.
- With a maxed rank Trick Mag mod, the Akzani can increase its capacity to hold 760 rounds of ammo.
- The Akzani have very similar stats to the Dex Furis, though the former has lower overall damage and half status chance.
