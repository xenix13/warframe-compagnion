A relic from the Great Plague, the Mire Infested sword is a brutal weapon. It not only cuts into the enemy but it infects the wounds of its victim with a toxic nerve poison. Deals Toxin damage on ground slam attacks.

The Mire is an Infested longsword with innate Toxin damage.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Toxin damage.
- Slam Attacks have a forced Toxin status effect.
- Can use the Mire-exclusive Toxic Blight mod.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crimson Dervish, Swooping Falcon, and Vengeful Revenant.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fastest attack speed of all swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High status chance (31.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High status chance (31.00%)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High status chance (31.00%)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (9.00%)
- Low total damage (158)
- Low damage × attack speed (178.31)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (9.00%)
- Below average total damage (316)
- Low damage × attack speed (356.63)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (9.00%)
- Below average total damage (474)
- Low damage × attack speed (534.95)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
