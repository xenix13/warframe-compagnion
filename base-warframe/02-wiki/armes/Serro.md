Developed as an energy saw to scrap obsolete ships, this tool was quickly banned when rebels in the Sedna region discovered it also made an excellent weapon.

The Serro is a Corpus polearm with energy razors attached to one end that deals Electricity damage.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Bleeding Willow and Twirling Spire stances.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (234)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (468)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (702)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third lowest critical chance of all polearms, after Orthos/ Cassowar and Tonbo.
- Tied with Orthos and Pupacyst for the second lowest critical multiplier of all polearms, after Cassowar.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Below average damage × attack speed (223.16)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (446.32)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- When combining elements, the innate Electricity damage is added last.
- The Serro has a chance to inflict Blast status effect on its jump attacks.
- The Serro is one of the few Clan Tech weapons that doesn't require Forma to craft, along with the Nami Skyla, Amprex, Anku, and Tonbo.
- With maxed rank dual-stat mods Virulent Scourge, Voltaic Strike, Volcanic Edge, and Vicious Frost the Serro can achieve a status chance of 85%. Drifting Contact and Melee Prowess increases this further to 98.75%.
