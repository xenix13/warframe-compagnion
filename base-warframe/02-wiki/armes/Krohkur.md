This hooked blade rewards critical hits to those skilled enough to strike with finesse.

The Krohkur is a standalone Grineer sword sporting high base damage, critical chance and critical multiplier, and good status chance, but with slow attack speed.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000. This weapon is a Weapon crafting ingredient (x2) for Twin Krohkur.

- Krohkur + Krohkur → Twin Krohkur

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crimson Dervish, Swooping Falcon and Vengeful Revenant.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third highest base damage of all swords, behind Dark Sword and Pangolin Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (29.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (29.00%)
- Above average total damage (434)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (29.00%)
- Above average total damage (651)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Low status chance (19.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
