The sensual lines of these golden Kamas have long been celebrated as a masterwork of Tenno weapon smithing.

The Dual Kamas Prime are the Primed versions of the Dual Kamas, featuring higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance. It was released alongside Trinity Prime and Kavasa Prime Collar.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (160)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Below average damage × attack speed (224.64)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (320)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Below average total damage (480)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Dual Kamas Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Dual Kamas (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (160.00 vs. 96.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 8 vs. 4.8)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 32 vs. 9.6)
- Higher Slash damage ( 120 vs. 81.6)

- Higher total damage (160 vs. 96)
- Higher base critical chance (20.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 1.50x)
- Higher base status chance (25.00% vs. 15.00%)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (8 vs. 1)
