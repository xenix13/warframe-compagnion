The Scoliac Whip is a nasty parasitic growth of sinew and bone that inflicts heart stopping Toxin damage with attacks.

The Scoliac is an Infested whip with Toxin damage on its slam attacks.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Slam attack deals Toxin damage, bypassing Shields.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Coiling Viper and Lashing Coil (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average status chance (29.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Low total damage (150)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low damage × attack speed (199.68)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Low total damage (300)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (399.37)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Low total damage (450)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (599.06)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
