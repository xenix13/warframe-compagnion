The glorious original Orokin casting of Gara’s signature glass hammer. Deceptively light and swift, while remaining lethally effective.

Volnus Prime is the primed variant of Volnus, with improvements to its damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance. Volnus Prime was released alongside Gara Prime and Astilla Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Volnus for the fastest attack speed of all hammers.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High total damage (250)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack speed (1.200x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High status chance (34.00%)
- High damage × attack speed (348)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High total damage (500)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (696)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.200x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High total damage (750)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (1044)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.200x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low crit multiplier (1.80x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Volnus Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Volnus (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (250.00 vs. 220.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 55 vs. 48.4)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 80 vs. 70.4)
- Higher Slash damage ( 115 vs. 101.2)

- Higher total damage (250 vs. 220)
- Higher base critical chance (20.00% vs. 18.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (1.80x vs. 1.60x)

## Notes

- As a Signature Weapon, Volnus Prime gains a bonus effect with Gara or Gara Prime, gaining 100% additional radial slam attack damage.
