LEVERIAN ENTRY

The Tekko are, perhaps my favorite pieces in this gallery. Note the intricate, ornate mouldings, the complex blades. Quite the contrast from Atlas's otherwise workmanlike appearance.

The beauty and craftsmanship conceal the true purpose of the Tekko, as indentations found in cultist's skulls attest.
I have to wonder what frenzied dance would have been interrupted, or, if the whiplash strikes and jabs of the Tekko might have blended into the crowd's fitful celebration?

The Tekko are Atlas' signature fist weapons. Armed with razors, they boast high critical chance at the expense of range and status chance. Additionally, they gain increased status chance while wielded by Atlas.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance polarity has polarity, matches Seismic Palm and Gaia's Tragedy stances.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Slowest attack speed of all fists.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (160)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (190.73)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (320)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low damage × attack speed (381.47)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average total damage (480)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low damage × attack speed (572.20)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

## Notes

- As a Signature Weapon, Tekko gains a bonus effect with Atlas or Atlas Prime, gaining an additional 6% base Status Chance, for a total of 16%.

## Tips

- With a rank-10 Blood Rush, the Tekko can achieve over 100% critical chance at a 5x combo multiplier, allowing this weapon to deal orange crits.
