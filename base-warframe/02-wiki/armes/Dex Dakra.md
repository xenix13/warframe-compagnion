CODEX
A gift from the Lotus to commemorate the awakening of the first Tenno from Cryo stasis.

The Dex Dakra is a Dex unique dual-sword melee weapon released in celebration of WARFRAME's 2 Anniversary.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Swirling Tiger stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Dual Cleavers and Dual Skana for the slowest attack speed of all dual swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Low total damage (142)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.883x animation speed)
- Very low damage × attack speed (145.44)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Low total damage (284)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (274.42)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Low total damage (426)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low damage × attack speed (411.63)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Despite being a (hypothetical) variant of the Dakra Prime, the two weapons do not share Riven Mods; they both have their dedicated Rivens.
