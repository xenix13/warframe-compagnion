A blade forged using dark metals, capable of delivering high-damage charge attacks.

The Dark Sword is a longsword that deals Radiation damage.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Slam Radial Damage deals Toxin damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Iron Phoenix and Rising Steel (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest base damage of all swords.
- Highest status chance of all swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High total damage (260)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (40.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High total damage (520)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (40.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High total damage (780)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (40.00%)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Below average damage × attack speed (221.99)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (443.98)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (665.98)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Slam attacks deal Toxin damage.
