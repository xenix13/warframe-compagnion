Narin's signature pistols strike with winter's chilling bite. Reloading creates a cloud of freezing mist that inflicts Cold Status Effect on enemies that enter it and expands per round reloaded.

Aksondol are Narin's signature Dual Pistols, which on reload, emit a blast that inflicts Cold Status Effect per round expended.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Cold and Puncture damage.
- Upon reloading, the weapon emits a blast that inflicts Cold status procs scaled at a 1:1 ratio (1m blast radius per 1 status proc) based on the number of rounds expended; this effect can be further increased using mods such as Slip Magazine.
- Blast requires line of sight to inflict status procs.
- Blast radius does scale with Fulmination ( Primed) and Magazine Size.
- Blast wave's Cold and Freeze status effects are affected by mods to Status Duration.
- Blast is centered on the wielder's position at the beginning of the reload.
- Despite the blast wave visually traveling for about 1 second, it applies instantly to enemies in range and has no lingering field.

- Blast cannot be refreshed by cancelling reload. Once a blast has occurred, another blast will not occur until a manual reload of Aksondol has been completed.
- Blast will not trigger off passive reloads from mods such as Eject Magazine.
- Blast will not trigger from activating Blood Forge.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (24.00%)
- High burst DPS (775.04)
- Above average sustained DPS (336.97)
- Above average total damage (140)
- Very high ammo pickup (60)
- High ammo max (210)
- Above average status chance (22.00%)
- High damage per shot (193.76)
- High crit multiplier (2.60x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low magazine size (8)
- Very low reload speed (2.60 s)

## Notes

- Status duration mods Perpetual Agony and Augur Seeker increase the duration of Cold and Freeze effects, including from the reload blast, resulting in a combined 8.4 seconds of Frozen effect on enemies and 16.8 seconds of Cold.
- The reload blast radius can be increased by using magazine size mods and Fulmination mods.
- With Primed Fulmination, Primed Slip Magazine, Tainted Clip and Ice Storm, Aksondol has a magazine size of 20, and a reload blast radius of about 28.8 meters.

- The Cold and Freeze effects applied by the Aksondol's reload does not count as inflicting a status effect for the purposes of Shivering Contagion, or Cascadia Empowered.
- Although Secondary Encumber does count it, Encumber's limit of 1 bonus proc per tick significantly limits its synergy, as the blast wave's effects all occur within one tick.
- The blast wave has no interaction with Primary Frostbite, although Aksondol's weapon fire can trigger it if Cold is a final element.

- The blast wave can trigger Arcane Ice Storm if it applies enough Cold to Freeze an enemy.
- As Aksondol's default magazine size is 8, either enemies must have 2 or more pre-existing Cold statuses, or a magazine size mod with a bonus of at least +20% must be equipped, for it to trigger Arcane Ice Storm.
