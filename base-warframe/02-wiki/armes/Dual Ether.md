Fast and precise. The Dual Ether cuts so cleanly that it was considered by the Tenno to be a more humane weapon for 'cleansing' infested allies. Capable of hitting multiple targets.

The Dual Ether swords are the dual wield variant of the Ether Sword.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Swirling Tiger.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Nami Skyla Prime for the third highest base damage of all dual swords, behind Twin Basolk and Twin Krohkur.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average status chance (28.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (180)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (216)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (360)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (432)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (648)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
