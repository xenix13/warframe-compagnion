Knell Prime is the Primed variant of the Knell pistol, with enhancements to Damage, Critical Chance, Critical Multiplier, and Status Chance. Knell Prime was released alongside Harrow Prime and Scourge Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon primarily deals Puncture damage.
- Landing a successful Weak Point Hit grants the "Death Knell" buff, granting Infinite Ammo, increasing the final critical multiplier by +0.5x and increasing final status chance by 20%. Buffs can stack up to 3 times, with each stack lasting 2 seconds and can be refreshed on subsequent Weak Point Hits. Stacks decay one at a time, each lasting the full 2 seconds.
- Scope can be used even in Bleedout.

- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (40.00%)
- High total damage (180)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Above average accuracy (0.25° to 6.00°)
- Above average fire rate (5.00 attacks/sec)
- High damage per shot (252)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Can only load one shot at a time (two when used by Harrow/ Harrow Prime).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low burst DPS (126)
- Very low sustained DPS (114.54)
- Very low magazine size (1)
- Low reload speed (2.00 s)
- Very low ammo max (20)
- Low status chance (10.00%)

## Notes

- When used by Harrow or Harrow Prime, the Knell's magazine size is increased by 1 round, for a total of 2 rounds.
- Death Knell increases final critical multiplier by +0.5x and final status chance by +20%. It also grants unlimited ammo. The buff lasts for 2 seconds and can be refreshed.
- Critical Multiplier and Status Chance bonus can stack up to 3 times. When the buff times out, one stack is lost and the buff duration resets.
- Critical Multiplier bonus is calculated as:
Crit Multiplier = 2 × (1 + Crit Damage Mods) + 0.5 × Number of Stacks
- The buff triggers on weak points (however, some exceptions include the backpacks of some MOA variants). Shooting weak points generated by either Sonar or Detect Vulnerability will also trigger the buff.
- Weak Point Hits on corpses can proc Death Knell.
- Individual Multishot bullets can proc Death Knell and can generate max stacks instantly from a single shot.
- As an indication that this buff is active, the scope design while zoomed in will evolve, as well as showing the Death Knell buff in the UI.
- The scope shows a 1.5x, 2.0x or 2.5x Critical Multiplier for the number of stacks respectively. However this does not represent the actual buff.
