Accuracy combined with a massive magazine make the Soma assault rifle a formidable weapon.

The Soma is a Tenno assault rifle with very high critical potential, very high fire rate that is achieved after a spool-up period, and a large magazine capacity. Its lackluster base damage is largely supported by its incredible critical stats, making the Soma a deceivingly powerful rifle. Coupled with its sustainable fire rate, the Soma is suitable for any distance engagement.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Requires a spool-up of 6 shots before optimal fire rate is achieved.
- Fire rate starts at 25% of the listed value, and increases by 15% per shot.

Advantages over other Primary Auto-Spool Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fully Spooled (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high accuracy (2.00° to 5.00°)
- Very high reload speed (3.00 s)
- Very high fire rate (15.00 attacks/sec)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Incarnon Form (wiki attack index 2)
- High burst DPS (537.6)
- Above average sustained DPS (444.29)
- Very high fire rate (7.00 attacks/sec)
- Very high multishot (8)
- High crit multiplier (3.00x)
- Very high average number of crits per shot (0.8)
- Very high reload speed (3.00 s)

Disadvantages over other Primary Auto-Spool Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Spool-up creates a warm-up period of weakness for fire rate, making short engagements undesirable.
- Fully Spooled (wiki attack index 1)
- Low burst DPS (288)
- Very low sustained DPS (198.62)
- Below average magazine size (100)
- Very low total damage (12)
- Very low ammo max (540)
- Very low status chance (7.00%)
- Low damage per shot (19.2)

- Incarnon Form (wiki attack index 2)
- Low average number of procs per shot (0.2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low accuracy (5.00° to 20.00°)
- Very low total damage (64)
- Very low status chance (2.50%)
- Very low ammo max (540)
- Very low damage per shot (76.8)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Soma (Fully Spooled), compared to Soma Prime (Fully Spooled):
- Equal base damage
- Lower base status chance (7.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Lower sustained DPS (198.62 vs. 235.10)
- Smaller magazine (100 vs. 200)
- Smaller max ammo capacity (540 vs. 800)
- Slower spool-up (6 rounds vs. 4 rounds)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (6 vs. 7)

## Notes

- This weapon can be treated like an upgraded Grakata, due to its higher critical chance, lower recoil and a larger magazine size. Although the spin-up time, slower maximum fire rate and much lower status chance must also be taken into account.
- Capable of shooting the first shot of the magazine with high accuracy, allowing shots to weak-points from afar.
- The Soma is able to cut enemy corpses, ideal for use with a Desecrating Nekros or for low to medium level enemies.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Soma Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Soma with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Soma Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 60 Rune Marrow, and 60 Silphsela.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by filling the Incarnon Transmutation gauge that appears under the reticle by achieving weakpoint hits and then pressing the Alternate Fire button. 20 weakpoints hits will completely fill the gauge. A full gauge is not required to activate Incarnon Form.
- Charges do generate when hitting weak points highlighted by Sonar or Detect Vulnerability.
- However, they do not generate from hits on Crystallize's crystalline growths.

- Weakpoints on corpses or Invulnerable targets do not build charges.
- Individual Multishot bullets can build charges.

- Mode switching is not instantaneous and has an animation equal to the weapon's reload speed.
- Swapping to and from Incarnon Form will fully reload the normal form's magazine.
- Instead of drawing ammunition from its reserves, the Soma's Incarnon Form uses a separate "magazine" with each charge producing 10 rounds to a maximum of 200. Once all ammunition is expended, the Soma reverts to its normal form.
- Incarnon Form's magazine is not affected by mods.
- Incarnon Form is not affected by Ammo Efficiency (such as Energized Munitions).
- Charges and Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, charges and Incarnon Form are removed upon death.

## Tips

- Rifle Ammo Mutation can help regain lost ammunition if the user has ammo conservation problems or if the Soma is unranked, but leaves less room for damage mods, Alternatively, use Rifle Scavenger or Squad Ammo Restores. Using Carrier's Ammo Case is also a viable method.
- Firing in bursts helps with ammo consumption. When well-modded, the Soma is capable of dispatching many common enemies with 1 or 2 shots.
- The Soma's critical chance can be boosted up to 75% with a maximum-rank Point Strike.
- With a maximum rank Vital Sense and a Hammer Shot mod, the Soma's critical multiplier can be increased to 8.4x.
- Once the weapon is fully-spooled, some skills (like Iron Skin) may be activated without losing the firing speed.
- Similarly, it retains its fast firing rate if you stop firing for short periods. This lets the wielder change targets without wasting large amounts of ammunition in between separate enemies, as long as they do so quickly. This can also be used to keep tighter firing spreads at long ranges, essentially "bursting" rounds with quick pauses between to reduce recoil.
- The Soma's magazine is reloaded before the reload animation actually finishes, as little as halfway through the animation itself. Thus, canceling the reload animation still counts as a reload, essentially cutting the reload time by half.
- This weapon has a slight wind up time, much like the Gorgon (only less prominent). This can be taken advantage of by tapping the fire button, making it fire single bullets at a time, which can be effective for sniping weaker units.
- The significantly tighter spread at long range gives the Soma an advantage over the Gorgon and Supra for picking off heavy units, but the latter two are more effective at clearing large groups of enemies.
