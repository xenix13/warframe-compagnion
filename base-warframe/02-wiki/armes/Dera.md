The Dera is a repeater that fires super-heated plasma.

The Dera is a Corpus energy assault rifle and the weapon of choice by Crewmen. Its perfect accuracy allows it to be used at long ranges, in spite of projectile travel time.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average magazine size (45)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Very high accuracy (0.00° to 0.00°)
- Above average reload speed (1.80 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- High fire rate (11.25 attacks/sec)

- Incarnon Form (wiki attack index 2)
- High burst DPS (950.4)
- High sustained DPS (880)
- Above average magazine size (45)
- Above average total damage (330)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Very high accuracy (0.80° to 1.00°)
- Above average reload speed (1.80 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- Above average damage per shot (475.2)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Projectiles have travel time, making long range shots more difficult.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Below average burst DPS (353.7)
- Below average sustained DPS (243.93)
- Low total damage (30)
- Very low damage per shot (31.44)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.60x)

- Incarnon Form (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average fire rate (2.00 attacks/sec)
- Low status chance (18.00%)

## Notes

- The projectiles may possibly obstruct view when firing if the energy color is a bright color such as yellow, blue, or green.
- The projectiles ricochet off of a Nullifier Crewman's bubble.
- Due to the projectiles firing from alternating barrels, this has a convergence effect where the bolts will meet at the point the crosshair is on. This will result in the bolts crossing in an 'X' if they miss their target.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Dera Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Dera with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Dera Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 80 Eevani, and 70 Ueymag.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by filling the Incarnon Transmutation gauge that appears under the reticle by achieving weakpoint hits and then pressing the Alternate Fire button. 25 weakpoints hits will completely fill the gauge. A full gauge is not required to activate Incarnon Form.
- Charges do generate when hitting weak points highlighted by Sonar or Detect Vulnerability.
- However, they do not generate from hits on Crystallize's crystalline growths.

- Weakpoints on corpses or Invulnerable targets do not build charges.
- Individual Multishot bullets can build charges.

- Mode switching is not instantaneous and has an animation equal to the weapon's reload speed.
- Swapping to and from Incarnon Form will fully reload the normal form's magazine.
- Instead of drawing ammunition from its reserves, the Dera's Incarnon Form uses a separate "magazine" with each charge producing 2 rounds to a maximum of 50. Once all ammunition is expended, the Dera reverts to its normal form.
- Incarnon Form's magazine is not affected by mods.
- Incarnon Form is not affected by Ammo Efficiency (such as Energized Munitions).
- Charges and Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, charges and Incarnon Form are removed upon death.
