Gilded rails adorn these two vicious bolt-launching pistols.

Akbolto Prime is the Prime variant of the Akbolto. Despite having lower base damage than its normal counterpart, the Akbolto Prime possesses one of the best critical chances of any pistol, along with a larger magazine and reloads twice as fast. It was released alongside Mirage Prime and Kogake Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- On kill, bodies will follow the bolt that killed them, damaging anyone in their path and pinning the corpse to walls.
- Possesses four innate polarities; three and one .
- Has the highest amount of innate polarities of any secondary weapon.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Good accuracy.
- Good ammo efficiency.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (36.00%)
- Above average sustained DPS (311.87)
- High magazine size (40)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Above average accuracy (0.50° to 7.00°)
- Above average reload speed (1.30 s)
- High ammo max (210)
- Above average fire rate (5.00 attacks/sec)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.80x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Projectiles have travel time.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (44)
- Below average status chance (14.00%)
- Below average damage per shot (72.51)
