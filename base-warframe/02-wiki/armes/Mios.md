This bone-forged sword includes a tendon-whip to distract and maim the enemy.

The Mios is an Infested Blade and Whip. It is the third fan-made weapon designs from DE's Community Melee Weapon Contest to be implemented, preceded by the Kronen and the Silva & Aegis.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Slam Radial Damage deals Toxin damage, bypassing Shields.
- Innate polarity.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Defiled Snapdragon and Tainted Hydra (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average total damage (177)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (227.48)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average total damage (354)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average total damage (531)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- The fourth attack of the stanceless/Claws of the Drake combo as well as the Heavy Attack use the whip to ragdoll and pull enemies towards the player.
- The Slam Radial Damage has a 25% chance to inflict the Toxin status effect, but is unaffected by status chance mods.
