CODEX
A gift from the Lotus to commemorate the eleventh anniversary of the first Tenno waking from Cryo stasis.

The Dex Nikana is a Dex unique nikana released in celebration of WARFRAME's 11 Anniversary. Gains Melee Combo every 11 hits, but max multiplier is reduced to 11x.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Gains 1x Melee Combo every 11 hits (down from 20), but max multiplier is reduced to 11x (down from 12x).
- Lifesteal is applied to attacks when a Daikyu modded with Amalgam Daikyu Target Acquired is equipped.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (168)
- Low status chance (18.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (232.51)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (336)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average total damage (504)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
