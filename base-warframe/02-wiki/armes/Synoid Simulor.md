The Synoid Simulor is the Cephalon Suda variant of the Simulor, Featuring overall improved stats, the Synoid Simulor also features an innate Entropy effect.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Magnetic damage, and shooting through orbs grants bonus Electricity damage.
- This weapon fires bouncing orbs that can merge for explosive damage, and will expire after 20 seconds.
- Merging increases the damage of subsequent explosions.
- Merging four orbs creates a singularity that deals damage over time and pulls items.
- Alternative fire detonates the orbs.
- Expired or detonated orbs explode, dealing damage.
- Shots through the orb deal increased Electricity damage with increased critical chance and critical multiplier. Firing through more than one orb will accumulate some of these effects.
- Innate Entropy effect.
- Can equip the Simulor-exclusive Spontaneous Singularity mod.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- The orbs can be used as a damage buffer by shooting through them.
- Detonation nature of the weapon allows for setting up traps for your enemies.
- Innate Entropy effect provides additional AoE damage, an energy restore, and an energy increase.

## Notes

- Only 4 orbs can be deployed by one Simulor at a time. It cannot fire more until one expires or is manually detonated using Alternative Fire (Default Mouse3 /R. Thumbstick ).
- Orb explosions have a guaranteed Electricity proc. It is affected by electricity mods even if they have combined into other elements like Corrosive
- Orbs bounce harmlessly off of enemies, allies, and terrain before floating to a stop.
- Orbs travel 12 meters, affected by Projectile Speed. Bouncing decreases velocity by 50% and potential distance by 33%.

- Orbs are attracted to each other for the purpose of stacking them. Stacking orbs increases their attraction.
- Orbs have 3 states: Unstacked, Partially Stacked, and Fully Stacked.
- Unstacked orbs are always fired from the Simulor regardless of multishot level.
- Lifespan of 1 second.
- Explosion radius of 5 meters +1 meter per multishot (uncapped).
- Deals 240 / 480 / 720 / 960 Magnetic damage depending on multishot (capped at 960).
- Explosion has 100% falloff despite the UI listing 60% falloff.
- Attraction radius of 4m from other orbs.
- Orbs with a multishot value of 5 or higher are unable to stack into their higher-duration forms.

- Stacking orbs will produce one of two types depending on the value of orbs that stack. A full stack needs an orb value of 4.
- The act of stacking creates a small explosion of typically 125 Magnetic damage in a 4m radius with 0% falloff despite the UI listing 60% falloff. If two orbs with values of 2 combine, it will instead deal 150 damage in 5 meters. Despite the UI listing that "Per Stack" damage can go up to 175, it is not possible.

- Partially Stacked orbs are an uncommon product of two orbs that were not strong enough to create a Fully Stacked orb. They only exist with orb values of 2 or 3.
- Lifespan of 20 seconds. Resets if orb value increases from 2 to 3.
- Deals 480 or 720 Magnetic damage upon exploding.
- Explosion radius of 6 or 7 meters.
- Explosion has 100% falloff despite the UI listing 60% falloff.
- Attracted less to other orbs. Attraction radius of 5 or 8 meters from other orbs.

- Fully Stacked orbs are created by stacking orbs to a minimum orb value of 4. It will continue to attract and consume additional orbs, but they will only be deleted with no added effects.
- Enemies within 5m of the orb, not affected by Firestorm, are dealt 50 Magnetic damage every 0.25s that cannot crit or inflict status. It has 0% falloff despite the UI listing 60% falloff.
- Lifespan reset to 20 seconds. Does not refresh with added orbs.
- Deals 960 Magnetic Damage upon exploding.
- Explosion radius of 8 meters.
- Explosion has 100% falloff despite the UI listing 60% falloff.
- Not attracted to other orbs. Other orbs within 8 meters are very attracted.
- Attracts Pickups that have not yet settled on the ground.

- Orbs have a physical 0.5m radius that obstructs enemy fire.
- Shots fired through the orbs receive certain buffs and effects. Firing through more than one orb will accumulate some of these effects.
- Shots fired through an orb have a 0.66x final damage multiplier applied. Firing through multiple orbs will stack this multiplier, i.e. firing through two orbs will result in a 0.66 × 0.66 = 0.4356x final damage multiplier.
- Firing through an orb grants an Electricity damage bonus that depends on how many orbs where merged into the orb fired through. This bonus stacks with multiple orbs when firing through multiple orbs.
- An unmerged orb will grant +100% Electricity damage.
- A two stack merged orb will grant +300% Electricity damage.
- A three stack merged orb will grant +400% Electricity damage.
- A four stack merged orb will grant +500% Electricity damage.

- Shots fired through an orb will gain additional +25% flat critical chance, unaffected by critical chance mods. This bonus stacks with multiple orbs when firing through multiple orbs.
- Shots fired through an orb will receive +25% critical damage, additive to critical damage mods. This applies only once, regardless of how many orbs were fired through.

- Fully Stacked orbs will only pull in items that are created after the orb's creation. Dropped items on the floor will not be retroactively added to the vacuuming effect.

## Tips

- Increasing projectile travel distance using Jet Stream can be used to shoot orbs further, allowing singularities to be set up at longer distances. This may be counterproductive however, as it causes singularities to become increasingly less controllable, potentially overshooting their intended targets or bouncing out of reach. Depending on how the Synoid Simulor is intended to be used, consider using Overextended to adjust Jet Stream's Projectile Speed bonus to a manageable level.
- Detonating orbs manually can be used for crowd control, as their guaranteed Electricity status can stun enemies even if they don't deal enough damage to kill them.
- Adding Multishot above 100% is unadvised, unless 300% Multishot can be reached. This is because having anywhere between 100-200% Multishot, will still require that 2 shots be fired in order to gain 4 stacks and form a singularity. This wastes the additional 3rd pre-merged orb generated from the Multishot, unless the orbs are detonated individually after every shot without merging into a singularity.
- Mirage is the only Warframe who can exceed the orb limit by using Hall of Mirrors, theoretically able to sustain 19 full stacks at once if cleverly rotated between the holograms.
- Any projectiles that were fired by a hologram will explode when the hologram expires.
