A set of ceremonial daggers from the Orokin era, the Fang Prime's blades resonate violently as they strike. This allows them to pierce hardened materials like armor with ease.

The Fang Prime is the primed version of the dual Fang daggers, acting as a direct upgrade.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Gnashing Payara and Biting Piranha (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Grants a 5% Movement Speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (756.14)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (178)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (356)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
