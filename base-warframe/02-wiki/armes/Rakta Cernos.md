A finely tuned instrument of assassination.

The Rakta Cernos is a modified Cernos bow exclusively available to the Red Veil. It features the fastest charge time of all bows, increased base damage, and innate Blight effect, at the expense of slightly lower critical chance and status chance.

## Characteristics

This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.

Advantages over other Primary Bow weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Innate Blight effect.
- Uncharged Shot (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (35.00%)
- Very high ammo pickup (15)
- Very high ammo max (72)
- Very high fire rate (1.00 attacks/sec)

- Charged Shot (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average burst DPS (1057.5)
- High sustained DPS (746.47)
- Above average total damage (470)
- Very high ammo pickup (15)
- Very high ammo max (72)
- Very high fire rate (1.00 attacks/sec)
- Above average damage per shot (634.5)

Disadvantages over other Primary Bow weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Innate Blight effect can put enemies into minor alert status during stealth.
- Uncharged Shot (wiki attack index 1)
- Low reload speed (0.60 s)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Charged Shot (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (35.00%)
- Low reload speed (0.60 s)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (2.00x)
