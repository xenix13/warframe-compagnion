A high-capacity, high-damage enforcer, this weapon can switch between the high crit semi-automatic and full automatic firing modes.

The Stradavar is a Tenno assault rifle featuring the ability to switch between semi- and fully-automatic firing modes by using the Alternate Fire.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact and Puncture by a slight margin on its full-auto mode, and Puncture damage on its semi-auto mode.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Full-auto mode has negligible recoil.
- Semi-auto mode has high accuracy when aiming.
- Full Auto Mode (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- High magazine size (65)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- High fire rate (10.00 attacks/sec)

- Semi-Auto Mode (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (28.00%)
- High magazine size (65)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- High fire rate (5.00 attacks/sec)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Semi-auto mode has the second-lowest damage of all semi-automatic rifles, after Tiberon Prime.
- Full Auto Mode (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average burst DPS (347.2)
- Below average sustained DPS (265.50)
- Low total damage (28)
- Low accuracy (6.00° to 8.00°)
- Low status chance (12.00%)
- Low damage per shot (34.72)
- Below average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Semi-Auto Mode (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average burst DPS (320)
- Low total damage (50)
- Below average accuracy (0.00° to 7.00°)
- Low status chance (16.00%)
- Low damage per shot (64)
- Very low crit multiplier (2.00x)
