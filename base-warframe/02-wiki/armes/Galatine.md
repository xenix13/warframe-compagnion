With massive charged power and the ability to hit up to five foes in a single swing, the Galatine sword is the heavy artillery of melee weapons.

The Galatine is a heavy greatsword boasting impressive attack speed for a heavy blade, falling short in critical chance.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000. It is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Paracesis.

- Galatine → Paracesis

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Cleaving Whirlwind and Tempo Royale stances.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with the Galatine Prime and Masseter for the fastest attack speed of all heavy blades.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all heavy blades, after Gram.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average total damage (182)
- Low status chance (20.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (200.2)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (400.4)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (600.6)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

## Notes

- The Galatine can be used to stun-lock enemies (a characteristic of heavy melee weapons), as well as land easy headshots with its overhead chops.
- As with other heavies, the overhead chops cause small AoE damage.

## Tips

- Due to its Slash damage, the Galatine is well suited for use with a Desecrate Nekros.
- The Galatine's reach is impressive, making it very useful when paired with Primed Reach.
