A different take on the Grineer assault rifle, the Karak Wraith features unique styling.

The Karak Wraith is the Wraith variant of the Karak assault rifle, with higher damage, critical stats, status chance, and magazine size.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Low recoil.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High magazine size (60)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- High fire rate (11.67 attacks/sec)
- Above average status chance (25.00%)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (13.00%)
- Below average burst DPS (408.80)
- Below average sustained DPS (294.31)
- Low total damage (31)
- Low damage per shot (35.03)
- Below average crit multiplier (2.00x)
