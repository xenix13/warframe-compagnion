A different take on a familiar combat knife, the Machete Wraith features a unique styling.

The Machete Wraith is the Wraith variant of the Machete, featuring increased base damage and attack speed. Originally obtained from completing at least 25 missions during The Gradivus Dilemma, the Machete Wraith is the second Wraith weapon to be released, after the Strun Wraith.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sundering Weave, Cyclone Kraken and Rending Wind (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all machetes, behind Slaytra
- Second highest status chance of all machetes behind Slaytra.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (33.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low total damage (240)
- Low damage × attack speed (313.37)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low total damage (360)
- Low damage × attack speed (470.05)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
