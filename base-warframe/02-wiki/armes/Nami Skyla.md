Like the surge and crash of storm waves, a master of the Nami cutlass and Skyla dagger turns these two separate weapons into one fluid attack.

The Nami Skyla is a sword-dagger combination.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low total damage (125)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (120.35)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low total damage (250)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (240.71)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low total damage (375)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (361.06)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
