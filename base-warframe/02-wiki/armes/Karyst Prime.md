This master blade is the heavier, oversized and far more lethal version of the signature weapon of an extinct order of Tenno assassins.

The Karyst Prime is the primed variant of the Karyst, sporting improvements to its damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and range, at the cost of even lower attack speed. Karyst Prime was released alongside Inaros Prime and Panthera Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matching Pointed Wind and Stinging Thorn stances.
- Can use the dagger-exclusive Covert Lethality mod.
- Passive: Grants a 10% Movement Speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest base damage of all single daggers, and second-highest base damage of all melee weapons behind Arca Titron.
- High follow through.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Very high total damage (344)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (295.52)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Very high total damage (688)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (591.05)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Very high total damage (1,032)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (886.58)
- High status chance (26.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack speed (0.667x animation speed)
- Low attack range (2.20 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
