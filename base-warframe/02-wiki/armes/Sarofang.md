Mete out the sharp pain of justice. Heavy Slam attacks create a vortex that pulls in lifted enemies when the Sarofang's Combo Multiplier builds to 8x. In Voruna's hands, the Sarofang only requires a 5x Combo Multiplier to summon the vortex.

The Sarofang is Voruna's signature axe heavy blade. Heavy Attack Slams with an 8x Combo Multiplier creates a vortex that pulls in hit enemies. When wielded by Voruna, the pull requirement is reduced to 5x Combo Multiplier.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Rending Crane and Noble Cadence (Conclave only) stance.
- Performing a Heavy Attack Slam with an 8x Combo Multiplier (5x with Voruna ( Prime)) briefly pulls enemies within 9m toward the center of the slam.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)
- Above average damage × attack speed (280.8)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (561.6)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (600)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (842.4)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low status chance (20.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
