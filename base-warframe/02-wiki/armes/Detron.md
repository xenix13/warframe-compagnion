The sleek lines of the Detron conceal its ferocious nature. The hand cannon's semi-automatic action takes down enemies with speed and efficiency.

The Detron is a pistol shotgun that shoots Radiation pellets. Player version is crafted from drops of Zanuka Hunter, while NPC version is used by Detron Crewmen

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Radiation damage.
- Can use the Detron-exclusive Thermagnetic Shells.

Advantages over other Secondary Shotgun Sidearm weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Very ammo efficient.
- Innate multishot of 7 pellets.
- Fast Projectile Speed.
- Medium spread.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (280)
- Very high reload speed (1.05 s)
- High status chance (12.86%)

Disadvantages over other Secondary Shotgun Sidearm weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 62.31% from 13m to 22m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Projectiles have travel time.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (4.00%)
- Below average maximum falloff distance (22.0 m)
- Low burst DPS (951.04)
- Below average sustained DPS (559.67)
- Low fire rate (3.33 attacks/sec)
- Below average multishot (7)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
- Below average active falloff slope (14.4m/%)
- Very low average number of crits per shot (0.28)
- Below average accuracy (12.00° to 16.00°)
- Low magazine size (5)
- Low damage per shot (285.6)

## Notes

- Neither crafted Detron nor its components and blueprint are tradeable.
- The damage listed is the combination of all seven pellets' damage values, the actual damage per pellet is 40 Radiation.

## Tips

- Use Zanuka Hunter Beacon to summon Zanuka Hunter.
- The Detron's Index mod, Thermagnetic Shells, causes enemies killed by it to explode, dealing radial Magnetic damage to all nearby enemies.
