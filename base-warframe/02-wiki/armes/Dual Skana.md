A shorter Skana in each hand. Can hit multiple targets.

The Dual Skana is the dual wield version of the Skana.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000, it is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Akjagara, Redeemer and Dark Split-Sword (Heavy Blade).

- Akbolto + Dual Skana → Akjagara
- Dual Skana → Dark Split-Sword (Heavy Blade)
- Dual Skana + Vasto → Redeemer

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Dual Cleavers and Dex Dakra for the slowest attack speed of all dual swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low total damage (120)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Low status chance (16.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (102.45)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)

- Very low damage × attack speed (204.91)
