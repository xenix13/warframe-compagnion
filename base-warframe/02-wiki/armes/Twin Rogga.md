These twin barrels of destruction are the signature pistols of the Kuva Guardians.

The Twin Rogga are dual Grineer pistols introduced in Update 19.0 (2016-11-11), and are the Kuva Guardian's sidearm of choice. These shotgun pistols deal heavy damage with low spread complemented by its very high status chance, but are hampered by their low magazine size and heavy damage falloff.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Has a and a polarities.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all shotgun-type secondaries, behind alt-fire Euphona Prime, and highest base damage of all dual secondaries.
- Very high overall status chance.
- Innate multishot of 15 pellets. Highest pellet count of all shotgun-type secondaries.
- Tight spread, useful for single targets.
- Efficient ammo economy.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high average number of procs per shot (0.99)
- Above average maximum falloff distance (20.0 m)
- Very high burst DPS (1938.75)
- Very high sustained DPS (674.34)
- Very high multishot (15)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)
- Very high average number of crits per shot (1.5)
- Very high total damage (705)
- Very high damage per shot (775.5)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 21.28% from 10m to 20m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Inefficient against groups, unless using Punch Through mods.
- Extremely low magazine size of just two rounds; requires frequent reloading.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low fire rate (2.50 attacks/sec)
- Very low accuracy (10.00° to 15.00°)
- Very low magazine size (2)
- Low ammo pickup (20)
- Very low status chance (6.60%)
- Low ammo max (120)

## Tips

- Using both Quickdraw and Stunning Speed can shorten the frequent reloads.
- With rank 5 Slip Magazine and at least rank 3 Tainted Clip, the magazine size can be increased to 4.
- With a rank 10 Primed Slip Magazine, a rank 1 Tainted Clip or Ice Storm can be used for the same result.
- With Slip Magazine, Tainted Clip and Ice Storm all at max rank, a magazine of 5 can be reached. Primed Slip Magazine will not further increase magazine size, but allows Tainted Clip to be only rank 2, minimizing the reload speed penalty.

- Barrel Diffusion can increase the number of pellets to 33, while adding Lethal Torrent increases pellet count to 42. This, combined with its high status chance, allows for a very high number of procs per shot.
- The frequent reloading provides constant chances of triggering Arcane Awakening.
- The Helminth skill Energized Munitions or Arcane Pistoleer are extremely effective in enhancing this weapon.
