The Nikana reintroduces a lost Tenno blade and with it another fragment of the old ways.

The Nikana is a Tenno blade that is heavily based on the ancient traditional katana, made to slice targets elegantly with precision and speed. It can be researched from the Tenno Lab in the dojo.

It is a weapon crafting ingredient for Dragon Nikana.

- Nikana → Dragon Nikana

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Decisive Judgement, Tranquil Cleave, Blind Justice and Fateful Truth (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate and polarity.
- Lifesteal is applied to attacks when a Daikyu modded with Amalgam Daikyu Target Acquired is equipped.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all nikanas.
- Lowest critical chance, status chance, and attack speed of all nikanas.
