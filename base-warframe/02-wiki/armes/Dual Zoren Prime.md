Dual Zoren Prime is the Primed counterpart of Dual Zoren hatchets. In addition to being a direct upgrade, the user gains a burst of speed during a melee slide attack in midair. Dual Zoren Prime was released alongside Lavos Prime and Cedo Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Airborne Melee Slide Attack will boost the user's Movement Speed which lasts until landing.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Swirling Tiger stance.
- Innate one and two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Above average damage × attack speed (290.5)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average damage × attack speed (581)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average damage × attack speed (871.5)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (140)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Below average status chance (22.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low total damage (280)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low total damage (420)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
