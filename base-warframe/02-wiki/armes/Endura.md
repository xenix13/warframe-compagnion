The delicate form of this Tenno rapier conceals its ferocious and resilient force.

The Endura is a Tenno rapier with immense damage and status chance.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Vulpine Mask and Cunning Aspect (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest base damage of all rapiers.
- Highest status chance of all rapiers.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (36.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (600)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low damage × attack speed (201.74)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (403.48)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (605.22)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- The weapon's high status chance, combined with the forced Bleed procs from Vulpine Mask and its decent Slash damage, makes it work well with Condition Overload, Relentless Combination, Body Count and Drifting Contact mods.
