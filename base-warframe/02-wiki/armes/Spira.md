A flash of red ribbon is the last thing most victims of these throwing-daggers see.

The Spira are large Tenno throwing knives with high damage but low throwing speed.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Ragdolls and pins the target to the wall on a kill.
- Ragdolled bodies fly in a straight line and can damage other enemies in its path.

- Capable of using the thrown-exclusive mod Concealed Explosives.
- Two polarity slots.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Spira Prime for the highest critical chance of all throwing-type secondaries.
- Silent.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (30.00%)
- Above average total damage (82)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Very high accuracy (0.00° to 0.00°)
- High reload speed (1.00 s)
- High ammo max (210)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Projectiles have travel time and travel in an arc, making it hard to hit at longer ranges and achieve headshots.
- Second lowest fire rate of all throwing-type secondaries, behind Pox.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average burst DPS (266.5)
- Below average sustained DPS (213.2)
- Below average magazine size (10)
- Low fire rate (2.50 attacks/sec)
- Very low status chance (8.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Spira (Normal Attack), compared to Spira Prime (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage per projectile (82.00 vs. 60.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 8.2 vs. 6)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 49.2 vs. 48)
- Higher Slash damage ( 24.6 vs. 6)

- Higher total damage (82 vs. 60)
- Lower base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 3.00x)
- Lower base status chance (8.00% vs. 14.00%)
- Higher average damage per tap (106.6 vs. 96)
- Lower burst DPS (266.5 vs. 319.68)
- Lower sustained DPS (213.2 vs. 264.60)
- Lower fire rate (2.50 attacks/sec vs. 3.33 attacks/sec)
- Smaller magazine (10 vs. 12)
- Slower reload time (1.00 s vs. 0.75 s)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (8 vs. 10)

## Notes

- There is a slight delay after firing a knife before being able to reload, which requires pausing before trying to reload, or tapping the reload button several times.
- The right handed throw does not land at the same place as the left throw, landing lower and slightly to the right of your aim.
- This can be counteracted, however, by holding a datamass or a power cell.

## Tips

- Projectile Speed mods will reduce projectile drop and increase projectile speed.
- For effective use of non-hitscan weapons, it is strongly recommended to learn how to lead your target. In short, leading means aiming ahead of your target so the projectile still hits him. It can be a challenge to learn it, low level Grineer are recommended for practicing
