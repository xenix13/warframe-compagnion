Motorized saw claw weapon.

The Ripkas are chainsaw claws originally designed for cutting scrap metal and overgrown wildlife, but function well enough for close combat, with decent critical potential and a high ratio of Slash damage.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Vermillion Storm, Four Riders, Malicious Raptor and Scarlet Hurricane minor (Conclave only) stance.
- Can use the Ripkas-exclusive mods, Hunter's Bonesaw and Amalgam Ripkas True Steel.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.8x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.8x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (173)
- Low attack speed (0.883x animation speed)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (183.31)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (346)
- Low damage × attack speed (345.86)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Below average total damage (519)
- Low damage × attack speed (518.79)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- The Ripkas requires Tellurium for construction, which is a resource only found on Archwing missions, Empyrean (both in space and on foot), submersible missions on Uranus, or Alerts.
- The Ripkas require the Ankyros and Dual Cleavers weapons to build. The total materials required to fully build this weapon include: 700 Nano Spores, 1,900 Salvage, 1,400 Ferrite, 1,350 Alloy Plate, 2 Tellurium, 1 Forma and 150,000 Credits 150,000.
- If the Forma was crafted, another 1 Morphics, 1 Neural Sensors, 1 Neurodes, 1 Orokin Cell, and 35,000 Credits 35,000 are added to the total cost.
