Forged by a master gunsmith, this Tenno burst rifle will strike down targets with swift precision.

The Tiberon is a Tenno-made three round burst-fire rifle. Compared to most burst-fire rifles, the Tiberon is much more accurate and primarily deals Puncture damage, albeit at the cost of reduced status chance.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Can use the Tiberon-exclusive Critical Precision mod.
- Can equip the Tiberon-exclusive Measured Burst mod (Conclave only).
- Comes with a polarity.

Advantages over other Primary Burst Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Very low recoil.
- Third highest fire rate of all burst-fire rifles, behind Paracyst and Burston Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High burst DPS (400.30)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- High accuracy (0.00° to 6.00°)
- High ammo max (540)
- High fire rate (6.67 attacks/sec)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.40x)

Disadvantages over other Primary Burst Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Linearly less efficient – sometimes a target may only die with four bullets, which requires an entire second burst to be fired.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average magazine size (30)
- Low total damage (44)
- Very low reload speed (2.26 s)
- Low status chance (16.00%)
- Below average damage per shot (180.04)

## Notes

- Like the Sybaris, the Tiberon's burst has a very tight shot grouping, which combined with its high base accuracy and negligible recoil makes it better at long-range shooting than other burst-fire weapons, such as the Burston or Hind.
- The Tiberon is a very strong candidate for a tenno Specter to use thanks to its high damage per second combined with high accuracy.
- Has a higher damage per bullet than other low-tier burst fire weapons, like Burston or Hind.
