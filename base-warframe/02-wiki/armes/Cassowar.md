Light and nimble, the twin blades of this polearm will swoop and slice through throngs of hardened foes.

The Cassowar is a Tenno polearm with among the highest attack speed and status chance of all polearms, but has the lowest critical chance and critical multiplier of any polearm.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Shimmering Blight and Argent Scourge (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate and polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with the Orthos Prime for the fastest attack speed of all polearms.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average status chance (28.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third lowest base damage of all polearms, after Orthos and Tonbo.
- Lowest critical chance and critical multiplier of all polearms.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Below average total damage (188)
- Below average damage × attack speed (225.23)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.40x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.40x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.40x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Tips

- Can be built for status and Condition Overload to boost its damage output.
- Cassowar's high attack speed allows it to proc statuses quickly.
