Drawing from its huge quad-chambered clip, the Tetra is capable of unleashing a sustained barrage of lethal energy bolts.

The Tetra is a Corpus assault rifle sporting a large magazine size for sustained fire. The rifle features an average rate of fire and fair accuracy for mid to long range combat.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Can use the Tetra-exclusive Focused Acceleration and Kinetic Ricochet mods.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High magazine size (60)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- Above average fire rate (6.67 attacks/sec)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Projectiles have travel time.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (4.00%)
- Very low burst DPS (217.70)
- Low sustained DPS (178.10)
- Low total damage (32)
- Below average accuracy (3.00° to 8.00°)
- Below average status chance (20.00%)
- Very low damage per shot (32.64)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

## Tips

- With a large portion (80%) of its total damage relying on Puncture, it is somewhat more viable to equip Piercing Hit and/or Piercing Caliber than with most weapons.
- Similarly, because the Tetra has no Slash Damage, Sawtooth Clip and Fanged Fusillade are completely useless.

- With its high recoil, Stabilizer is a good choice to make this weapon more useful at longer ranges.
- While the Tetra has less accuracy than the Dera and weaker overall firepower than the Supra, it deals more damage than the former and has better handling than the latter, making it a good middle-ground weapon in terms of performance. It is also the only Corpus Primary energy weapon whose blueprint can be purchased off the Market, making it more accessible than the clan tech Dera and Supra.
- Third lowest fire rate of all fully automatic rifles, after Buzlok and Panthera.
