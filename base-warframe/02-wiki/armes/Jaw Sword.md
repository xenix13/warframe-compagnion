A blade with a serrated edge that delivers strong quick attacks.

The Jaw Sword has the unique look of two interlocking pieces that form its signature jaw-like structure.

The Jaw Sword can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000 and the blueprint for 2,500 Credits 2,500.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Can use the Jaw Sword-exclusive Blade of Truth mod.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Iron Phoenix and Rising Steel (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Skana for the second lowest base damage of all swords, after Cronus.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Very low total damage (120)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low status chance (16.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (114.44)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Very low total damage (240)
- Very low damage × attack speed (228.88)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Very low total damage (360)
- Very low damage × attack speed (343.32)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
