The Magistar Mace wields justice and truth in the form of bone crushing blows.

The Magistar is a heavy, flanged mace.

It is a weapon crafting ingredient for the Sibear.

- Magistar → Sibear

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Shattering Storm and Crashing Havoc (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (420)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (630)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third lowest base damage of all hammers, after Kuva Shildeg and Fragor/ Jat Kittag.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (209.91)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (419.83)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (629.74)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

## Notes

- As with other heavy weapons, the Magistar's wall attack is a horizontal circular swing that slowly swings the weapon around the Warframe, allowing it to hit enemies more easily compared to other wall attacks that require pinpoint precision. Unfortunately, this prevents the Magistar from using its wall attack to launch itself upwards, as it will perform the maneuver horizontally regardless of where the Warframe is looking, making it fall instead.
- The Magistar also shares another unusual property with other heavy weapons, namely its tendency to shake the screen violently while blocking attacks.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Magistar Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Magistar with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Magistar Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 150 Aggristone.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
