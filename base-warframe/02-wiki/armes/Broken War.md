A fragment of Stalker's War Sword, a symbol of his defeat.

Broken War is a sword formed from a portion of the War greatsword, the weapon given to Stalker by the Sentient Hunhow.

This weapon is a weapon crafting ingredient for War.

- Broken War → War

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crimson Dervish, Swooping Falcon, and Vengeful Revenant.
- Innate and polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest critical chance of all swords, behind Dakra Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (531.08)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (796.62)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (187)
- Low status chance (20.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

## Notes

- The listed Mastery Rank requirement only applies to the construction of additional copies of the Broken War. The copy of Broken War awarded at the end of The Second Dream is usable at any rank.
- The hilt and blade required to craft Broken War are tradeable; its blueprint is not.
- Uniquely, if no stance is equipped on the sword, then it will use Vengeful Revenant's full moveset instead of the normal stanceless moveset for swords.
- The Broken War is used to construct War, which will remove it from the inventory.
- Because the Broken War received from completing The Second Dream quest comes with a free catalyst, it is recommended not to use it to craft War, as the pre-installed catalyst will not carry over into the crafted War. This is especially advisable if you plan to buy Hunhow's Gift in the future, as this will allow you to obtain both a Broken War and War with pre-installed catalysts and avoid having duplicates.
