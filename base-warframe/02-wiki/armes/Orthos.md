The Tenno forged Orthos is a rare double bladed staff. Those who take the time to master it speak of its impressive striking distance and ability to hit multiple targets.

The Orthos is a Tenno polearm that boasts a long reach and good status chance at the expense of critical chance and critical multiplier.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Bleeding Willow and Twirling Spire stances.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all polearms, after Tonbo.
- Tied with Cassowar for the second lowest critical chance of all polearms, after Tonbo.
- Tied with Pupacyst and Serro for the second lowest critical multiplier of all polearms, after Cassowar.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Below average total damage (185)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low status chance (18.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (174.73)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (349.46)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (524.20)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

## Notes

- The Orthos will extend slightly when drawn, and retract back after "sheathing".

## Tips

- Being one of the longest melee weapons (after Orthos Prime), the Orthos benefits greatly from Reach; a maxed out Reach will allow you to strike through multiple enemies, bypass Grineer Shield Lancer's shields, and strike Infested Venomous Eximus without touching the toxic aura.
- The average range of this weapon with a maxed Reach is 4.1 meters.
