Sobek is a rapid-firing Grineer shotgun that has reduced damage per shot but boasts extra large clip capacity.

The Sobek is a Grineer automatic shotgun that has a large 20-round drum magazine. While it suffers a very low reload speed and rate of fire, it is effective at laying down suppressing fire. It is used by the Grineer Trooper and Executioner Nok.

This weapon can be sold for 7,500 Credits 7,500.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Innate multishot of 5 pellets.
- Can use the Sobek-exclusive Shattering Justice, Acid Shells, and Clip Delegation mods.

Advantages over other Primary Shotgun weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Large magazine size.
- High ammo reserves.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average maximum falloff distance (30.0 m)
- Above average accuracy (6.00° to 16.00°)
- High magazine size (20)
- High ammo max (240)

Disadvantages over other Primary Shotgun weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 50% from 20m to 30m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Slowest fire rate of all fully automatic shotguns.
- Tied with Tigris for the second lowest pellet count of all pellet shotguns, after Exergis.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average average number of procs per shot (0.81)
- Low crit chance (11.00%)
- Below average burst DPS (971.25)
- Below average fire rate (2.50 attacks/sec)
- Below average multishot (5)
- Below average active falloff slope (20.0m/%)
- Below average average number of crits per shot (0.55)
- Very low ammo pickup (15)
- Low reload speed (2.70 s)
- Below average damage per shot (388.5)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Tips

- Modding the weapon with its exclusive Shattering Justice and Acid Shells gives the Sobek very good crowd control capabilities.
