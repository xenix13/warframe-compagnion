This Orokin variation of the Ankyros is superior to its successor in every way.

The Ankyros Prime is the Prime version of Ankyros fists, acting as a direct upgrade. It was released alongside Rhino Prime and Boltor Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Fracturing Wind and Quaking Hand (Conclave only) stance.
- Two innate polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (128)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Low status chance (16.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (222.72)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low total damage (256)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Below average damage × attack speed (445.44)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low total damage (384)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
