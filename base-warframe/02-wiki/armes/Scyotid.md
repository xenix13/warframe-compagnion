Go on the hunt with Oraxia's signature secondary. Fling a volley of toxin-laced barbs, or drop a cluster snare that scatters on impact, seizing nearby enemies and dragging them together.

Scyotid is Oraxia's signature throwing knives. Dealing Toxin damage, its Alternate Fire throws a snare that binds and pulls in enemies.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Toxin damage.
- Primary fire has innate 1.6 meter Punch Through.
- Alternate Fire throws a webbed snare that damages, paralyzes for ~6 seconds, and groups up to 8 enemies within 15 meters.
- Number of pulled enemies is multiplied by Multishot.
- Radius is not affected by Fulmination ( Primed).
- Damage is not affected by mods, nor does it combine with or inflict modded elements.
- Consumes 4 ammo per shot.
- Can be lowered with ammo efficiency.

- Slowing effects may prevent the pulling effect from working efficiently (e.g. Gloom).
- Require a line of sight between the target and the impact point.
- Enemies will be pulled horizontally towards impact point.
- Aiming at walls or ceilings can be an efficient way to attract more enemies due to having an unobstructed line of sight.

- Innate polarity.

## Notes

- As the alternate fire is unable to crit, repeated uses will build stacks of Secondary Enervate without consuming them.
- If the weapon is to be used solely for its alternate fire grouping ability, methods of maximizing Multishot (and therefore the number of pulled enemies) include:
- Barrel Diffusion (or variants)
- Lethal Torrent
- Using Conjunction Voltage in combination with mods that increase Electricity damage and Status Chance
- Using Riven Mods that increase Multishot or Electricity (in combination with Conjunction Voltage), or reduce Toxin damage (to boost the chance of Electric procs).
