Between measured staccato and staggering crescendo, Stradavar Prime never fails to call down a devastating finale.

The Stradavar Prime is the Primed variant of the select-fire Stradavar, sporting increased accuracy, critical multiplier, magazine size, and slightly higher damage on its full-auto mode, in addition to increased status chance and built-in punch through on its semi-auto mode but at some expense to fire rate. It was released alongside Equinox Prime and Tipedo Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact and Puncture by a slight margin on its full-auto mode, and Slash damage on its semi-auto mode.
- Innate three polarities.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Full-auto mode:
- High accuracy when shooting from the hip.
- Negligible recoil.

- Semi-auto mode:
- Very high accuracy when aiming.
- Innate 1 meter punch through.

- Full Auto Mode (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- High magazine size (90)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- High ammo max (720)
- High fire rate (10.00 attacks/sec)
- High crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Semi-Auto Mode (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high magazine size (90)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- Very high ammo max (720)
- Above average fire rate (3.33 attacks/sec)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.80x)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Semi-auto mode consumes 2 rounds per shot.
- Full Auto Mode (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (30)
- Below average accuracy (3.00° to 5.00°)
- Low status chance (12.00%)
- Low damage per shot (41.52)

- Semi-Auto Mode (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (80)
- Below average status chance (22.00%)
- Below average damage per shot (123.2)
