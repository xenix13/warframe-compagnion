The Scindo is a heavy war axe crafted by the Tenno. Reduced speed and agility is balanced by heavy, devastating strikes capable of connecting with multiple targets at once.

The Scindo is a war axe of the heavy blade category, boasting better damage than the Gram and Galatine, but with weaker critical multiplier and status chance.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Rending Crane and Noble Cadence (Conclave only) stance.
- Can equip the Manticore skin, which provides +15% damage, but -15% attack speed.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (600)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest critical multiplier and status chance of all heavy blades.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (197.15)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (394.31)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (591.46)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Scindo (Normal Attack), compared to Scindo Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (200.00 vs. 250.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 20 vs. 25)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 20 vs. 25)
- Lower Slash damage ( 160 vs. 200)

- Lower total damage (200 vs. 250)
- Lower base critical chance (15.00% vs. 26.00%)
- Lower base critical multiplier (1.50x vs. 2.40x)
- Lower base status chance (10.00% vs. 24.00%)
- Shorter melee range (2.60 m vs. 2.70 m)
- Lower attack speed (0.917x animation speed vs. 0.967x animation speed)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (2 vs. 8)

## Notes

- When performing a normal attack, while in the middle of a swing, you are not affected by knockback, e.g. Infested Volatile Runner explosions.
