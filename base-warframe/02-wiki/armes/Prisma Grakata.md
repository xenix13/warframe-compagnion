Encased in plates refined from pure Prisma crystals, this rare element gives this assault rifle a radiant luster.

The Prisma Grakata is the Prisma variant of the Grakata assault rifle, featuring enhanced fire rate, reload speed, critical damage, magazine, and ammo reserves.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Can use the Grakata-exclusive Brain Storm and Wild Frenzy mods.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high magazine size (120)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- Very high ammo max (1,000)
- Very high fire rate (21.67 attacks/sec)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- High recoil.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (15)
- Very low damage per shot (20.62)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Prisma Grakata (Normal Attack), compared to Grakata (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage per projectile (15.00 vs. 11.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 6 vs. 4.4)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 5 vs. 3.7)
- Higher Slash damage ( 4 vs. 2.9)

- Higher total damage (15 vs. 11)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.50x vs. 2.00x)
- Higher base status chance (21.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Higher average damage per tap (20.62 vs. 13.75)
- Higher burst DPS (446.94 vs. 275)
- Higher sustained DPS (328.35 vs. 152.77)
- Higher fire rate (21.67 attacks/sec vs. 20.00 attacks/sec)
- Larger magazine (120 vs. 60)
- Larger max ammo capacity (1,000 vs. 750)
- Faster reload time (2.00 s vs. 2.40 s)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (11 vs. 5)

## Tips

- Due to a high rate of fire and a high critical chance, Critical Delay can be used with the Prisma Grakata to lower the fire rate (effectively making the weapon easier to handle and reduces ammo cost) and increase the critical chance at the cost of slightly lower overall DPS.
- Compared to other rifles with high rates of fire, the Prisma Grakata has a high chance to cause status effects, which goes well with builds designed to weaken, confuse, or stun enemies.
