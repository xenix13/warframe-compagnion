Once the blade weapon of choice for an exclusive order of Tenno assassins, the Karyst dagger grew to become a symbol of honor and duty for all Tenno

Karyst is a Tenno single dagger that deals innate Toxin damage.

The Karyst Dagger can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Toxin damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matching Pointed Wind and Stinging Thorn stances.
- Can use the dagger-exclusive Covert Lethality mod.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Grants a 10% Movement Speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High total damage (273)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High total damage (546)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High total damage (819)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High status chance (26.00%)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low attack speed (0.750x animation speed)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Below average damage × attack speed (225.22)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.750x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)

## Notes

- When combining elemental mods, the Karyst's innate Toxin damage is added last.
- Jump attacks will always inflict the Toxin proc, regardless of the weapon's current elemental mods.
- When equipped without a Stance mod, the Karyst adds a 4th lunging stab on its normal attack combo that propels the Warframe a good distance forward, which is useful for catching up to moving enemies. The attack also has a 100% chance of inflicting Slash bleed proc. This effect is lost if one uses the Pointed Wind stance, which replaces the lunge with a sweeping slash.
- If the lunge lands behind an unalerted enemy, a stealth attack will be performed instead.
