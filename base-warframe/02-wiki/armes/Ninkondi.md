With its lightning-fast strikes, the Ninkondi pays respect to the old ways.

The Ninkondi are a set of Nunchaku of Tenno design, which pay homage to ancient techniques.

It is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Shaku.

- Ninkondi → Shaku

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Atlantis Vulcan and Mafic Rain (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High status chance (35.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Very high status chance (35.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Very high status chance (35.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average total damage (190)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.31 m)
- Below average damage × attack speed (209)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (418)
- Below average attack range (2.31 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (627)
- Below average attack range (2.31 m)
