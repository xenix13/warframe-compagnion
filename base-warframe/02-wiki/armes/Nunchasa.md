Narin's signature bow is the north wind's icy messenger. Alt Fire unleashes a charged blizzard arrow that freezes enemies it touches and leaves behind a chilling path.

Nunchasa is Narin's signature bow. Attacking enemies builds charges to use the Alternate Fire: a charged wide Cold projectile.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Cold and Puncture damage on its primary fire and primarily Cold damage on its Alternate Fire.
- Hits on enemies build up alt-fire charges, represented by five icicles around the lower portion of the reticle, each being split in half. 5 charges are needed to fully charge the alt-fire, with direct hits generating half a charge and weak point hits one charge.
- Hits created through Multishot, and Punch Through contribute to charging the Alt-fire. Additionally, Blast status effects grant charge, but not upon their death detonation. Extra Hit effects (such as Toxic Lash) do not grant extra charge.
- Alt-fire unleashes a 2 meter wide projectile with infinite body Punch Through and forced Freeze on enemies caught within the radius, while leaving behind a trail that inflicts 1 Cold proc per second on enemies that walk into it.
- Trail lasts for 10 seconds.
- Has a headshot multiplier of 1x.
- Projectile and trail width is unaffected by Firestorm ( Primed).

- Two innate polarities.
