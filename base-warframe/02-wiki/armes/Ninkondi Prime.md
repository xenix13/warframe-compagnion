An ancient weapon imbued with a new truth.

The Ninkondi Prime is the primed variant of the Ninkondi Tenno Nunchaku, sporting overall higher stats and added physical damage, at some expense to its Electricity damage. Ninkondi Prime was released alongside Wukong Prime and Zhuge Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Atlantis Vulcan and Mafic Rain (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance of all nunchaku.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (234)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high status chance (36.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (306.07)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (468)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average damage × attack speed (612.14)
- Very high status chance (36.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (702)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High damage × attack speed (918.21)
- Very high status chance (36.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.31 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average attack range (2.31 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average attack range (2.31 m)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
