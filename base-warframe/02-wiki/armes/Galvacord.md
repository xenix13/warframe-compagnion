Shock and maim with this heavyweight retractable whip.

Galvacord is a Corpus heavy retractable whip with Electricity damage and very high status chance, but very slow attack speed.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matching the Burning Wasp stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Secura Lecta for the highest status chance of all whips.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (420)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (630)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (12.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low attack speed (0.750x animation speed)
- Low damage × attack speed (172.62)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (12.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low damage × attack speed (345.24)
- Very low attack speed (0.750x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (12.00%)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low damage × attack speed (517.86)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.750x animation speed)
- Low crit multiplier (1.80x)
