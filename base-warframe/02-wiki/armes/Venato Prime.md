Fashioned from the willingly-donated bone of the Sentient Ur-Hatho and embellished by Orokin luxurator Sembik Vol, Venato Prime was to be wielded against “common enemies” of the united peoples – had any ever emerged from the darkness beyond Tau.

Venato Prime is the Primed counterpart of the Venato scythe, acting as a direct upgrade and has the ability to apply Tau status when applying another Status Effect. Venato Prime was released alongside Caliban Prime and Vadarya Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Applying a Status Effect has a 100% chance to proc a Tau status.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Stalking Fan stance.
- Can use the Venato-exclusive Sentient Incision mod.
- Innate polarity.
- When wielded by Caliban/ Caliban Prime, attacks have a 50% chance to generate an additional Melee Combo point.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (34.00%)
- Above average total damage (245)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.166x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High status chance (32.00%)
- Very high damage × attack speed (411.93)
- High crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (34.00%)
- Above average total damage (490)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high damage × attack speed (823.87)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.166x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high crit chance (34.00%)
- High total damage (735)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high damage × attack speed (1235.80)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.166x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Highest innate block angle of all melee weapons, tied with Tenet Grigori and Venato of scythes, and tied with Ghoulsaw, Vastilok, and Tenet Agendus.

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- No numerical disadvantages.

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
