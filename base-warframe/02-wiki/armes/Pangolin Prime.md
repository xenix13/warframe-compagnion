A classic Tenno blade, beautifully reimagined.

The Pangolin Prime is the primed variant of the Pangolin Sword, acting as a direct upgrade. It was released alongside Titania Prime and Corinth Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Slam Radial Damage deals Impact damage.
- Slam Radial Damage has a guaranteed Slash proc in addition to the Impact proc.

- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crimson Dervish, Vengeful Revenant and Swooping Falcon.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all swords, behind Dark Sword.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- Above average total damage (248)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (298.36)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High total damage (496)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (596.73)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High total damage (744)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (895.10)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Pangolin Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Pangolin Sword (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (248.00 vs. 150.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 12.4 vs. 7.5)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 37.2 vs. 22.5)
- Higher Slash damage ( 198.4 vs. 120)

- Higher total damage (248 vs. 150)
- Higher base critical chance (26.00% vs. 8.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.20x vs. 1.50x)
- Higher base status chance (30.00% vs. 22.00%)
- Lower Block Angle (50° vs. 55°)
- More polarities ( vs. )
- Higher Mastery Rank required (14 vs. 3)

## Notes

- Guaranteed Slash proc on slam radial damage.
- Slam radial damage and proc damage is affected by Seismic Wave, base damage mods (e.g. Pressure Point) and faction mods (e.g. Smite Grineer).
- Does not affect heavy slam attacks.
