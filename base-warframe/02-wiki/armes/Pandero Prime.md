Pandero Prime is the Primed variant of the Pandero semi-automatic pistol, sporting increased damage and status chance. Pandero Prime was released alongside Octavia Prime and Tenora Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Alternate Fire shoots all remaining ammunition in the magazine in a quick burst.
- Benefits from magazine increasing mods to further increase the burst's damage.
- Prevents Zoom.
- This prevents the buffs from Hydraulic Crosshairs and Sharpened Bullets from affecting this attack as a result.

- Has high Recoil and wide spread, making it inaccurate beyond short range.

- Innate polarity.

## Notes

- As a Signature Weapon, Pandero Prime gains a bonus effect with Octavia or Octavia Prime, where headshot kills with the Alternate Fire increases reload speed by 20%.
- No weak spots other than actual heads will trigger this effect.
- Headshots on invulnerable enemies will still grant this effect, but not with invulnerable bosses.

- The Alternate Fire of the Pandero Prime will eject all of the remaining ammunition in the magazine in a single burst.
- The base firerate of the alt-fire is 10 rounds per second.
- Increasing the magazine size of the weapon such as with Slip Magazine and using recoil reduction mods like Steady Hands can help to make its damage output more consistent and reliable.
- If buffed with unlimited ammo such as from the Reactant Buff, the weapon will only fire its magazine amount at a time, with small delays between each trigger pull.
- Can be canceled with quick melee.
- Aiming will be disabled for the duration of the burst; hence the alternate fire cannot benefit from Acolyte Mods.
