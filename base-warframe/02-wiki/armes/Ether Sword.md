A single blade for more precision. The Ether Sword cuts so cleanly that it was considered by the Tenno to be a more humane weapon for 'cleansing' infested allies.

The Ether Sword is a longsword type weapon and is part of the Ether weapon family, capable of inflicting Radiation procs on its slam attacks.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Slam Radial Damage deals Radiation damage.
- Slam Attacks have a guaranteed Radiation proc in addition to the Impact proc.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Iron Phoenix and Rising Steel (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest status chance of all swords, behind Dark Sword.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (34.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average total damage (192)
- Low damage × attack speed (201.6)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (403.2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (604.8)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- While both the normal Slam Radial Damage as well as the Heavy Slam Radial Damage deal 100% Radiation damage, only the normal Slam guarantees a Radiation proc.
