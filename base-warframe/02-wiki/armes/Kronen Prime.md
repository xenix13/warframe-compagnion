The Kronen Prime is the Prime version of the Kronen tonfas, acting as a direct upgrade. Kronen Prime was released along with Zephyr Prime and Tiberon Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Gemini Cross and Star Divide (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate and polarity.
- +10% to Bullet Jump velocity when wielded. (See #Bugs)

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest attack speed of all tonfas, tieing with Praedos.
- Third highest base damage of all tonfas, behind Ohma and Prisma Ohma.
- Second highest status chance of all tonfas, behind Telos Boltace.
- Grants a 10% boost to Bullet Jump velocity when equipped (melee-mode recommended to avoid auto-swap on aim).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (34.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (302.60)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (424)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (605.21)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (636)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (907.82)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- No numerical disadvantages.

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Kronen Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Kronen (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (212.00 vs. 130.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 21.2 vs. 13)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 21.2 vs. 13)
- Higher Slash damage ( 169.6 vs. 104)

- Higher total damage (212 vs. 130)
- Higher base critical chance (22.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 1.50x)
- Higher base status chance (34.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Higher attack speed (1.170x animation speed vs. 1.080x animation speed)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (13 vs. 3)
