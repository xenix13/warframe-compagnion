A beautifully forged instrument of devastation.

Fragor Prime is the Prime variant of the Fragor, featuring higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance, at the expense of attack speed. It also has an initial combo count of 30. It was released alongside Vauban Prime and Akstiletto Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crushing Ruin stance.
- Can equip the Brokk skin, which provides +5% attack speed, but -1s combo duration.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has an initial Combo Count of 30 (2.0x initial heavy attack multiplier).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (40.00%)
- High total damage (270)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)
- High damage × attack speed (345.6)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (40.00%)
- High total damage (540)
- High damage × attack speed (691.2)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high crit chance (40.00%)
- High total damage (810)
- High damage × attack speed (1036.8)
- Above average attack range (2.60 m)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Very low attack speed (0.800x animation speed)
- Low status chance (18.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.800x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.800x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Fragor Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Fragor (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (270.00 vs. 200.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 189 vs. 140)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 40.5 vs. 30)
- Higher Slash damage ( 40.5 vs. 30)

- Higher total damage (270 vs. 200)
- Higher base critical chance (40.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.50x vs. 2.00x)
- Higher base status chance (18.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Longer melee range (2.60 m vs. 2.50 m)
- Lower attack speed (0.800x animation speed vs. 0.833x animation speed)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (12 vs. 2)

## Tips

- Thanks to its passive ability, Blood Rush will always be active, guaranteeing at least a 56% critical chance. This can be stacked with Corrupt Charge, granting a minimum combo multiplier of 4.0x and 88% critical chance, but reducing the combo time. This strategy may be more efficient if a combo duration mod is used (e.g., Body Count) to offset the effects of Corrupt Charge.
