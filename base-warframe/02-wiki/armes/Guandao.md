Harvest the enemy with this towering polearm.

The Guandao is a Tenno polearm with a massive blade, giving it unparalleled range compared to most polearms and equally impressive damage and critical chance, at the expense of slowing its attack speed.

This weapon can be sold for Credits5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Shimmering Blight and Argent Scourge (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Above average total damage (606)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (4.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (224.80)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Guandao (Normal Attack), compared to Guandao Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (202.00 vs. 240.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 50.5 vs. 60)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 10.1 vs. 12)
- Lower Slash damage ( 141.4 vs. 168)

- Lower total damage (202 vs. 240)
- Lower base critical chance (28.00% vs. 32.00%)
- Lower base critical multiplier (2.20x vs. 2.40x)
- Lower base status chance (4.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Lower attack speed (0.833x animation speed vs. 1.000x animation speed)
- Lower Combo Duration (5.0 s vs. 6.0 s)
- Less polarities ( vs. )
- Lower Mastery Rank required (4 vs. 12)

## Notes

- The Guandao is included in the Guandao Collection, along with the Zakti and Mozi Syandana, for 345 Platinum 345.

## Tips

- When going up against armored opponents, one should consider building for Corrosive due to the weapon's low status chance.
