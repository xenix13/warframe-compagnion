With its crescent shaped blades flying in sweeping arcs and striking at tremendous speed, the Tipedo demands focus from those wishing to unlock its true power.

The Tipedo is a bladed staff weapon with immense attack speed.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000. It is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Lesion.

- Tipedo → Lesion

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance has polarity, matches Flailing Branch stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fastest attack speed of all staves.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.330x animation speed)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.330x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.330x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all staves, after Mk1-Bo.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (124)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low status chance (20.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (197.90)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low total damage (248)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (395.80)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Low total damage (372)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (593.71)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
