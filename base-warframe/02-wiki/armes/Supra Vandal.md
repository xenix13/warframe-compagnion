A customized version of the powerful Supra, featuring a metallic finish and Lotus decal.

The Supra Vandal is the Vandal version of the Supra assault rifle, sporting higher critical chance and critical multiplier, accuracy, and magazine size and max ammo.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Requires a spool-up of 4 shots before optimal fire rate is achieved.
- Fire rate starts at 40% of the listed value, and increases by 20% per shot.

- Can equip the Supra-exclusive Directed Convergence and Entropy Burst mods.

Advantages over other Primary Auto-Spool Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fully Spooled (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high burst DPS (580)
- Very high sustained DPS (515.55)
- Very high magazine size (300)
- Very high total damage (40)
- Very high accuracy (3.00° to 4.00°)
- Very high reload speed (3.00 s)
- Very high ammo max (1,600)
- Very high status chance (30.00%)
- Very high damage per shot (46.4)

Disadvantages over other Primary Auto-Spool Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Projectiles have travel time, making long range shots more difficult.
- Spool-up creates a warm-up period of weakness for fire rate, making short engagements undesirable.
- Fully Spooled (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Very low fire rate (12.50 attacks/sec)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Supra Vandal (Fully Spooled), compared to Supra (Fully Spooled):
- Equal base damage
- Higher base critical chance (16.00% vs. 12.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 1.80x)
- Higher average damage per tap (46.4 vs. 43.84)
- Higher burst DPS (580 vs. 548)
- Higher sustained DPS (515.55 vs. 453.51)
- Larger magazine (300 vs. 180)
- Larger max ammo capacity (1,600 vs. 1,080)
- Faster spool-up (4 rounds vs. 5 rounds)
- More accurate (28.6 vs. 14.3)
- Tighter first-shot spread (3.00° vs. 6.00°)
- Tighter maximum spread (4.00° vs. 8.00°)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (14 vs. 12)

## Notes

- Cancelling the reload animation on its third-quarter period counts as a reload, much like the Soma.

## Tips

- Supra Vandal has a decent fire rate like the Gorgon, but it is less accurate at longer distances. Firing in short bursts may help with accuracy.
- Alternatively, use the mod Stabilizer or Vile Precision reduce the high recoil, making it more controllable to fire.
- To counter the weapon's quick ammo consumption, bring Squad Ammo Restores, Rifle Ammo Mutation, Carrier with Ammo Case, Vigilante Supplies and/or the Rifle Scavenger aura.
- Shred or Metal Auger can help hit more enemies, somewhat balancing the high ammo consumption.
- Due to the large magazine size of 300 bullets, Wildfire is a viable option for this weapon as it can add Heat damage and provided a sizeable increase to the magazine size.
- Despite being listed as a projectile-based weapon, Galvanized Aptitude is additive to base damage increments like Primary Plated Round and Serration.
