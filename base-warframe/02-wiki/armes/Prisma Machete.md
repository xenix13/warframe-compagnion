The addition of Prisma Crystal allows this otherwise barbaric weapon to elegantly hack through any foe.

The Prisma Machete is the Prisma variant of the Machete, featuring overall improved stats.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sundering Weave, Cyclone Kraken and Rending Wind (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (31.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (193)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low damage × attack speed (200.87)
- Low crit multiplier (1.90x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (414.48)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low crit multiplier (1.90x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (621.73)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low crit multiplier (1.90x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
