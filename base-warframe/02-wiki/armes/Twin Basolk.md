These nasty, little hatchets slice through steel as if it were flesh.

The Twin Basolk are a pair of Heat-based Grineer axes.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Heat damage.
- Can use the Twin Basolk-exclusive Rift Strike mod.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Swirling Tiger stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all dual swords, behind Twin Krohkur.
- Highest status chance of all dual swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high status chance (40.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (420)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high status chance (40.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (630)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high status chance (40.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Below average damage × attack speed (220.5)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (441)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (661.5)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Due to the crafting requirements including Atomos and Dual Zoren, without spending platinum, Twin Basolk costs 180,000 Credits 180,000, 4 Morphics, 2 Argon Crystal, 2,400 Salvage, 1,300 Polymer Bundle, 1,100 Ferrite, 800 Alloy Plate and 800 Rubedo. The lowest possible time spent crafting the Twin Basolk is 36 hours, assuming the Atomos and Dual Zoren are finished at the same time (although the Dual Zoren can be started any time within 12 hours of starting Atomos, and still retain the 36 hour total build time).
