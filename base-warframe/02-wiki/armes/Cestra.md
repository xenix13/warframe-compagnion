Compact and deadly, the Cestra discharges bolts of energy at an increasingly rapid rate.

The Cestra is a Corpus-built sidearm added in Update 11.0 (2013-11-20). In essence, It is a scaled-down version of the Supra, where it has a brief spooling period before reaching its maximum fire rate.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000; it is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Dual Cestra (x2).

- Cestra + Cestra → Dual Cestra

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.

- Very high accuracy.

- Very high ammo pickup (40)

- Very high ammo max (420)
- High fire rate (6.67 attacks/sec)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Projectiles have travel time.
- Requires a spool-up of 6 shots before optimal fire rate is achieved. Fire rate starts at 60% of the listed value, and increases by 8% per shot.
- High recoil when fully spooled.
- Poor ammo economy.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Cestra Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Cestra with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Cestra Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 150 Saggen Pearl, and 80 Kovnik.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below;
- Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

#### Overview

- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Mode switching is not instantaneous and has an animation equal to the weapon's reload speed.
- Incarnon Form is activated by filling the Incarnon Transmutation gauge that appears under the reticle by achieving weakpoint hits and then pressing the Alternate Fire button. 15 weakpoints will completely fill the gauge. A full gauge is not required to activate Incarnon Form.
- Charges do generate when hitting weak points highlighted by Sonar or Detect Vulnerability.
- However, they do not generate from hits on Crystallize's crystalline growths.

- Weakpoints on corpses or Invulnerable targets do not build charges.
- Individual Multishot bullets can build charges.

- Instead of drawing ammunition from its reserves, the Cestra's Incarnon Form uses a separate "magazine" with each charge producing 10 rounds to a maximum of 150. Once all ammunition is expended, the Cestra reverts to its normal form.
- Incarnon Form's magazine is not affected by mods.
- Incarnon Form is not affected by Ammo Efficiency (such as Energized Munitions).
- Charges and Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, charges and Incarnon Form are removed upon death.

- Swapping to and from Incarnon Form will fully reload the normal form's magazine.

## Tips

- Pistol Ammo Mutation (or its Primed counterpart), Trick Mag, Pistol Scavenger or Squad Ammo Restores can be used to counteract the Cestra's high rate of ammo consumption.
- While having good base accuracy, the Cestra has significant recoil that scatters its shots, making it ineffective for long range shooting. Steady Hands is a must if one plans to utilize the Cestra's high base accuracy effectively.
