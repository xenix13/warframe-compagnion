A ferocious melee weapon crafted by the Grineer and carried by their elite melee forces.

The Machete is a Grineer melee weapon favored by Scorpions.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sundering Weave, Cyclone Kraken and Rending Wind (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all machetes, after Kama.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low total damage (120)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (115.54)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low total damage (240)
- Very low damage × attack speed (231.08)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low total damage (360)
- Very low damage × attack speed (346.62)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

## Notes

- The weapon was removed from the Market in Update 10.4 (2013-10-16).
- PC and PS4 players who logged in between July 9, 2014 and July 16, 2014 received a Machete blueprint as a free gift.
- The blueprint was given from completing One Thousand Cuts - Mission 3.
- The blueprint and weapon were available for purchase between May 25th and June 1st, 2017 — 25,000 Credits 25,000 and 90 Platinum 90 respectively.

- As of Update 20.6 (2017-05-24), Machete is available as a random login reward, in the form of a blueprint.
