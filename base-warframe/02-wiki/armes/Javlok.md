The Javlok is a Grineer speargun that fires superheated projectiles dealing Heat damage. Its Alternate Fire allows the weapon itself to be thrown at enemies, dealing an area-of-effect damage which is boosted by the remaining ammunition in the magazine.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Heat damage.
- Primary Fire shoots super-heated slugs that explodes on impact, damaging all enemies in a 2.4 meter radius.
- Automatic charge-up.

- Alternate Fire throws the Javlok, which inflicts a guaranteed Impact proc and creates a 6 meter explosion.
- Throwing the spear consumes the entire magazine, then reloads the weapon.
- Explosion deals 20% additional damage per round in the magazine.
- Increasing magazine size thus increases potential damage.

- Initial hit and explosion apply status separately.
- Explosions does not need direct line of sight to deal damage and will penetrate walls.
- Reloads 33% of the magazine per second while deployed or holstered.
- Explosions benefit from Firestorm ( Primed) and Cautious Shot.
- Can use the Javlok-exclusive Amalgam Javlok Magazine Warp mod.
- Innate two polarities.

## Notes

- Primary fire projectiles are affected by punch through, although it only affects map geometry, similar to the Grattler.
- The ability to equip the Adhesive Blast mod was removed in Update 21.3.1, likely due to it having no effect or benefits if it did.

### Alternate Fire

- The Javlok's secondary fire throws the weapon itself at the target, inflicting 150 physical damage to the impaled target and 300 area-of-effect Heat damage to all enemies within the impact radius.
- For each round remaining in the magazine when thrown, an additional 20% damage will be added to the 300 AoE damage. For example, with just one round left the throw will deal 150 physical damage and 300 × (1 + 1 × 20%) = 360 Heat damage. A full magazine of 6 will deal 150 physical damage and 300 × (1 + 6 × 20%) = 660 Heat damage.
- The damage bonus is independent of other modifiers like Serration, crits, and anti-faction mods.

Area of Effect Damage = 300 × (1 + [0.2 × Ammo in Magazine]) × (1 + Damage Mods) × (1 + Elemental Mods) × (0.2 × [1 + Critical Chance Mods]) × (2 × [1 + Critical Damage Mods])
- 33% of the magazine is reloaded every second when the Javlok is deployed or holstered.
- Throwing the Javlok will consume all of the remaining ammo in the magazine.
- The Javlok's secondary fire has a max throwing range of 48 meters.
- The arc Javlok will take when thrown is affected by the angle of the throw.
- Throwing distance can be increased with flight speed modifiers, such as Terminal Velocity and Zephyr's Jet Stream augment.

- In addition to the secondary fire's AoE inflicting Heat damage, it also has a guaranteed Impact proc.
- Alt-fire is not affected by Multishot.
- However, the Arsenal may still report that it is (see Bugs).

- Alt-fire is not affected by Ammo Efficiency. It does not increase damage of the attack and the entire magazine still gets consumed.
- If the Javlok directly hits an enemy and the enemy survives the explosion, the Javlok will remain impaled on the enemy.

## Tips

- Both the primary and secondary fire of the Javlok can be controlled by Ivara's Navigator.
- It's highly recommended to use Wildfire when modding the Javlok, as both the Heat damage and magazine capacity boost will improve the weapon's thrown damage.
- Similarly, mods such as Amalgam Javlok Magazine Warp and Tainted Mag will also further increase the thrown damage. The decreased reload speed of the latter is also circumvented by the weapon's innate magazine replenishment when thrown or holstered.

- The Javlok's primary fire mode has an Alarming noise level, but the alt-fire throw itself is considered silent and therefore will not break Ivara's Prowl.
- The Javlok works well in conjunction with Vauban's Bastille's Vortex part as the AoE damage dealt by the alt-fire will hit all enemies trapped in the Vortex.
- Secondary fire will pierce and hit the head of an enemy manning a Rampart even without punch through.
- Secondary fire is unaffected by mods that require you to aim to gain benefits (e.g., Argon Scope, Bladed Rounds) on account of the weapon being in flight. It's advised to mod accordingly if you plan on using the alt-fire extensively.
- Javlok Capacitors, which drop from Grineer Prosecutors, can be farmed on Ceres.
- In close-quarters where the extra velocity isn't needed, throwing the Javlok without charging it can make it easier to avoid staggering yourself due to the explosion delay and range indicator.
- The high Punch Through of the non-charged version also makes it harder to accidentally stagger yourself by hitting enemies or allies that get too close.
