Tear and maim with this agile, lightweight glass hammer. When Gara wields her signature melee weapon, it gains additional Slam Radial Damage.

The Volnus is Gara's signature glass-themed hammer, boasting a high attack speed and status chance but low critical chance and critical multiplier. Radial slam attacks are much stronger when wielded by Gara.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Volnus Prime for the fastest attack speed of all hammers.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (220)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack speed (1.200x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (292.51)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (440)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (585.02)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.200x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (660)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (877.53)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.200x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (18.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.60x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (18.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.60x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (18.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.60x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Lower base damage (220.00 vs. 250.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 48.4 vs. 55)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 70.4 vs. 80)
- Lower Slash damage ( 101.2 vs. 115)

- Lower total damage (220 vs. 250)
- Lower base critical chance (18.00% vs. 20.00%)

## Notes

- As a Signature Weapon, Volnus gains a bonus effect with Gara or Gara Prime, gaining 100% additional radial slam attack damage.
