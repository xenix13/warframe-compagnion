The blade of the world-destroyer reaches its apotheosis, severing conflict itself. In forging this weapon, you have brought unrealized dreams to their ultimate conclusion.

War Prime is the Primed variant of Hunhow's War heavy blade, sporting increased Damage, Critical Multiplier, and Status Chance.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Every hit on enemies that are not ragdolled procs an Impact status effect.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Cleaving Whirlwind and Tempo Royale stances.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High total damage (270)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.20 m)
- High status chance (32.00%)
- Very high damage × attack speed (389.21)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High total damage (500)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (720.76)
- Very high attack range (3.20 m)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (26.00%)
- High total damage (810)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high damage × attack speed (1167.63)
- Very high attack range (3.20 m)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- War Prime (Normal Attack), compared to War (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (270.00 vs. 250.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 194.4 vs. 120)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 32.4 vs. 60)
- Lower Slash damage ( 43.2 vs. 70)

- Higher total damage (270 vs. 250)
- Higher base critical multiplier (3.20x vs. 2.60x)
- Higher base status chance (32.00% vs. 26.00%)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (15 vs. 10)

## Notes

- War Prime cannot be sold for Credits nor traded, and only one copy of Hunhow's Trinket or War Prime can be owned.
