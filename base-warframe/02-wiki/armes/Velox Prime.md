This sidearm’s circular design evokes Protea Prime’s perception of time.

Velox Prime is the Primed counterpart of Velox ammo efficient automatic sidearm, sporting increased damage, critical multiplier, status chance, fire rate, and magazine size. Velox Prime was released alongside Protea Prime and Okina Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Reloads 50% faster from a fully depleted magazine.
- Can use the Velox-exclusive Velox Conclusion mod.
- Innate two and one polarities.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Innate 20% Ammo Efficiency.
- When wielded by Protea/ Protea Prime, ammo efficiency is increased to 40%.

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High burst DPS (523.26)
- Above average sustained DPS (320.73)
- High magazine size (70)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Very high ammo max (520)
- Very high fire rate (17.00 attacks/sec)
- High status chance (32.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (14.00%)
- Low total damage (27)
- Very low reload speed (2.60 s)
- Low damage per shot (30.78)
