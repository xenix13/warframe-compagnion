The Nepheri are the signature Dual Daggers of Archon Amar. Has a unique neutral combo that allows its user to spit four Heat fireballs.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Heat damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Spinning Needle stance.
- Unique neutral combo that slashes forward twice with both daggers and then hurls four consecutive fireballs.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has mid-ranged projectile attack.

## Notes

- Each fireball deal 50% of the weapon's base damage.
- Has a 21% Critical Chance, 2.3x Critical Multiplier, and 33% Status Chance.
- Fireballs have a range of 19 meters (0.2s lifetime).
- Projectile range can be increased with Zephyr's Jet Stream.

- Fireballs have Infinite Body Punch Through.
- Fireballs have a guaranteed Impact proc against enemies within 10 meters and has a chance to proc Heat and other modded elements.
- Fireballs cannot proc Shattering Impact.
- Projectile speed is unaffected by Attack Speed.
- Fireballs do not build or sustain combo count.
- Fireballs are Alarming.

- Because the Fireball attack requires a neutral combo, Tenno Specters will use it when attacking enemies when in range.

## Known Bugs

- Condition Overload is multiplicative to base damage sources on direct hits from fireballs.
