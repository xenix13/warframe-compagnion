The Destreza is a Tenno rapier that focuses on critical chance.

This weapon can be sold for 7,500 Credits 7,500.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Vulpine Mask and Cunning Aspect (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest status chance of all rapiers.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (158)
- Very low status chance (14.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (202.24)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (316)
- Below average damage × attack speed (404.48)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average total damage (474)
- Below average damage × attack speed (606.72)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Due to the innate ability to apply a slash proc on the first strike, in combination with the high critical chance and ability for a Blood Rush/ Body Count build, this weapon works very well with Relentless Combination.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Destreza Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Destreza with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Destreza requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 60 Rune Marrow.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below;
- Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack, a Slam Attack or a Heavy Slam Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
- Despite the in-game description only listing Heavy Attacks (and consequently Heavy Slams) as able to activate Incarnon Form, non-Heavy Slams may be used as well and may be preferred as they do not reduce your Melee Combo. This behavior is shared by all melee Incarnon weapons released since Thalys, which also includes Obex.
