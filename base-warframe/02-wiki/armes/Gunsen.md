Lacerate attackers with two razorsharp warfans.

The Gunsen is a Tenno bladed Warfan. The weapon's impressive speed complements its high status chance and good Slash damage.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Slicing Feathers and Votive Onslaught stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average status chance (28.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- High status chance (28.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Low total damage (160)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Below average damage × attack speed (217.15)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Below average total damage (320)
- Below average damage × attack speed (434.30)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Below average total damage (480)
- Below average damage × attack speed (651.45)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Gunsen (Normal Attack), compared to Gunsen Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (160.00 vs. 225.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 12.8 vs. 9)

- Lower Slash damage ( 128 vs. 202.5)

- Lower total damage (160 vs. 225)
- Lower base critical chance (16.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Lower base status chance (28.00% vs. 34.00%)
- Shorter melee range (1.70 m vs. 1.90 m)
- Less polarities ( vs. )
- Lower Mastery Rank required (8 vs. 12)

## Notes

- Warfan slams hit twice.
- Second hit of a heavy slam will use the stats of a normal slam.

- The first and only melee weapon to have no imposed "roll" on wall attacks.
- The stealth finisher physically moves the player's Warframe and the execution target forward, potentially throwing the player into bottomless pits as well as moving the target into line of sight of other enemies, alerting them when they otherwise would not have seen it.
