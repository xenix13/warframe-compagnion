CODEX
A customized version of the elusive Snipetron, with metallic finish and Lotus decal.

The Snipetron Vandal is the Vandal version of the Snipetron, sporting an increase in magazine size and reload speed, but sacrifices Impact and Slash damages in order to boost its Puncture damage.

This weapon can be sold for 7,500 Credits 7,500

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Two zoom modes:
- First zoom has 2.5x zoom distance and +30% headshot damage.
- Second zoom has 6.0x zoom distance and +50% headshot damage, making it ideal for long-range combat.

- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Primary Sniper Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Pinpoint accuracy while zoomed in; shots will hit exactly on the reticle dot, perfect for headshots.
- Innate 3 meter punch through.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High reload speed (2.00 s)
- Very high ammo max (72)
- Above average fire rate (2.00 attacks/sec)

Disadvantages over other Primary Sniper Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 50% from 400m to 600m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- High recoil.
- Inaccurate beyond short range when fired from the hip.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low active falloff slope (400.0m/%)
- Low burst DPS (512)
- Low sustained DPS (307.2)
- Below average magazine size (6)
- Below average total damage (200)
- Very low ammo pickup (15)
- Very low accuracy (0.00° to 15.00°)
- Low damage per shot (256)
- Very low status chance (16.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (2.00x)

## Tips

- While there are significantly more high-powered snipers, this (and the original Snipetron) is the only Puncture-based weapon in this category.
- When aiming, try to line up your shot before zooming, then zoom, adjust for error, and fire. This may take some practice, but it's much easier and faster than firing from the hip or zooming haphazardly.
- Carry a good melee or close-range sidearm to engage foes at close range, since the Snipetron Vandal is inefficient and difficult to use properly at this distance.
- As a general note, ammo consumption should not be problematic, provided players make full use of their abilities and melee weapons in conjunction with Snipetron Vandal.
- With maximized Split Chamber and Point Strike you have a 48.44% chance of landing one critical hit, with a 35.16% chance to land 2 critical hits and a 16.41% chance to not land a critical hit at all.
- Use the Sniper Ammo Mutation mod to greatly relieve the low total ammo and ammo drop chance for sniper rifles. Alternatively, bring Squad Ammo Restores or use Carrier's Ammo Case.
