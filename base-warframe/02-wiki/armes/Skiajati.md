CODEX
Forged of steel grafted from Umbra's flesh, this elegant nikana unites with its Warframe to form a single being of seething purpose.

Skiajati is Excalibur Umbra's signature nikana, with the unique ability to turn its user Invisible on finisher attacks.

This weapon cannot be sold.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Decisive Judgement, Tranquil Cleave, Blind Justice and Fateful Truth (Conclave only) stance.
- Finishers temporarily turn the user invisible for 6 seconds.
- Two innate polarities.
- Lifesteal is applied to attacks when a Daikyu modded with Amalgam Daikyu Target Acquired is equipped.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest attack speed of all nikanas.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.10x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all nikanas, after Nikana.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average total damage (175)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average total damage (350)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Below average total damage (525)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Does not have a Signature Weapon bonus with Excalibur Umbra, likely due to already having a unique trait.
- All the Mastery Rank points that would normally be granted from ranking up the weapon will be automatically received upon acquiring it.
- Despite its mastery rank of 11, players of lower ranks can acquire and use this weapon.
- Standing Finisher attacks with this weapon turn the user invisible for 6 seconds. Attacking or using an ability will break the invisibility, similar to the cloak provided by Shade and its Ghost precept.
- Ground Finisher, Mercy, and Ash's Blade Storm do not activate the invisibility effect.
- The invisibility period is not affected by mods.
- Performing a finisher while under the invisibility effect of the Skiajati will refresh the cloak's duration.
- Performing a Mercy Kill will not break the invisibility effect but won't refresh either.

- Invisibility is not affected by Nullifier bubbles.

- The cloth piece is a fixed part of the sheath and not a removable cosmetic.
- Due to the special ability of this weapon, stances with finishers in them would be useful in proccing the effect. However, there are no Nikana stances containing finishers.
