Deliver crushing blows with this Grineer built, jet powered mace.

The Jat Kittag is a Grineer hammer that utilizes a built-in jet engine to empower the weight of its blows.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Slam Attacks have 100% status chance and innate Blast damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crushing Ruin stance.
- Innate and polarities.
- Can use the Jat Kittag-exclusive Vulcan Blitz mod.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- Very high status chance (100.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (600)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- Very high status chance (100.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Fragor for the lowest base damage of all hammers.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (230)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Tips

- When built for Blast and Impact, the Jat Kittag is more than capable of sending enemies into or through the ceiling with a well placed slam attack, or to the map boundaries if there is no ceiling present. This can instantly kill some enemies; such as by launching into the walls in Captain Vor's boss room on Mercury.
