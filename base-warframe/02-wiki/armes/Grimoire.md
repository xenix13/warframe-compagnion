CODEX
Claim the power of Albrecht Entrati's knowledge. Customize the Grimoire with Tome Mods that enhance Warframe Abilities and reward strategic combat. Its alternate attack releases a voltaic orb with guaranteed Electricity Status.

The Grimoire is the prized diary of the prominent Orokin scientist, Albrecht Entrati. It is a unique secondary weapon that casts bolts of Electricity, while its Alternate Fire throws a large voltaic orb that can slowly travel forward and bounce around the environment, electrocuting any enemies in its path.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals pure Electricity damage.
- Primary fire hurls fully automatic arcane energies.
- Has infinite ammo; does not need to reload.
- Shots explode in a 1 meter radius on impact with a surface or enemy.
- Explosions has linear Damage Falloff from 100% to 20% from central impact.
- Initial hits and explosions apply status separately.
- Explosions do not need direct line of sight to deal damage and will penetrate walls.

- Punch-Through is disabled on the primary fire.

- Alternate Fire summons a voltaic orb that launches electric bolts capable of leaping between enemies, and applying a forced Electricity proc on all targets. Orb attacks 1 enemy within 6 meters every second with an electric bolt that then chains to an additional 2 enemies within 6 meters of the bolt's target.
- The bolt and the chained hits both count as "Alt-Fire hits" for Invocation mods like Vome and Netra, and both can hit weakspots.
- Each chain hit does the same amount of damage as the initial electric bolt. Each attack makes an individual roll for status and critical chance.
- Despite being listed as having 350 base damage, the alt-fire's actual in-game base damage value is 280. Other base damage values are accurate.

- Number of chains is affected by Multishot. Rather than rolling a chance for an additional shot, Multishot here functions in steps. The number of chain hits increases by 1 for every 33.3% of Multishot added.
- For example, a maxed Amalgam Barrel Diffusion and Lethal Torrent would add 170% Multishot, and launch an orb which strikes an enemy, and then chains an additional 7 times. With at least 234% added Multishot, Xata Invocation can be maxed out with 10 stacks by a single bolt and its 9 subsequent chain hits.

- Chain seek radius is centered on the most recent target, and updates between chain hits, allowing for an orb with a high multishot value to leap to enemies well beyond the visually indicated 6m range.

- Alt-Fire requires a fully filled meter beneath the reticle in order to fire. The meter takes 45 seconds to completely recharge. Hitting enemies with the primary fire reduces recharge time by 1 second per hit.
- Multishot hits restore charge meter.
- Picking up secondary or universal ammo while Grimoire is held reduces recharge time by 10 seconds. Ammo pickups from Pistol Ammo Mutation do not reduce recharge time.
- Radial damage, Extra Hit mechanics, and attacks from specters do not restore charge meter.

- Base projectile speed is a fairly slow 6.0 m/s and can be increased with Lethal Momentum.
- The rate of bolt attacks from the voltaic orb is not affected by Fire Rate.
- Fire rate increases the speed of animation that creates the voltaic orb.

- The "active radial attack" refers to the explosion at the end of the voltaic orb's duration, which has linear Damage Falloff from 100% to 20% from central impact.
- The orb's electric bolt and its chaining hits are not affected by damage falloff or by Fulmination ( Primed).

## Notes

- The Grimoire can use a unique set of mods called Tome Mods in addition to standard Secondary Weapon mods.
- Some weapon stats, including modded damage and multishot, are updated dynamically for already-launched voltaic orbs (i.e., benefits from Conjunction Voltage and Arcane Precision stacks created after it was launched).
- Other weapon stats, including crit chance, are snapshotted at time of using Alt-Fire.
- The stats are then inherited by the bolt attack and its chain hits, allowing for Secondary Enervate to apply for the full duration of an orb, as well as buffs like Secondary Surge, Ballistic Battery, and Galvanized Crosshairs to apply if they were active at the time of firing.

- Voltaic orbs do not need line of sight on their targets, and can launch electric bolts at enemies that are behind walls.
- However, the orb itself bounces when it hits level geometry, while slowing nearly to a halt. This can be useful for keeping it in range of a stationary target.

- When a voltaic orb hits a Nullifier bubble, it is not destroyed. The orb just bounces off the bubble, and cannot target enemies inside of it.
- The voltaic orb will target invulnerable enemies, and hits on invulnerable enemies will stack Invocation effects.
- Orb will not target enemies in a 'defeated' state (ex: kneeling Adversaries and Thrall enemies), nor damageable allies (ex: those created by Enthrall.
- Alt-fire meter is charged when attacking damageable allies, but not invulnerable or defeated enemies.

- Enemies with Overguard will still be struck by the voltaic orb and targeted by chain lightning.
- The alt-fire orb technically fires new shots (similar to Azima alt-fire). This bypasses single-hit rules that normally apply to all projectiles guided by Navigator.
- Although the weapon can proc headshots, that is mostly from the Electricity procs rather than the the chain hits, which seem to leap between the centers of enemy hitboxes.
- Because all of the procs from chain hits happen simultaneously, has limited synergy with Secondary Encumber, as only 1 extra proc can occur per tick.
- Alt-Fire and the radial effect of the primary attack do not receive any damage bonus from Galvanized Shot.
- Cannot be equipped by On Call Crew.
- The Alt-Fire voltaic orb disappears when casting Razorwing or when switching between Sirius & Orion.
- On casting Reroot, the orb does not disappear, and can activate Invocation mods while Nokko is hidden, appearing on-screen after Reroot ends.

- The uncontrolled frame of Sirius & Orion can use the Grimoire, but their kills will not activate Canticle mods, and they seem not to cast Alt-Fire.
- Tome mod buffs do not have any interaction with Secondary Kinship.
- If the Grimoire has negative damage (such as from a negative damage riven of over -100%), it will no longer charge the alt fire. Tome mods that proc on hit will still work, however.

## Tips

- With infinite ammo and no reload, the Grimoire can benefit greatly from Fire Rate mods to increase damage output.
- Multishot significantly helps charging alternate fire meter, as individual projectile hits contribute toward charging it.
- Lethal Momentum increases the speed of both primary and Alt-Fire projectiles.
- To avoid overshooting enemies with the Alt-Fire, aim the orb at a surface near them. When it bounces, it will lose almost all momentum, creating a nearly stationary area of effect, which can help charge up Invocation mods.

- Due to its pure Electricity base damage, the orb can offer crowd control by efficiently applying Radiation to large groups of enemies.
- Quickly dropping an Alt-Fire orb with Secondary Fortifier on a group of enemies with Eximus units mixed in grants continuous Overguard gain, while allowing the wielder to perform other actions.
