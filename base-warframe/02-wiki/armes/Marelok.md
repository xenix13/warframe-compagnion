The Marelok combines the stopping power of a rifle with the portability of a pistol.

In essence, the Marelok pistol is a shortened Grinlok that has a higher fire rate, at the cost of lower damage and accuracy, as well as reduced critical multiplier, status chance, and magazine size.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Can use the Marelok-exclusive Shrapnel Rounds mod.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Very good ammunition efficiency.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High total damage (160)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- High ammo max (210)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average damage per shot (172)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Inaccuracy is noticeable over long range.
- High recoil.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average sustained DPS (221.12)
- Low magazine size (6)
- Low accuracy (5.00° to 15.00°)
- Very low fire rate (2.00 attacks/sec)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Marelok (Normal Attack), compared to Vaykor Marelok (Normal Attack):
- Equal base damage
- Lower Impact damage ( 80 vs. 96)
- Higher Slash damage ( 64 vs. 48)

- Lower base critical chance (15.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Lower base status chance (30.00% vs. 35.00%)
- Lower average damage per tap (172 vs. 176)
- Lower burst DPS (344 vs. 352)
- Lower sustained DPS (221.12 vs. 263.98)
- Smaller magazine (6 vs. 10)
- Less polarities ( vs. )
- Lower Mastery Rank required (7 vs. 10)
- No innate Justice effect

## Notes

- Due to the Marelok's low accuracy, additional bullets generated via Multishot will sometimes hit enemies behind or next to the main target, giving the illusion of Punch Through.
- For this reason, Magnum Force's accuracy penalty is very noticeable at higher ranks.

## Tips

- The Marelok is essentially a cut-down Grinlok, trading out accuracy for increased firepower and rate of fire. Like the Grinlok, its base stats are well-suited for builds that maximize the chance and damage of critical hits, and those that cause many status effects.
- The Marelok is a great sidearm for dealing with enemies at short to mid range for players who have a sniper or bow for their primary weapon. The Marelok's firepower is strong enough to be considered a sub-primary weapon.
- In order to reach the full fire-rate, one must fire the next shot just before the "lever-action" animation is completed.
