Syam is at home in the hands of a focused warrior. Its Heavy Attacks send forth shockwaves.

The Syam is a Duviri Nikana wielded by Dax Gladius. Its unique Heavy Attacks send out fast Heat shockwaves, cutting down foes at a long distance.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash and Puncture damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Decisive Judgement, Tranquil Cleave, and Blind Justice stance.
- Unique Heavy Attacks releases shockwaves that travel up to 230 meters and pierces targets.
- The first Heavy Attack releases a roughly 8 meter wide horizontal shockwave that deals 500 Heat damage with a forced Impact proc.
- The second Heavy Attack releases a narrow vertical shockwave that deals 1500 Heat damage.
- Shockwaves share the same status chance and critical as the normal attacks.
- Shockwave damage is affected by melee damage mods, Condition Overload, elemental mods, and Melee Combo.
- Heat damage will combine with other elements as expected.

- The shockwave can travel over obstacles wide enough to accomodate it, including uneven terrain and staircases.
- The shockwave only occurs when the Syam is wielded by Warframes, not the Drifter.

- Blocking can reflect projectiles back to the enemy firing them.
- Lifesteal is applied to attacks, including the unique shockwave attack, when a Daikyu modded with Amalgam Daikyu Target Acquired is equipped.

### Drifter Combat

- Power Strike (blocking + heavy attack, default RMB + MMB ) releases two spinning slashes in quick succession, inflicting Lifted.

## Notes

- Unlike other Nikanas, the Syam does not force proc Slash on a heavy attack. Slide heavy attacks function as per normal and will proc slash.
- Only applies Killing Blow's bonus to projectile hits if the user is in a Wind Up animation while the attack lands.
- Other bonuses related to Heavy Attacks, like the combo multiplier and 2x value of True Steel type mods seem to be working normally.
