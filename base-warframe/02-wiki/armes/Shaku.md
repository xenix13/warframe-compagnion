With these blazing fast nunchaku, the enemy will never know what hit them.

The Shaku are a set of Impact-based Tenno Nunchaku, featuring a fast attack rate. The Shaku are considered an alternative to the Ninkondi rather than a direct upgrade, despite using the Ninkondi as a component.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Atlantis Vulcan and Mafic Rain (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fastest attack speed of all nunchaku.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- High status chance (34.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (18.00%)
- Below average total damage (180)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.21 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (18.00%)
- Below average total damage (360)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.21 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (18.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.21 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
