A strong blade capable of inflicting fire damage to its foes.

The Heat Sword is a sword that radiates Heat on its slam attacks and ground finishers.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Normal Slam Attack has a forced Heat proc.
- Stance slot has a polarity, which matches Crimson Dervish, Swooping Falcon and Vengeful Revenant.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (20.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (20.00%)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Low total damage (147)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low status chance (20.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (138.16)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Low total damage (294)
- Very low damage × attack speed (276.33)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Low total damage (441)
- Low damage × attack speed (414.50)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
