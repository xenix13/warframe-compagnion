An intimidating Tonfa set designed to match the briary design of the Boltor.

The Boltace are a pair of bladed Tonfas, sharing the same aesthetic style as the Boltor.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Gemini Cross and Star Divide (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average status chance (28.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Ohma for the slowest attack speed of all tonfas.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Below average total damage (176)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low damage × attack speed (166.23)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Below average total damage (352)
- Low damage × attack speed (332.46)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Below average total damage (528)
- Low damage × attack speed (498.70)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Boltace (Normal Attack), compared to Telos Boltace (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (176.00 vs. 210.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 17.6 vs. 21)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 140.8 vs. 178.5)
- Higher Slash damage ( 17.6 vs. 10.5)

- Lower total damage (176 vs. 210)
- Lower base critical chance (6.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Lower base critical multiplier (1.50x vs. 2.00x)
- Lower base status chance (28.00% vs. 35.00%)
- Lower attack speed (0.917x animation speed vs. 1.080x animation speed)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (4 vs. 11)
