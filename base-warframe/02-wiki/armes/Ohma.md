CODEX
Bring the enemy to its knees with these twin electrified tonfas.

The Ohma are a pair of electrified, Corpus-built Tonfas that are Tia Mayn's weapon of choice. Slam attacks from the Ohma will arc to nearby enemies, electrifying them in the process.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sovereign Outcast stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (224)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (448)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (672)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Below average damage × attack speed (236.21)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Ohma (Normal Attack), compared to Prisma Ohma (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (224.00 vs. 250.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 76 vs. 82)
- Lower Slash damage ( 38 vs. 44)
- Lower Electricity damage ( 110 vs. 124)

- Lower total damage (224 vs. 250)
- Lower base critical chance (15.00% vs. 30.00%)
- Lower base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 2.20x)
- Higher base status chance (30.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Lower attack speed (0.917x animation speed vs. 1.000x animation speed)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (8 vs. 12)

## Notes

- Damage caused by the Ohma (including damage over time effects from status effects) will cause an arc of lightning to emit from the Ohma striking the target, this arc has no additional effects and deals no damage.
