Equipped with a huge magazine and impressive rate of fire, the GRAKATA is the preferred weapon of Grineer Lancers.

The Grakata is a Grineer assault rifle standard issue for Lancers, Hellions and Commanders, capable of unloading a quick barrage of bullets upon enemies. The low base damage is offset by the Grakata's high critical chance and status chance. Coupled with its high fire rate, the Grakata is able to inflict large amounts of damage and statuses in short periods of time, at the cost of being extremely ammo inefficient and hard to control in sustained fire with its absurd recoil.

This weapon can be sold for 7,500 Credits 7,500. This is a weapon crafting ingredient (x2) for Twin Grakatas.

- Grakata + Grakata → Twin Grakatas

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Can use the Grakata-exclusive Brain Storm and Wild Frenzy mods.

Advantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- High magazine size (60)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- High ammo max (750)
- Very high fire rate (20.00 attacks/sec)

Disadvantages over other Primary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- High recoil.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low burst DPS (275)
- Low sustained DPS (152.77)
- Very low total damage (11)
- Below average reload speed (2.40 s)
- Below average status chance (20.00%)
- Very low damage per shot (13.75)
- Below average crit multiplier (2.00x)

## Notes

- This weapon can be treated as a side-grade to the Soma, trading critical chance, magazine capacity, recoil, damage, and critical damage multiplier in exchange for a much greater status chance, rate of fire and maximum ammo capacity. it also does not require a spin-up period to fire at full speed.
- Due to its inefficient ammo economy, one can find themselves running out of ammo very quickly.
- With a fully ranked Ammo Drum, the Grakata can hold up to 1,035 rounds, magazine included.

## Tips

- The Grakata has a high chance of cutting corpses into pieces despite the fact that the Grakata's primary damage type is Impact, making it an ideal candidate for a Desecrating Nekros to bring along, as the game considers the cut corpses as separate entities, essentially giving multiple corpses to desecrate from a single enemy and thus yielding more chances to create more drops.
- Holding the trigger down and spraying bullets is not recommended, as the strong recoil of the weapon can easily waste of a lot of ammo. The Grakata's reload time also becomes far more of a problem on full auto, as the weapon discharges its entire magazine in 3 seconds without mods. Firing short bursts helps to increase your accuracy. Aim low when firing longer bursts to compensate for recoil.
- If you're leveling the weapon and running low on ammo, remember that weapons gain experience for assists as well, so one can damage an enemy with just one shot and then finish him with other weapon (Melee is recommended to bypass holster times).
- Using Rifle Ammo Mutation and/or Ammo Case can also help to recover ammo lost due to the weapon's habit of burning through ammo quickly. Alternatively, Vigilante Supplies can be used, trading out some ammo conversion efficiency for a minor boost to DPS.
- There are various mods you can use to counteract the Grakata's high recoil.
- Stabilizer is recommended as it reduces the recoil without compromising its fire rate.
- Critical Delay can be used as an alternative as the increased critical chance brings higher damage output and the lower fire rate decreases the recoil. However, this will lower overall DPS and it cannot be equipped simultaneously with Point Strike.
- Vile Precision can be used as another alternative, reducing both recoil and fire rate. It will increase accuracy on medium and long distances, albeit at the cost of lowering overall DPS.
- Reducing the fire rate with the previously mentioned mods does also help with ammo efficiency as well, since firing slower means consuming ammo slower. Some players may find that this benefit more than compensates for the loss of DPS.

- Heavy Caliber is a good choice for the Grakata, as the weapon's deceptively high accuracy mitigates the accuracy penalty generated by the mod, reducing the overall bullet spread. However, this is difficult to fully utilize without the simultaneous addition of one of the mods mentioned above as Heavy Caliber's accuracy penalty will be greatly exacerbated with the Grakata's high recoil.
- The Grakata will benefit the most from Hammer Shot, as the weapon has both a high status chance and critical chance, and will receive extra critical damage alongside a boost to status chance.
- Due to its high status chance and incredibly high firing speed, Grakata is able to proc many status effects rapidly.
- Although a large portion of procs will be physical-damage based, the Grakata is still easily capable of causing all possible procs in a single burst.
- Area-of-effect procs such as Gas and Electricity damage are effective at all levels. Electric can also be used to stun targets.
- The Gas and Electricity combination can be remarkably effective against thick swarms of lower level light Infested.

- Grakata is ideal for rapidly stacking Corrosive effects to quickly strip higher level armored enemies of their thick armor (up to 80%), or for using Radiation for crowd control.
- To significantly decrease time-to-kill for higher level content, Viral elemental procs are recommended, to increase damage to enemies health by a significant amount.
