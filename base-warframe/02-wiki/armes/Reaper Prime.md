Reaper Prime is an ornamental scythe, with a blade forged from tempered Rubedo.

The Reaper Prime is a standalone prime melee Scythe boasting high critical chance, status chance, and attack speed. It was released alongside Frost Prime and Latron Prime.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Reaping Spiral and Shadow Harvest (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest critical chance of all scythes.
- Tied with the Hate for the highest critical multiplier of all scythes.
- Tied with Anku, Ether Reaper, and Tenet Grigori for the fastest attack speed of all scythes.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High damage × attack speed (329.4)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (658.8)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- Above average total damage (600)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (988.2)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- No numerical disadvantages.

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
