Kogake Prime is the Prime variant of the Kogake, sporting higher damage and one of the highest status chance of all melee weapons, at some expense to critical chance and critical multiplier. It was released alongside Mirage Prime and Akbolto Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Brutal Tide and Vicious Approach(Conclave only) stance.
- Innate three polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Grants a 5% Movement Speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (242)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high status chance (38.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (484)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High total damage (726)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (750.95)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Kogake for the second lowest attack speed of all Sparring weapons, after Korrudo.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (16.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Low crit multiplier (1.80x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Kogake Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Kogake (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (242.00 vs. 120.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 169.4 vs. 84)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 36.3 vs. 18)
- Higher Slash damage ( 36.3 vs. 18)

- Higher total damage (242 vs. 120)
- Lower base critical chance (16.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Lower base critical multiplier (1.80x vs. 2.00x)
- Higher base status chance (38.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (10 vs. 2)
