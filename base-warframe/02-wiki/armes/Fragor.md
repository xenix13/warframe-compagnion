A large two-handed hammer, the Fragor requires great strength to wield, but impacts with enough force to send groups of enemies tumbling.

The Fragor is a large hammer with high Impact damage.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crushing Ruin stance.
- Can equip the Brokk skin, which provides +5% attack speed, but -1s combo duration.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (600)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Jat Kittag for the lowest base damage of all hammers.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (199.92)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (399.84)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low follow through (0.4x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (599.76)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

## Notes

- Sometimes enemies killed by Fragor will be severely rag-dolled and fly out of the map.

## Tips

- Using Fury is highly recommended to offset the very slow attack speed.
- The Fragor's large slam radius can be used to knock down several enemies at once allowing all of them to be hit with one finisher attack
- Crouching will allow you to hit knocked down enemies with Fragor's horizontal swings.
- Using a more defensive frame can allow you to absorb damage while its slower attack animations are being completed.
- Using North Wind in conjunction with using Fragor's slam attack gives you a chance of freezing enemies when you knock them down and makes them take longer to stand up.
