The Akbronco Prime is the dual wielding version of the Bronco Prime. Compared to Akbronco, they sport increased damage, status chance, fire rate, slightly longer damage falloff range, and double the magazine size, at the cost of slower reload speed.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Can use the Akbronco Prime-exclusive Damzav-Vati mod.
- Two innate polarities.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Bronco Prime for the third-highest base damage of all shotgun secondaries, behind Twin Rogga and Euphona Prime's alt-fire.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High average number of procs per shot (0.90)
- Very high burst DPS (1606.43)
- Very high sustained DPS (724.33)
- Very high multishot (7)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)
- Very high average number of crits per shot (0.42)
- Very high total damage (350)
- High ammo max (210)
- Very high damage per shot (371)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 26% from 9m to 18m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (6.00%)
- Below average maximum falloff distance (18.0 m)
- Very low accuracy (18.00° to 36.00°)
- Low magazine size (8)
- Low ammo pickup (20)
- Low reload speed (2.25 s)
- Below average status chance (12.86%)

## Notes

- The damage is actually a combination of all the pellets' damage values; the actual damage per pellet is 40 Impact, 5 Puncture and 5 Slash.
- Like many of the older weapons, during a reload the Akbronco Prime's clip will be reloaded before the reload animation is actually finished; if the reload is almost finished, the animation can be interrupted (by player or by enemy) and the weapon will still be reloaded. The point where the clip is reloaded occurs about 3/4's of the way through the animation.

## Tips

- Weapon's pellet spread can be tightened by aiming down sights.
- Quickdraw or Stunning Speed can be used to compensate for the slow reload speed of the Akbronco Prime, in order to maintain a decent DPS.
- Using a maxed Seeker mod is recommended to upgrade its status spreading capability.
