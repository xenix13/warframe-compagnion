When they go high, go low. Powerful pneumatic grips and toecaps amplify every blow. Four ways to deal twice the pain.

The Korrudo is a short-range, Grineer Sparring melee weapon modeled after the Tusk Thumper's pneumatic groundpounder. It inflicts devastating blows thanks to its high damage, critical chance, and critical multiplier, only limited by its slow attack speed and minimal status chance.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Brutal Tide and Vicious Approach (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Grants a 5% movement speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (31.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (31.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (31.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Slowest attack speed of all sparring weapons.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (193)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (9.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (235.52)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
