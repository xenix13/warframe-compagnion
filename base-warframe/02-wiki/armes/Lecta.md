Half taser, half whip. The Corpus Lecta, delivers a deadly shock to anything it touches.

The Lecta is a Corpus-made Electricity whip.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matching the Burning Wasp.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low total damage (101)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (103.52)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low total damage (202)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (207.05)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low total damage (303)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (310.57)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Lecta (Normal Attack), compared to Secura Lecta (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (101.00 vs. 176.00)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 20 vs. 30)
- Lower Slash damage ( 25 vs. 66)
- Lower Electricity damage ( 56 vs. 80)

- Lower total damage (101 vs. 176)
- Lower base critical chance (5.00% vs. 15.00%)
- Lower base status chance (25.00% vs. 30.00%)
- Lower attack speed (1.000x animation speed vs. 1.250x animation speed)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (0 vs. 8)

## Notes

- With maxed rank dual-stat event mods Virulent Scourge, Voltaic Strike, Volcanic Edge and Vicious Frost the Lecta can achieve a status chance of 85%. Adding Melee Prowess increases this to 107.5%.
