The PROVA is a melee weapon that delivers a high-intensity shock to its target.

The Prova is a Corpus machete with Electricity systems to shock its opponents. It is the go-to weapon used by Prod Crewmen, and a backup weapon of all humanoid Corpus units in the event they are disarmed.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals both Impact and Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sundering Weave, Cyclone Kraken and Rending Wind (Conclave only) stance.
- Can use the Prova-exclusive Static Discharge mod.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average status chance (16.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (16.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third lowest base damage of all machetes, after Machete and Kama.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (14.00%)
- Very low total damage (128)
- Low status chance (16.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (145.92)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (14.00%)
- Low total damage (256)
- Low damage × attack speed (291.84)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (14.00%)
- Low total damage (384)
- Low damage × attack speed (437.76)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Prova (Normal Attack), compared to Prova Vandal (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (128.00 vs. 198.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 52 vs. 80)
- Lower Electricity damage ( 76 vs. 118)

- Lower total damage (128 vs. 198)
- Lower base status chance (16.00% vs. 32.00%)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (3 vs. 8)

## Notes

- Unlike the Prova Vandal, the regular Prova does not have a guaranteed Electricity procs on its slam attack.
