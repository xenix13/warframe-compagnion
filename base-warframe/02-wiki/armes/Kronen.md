The Kronen resurrects a lethal fighting style once thought lost to the ages.

The Kronen is a pair of bladed Tonfas. They are the second of the fan-made weapon designs from DE's Community Melee Weapon Contest to be implemented, the first being the Silva & Aegis.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000, it is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Boltace.

- Kronen + Boltor → Boltace

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Gemini Cross and Star Divide (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Telos Boltace for the second fastest attack speed of all tonfas, behind Kronen Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all tonfas.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low total damage (130)
- Low status chance (20.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (147.42)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low total damage (260)
- Low damage × attack speed (294.84)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low total damage (390)
- Low damage × attack speed (442.26)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Kronen (Normal Attack), compared to Kronen Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (130.00 vs. 212.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 13 vs. 21.2)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 13 vs. 21.2)
- Lower Slash damage ( 104 vs. 169.6)

- Lower total damage (130 vs. 212)
- Lower base critical chance (10.00% vs. 22.00%)
- Lower base critical multiplier (1.50x vs. 2.00x)
- Lower base status chance (20.00% vs. 34.00%)
- Lower attack speed (1.080x animation speed vs. 1.170x animation speed)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (3 vs. 13)

## Notes

- The last attack of the Kronen's basic combo can hit enemies behind the player.
- The Kronen counts towards the "Dagger Proficiency" achievements.
