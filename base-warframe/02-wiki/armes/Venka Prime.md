The Venka Prime are the Prime variant of the Venka, sporting higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and attack speed. It also uniquely has a higher Melee Combo Multiplier than all other melee weapons. It was released alongside Valkyr Prime and Cernos Prime.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Vermillion Storm, Four Riders, Malicious Raptor and Scarlet Hurricane (Conclave only) stance.
- Only weapon with a 13.0x melee combo multiplier after 240 hits.
- Innate and polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fourth highest base damage of all claws, behind Keratinos, Garuda Talons and Garuda Prime Talons.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (32.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average attack speed (1.050x animation speed)
- Above average damage × attack speed (298.46)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (32.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (596.93)
- Above average attack speed (1.050x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high crit chance (32.00%)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (895.40)
- Above average attack speed (1.050x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (188)
- Low attack range (1.80 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low attack range (1.80 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low attack range (1.80 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Venka Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Venka (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (188.00 vs. 140.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 9.4 vs. 7)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 37.6 vs. 35)
- Higher Slash damage ( 141 vs. 98)

- Higher total damage (188 vs. 140)
- Higher base critical chance (32.00% vs. 15.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.60x vs. 2.00x)
- Higher base status chance (24.00% vs. 15.00%)
- Longer melee range (1.80 m vs. 1.75 m)
- Higher attack speed (1.050x animation speed vs. 1.000x animation speed)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (14 vs. 4)

## Notes

- The Venka Prime has a modified melee combo multiplier that can reach 13.0x combo at 240 consecutive hits instead of the usual 12.0x combo at 220 hits.

## Tips

- Slash Dash benefits from Venka Prime's enhanced combo multiplier, the rest of the former pseudo-exalted abilities such as Landslide have their own combo counter now.
