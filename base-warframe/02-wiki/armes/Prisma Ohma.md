The Prisma Ohma are the Prisma variant of the Ohma, featuring enhanced damage, critical chance, critical damage, and attack speed, but with reduced status chance.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sovereign Outcast stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- High total damage (250)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High damage × attack speed (340)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- High total damage (500)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (680)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- High total damage (750)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High damage × attack speed (1020)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low status chance (20.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- No numerical disadvantages.

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- No numerical disadvantages.

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Prisma Ohma (Normal Attack), compared to Ohma (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (250.00 vs. 224.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 82 vs. 76)
- Higher Slash damage ( 44 vs. 38)
- Higher Electricity damage ( 124 vs. 110)

- Higher total damage (250 vs. 224)
- Higher base critical chance (30.00% vs. 15.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.20x vs. 2.00x)
- Lower base status chance (20.00% vs. 30.00%)
- Higher attack speed (1.000x animation speed vs. 0.917x animation speed)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (12 vs. 8)

## Notes

- Damage caused by the Prisma Ohma (including damage over time effects from status effects) will cause an arc of lightning to emit from the Prisma Ohma striking the target. This arc has no additional effects and deals no damage.
