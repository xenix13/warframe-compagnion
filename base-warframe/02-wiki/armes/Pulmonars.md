Pummel and infect with these two pulsating hunks of flesh connected by a ligament. Take-hold of the malignant evolution of an ancient martial weapon.

Pulmonars are Infested Nunchaku that deal Viral damage.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Impact and Viral damage.
- Has a 9-second combo duration.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Atlantis Vulcan and Mafic Rain (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high total damage (290)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average attack range (2.51 m)
- High status chance (33.00%)
- High damage × attack speed (331.61)
- High crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High total damage (580)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High damage × attack speed (663.22)
- Above average attack range (2.51 m)
- Very high status chance (50.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high total damage (870)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High damage × attack speed (994.84)
- Above average attack range (2.51 m)
- Very high status chance (50.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.30x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
