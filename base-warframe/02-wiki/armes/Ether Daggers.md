A shorter version of the Ether blade that allows for a more focused attack on a single target.

The Ether Daggers are pair of daggers using the same technology as the Ether Sword.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matches Sinking Talon stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Grants a 5% Movement Speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (224)
- High follow through (0.8x)
- High status chance (30.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (448)
- High follow through (0.8x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (672)
- High follow through (0.8x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Low damage × attack speed (201.51)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Below average damage × attack speed (403.03)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Below average damage × attack speed (604.55)
- Low attack range (1.75 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Low crit multiplier (1.80x)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Stealth attacks do +900% damage instead of the normal bonus of +700% damage.
