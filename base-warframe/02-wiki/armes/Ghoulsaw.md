The cruelest of weapons built for the crudest of Grineer soldiers. Rips through metal like flesh, and through flesh like day-old pudding.

The Ghoulsaw is a Grineer heavy saw melee weapon used by Ghoul Rictus. It is swung around with wide sweeps as the sawblade shreds enemies apart, and can be uniquely ridden on.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance polarity matches Butcher's Revelry.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Perfect Follow Through; does not lose damage when hitting multiple targets.
- Very large Block Angle.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high block angle (90°)
- Very high follow through (1.0x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (90°)
- Very high follow through (1.0x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (90°)
- Very high follow through (1.0x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Low Stance Combo damage per second.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (197)
- Low attack range (2.15 m)
- Below average damage × attack speed (223.59)
- Low crit multiplier (1.90x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (447.19)
- Low attack range (2.15 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low crit multiplier (1.90x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Low attack range (2.15 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low crit multiplier (1.90x)
