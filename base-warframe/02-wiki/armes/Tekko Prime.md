As merciless and indestructible as Atlas Prime himself, Tekko Prime crush and split with tectonic force.

The Tekko Prime are the Prime variant of the Tekko, sporting higher damage, critical multiplier, status chance, and attack speed. It was released alongside Atlas Prime and Dethcube Prime. Additionally, they gain increased status chance while wielded by Atlas.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance polarity has polarity, matches Seismic Palm and Gaia's Tragedy stances.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Highest attack range out of all Fist melees.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average damage × attack speed (766.8)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.40x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (180)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.35 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (360)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.35 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.35 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Tekko Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Tekko (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (180.00 vs. 160.00)
- Higher Impact damage ( 39.6 vs. 32)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 25.2 vs. 16)
- Higher Slash damage ( 115.2 vs. 112)

- Higher total damage (180 vs. 160)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.40x vs. 2.00x)
- Higher base status chance (26.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Longer melee range (1.35 m vs. 1.25 m)
- Higher attack speed (1.000x animation speed vs. 0.917x animation speed)
- More polarities ( vs. )
- Higher Mastery Rank required (12 vs. 6)

## Notes

- As a Signature Weapon, Tekko Prime gains a bonus effect with Atlas or Atlas Prime, gaining an additional 6% base status chance, for a total of 32%.
