Octavia's pistol hammers foes with single shots or a rapid volley of bullets. When wielded by Octavia, alt-fire Headshot Kills increase Reload Speed.

The Pandero is Octavia's signature Pistol, firing semi-automatic shots with sheer power complemented by its high Critical Chance and Multiplier. The Alternate Fire bursts out all remaining rounds in the Magazine as a single full-automatic burst, but disables Aiming, has high recoil, and is wide spread to hinder its range. When wielded by Octavia or Octavia Prime, the Pandero will reload faster after a headshot Alternative Fire kill.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Alternate Fire shoots all remaining bullets in the magazine in a quick burst.
- Benefits from magazine increasing mods to further increase the burst's Damage.
- Prevents Zoom.
- This prevents the buffs from Hydraulic Crosshairs and Sharpened Bullets from affecting this attack as a result.

- Has high Recoil and wide spread, making it inaccurate beyond short range.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- High ammo efficiency.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- High reload speed (1.00 s)
- High ammo max (210)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.80x)

- Alt-Fire (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (30.00%)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Very high reload speed (1.00 s)
- High ammo max (210)
- Above average fire rate (4.17 attacks/sec)
- High crit multiplier (2.80x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Primary fire has noticeable recoil.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low magazine size (8)
- Low accuracy (0.50° to 12.00°)
- Low fire rate (3.00 attacks/sec)
- Low status chance (10.00%)

- Alt-Fire (wiki attack index 2)
- Low magazine size (8)
- Below average total damage (72)
- Low accuracy (6.50° to 18.00°)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Below average damage per shot (110.88)

## Notes

- As a Signature Weapon, Pandero gains a bonus effect when wielded by Octavia or Octavia Prime, where headshot kills with the Alternate Fire increases Reload Speed by 20%.
- No weak spots other than actual heads will trigger this effect.
- Headshots on invulnerable enemies will still grant this effect, but not with invulnerable bosses.

- The Alternate Fire of the Pandero will eject all of the remaining Ammunition in the Magazine in a single burst.
- The base Fire Rate of the Alternative Fire is 10 rounds per second
- Increasing the Magazine Size of the Weapon such as with Slip Magazine and using Recoil reduction Mods like Steady Hands can help to make its Damage output more consistent and reliable.
- If buffed with unlimited Ammo such as from the Reactant Buff, the Weapon will only fire its current magazine at a time, with small delays between each trigger pull.
- Alternative Fire be canceled using Quick Melee.
- Aiming will be disabled for the duration of the burst, and so the Alternate Fire cannot benefit from Acolyte Mods.
