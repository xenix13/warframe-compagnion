Forged by a forgotten master, these exquisite blades are bounty from a golden-age long gone.

The Nami Skyla Prime is the Prime variant to the Nami Skyla, sporting higher damage, critical chance and status chance, and attack speed. It was released alongside Hydroid Prime and Ballistica Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.
- Two innate and one polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Dual Ether for the third highest base damage of all dual swords, behind Twin Basolk and Twin Krohkur.
- Second highest status chance of all dual swords, behind Twin Basolk.
- Fastest attack speed of all dual swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.330x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (34.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (292.06)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average damage × attack speed (584.13)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.330x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High damage × attack speed (907.80)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.330x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.20x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (180)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (360)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Nami Skyla Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Nami Skyla (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (180.00 vs. 125.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 18 vs. 18.75)
- Higher Puncture damage ( 36 vs. 18.75)
- Higher Slash damage ( 126 vs. 87.5)

- Higher total damage (180 vs. 125)
- Higher base critical chance (22.00% vs. 10.00%)
- Higher base critical multiplier (2.00x vs. 1.50x)
- Higher base status chance (34.00% vs. 15.00%)
- Higher attack speed (1.330x animation speed vs. 0.917x animation speed)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (11 vs. 2)
