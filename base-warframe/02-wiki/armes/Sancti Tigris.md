The Sancti Tigris is a Tigris variant available only from New Loka. Featuring increased damage, reload speed, and critical chance at the cost of lowered critical damage and accuracy, the Sancti Tigris also comes with an innate Purity effect.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Can use the Tigris-exclusive Combat Reload mod.
- Innate multishot of 6 pellets.
- Unique trigger type allows for two rapid shots against tough targets, or careful shot management against many weaker opponents. First shot on button press, the second on button release.
- Innate Purity effect.
- Innate , , and polarities.

Advantages over other Primary Shotgun weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third highest damage of all primary shotguns, behind Tigris Prime and Exergis.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high burst DPS (2709)
- High sustained DPS (1083.6)
- Above average average number of crits per shot (0.9)
- Very high total damage (1,260)
- Very high reload speed (1.50 s)
- Above average ammo max (120)
- High damage per shot (1354.5)

Disadvantages over other Primary Shotgun weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 42.86% from 8m to 20m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Extremely low magazine size of just two rounds; requires frequent reloading.
- Low pellet count.
- Inefficient against groups, unless using Punch Through mods.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (15.00%)
- Very low maximum falloff distance (20.0 m)
- Low fire rate (2.00 attacks/sec)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
- Low accuracy (9.00° to 22.00°)
- Very low magazine size (2)
- Very low ammo pickup (15)

## Tips

- The Sancti Tigris has a high chance of cutting corpses into pieces, making it an ideal choice for a Desecrating Nekros to bring along, as the game considers the cut corpses as separate entities, essentially giving multiple corpses to desecrate from a single enemy and thus yielding more chances to create more drops.
- While the Sancti Tigris is less effective against targets vulnerable to Puncture and Impact damage types, it still deals an obscene amount of damage against targets with these resistances, dealing more damage than some weapon's primary damage types.
- The innate polarity matches for Tainted Shell; this mod has a high capacity requirement but is very efficient on the Tigris as the duplex trigger is hardly affected by the fire rate drawback.
- The decrease in fire rate increases the delay before the Tigris automatically reloads after emptying its magazine. This can be remedied by pressing the Reload button right after firing the second shot.
