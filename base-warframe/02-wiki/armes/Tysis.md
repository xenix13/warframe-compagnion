The Tysis pistol spits out caustic darts that corrode their victims from within.

The Tysis is an Infested sidearm that fires Corrosive darts that deals damage over time supported by its incredibly high status chance that allows it to wear down even the heaviest of armor with ease, but has low critical stats.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Corrosive damage.
- On kill, bodies will follow the dart that killed them, damaging anyone in their path and pinning the corpse to walls.

## Notes

- The 81 Corrosive damage is dealt in 3 ticks of 27 damage, over the course of 2 seconds. This allows a single dart to proc multiple times.
- Each multishot creates DoT individually.
- If the dart punches through an enemy, they will instead receive 1 tick.

- Tysis needles retain their course after piercing their first target, possibly penetrating multiple targets without losing accuracy.
- Tysis needles will be invisible if the energy color is Black, making target leading harder as you can't estimate travel speed and deviation effectively.
- The in-game arsenal is incorrect, as it indicates a single attack containing 9 Impact, 23 Puncture, 17 Slash, and 27 Corrosive damage with an unspecified DoT effect and a second attack containing 40 Impact. In truth, the second attack doesn't exist, the primary projectile attack only uses the specified physical damage types and values, and the DoT effect only uses the stated Corrosive damage. As such, the base damage listed in-game (76) is incorrect as it is split between the two attacks (49 and 27, respectively).

## Tips

- A status chance of 260% may be obtained without any riven mods by combining Stunning Speed, Sure Shot, Jugulus Spines or Carnis Stinger, Jolt, Pistol Pestilence, Scorch and Frostbite (for a maximum of 305% with Embedded Catalyzer).
- With its high status chance, the Tysis may be used as a softening weapon against Grineer. Corrosive effects stack, allowing another weapon to easily deliver the killing blow.
- The Tysis' main advantage over other status primer secondaries (eg. Kompressa, Epitaph) is its pinpoint accuracy; this is especially relevant when using Xata's Whisper, as Void status effects inflicted upon the body make it impossible to land headshots with your main damage-dealing weapon.
- When using Cascadia Empowered, only the initial hit can trigger the bonus damage on status effect.
