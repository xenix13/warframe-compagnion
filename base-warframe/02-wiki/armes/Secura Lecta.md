The Secura Lecta is the Syndicate variant of the Lecta, exclusive to The Perrin Sequence. Enemies killed by this weapon will drop additional credits, scaling with the user's Mastery Rank.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Enemies killed by this weapon yield additional credits.
- Innate +50% Attack Speed for Finishers.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matching Burning Wasp.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Galvacord for the highest status chance of all whips.
- Tied with Scoliac for the fastest attack speed of all whips.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- High status chance (30.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (176)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (236.5)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (352)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (528)
- Very low block angle (45°)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

## Notes

- Bonus credits are gained from direct kills with the Secura Lecta equipped, or from Damage over Time effects caused by the Secura Lecta even if holstered.
- The Credit Boost function for the Secura Lecta scales with Mastery Rank. At Mastery Rank 8, it gives an average 2x Credits from kills. This bonus increase with player Mastery Rank. At Mastery Rank 18 you'll get on average 4x Credits from kills.(capped)
- Bonus credits stacks with any active Credit Boosters for increased payout.
- Can be combined with Hydroid's Pilfering Swarm Augment to further increase earnings ( Nekros' Desecrate does not add to further increase earnings)
- Nova's Molecular Prime does not have any negative impact on the credit boost, even if enemies get killed by Molecular Prime's explosion.
- While the Secura Lecta will grant credits on kills made in Duviri and The Undercroft, they will not be given to the player on mission completion.
