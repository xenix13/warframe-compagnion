Dual Lex pistols double the amount of high caliber lead you can throw at the enemy, but accuracy and reload time suffer.

The Aklex are a pair of Lex pistols. Compared to a single Lex, the Aklex have a higher rate of fire and magazine size. However, they have no polarity slot, lower accuracy, and a slower reload speed.

This weapon can be sold for ‍5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Very good ammunition efficiency.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (20.00%)
- Above average total damage (130)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- High ammo max (210)
- Above average damage per shot (156)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low burst DPS (246.48)
- Low sustained DPS (176.68)
- Below average magazine size (12)
- Very low accuracy (0.50° to 20.00°)
- Very low reload speed (3.00 s)
- Very low fire rate (1.58 attacks/sec)
- Low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Aklex (Normal Attack), compared to Lex (Normal Attack):
- Equal base damage
- Higher burst DPS (246.48 vs. 168.48)
- Higher sustained DPS (176.68 vs. 118.39)
- Higher fire rate (1.58 attacks/sec vs. 1.08 attacks/sec)
- Larger magazine (12 vs. 6)
- Slower reload time (3.00 s vs. 2.35 s)
- Less accurate (9.8 vs. 16)
- Wider maximum spread (20.00° vs. 12.00°)
- Higher Mastery Rank required (4 vs. 3)

- Aklex (Normal Attack), compared to Aklex Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage per projectile (130.00 vs. 150.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 13 vs. 15)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 104 vs. 120)
- Lower Slash damage ( 13 vs. 15)

- Lower total damage (130 vs. 150)
- Lower base critical chance (20.00% vs. 25.00%)
- Lower base status chance (10.00% vs. 25.00%)
- Lower average damage per tap (156 vs. 187.5)
- Lower burst DPS (246.48 vs. 500.62)
- Lower sustained DPS (176.68 vs. 333.61)
- Lower fire rate (1.58 attacks/sec vs. 2.67 attacks/sec)
- Smaller magazine (12 vs. 16)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (4 vs. 15)

## Notes

- Despite the description of the Aklex saying that its reload time suffers, its actual reload time is quite efficient, spanning a mere 1.27 times its original reload duration, as most akimbo pistols have twice the reload time.
- Despite the accuracy mentioned in its statistics is poor, its actual accuracy is quite impressive, even when against long-ranged targets, leaving only the recoil to be held responsible for this.

## Tips

- A Stunning Speed mod synergizes well with this weapon, reducing its long reload and increasing its high status chance further (although not by much).
- Steady Hands makes the Aklex's high recoil much easier to control and allow for more accurate shots on the target in a shorter span of time without having to readjust after shots as often.
