Razor sharp battle-hatchets, designed to compliment the Soma

The Dual Raza are a pair of hatchets styled after the Soma assault rifle, notable for its high critical multiplier.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third lowest base damage of all dual swords, after Dual Kamas and Dual Zoren.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (110)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (151.30)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low total damage (220)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low damage × attack speed (302.61)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low total damage (330)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low damage × attack speed (453.91)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
