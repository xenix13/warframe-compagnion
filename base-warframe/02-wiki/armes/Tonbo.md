During the darkest days of the Old War many a Tenno relied solely on the Tonbo to keep them alive.

The Tonbo is a Tenno polearm that specializes in high status chance which combines with its high Slash damage to consistently bleed enemies.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Slam Attacks have a chance to proc Blast.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Shimmering Blight and Argent Scourge (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all polearms.
- Lowest critical chance of all polearms.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Below average total damage (184)
- Low damage × attack speed (193.2)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (386.4)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (579.6)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Even though the weapon requires Oxium to construct, the Tonbo is quite inexpensive for a weapon exclusive to Clan Research, as most Clan Research-exclusive weapons typically require Forma or other rare resources such as Argon Crystals.
- The ground slam has a chance to inflict a Blast proc.
