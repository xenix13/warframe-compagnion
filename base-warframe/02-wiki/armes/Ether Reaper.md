With a blade forged from the same material as the Ether Sword, the Ether Reaper delivers quick death to its victims.

The Ether Reaper is a scythe weapon built from the same materials as the Ether Sword.

It can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- stance polarity matches Stalking Fan.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Anku, Reaper Prime, and Tenet Grigori for the fastest attack speed of all scythes.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.70 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all scythes, after Anku.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (180)
- Low status chance (20.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (233.28)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (360)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- Though implied by the description, this weapon lacks the Ether Sword's guaranteed radiation proc on slam attacks.
