CODEX
A gift from the Lotus to commemorate the third anniversary of the first Tenno waking from Cryo stasis.

The Dex Sybaris is the Dex variant of Sybaris burst-fire rifle, sporting higher critical chance, fire rate, magazine size, and faster reload, but with a slight reduction to damage. It was added in celebration of WARFRAME's 3 Anniversary.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.

Advantages over other Primary Burst Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high crit chance (35.00%)
- Above average burst DPS (337.16)
- High sustained DPS (513.58)
- High total damage (75)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Above average accuracy (2.00° to 5.00°)
- Very high reload speed (1.50 s)
- High ammo max (540)
- Above average damage per shot (202.5)

- Incarnon Form (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high crit chance (30.00%)
- Above average burst DPS (511.48)
- Above average sustained DPS (407.27)
- Above average total damage (96)
- Above average accuracy (2.00° to 5.00°)
- Very high reload speed (1.50 s)
- Above average damage per shot (614.4)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Primary Burst Rifle weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low magazine size (14)
- Very low fire rate (3.33 attacks/sec)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Incarnon Form (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average magazine size (14)
- Very low fire rate (3.33 attacks/sec)
- Very low status chance (20.00%)

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Sybaris Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Sybaris with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

Installing the Sybaris Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 150 Saggen Pearl, and 70 Ueymag. When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is activated by filling the Incarnon Transmutation gauge that appears under the reticle by achieving weakpoint hits and then pressing the Alternate Fire button. 25 weakpoints (or 13 bursts) will completely fill the gauge. A full gauge is not required to activate Incarnon Form.
- Charges do generate when hitting weak points highlighted by Sonar or Detect Vulnerability.
- However, they do not generate from hits on Crystallize's crystalline growths.

- Weakspots on corpses or Invulnerable targets do not build charges.
- Individual Multishot bullets can build charges.

- Mode switching is not instantaneous and has an animation equal to the weapon's reload speed.
- Swapping to and from Incarnon Form will fully reload the normal form's magazine.

- Instead of drawing ammunition from its reserves, the Sybaris's Incarnon Form uses a separate "magazine" with each charge producing 8 rounds to a maximum of 200. Once all ammunition is expended, the Sybaris reverts to its normal form.
- Incarnon Form's magazine is not affected by mods.
- Incarnon Form is not affected by Ammo Efficiency (such as Energized Munitions).
- Charges and Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, charges and Incarnon Form are removed upon death.
