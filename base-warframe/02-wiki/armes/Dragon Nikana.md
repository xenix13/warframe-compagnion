The Dragon Nikana is forged from ancient Tenno steel. This is a master's weapon, only the most worthy of Tenno may wield it.

The Dragon Nikana is a powerful katana of Tenno design, acting as a direct upgrade of the Nikana.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Decisive Judgement, Tranquil Cleave, Blind Justice and Fateful Truth (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate and polarities.
- Lifesteal is applied to attacks when a Daikyu modded with Amalgam Daikyu Target Acquired is equipped.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all nikanas, behind Nikana Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average total damage (188)
- Below average status chance (22.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (229.36)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

## Notes

- Despite what its name suggests, Dragon Nikana is not a variant of the Nikana, so its considered its own weapon in regards to Riven Mods. In other words, Nikana Riven Mods cannot be equipped on the Dragon Nikana and vice versa.
- The default strikes of the Nikanas lash out quickly, but have noticeably longer delay before the next swing. This should be noted if you are particular about hit timing.
- Before Hotfix 13.0.1 (2014-04-10), the Dragon Nikana appeared alongside the normal Nikana in the Market with the same name as the regular version, causing some confusion due to its superior stats despite looking like an identical weapon. This has been changed to reflect the Dragon Nikana being a separate weapon.
- The Dragon Nikana was previously unlockable via Clan research, requiring the Nikana to the researched. Hotfix 13.0.3 (2014-04-10) removed the Dragon Nikana from the Tenno Lab, and a conversion blueprint was made available in the Market to allow Nikana users to upgrade to the Dragon Nikana.
