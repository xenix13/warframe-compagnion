CODEX
Butcher your foes with brutal blades in each hand.

The Dual Cleavers are Grineer blades, with singles used by Butchers.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000. It is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Ripkas.

- Ankyros + Dual Cleavers → Ripkas

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Can use the Dual Cleavers-exclusive Justice Blades mod.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Dual Skana and Dex Dakra for the slowest attack speed of all dual swords.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low total damage (157)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (196.17)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average total damage (314)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (392.34)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average total damage (471)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (588.51)
- Very low attack range (1.70 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low attack speed (0.833x animation speed)
