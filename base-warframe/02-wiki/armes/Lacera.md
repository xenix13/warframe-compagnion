In the practiced hands of a master, this blade and whip becomes exceedingly devastating.

The Lacera is a Tenno Blade and Whip possessing extreme status chance.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Electricity damage.
- Slam Radial Damage deals Electricity damage.
- Innate polarity.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Defiled Snapdragon and Tainted Hydra (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (45.00%)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (432)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (45.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (648)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high status chance (45.00%)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Below average damage × attack speed (207.97)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (415.95)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (5.00%)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (623.92)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Lacera (Normal Attack), compared to Ceti Lacera (Normal Attack):
- Equal base damage
- Lower base critical chance (5.00% vs. 20.00%)
- Higher base status chance (45.00% vs. 40.00%)
- Lower attack speed (0.917x animation speed vs. 1.080x animation speed)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (7 vs. 12)

## Notes

- The Lacera is one of the Clan Tech weapons that does not require a Forma to build, nor does it require any Clan Research materials.
- ​When combining elements the innate Electricity damage is added last, unless another mod adds this type.
- The fourth attack of the stanceless/Claws of the Drake combo as well as the Heavy Attack use the whip to ragdoll and pull enemies towards the player.
- The Slam Radial Damage has a good chance to inflict the Electricity status effect, but is unaffected by status chance mods.
- Its original color scheme is similar to Loki's Immortal skin.
