Orokin-era Grineer plated their scythes with gold as a symbol of dedication to their Golden Lords. Gains a percentage of Critical Chance with each Status Type affecting the target.

Galariak Prime is a standalone Prime Scythe used by ancient Orokin Era Grineer soldiers. Its Critical Chance increases for each individual Status Effect type on the target.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Gains a flat +10% Critical Chance bonus per unique Status Effect type on the target hit.
- Valid status effects are Impact, Puncture, Slash, Heat, Cold, Electricity, Toxin, Radiation, Gas, Magnetic, Viral, Corrosive, Void, and Tau.

- Stance slot has polarity, matching Stalking Fan stance.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (234)
- High block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack speed (1.083x animation speed)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High status chance (32.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (325.64)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high total damage (702)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high damage × attack speed (976.94)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.083x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high total damage (1,170)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Very high damage × attack speed (1628.23)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- High attack speed (1.083x animation speed)
- High crit multiplier (2.50x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes
