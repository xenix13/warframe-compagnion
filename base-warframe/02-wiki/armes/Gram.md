The heavy two-handed sword known as Gram requires great strength to wield, but is notorious for its splitting power. Can hit multiple targets with each strike.

The Gram is a Tenno heavy blade, which boasts good damage and balanced critical and status performances, considered the baseline of heavy blade weapons.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Rending Crane and Noble Cadence (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.80 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.80 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.80 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all heavy blades.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Low total damage (160)
- Below average attack speed (0.950x animation speed)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (174.8)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (320)
- Low damage × attack speed (349.6)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Below average attack speed (0.950x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (15.00%)
- Below average total damage (480)
- Low damage × attack speed (524.4)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Below average attack speed (0.950x animation speed)
