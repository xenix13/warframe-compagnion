Inflict maximum devastation with this massive Grineer trident.

The Sydon is a Grineer trident wielded by the Shock Draga. Its high Puncture damage makes it effective against heavily-shielded targets, which combined with its high status chance allows its wielders to make short work of Corpus threats.

Can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage.
- Slam Attacks have innate Blast damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Shimmering Blight and Argent Scourge (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Third highest Puncture damage of all melee weapons – effective against armor.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (225)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.90 m)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (450)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.90 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (675)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- High attack range (2.90 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Below average damage × attack speed (226.95)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (10.00%)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Higher base damage (225.00 vs. 213.00)

- Lower base critical chance (10.00% vs. 21.00%)
