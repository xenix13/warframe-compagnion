Gunsen Prime is the Primed counterpart of the Gunsen warfan, acting as a direct upgrade. Gunsen Prime was released alongside Wisp Prime and Fulmin Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Slicing Feathers and Votive Onslaught stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average total damage (225)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- High status chance (34.00%)
- Above average damage × attack speed (315.9)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average total damage (450)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- High damage × attack speed (631.8)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average total damage (675)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- High damage × attack speed (947.7)
- Very high status chance (34.00%)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low attack range (1.90 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low attack range (1.90 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Below average crit chance (20.00%)
- Low attack range (1.90 m)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Gunsen Prime (Normal Attack), compared to Gunsen (Normal Attack):
- Higher base damage (225.00 vs. 160.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 9 vs. 12.8)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 13.5 vs. 19.2)
- Higher Slash damage ( 202.5 vs. 128)

- Higher total damage (225 vs. 160)
- Higher base critical chance (20.00% vs. 16.00%)
- Higher base status chance (34.00% vs. 28.00%)
- Longer melee range (1.90 m vs. 1.70 m)
- More polarities ( vs. )
- Higher Mastery Rank required (12 vs. 8)

## Notes

- Warfan slams hit twice.
- The second hit of a heavy slam will use the stats of a normal slam.
