Fires seven beams of continuous biochemical energy which depletes the life from any surface they contact.

The Phage is an Infested shotgun that fires seven Viral beams in a wide spread. Aiming focuses these beams together, allowing the Phage to perform well against groups or individual targets as needed.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Viral damage.
- Innate multishot of 7 beams.
- Firing from hip shoots beams in a spread, while firing while aiming focuses the beams on the crosshairs over a span of 0.6s.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Primary Shotgun weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Very high overall status chance.
- Innate Viral damage allows for Viral procs, amplifying damage dealt to health.

- Tied with Convectrix and Phantasma for the highest fire rate of all shotguns.
- Good ammo efficiency.
- Consumes 0.5 ammo per tick of damage.
- No damage falloff.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High average number of procs per shot (1.08)
- High average number of crits per shot (1.33)
- High accuracy (2.00° to 2.00°)
- Very high magazine size (90)
- Above average reload speed (2.00 s)
- Above average multishot (7)
- Very high ammo pickup (80)
- Very high ammo max (720)
- Above average crit chance (19.00%)
- Very high fire rate (12.00 attacks/sec)

Disadvantages over other Primary Shotgun weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second lowest base damage of all shotguns, after Convectrix.
- Damage ramps up from 70% to 100% over 0.6 seconds when firing. After firing stops for 0.8 seconds, the damage decays back to 70% over 2 seconds.
- Wide initial spread.
- Beam focus time cannot be modified by any means.
- Loses focus if firing is interrupted or released.
- Limited range of 25 meters.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low burst DPS (499.8)
- Low sustained DPS (441)
- Very low total damage (35)
- Very low damage per shot (41.65)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

## Notes

- The time required for the beams to fully focus is approximately 0.5 seconds.
- Sprinting, falling, or otherwise interrupting your movement will result in the Phage's focus being reset to its maximum spread.
- Casting Nova's Antimatter Drop will not reset Phage's focus. In theory, this makes Nova and Phage a strong pairing as Nova can cast Antimatter Drop and immediately deal damage to the orb.

- Vicious Spread and Tainted Shell will only affect the initial spread of the beams. The time taken for the beams to converge, as well as the spread of the beams once focused, will remain the same. This makes mods with spread components ineffective against the accuracy of the weapon when the beams are focused.
- Multiple beams hitting the target at the same time will combine into a single damage tick, similar to the Phantasma.
- Like other Continuous Weapons, combined beams will also combine the chance for status procs per ammo used, resulting in 108.5% combined status chance before any mods.
- Multishot mods, such as Hell's Chamber, appear to give slightly less of an effect than they should. Instead of scaling off of an innate value of 7.0, they scale off of a value of 6.0. This means that Hell's Chamber will increase the value from 7.0 to 14.2. This behavior is shared with the Phantasma.

## Tips

- The beams' focus can be paused at intermediate stages by releasing aim. This can be used to dispatch different groupings of enemies more efficiently.
- Due to its spread being controllable and damage being continuous, this weapon is quite effective at destroying Sabotage reactor cores.

- Using Seeking Force or Seeking Fury will allow the beams to go through more objects.
- It is possible to get 3 different elemental combinations on the same build - any two elements plus the base Viral damage.
- Modding for Corrosive and Heat with the innate Viral makes Phage uniquely effective versus Grineer, as most weapons are unable to use both Corrosive and Viral together.
- Primary Debilitate works very well with the Phage and Corrosive riven since both the electricity, and poison stack can stack infinitely (it also you to save a mod slot for optionally modding blast to take down group of ennemies)
- Grendel Gastroparesis works very well due to the nature of the Gas augment being another instance of hit instead of increasing weapon damage. Since Gas is made from two status who can stack infinitely and proc at every single individual hit of the Phage (7), you can make the Phage scale to even lvl cap ennemies quite easily.

- Sinister Reach can be equipped on the Phage, increasing its range from 25m to 37m, making it capable of hitting enemies at a much longer range.
- Violet Archon Shard can be used to buff the weapon overall DMG and Electricity chance, a Emerald Archon Shard can also be used to allow to the Phage to fully armor strip enemies whenever Corrosive and Corrosive Projection is used.
