The uniquely styled Viper Wraith is a powerful variation on the standard machine-pistols.

The Viper Wraith is the Wraith version of the Viper that has higher damage with a slight boost to Impact damage in exchange for lower Slash, and higher critical chance, larger magazine size, and better accuracy, but with reduced status chance and reload speed.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Can use the Viper-exclusive Skull Shots and Stinging Truth mods.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- High accuracy.
- Tied with Viper for highest fire rate of all single-handed pistols.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average magazine size (20)
- Very high ammo pickup (40)
- Above average accuracy (1.00° to 6.00°)
- Very high reload speed (0.80 s)
- Very high ammo max (420)
- Very high fire rate (14.38 attacks/sec)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Inefficient ammo economy.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Below average burst DPS (308.01)
- Below average sustained DPS (195.54)
- Very low total damage (18)
- Low status chance (9.00%)
- Very low damage per shot (21.42)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.
