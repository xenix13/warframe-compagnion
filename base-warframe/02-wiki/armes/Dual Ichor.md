The fast and brutal Dual Ichor axes will quickly tear through enemies with a high probability of inflicting critical damage.

Dual Ichor is a pair of twin Infested short axes resembling a bony or chitinous material, wielded in each hand, inflicting Toxin damage.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Toxin damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matching Swirling Tiger stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (122)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Very low status chance (15.00%)
- Low damage × attack speed (197.64)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low total damage (244)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (395.28)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Dual Ichor with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 60 Rune Marrow and 80 Kovnik.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
