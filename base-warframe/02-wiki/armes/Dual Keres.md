Tear through any target with these vicious, claw-like blades. The signature swords of Khora.

The Dual Keres are Khora's signature Dual Swords sporting high Slash, critical chance, and critical multiplier.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Has a 20% Heavy Attack Efficiency when wielded by Khora/ Khora Prime.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Carving Mantis, Crossing Snakes and Dividing Blades (Conclave only) stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second fastest attack speed of all dual swords, behind Nami Skyla Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- High crit chance (28.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)
- Very high attack speed (1.250x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (115)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low status chance (14.00%)
- Below average damage × attack speed (208.15)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low total damage (230)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (416.3)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low total damage (345)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Below average damage × attack speed (624.45)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

See WARFRAME Wiki:Stat Comparison/Percentiles for more details.

Comparisons:

- Dual Keres (Normal Attack), compared to Dual Keres Prime (Normal Attack):
- Lower base damage (115.00 vs. 180.00)
- Lower Impact damage ( 13.8 vs. 21.6)
- Lower Puncture damage ( 34.5 vs. 54)
- Lower Slash damage ( 66.7 vs. 104.4)

- Lower total damage (115 vs. 180)
- Lower base critical chance (28.00% vs. 32.00%)
- Lower base status chance (14.00% vs. 24.00%)
- Lower Mastery Rank required (7 vs. 14)
