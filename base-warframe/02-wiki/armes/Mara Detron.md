For Orokin-era smugglers, this fearsome handheld shotgun was a favored tool for 'dispute resolution'.

The Mara Detron is a Void Trader-exclusive variant of the Detron that has altered stats along with a unique animated skin.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Radiation damage.
- Can use the Detron-exclusive Thermagnetic Shells mod.

Advantages over other Secondary Shotgun Sidearm weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Very high overall status chance.
- Good accuracy.
- Innate multishot of 7 pellets.
- Fast Projectile Speed.
- Very ammo efficient.
- Medium spread.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- High average number of procs per shot (0.95)
- High maximum falloff distance (30.0 m)
- High active falloff slope (22.4m/%)
- High accuracy (6.00° to 9.00°)
- Above average magazine size (8)
- Above average total damage (280)
- Very high reload speed (1.05 s)
- Very high status chance (13.71%)

Disadvantages over other Secondary Shotgun Sidearm weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Has linear damage falloff from 100% to 37.5% from 16m to 30m target distance (distances are affected by Projectile Speed).
- Projectiles have travel time.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (8.00%)
- Low burst DPS (969.69)
- Low fire rate (3.33 attacks/sec)
- Below average multishot (7)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)
- Low damage per shot (291.2)

## Tips

- The Mara Detron's increased fire rate means that mods like Lethal Torrent make it fire faster than can be clicked manually. Turning on the fire manual trigger weapons continuously setting can alleviate this.
- The Mara Detron is a secondary shotgun so Multishot mods Lethal Torrent and Barrel Diffusion will increase the pellet count.
- As the Mara Detron is essentially an improved Detron, refer to the Detron's Tips for more upgrade/optimization possibilities.
