The Obex system multiplies the kinetic energy of a Tenno's kicks and punches, delivering deadly blows to any target.

The Obex is a set of Corpus-built hand and foot guards designed for close combat.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Slam attack deals Electricity damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Brutal Tide and Vicious Approach (Conclave only) stance.
- Can use the Obex-exclusive Entropy Detonation mod.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Prisma Obex for the third highest critical chance of all sparring weapons, behind Hirudo and Korrudo.
- Grants a 5% movement speed bonus when held (i.e. melee mode or quick melee).
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Above average attack speed (1.000x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Tied with Kogake for the lowest base damage of all sparring weapons.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (120)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (150)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low total damage (240)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low damage × attack speed (300)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low total damage (360)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Low damage × attack speed (450)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

## Notes

- Slide attack does not involve spinning, but rather causes the Warframe to perform a handspring, finishing with a kick that sends enemies flying, ragdolling them.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Obex Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Obex with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Obex requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 100 Nacreous Pebble, and 80 Lamentus.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below;
- Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack, a Slam Attack or a Heavy Slam Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 90 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
- Despite the in-game description only listing Heavy Attacks (and consequently Heavy Slams) as able to activate Incarnon Form, non-Heavy Slams may be used as well and may be preferred as they do not reduce your Melee Combo. This behavior is shared by all melee Incarnon weapons released since Thalys, which also includes Destreza.
