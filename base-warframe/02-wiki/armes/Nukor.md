The Nukor is a Grineer sidearm that fires a microwave-like frequency beam that inflicts Radiation damage, boasting an extreme critical multiplier is heavily offset by its almost nonexistent critical chance. It also has a unique status effect called Microwave that enlarges enemy body parts.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals Radiation damage.
- Can inflict a unique status effect called Microwave, enlarging the body part that was shot at.
- Damage ramps up from 30% to 100% over 0.6 seconds when firing. After firing stops for 0.8 seconds, the damage decays back to 30% over 2 seconds.

Advantages over other Secondary Held Pistol weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest critical multiplier of all weapons in the game, behind Kuva Nukor.
- Pinpoint accuracy.
- Magnum Force's accuracy penalty is minimal on this weapon.

- Consumes 0.5 ammo per tick of damage.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high accuracy (0.00° to 0.00°)
- Very high crit multiplier (4.00x)

Disadvantages over other Secondary Held Pistol weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Limited range of 25 meters.
- Pulsing weapon effect blocks the reticle, making it harder to make consistent headshots with the weapon shooting at moving targets.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (3.00%)
- Low burst DPS (239.8)
- Low sustained DPS (199.83)
- Below average magazine size (50)
- Below average ammo pickup (20)
- Very low reload speed (2.00 s)
- Below average ammo max (210)
- Low fire rate (10.00 attacks/sec)
- Low damage per shot (23.98)

## Notes

- Nukor can proc an invisible Status Effect called Microwave that causes the part of the enemy hit to become larger.
- Microwave procs have infinite duration, but the visual effect does not.
- Microwave counts as an individual status for Condition Overload, Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Savvy, and Galvanized Shot.
- Some enemies are not affected by this Status Effect, at least visually, for example Liches

## Tips

- There are several ways to make use of the Nukor's high critical damage by adding flat critical chance bonuses:
- An Adarza Kavat with Cat's Eye can give your weapons an additive 60% boost to critical chance.
- Likewise, Smeeta Kavat's Charm can guarantee orange crits as one of its possible effects.
- Using the Secondary Fire of the Mutalist Quanta and afterwards firing the Nukor through the orb adds a flat +25% critical chance, in addition to +25% critical damage and extra Electricity.
- Harrow's Covenant can add up to +50% additive crit chance, or +200% on headshots, after absorbing enough damage.
- A fully ranked Arcane Avenger has a 21% chance upon receiving damage to grant +45% flat critical chance.
- Secondary Enervate can gradually increase the Nukor's critical chance until a set number of tier 2/orange/big critical hits.

- With a maxed Primed Target Cracker and Hollow Point, the Nukor can achieve a critical damage multiplier of 10.8x, the highest critical damage multiplier value achievable outside of red crits.
- It is however ill-advised to use Hollow Point if attempting a critical damage build due to the Nukor's extremely low critical chance.

- The Nukor's short range, high accuracy and cone of fire means that Magnum Force can be used to provide additional damage without severe penalty.
- The Nukor can be made into an effective anti-Sentient weapon by adding Cold damage which, in combination with its innate Radiation damage, allows the Nukor to deal extra damage against Sentients while freezing them with Cold status effects.
