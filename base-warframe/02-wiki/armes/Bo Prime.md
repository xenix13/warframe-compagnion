A classic Orokin weapon, Bo Prime creates elegant fury on the battlefield.

The Bo Prime is the Prime variant of the Bo staff, featuring higher damage, status chance, and attack speed. It was released alongside Loki Prime and Wyrm Prime.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Flailing Branch stance.
- Innate and polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second highest base damage of all staves, behind Broken Scepter.
- Tied with Tipedo Prime for the highest critical chance of all staves.
- Highest critical multiplier and status chance of all staves.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- High status chance (32.00%)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average damage × attack speed (526.14)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (24.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Above average damage × attack speed (789.21)
- Very high attack range (3.00 m)
- High attack speed (1.080x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (2.60x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

## Incarnon Genesis

- Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn Bo Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Bo with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
- Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.

- Installing the Bo Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 80 Kovnik.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
