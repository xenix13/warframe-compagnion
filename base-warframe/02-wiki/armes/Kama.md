The Kama is a Tenno style hatchet, capable of making short work of any foe.

The Kama is a Tenno sickle.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000; it is also a weapon crafting ingredient for the Dual Kamas (x2).

- Kama + Kama → Dual Kamas
Dual Kamas required for Dual Raza

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matching Sundering Weave, Cyclone Kraken and Rending Wind (Conclave only) stance.
- Innate two polarities.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Fastest attack speed of all machetes.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- High follow through (0.7x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all machetes.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Low crit chance (12.00%)
- Very low total damage (90)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (115.40)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Low crit chance (12.00%)
- Very low total damage (180)
- Very low damage × attack speed (230.81)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.80x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Low crit chance (12.00%)
- Very low total damage (270)
- Very low damage × attack speed (346.22)
- Below average attack range (2.40 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low crit multiplier (1.80x)
