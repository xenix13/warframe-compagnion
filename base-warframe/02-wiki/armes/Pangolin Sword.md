A unique blade used by an old Tenno clan.

The Pangolin Sword is an ancient Tenno sword with similar aesthetics to the Fang.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000.

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Slam Radial Damage deals Puncture damage.
- Slam Radial Damage has a guaranteed Slash proc in addition to the Impact proc.

- Stance slot has polarity, matches Crimson Dervish, Vengeful Revenant and Swooping Falcon.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average block angle (55°)
- Above average follow through (0.6x)
- Above average attack range (2.50 m)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Low total damage (150)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Below average status chance (22.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (143.05)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Low total damage (300)

- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Low attack speed (0.917x animation speed)
- Very low crit multiplier (1.50x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very low crit chance (8.00%)
- Low total damage (450)

## Notes

- Guaranteed Slash proc on slam radial damage.
- Slam radial damage is only affected by Seismic Wave.
- Does not affect heavy slam attacks.
- The slam radial damage has a 22% chance to inflict the Puncture status effect in addition to the Slash and Impact procs, but is unaffected by status chance mods.
