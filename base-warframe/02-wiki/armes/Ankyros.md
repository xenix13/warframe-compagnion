A pair of mighty gauntlets, the Ankyros were designed by the Tenno for close quarters combat. What they lack in range they make up for in speed.

The Ankyros are a pair of gauntlets that can deliver blows quickly.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000. It is also a weapon crafting ingredient for the Ripkas and the Tekko.

- Ankyros → Tekko
- Ankyros + Dual Cleavers → Ripkas

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Stance slot has a polarity, matches Seismic Palm and Gaia's Tragedy stance.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Second fastest attack speed of all fists, behind Ankyros Prime.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Very high follow through (0.9x)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (90)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (126.36)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low total damage (180)
- Very low block angle (50°)
- Very low damage × attack speed (252.72)
- Very low attack range (1.25 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)
