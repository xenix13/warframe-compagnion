CODEX
Short axes held in each hand. High attack speed and can hit multiple targets. Delivers a high percentage of critical strikes.

The Dual Zoren is a pair of short, stylized axes wielded in each hand. Each hatchet is styled after the Scindo.

This weapon can be sold for 5,000 Credits 5,000; it is also a weapon crafting ingredient for Twin Basolk.

- Dual Zoren + Atomos → Twin Basolk

## Characteristics

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Stance slot has polarity, matches Swirling Tiger stance.
- Innate polarity.

Advantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- High block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)
- Above average crit chance (25.00%)
- Very high block angle (60°)
- Very high attack speed (1.170x animation speed)
- Very high crit multiplier (3.00x)

Disadvantages over other Melee weapons (excluding modular weapons):

- Lowest base damage of all melee weapons.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)
- Very low total damage (70)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Very low status chance (5.00%)
- Very low damage × attack speed (122.85)

- Slam Attack (wiki attack index 2)
- Very low total damage (140)
- Very low follow through (0.5x)
- Very low damage × attack speed (245.7)
- Low attack range (2.30 m)
- Very low status chance (10.00%)

- Heavy Slam Attack (wiki attack index 3)

- Low attack range (2.30 m)
