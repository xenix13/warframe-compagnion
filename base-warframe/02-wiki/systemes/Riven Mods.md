Cephalon Samodeus' grand creations. Each mod is malleable and unique, inspired by and meant for a single weapon. Complete a Riven Mod's challenge to unveil its possibilities, both rapturous and unfavorable.—In-game description

Riven Mods are special mods for Primary Weapons, Secondary Weapons, Melee Weapons, Archguns, and Robotic Weapons. Riven Mods are completely unique; no two are the same, even if they have the same attributes, as their numbers will differ.

Riven Mods are unique compared to other Mods in a variety of ways:

- Rivens can carry two or three different bonuses, with a possibility for a negative bonus but increases the potency of bonuses.
- Rivens can either be Madurai, Naramon, or Vazarin polarities, regardless of its attributes.
- Rivens have their own Mastery Rank requirement to use, ranging from 8 to 16.
- Rivens can only be used by a single weapon (and its variants).
- Riven bonuses and negatives can be rerolled with the use of Kuva.
- Upgrade costs and Trading Credit taxes of Riven mods are equivalent to those of Rare mods.

## States

Upon receiving a Riven mod, it is listed as a generic Riven Mod for a weapon type such as Rifle Riven Mod. In this state, it will not take up a Riven slot.

Upon equipping or revealing a generic Riven mod, it becomes Veiled. In this state:

- It will take up a Riven Slot. Attempting to equip will prompt a confirmation screen warning this, as well as how many available Riven slots the player has.
- It reveals a challenge that must be completed in order to become Unveiled. The weapon with the equipped Riven must be in the player's equipped loadout in order to partake in the challenge.
- It will not take up mod capacity, upgraded, grant any bonuses or curses.
- Can only be equipped on a weapon of the same class that itself:
  - Veiled Rifle Rivens can only be equipped on non-shotgun primaries.
  - Veiled Shotgun Rivens can only be equipped on shotguns.
  - Veiled Pistol Rivens can only be equipped on secondaries.
  - Veiled Melee and Zaw Rivens can only be equipped on melee weapons.
  - Veiled Kitgun Rivens can be equipped on either primaries or secondaries.
  - Veiled Robotic Weapon Rivens can be equipped on any Robotic weapon.
  - Veiled Archgun Rivens can only be equipped on Archguns.

Once Unveiled, the Riven's stats, polarity, and mod space are revealed, and can now be upgraded. It also now has a required Mastery Rank to equip.

## Challenges

- The weapon used to complete a challenge is entirely irrelevant to the resulting Riven.
- It is possible to complete the challenges using any equipped weapon or Warframe ability, i.e. it does not need to be the weapon on which the Riven is currently equipped (however the Riven must be equipped on an item within the current loadout while attempting the challenge).
- You can unveil multiple Riven mods simultaneously during a single mission by equipping a veiled Riven mod on each applicable piece of gear (e.g., one on your primary weapon and one on your secondary). This is especially efficient if the Rivens share the same challenge requirements.
- If its conditions are met, the Riven will be revealed independently of mission success. It will also be automatically removed from the weapon used to reveal it if that weapon is not the Riven's dedicated type.
- Completing the challenges also gives affinity.
- Certain challenge modifiers are listed below for the sake of clarity:
  - Being alone - No Companions equipped. Venari is exempt from this modifier as she is instead tied to Khora's abilities. Not to be confused with Solo mode.
  - With an active pet - Generally refers to Beast-type companions and Hounds. Venari is excluded from this modifier.
  - Having a Dragon Key equipped - any Dragon Key can be used for this challenge, unless specified.

## Attributes

Rivens are distinct from regular mods in that they are inconsistent in polarity (randomly chosen between , , or ), Mastery Rank requirement (chosen between MR 8-16), and attribute values, and they have between two and four attributes. Two or three of these attributes will be positive, and one might be negative.

### Naming

Riven mods draw their names from a pool of 31 options, indicated by the randomized/rerolled attributes that the new mod has. Names consist of a prefix and/or a core and suffix, and follow "Prefix-CoreSuffix" or "CoreSuffix" pattern.

- Whether a stat is shown as a prefix, core, or suffix is determined by the magnitude of the randomized modifier on that stat. The prefix and core are determined by the stats with the highest and second highest randomized modifier, respectively. The suffix is always the stat with the lowest randomized modifier.
  - For example, Boltor Sati-critaata has Multishot as the highest stat, Critical Chance as the second highest stat, and Base Damage as the lowest stat.

### Attribute Value Formula

The actual stat values for a riven are randomly chosen between 90% and 110% of the base value, multiplied by the default Riven Disposition constant of 1.2x, and then multiplied by the following weighted bonus or malus values.

Type of Stats | Bonus | Malus/Negative/Curse
2 Bonus, 0 Curse | 0.99 | -

▾Detailed Explanation of Attribute Value Formula ▾

For ordinary rifle, shotgun and pistol Riven attributes, the attribute modifier before display conversion and rounding has the following mathematical form:

- v = b × 10 × (r + 1) × D × L × W

- W = 0.9 + 0.2u

The game performs the calculation step by step using 32-bit floating-point arithmetic.

- v is the attribute modifier before display conversion and rounding.

- b is the attribute's base coefficient for the Riven's weapon class, listed in the tables below.

- r is the Riven's rank, from 0 to 8. The factor 10 is a fixed scaling constant.

- D is a constant Riven Disposition value of 1.2.

- L is the bonus or malus multiplier from the table below, determined by the number of positive and negative attributes.

- u is the attribute's normalized quality value, with 0 ≤ u ≤ 1.

- W is the variation multiplier, ranging from 0.9 to 1.1; u = 0.5 gives W = 1.

Before display rounding, percentage modifiers use 100v%, except Ammo Efficiency and Chance to Gain Combo Count, which use −100v%. Faction damage uses ×(1 + v).

The bonus and malus multipliers (L) are:

3 Bonus, 0 Malus | 0.75 | 0

## Cycling (Rerolling)

Another unique aspect of a Riven mod's attributes is their ability to cycle (reroll) the attributes via the Cycle function in the Mods segment in the Orbiter. A Riven mod is cycled by spending Kuva, which can be obtained from many different sources (see: Kuva Acquisition). Popular sources include the Steel Path and Arbitrations reward shops, The Icebind, and multiple weekly Vendors (notably Palladino in Iron Wake and Archimedean Yonta in the Chrysalith).

Cycling a Riven mod affects the number of attributes, as well as the final numerical attribute, but not the current weapon designation, mod rank, polarity or Mastery Rank requirement. After cycling a mod, both the old version of the mod and the new version are displayed, and players can choose to keep the version they prefer.

Cycling a Riven mod starts off with 900 Kuva 900 Kuva for the first cycle and increases on subsequent cycles of the same mod, capped at 3500 Kuva 3500 Kuva. The number of cycles is indicated by a counter-/anti-clockwise arrow at the bottom right of the Riven mod.

## Trait Locking

Traits can be locked when cycling Riven Mods to prevent them from being lost on subsequent cycles; however, the Kuva cycle cost is doubled if a trait is locked. This feature can only be accessed when in the "Cycle Riven" menu and only applies to the instance in which it is enabled; i.e., a locked trait will need to be re-locked each time the menu is opened. Locked traits will be displayed with grey text and icons beside them. A Riven Mod with a locked trait will also prevent the number and layout of traits from changing on subsequent cycles, e.g., a Riven Mod with two positive traits and one negative trait will always have two positive traits and one negative trait until the lock is removed.

While spliced traits are also locked upon being spliced, they do not increase the Kuva cost of rerolling, nor do they prevent the manual locking of another trait.

## Splicing

With the introduction of the Riven Splicer, obtained exclusively in The Icebind, certain traits can be combined to form new traits that are otherwise unobtainable within the normal trait pool. This feature can be accessed by selecting the "Riven Splicing" option when in the "Riven" section of the Mod menu. When selected, a menu of all owned Riven Mods with valid trait combinations will be displayed.

Some important distinctions of note:

- Traits used to form a spliced trait are consumed.
  - These traits can, however, reappear on the same Riven Mod in subsequent cycles. For example, it is possible to have a Riven Mod with Viral, Cold, and Toxin. For more details, see Elemental Damage Combinations.
  - A random trait will be added to maintain the number of traits on the Riven Mod; however, the layout of positive and negative traits is locked and will not change with subsequent cycles. For example, a Riven Mod with two positive traits and one negative trait will always have two positive traits and one negative trait until the spliced trait is removed.
  - If one of the consumed traits is negative, the random trait will also be negative. The spliced stat will always be positive.
- It is possible for a Riven Mod to have multiple valid trait combinations, but only one spliced trait can be on a single Riven Mod.
- Spliced traits are locked and will not change with subsequent cycles, but are not considered locked traits and will not double the Kuva cycle cost.
  - Since they are not considered locked traits, a separate trait can still be locked after splicing. This is the only way to lock two traits on the same Riven.
  - The lock on the spliced trait can be removed, allowing it to be cycled off if desired. If the lock is unintentionally removed, exiting the Riven menu and re-entering will re-lock the spliced trait.
- The spliced trait inherits the higher random value out of the consumed stats, rather than generating a new one.
  - This applies even if the negative trait is the 'higher' (greater negative) of the two.
  - Community tools for assessing these random values will often flip the positive and negative end for negative traits, making lower values the "positive" end. The true random value of the negative is used when splicing however.

## Transmutation

If a player has excess Rivens and are comfortable with doing away with several of them, a Riven Transmuter allows them to sacrifice four Rivens in their inventory for a new, veiled Riven mod.

Two Riven Transmuters are acquired from the capture of an Eidolon Hydrolyst. Alternatively, they may be purchased from Loid in Sanctum Anatomica for 180 Vessel Capillaries each, or from Palladino in Iron Wake on Earth for 10 Riven Slivers each.

The new Riven will always be of the same type as one of the transmuted Rivens. As a consequence, if four of the same type are transmuted (e.g. only rifle Rivens), then the new mod will be of the same weapon class. Upon transmutation, the four selected Riven mods and the Riven Transmuter will be consumed.

## Notes

- Riven mods are not saved in a player's Codex.
- It is possible to complete multiple Riven mod challenges within a single mission, assuming the player qualifies for them simultaneously.
- While it is possible to equip a veiled Riven mod onto Robotic Weapons, the challenge will not appear for the player.
- Typing [Riven mod] in the in-game chat and selecting the mod will let other players in the same chat channel see its bonuses.
  - Typing out the name of the actual Riven mod is an alternative way of representing the player's mod in chat.
  - Players cannot link Riven mods that they do not own.
- Base (unranked) Endo value of Riven mods is affected by the Mastery Rank requirement, weapon type, and number of cycles.
- A Riven Mod's chosen weapon is determined upon its unveiling; thus a Riven mod can be obtained, saved, and unveil itself to be for a weapon that did not exist when the Riven mod itself was first obtained.
- Riven mods can be used on variants of a particular weapon, including MK1, Prime, Vandal, Wraith, Dex, Prisma, Mara, and Syndicate variants.
  - Being entirely separate weapons, Riven mods for the Mutalist Cernos and the Mutalist Quanta are not compatible with the normal Cernos or Quanta respectively, and vice versa. The same restriction applies to Dual Sidearms and Single Sidearms. Several Melee weapons such as Dex Dakra and Dakra Prime, Nikana (Prime) and Dragon Nikana are also considered separate melee weapons.
- Riven mods will not generate for Exalted Weapons.
- If a Riven mod includes multiple elemental damage bonuses, the elements listed are applied in bottom-up order.
  - An elemental damage mod installed before the Riven mod will interact with the last element listed, rather than the first.
  - An elemental damage mod installed after the Riven mod will only interact with the Riven if it has 1 or 3 elements, and will combine with the first one listed. This applies to the Valence bonus from an Adversary Weapon as well.
  - For example, if a Heat mod is placed directly before a Riven mod that grants Electricity first and Cold second (which would provide Magnetic by itself), it will combine to provide Blast and Electricity rather than Radiation and Cold. Putting said Heat mod directly after the Riven mod will keep the Magnetic damage intact.
- Currently, the lowest Mastery Rank requirement to equip, trade, or sell a Riven mod is 8, and the highest is 16.
- The total Endo gain from dissolving a Riven mod is equal to the following formula: (100 × (Riven Mastery Rank - 8)) + ⌊22.5 × 2Mod Rank⌋ + (200 × Rerolls) - 7.
  - For example, the base value for an unranked, unrolled mod with a Mastery Rank requirement of 13 would be: (100 × (13 − 8)) + ⌊22.5 × 2^0⌋ + (200 × 0) - 7 = 515.
- Since Hotfix 23.8.2 (2018-09-19), trading Rivens requires mastery rank 8 or more.
- As of Update 24.8 (2019-04-24) veiled Archgun Rivens can be purchased from Arbitrations Honors for 35 Vitus Essence.
  - Archgun Rivens can be equipped and unveiled with Atmospheric Archguns.
- Although Beast Companions had their weapons separated in Update 37.0, Veiled Companion Riven mods can still only become mods for Robotic Weapons.

## Trivia

- Riven mods feature a dynamic image, which constantly shifts between the images of the bonuses. For example, a Riven Rifle mod that affects Critical chance and Critical damage (positively or negatively) will shift between Point Strike's image and Vital Sense's image.
  - Interestingly, Riven mods that affect Status Chance use the images of Thunderclap, Crowd Control, Stopping Power, or Staggering Force, as opposed to Rifle Aptitude, Shotgun Savvy, Sure Shot, or Melee Prowess.
  - Riven mods for Kitguns will use images of Secondary mods even when equipped on Primary Kitguns.

Update 44.1 (2026-10-07) RIVEN EXPANSION: RIVEN SPLICING

The second half of our Riven Expansion arrives: Riven Splicing!

With the use of Riven Splicers, obtained exclusively from The Icebind gamemode, specific traits can be combined to create all-new spliced traits! When selecting a Riven Mod, a new Riven Splicing menu will appear. Opening this menu will display all owned Rivens, detailing which Rivens have valid trait combinations.

Select any lit up Riven Mod to see which traits can be spliced!

Image Description: A view of the new Riven Splicing menu. On the left side of the screen is a list of Rivens, with incompatible Rivens grayed out with a “No Compatible Traits” label on top of them. The right side of the screen shows the possible Splicing options for the Selected Riven, in this case a Hikou Riven. The screen offers the choice to merge +88.5% room and -222.6% Damage traits into a +285.1% Weakpoint Trait and a ??? Trait.

Spliced traits require a combination of two specific traits to splice together, with different combinations creating different outcomes! Combinations are pre-selected and confirmation is required before applying a spliced trait to your Riven. We have listed all combinations at the bottom of this section, and you can view the combinations possible on a specific Riven with the “Show All Possible Splices” option in the Riven Splicing menu. This means you’ll always know what spliced trait you’re about to receive before you make a decision.

If multiple spliced traits are available from a single Riven Mod, a “Change Selected Trait” option appears, allowing you to choose which spliced trait you want!

As an example: A Riven Mod with +Heat Damage, +Cold Damage, and +Toxin damage will have 3 spliced traits to choose from: +Blast Damage, +Gas Damage, and +Viral Damage. Which spliced trait goes on the Riven Mod is your choice!

Image Description: A pop-up for deciding which Trait to Splice. The options provided are “Weak Point Damage” or “Weak Point Critical Chance”

When traits are spliced, the two required traits are consumed, with the spliced trait AND a second random trait taking their place. The random trait is one you may receive from cycling normally, including one just used in making the spliced trait, with one change. If one of the consumed traits was a negative bonus, the random trait will also be a negative; if both consumed traits were positive, it will be a positive. This ensures your Riven keeps the same layout of positive and negative traits it has before combining!

After splicing, the total number of positive and negative traits will no longer change on the Riven Mod, no matter how many times it is cycled after the fact. This can be undone by removing the spliced trait from the Riven.

Image: The end-result of a successful Riven Splicing. A Hikou Riven Mod is in the center of the screen, featuring the new +274.1% Weak Point Damage trait, as well as the random -188.3% Damage trait.

Once a spliced trait is on your Riven, it’s there to stay! It is locked, but is not considered a locked trait — meaning it will not double your Kuva cost. Should you ever decide to remove the trait, the lock can be removed and the spliced trait can be cycled off, allowing a new trait to be spliced on! Utilize both a spliced trait and trait locking to lock two traits at once, making cycling for the last trait or two you want even easier.

As an example: Let’s say you had a Riven Mod with 3 Positive Traits and 0 Negative traits, and chose a + Viral spliced trait. No matter how many times the Riven is cycled, it retains +Viral Damage and 2 other positive traits, with 0 negative traits. You could even lock one of the other traits, guaranteeing two traits and cycling for the perfect final trait!

The Mod has two locked traits: one of them is the Spliced Trait (+274.1% Weak Point Damage), and another is a locked trait via the Trait Locking system (in this case, +164.3% Critical Chance). A second new feature will join Glacial Defiance: Riven Splicing. We’ll share a little information about Riven Splicing below, for a more detailed preview of the system you can check out our Dev Workshop.

TRAIT LOCKING (LIVE)

One of the primary requests we’ve received for Riven Mods is to allow players to lock a stat to prevent it from being Cycled. Now, you can do so with a system called Trait Locking!

One trait (stat) can be locked at a time while cycling a Riven, freezing it until the lock is removed or the Cycling menu is closed.

There is no cost or item required to lock a trait. Locks can be removed and re-added for free! However, when cycling a Riven Mod with a locked trait, the Kuva cost is doubled. Locking a trait is entirely optional, and Rivens can still be cycled as usual without a lock.

How to Lock a Riven Trait:

1. Go to the Mod Segment in your Base of Operations

2. Select the Riven Mod you’d like to cycle and select the “Cycle Riven” option

3. Select the “Lock Trait” button

4. Select the trait you wish to lock. The locked trait will appear greyed out with a lock icon on the Mod and will also be listed above the “Lock Trait” button.

5. Begin cycling your Riven with the locked trait! Notice that once locked, it will not change while cycling.

It is important to note that a trait needs to be re-locked each time you open this menu, as they are not maintained once the menu has been closed.

Cycling a Riven with a locked trait prevents the number of traits on the Mod from changing. This means a Mod with 3 positive traits and 1 negative trait will always cycle into another version with 3 positive traits and 1 negative trait if one of the traits is locked. To cycle into a different number of traits, unlock all traits and cycle again.

RIVEN SPLICING (LAUNCHING AT A LATER DATE WITH GLACIAL DEFIANCE)

Riven Splicing combines two traits on a Riven together to form a new spliced trait which is then locked on the Riven (without consuming a Trait Lock). Riven Splicing will require Riven Splicers, which will be rewarded from Glacial Defiance when it releases sometime before or on October 15 (release window PSA for more info). For more details about Riven Splicing, refer to our Dev Workshop.

Update 32.0 (2022-09-07)

RIVEN CAPACITY SYSTEM CHANGES

Those familiar with Rivens may be aware that they come in two states: Veiled and Unveiled. Both of these kinds of Rivens take up Riven Slots and can be equipped to weapons.

After many years in the case of Riven collectors vs. database restrictions, we've added a third state to Rivens that allows them to be stackable and not take up Riven Slots. All newly acquired Rivens will be in this indistinct state, identified by ‘???’ in place of ‘Veiled’ in their Mod label and a new Mod Icon, meaning you will be able to continue to earn Rivens as rewards beyond your Riven Slot capacity.

In this new, these Mods will not show Riven Challenges or take up a Riven Slot until they are equipped onto a weapon. The Mod description for this new Mod type explains this mechanic as well.

Here is a breakdown of the three Riven states for clarity:

‘???’ Rivens

- All new Rivens obtained via Rewards, Palladino, etc.
- Stackable and will not take up Riven Slots
- Riven Type (Archgun, Rifle, etc.) is revealed, but not the Challenge
- Tradeable

Veiled

- What an Indistinct Riven becomes once equipped to a Weapon
- Challenge Revealed
- Takes up Riven Slots
- Tradeable

Unveiled

- Challenge Completed
- Stats are revealed and can be cycled via Kuva
- Tradeable

Since the only number we have to track with this new Riven state is the quantity owned, the database load is greatly diminished, and you can keep as many as you want! Only once a mod of this type is equipped onto a weapon does the challenge reveal itself, making the Riven apply to your account cap. This is a particularly useful change for Sorties, as you will no longer be locked out of playing them if you are at your maximum Riven cap.

A confirmation screen will appear after attempting to equip the Riven, and will display the amount of Riven slots you have available before you commit to equipping it and using up a slot.

Acquisition of new Riven mods from Vendors and other reward drops now give Indistinct Rivens.

Update 28.0 (2020-06-11)

Riven Disposition Changes:

When we introduced separate Dispositions for each weapon variant back in November 2019, we planned to give new weapons a Disposition of 1 (the “average”), or matching the lowest member of the weapon family if it already had a Disposition lower than 1. Unfortunately, this meant that powerful new weapons (like the Kuva Lich collection) might have Rivens that start too strong, forcing reductions in accordance with our balance methodology.

In order to prevent this situation, we will be changing this policy: going forward, all new weapons will be released with the minimum Riven Disposition of 0.5. For The Deadlock Protocol, this includes the Dispositions on newly added Primary Kitguns, in addition to regular weapons (secondary Kitgun Dispositions will remain as-is.)

Full details can be found here: https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1199009-riven-disposition-procedure-for-new-weapons/
