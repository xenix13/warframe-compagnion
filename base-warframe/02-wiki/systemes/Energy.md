# Energy Capacity (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Capacity

> Start missions with |AMOUNT| Energy

—In-game Description

Energy of a Warframe is a resource used mainly to cast abilities. Warframes have 4 abilities that at base, cost up to 100 energy to cast. Almost every Warframe has their own energy-pool with varying capacities depending on the types and costs of their various abilities, with the exceptions of Hildryn, who has no energy but uses shields, and Lavos, having no energy but instead using a cooldown system.

Like health and shields, a Warframe's energy capacity scales with their rank and through the use of mods or buffs. It is also worth noting that the words "power" and "energy" are often used interchangeably by members of the community.

## Starting Energy

Starting Energy refers to how much energy a Warframe starts a mission with, when reviving from Death, or going through a Sanctuary Onslaught conduit.

### Formula

Starting Energy can be increased with unspent mod capacity. The formula followed is:

- Final Starting Energy=Starting Energy+(5×Free Mod Capacity)

Preparation or an Amber Archon Shard can increase starting energy based on a percentage of maximum energy, stacking additively with each other. Including their effect, the formula becomes:

- Final Starting Energy=Starting Energy+(5×Free Mod Capacity)+(Energy Total×Mod Multiplier)

Harrow's passive ability causes him to always spawn with full energy.

## Increasing Energy Capacity

- Endurance Drift
- Flow
- Primed Flow
- Archon Flow
- Adrenaline Boost
- Arcane Battery +0.3 Max Energy per Armor point
- Azure Archon Shard +50 ( +75) Max Energy
- Entropy Radial effect +25% Energy Max

- Equivalent mods Auxiliary Power and Necramech Flow exist for the Archwing and Necramech.
- Flow, Primed Flow, and Archon Flow have mod incompatibility, meaning that only one of them can be equipped at a time.
- Adrenaline Boost is exclusive to the Conclave PvP gamemode.
- Also see Energy capacity-boosting Arcane Helmets.
- The Syndicate Radial Effect from Cephalon Suda's weapons and weapon augments, called Entropy, increases the base energy of frames by 25% for 30 seconds, in addition to its other effects.
  - Entropy is calculated similarly to mods like Flow.

### Formula For Modded Energy Capacity

These mods increase the energy capacity of a Warframe with the following the formula:

Energy_(total)=Energy_(max)×(1+Mod Multiplier)+Flat Bonuses

So taking Volt Prime as an example, with 200 base energy and 300 max energy he will have:

- With Flow: 300×(1+1.0)=600 max energy.
- With Primed Flow: 300×(1+1.85)=855 max energy.
- With Primed Flow with an Entropy effect active, and a Tauforged Azure Archon Shard: 300×(1+1.85+0.25)+75=1005 max energy.

## Restoring Energy

Energy does not regenerate over time by default. Early in the game, Energy Regen can be acquired from the auras Energy Siphon and Dreamer's Bond, and later from Energy Nexus.

The support-role Warframes Trinity and Harrow restore energy to the squad in large quantities, Nokko creates energy-restoring Brightbonnet mushrooms, and Protea makes a Dispensary that pulls Energy orbs out of thin air, among many other Energy-restoring abilities.

The mods Hunter Adrenaline and Rage turn enemy attacks on a Warframe's health into usable energy, a risky tactic with potentially high reward, if the frame's Health and Armor and are sufficient. With Equilibrium, gathering Health orbs restores Energy, an effective increase in energy gain on any frame.

Weapons with the Syndicate Radial Effect Blight or Entropy restore energy when they've gained enough Affinity in battle to activate their radial effect, in addition to unleashing a damaging blast to nearby enemies.

The Zenurik Focus School enables passive energy regen for the squad with its first ability, Wellspring, and increases the effectiveness of energy orbs with its passive Energy Pulse.

Tips & Tricks

- This ability gains no benefits from increasing health. Having lower max health allows flat HP regeneration such as health orbs or Oberon's Renewal to generate more energy more quickly.
- Synergizes with Blood Altar's continuous healing to give Garuda a near infinite supply of energy.
- Despite sacrificing HP, Bloodletting is an effective way of shedding off any harmful status effects (especially slowing effects and DoT procs from Slash and Toxin damage), possibly improving her survivability better than not removing the procs.
- Bloodletting is a powerful ability to combine with Helminth-infused toggled abilities that constantly drain energy, the most notable being Sevagoth's Gloom as it provides Garuda with another self-healing ability besides Blood Altar to constantly regenerate her energy reserves.
  - The Gloom and Bloodletting combo makes Garuda viable at completing Steel Path missions alone, including missions not normally frequented by players such as the Rathuum nodes and the Zealoid Prelate battle in Deimos.
  - Seeking Talons also synergize extremely well with this build as the damage-over-time of her quills also triggers Gloom healing and, subsequently, powers Bloodletting. The quills' homing ability also offers little chance for enemies to escape and can even track and hit hiding enemies, ensuring Garuda can be healed by Gloom as long as they stay within the aura.
  - Molt Reconstruct can work as a stand-in for Gloom's regeneration by allowing any energy spent to heal a large portion of Garuda's health. Often healing her to full in a single cast.
    - Bloodletting itself will not restore health however, as it does not expend any Energy.
- Equipping Blood Forge and using Bloodletting allows Garuda to quickly reload otherwise slow-reloading weapons (such as the Strun, Nagantaka, Tenet Envoy and Kuva Zarr) and increase their overall DPS potential. It comes at the risk of getting struck while already losing her Health to reload, thus she should consider safeguards such as a nearby Blood Altar or equipping Quick Thinking and/or Gladiator Finesse, the mods accounting for her replenished Energy while using Bloodletting.
  - Slotting Azure Archon Shards for extra Energy while also equipping Quick Thinking will make Garuda deceptively durable due to the resulting increased efficiency of regaining Energy via Bloodletting.
  - Slotting Azure Archon Shards for extra Armor instead of extra Energy grants Garuda more effective hitpoints since Quick Thinking benefits from Armor.
- If a Garuda player wishes to take advantage of the reworked passive to further amplify damage output, Helminth can replace Bloodletting with another damage-related self buff ability, such as Xaku's Xata's Whisper or Mirage's Eclipse, as these abilities synergize well with the damage buff and Garuda's own Seeking Talons, while still maintaining self-survival options with Dread Mirror and Blood Altar.
  - In exchange for losing this ability to generate Energy, Garuda can equip Rage or Hunter Adrenaline so she gains Energy while taking damage - which will be offset by a nearby Blood Altar.

### Limits on Energy Gain for Channeled Ability Users

Passive energy regeneration is disabled during most channeled abilities. Sources that restore energy in pulses, like Squad Energy Restore, Energy Vampire and Thurible, are technically a form of Energy Regen, and are disallowed for channeled ability users as well.

- Instantaneous sources of energy, such as Energy Orbs, Arcane Energize, Brightbonnet, and Spellbound Harvest are capable of restoring energy to a frame while it has a channeled ability active.
  - For a list of instant energy sources that apply during channeled abilities, see Channeled_Abilities#Instant_Energy.
- Channeled abilities that can receive passive energy regen include Noctua, and conditionally Gloom and Pacify & Provoke, if there are no enemies in range.

Passive energy regeneration will be paused while casting regular abilities as well, for about 1 second. Due to this factor, passive energy regeneration will slightly less impactful the more frequently that abilities are cast.

- However, casting a regular ability will not interfere with energy pulses from sources like Energy Vampire or Squad Energy Restore, as the energy drain will occur from the end point of the pulse, rather than when it is cast. For example: if halfway through the 100 Energy Pulse of a Squad Energy Restore (Large), an ability is cast that costs 25 Energy, the Warframe's energy will instantly leap to 100 Energy, and then subtract the 25 Energy drain.

## Energy Drain

There are also some instances where a Warframe's energy can be expended and/or stopped from regenerating.

- On top of its regular effects, a Magnetic proc will drain 120 energy over 3 seconds, with each tick dealing 30 energy drain, and the first tick happening instantly.
  - The same is true regarding purple energy drain fields created by Energy Leech Eximus units (also called Parasitic Eximus if Infested) that drain 30 energy and scramble the HUD.
- Nightmare Mode's Energy Drain condition slowly drains 15 energy per second throughout the mission.
- In Disruption missions, one of the negative Conduit effects is "Energy Drain Mode", draining Warframe energy by -5 energy/second (base value, increases by round count) until the Demolisher is stopped, or for the entire round if it destroys the Conduit.
- In the Index, the unique debuff 'Financial Stress' accrues on players for every Index Point they pick up, reducing their max health, and draining their energy by -1 energy/second for each Index Point carried.
  - A similar debuff called Resurgence Stress exists in Arbitrations and Temporal Archimedea while carrying Resurgence Tokens to revive a dead player.
- In Netracell missions, carrying the Exhaustion Keyglyph causes the bearer to lose 2 energy per second for every enemy within 10 meters for the duration of the mission.
- In Deep Archimedea and Temporal Archimedea, the Vampyric Liminus Risk Variable adds invincible Liminus enemies who drain the energy of Warframes within 5 meters at a rate of -25 energy/second.
  - There are also optional Personal Modifiers that cause the player to take 5% maximum energy loss upon health damage, lose 2 energy per second for every enemy within 10 meters, or have 75% reduced maximum energy in exchange for greater mission rewards when selected.
  - In Temporal Archimedea, the Vamp Rock Deviation alters the Hollvania defense mission so that Flare's health can be restored by standing near him, but steadily drains player energy and disables abilities while nearby.

### Energy Gain Reduction

There are some effects that disable or reduce passive energy regeneration:

- Sorties with the Energy Reduction and Archimedea missions with the Constricted personal modifier selected reduce a Warframe's Total Energy capacity by 75%. Energy Reduction Sorties also reduce passive energy gain from sources like Energy Siphon and Wellspring by 75%.
- In Conclave, No Current Leap nullifies passive energy regeneration in exchange for increased mobility.

There are some effects that totally disable energy gain:

- In Faceoff, there is a Penalty labeled "Disable Energy Orbs" which not only disables collection of energy orbs, but all forms of energy regeneration during its 60 second duration.
  - This Penalty can occur in Temporal Archimedea missions while the "Comepetitive Streak" Risk Variable is active, which randomly activates Faceoff Penalties every 25 seconds.
- While Harrow is channeling energy into his Thurible, he cannot regain energy from any source, including Energy Orbs.

## Effective Energy Capacity

### Casting Effective Capacity

Similarly to how one can define the concept of "Effective Health" by weighting the number of hit-points with the corresponding damage reduction, one can also define the concept of casting effective energy capacity by weighting the energy capacity using the Ability Efficiency factor:

Effective Energy Capacity_(casting)=(Energy Capacity)/(2−Ability Efficiency)

Since casting cost can not be reduce under 25% of the base cost, the highest possible upper-bound for the effective energy capacity is:

Effective Energy Capacity≤4×Energy Capacity

### Toggle Effective Capacity

Since the efficiency of toggle abilities depends also on the duration, in that case the formula becomes:

Effective Energy Capacity_(toggle)=(Energy Capacity×Ability Duration)/(2−Ability Efficiency)

For the case where no energy is recovered and a constant rate of energy is drained, one can also consider the effective toggle duration as follows:

Effective Toggle Duration=(Effective Energy Capacity_(toggle))/(Base Drain Rate)

Effective Toggle Duration=(Energy Capacity×Ability Duration)/(Base Drain Rate(2−Ability Efficiency))

Effective Toggle Duration=(Energy Capacity)/(Modified Drain Rate)

Due to the definition of the terms, there are many ways in-which the formula for effective toggle duration can be expressed.

Apart from toggle duration, this can be used to work out the effective duration granted per energy pick up.

### Examples

Suppose a Prowling Ivara has a modded drain of 0.25 energy/sec. An orb of 50 energy would provide:

(50)/(0.25)=200 seconds

Considering the same Ivara with full energy pool of 744 capacity, she can remain in prowl for:

(744)/(0.25)=2,976 seconds≈50 minutes
