CODEX

Energy shield blocks projectiles and nullifies powers

The Nullifier Crewman is a specialized Corpus Sniper Crewman carrying a power disruptor on his back. When alerted, the nullifier deploys an orbiting Projector Drone that generates a Warframe-suppressing field beneath it. The spherical field not only prevents projectiles from entering (similar to Frost's Snow Globe), but also prevents any Warframe abilities from being cast within its radius, destroys most powers and cancels out any active buffs as soon as players enter it.

Firing at the globe causes it to gradually shrink and eventually dissipate altogether, forcing the drone back into the backpack, from where it will re-emerge after a brief period. The Projector Drone itself resides at all times outside the field, orbiting above the Crewman, therefore destroying it will permanently remove the Crewman's ability to create the barrier.

## Tactics

### Mechanics of the bubble

- The energy sphere will remove any buffs active on Warframes caught inside it, and will render the Nullifier immune to direct targeting by all Warframe powers, including AoE and indirect powers. The sphere can also deactivate Guard Mode of a Voidrig. However, while inside the sphere the Nullifier Crewman will have no protection against weapon attacks, and Warframes can walk through the field at will.
- Nullifier fields absorb hitscan projectiles and deflect physical projectiles.
  - Certain ability projectiles (e.g. the Shuriken projectiles) can home in on the Nullifier, but if the bubble is present then they will bounce off of it like normal projectiles.
- Splash damage from explosive weapons and powers originating outside the field are nullified, dealing no damage to anything inside, and each explosion counting as only a single hit to the field. A direct hit from the alternate-fire projectile of a Stahlta will actually bounce off the field and get deflected elsewhere.
- Immune to Lifted, Ragdoll, and Knockdown effects while bubble is active.

### Breaking the bubble

- Nullifier bubbles use the following mechanic to determine damage dealt to it:
  - Nullifier bubbles have a minimum and maximum damage per shot dealt to it – 100 damage at minimum, and 400 damage maximum. Any hit that deals less than 100 damage will be raised to that amount, while any shot that deals more than 400 damage will be lowered to that amount. The adjusted damage values are displayed by the damage indicator.
    - If the attack deals Magnetic damage, the Nullifier bubble takes 300 - 1200 damage instead.
  - The damage dealt is then altered by the Nullifier bubble's shrink rate per shot, which is 6% of its current size.
  - The formula for determining damage to the Nullifier bubble is: Damage ÷ 100 × Shrink Rate.
    - Example A: A Braton hits the bubble for 18 damage. Since the bubble can only receive a minimum of 100 damage, the bubble takes 100 damage from that hit. The damage (100) is then divided by 100, which equals 1.0. This value is then multiplied by the shrink rate of 6% (0.06), giving a value of 0.06, which results in the bubble being reduced by 6% of its current size.
    - Example B: An Opticor hits the bubble for 500 damage. Since the bubble can only receive a maximum of 400 damage, the bubble only takes 400 damage from the shot. The calculation is thus: 400 ÷ 100 × 0.06 = 0.24, hence the bubble will shrink by 24% of its current size.
  - The rate of shrinkage is based off the current size of the bubble.
- The Nullifier field has Object health type which does not register Critical Hit damage or status effects.
  - Weapons that heavily depend on critical hits such as Soma, Synapse or Amprex can be less effective against it if the non-critical DPS value is below 400.
  - Weapons with high multishot, high base damage, fast fire rate and Magnetic are most effective at dealing with Nullifiers.
  - The Projector Drone orbiting above the Crewman is vulnerable to Critical damage, and therefore one is advised to aim for it with weapons relying on crits.
    - The drone is located near the top of the Nullifier's facing side, and can hide itself on the opposite end of the barrier dome respective to the player if the Nullifier is running/facing away.
- Using a Miter with the Neutralizing Justice mod can sometimes instantly and permanently destroy the bubble.
- Using Void Sling twice into a Nullifier Crewman will instantly remove their bubble and knock them down.

### Behavior

- Being a sniper unit, Nullifiers are far less accurate up close, but get more accurate at medium and long ranges.
  - Despite being a Sniper Crewman, the Nullifier Crewmen's behavior is a bit different; they will not attempt to bash enemies who get too close, and much like Arctic Eximus they will not attempt to take cover or run away. Nearby allies will also head inside the Nullifier Crewman's sphere, making it harder to kill them.
- Vapos, Orm, Vorac, Axio and Taro nullifiers will try to get close to enemies and hit them with their 2 Provas

## Notes

- While they can be Mind Controlled when their bubbles are down, they will dispel the control when the bubble resumes, regardless of the fact that the Nullifier is now allied and should revert his bubble to dispel enemy powers instead.
- Augments like Ice Wave Impedance are also nullified; the frozen path is destroyed wherever a Nullifier bubble touches.
- All of Vauban's powers are nullified as well, but only if the Nullifier bubble touches the grenade.
- If a Nullifier spawns as an Arctic Eximus, It is possible for them to deploy both Snow Globe and their regular Nullifier bubble.
- Attacking the bubble will alert the Nullifier.
- Bubbles can erode Nidus mutation stacks when inside.
- Octavia's Mallet can damage the Nullfier's bubbles, despite being an ability.
- Currently, Nullifier Crewmen can completely cancel Tauron Strikes. This is likely a bug.

## Tips

- Nullifiers deactivate their bubbles when not alerted.
- The Nullifier's bubbles can be destroyed by melee attacks if they connect on the edge of its radius, which can be utilized by abilities such as:
  - Excalibur's Exalted Blade
  - Ivara's Artemis Bow
  - Wukong's Primal Fury
  - Valkyr's Hysteria
- Miter's Neutralizing Justice and Hildryn's Balefire Surge augments can instantly destroy nullifier fields.

## Trivia

- Nullifier Crewmen seem to wear the same uniform that The Sergeant wears, albeit with yellow chest straps rather than white ones.
- Oddly, the Codex image of Nullifier Crewmen seems to represent a standard Crewman with a black uniform and a jetpack used by the three Ranger variants in Archwing missions, whilst the in-game model has an additional helmet extension and a unique "core" pack on its back.
- When summoned from Nekros, their bubbles will be the caster's energy color.
- A variant of the Nullifier Crewman can appear as a Capture target, the Nullifier Target.
- Despite using a Lanka which is Electricity based, it deals mostly Puncture damage as shown by Adaptation.
- In the Deepmines, Nullifier Crewmen are titled as Obviators, but otherwise function identically.

Update 40.0 (2025-10-15)

NULLIFIER BUBBLE INTERACTION CHANGES

Changed how the following Warframe’s Abilities interact with Nullifier bubbles.

Frost’s Snow Globe: Nullifier bubbles now drain Snow Globe's life by 20% per second, and cannot gain strength when a Nullifier bubble is touching it.

Limbo’s Cataclysm: Now shrinks 4 times faster when touching a Nullifier orb, instead of being removed instantly.

Update: Specters of the Rail 0.0 (2016-07-08)

Abilities & Augments vs Nullifiers

- Abilities will not affect players or enemies within a Nullifier bubble.
- Effects of an ability on both players and enemies will be removed when entering Nullifier bubbles.
- A Warframe’s active abilities will be deactivated upon entering a Nullifier bubble.
- Projectiles spawned by an ability will be deflected when they encounter a Nullifier bubble. For example, Ember’s Fireball and Ash’s Shuriken will be deflected.
- Abilities that exist independent of their creator will not be deactivated if the Warframe enters a nullifier, but will be destroyed if touched by the bubble itself. For example, Frost’s Snowglobe will not disappear when Frost enters the bubble, but will be destroyed if the bubble comes into contact with the Snowglobe sphere.

Other Changes

- Polished the hit detection of melee weapons within Nullifier bubbles. Some weapons, like the Tonbo for example, would strike the Nullifier bubble first, followed by the Nullifier agent in the same strike. However, the damage would only result on the bubble! The weapons can now strike both the bubble and the agent on the same strike, and the agent will take damage as long you are inside the bubble for the attack.
