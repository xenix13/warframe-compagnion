The factions in WARFRAME are the civilizations and groups fighting for supremacy in the Origin System; their conflict is the backdrop of the game's missions. Because almost nothing in the game indicates an alien origin of any faction, it is assumed that they all are human, trans-human, or created by humans, with the exception of The Murmur.

The only playable faction is the Tenno, although some quests will let you play as a member of another faction for their duration or part of it. Most factions are presented as adversaries, while the Syndicates and the Wild are mostly neutral, but can act as allies or enemies to the Tenno. Enemy factions include:

- Grineer,
- Corpus,
- Infested,
- Corrupted (listed as Orokin in-game),
- Sentient,
- Stalker,
- Narmer,
- The Murmur,
- Scaldra,
- Techrot,
- and the Anarchs.

## Main Factions

### Tenno

They were called Tenno. Warriors of blade and gun: masters of the Warframe armor. Those that survived the old war were left drifting among the ruins. Now they are needed once more.—Warframe Official Website

Legendary warriors of the Orokin Empire that fell long ago, wielding the versatile and overwhelming power of the Warframes, the Tenno have been awakened by their guide Lotus and dedicated themselves to the protection of the innocents, the people caught in the conflicts between those that seek to dominate the Origin System.

### Grineer

They say there is power in numbers - the Grineer are living proof. Wretched from centuries of cloning that has left their genetics in decay, this horde expands their vile empire through brute force, conquering anyone who tries to stop them.—Warframe Official Website

Clones created by the Orokin to serve as slaves, disposable labourers and cannon fodder in the field of battle, the Grineer banded together as brothers and sisters under the leadership of the Grineer Queens when the empire fell. With their numbers unending, their limbs replaceable and ruthless brutality, they seek to create an empire of their own to place themselves in the positions of those that once oppressed them.

### Corpus

The Corpus embody the cold vacuum of space - heartless and bent on taking anything left unprotected. Driven by profits, the Corpus Board’s small numbers are supplemented by their legion of robot proxies, optimized for quick and efficient destruction.—Warframe Official Website

Money is often said to rule the world, but the Corpus have taken this phrase and made it a reality. A conglomerate of cutthroat entrepeneurs, shrewd conmen and reckless inventors united by the simple desire for more profits, the Corpus wish to monopolize the entire Origin System under the fingers of the Board of Directors, inspired by the cunning and rapid success of their long-lost founder Parvos Granum. They may not have the Grineer's raw numbers, but are armed with the most advanced and cost efficient robots and technology that money can buy.

### Infested

The Infestation is more than a disease - it is a horror of twisted flesh, monsters made into reality. The blight consumes its victims, transforming them into unrecognizable atrocities controlled by rabid impulse. The Infested are a single-minded scourge that belongs in the realm of nightmares, but instead chooses to invade ships and colonies, leaving only death and destruction in its wake.—Warframe Official Website

No plague is more dangerous than the Infestation, for none can escape a disease that can think. An ancient horror that haunted even the mighty Orokin, the Technocyte Virus infects everything it comes into contact with; be it flesh, machine or the very ground itself; and connects it to a hive mind intelligence with a singular goal: spread further. Once contained between the fall of the Orokin and the present day, the ambition and greed of would-be conquerors released the Infestation back into the wild, indiscriminately twisting its victims into misshapen monstrosities that carry the infection further and further.

### Orokin/ Corrupted

The highly revered Orokin civilization built sovereignty on a culture of art, technology and architecture. To prove oneself worthy of elevated social status, one must face Orokin trials in the golden and majestic Halls of Ascension. At one time a utopian society of omniscient leadership, the great Orokin Era ended in a divine realization of their own ignorance.—"Orokin" Cephalon Fragment

The single greatest and most powerful civilization in human history, the immortal Orokin spread humanity past the borders of Earth and colonized the entirety of the Origin System, from Mercury all the way to Pluto. Indescribable wealth, eternal life and advanced technology that bordered on sorcery defined the Orokin, but this was a luxury reserved only for the highest echelons of the empire, for their immense power and influence was matched by arrogance and hedonism. Widespread cruelty, constant infighting and excessive consumption of resources among the noble Orokin houses led to the collapse of the empire. Now the Orokin are but a distant memory, with only a few remnants of it surviving to the present, such as the neural sentries that take control of anyone foolish enough to enter the halls of their dominion.

### Sentient

Whispers from the Old War have begun to escape the deepest shadows. What terrible secret calls out to be discovered?—Warframe Official Website

The greatest foes of the Orokin Empire, a species of self-aware machines that waged a devastating conflict against the Orokin that would become known as the "Old War". Able to render the advanced weaponry of the Orokin worthless against them, it is the Sentients that the Orokin designed the Warframes to counter. Although they were ultimately defeated, the destruction suffered in the Old War and that which followed caused the final collapse of the empire. Imprisoned and sleeping within ancient tombs, the Sentients are rumored to still be down in those depths, waiting for someone to release them.

### Stalker

Some have walked these desolate worlds while you have slept. Some like me. I remember what you did. I remember the day. [...] Now I hunt, dividing your numbers. Watching from that dark place, cataloging your sins, I am the ghost of retribution. You may forget but you are not innocent.—Stalker Codex Entry

One of the last remnants of the Orokin Era, the Stalker is an enigmatic, vengeful shadow that follows every footstep of the Tenno. Armed with bow, knives, scythe and a Warframe of his own, the Stalker desires naught but vengeance against the Tenno in the names of his dead masters. At first hunting on his own, the Acolytes would eventually join his cause and now spend their time hunting those walking the Steel Path.

### Syndicates

Syndicates are groups of interest operating throughout the Origin System separate from the Corpus, Grineer, and the Tenno. These groups have their own ideologies and goals regarding the fate of the system, with some inevitably in disagreement with at least one other syndicate, though alliances between said syndicates exist.

### Wild

Wild, Unaffiliated, or Neutral lifeforms are those that do not side with any of the major factions, including wild animals.

### Narmer

All as one. A nightmare is over. Finally we see clearly. Finally we speak freely. Savor the taste of liberty my friends. Don't ever relax your vigilance. All as one. Future generations won't remember the Tenno oppression, but we do. We bear the scars, we mourn the dead, and we must never allow ourselves to forget. All as one.—Ballas

### The Murmur

In its calcified chaos, the Indifference is disjointed even from itself.—Shuffling Fragment Codex Entry

Creations of the Indifference, the Murmur are constructs of lime stone and Void energy made for the sole purpose of acting as both its legions and its heralds, working without need or want to break their master free from the confines of the Void.

### Scaldra

Ruthless aggression was Höllvania's answer to the Techrot. Wielding highly corrosive Efervon, Scaldra soldiers now patrol the streets to purge any Infestation. With martial law now declared, the Scaldra have total control. Led by Major Neci Rusalka, her command brings the entire military might of Höllvania to bear against you.—Official Warframe Website

### Techrot

Take down the Techrot in all its myriad forms. Left to fester in Höllvania's underground, the rapidly mutating Infested has created all new threat varieties capable of wreaking havoc on the unprepared.—Official Warframe Website

### Anarchs

Though The Old Peace brought unity to the Orokin and the Sentient, the Anarchs did not believe in this peace and splintered into a separatist faction. They are primarily comprised of Dax soldiers, but have also incorporated clone-bred Grineer as well as hijacked Sentients and Warframes at their disposal.
