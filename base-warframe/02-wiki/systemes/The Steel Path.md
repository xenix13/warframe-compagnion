The Steel Path

Teshin has a new challenge only the most worthy Tenno can accept. Greater difficulty and greater rewards await. Can you reclaim the Origin System and walk the Steel Path?

The Steel Path (also known as the abbreviation SP) is a difficulty modifier exclusive to the Origin System hosted by Teshin, testing the Tenno's perseverance against significantly more durable enemies for greater rewards. It was introduced in Update 28.1 (2020-07-08).

- Enemy Level +100 (Only +50 On Archwing / Railjack)
- Enemy Health 250%
- Enemy Shield 250%
- +100% Resource Drop Chance
- +100% Mod Drop Chance (excluded from Railjack)

## Mechanics

### Overview

In order to access The Steel Path, players must have completed all nodes accessible prior to The New War on the connected Star Chart (excluding Mutalist Alad V and Jordas Golem Assassinations on Eris, and Brutus on Uranus. This also means players do not need to have unlocked or completed any missions in the Zariman Ten Zero, Albrecht's Laboratories, or Höllvania tile sets).

Note that this also entails completing quest-locked nodes, including the Tyana Pass node on Mars, Follie's Hunt on Venus, Ropalolyst node on Jupiter, Saya's Visions on Earth, and all nodes on Lua and Kuva Fortress, which require completing all main quests up to Chimera Prologue. The player must also be Mastery Rank 5 to access Vay Hek's node on Oro, Earth. Speaking to Teshin on any Relay will prompt "Steel Path?" which displays the uncompleted nodes, quests, and mastery rank requirement.

Once the player meets these requirements, Teshin will invite the player to challenge The Steel Path; they must meet him on any Relay and ask him about "Steel Path?" to unlock the mode. Once players have The Steel Path unlocked, it will be displayed as a toggle on the right side of the Star Chart screen.

To unlock The Steel Path also in Duviri, the player must have additionally completed The Duviri Paradox quest and the three base Duviri nodes (The Duviri Experience, The Lone Story and The Circuit) in normal mode, but does not require Isleweaver. If the player toggles on The Steel Path and attempts to start a Duviri mission without having completed these nodes, they will be stopped and notified which nodes must first be cleared.

The Steel Path only affects normal missions, Dark Sectors, Junctions, Mission Locators, and Duviri. It does not apply to Nightmare Mode, Syndicates, or Invasions. The Steel Path also adds a 6th tier Landscape Bounty.

### Incursions

Incursions will only appear in the Alerts panel when the player has toggled their Star Chart mode to "Steel Path".

Each day at 00:00 UTC, 6 Steel Path Incursions Alert missions will be made available to the player on the Star Chart. A player can attempt them regardless of whether they have unlocked the node where the Incursion is located, and completing the Incursion will unlock that node. Incursions can appear on any node except Landscapes and Junctions, and they always appear globally on the same nodes for each player, to aid with matchmaking. Each Incursion always awards 5 Steel Essence (unaffected by any boosters) and some may also have an additional Credits reward.

Some mission types in Incursion have additional conditions to make them even more challenging than regular Steel Path missions:

- Spy Incursions require 2 Data Vaults (instead of normal 1 vault).
- Interception Incursions requires 2 waves (instead of normal 1 wave).
- Excavation Incursions requires 200 Cryotic (instead of 100 Cryotic).

### Rules

- Players must re-unlock Star Chart nodes on The Steel Path starting from E Prime, Earth, as if starting the Origin System from a fresh state.
  - Dakata, Kuva Fortress, the individual Jordas Golem and Mutalist Alad V Assassination Eris nodes, and Bounties are also immediately available.
    - Plains of Eidolon, Orb Vallis, and Cambion Drift gain a 6th tier Bounty, while Zariman Ten Zero, Albrecht's Laboratories, and Höllvania gain a Steel Path variant for all available bounties.
    - Completion of the Plains of Eidolon, Orb Vallis, and Cambion Drift Bounties are not required in order to mark the node as completed. Entering the Landscape with the 6th tier Bounty selected and immediately exiting to the hub town will also unlock their adjacent nodes.
  - Steel Path Eidolons can only be fought at night, either in the 6th tier Plains of Eidolon Bounty, or by having selected the Steel Path mode from the Navigation Screen before entering the Plains of Eidolon.
  - Junctions on The Steel Path do not have any associated tasks and can thus be entered as soon as the adjacent node is cleared.
    - If the player has an account made early in the game's history and had cleared the Star Chart before junctions were implemented, the player will have to go back to the normal mode junction and clear its prerequisites to be able to challenge it in Steel Path mode.
- All enemies have their level increased by 100, and gain an additional +150% (to a total of 250%, or 2.5x) bonus to health and shields.
  - Bounties have a starting level of 100 with the aforementioned modifiers. Players must be Mastery Rank 10 to access these bounties.
  - Enemies in Archwing and Empyrean have their level increased by 50.
    - Enemies in Grineer Sealab underwater Archwing still have their levels increased by 100.
  - The Eidolon Teralyst's level is increased to 110, the Eidolon Gantulyst to 120 and Eidolon Hydrolyst to 130.
  - Enemies in Duviri have their level increased by 20, but with no changes to Health or Shields. The Orowyrm boss fight gains additional mechanics.
    - Enemies in The Undercroft, The Circuit, and Isleweaver use the default Steel Path modifiers.
- Team Bonus Consumables have a one-minute cooldown.
- Enemy spawn rates during Endless missions are scaled to match the spawn rate for a full squad, even when solo.
- Acolytes can spawn in any Steel Path mission except in Archwing, Assassination, Ascension, Duviri, and Empyrean.
  - Multiple Acolytes can spawn each mission in roughly 3-7 minute intervals depending on the kill rate of enemies, with an average of 4 1/2 minutes in Survival. This includes non-endless mission types such as Exterminate, and Landscapes (regardless of whether a bounty is currently active).
    - In non-endless missions, a max of 3 Acolytes will spawn.
    - Due to being tied to kill rate, Acolytes can stop spawning if every enemy in a non-endless mission is killed.
    - In Survival, an Acolyte can still be spawned after the player runs out of Life Support, as long as there remain enemies to be killed in order to summon the Acolyte.
    - Acolytes will not spawn from kills made by Specters or On Call Crew.
  - Acolytes will still spawn in submersible missions on Uranus.

## Rewards

### Mission

- Mission nodes and Junctions award Mastery Points that are separate from normal Star Chart missions. Like normal missions, points are only awarded after the first completion.
  - Jordas Golem and Mutalist Alad V Assassinate nodes are not required to reach maximum Mastery (27,569) from Steel Path missions.
- All missions in The Steel Path have a +100% resource drop chance increase and +100% mod drop chance increase over the same mission node in normal mode.
  - These bonuses stack additively with True Master's Font blessings, Resource Drop Chance Booster and Mod Drop Chance Booster, and Dark Sector bonuses.
- 6 Incursion missions in the Star Chart are randomly picked as a daily Alert mission, each rewarding 5 Steel Essence (unaffected by any boosters) and some may also have an additional Credits reward.
  - Missions are fully randomized each day, independent of previous days, instead of cycling through a randomly ordered list.
    - This causes some missions to occur much more frequently than others, or even on multiple consecutive days.
- Bounty variants of the Zariman Ten Zero award 50% more Voidplumes, while variants of Albrecht's Laboratories and Höllvania Central Mall award 50% more Standing Standing Standing. They also award 1 Steel Essence.
- Eidolon Teralyst, Eidolon Gantulyst, and Eidolon Hydrolyst are guaranteed to drop 1 Steel Essence.
- Acolytes are guaranteed to drop 2 Steel Essence (affected by Resource Boosters and Beast Companion's Loyal Retriever/Resourceful Retriever), as well as one random Steel Path Arcane (Primary Deadhead, Primary Dexterity, Primary Merciless, Secondary Deadhead, Secondary Dexterity, or Secondary Merciless).
- All Eximus enemies have an increased chance to drop Riven Slivers.
- Fully clearing a planet's mission nodes awards a respective Decoration and Emote, as well as 25 Steel Essence.
  - Deimos additionally rewards a decoration and emote for the retired Orokin Derelict.
    - Players who have already completed the Orokin Derelict before its removal on Update 29.0 (2020-08-25) will receive a second statue.
  - The Index is not required for Neptune completion.
  - Jordas Golem and Mutalist Alad V Assassination nodes are not required for Eris completion.
  - Dark Refractory requires two The Descendia runs; one from starting at Inferum 1 and another from starting at Inferum 21.
  - Empyrean requires all missions instead of just the ones on a singular Proxima, excluding Free Flight, Lich Confrontations, Scoria's Angel, and The Kuva Wytch.
- Opening a Void Relic in Steel Path Void Fissures awards 1 Steel Essence.
- Clearing an Empyrean mission awards 2 Steel Essence.
- Certain missions have increased shop resource rewards:
  - Completion of Duviri's The Duviri Experience, The Lone Story, and Isleweaver awards 5 Steel Essence, 15 Pathos Clamp (up from 10), and 22 Drifter Intrinsics (up from 15).

  - Conjunction Survival Yuvarium, Lua awards 5 Lua Thrax Plasm (up from 3) per rotation, while Circulus, Lua awards 6 Lua Thrax Plasm (up from 5) per rotation.
  - Abyssal Zone, Ceres awards 8, 11, or 12 Vainthorn (up from 6-8).

  - Deepmines Bounties award 15-19 Fergolyte (up from 11-15), 1 additional Mushroom Harvest spot, and 1 Steel Essence.

    - Hunhullus, Dactolyst, and the Prime Vanguard drops 3 Steel Essence.
    - Commandeered Ash Prime, Commandeered Caliban Prime, Commandeered Ember Prime, Commandeered Mesa Prime, Commandeered Protea Prime, and Commandeered Volt Prime, outside of the Vanguard boss fight, have a chance to drop 2 Lyroic Bridge, Ren Hypercore, or Ascaris Prime, and are guaranteed to drop 1 Tekolyst Arcane and 1 Steel Essence.
- Super Bosses can be challenged which awards a Sumdali. Additionally defeating them alone with a boss-specific Warframe awards their respective Accolade Glyph.

### The Circuit

- Main article: The Circuit#The Steel Path Circuit

The Circuit has its own set of Steel Path rewards, most notably Incarnon Genesis adapters. Two out of five adapters are selectable per week and are awarded of The Circuit's 5th and 10th award tiers, one time per week.

## Tips

- Due to drastically increased enemy shields, it is advisable to bring abilities that can remove enemy defenses such as Nyx's Psychic Bolts, Xaku's The Lost, Caliban's Fusion Strike, Styanax's Tharros Strike (Helminth subsumable), Hildryn's Pillage (Helminth subsumable), or Unairu's Magnetic Flare and Caustic Strike. Alternatively, bring weapons or abilities which can bypass enemy defenses.
- Harrow's Condemn and Khora's Ensnare abilities can completely immobilize acolytes, trivializing the fight against them. Both of these abilities are subsumable.
- Archon shards can alleviate the weaknesses of warframes that would be otherwise too fragile to take on Steel Path.
- Most enemies in Steel Path are still powerless against stealth abilities. Though, collateral damage from enemy attacks on other targets can still endanger an invisible warframe.
- Weapons that can frequently apply impact procs will allow you to execute mercy kills quicker on heavy targets.
- It is highly recommended to focus on primary objectives first and save as many downed allies as possible before an acolyte arrives. This enables an easier extraction, if one is inadequately geared to take on acolytes.

## Trivia

- The Steel Path was originally known as "Hard Mode" when first pitched by the Development Team.
- Originally, two different tiers of hard mode were planned: one was meant to be a +60 enemy level Star Chart, and the other, called "Ultra Hard Mode", a +100 level. The first tier was removed in order to not dilute the matchmaking.
- Circulus, Lua's level 180-200 is the highest starting level of enemies in the Steel Path.
- Missions can still sometimes change objectives mid-mission, such as Capture changing to Exterminate (after capturing the target), but Teshin has no voice lines to reflect this, so he will simply repeat his original voice line.
