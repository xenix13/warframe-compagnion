Coda weapons are special Infested/Techrot weapons.

Rather than being directly awarded from the Technocyte Coda, vanquishing the adversary instead drops Live Heartcells, 10 of which can be used to purchase Coda weapons from Eleanor. Unlike weapons directly acquired from Kuva Liches and Sisters of Parvos, the Mastery Rank requirement is enforced on these store-bought weapons. Each offering has a random progenitor bonus damage type and percentage increase, which is cycled every 4 days at 0:00 UTC. Only one of each weapon can be purchased per rotation.

The following Coda weapons are upgraded versions of existing Infested weapons.

- Coda Bubonico (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, status chance, fire rate, and magazine capacity.
- Coda Catabolyst (MR 17) - Higher damage, status chance, and magazine size; reload tosses three grenades.
- Coda Caustacyst (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance; Corrosive pools from heavy attack are larger with longer duration.
- Coda Hema (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, fire rate, and magazine size.
- Coda Hirudo (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance.
- Coda Mire (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance.
- Coda Pathocyst (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance; maggots spawned from throws are stronger.
- Coda Pox (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, and magazine capacity.
- Coda Sporothrix (MR 17) - Higher damage, critical chance, status chance, magazine size, reload speed, and explosion radius.
- Coda Synapse (MR 17) - Higher damage, status chance, magazine size, punch through, and beam length.
- Coda Tysis (MR 17) - High damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and magazine capacity.

The following Coda weapons are unique, and are not based on existing Infested weapons.

- Coda Bassocyst (MR 17) - Shotgun that shoots sonic projectiles, with an Alternate Fire that launches head-latching mites and teleports the user to enemies opened to Parazon Mercy Finishers.
- Coda Motovore (MR 17) - Strengthens physical damage mods and gains a bonus and altered appearance based on the highest physical damage stat: attack speed ( Impact), range ( Puncture), or status chance ( Slash).
- Dual Coda Torxica (MR 17) - Dual rapid-fire pistols that tags enemies to spread spores upon their death, inflicting Cold status effect over time and Damage Vulnerability.

## Mechanics

### Overleveling

This section is transcluded from Weapons § Overleveling. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.

Certain weapons have the ability to increase their maximum rank beyond the default of Rank 30. This can be done by applying Forma onto the weapon, each of which increases their maximum rank by 2, up to a maximum of Rank 40 after 5 polarizations.

Weapons that can be overlevelled includes: Necramechs, Paracesis, Kuva Weapons, Tenet Weapons, and Coda Weapons. Overleveled weapons may also gain unique rank bonuses, which can be seen under the stats window when viewing the weapon's Mod configuration.

- Each additional rank beyond 30 also gives Mastery Rank experience, giving 4,000 points in total for weapons, and 8,000 total for Necramechs.
- Mod capacity from the additional ranks gets doubled by Orokin Catalyst or Orokin Reactor, allowing for a total of up to 80 mod capacity.
- A overlevelled melee weapon's stealth damage bonus does not increase past the usual maximum of 7, reached at Rank 30.

### Elemental Bonus

The Coda weapons additionally have bonus damage of one damage type which can either be Impact, Heat, Cold, Electricity, Toxin, Magnetic, or Radiation, ranging from 25-60% of the weapon's base damage determined randomly, with lower percentages being more common. The damage type of this bonus damage is randomly generated when Eleanor's shop refreshes. This additional bonus damage applies as weapon base damage, meaning elemental mods and status that scale from base / modified base damage will be affected.

A rank 40 Coda weapon can have an Elemental Vice applied, which enables the Valence Override option. This allows the player to freely swap between all available bonus damage types. This does not affect the damage percentage.

### Valence Fusion

This section is transcluded from Valence Fusion § Adversary Weapons. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.

Valence Fusion combines two of the same Kuva, Tenet, or Coda weapons into one with a higher Valence Bonus, multiplying the highest bonus of the two weapons by 1.1, rounding the result down to the first decimal place, and capping at 60%. Valence Fusion can be found under the Actions button while modding a weapon. After selecting Valence Fusion, a player is required to choose another of the same Kuva/Tenet/Coda weapon from the inventory to fuse it with (weapons in the Foundry or equipped by a converted Kuva Lich or Sister of Parvos are not eligible donors). If the weapon's Valence element differs, the player will be prompted to choose between the two available elements.

The final value of the Valence Bonus after performing Valence Fusion is given by the formula:

Final Bonus=min(1.1×max(Bonus1,Bonus2),60)

The result is rounded down to the first decimal place. If the post-Fusion bonus is greater than or equal to 58%, the value is rounded up to 60%. This means that fusing with a copy that has a Valence Bonus of at least 52.8% will result in a perfect 60% bonus.

Any other properties (Orokin Catalyst, Forma, Stance Forma, Exilus Weapon Adapter, Focus Lens, or Elemental Vice) do not transfer from the copy that is sacrificed to fuel the fusion, meaning the order of fusion is important. One should enter the Upgrade UI for the weapon they want to use, then pick the weapon they want to sacrifice from the Valence Fusion UI.

After selecting the donor weapon to be used in the fusion, a confirmation screen displays the recipient weapon's new damage type and Valence Bonus, and warns that the transfer will consume the selected donor weapon. Players are then prompted to type FUSE to confirm the fusion.

- Example: Kuva Kohm (A) has 40% Heat Damage and an Orokin Catalyst. Kuva Kohm (B) has 52% Radiation damage. The player has two options: to fuse (A) into (B) or to fuse (B) into (A).
  - Since (B)'s 52% is higher than (A)'s 40%, the fusion will result in (B)'s bonus being multiplied by 1.1, resulting in 57.2%.
  - Fusing (B) into (A): Equip (A), enter the Upgrade screen, then Actions, then Valence Fusion, and select (B). The result of this fusion will be a Kuva Kohm with 57.2% Heat or Radiation (the player chooses which one) damage with an Orokin Catalyst. (B) will be destroyed.
  - Fusing (A) into (B): Equip (B), enter the Upgrade screen, then Actions, then Valence Fusion, and select (A). The result of this fusion will be a Kuva Kohm with 57.2% Heat or Radiation (the player chooses which one) damage without an Orokin Catalyst. (A) will be destroyed.

For Adversary Weapons and same-tier Railjack Armaments, the maximum % bonus (60%) can be achieved after the following number of fusions:

Minimum Initial Value | Maximum Number of Fusions
58.0% | 0 (automatically rounded up to 60.0%)
52.8% | 1
48.0% | 2
43.7% | 3
39.8% | 4
36.2% | 5
33.0% | 6
30.0% | 7
27.3% | 8
25.0% | 9

For example, a Tenet Envoy with a 40% Toxin bonus will need at most 4 Fusions to reach the 58% break point and be automatically rounded up to the maximum Valence Bonus of 60% (fewer will be needed if the donor Envoy has a higher Valence Bonus than 43.7%).

There are reports of 52.7% Valence Weapons fusing to both 57.9% and 60.0%. This may mean there are more than the displayed three significant figures tracked for each weapon. If so, then values just under those displayed in the table (i.e. 47.9% instead of 48.0%) may sometimes be sufficient.

## Notes

- The total number of Coda weapons is 14, requiring 70 Forma to Overlevel them all to max rank.
- An empty weapon slot is required to purchase a Coda weapon from Eleanor.

## Trivia

- While the 3 unique Coda weapons are created from Techrot assimilating 90s technology, the other Coda weapons are chosen from the "true" infested weapons composed purely of infested tissue, rather than the weapons created from the Infestation assimilating other factions' weapons or tools (e.g. Pupacyst or Mutalist Quanta).
