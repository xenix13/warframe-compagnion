Fuses with a Warframe to unlock the Exilus Mod Slot.—In-Game Description

The Exilus Warframe Adapter is a special item that can be fused with a Warframe to unlock an Exilus Mod Slot, which is a special additional slot that can be used solely for Exilus Mods. Each Warframe can only have a single Exilus Slot active, and any eligible mods used on the slot will consume mod capacity, like normal mods. An Exilus slot can be polarized using Forma, and its polarity can be swapped with that of another slot as of Techrot Encore.

## Acquisition

### Renewable

- Can be purchased from the Market for 20 Platinum 20 under Equipment → Components.
- A blueprint can be purchased from Cephalon Simaris after completion of the Natah Quest for 50,000 Standing 50,000.
- A blueprint can be purchased from Teshin as a Rank 5 Conclave offering for 75,000 Standing 75,000.
- A blueprint can be obtained from some Invasions.
- A blueprint can appear as a reward in either Tier 3 or Tier 4 of The Circuit on The Steel Path at a 4.43% chance.
- A fully built Adapter can be obtained from Sorties or Archon Hunts at a 2.5% chance.
- A fully built Adapter can sometimes be purchased from Acrithis's shop for 20 Pathos Clamps.
- Fully built Adapters can sometimes be obtained from Nightwave ranking rewards, or 1999 Calendar rewards.
- Fully built Adapters are also sometimes given out as Twitch drops on Devstreams.
- A Fully built Adapter can be obtained from Void Surplus

### Non-renewable

- A fully built Adapter is obtained automatically after completing the Natah Quest.
- A fully built Adapter is also rewarded after completing the final mission in A Man of Few Words Quest.
- A fully built Adapter is rewarded on the 150- and 450-Day Daily Tribute Milestones. It can also be obtained on the 50-Day Daily Tribute Milestone via the Awakened Luna Cache.
- The Exilus Cache (3x Exilus Warframe Adapter) is available as a reward after 1050-Daily Tribute Milestones as a rotation Evergreen Cache.
- A fully built Adapter was rewarded to players as a token of apology for server issues on 13th September 2015.

## How to Use

To use an Adapter on PC, click on a Warframe's locked Exilus Slot, which will open a prompt asking for confirmation to use the Adapter to unlock the slot. If a locked Exilus Slot is clicked without Adapters being available, a prompt will appear which will ask whether the player wishes to purchase an Adapter from the Market.

On Console, you must try to equip an Exilus mod on a locked Exilus slot with an Adapter in your inventory. If you do, a dialogue box will open asking if you want to fuse the adapter with the current Warframe. Once unlocked, you can change the polarity of the slot with Forma.

## Trivia

- The Adapter was introduced in Update 17.0 (2015-07-31).
- Adapters are sometimes referred to as tomatoes, similar to how Orokin Catalysts and Orokin Reactors are referred to as blue/golden potatoes.
- During Devstream 100 on November 3rd, 2017, a glitch resulted in viewers whose Twitch accounts were linked to their Warframe accounts receiving multiple in-game mails with fully built Adapters. It is reported that some players received as many as 30 Adapters.
- During the Natah quest Tyl Regor remarks that Exilus was a kind of "primordial Forma, an engine for self-manipulation".
