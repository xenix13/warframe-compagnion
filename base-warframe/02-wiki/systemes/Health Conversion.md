Health Conversion is a Warframe mod that grants additional Armor for every Health Orb picked up, up to a maximum of 3 stacks of bonus armor. Taking health damage when a stack is present will consume 1 stack after a 3 second delay.

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired from Cephalon Simaris for 100,000 Standing 100,000.

## Notes

- Picking up Health Orbs will grant 450 armor, stacking up to 3 times, for a maximum Armor bonus of 1350.
  - The Armor bonus is added to the Warframe's total armor and does not increase the Warframe's base armor.
    - Mods like Steel Fiber that multiply a Warframe's base armor do not increase the effects of Health Conversion, while those that multiply total armor, like Ironclad Charge, do.
  - The stacks are shown as glowing orange blocks orbiting the player's Warframe.
    - Tempered Benison shares its stack limit with Health Conversion, allowing up to 5 motes between the two buffs.
- Universal Orbs will also add an armor stack upon collection.
  - Energy Orbs picked up while using Equilibrium do not provide armor stacks.
- This mod allows Health Orbs to be picked up while at maximum Health and less than 3 stacks are present.
- Taking Health damage will consume a stack after 3 seconds.
  - Only damage dealt to health will consume a stack.
  - The 3 second stack consume timer is not affected by Ability Duration.
  - Only one stack can be consumed at a time. If additional health damage is taken within the 3-second consume time, no additional stacks will be consumed.
  - Health damage from using Garuda's Bloodletting, Hema's reload mechanism, or Nekros' Desecrate with Despoil augment will not consume a stack.
  - Health damage from Combat Discipline, however, will consume a stack.

## Tips

- Most effective on Warframes that can reliably spawn Health Orbs, such as:
  - Citrine's Fractured Blast
  - Dagath's Grave Spirit
  - Follie's Passive
  - Nekros' Desecrate
  - Nezha's Blazing Chakram and Reaping Chakram
  - Nova's Passive and Molecular Prime
  - Oberon's Reckoning
    - The bonus armor from Health Conversion is increased by Oberon's Renewal armor buff, and the damage invulnerability provided by his Righteous Negation Passive helps to preserve stacks.
  - Oraxia's Mercy's Kiss
  - Protea's Dispensary – refills 100 Health
  - Trinity's Pool of Life
  - Uriel's Passive
  - Voruna's Lycath's Hunt
  - A Sentinel/MOA (Companion) equipped with Synth Deconstruct
  - Broken Scepter interacting with organic corpses
- Effective on Warframes with Arcane Battery, with the mod granting an additional 405 energy maximum at max stacks with the Arcane.
  - However, losing a stack after health damage will also deplete energy overflow from Arcane Battery. Warframes with reliable shield gate or reliable health orb generation (or Overguard generation to avoid health damage entirely) are recommended for this synergy.
- Also effective with Primary Weapons using Primary Bulwark, allowing Warframes with low base Armor to easily achieve the maximum 500% damage buff.
  - Similar to Arcane Battery, losing a stack after health damage will cause a significant loss in primary damage output, so Warframes with similar orb generating ability or Overguard generation are recommended.

## Bugs

- Entering an Archwing in Free Roam areas (Orb Vallis, Cambion Drift and Plains of Eidolon) as well as entering the Arsenal in the Simulacrum causes Health Conversion's Armor stacks to persist visually or disappear entirely, being unable to be regained without being downed and self-reviving, leaving the open world zone and re-entering or swapping any weapon or Warframe and exiting the Arsenal. This bug also causes Arcane Battery and Primary Bulwark to be permanently unable to benefit from Health Conversion's Armor in open world zones if Health Conversion was active before entering an Archwing unless the player is downed and self-revives. Necramechs, Atomicycles and K-Drives in open world zones all do not appear to trigger this bug.
