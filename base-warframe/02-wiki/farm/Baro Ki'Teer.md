Baro Ki'Teer (alternative spelling: Baro Ki'teer), also known as the Void Trader, is a character known for his love of the exotic and the luxurious, especially those involving the Orokin Void. He involves himself with the Tenno by selling various rare artifacts and valuable items unavailable anywhere else.

Baro Ki'Teer makes appearances within the Concourse section of the Tenno Relays, although his presence is not constant; he makes appearances every two weeks, and is only available for trading for up to 48 hours after surfacing before he disappears once again. A countdown timer is displayed on all Orokin Ducats Kiosks in all relays, indicating the time until Baro Ki'Teer will next become available for trading. While the Void Trader is present on a relay, the countdown timer on the Ducat Kiosks changes to display the time remaining until he leaves.

The next visited relay's name is always shown in all Ducat Kiosks in all Relays, while 24 hours prior to his arrival additionally an icon will be displayed on the Star Chart near the relay where the Void Trader will be during his next visit, which will slowly move along the path to the relay during this period. Once he has arrived, players will receive a message from Baro Ki’Teer in their inbox (default Esc > COMMUNICATION > INBOX), notifying them as to which relay and on what planet he can be found. Players can also query Baro's status at any time (both prior and during his appearance), by sending when baro chat message in either Region or Relay Chat channels.

## Lore

### General

Little is known about Baro Ki'Teer aside from his conceited attitude, to the extent where he looks down on Tenno without Prime gear. In spite of this, Baro Ki'Teer has shown a willingness to aid the Tenno for a price, as he was the one who provided the data to construct the Archwings. Much of his dialogue heralding his arrival suggests that he has access to the Void and has entered it to locate rare treasures, an impressive feat indeed, considering the rarity of some of the items suggests a great deal of risk.

### Operation Cryotic Front

Referring to himself as a "Mystery Associate", Baro Ki'Teer anonymously contacts the Tenno, offering his assistance to combat the threat of the Balor Fomorians. He explains that he will give them the technology needed to defeat the Fomorians (as well as additional rewards for exceptional performance), but in exchange the Tenno must harvest Cryotic, which (at the time) can only be excavated on certain planets of the Solar System. As the Tenno collect Cryotic and get additional rewards, Baro Ki'Teer reveals that he needs the Cryotic to preserve the Martian Oasis Jellyfish, a delicacy that spoils easily during transport.

In a series of deals following the event, Darvo reveals Baro Ki'Teer's identity to the Tenno, commenting that he does this to make him appear mysterious to his clients. He reveals that he decided to sample the Jellyfish, exchanging a crate full of Prime blueprints and two Argon Crystals to do so, only for his throat to swell up for a few days as a result. This suggests that Darvo revealed Baro Ki'Teer's identity solely to spite him.

### Operation: Gate Crash

Shortly after the events of Operation Cryotic Front, the opening message for Operation: Gate Crash reveals that Baro Ki'Teer gave the Tenno a datamass containing information about an ancient piece of technology; the Archwing, the centerpiece of the Tenno's plans to defeat Vay Hek's Balor Fomorians. Whether or not Baro Ki'Teer was involved further in this operation is left unsaid.

### Sands of Inaros

Baro Ki'Teer requests the Tenno's assistance in raiding a temple located on Mars in search of treasure, derisively calling its previous colonizers (prior to Grineer takeover) "Sky Worshippers". As the Tenno explore the temple, a mysterious female voice begins narrating the legend of " Inaros the God King" who protected the Sky Worshippers from "Golden Skymen" and the Infestation, which begins to unnerve the Void Trader. The Tenno collect a Sacred Urn from the Temple, and Baro Ki'Teer urges them to fulfill the Urn's inscriptions to uncover more of the Temple's secrets.

As the Tenno explore further into the temple Ki'Teer becomes increasingly distressed by the narrator's story. Towards the end of the quest it is revealed that Baro Ki'Teer originated from Mars, and that the narrator's voice belonged to his late mother. Baro Ki'Teer was the sole survivor of a Grineer attack on his village, and despite praying to Inaros, the God King never arrived, resulting in his resentment towards his former home. At peace with his past, Baro Ki'Teer thanks the Tenno, remarking that the God King will rise again.

### Naberus

During the season of Naberus in 2025, Baro seemingly changed his fashion and mannerisms to celebrate the holiday, adorned in red and speaking in odd, nihilistic poetry instead of his more confident and transactional behavior. He arrived weekly instead of bi-weekly and his new "Eskhatos" offerings had strange prices ending in seemly-random digits instead of being rounded up to multiples of five.

At the end of the season all of the red-colored messages from Baro disappeared, and a new message from Baro was sent apologizing for missing all of his planned appearances in the previous month due to a malfunction in his ship's Void Drive, implying that the "Naberus Baro" was an imposter from the Void. Digital Extremes later confirmed that "Naberus Baro" is not the same person as the standard Baro.

On his return in 2026, Naberus Baro revealed four different future timelines he had traversed:

- One dominated by the Grineer, with the Elder Queen beautiful and ageless and the Worm Queen ever-loyal. He had conversed with Tyl Regor, and described him as a cultured man, a sculptor of marble and flesh, at whose touch disease fled. He had also witnessed the Elder Queen invoking the Continuity. However, their greed awakened an even greater greed, and the devourers of the weak were themselves devoured in turn. Baro left the Elder Queen, who wept as she tore out her eyes to avoid seeing The Indifference.
- One dominated by the Corpus, where the Sisters of Parvos remained powerful and the "Glass Eminence" recruited powerful mercenaries. The Corpus Serumancers had reverse-engineered Albrecht Entrati's Protoframe technology to anyone who could afford the proceedure; even Cephalon Parvos was obligated to pay the asking price. However, the pyramids of commerce and prosperity eventually toppled as Baro sipped tea in the empty Granum Void, contemplating the hubris of the merely human.
- One dominated by the Infested, laying waste to all in their path. Humanity was brought to the brink of extinction, but the two sanctuaries that remained were in conflict with each other despite the threat, as Baro sold weapons to both sides to profit. However, the leaders of the two sanctuaries were eventually slain, destabilizing their adherents who made a last-ditch attempt to ally but were overwhelmed by the monstrosities. The monsters were left to roam alone with no more to prey upon, which Baro described as "lasting peace".
- One dominated by an Orokin Empress (implied to be Executor Nitokh), with golden, bloodstained spires that pierced the heavens. The petitioners would line up before the Empress, offering their own flesh and committing self-mutilation to gain her favor. In a selection for a new court favorite, each candidate was covered in tar, with the unchosen set ablaze. However, as the line of supplicants decreased, the court eventually turned on itself; Baro left as the Empress and her consort fought each other, pondering what merit it is to subjugate only ashes.

After the end of the season, the real Baro returned, fearfully requesting that the Tenno not ask where he has been and wondering aloud whether everyone was "truly doomed". This hints that he may have seen the same timelines that Naberus Baro had visited.

## Trivia

- Baro Ki'Teer's name may be a play on the words "baroque attire", in reference to his trade of opulent cosmetics, artwork, and other goods.
  - Japanese players often refer to him as バロ吉 (Baro kichi), replacing the latter part of his name with the similar-sounding kanji meaning "good fortune."
- The Ducats he uses as exclusive currency are named after a real-life currency, made from gold or silver and used in Europe from the late Middle Ages up until the 19th century; by the 20th century they had become bullion coin used by traders and investors.
- Baro Ki'Teer will react to players differently if they have any Prime weapons or Warframes equipped, treating them somewhat more respectfully.
  - Prime Access accessories and armor do not trigger these variant lines.
  - He will also revere players using Inaros or Inaros Prime. He will still scoff in disdain at an Inaros that does not make a purchase, however.
- The in-game timer indicating how long until Baro Ki'Teer leaves the Relay is slightly inexact, often being off by a minute more or less.
- When Baro Ki'Teer arrives in a relay, he arrives in a blue flash that emits small particles.
- During TennoCon, Baro can be visited in a special relay accessible by anybody who purchases a ticket. His inventory contains almost everything he has ever sold.
  - During TennoCon 2026, Baro did not bring the Eskhatos equipment or related items from his Naberus appearance. This was explained in Devshorts 110 as Naberus Baro (or "Spooky Baro") not being the same person as the original Baro.
- Prior to Hotfix 31.7.1 (2022-07-28), Baro Ki'Teer's inventory was separate between PC and PS4/XBox/Nintendo Switch users.
  - Following the inventory sync, Baro send an inbox message to all Tenno referencing a meeting with a "dashing stranger" who shook his hand to make a deal to "refine all [of Baro's] selves into a single perfect individual".
    - In much the same fashion as the Operator and Drifter, this "refined" Baro appears to have a remaining counterpart in the form of "Eskhatos" Baro.
- Naberus Baro wears Holsom Yurr's armor from Nova's Leverian entry. His icon in the Star Chart was also colored red instead of white.
  - On his 2025 final visit, he spoke the Requiem Poems for Lohk and Xata as well as Netra and Khra in reverse. Before leaving he also said "Rap. Tap. Tap.", a line commonly associated with Rell and The Man in the Wall.
  - Notably, all of Baro's inbox messages during this event were signed as Ki'Teer or, in the first week's message, as Ki-Teer; every other inbox message Baro sends is signed as Baro Ki'Teer.
  - He does not have special dialogue to players using Inaros or Inaros Prime.
    - Baro Void-Signal still appear to a player with Inaros Prime equipped as usual.
- The doomed future dominated by the Corpus mentioned by Naberus Baro was first hinted at in Jade Shadows: Constellations, with Ryoku and Vena both being Protoframes from the future and the quest alluding to the existence of a "Cephalon Parvos".
