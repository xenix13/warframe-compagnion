# Archon Mods (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Mods

Archon Mods

- For the set mods that grant additional bonuses when multiple mods from the set are equipped, see Archon Set Mods.

Archon Mods are alternative versions of Warframe Mods. They have higher maximum drain than their original counterparts but with stats equivalent to their primed counterpart (if any), and possess unique effects related to Warframe abilities. They are classified as Legendary Mods, and thus their rank-up Credit and Endo costs follow other Legendary mods.

## Acquisition

They're sold by Chipper of Kahl's Garrison for 40 Stock, requiring players to be at Rank 3 - Fort to purchase.

## List of Archon Mods

- Archon Continuity
- Archon Flow
- Archon Intensify
- Archon Stretch
- Archon Vitality
