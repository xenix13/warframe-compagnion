Doubles the chance of resource drops.—In-game Description

The Resource Drop Chance Booster doubles the chance of resource drops. The increased drop table chance is capped at 100% and cannot go any higher (i.e: double rolling is impossible).

## Acquisition

Resource Drop Chance Boosters can be bought from the Market with Platinum. A 3-day booster costs 40 Platinum 40, a 7-day booster costs 80 Platinum 80, and a 30-day booster costs 200 Platinum 200.

Can be obtained randomly from Daily Tribute. The booster's duration (in hours) depends on the total amount of login days: (3 × [1 + Day ÷ 100]).

A 3-day booster can be obtained as a reward from Sorties.

A 1-day booster can be obtained as a reward from the Void Surplus offering for sale by Baro Ki'Teer.

## Effectiveness

- Boosters only applies to in-mission pickups. It will not apply to end-of-mission rewards.
- It will increase the number of Fish spawns in open worlds.
- It will increase the chance of a Mining Vein spawning as a gem vein (blue), at the expense of reducing the number of ore vein spawn (red).
- It will increase the chance of Dojo Color Pigments dropping.

## Notes

- A Resource Drop Chance Booster becomes active immediately upon acquisition and runs out in real-time (not time present in game).
- The booster is time-cumulative, thus acquiring additional boosters will add up on the duration timer.
- The whole team will benefit from the drop chance boost (like the Mod Drop Chance Booster) regardless of who performs the kill and how many boosters are active within the squad (thus not stackable by multiple player boosters).
- The doubled resource drop chance in Steel Path missions can be amplified to at the utmost of 4x with a Resource Drop Chance Booster (regardless of how many boosters are active in a squad).
- It synergizes with the Resource Booster resulting in up to 4x effective resources acquired.

## Patch History

Update 29.0 (2020-08-25)

- Fixed Resource Drop Chance Boosters not affecting Riven Slivers.

Hotfix 24.2.15 (2019-02-15)

- Made drop chance/amount Boosters apply to all scans that yield Resources.

Update 22.12 (2018-02-09)

- Resource Drop Chance Boosters now affect Mining and Fishing, doubling the chance to get gems (denoted by blue ore veins) and the base number of fish spawns! (Previously only regular Resource Boosters affected the Plains).

Update 15.11 (2015-01-21)

- Resource boosters now apply to Dojo color pigment drops. (Note: because pigments drop at 100% from their enemy types, for pigments, drop chance boosters are treated as drop amount boosters)

Update 13.0 (2014-04-09)

- Fixed Resource boosters not applying exclusively to Resource acquisition.

Update 12.0 (2014-02-05)

- Reworked menu profile bar to accommodate new boosters.

Update 11.0 (2013-11-20)

- Introduced.
