# Primed Mods (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primed_Mods

Primed Mods

Primed Mods are enhanced versions of their original non-primed mod counterparts, featuring additional ranks and with it greater bonuses.

These mods cannot stack with their standard counterparts, though they can stack with mods that provide the same bonus (i.e. Primed Continuity cannot stack with its standard counterpart, but it can stack with Constitution and/or Narrow Minded and receive penalties from Fleeting Expertise).

## Acquisition

Most Primed Mods are sold from Baro Ki'Teer. Four Primed Mods are only available as Daily Tribute rewards: Primed Fury, Primed Vigor, Primed Shred, and Primed Sure Footed. These are offered on Days 200, 400, 600, and 900, allowing you to choose one of the three available from that set.

As they are Legendary Mods, keep in mind that trading them will cost the buyer 1,000,000 Credits 1,000,000 in trade tax. Primed Mods obtained as Daily Tribute rewards cannot be traded.
