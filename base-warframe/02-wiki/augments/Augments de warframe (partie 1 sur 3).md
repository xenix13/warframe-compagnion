## Abating Link

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Abating_Link

Link Augment: Reduces Armor Rating by 60% on enemies targeted by Link.

Abating Link is a Warframe Augment Mod for Trinity's Link that reduces the Armor of all connected enemies.

## Stats

1 | 40% | 7
2 | 50% | 8
3 | 60% | 9

## Acquisition

## Notes

- Armor reduction is affected by Ability Strength, fully removing Armor at 167% Ability Strength.
- Armor reduction is temporary, enemies will regain their Armor when no longer tethered.

## Tips

- With one player using Corrosive Projection, 100% Armor reduction can be achieved at 137% Ability Strength.

## Accumulating Whipclaw

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Accumulating_Whipclaw

Whipclaw Augment: Hitting 3 enemies will grant a 35% stacking Damage Bonus to subsequent Whipclaws. Bonus will decay after 10s.

Accumulating Whipclaw is a Warframe Augment Mod for Khora's Whipclaw that grants a stacking damage bonus to subsequent Whipclaws upon hitting at least 3 enemies in a single cast, which will decay after 10 seconds.

## Stats

Rank | Damage Bonus | Decay Delay | Cost
0 | +10% | 10s | 6
1 | +15% | 10s | 7
2 | +20% | 10s | 8
3 | +35% | 10s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Exalted under the Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.
- This mod can periodically be acquired from The Circuit.

## Notes

- Damage bonus is additive to the one provided by Ability Strength.
  - This diminishes the effectiveness of modding for Ability Strength alongside the Augment.
- Damage bonus caps at 350%, or after hitting 3 or more enemies 10 times.
  - Whipclaw itself must hit the enemies to get a bonus: hitting them through Strangledome's range extension alone does not count.
  - Only one stack is granted per cast of Whipclaw; hitting 6 or more enemies will not grant additional stacks.
- Damage bonus decays at a rate of 2.5% per second.
- Damage bonus and decay rate are not affected by mods.
- Damage bonus resets after falling or being nullified.
- The -2.5% per second decay starts after the 10 second "grace period" you get after hitting 3 enemies with one Whipclaw. Meaning that, after those first 10 seconds, it would take another 14 seconds for it to gradually remove 35% worth of buffs.
- The long decay time makes it very easy to keep this buff maxed or near maxed for an entire mission in most cases.

## Aegis Gale

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Aegis_Gale

Aegis Storm Augment: Balefire has an Alternate Fire during Aegis Storm. The blast deals 15% of Hildryn's Max Shield as additional damage.

Aegis Gale is a Warframe Augment Mod for Hildryn's Aegis Storm that grants Balefire an Alternate Fire during flight, firing a volley of projectiles whose damage scales with Hildryn's max Shields.

## Stats

Rank | Additional Damage from Max Shields | Cost
0 | 9% | 6
1 | 11% | 7
2 | 13% | 8
3 | 15% | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Partner under The Perrin Sequence, or the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Alternate Fire has no charge time, immediately beginning a burst-fire volley of 4 projectiles. Each individual shot in the volley has the same stats as an uncharged Balefire shot, including:
  - 100 shield cost (400 per volley).
  - 500 base damage plus a bonus 15% of Hildryn's max shields, all dealt as pure Electricity damage.
    - Bonus damage is added to the shot's base damage before weapon mods are applied, and thus can be increased by mods such as Hornet Strike and Heated Charge.
    - Bonus damage is calculated upon casting Aegis Storm, snapshotting Hildryn's current ability strength and maximum shield capacity. Does not consider Overshields.
  - 5% critical chance.
  - 1.5x critical multiplier.
  - 10% status chance.
- Has an approximate Fire Rate of 1.6 shots per second when constantly holding down the alt-fire button, affected by fire rate bonuses.
  - There is a delay of approximately 0.4s between each shot in the volley, and 1.3s between the last shot in one volley and the first shot of the next, giving a total time of 2.5s per 4 shots.

## Tips

- Arcane Expertise synergizes incredibly well with this augment. It will cause ability strength to increase both her maximum shields and the alt-fire's scaling with max shields, giving the alt-fire quadratic scaling with strength.
  - Single use buffs like Energy Conversion and Empower will increase Hildryn's max shields while active. Both the shield and strength bonus will be snapshotted upon casting Aegis Storm and applied to the alt-fire's damage for the duration of the ability cast.
- Precision Intensify also has amazing synergy. When Balefire is summoned by casting Aegis Storm, Balefire will receive the massive strength bonus from the mod. The strength bonus also gives a large increase to the max shield scaling on the alt-fire.
  - When combined with Arcane Expertise, the strength bonus will be converted into +90% max shield capacity whenever Aegis Storm is Hildryn's currently selected ability (by default Mouse Wheel on PC). This grants a huge increase to both max shield capacity and max shield scaling at the same time.
- Primed Redirection on its own is a huge increase to the alt-fire damage, but also makes each point of ability strength an even more potent damage increase.
- As the alt-fire has an extremely sluggish fire rate, some form of fire rate bonus is recommended, such as Arcane Velocity or Anemic Agility (or at least Lethal Torrent).
- Like with Balefire's main firing mode, the alt-fire suffers from an incredibly low critical chance. Secondary Enervate can allow it to make use of critical hits, granting a large damage multiplier when combined with a critical damage bonus like Primed Target Cracker or Tenacious Bond.

## Airburst Rounds

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Airburst_Rounds

Airburst Augment: Each enemy hit by Airburst increases secondary damage by 40% for 14s.

Airburst Rounds is a Warframe Augment Mod for Zephyr that provides a damage bonus for secondaries for every enemy hit with Airburst.

## Stats

Rank | Damage bonus per enemy | Duration | Damage Bonus Cap | Cost
0 | +10% | 5s | 500% | 6
1 | +20% | 8s | 500% | 7
2 | +30% | 11s | 500% | 8
3 | +40% | 14s | 500% | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Flawless in New Loka or the rank of Exalted under Red Veil and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Total maximum damage bonus is capped at 500%.
  - Maximum damage bonus is not affected by Ability Strength.
- Damage bonus per enemy hit is affected by Ability Strength.
  - Enemies struck by both the Airburst projectile directly and the explosion will provide twice the bonus per enemy hit this way.
- Damage bonus additively stacks with base damage mods such as Hornet Strike.
- Duration is affected by Ability Duration.
- Duration can be refreshed by recasting the ability and hitting at least one enemy.
  - Refreshing keeps the highest bonus between the old and new Airbursts.
- Applies to Hildryn's Balefire Charger, Dante's Noctua, and Jade's Glory.
- Does not apply to Mesa's Regulators and Titania's Dex Pixia.

## Tips

- Cast an Airburst vortex once to group enemies to strike them all directly with a second Airburst, maximizing the bonus per enemy.

## Assimilate

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Assimilate

Absorb Augment: Nyx can use weapons during Absorb and its duration is infinite, but it consumes 6.5 Energy/s and its area of effect is halved.

Assimilate is a Warframe Augment Mod for Nyx that changes Absorb into a channeled ability and allows use of weapons and basic maneuvers like rolling, at the cost of reducing Absorb's radius by half.

## Stats

Rank | Energy Drain per second | Cost
0 | 11 | 6
1 | 9.5 | 7
2 | 8 | 8
3 | 6.5 | 9

## Acquisition

- Can be acquired by attaining the rank of Maxim under the Arbiters of Hexis, or the rank of Flawless under New Loka, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.
- This mod can periodically be acquired from The Circuit.

## Notes

- Players can perform the revive action, fire primary/secondary weapons, melee attacks, jump, and interact with objects (hacking terminals, power cells, etc.) during Assimilate.
- While Assimilate is active, the invulnerability bubble extends to robotic companions (such as Sentinels, MOAs, and Hounds) floating or attached near Nyx, rendering them completely immune to damage and status effects.
  - Beast companions (such as Kavats and Kubrows) do not stay fixed inside the bubble when pathing away from Nyx, so they can still take damage.
- Self-stagger from explosive weapons like the Lenz or Ogris will notapply when this augment is active.
- Assimilate will end if Nyx runs out of energy, or if deactivated by pressing the ability key again (default 4 ).
  - Nyx cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, Wellspring, Rage, and/or Hunter Adrenaline while Assimilate is active.
  - The energy drain from Assimilate will not be converted to Shields by Brief Respite or the Augur Mods.
  - Energy can be replenished by Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Equilibrium, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, and Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes while the ability is active.
- Allows ally bullets and projectiles to pass through Absorb.
- Ally bullets and projectiles that passed through Absorb with Assimilate have their Critical Damage doubled, similar to Electric Shield.
  - Critical damage bonus doesn't stack with Electric Shield.
  - Doesn't apply to Nyx' own attacks.

## Tips

- Rift Strike can be used to reach distant enemies and gain ground, though players will need to perform Heavy Attacks.
- Electromagnetic Shielding can be used to absorb some of the damage that nearby allies receive while also retaining a fast movement speed by using Final Harbinger's Dark Light combo.
- Some melee weapons and combos may be used to bypass the movement penalty, such as the Blind Justice Nikana stance's Guiding Light combo.
- Using Dispatch Overdrive will grant you more movement speed during Assimilate.
- Switching to the Operator or Drifter can allow the player to use Void Dash and get to objectives quicker while still keeping Assimilate active on Nyx.
- While equipping Assimilate, casting Absorb during Aim Glide will not stop or slow down the maneuver until Nyx touches the ground.
- As Nyx cannot slide or sprint during Assimilate, negative % Friction mods such as Cunning Drift, Maglev, and Streamlined Form or Sprint Speed mods such as Rush or Speed Drift provide no benefit.
- Assimilate prevents the health loss from Combat Discipline, while still being able to heal allies and trigger Arcane Avenger.
- Amalgam Barrel Diffusion will increase roll distance and speed.
- As Assimilate still allows the use of Nyx's other abilities, mobility related subsumes like Reave, Wrathful Advance, Fire Walker or Infested Mobility may be of great use.

## Biting Frost

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Biting_Frost

Frost gains +200% Critical Chance and +200% Critical Damage against frozen enemies.

Biting Frost is a Warframe Augment Mod for Frost's passive, gaining increased Critical Chance and Critical Damage on enemies frozen by 10 Cold status effects.

## Stats

Rank | Crit Chance | Crit Damage | Cost
0 | +130% | +130% | 6
1 | +150% | +150% | 7
2 | +175% | +175% | 8
3 | +200% | +200% | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Critical Chance and Multiplier bonuses apply to all weapon types, including primary, secondary, and melee.
  - Bonuses are not affected by Ability Strength.
  - Bonuses are additive to similar mods such as Point Strike and Vital Sense.
- Effects that rapidly apply 10 Cold stacks include:
  - Frost's Freeze's direct hit
  - Frost's Snow Globe's activation while within its range
  - Frost's Avalanche
  - Gauss' Thermal Sunder double cast (Helminth subsumable) or with Redline active
  - Voruna's Fangs of Raksh (applies 5 random status effects which may include Cold)
  - Itzal's Cosmic Crush with Cold Snap augment
  - Cyte-09's Neutralizer's alternate fire grenade
- Enemies are not "frozen" by:
  - Petrify
  - Condemn
- Also affects abilities which are treated as weapons, such as Breach Surge sparks.
  - Most of these abilities have 0% base critical chance, meaning the critical chance buff has no effect.
    - The critical damage bonus can still be taken advantage of when combined with flat critical chance bonuses that affect abilities, such as Arcane Avenger and Covenant.
- Enemies that have status caps, such as units with Overguard active or Acolytes will not be affected by Biting Frost as they cannot be frozen.

Metamorphosis Augment: Equinox's other half breaks free for 10s, dealing 300% Damage.

Duality is a Warframe Augment Mod for Equinox that causes her opposite form to split from her when casting Metamorphosis, creating a Specter armed with the weapon Equinox was using during casting.

## Stats

Rank | Duration | Damage | Cost
0 | 7s | 100% | 6
1 | 8s | 150% | 7

## Acquisition

- Can be acquired by attaining the rank of Maxim under the Arbiters of Hexis, or the rank of Flawless under New Loka, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- The duration of the specter is affected by Ability Duration.
- The damage output of the specter is not affected by Ability Strength.
- The specter is an allied NPC that copies the weapon Equinox had equipped before casting Metamorphosis, and uses it to autonomously attack enemies.
  - Only one Duality specter from the player can exist at a time. If Metamorphosis is cast again before the specter's duration expires, the specter will die immediately before a new specter is spawned.
  - The specter is counted as a summon and is affected by Summoner's Wrath, Arcane Camisado, and Theorem Infection.
    - Summoner's Wrath acts as a faction damage bonus, stacking multiplicatively with the damage bonus from Duality and additively with faction damage mods on the specter's equipped weapon.
  - The specter will not use Warframe Abilities.
  - The specter will not swap between different weapons.
  - On weapons with an Alternate Fire mode, only the primary mode will be used.
    - Weapons with multiple modes, like Kuva Hind and Hystrix, will always be used in the mode that they start the mission in, regardless of which mode is being used before casting Metamorphosis.
    - The specter cannot use incarnon modes of weapons. However, the normal mode will benefit from relevant incarnon perks.
  - The specter is considered to be aiming down sights for the purpose of most weapon effects, such as AX-52's passive.
    - Sniper rifles are an exception as they will always be considered to be hip-fired, incurring the hip-fire accuracy penalty on sniper rifles.
  - The specter has slightly reduced accuracy (most obvious on perfectly accurate weapons like Dera Vandal).
  - The specter has its own magazine for ammo, but will deplete the player's ammo reserves upon reloading.
    - However, the specter can reload for a full magazine even if there is 0 ammo reserve left.
    - Since the specter only consumes ammo upon reloading, if it dies before it can finish the reload animation (e.g., Metamorphosis is cast again), no ammo will be consumed from the ammo reserve.
  - The specter will sometimes intentionally collect ammo drops for the player, which restore the player's ammo reserve.
  - The specter has a targeting range of 150 meters; it will not attack enemies further away than this even if it has line of sight to them.
    - Similarly, even if enemies are within this range, if the specter cannot detect them via its senses, the specter can end up in states where it will neither seek nor attack enemies; the specter can even forget about enemies that it has already attacked if it can no longer see them due to terrain.
  - The specter will teleport to the player if it is more than 50 meters away for 5 seconds and not currently in combat.
  - Kills made by the specter contributes to Warframe affinity and has no effect on weapon affinity.
- The specter's health, shields, energy max, and other base stats will mimic Equinox or Equinox Prime's, and can be boosted by mods and other bonuses.
  - Sprint speed is an exception. The specter will always have Equinox's base sprint speed of 1.15, unaffected by sprint speed increases. However, it will be affected by movement speed bonuses such as Arcane Camisado or Speed.
  - The specter also has 200 base armor, rather than Equinox's 135 or Equinox Prime's 160. This is actually due to the 200 armor minimum for non-player units. Armor bonuses like Steel Fiber on the specter will scale with this value rather than Equinox's own armor.
- The specter inherits effects from many different sources on the player's loadout, primarily Mods equipped on Equinox and her weapons, but also some Arcanes, Archon Shards, weapon passives, and Incarnon perks.
  - Permanent / passive stat bonuses will always apply to the specter, such as Arcane Expertise, Serration, and Azure Shard buffs.
  - Permanent unique effects will usually apply to the specter, such as Reflection, Arcane Ice, or Primary Acuity.
  - Temporary bonuses will not be applied to the specter, such as Adaptation, Galvanized Mods, and Primary Merciless. However, any permanent bonuses from these sources will still work on the specter, such as the permanent 80% Multishot on Galvanized Chamber or the 30% Reload Speed on Primary Merciless.
    - The specter can trigger certain temporary bonuses for the player, but the specter itself will not benefit from them, such as Arcane Fury and Arcane Trickery.
  - Conditional effects will sometimes apply to the specter if their buff does not have a limited duration, but instead activates and deactivates when a condition is met. For example, Cascadia Overcharge will take effect any time the specter has overshields, and Hata-Satya will stack up its bonus until the specter reloads.
  - The specter does not inherit any self-only buffs like Xata's Whisper that Equinox had on her before the specter was spawned, nor does the specter copy any self-only buffs that are cast while the specter is active. However, AoE buffs like Roar or Warcry can be applied to the specter by casting them while the specter is active.
- The specter works somewhat differently with melee weapons compared to guns:
  - The specter's 3x damage multiplier does not apply to melee weapons (bug). Instead, it will receive the +700% stealth damage bonus, regardless of enemies being aware of Equinox or the specter itself.
    - As the stealth damage bonus stacks additively with critical damage, critical hits are less valuable for the specter. For example, a weapon with 3x critical damage will deal 8x damage on a stealth attack and 10x on a critical stealth attack.
  - The specter will only perform the neutral combo of whatever stance is equipped on the weapon.
  - The specter does have its own combo counter. It does not copy the player's current combo count when spawned, instead spawning with 0 combo count.
    - Is affected by initial combo, combo count chance, and combo duration bonuses.
    - Combo scaling mods such as Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds do not work on the specter, despite it having a combo count.
  - The specter may perform heavy attacks on low health enemies, specifically when they are within the mercy kill threshold. The heavy attack's damage will scale with the specter's current combo count, and deplete its combo count afterwards.
    - Is affected by heavy attack efficiency and heavy attack wind-up speed bonuses.
    - Cannot trigger Tennokai.
    - Can trigger various unique heavy attack effects from weapons like Syam or Zenistar.
    - Will perform repeated heavy attacks against enemies blinded by certain sources, specifically Radial Blind and Blinding Reave. Other blinds will not trigger this behavior, including Desiccation and Breach Surge.
  - The specter will perform ground finishers on knocked down enemies.
    - Is affected by finisher damage bonuses.
  - The specter will attempt to block incoming attacks from in front of it while approaching enemies.
- Can benefit from the increased critical damage from Cold procs and critical chance from Puncture procs, as it deals weapon damage instead of ability damage.
- If the specter wields a sniper rifle, it will spawn with 0 sniper combo but be able to build up combo by landing shots, taking advantage of the sniper damage multiplier in the process.
- The specter can benefit from the Condition Overload effects of Cedo, Secondary Shiver, Condition Overload, and incarnon perks (such as Burston incarnon's Fatal Affliction). However Galvanized Aptitude, Galvanized Shot, and Galvanized Savvy cannot be triggered by the specter.
  - Guns with additive CO will have the CO damage bonus not be affected by the clone's 3x damage multiplier, giving the CO bonus reduced effectiveness.
    - For example on Cedo, each pellet deals 30 damage normally, so 90 for the clone. With Cedo's innate +60% damage per status, an enemy with 3 statuses should take 90 * (1 + 0.6 * 3) = 90 * 2.8 = 252 damage. Instead, they will only take 90 + 30 * (0.6 * 3) = 90 + 30 * 1.8 = 144 damage. The CO bonus scales with 30 damage rather than 90.
  - Guns with multiplicative CO will behave multiplicatively as expected, with the specter getting the full effectiveness.
  - Condition Overload on melee weapons will correctly apply additively (to normal swings) or multiplicatively (to certain projectiles), getting the full effect in both scenarios.
- If a Gear item such as a Codex Scanner or Archgun Deployer is equipped when Metamorphosis is cast, the specter will spawn with the equipped Primary Weapon, regardless of which weapon was most recently used. If no Primary Weapon is equipped, then the specter will spawn with the equipped Secondary Weapon. If no Primary or Secondary Weapon is equipped, then the specter will spawn with the equipped Melee weapon.
- The specter will gradually take damage if it walks within range of a Nullifier Bubble or a Nul Comba/Scrambus aura.
- Evensong's 7 forced Puncture procs will not be applied from shots fired by the specter.

## Tips

- The specter is spawned from the casting Equinox at the same time as the mode shift. Using this, it can be dropped into groups of enemies, shed while sliding, etc. In this way, it can be used as a lethal decoy or expendable flanking ally.
- The specter can't cause harm to itself or the casting Equinox using explosive weapons. As a result, it can be effective to equip it with a powerful AoE weapon such as the Kuva Zarr, cast Metamorphosis, and create the clone as a temporary fire support, acting as a decoy when necessary to group enemies.
- The specter spawns with the same Health, Shields, Armor and Energy values as the player character at the start of the mission, or "on spawn." Because of this, combining Primed Flow and Preparation can allow the specter to make great use Primary Overcharge or Quick Thinking.
- Combining Catalyzing Shields for a static 1.33s shield gate with Fast Deflection and Vigilante Vigor for reduced shield recharge delay can allow the specter to consistently survive an occasional shot, regardless of enemy level.
- Primed Sure Footed will make the specter completely immune to knockdowns, improving its survivability and allowing it to output uninterrupted gunfire or melee attacks.
- Summoner's Wrath acts as a 75% faction damage bonus, drastically increasing the specter's damage, especially from status procs.
- Subsumed Well of Life helps the specter in a number of ways, giving it free access to knockdown immunity, status immunity, healing, and a revive if it is killed (with 5 seconds of invincibility afterwards). The specter may target the Well of Life target, wasting the specter's limited lifespan. However, high ability range will allow the specter to benefit from the ability without it being close enough to get distracted by the invincible target.
- Subsumed Quiver can grant consistent stealth to the specter by shooting it with a stealth arrow, increasing its survivability.
  - Empowered Quiver can also grant it status immunity.
  - Arcane Crepuscular synergizes quite well, granting a flat +3.0x critical damage to the specter while invisible.
- Subsumed Radial Blind can blind nearby enemies and cause the specter to repeatedly heavy attack them when equipped with a melee weapon. This works well with strong heavy attack weapons such as Syam.
- Secondary Encumber as well as Emerald Archon Shard (Increase max stacks of Corrosive) fully works on the specter, making a great tool for both priming and armor stripping enemies.
- High fire rate on full auto weapons seems to negatively affect the accuracy of the specter. Utilizing a weapon with a semi-auto or charged Trigger Type may be beneficial to waste less ammo.

## Infernum

Infernum is a Warframe Augment Mod for Uriel's Infernalis that causes volleys of fireballs to be released during Infernalis's flight, with the volleys growing larger for a short time after collecting a Demonium Rune.

## Notes

- While flying with Infernalis, Uriel releases 4 fireballs per second which home onto targets in Line of Sight within 50 meters. Fireballs deal 1500 Heat direct hit damage, plus an additional 1500 Heat AoE damage in a 5 meter radius with a forced Heat proc.
  - The AoE damage has 100% damage falloff over its radius.
  - If there are no targets, fireballs will shoot in the direction of the aiming reticle with a ~45° spread to either side.
  - The fireballs will not target Nullifier Crewman-type enemies.
    - You can fly at the ground near a nullifier to aim the fireballs and damage the globe with explosions.
    - If another enemy is under a nullifier globe, the fireballs will target it, swirl around the orb unable to penetrate it, then eventually explode and damage the globe.

  - Duration is calculated at the time of pickup, allowing the use of Arcane Concentration.

  - Fireballs have 0% critical chance and 1.0x critical damage, requiring additive bonuses in order to gain any effect.
  - Both the direct hit and AoE damage have 0% status chance (although the AoE has a forced heat proc), so Nourish will only increase the direct hit and AoE damage. It will not allow for Viral procs nor will it increase the resulting heat status damage.
    - For example, at 100% ability strength a subsumed Nourish will increase the total damage of a single fireball from 6750 damage (1500 direct hit + 1500 AoE + [5s * 750 heat status damage per tick] = 6750) to 8100 damage (1.45 Nourish damage multiplier * [1500 direct hit + 1500 AoE] + [5s * 750 heat status damage per tick] = 8100).

  - Using flat critical chance buffs like Arcane Avenger, the fireballs may trigger Arcane Acceleration, Arcane Fury, Arcane Momentum, Arcane Tempo, and Arcane Velocity.
- Each fireball direct hit grants 1% Brimstone charge.
- The duration of the fireball buff when picking up runes is still affected by Uriel's modded Ability Duration even if Demonium has been replaced via Helminth, despite the rune's weapon buff being locked to 100% duration in this case.
- Uriel's Infernalis flight speed does not affect the cast rate of fireballs.
- Fireballs can hit weak points, allowing them to activate arcanes such as Arcane Consequence, Arcane Precision, and Arcane Rage.
  - The parkour velocity from Arcane Consequence will not be applied during flight however, requiring the flight to be stopped then restarted to gain the bonus.
  - "On headshot kill" and "on weakpoint kill" conditions cannot be triggered by the fireballs.

- For quick completion of low level missions, combine Arcane Arachne and Proton Pulse to gain a large fireball damage boost and massive Infernalis flight speed boost, both from a simple wall latch + wall dash maneuver.
  - Conversely, if one desires more Brimstone charge it is worth considering removing sources of parkour velocity so that more fireballs will be launched towards groups Uriel passes by.
- Archon Vitality can be used to increase the number of heat procs to two per fireball.
- If one intends to spend an extensive time flying, it is advisable to avoid free-roam companions such as Kubrows, Kavats, Predasites, or Vulpaphylas as they will spend much of their time attempting to catch up, unlike a sentinel.
  - Furthermore, usage of aerial mods such as Agility Drift, Aerial Bond, Aviator, Aerodynamic and so forth may be advisable in these conditions.
    - Note that kills from fireballs will not count for Aerial Bond's recovery timer reduction but the cold aura works and accumulates properly.
- Sacred Annihilation from The Descendia applies to the fireballs, allowing them to potentially instant kill targets with each hit.
- The runes from other Uriel's Demonium will also trigger the increased fireball rate.

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Maxim under Arbiters of Hexis, or the rank of Exalted under Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Gives each damage tick from a Light's Judgment well a 5% chance of spawning a Jade Light beam lasting 3 seconds, up to a maximum of 8 beams.
  - Beams cannot target most Bosses, except Acolytes.
  - Each enemy can only have one beam, but one beam can damage multiple enemies if they are close enough.
  - The arsenal only displays the duration rounded to the nearest full second, despite the unrounded number being used for the ability.
- The augment uses the well's stats as it is formed. In other words, the well snapshots Jade's Ability Strength and Ability Duration at the moment it lands on a surface, and those snapshotted values are used for the augment.
- The Jade Light beams initially deal 100 Heat damage with a guaranteed Status Effect, and each subsequent hit will deal double the damage of the previous one. Damage is dealt immediately as the beam spawns, then once every half second plus a 1-frame delay.
  - Damage is affected by Judgment, naturally boosting the initial base damage to 150.
  - Because of the delay, reaching an exact multiple of 16.67% Duration will not yield an additional tick of damage; a minuscule amount of extra Duration is required beyond that. For example, 100% Ability Duration will give a beam duration of 3s, but a tick of damage will not be dealt at the 3 second mark. With 101% Duration however, an additional tick will occur at the 3 second mark, for a total of 7 ticks instead of 6.
- Like the Jade Light Eximus, the beam comes from a destructible orb, though enemies will not deliberately target it. Nullifier bubbles can destroy the orb on contact.
  - Demolisher pulses will not destroy the orb.
  - Unlike the Jade Light Eximus, the orbs from this augment are attached to their target's exact position.
- The beams take on Jade's primary energy color, however, client players will always see the default light green of Jade Light beams (including for Jade herself if she is not host).

## Tips

- Enemies affected by multiple wells will take damage from each, which increases the amount of times the roll for a beam is performed.
- Due to the exponential growth of the beam's damage, focusing on Ability Duration can lead to higher damage values. Ability Strength shouldn't be completely ignored however, as it is still important for increasing the beam spawn chance.
  - Arcane Concentration increases the well's duration, even if Concentration was not active prior to casting, though the well must be formed before Concentration expires.
