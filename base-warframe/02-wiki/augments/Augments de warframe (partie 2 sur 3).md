## Larva Burst

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Larva_Burst

Larva Augment: Reactivate Larva to detonate and deal 600 Toxin Damage in a 8m radius. This damage and Status Effect stacks for every enemy grabbed by the Larva.

Larva Burst is a Warframe Augment Mod for Nidus's Larva that allows the player to detonate Larva by recasting the ability, dealing Toxin damage proportional to the enemies trapped in Larva beforehand in a small area of effect.

## Stats

Rank | Toxin Damage Per Enemy | Radius | Cost
0 | 300 | 2m | 6
1 | 400 | 4m | 7
2 | 500 | 6m | 8
3 | 600 | 8m | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under the Steel Meridian, or the rank of Partner under the The Perrin Sequence, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Explosion triggers upon reactivating Larva while it is active. Reactivation detonates Larva and does not cost energy.
- Upon detonation, Larva explodes in an 8m radius, dealing 600 Toxin damage multiplied by the number of enemies initially caught by Larva.
  - Explosion does not require line of sight and bypasses obstacles in the environment to damage all enemies in range.
  - Explosion has 100% Damage Falloff over its radius.
    - Has virtually no falloff against enemies caught by Larva, due to them being at the center of the explosion.
- Each enemy hit by the explosion will receive 1 Toxin Status Effect multiplied by the number of enemies initially caught by Larva.
  - The damage of each Toxin stack is equal to 50% of the explosion damage received by the enemy, dealt once per second for 6 seconds.
    - Damage falloff will reduce the explosion damage received by far away enemies, and thus the DoT damage from the resulting Toxin procs.
- On detonation, each remaining enemy inside Larva has a 50% chance to generate 1/5th of a Mutation stack. The stack chance is capped at 100% chance per enemy at 200% Ability Strength.
  - Nearby enemies hit by the explosion will not generate additional stacks, only enemies that are within Larva when it is detonated.
  - Captured enemies that are killed before detonation will not generate stacks upon detonation. However, enemies still have a 50% chance to generate 1/5th of a stack if they are killed by any source while captured by Larva (a feature of the un-augmented version of Larva).
- Explosion and Toxin DoT damage are not affected by Mutation stacks.
- Damage and Toxin stack count are calculated based on the number of enemies grabbed during the initial cast, meaning killing enemies caught in Larva will not reduce explosion or Toxin DoT damage.
- Grabbed enemies are briefly launched up in the air on detonation.

## Tips

- As both the explosion damage and Toxin stack count scale with the number of enemies caught, the total ability damage scales quadratically with the number of enemies.
  - While the ability can quickly dispatch large groups, it may sometimes be preferable to instead use Virulence on the enemies grouped up by Larva, as the ability can generate a significant amount of energy.
- Emerald Archon Shards can be used to increase the Toxin status damage from the explosion.
- Roar will increase the initial explosion damage and then further increase the Toxin status, drastically increasing the overall damage dealt.
- Particularly effective against Narmer due to their weakness to Toxin, as well as the Corpus due to Toxin ignoring shields.
- Ancient Disruptors will reduce the damage dealt, and Toxic Ancients will eliminate toxin damage from nearby Infested.

## Lasting Covenant

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lasting_Covenant

Covenant Augment: Headshot kills increase Critical Chance bonus duration by 3s.

Lasting Covenant is a Warframe Augment Mod for Harrow that increases Covenant's critical chance buff duration each time an enemy is killed with a weak point hit.

## Stats

Rank | Duration Increase | Cost
0 | 1s | 6
1 | 1.5s | 7
2 | 2s | 8
3 | 3s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Maxim under the Arbiters of Hexis, or the rank of Exalted under the Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.
- This mod can periodically be acquired from The Circuit.

## Notes

- Bonus duration awarded from weak points is affected by Ability Duration.
- The duration of an active critical chance buff cannot exceed three times the buff's base duration.
  - With an Ability Duration of 130%, Covenant would grant 15.6s of critical chance buff initially, and bonus time would cap at 46.8s.
  - Buff duration can be continually replenished up to the cap with additional weak point kills, allowing for unlimited duration as long as the user continues to land weak point kills frequently enough.

## Tips

- Synergizes well with Harrow's first ability, Condemn, as it puts enemies in a position that allows for easier headshots.

## Loyal Merulina

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Loyal_Merulina

Merulina Augment: Yareli no longer rides Merulina. Instead, Merulina follows her and casts Sea Snare on nearby enemies. 2s cooldown.

Loyal Merulina is a Warframe Augment Mod for Yareli that disables riding Merulina in exchange for it shooting Sea Snares at enemies as it follows her.

## Stats

Rank | Cooldown | Cost
0 | 5s | 6
1 | 4s | 7
2 | 3s | 8
3 | 2s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, or the rank of Flawless under the New Loka, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Cannot be equipped alongside Merulina Guardian.
- Yareli still receives the Damage Redirection as if she was still riding Merulina.
  - Merulina's armor no longer affects the redirected damage it takes. This includes armor gained from Azure Archon Shards.
- Yareli will lose her immunity to stagger status effects from enemy targets (such as Knockdown); however she will still be immune from self-inflicted stagger by weapons she uses. It's unclear if this is intentional or not, as riding Merulina normally provides complete status immunity, including knockdown immunity from enemies.
- Loyal Merulina's Sea Snares inherits the duration, range, damage per second, damage increase per second, and damage vulnerability stats of Yareli's own Sea Snares. This is still the case even if Yareli's Sea Snares ability is overridden by another Helminth ability.
  - Sea Snare cooldown is affected by Ability Efficiency, reducing the cooldown to a minimum of 0.5 seconds at 175% efficiency.
    - Ability Snap Shots Ability Efficiency on cast
      - Using Arcane Sculptor activated by Aquablades can provide max 175% efficiency
  - Duration of Sea Snares cast by Merulina are shown on Merulina's ability icon.
  - Unlike Yareli's version of the ability, Loyal Merulina's Sea Snares are cast one at a time rather than in groups of five.
  - Damage vulnerability from the Sea Snares from Loyal Merulina stack with regular Sea Snares from her 1 and is multiplicative like all forms of damage vulnerability.
  - Loyal Merulina's Sea Snares will grant stacks of Arcane Camisado, but does not scale with summon damage bonuses, like from Summoner's Wrath.

## Magnetized Discharge

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magnetized_Discharge

Magnetize Augment: Recast on the target to detonate. Enemies hit have a 50% chance to be disarmed. Passive: +45% Magnetize Range.

Magnetized Discharge is a Warframe Augment Mod for Mag's Magnetize that allows manual detonation by recasting the ability on an active Magnetize bubble. Enemies hit by the detonation have a chance to be disarmed. Additionally increases Magnetize's range.

## Stats

Rank | Disarm Chance | Bonus Magnetize Range | Cost
0 | 12.5% | 20% | 6
1 | 25% | 25% | 7
2 | 37.5% | 35% | 8
3 | 50% | 45% | 9

## Acquisition

- Can be acquired by attaining the rank of Partner under The Perrin Sequence, or the rank of Flawless under New Loka, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Press 2 while aiming at a Magnetize bubble to detonate it for no energy cost. Enemies caught in the explosion have a 12.5% / 25% / 37.5% / 50% chance to be disarmed by the blast.
  - Disarm chance is affected by Ability Strength. 100% disarm chance is achievable with at least 200% Ability Strength.
- Both manual detonation and Magnetize bubble duration expiring can trigger the disarm chance.
- Manual detonation will only detonate the targeted Magnetize bubble. If overlapping sections of 2 or more Magnetize bubbles are targeted, the closest bubble will be targeted and explode.
- If you are standing inside a bubble it will still make it explode even if you aim on a target outside the bubble.
- Passive range increase is additive with other Ability Range increases.

## Tips

- This augment is useful whenever hostile Nullifier Crews are expected to appear, to allow Mag to detonate the bubble early instead of letting it be cancelled out by a Nullifier bubble and waste the accumulated damage.

## Merulina Guardian

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Merulina_Guardian

Merulina Augment: Enemies eliminated during Sea Snares heal 20% of Merulina's health. Upon healing, gain +200% Reload Speed and Fire Rate on Secondary weapons for 20s.

Merulina Guardian is a Warframe Augment Mod for Yareli's Merulina that causes enemies killed while affected by Sea Snares to heal Merulina and grant increased Reload Speed and Fire Rate to secondaries for a duration.

## Stats

Rank | Healing | Reload Speed/Fire Rate | Duration | Cost
0 | 5% | +50% | 8s | 6
1 | 10% | +100% | 12s | 7
2 | 15% | +150% | 16s | 8
3 | 25% | +200% | 20s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, or the rank of Flawless under New Loka, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Heal is affected by Ability Strength.
- Buff duration is affected by Ability Duration.
- Reload Speed and Fire Rate bonuses are not affected by mods.
- Buff only triggers if Merulina isn't at full HP.

## Tips

- You can run out of ammo very quickly with the high fire rate and reload speed buff gained from activating the augment, especially on weapons that consume a lot of ammo/are not so ammo efficient by default, like the Atomos or the Twin Grakatas. To counteract this, it would be best to either slot Pistol Ammo Mutation/Primed Pistol Ammo Mutation in the exilus slot of your weapon, run Pistol Scavenger as your aura mod, or use Arcane Pistoleer if your weapon of choice is compatible.
  - Alternatively, you may consider firing your weapon in short bursts instead, to avoid potential ammo wastage.
  - Weapons with infinite ammo, such as Grimoire and Tenet Plinx, or charges, such as Incarnons, are able to bypass these ammo issues.
- The healing done to Merulina can trigger Archon Intensify.
- Can use the aura mod Combat Discipline to trigger the augment's bonuses consistently and without risk (in case there's no damage to receive from enemies attacking Yareli while riding Merulina), as the 90% damage reduction from Merulina minimizes the mod's health loss to only 1 point per kill.
  - The health damage to Yareli can be completely circumvented by using Combat Discipline at rank 2. While this means it provides 6 less mod capacity compared to a fully ranked counterpart, it prevents accidental deaths in missions where health orbs are scarce, or disabled entirely.
- Since semi-auto weapons have a fire rate cap of 10, any semi-auto with over ~3.33 base fire rate will not be able to use the full fire rate buff.
  - If Reinforced Bond and Amalgam Furax Body Count are installed, semi-auto weapons with a base fire rate above ~2.46 will have a portion of this buff wasted.
- Semi-Pistol Cannonade will prevent the weapon it is equipped on from receiving the 200% fire rate buff, but it will still benefit from the 200% reload speed.
- While the fire rate and reload speed buffs are not affected by mods, investing in Ability Strength is still beneficial. Since the damage vulnerability from Sea Snares scales with strength, investment in this stat will make it easier to kill an enemy caught by the snare and proc this augments buffs.

## Molecular Fission

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Fission

Molecular Prime Augment: Enemies hit by Null Stars are primed. When killed, primed enemies will restore a Null Star charge and have a 100% chance of restoring two.

Molecular Fission is a Warframe Augment Mod for Nova's Molecular Prime that makes primed enemies restore Null Star charges when killed and causes Null Star particles to inflict Molecular Prime.

## Stats

Rank | Chance to restore additional charge | Cost
0 | 25% | 6
1 | 50% | 7
2 | 75% | 8
3 | 100% | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.
- This mod can periodically be acquired from The Circuit.

## Notes

- At max rank, two charges are restored per kill.
  - Chance for additionally restored charges is not affected by Ability Strength.
  - Kills by Allies or Companions on primed enemies will replenish the null star particle count up to the cap.
- Number of charges is capped at the number you would get from using Null Star, which is affected by Ability Duration.
  - Maximum number of charges that Molecular Fission will restore is based on a snapshot of Ability Duration at the time of casting of Null Star, which includes temporal buffs like Ris Invocation or Arcane Concentration.
- Molecular Fission can produce a constant supply of null star particles over the duration of combat, providing a low effort addition to outgoing ability damage for as long as the player doesn't run out of active particles.
- Neutron Star may be better suited for quickly replacing large numbers of null star particles than Molecular Fission, with the added benefit of spreading large amounts of Heat and Blast.
- Particles will not be spawned unless at least one particle is active, requiring a minimum of one Null Star cast per mission.
- Null Star particles prime enemies with slowed or sped up version of Molecular Prime, depending on which version was used last. By default it has a slow effect.
  - The version will be changed right after casting Molecular Prime and doesn't require recasting.
- Can't be equipped on subsumed Null Star, as other Warframes don't have Molecular Prime ability.

## Parasitic Vitality

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Parasitic_Vitality

Parasitic Link Augment: Nidus and any ally he's bound to gain 4% Max Health per Mutation during Parasitic Link.

Parasitic Vitality is a Warframe Augment Mod for Nidus that allows Parasitic Link to grant both Nidus and his linked ally bonus Health based on their Base Health, scaling with each Mutation stack.

## Stats

Rank | Health Bonus | Cost
0 | 1% | 6
1 | 2% | 7
2 | 3% | 8
3 | 4% | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Partner under The Perrin Sequence, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Bonus Health scales with Mutations stacks, up to 200x at full mutation for +800% Health, and scales with Ability Strength.
  - This can be further increased by utilizing Abundant Mutation which increases Mutation stacks up to 500. This increases unmodded health by up to +2000%.
  - Bonus is additive to other sources of percentage health increases such as Vigor and Gladiator Resolve.
  - Bonus scales off of base Health, i.e. before any mods or other health bonuses such as Arcane Blessing or Azure Archon Shard's health bonus.
  - The number of Mutation stacks needed to be on par with a maxed Vitality at base Ability Strength is 25 . Increasing Ability Strength will lower this floor .
- Counts as a Vitality mod, thus cannot be used in conjunction with Vitality, Archon Vitality, or Umbral Vitality.
  - The ally linked however, will gain the bonus regardless of their mods.
- Nidus will still gain the bonus health even if linked to an enemy.
- When the link breaks, there is a 5 second countdown before Parasitic Vitality's health bonus disappears until Nidus links with a new target.
- When Nidus gains a mutation stack, his maximum will be updated to reflect the new value and he heals in the process, often enough for a full heal whenever gaining a stack.
  - The healing gained is modified by current health, max health modifiers other than Parasitic Vitality, new stack count, and strength. With a fully ranked augment, gaining a stack heals:
    - Healing received = Current Health × 4% × (1 + Ability Strength) × Mutation Stack Count × (Base Health ÷ Max Health without augment) .
  - This means that modifiers which increase max health, such as Vigor, Arcane Blessing, or Elemental Ward reduce the healing effect (Possible bug).

## Tips

- While this augment can provide Nidus and his linked ally with plenty of health, potentially up to the quintuple digits with proper modding, keep in mind that his innate health regeneration will not scale to account for the increased health. The easiest way to rectify this is for Nidus to regularly cast Ravenous, but other methods may also be considered:
  - Sources of percentage-based healing, such as Blood Altar, Arcane Grace, Arcane Victory, or Magus Repair.
  - Weapons or abilities with Life Steal, such as Gloom, Hirudo, or Winds of Purity.
    - Sancti Magistar can uniquely heal All allies within 15 meters making it a weapon of choice to sustain allies or companion affected by Parasitic Vitality all while healing Nidus and granting him 20% chance to resist Status Effect
  - The Companion mods Hunter Recovery and/or Synth Deconstruct, particularly on Companions that can damage whole groups of enemies at once such as Panzer Vulpaphyla with Viral Quills, or Hounds with Synergized Prospectus.
    - Hounds could also heal nearby players by copying Eximus powers via Null Audit. Potentially healing other player affected by Parasitic Vitality .
- Using Link Vitality will let Parasitic Vitality affect Nidus companion's Health even when he is linked to another ally or enemy.
  - Link Vitality seems to prevent the healing aspect of Parasitic Vitality, thus causing your companion's max health to be doubled from having both, but no healing to occur, leaving current health at a fraction of a percent of the new max health.

## Resonance Pulse Cooldown

In this section:

- The initial cast of Sonar will be referred to as a Sonar pulse.
- The subsequent pulse created by killing an enemy's highlighted weakspot will be referred to as a Resonance pulse.

When a Sonar marked enemy is killed and a Resonance pulse is released, Resonance pulse cannot be triggered again until the currently active Resonance pulse reaches its maximum range. Because the Resonance pulse has a fixed 20 m/s wave propagation speed, this means that increasing range will also increase the cooldown between Resonance pulses, as the pulse has further to travel before reaching its maximum range. A Resonance pulse can still trigger even if the Sonar pulse from the initial cast of Sonar is still active.

Resonance pulses have the same 35 meter range as Sonar, giving Resonance an effective base cooldown of 1.75 seconds, scaling inversely with range. For example, 34% range will give a cooldown of 0.6s between Resonance pulses, while 280% range would give a cooldown of 4.9s.

- Although there is no limit on the number of possible Sonar pulses active at once, there can only be one active instance of Resonance pulse.
- At times, the Resonance cooldown does not seem to apply if the two consecutive triggers are made by different players.

## Notes

- The duration of additional triggers is only affected by the remaining duration of the original sonar, so once the original's duration expires all consecutively created weak spots will expire as well.
- The effects of multiple pulses will stack, rather than overriding each other. Multiple pulses highlighting a single target can cover multiple body parts simultaneously, or sometimes cover the same body part multiple times. See Sonar for detailed highlight mechanics.
  - Resonance can generate spots over the Sonar's visual limit, it may have no limit of its own.
- Pulse range is the same as the base ability (35m) and is affected by Ability Range.
  - Each resonance pulse originates from the location of the target killed, effectively increasing the normal range of the ability if enemies are killed beyond the original casting point.
- Resonance can be triggered by Gas, Electricity, and Blast kills.
- Resonance can be triggered by Ability kills if the enemy torso was highlighted, as most abilities target the torso.
