## Ulfrun's Endurance

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ulfrun's_Endurance

Ulfrun's Descent Augment: During Ulfrun's attack, enemies that die from Slash Status within 20m restore Voruna's charges.

Ulfrun's Endurance is a Warframe Augment Mod for Voruna that restores Ulfrun's Descent charges whenever a nearby enemy dies from Slash status effect.

## Stats

Rank | Range | Cost
0 | 5m | 6
1 | 10m | 7
2 | 15m | 8
3 | 20m | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Exalted under Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Range is affected by Ability Range.
- Despite its description, the augment only restores a charge if the enemy directly targeted by the lunge attack dies instantly or due to the Slash procs from abilities such as Ulfrun's Descent or Fangs of Raksh while in range. This also applies to helminth abilities such as Aquablades, as long as the Slash proc is caused by an ability Voruna has cast. This includes inflicting a Slash proc with a weapon, then duplicating it with Sickening Pulse, since the duplicated procs are created by an ability. Slash procs made by ally abilities do not count for restoring charges.
  - Nearby enemies who die due to the radial Slash damage do not restore charges.

## Tips

- When targeting a durable enemy, it is best to wait for them to die due to the innate Slash proc instead of lunging for a second time to save charges.
- Equipping the augment is the only way to reach the maximum stacks of the damage multiplier, Critical Chance, and Critical Damage, as they can only be achieved after 5 direct kills.
- Using Fangs of Raksh can apply Slash procs, making it possible to regenerate charges without spending them, and can restore multiple charges due to the status effects spreading when the target dies.

## Untime Rift

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Untime_Rift

The Vast Untime Augment: Creates a Rift that connects to enemies suffering from Void Status Effect within 15m, doubling debuffs applied by The Vast Untime and maintaining Void Status.

Untime Rift is a Warframe Augment Mod for Xaku's The Vast Untime that creates a void rift which tethers to enemies affected by the Void status effect, pausing the duration of the status effect as long as they are within range, while also doubling the Void Damage Vulnerability and Slow effects from The Vast Untime.

## Stats

Rank | Range | Cost
0 | 9m | 6
1 | 11m | 7
2 | 13m | 8
3 | 15m | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, or the rank of General under the Steel Meridian, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Instead of doubling The Vast Untime's Damage Vulnerability, this augment applies a second instance of it, meaning enemies take 125% more Void damage instead of 100%.
- Because Xata's Whisper is the only way for Xaku to gain Void status, this ability is effectively mandatory to properly use this augment mod.
- Untime Rift will continue to tether to new enemies affected by Void status after the initial casting of The Vast Untime.
- The Slow is capped at 95%, with Untime Rift it can be achieved using less Ability Strength.

## Valence Formation

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valence_Formation

Passive Augment: Casting an Ability imbued with extra Elemental Damage applies that Element as a 200% bonus to your weapons with guaranteed Status for 20s.

Valence Formation is a Warframe Augment Mod for Lavos that allows weapons to gain the corresponding element of an ability whenever it is cast, with a guaranteed status effect.

## Stats

Rank | Elemental Damage | Duration | Cost
0 | 50% | 20s | 6
1 | 100% | 20s | 7
2 | 150% | 20s | 8
3 | 200% | 20s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Flawless under New Loka, or the rank of Exalted under the Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Upon casting an ability with an imbued element, grants a 200% Elemental Damage buff of the imbued element to all of Lavos's weapons for 20 seconds.
  - No buff will be granted when casting an ability with no element infused, or when casting Helminth abilities.
  - Only one elemental bonus can be active at a time. Casting another ability with a mixed element will replace it and refresh the duration.
- Bonus Elemental Damage will be added parallel to the weapon’s Elemental Damage, meaning it will NOT combine with elements on the weapon.
  - Damage bonus is additive to elemental damage bonuses like Heated Charge.
- Due to how status DoT damage is calculated, a Valence Formation buff for Heat, Electricity, Toxin, or Gas will also add 200% DoT damage to all procs of that specific element.
  - Blast damage is exempt, as its proc damage does not scale with elemental damage.
  - The bonus is additive to elemental damage bonuses like Heated Charge, and multiplicative to Faction Damage bonuses like Bane of Grineer and Status Damage bonuses like Rifle Elementalist.
  - The 200% bonus DoT damage also applies to matching status procs from all of Lavos's abilities, including Helminth abilities.
    - For example, a heat Valence Formation buff will triple the DoT damage from his Catalyze, heat-imbued Ophidian Bite, subsumed Thermal Sunder, etc. Note that the initial ability damage will be unaffected; only the resulting DoT will be increased.
    - Valence Formation buff is applied immediately on cast (before the ability finishes its animation), allowing the ability cast that grants the buff to also benefit from the bonus DoT damage (if applicable).
  - As the damage of the Gas status effect does not scale with equipped Heat and Toxin mods, Valence Formation is one of the few ways to increase its damage.
- Unlike most other buffs in the game, Valence Formation cannot be negated by nullifying bubble or enemy abilities, nor by jumping Out Of Bounds.
- Valence Formation is a Universal Weapon Bonus, meaning it not only applies to weapons but also to certain "weapon coded" abilities. Of the subsumable abilities, Valence Formation can give its elemental damage and guaranteed proc to Airburst (direct hits), Breach Surge, Null Star, Pyrotechnics, and Shuriken.
- Valence Formation is one of the only buffs in the game that affects the AoE explosion of Vile Discharge, though it will only add the guaranteed status effect, not the elemental damage.
  - However, the proc (except for Radiation) will affect Lavos if he is within range, unless he has Valence Block active to negate it.

## Tips

- This allows Lavos to freely deal with enemy weaknesses such as removing armor with Corrosive while wielding a Viral-based weapon, or dealing with shields by adding Magnetic damage.
- Shotguns are ideal weapons to utilize with this augment, as each pellet will gain the boosted damage and proc the element on every hit.
  - Conversely, beam weapons may be less effective than expected, as modding for multishot will not increase the number of hits.
- Allows you to trigger Melee Influence on demand without needing to mod Electric on your melee weapon.
- Allows you to generate chain reactions with Pax Seeker.
- Can damage the Profit-Taker Orb's shields, allowing Lavos to adapt when she changes her elemental weaknesses.
- In some cases, it can be beneficial to use an unranked version of this mod so that the innate status effects on the weapon are more likely to happen, while the element from Valence Formation will still be guaranteed on hit.
- While Valence Formation does alter status weighting, the selected status is still applied in addition to normal status procs, effectively letting you "double up" by modding status normally then stacking Valence Formation on top.

## Venari Bodyguard

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venari_Bodyguard

Venari Augment: Venari dies in Khora's place. Recovery timer increased to 150s. Killing enemies decreases the timer by 4s.

Venari Bodyguard is a Warframe Augment Mod for Khora's Venari that makes Venari die in Khora's place at the cost of an increased spawn timer, but every enemy killed decreases the spawn timer.

## Stats

Rank | Respawn Delay | Kill Time Decrease | Cost
0 | 150s | 1s | 6
1 | 150s | 2s | 7
2 | 150s | 3s | 8
3 | 150s | 4s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Exalted under Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Upon taking fatal damage, Khora becomes invulnerable for 3 seconds and has 50% of her health restored.
- Venari cannot be manually respawned after sacrificing herself. However, if she dies normally, she can be manually respawned.
- The kill and respawn timer, invulnerability duration and amount of health restored apparently can't be increased or decreased by mods.
- Considering the 150s cooldown and 4s cooldown reduction - you will have to kill ~37 enemies to immediately revive Venari and regain the Death Gate.
- This mod functions properly in Arbitrations, allowing a Khora player to have a buffer to being killed, as long as Venari is still alive.
- Oddly this mod is tagged as an augment for the Venari ability specfically, despite all of its effects relating to Khoras passive ability of simply having Venari and none of its effects synergising with the ability itself. This causes a minor gameplay issue, as if the ability is replaced via Helminth this augment cannot be equiped despite the fact it would function normally.

## Venom Dose

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Venom_Dose

Spores Augment: Hold to cast will grant all allies within 15m an additional 100% Corrosive Damage to their attacks for 40s.

Venom Dose is a Warframe Augment Mod for Saryn that allows Spores to be held on cast, creating a wave of energy traveling outward from the user that temporarily grants the caster and nearby allies additional Corrosive damage to all attacks.

## Stats

Rank | Corrosive Damage | Duration | Range | Cost
0 | 50% | 28s | 15m | 6
1 | 65% | 32s | 15m | 7
2 | 80% | 36s | 15m | 8
3 | 100% | 40s | 15m | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Exalted under Red Veil, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.
- This mod can periodically be acquired from The Circuit.

## Notes

- Damage bonus is affected by Ability Strength.
- Duration is affected by Ability Duration.
- Range is affected by Ability Range.
- Cannot be equipped at the same time as Revealing Spores.
- Adds Corrosive damage to the weapon, added alongside IPS and elemental mods.
  - Does not increase the damage of most abilities, including Exalted Weapons.
  - Does not merge with Toxin or Electricity.
  - For example: for a weapon with a base damage of 100 Impact per bullet, with the mod Infected Clip at max rank, and the buff from Venom Dose at max rank with 110 strength, the damage will be: 100 Impact, 90 Toxin, and 110 Corrosive.
    - In this situation, if a status procs, it will have 100/300 chance to be Impact, 90/300 chance to be Toxin, and 110/300 to be Corrosive.
- For more information on how this augment works, see Buff & Debuff page.
- Multiple casts of Venom Dose do not stack; the buff with the highest Ability Strength will take effect.
  - However if a weaker one with more Ability Duration is cast, it will take effect after the stronger buff expires.

## Volatile Recompense

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Volatile_Recompense

Recompense Augment: Daggers whirl in a ring around Kullervo for 25s, slashing nearby foes and dealing Blast Damage and Status Effect before returning to the ring.

Volatile Recompense is a Warframe Augment Mod for Kullervo's Recompense that has the surrounding daggers continuously rotate around him for a duration while inflicting Blast damage and status.

## Stats

Rank | Blast Damage | Duration | Cost
0 | 1000 | 10s | 6
1 | 1000 | 15s | 7
2 | 1000 | 20s | 8
3 | 1000 | 25s | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Flawless under the New Loka, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Daggers deal 1,000 Blast Damage on each hit with a guaranteed Blast status.
- Daggers are not consumed upon hitting an enemy and will continuously generate Overguard and melee combo per hit.
  - With this augment, two daggers will seek out enemies every second and remain embedded for three seconds before returning to orbit Kullervo, with any orbiting daggers indicated on the UI.     - If Kullervo's Overguard is depleted less than 0.1s after a dagger generates it, there will be an opening where attacks can damage his health before the next dagger can restore the gate.
- Cannot be recast while active.

## Wrecking Wall

Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wrecking_Wall

Containment Wall Augment: Strip 50% of armor and shields from struck enemies. Hitting 5 or more enemies will restart the duration of any Chyrinka Pillars and empower them.

Wrecking Wall is a Warframe Augment Mod for Qorvex's Containment Wall that grants it Armor and Shield strip. Additionally, hitting 5 or more enemies will refresh the durations of any active Chyrinka Pillars while empowering them, even if they were not directly hit.

## Stats

Rank | Armor and Shield Reduction | Cost
0 | 35% | 6
1 | 40% | 7
2 | 45% | 8
3 | 50% | 9

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of Genius under Cephalon Suda, or the rank of General under Steel Meridian, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- Enemy armor and shields are fully removed at 200% Ability Strength. With one Corrosive Projection, armor will be fully stripped at 164% Ability Strength.
  - Shield strip removes a percentage of the current maximum value, as such it has diminishing returns on subsequent casts against the same enemies. Armor strip, however, is a percentage of an enemy's total, requiring only two casts at base strength to fully strip.
  - Defenses will only be stripped once enemies are crushed by both walls.

## Tips

- As this augment refreshes empowered pillar duration, combine with Crucible Blast to prolong the inherited nuclear chain reaction on each pulse.
