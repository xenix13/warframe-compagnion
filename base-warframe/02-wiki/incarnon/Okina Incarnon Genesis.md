Okina Incarnon Genesis is an item that is used to add Incarnon upgrades to the Okina or Okina Prime.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Installing the Okina Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 20 Maw Fang, and 80 Lamentus.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.

Kills generate spectral daggers that target enemies, applying max Cold Status stacks. +100% Melee Damage +20% Sprint Speed +20% to Parkour Velocity | - | Kills create a spectral dagger that orbits the player, then seeks an enemy within 15 meters. Daggers deal damage and apply 10 forced Cold stacks to the target, then explode in a 4m radius, dealing AoE damage and applying 1 forced Cold stack to enemies hit. Up to 6 daggers can be active at once. Dagger hits do not add to the melee combo counter. Kills from the daggers will not generate new daggers, but kills from status procs created by daggers will. Dagger direct hits and explosions are not affected by melee damage bonuses, such as Incarnon form's innate +100% melee damage, Pressure Point, or Arcane Fury. However, direct hits and explosions are affected by universal base damage sources, such as Arcane Arachne and Vex Armor. Direct hits of daggers are affected by Condition Overload, while the explosions are not. Condition Overload effects are multiplicative to sources of universal base damage. Dagger seek range and AoE radius are not affected by melee range mods such as Reach. Dagger direct hit and AoE damage are affected by faction damage, elemental damage, critical stats, status stats, and most other effects. Dagger damage type can be changed with elemental mods. Modded elements will not change the element type of the forced cold procs, which are applied separately from natural status procs from the daggers. Dagger direct hits and AoEs can activate Melee Influence's buff, but only statuses procced from the dagger AoE will be spread by the effect. Forced cold procs will not be spread. Daggers can benefit from Melee Careen's damage multiplier against frozen enemies, if the enemy is already frozen.
