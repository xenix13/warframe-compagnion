Ceramic Dagger Incarnon Genesis is an item that is used to add Incarnon upgrades to the Ceramic Dagger.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Installing the Ceramic Dagger Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 80 Connla Sprouts.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.

## Known Bugs

- Direct hits of spectral daggers and explosions do not benefit from base damage sources like Primed Pressure Point, Spoiled Strike, Killing Blow and Arcane Fury.
- The +100 and +120 damage bonuses of Gun and Blade and Breacher's Opportunity do not apply either, however the +80 damage from Breacher's Opportunity does.
- Impact, Puncture, Slash and elemental damage mods work as intended.

- Condition Overload is multiplicative to base damage bonuses on spectral daggers, while being additive to them on physical melee hits.
- Evolution II: Gun and Blade
- Condition Overload ignores the +100 innate damage.

- Evolution II: Breacher's Opportunity
- Condition Overload ignores the +120 innate damage.

- Evolution IV: Red Right Hand
- Does not affect spectral dagger explosions.
- Affects only one of the two spectral daggers.
