Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis is an item that is used to add Incarnon upgrades to the Dual Ichor.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Installing the Dual Ichor Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 60 Rune Marrow and 80 Kovnik.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.

## Notes

- Clouds do not force toxin procs, nor will they proc toxin if the toxin damage is changed to something else.
- Clouds cannot proc physical status effects like Impact, Puncture, and Slash.
- Status effects procced by the clouds can heal with Healing Return.
- Enemies killed by clouds can spawn another cloud, and repeat the process of chaining clouds.

## Tips

- While Lasting Sting does not increase the cloud duration, it can still increase DoT status effects dealt by the clouds, potentially causing tougher targets to take more damage overall and die, creating another cloud.
- Clouds can trigger Melee Vortex and become self sustaining when modded with Magnetic.
- Pairs well with Furious Javelin and Roar.
