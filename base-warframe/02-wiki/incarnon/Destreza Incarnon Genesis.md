Destreza Incarnon Genesis is an item that is used to add Incarnon upgrades to the Destreza or Destreza Prime.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Installing the Destreza Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 60 Rune Marrow.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. The following Evolution tiers, their requirements for unlocking them, and their related perks, are shown below;
- Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack, a Slam Attack or a Heavy Slam Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.
- Despite the in-game description only listing Heavy Attacks (and consequently Heavy Slams) as able to activate Incarnon Form, non-Heavy Slams may be used as well and may be preferred as they do not reduce your Melee Combo. This behavior is shared by all melee Incarnon weapons released since Thalys, which also includes Obex.

## Notes

- Heavy attack projectiles always move parallel to the X axis and therefore cannot be aimed down or up.
- Heavy attack projectiles have a set duration of ~2 seconds.
- Projectile Speed scales with attack speed. Investing into high attack speed increases how far projectiles can reach before disappearing.

- Currently, normal slams can trigger the Incarnon mode activation.
- Reaching a 10x combo multiplier is tracked regardless of whether you start from 0x or drop down to 10x from a higher multiplier.
- The "Heavy Attack kills" trigger of Weighted Impetus and Incarnon Form's bonus Puncture damage is activated when the direct damage of a Heavy attack or spectral daggers gets a kill. However, a kill resulting from status damage from the forced procs of either will not count.
- Heavy attack projectiles trigger "On Heavy Attack" effects only during the Heavy Attack Wind Up animation.
- This includes effects like applying the Combo Multiplier and doubling the effect of True Steel and its variants.
- The above bonuses will apply to projectiles that have been already launched if the Destreza user is in the Wind Up animation while the projectiles connect. For this reason, increasing the speed of the Wind Up animation may be less desirable than usual.

- Spectral rapiers are affected by Damage Vulnerability, including melee-specific vulnerability from Paralysis.
- Base damage of spectral rapiers is the same with or without damage bonuses from EVO2.
- Heavy attack projectiles do not trigger Melee Influence buff nor a spread when the buff is active.
- Heavy attack projectiles are not affected by Melee Duplicate.

## Tips

- Combo Multiplier applies while the user is still in the Heavy Attack Wind Up animation, which lasts for only a few moments after the spectral rapiers spawn. This means that while heavy attacking with combo, the user should position themselves such that travel time for the rapiers is minimized in order to maximize damage output by the rapiers.
- Condition Overload can be a good choice for builds that make use of the spectral rapiers, as each projectile inflicts a forced Impact and Slash proc, priming that target for any other projectiles in the wave with at least two statuses, and with a high chance of inflicting Puncture for a third type.
- When using Melee Doughty, the bonus puncture damage is enough to enable using elemental damage while still receiving +10x CD when increasing base status chance via the Evolution IV bonus and using mods like Weeping Wounds and Galvanized Elementalist.
- Abilities that are coded as an Extra Hit like Toxic Lash and Xata's Whisper can increase the weapon's direct damage and inflict additional statuses without negatively affecting the weapon's damage distribution for the purposes of Melee Doughty.

- Forced Slash status procs from the spectral rapiers proc Relentless Combination and can help keep a heavy attack build at 12x when paired with heavy attack efficiency.
- All of the effects of Voruna's Shroud of Dynar apply to each of the spectral rapiers, including causing the rapiers to inflict a second forced Slash proc.
- Counting the hits from the spectral rapiers alongside the weapon itself, this allows for up to 12 forced Slash procs per heavy attack while Shroud of Dynar's buff is active.
- The held effect of Lycath's Hunt can further enable Destreza Incarnon by increasing heavy attack efficiency.

- It is possible to start at a 6x combo multiplier by raising Initial Combo to 100 or above.
- EVO3 Adept Reflexes (+20 initial combo), combined with the Corrupt Charge melee mod (+30), the Ready Steel aura mod (+24), and a Riven Mod with at least +26 Initial Combo will start the weapon at 6x multiplier, thereby making an Incarnon Transformation available at all times.

- EVO4 Decisive Stature is more suited to the Destreza Prime due to its higher base status chance. When maxed out, Destreza Prime would have 64% base critical chance, while the same buffs on the base Destreza yield only a 50.4% base crit chance.
- Decisive Stature is maxed out when the weapon's final status chance is 140%. On the Destreza Prime, this requires 600% added status chance.
- Piercing Stature maxes out at 100% Status Chance, Galvanized Elementalist with 120% Status Chance, Jugulus Barbs has 60%, and Weeping Wounds offers 40% per combo tier. With all of these factors combined, Decisive Stature's bonus can be maxed out at 8x combo or above.

## Known Bugs

- Destreza Incarnon's projectiles from heavy attack intermittently don't spawn.
- This appears to be primarily a bug within Simulacrum.

- Incarnon transformation sometimes do not occur if the heavy attack meant to transform the weapon is part of a chain of attacks. Ending the current combo and then heavy attacking seems to work.
