Magistar Incarnon Genesis is an item that is used to add Incarnon upgrades to the Magistar or Sancti Magistar.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Installing the Magistar Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroot, and 150 Aggristone.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.

## Tips

- Crushing Verdict's channeling requirement can only be met by a handful of channeled abilities that still permit melee actions:
- Baruuk's Elude.
- Chroma's Spectral Scream or Effigy.
- Cyte-09's Neutralize.
- Ember's Immolation when fully heated.
- Equinox's Mend or Maim.
- Ivara's Prowl.
- Nyx's Absorb with Assimilate.
- Oberon's Renewal.
- Sevagoth's Gloom when draining within enemy range. This is also the only ability on this list that can be transferred via the Helminth.

- The Incarnon-form's lifesteal exclusive to Sancti Magistar counts direct melee hits and slam radial damage (improved by Evolution III: Seismic Slam), but not status damage. High attack speed is advised to have regular healing.
- All lifesteal on the weapon heals allies.
- Lifesteal is counted on the final damage inflicted upon a target, and is therefore reduced by enemy damage reduction, armor and resistances.
- Lifesteal does not work on object health, but will work when transferred through Tornado.

## Known Bugs

- Evolution IV: Flashing Bleed can proc once for every hit, and only if there is no other Slash proc on the hit. This makes it only 30% to 20% effective instead of the perceived 50%.
- More than one Impact proc will only roll the chance once.
- A multi-proc hit with both a natural (or provided by Seeking Talons) Slash proc and an Impact proc will not roll Flashing Bleed at all.

- Condition Overload ignores damage bonuses of Crushing Verdict and Edge Of Justice as well as Stance multipliers.
