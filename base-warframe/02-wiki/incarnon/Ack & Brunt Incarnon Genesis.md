Ack & Brunt Incarnon Genesis is an item that is used to add Incarnon upgrades to the Ack & Brunt.

## Incarnon Genesis

- Installing the Ack & Brunt Incarnon Genesis requires 20 Pathos Clamps, 70 Dracroots, and 300 Ariette Scales.
- When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

### Overview

- Incarnon Form is charged by building Melee Combo to 6x, then using a Heavy Attack. Once activated, the Incarnon form lasts for 180 seconds.
- Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, Incarnon Form is removed upon death.

## Notes

- Spectral axes embed and explode a second time, but this is only visual.

## Known Bugs

- Direct hits of spectral axes and explosions do not benefit from base damage sources like Primed Pressure Point, Spoiled Strike, Killing Blow and Arcane Fury.
- Condition Overload is multiplicative to base damage on spectral axes, while being additive to it on physical melee hits.
- The +150 damage of both Evolution II perks is not affected by Stance multipliers.
- Evolution IV challenge counts 9x combo instead of 10x.
