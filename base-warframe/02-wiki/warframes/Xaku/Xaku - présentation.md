Broken Warframes adrift in the Void converged to create Xaku. Together, they deal high damage. Xaku proves that the sum of the whole is greater than its parts.

Release Date: August 25th, 2020

Of the Void ethereal, yet coalesced into corporeal shape, the fractured anomaly Xaku exists as an entity thrice entwined. Fused from the shattered remnants of three warriors, they bridge reality to the horrors from beyond the fold. Xaku reassembled in Update 29.0 (2020-08-25).

Xaku is an amalgamation of Warframes fused by the mysterious powers of the Void, blessed with high damage output. Their Passive grants Dodge chance to allow enemy attacks to harmlessly phase through them. Xata's Whisper imbues their weapons with Void damage. Grasp of Lohk seizes and Disarms enemy weapons to create armaments that hover around Xaku and auto-target nearby enemies. The Lost cycles through three abilities: Accuse forces enemies to become briefly allied, Gaze binds an enemy to project a field that removes enemy shields and armor, and Deny releases a beam that lifts enemies into aerial stasis. The Vast Untime sheds Xaku's fragments to emerge in their skeletal form, releasing a blast that applies Slow and Void Damage Vulnerability, increasing their movement speed and passive dodge chance, and pauses the duration of all other abilities.

## Notes

- When Xaku wields their signature Quassus/ Quassus Prime, the weapon's heavy attack ethereal daggers become more accurate.
- Xaku's ability kit is able to inflict Void damage, which provides bonus damage to Overguard and Zariman but is neutral to all other health types meaning its damage is neither increased or decreased against most enemies. Xata's Whisper and The Lost's Deny are also able to trigger the Bullet Attractor Status Effect and reset Sentient and Shadow Stalker's resistances.
- However, Xaku's Void damage lacks numerous benefits provided to Operators, such as destroying Kuva clouds in Kuva Siphons, bypassing Eidolon-type Sentient invulnerabilities, changing Profit-Taker Orb's shield weakness, or killing Thrax Centurion's and Thrax Legatus' spectral forms.

- Xaku possesses two Appearance Auxiliary Attachments: Corporeal, which keeps Xaku in their default form even during The Vast Untime; and Skeletal, which gives Xaku the appearance of their skeletal form that they take upon using The Vast Untime.
