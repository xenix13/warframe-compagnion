# Xata's Whisper (capacité de Xaku) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xata%27s_Whisper

Duration:20 / 30 / 30 / 35 s 

Range:N/A 

Misc: 1 (extra hit per damage instance) Sentient resistance reset (upon status proc) 

Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Whispers from the Void infuse Xaku with untold power, expending 25 energy to empower all equipped Weapons to inflict 1 Extra Hit per damage instance for 17% / 23% / 23% / 26% of total weapon damage as Void damage. Xata's Whisper lasts for 20 / 30 / 30 / 35 seconds.
- Casting Speed is affected by Natural Talent and Speed Drift.
- The extra Void damage hit can remove Sentient and Shadow Stalker's damage adaptation resistances upon triggering a Status Effect.
- Extra hit per damage instance does not provide extra Melee Combo or Sniper Rifle combo gain.
- Xata's Whisper duration appears on the ability icon, as well as beside Xaku's shield and health indicators as a buff icon with the damage bonus and timer.
- The color of the visual effect on the warframe's body is determined by the warframe's primary/left energy color.

- Xata's Whisper Void damage is based on a percentage of the total weapon damage.
- Damage bonus is a separate damage instance, and does not dilute weapon elements.
- Affected by base, elemental, critical, and faction damage mods (e.g. Serration, Hornet Strike, Pressure Point, Convulsion, Cleanse Grineer, Vital Sense, etc)
- The ability double dips on faction damage, and body part weaknesses.
- Xata's Whisper can proc the Void Status Effect based on the weapon's total status chance.
- The bullet attractor turns enemy projectiles towards themselves, effectively reflecting damage. This even applies to hit-scan weapons, which cannot exit the bubble.

- The Void damage applies to most weapon hits, including Acid Shells and Concealed Explosives, but not Bursting Mass or Pathocyst's maggots.
- Since the damage bonus is not applied to the weapon directly, and only activates on-hit, it will not be absorbed into Bursting Mass.

- The Void damage bonus does not allow Xaku's weapons to damage Eidolon Shields of the Eidolon Teralyst, Eidolon Gantulyst and Eidolon Hydrolyst, nor the ghostly energy form of the Eidolon Vomvalyst, nor the Thrax Centurion or Thrax Legatus enemies from the Zariman Ten Zero.
- Damage is affected by Ancient Disruptor auras and other enemies that reduce ability damage, unlike other weapon buffing skills.

- Can be recast while active to refresh the duration.
- Casting Xata's Whisper is an upper-body animation that allows movement while restricting other actions.
- On cast, Xaku's right shoulder and arm pieces visually detach then return to position. While active, Infested grey matter envelops their entire body as the Void veins pulse and glow with energy, while surrounded by a Void energy cloud near the torso.

Kit Interactions

- Grasp of Lohk Void replicas and The Lost's sub-abilities do not benefit from the additional Void damage bonus of Xata's Whisper.
- The Vast Untime renders enemies 50% more vulnerable to Void damage and pauses Xata's Whisper's duration timer while active.

Helminth

- Subsuming Xaku to the Helminth will offer Xata's Whisper and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- When placed on Chroma, Cyte-09, Mirage, Octavia, Oraxia, Rhino, Temple, and Uriel, Subsumed Xata's Whisper can only replace Vex Armor, Resupply, Eclipse, Amp, Silken Stride, Roar, Ripper's Wail, and Demonium respectively.

Tips & Tricks

- When fighting against Sentients, it is recommended to use and mod weapons with high status chance, in order to efficiently reset their damage resistances.
- Recast the ability to refresh its duration or activate The Vast Untime to pause Xata's Whisper's duration timer, effectively prolonging the buff on Xaku as a semi-permanent damage bonus for all equipped weapons.
- The Void Status Effects Bullet Attractor can be a good defensive tool against ranged enemies.
- Interaction with Blast:
- When an enemy is afflicted with one or more Blast stacks, Xata's whisper will trigger off the detonation(s). Additionally, the Faction Damage Bonus will be applied again (effectively triple-dipping), and Elemental Bonuses will apply to Xata's Whisper damage (despite Blast not being affected by Elemental Damage Bonuses).
- Consider the following example: A gun deals 100 damage per bullet, and we have Thermite Rounds, Rime Rounds, Stormbringer, Primed Bane of Grineer, and Xata's whisper at base strength: The initial hit will deal 100 × (1 + 0.6 + 0.6 + 0.9) × (1 + 0.55) = 480.5 damage, and Xata's whisper will deal 0.26 × 480.5 × (1 + 0.55) = 193.6415 damage (the Faction Damage Bonus is applied again). If the hit proc'd Blast, then the detonation damage will be 0.3 × 100 × (1 + 0.55)^2 = 72.075 (Elemental Damage doesn't apply to Blast detonations and the Faction Damage Bonus is applied again). Then, Xata's whisper will trigger off said detonation, dealing 0.26 × 72.075 × (1 + 0.55) × (1 + 0.6 + 0.6 + 0.9) = 90.0433 damage (as you can see, the Faction Damage Bonus is applied yet again, and the Elemental Damage Bonus is applied even though Blast detonations don't scale off Elemental Damage Bonuses.
