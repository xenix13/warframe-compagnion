# Whipclaw (capacité de Khora) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Whipclaw

Duration:N/A 

Range:5 / 7 / 8 / 10 m (cast range) 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 m (explosion radius) 

Misc: 200 % (explosion radius range cap) 2.0 x (Ensnare damage multiplier) 50 % (Strangledome damage distribution) 

Info

- Khora expends 25 energy to crack her whip toward the aiming reticle, creating an explosion up to 5 / 7 / 8 / 10 meters away in a 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 meters radius area around the first object or entity hit. All enemies within line of sight of the explosion receive 100 / 112 / 125 / 150 base damage as a melee strike, while enemies at the center are also disoriented briefly, causing them to receive Stagger and Ragdoll.
- Explosion radius is capped at 10 meters, achievable with 200% Ability Range.
- Melee range mods (e.g., Primed Reach) can increase the radius beyond the 10 meter cap.

- Ability Strength and Accumulating Whipclaw are additive with each other and multiplicative to base damage (e.g., Pressure Point, Arcane Fury).
- Damage is distributed evenly between Impact, Puncture, and Slash.
- Whipclaw has 25% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, and 25% status chance.
- Whipclaw is affected by Casting Speed.
- Whipclaw has a moddable Pseudo-Exalted Weapon, Whipclaw, which does not provide an actual equippable weapon but can be modded and slotted with Arcane Enhancements to boost the ability's performance.
- Cannot perform blocking or heavy attacks and as such cannot equip related mods (e.g., Parry, Reflex Coil, Mentor's Legacy, Melee Animosity) and does not have an Exilus Slot.
- Cannot benefit from Carnis Mandible's heavy attack set effect.

- Does not benefit from Attack Speed and as such cannot equip Fury.
- This means that Spoiled Strike provides no downside.

- Cannot perform Finishers and as such cannot equip Finishing Touch and Melee Crescendo.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Whipclaw.

- Whipclaw can be recast within a 5 second window to perform a repeating combo chain, granting 20 Ability Combo Counter points. Whipclaw gains 1x additional damage per combo multiplier, up to 12x damage at 12x combo.
- Ability Combo functions like Melee Combo and, as such, benefits from Combo Duration, Additional Combo Count Chance, and Initial Combo. It is also affected by Power Spike.

- Because Whipclaw is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Damage also affects Objects, bypasses obstacles in the environment, and does not diminish with distance.

- Whipclaw will strike toward the aiming reticle at the end of the animation, landing the attack when the whip is fully extended.
- The explosion radius is centered on the closest physical object or surface in the player's crosshair, or the farthest area Whipclaw is able to reach.

- Can be cast while moving and airborne.
- Casting Whipclaw is an upper-body animation that allows movement and parkour Maneuvers.
- Khora's whip visually consists of a handle that resembles the Hystrix's model, alongside living metal spikes lining the thong, spine, and fall; four claw-like extensions comprise the lash of the whip, ending in the metallic adornments on Khora's body that match Venari's selected battle posture. The whip is affected by Khora's chosen Appearance colors.
- Whipclaw's radial explosion, the whip's energy lights and travel trail, are affected by Khora's chosen energy color.

Weapon

Main article: Whipclaw (Weapon)
Whipclaw is Khora and Khora Prime's Pseudo-Exalted Weapon empowering her Whipclaw ability.

As a Pseudo-Exalted, the ability does not summon an actual useable weapon but can equip melee mods to enhance the ability itself.

- This weapon deals equal physical damage.
- Utilizes the pseudo-exalted/Ability version of the Melee Combo system, receiving a damage multiplier of 1.0x per Combo Counter up to the cap of 12.0x.
- Default Combo Duration is 5 seconds, with duration resetting on any combo generation.
- Duration is affected by mods (e.g., Body Count) and arcanes (e.g., Primary Dexterity).
- Duration is not affected by Amalgam Furax Body Count.

- Hitting at least one enemy on ability cast will increase combo count by 20 hits (+1.0x) up to a maximum counter of 220 (12.0x).
- Enemy corpses count as enemies for combo generation on ability cast.
- Combo count is affected by Additional Combo Count Chance mods (e.g., Quickening, True Punishment, Relentless Combination), but each proc of the effect only awards one additional hit alongside the initial 20. Stacking mods to reach above 100% chance can award additional hits.
- Combo count is not affected by the Rauta.

- Initial Combo is affected by mods (e.g., Corrupt Charge, Galvanized Reflex).
- Even if the combo UI is not shown, Initial Combo does provide the corresponding damage multiplier.

- Combo Decay is reduced by the Power Spike passive from the Naramon focus school.

- Cannot perform blocking or heavy attacks and as such cannot equip related mods (e.g. Parry, Reflex Coil, Mentor's Legacy, Melee Animosity) and does not have an Exilus Slot.
- Cannot benefit from Carnis Mandible's heavy attack set effect.

- Cannot perform Finishers and as such cannot equip Finishing Touch and Melee Crescendo.
- Does not benefit from Attack Speed and as such cannot equip Fury.
- This means that Spoiled Strike provides no downside.

- Using Scourge/Scourge Prime's Alt fire that creates headshot bubbles will make the weapon deal more damage

Kit Interactions

- Whipclaw inflicts 200% damage against enemies affected by Ensnare and refreshes the propagation effect on ensnared enemies.
- If at least one enemy captured by Strangledome is hit by Whipclaw, all other enemies in the same dome will each receive 50% of the total damage. This also works on a Strangledome cast by an ally Khora. Additionally, Whipclaw's Critical Chance and Status Chance will reroll on each enemy separately.

Augment

Main article: Accumulating Whipclaw
Accumulating Whipclaw is a Warframe Augment Mod for Khora's Whipclaw that grants a stacking damage bonus to subsequent Whipclaws upon hitting at least 3 enemies in a single cast, which will decay after 10 seconds.

Tips & Tricks

- Whipclaw's radial explosion forms when Khora's whip is fully extended. You can adjust your aim to hit your target or elsewhere during this animation.
- If your target is moving away too quickly during the animation, try to land the explosion on a nearby surface to catch your target in the area of effect.
- The explosion's area of effect will bypass terrain and obstacles to hit enemies through walls, which pairs well with radar mods such as Enemy Sense and Animal Instinct to detect nearby enemies.

- Cast Whipclaw directly on an enemy to knock it backward when it is hit by the radial burst.
- Cast Whipclaw on enemies entangled by Ensnare for double damage. Ensnared enemies that survive Whipclaw will propagate again to pull in new enemies.
- Cast Whipclaw on enemies captured by Strangledome. The initial target takes full damage from Whipclaw, while all other enemies in the dome take half damage. Damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, and status chance on all targets will benefit from your equipped melee mods.
- Critical chance and status chance will proc on each enemy separately, inflicting them with potentially different damage amounts and Status Effects.

- Whipclaw's radial explosion can bypass Arctic Eximus bubbles to form inside directly.
- Equip Arcane Fury on Khora to increase damage.
