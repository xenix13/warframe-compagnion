# Freeze (capacité de Frost) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Freeze

| 1 25 | Freeze A frigid energy blast that freezes targets in their tracks. Introduced in Update 6.0 (2013-01-29) | Strength:150 / 225 / 275 / 350 ( Cold main damage) 50 / 100 / 125 / 150 ( Cold area damage) 

Duration:5 / 7 / 12 / 15 s (freeze duration) 

Range:5 m (impact radius) 

Misc: 10 Freeze status procs on direct hit 6 Freeze status procs on impact radius 

Info

- Frost expends 25 Energy to launch an icy projectile in the direction of the reticle, inflicting 150 / 225 / 275 / 350 Cold damage and 10 Freeze procs. On impact, the projectile explodes in a 5 meter radius to inflict 50 / 100 / 125 / 150 Cold damage and 6 Freeze procs. Enemies that receive 10 Freeze stacks from Freeze are frozen in place for 5 / 7 / 12 / 15 seconds.
- Area damage affects the initial target, decreases with distance, and does not bypass obstacles in the environment.

- Freeze is a one-handed action. As such, it can be used while performing various maneuvers and actions without interruption.
- Can be recast on a frozen target multiple times, restarting the freeze timer.

Kit Interactions

- Casting Freeze onto the exterior of a Snow Globe will destroy it, triggering an icy explosion that damages and ragdolls enemies in an area of effect.

Augment

Main article: Freeze Force
Freeze Force is a Warframe Augment Mod for Frost that allows Freeze to be held on cast, creating a wave of energy traveling outward from the user that temporarily grants the caster and nearby allies additional Cold damage to all attacks.

Tips & Tricks

- Freeze procs can slow enemies with Overguard by up to 65%, and freeze others. This helps with aiming and crowd control.
- Arbitration Shield Drones cannot thaw fully frozen enemies, but they can protect them.
