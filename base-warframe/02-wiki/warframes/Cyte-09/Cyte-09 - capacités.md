# Cyte-09/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cyte-09/Abilities

## Passive

Cyte-09 gains Practiced Aim when he kills an enemy by damaging their Weak Points, increasing his Critical Chance against weak points by 1% up to a maximum of 300%, lasting for the entirety of a mission.

- Critical chance bonus is additive to similar mods such as Point Strike.
- Additional critical chance from Cyte-09's passive can raise the Critical Tier of his equipped weapon.

## Abilities

| 1 25 | Seek Plant an antenna that projects a forward wave scan. Detected enemies take increased Weak Point Damage, and become visible through walls. Weapons gain Punch through. Introduced in Update 38.0 (2024-12-13) | Strength:30% / 45% / 60% / 75% (weak point damage) 

Duration:20 / 25 / 30 / 35 s (antennae duration) 5 s (scan duration) 

Range:45 / 50 / 55 / 60 m (seek range) 10 m (weapon punch through against enemy bodies) 

Misc: 75° (angle) 3 s (pulse interval) Weak spots and silhouette highlight through terrain ∞ (weapon punch through against terrain) 3 (max antennae) 

Info

- Cyte-09 expends 25 energy to plant an antenna that scans enemies within 45 / 50 / 55 / 60 meters at a 75 degree frontal angle, lasting for 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 seconds. The antenna is an Invulnerable intangible stationary object that pulses a scanner wave every 3 seconds. When washed over by the wave, enemies are detected for 5 seconds, having their silhouette and weak spots highlighted through walls and receive 30% / 45% / 60% / 75% increased damage to weak spots. While an antenna is active, Cyte-09's primary and secondary Weapons gain 10 meter Punch Through against enemy bodies and infinite punch through against terrain. A maximum of 3 antennas can be planted.
- When casting the ability, Cyte-09 rises into the air then drops to the ground to slam the antenna in front of him. The antenna can be placed on solid surfaces and in mid-air.
- Enemies scanned by Seek are marked on the minimap for all players.
- Detected enemies are only highlighted through walls for the user and allied Cyte-09 Warframes.
- Allies do not benefit from scanned enemy weakspots from cyte09.
- Punch through applies to melees, allowing them to hit through walls.
- The weak point damage bonus stacks additively to weak point multiplier formula from Enemy Body Parts. For example, a weapon with max rank Primary Acuity and a 75% bonus from Seek will do (1.25 +3.5 + 0.75) x 1.5 = 8.25x damage to a Drahk's headshot weak spot. With a Primary Deadhead (or Secondary Deadhead) equipped, it becomes (1.25+ 3.5 + 0.75) x 1.5 x (1 + 0.3) = 10.725x damage to a Drahk's headshot weak spot with armor stripped.
- Scanned body weakpoints still benefit from Seek despite it scanning the head.

- When Cyte-09 has undergone Polarization via Forma, his primary energy color highlights the weak spots, while his secondary energy color highlights the enemy's silhouette.

Kit Interactions

- Seek highlights weak points for Cyte-09's 1% Critical Chance from weak point kills passive, Evade's duration extension and health restore, and Neutralize's ricochet.

Tips & Tricks

- When modded for high range, it helps quickly find and kill Demolishers. (except for enemy necramechs)

Bugs

- Arbitration Shield Drones will not dispel the marking.
- Ragdolled enemies will immediately lose all marking.
- The ability does not highlight weak spots if the enemy lacks a conventional "head". Deimos Saxum, Aurax Atloc Raknoid, Demolisher Juggernaut, etc. will not be detected, and will not take more damage.
- Tusk Seeker Drone has a head (according to Vesper 77 and Incarnon charging) but Seek does not highlight it. This does not seem to be a problem of non-humanoid shapes, as the ability does affect Rogue Culverin, Techrot Babau, and Deimos Jugulus.
- The ability can still highlight the silhouette of such enemies, but enemy necramechs (e.g Rogue Voidrig, Demolisher Bonewidow, etc.) will not be highlighted at all.

| 2 50 | Resupply Throw two Elemental Ammo Packs that instantly refill the active weapon's magazine, while granting the weapon an additional instance of the selected Elemental Damage and Status Effect. Reload clears the effect. Sniper Rifles gain extra damage. Hold the ability to select the Elemental Damage type. Introduced in Update 38.0 (2024-12-13) | Strength:10% / 15% / 20% / 25% (weapon damage bonus) 20% / 30% / 40% / 50% (sniper damage bonus) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:N/A 

Misc: Reload held firearm magazine 2 (ammo packs) 0.35 s (recast cooldown) Self damage bonus buff on cast Damage bonus loss on reload ∞ (ammo pack duration) 6 (maximum ammo packs) 

Info

- Cyte-09 expends 50 energy to instantly reload his currently equipped weapon while throwing forward 2 ammo packs. Looting the Ammo packs will instantly refill the equipped weapon's magazine, triggering an Extra Hit of 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% damage (20% / 30% / 40% / 50% for Sniper Rifles) that applies a forced status effect from the selected element. On cast, Cyte-09 automatically enhances his currently held primary weapon, secondary weapon, or Neutralize's Neutralizer sniper rifle with this bonus. Reloading the weapon (default R ) while buffed by Resupply will clear the elemental damage bonus. Ammo packs last indefinitely until collected and up to 6 ammo packs may be deployed on the field at once.
- All players in the squad have their own instances of Resupply's ammo packs, meaning that collecting an ammo pack for yourself will not take it away from a squad member.
- For allies, picking up an ammo pack uses the same value of Cyte-09's damage percentage that dropped the specific ammo pack for the Extra Hit.

- Resupply has a 0.35 second cooldown between casts.
- Resupply ammo packs use a unique model exclusive to Cyte-09, and display their elemental damage type icon overhead.
- All allied Tenno can see the elemental damage type icons on their mini-map to locate specific ammo packs.
- A fully loaded weapon can only gain one elemental damage bonus at a time. However, upon expending at least one ammo, a weapon that already has one of Resupply's elemental bonuses can gain an additional bonus by looting a Resupply ammo pack that was previously deployed.
- Resupply ammo packs only refill the current magazine, and will not restore any reserve ammo.
- Archguns and battery weapons (including Kitguns equipped with Pax Charge) do not benefit from Resupply ammo packs.

- Casting Resupply is a One-Handed Action. As such, it can be used while performing various maneuvers and actions without interruption.
- Hold the ability to open a Gear wheel to select the elemental damage type of the reload and ammo packs: Heat, Cold, Electricity, Toxin, Blast, Radiation, Gas, Magnetic, Viral, Corrosive, randomized, or previously selected. Tap the ability to deploy the last selected element.
- Resupply's forced Status Effect will not apply if the enemy is killed in a single bullet, such as when using Neutralizer to headshot enemies. This prevents area of effect status like Gas or Blast from being effective for crowd control unless firing at enemies that are tough enough to survive a single shot.
- Resupply's forced Status Effect is calculated using the extra instance's damage value as base. Main and extra hit have their own independent status procs. For example modded Heat with forced Electricity won't create Radiation. Faction elemental weaknesses affect the extra instance's base damage. A Murmur enemy with electric weakness will take : Base damage of extra instance x 0.5 x 1.5 x other DoT multipliers for the DoT damage calculation.
- By default, tap cast starts as randomize.
- Elemental bonus does not override the Toxin damage bonus from Oraxia's Silken Stride.
- Elemental bonus can be overridden by the Heat damage bonus from Uriel's Demonium, and vise versa.
- However, upon reloading to clear Resupply while Demonium is still active, Demonium's Heat bonus will be reapplied.

Kit Interactions

- Neutralize's Neutralizer is a sniper rifle, and thus receives the doubled damage bonus from Resupply's elemental bonus.

| 3 75 | Evade Jump backwards and become invisible for a short duration. Killing enemies on their Weak Points extends the duration and heals Cyte-09. Introduced in Update 38.0 (2024-12-13) | Strength:25 / 50 / 75 / 100 (health restore) 

Duration:10 s (invisible duration) 2 s (duration extend) 30 s (duration cap) 

Range:N/A 

Misc: Status cleanse on cast Shield recharge delay bypass on cast 5 m (backward dodge) 60 s (cooldown) 

Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Cyte-09 expends 75 energy to dodge backward 5 meters, cleansing himself of Status Effects, bypasses his Shield regeneration delay, and turns Cyte-09 and his Companion Invisible for 10 seconds. Weak point kills extend the ability's duration for 2 seconds to a maximum of 30 seconds and restores Cyte-09's Health by an 25 / 50 / 75 / 100. Has a cooldown of 60 seconds.
- Cooldown starts upon the initial cast of the ability. It can be effectively negated by landing enough weak point kills to continuously extend the ability's duration beyond the cooldown's timer.
- Maximum ability duration is a cap on current duration, not total duration (e.g. you cannot kill 1000 enemies in quick succession and gain a 2000-second ability duration; it will only ever extend to a maximum of the specified cap). However, it is possible to remain invisible indefinitely as long as one can continue to make weak point kills prior to the duration expiring completely.

- Cannot be recast while active or on cooldown.

Kit Interactions

- Seek highlights weak points, making it easier to extend Evade's duration and restore health by killing enemies on the highlighted spots.

Helminth

- Subsuming Cyte-09 to the Helminth will offer Evade and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- However, Subsumed Evade's maximum duration is reduced to 15 seconds and is capped at 25 seconds.
- Currently this helminth is bugged and the maximum duration is not capped at 25 seconds.

| 4 5 +10/shot +20/alt fire | Neutralize Summon the Neutralizer, Cyte-09's exalted Sniper Rifle. Bullets ricochet off Weak Points to seek out other nearby Weak Points. Alt fire lobs a Cold grenade that completely freezes enemies. Introduced in Update 38.0 (2024-12-13) | Strength:1x / 1.08x / 1.16x / 1.25x (damage multiplier) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:10m (ricochet range) 

Misc: 4 (number of ricochets) 20% (damage loss per ricochet) 10m (enemy stagger range on cast) 

Info

- Cyte-09 expends 5 energy to summon the Exalted sniper rifle Neutralizer. Cyte-09 staggers enemies within 10 meters when summoning Neutralizer.
- Neutralizer has its own unique statistics:
- Primary fire shoots semi-automatic sniper rounds.
- Consumes 10 energy per shot.
- Base damage is 255 as 85% Puncture, 10% Slash and 5% Impact, with a damage multiplier of 1x / 1.08x / 1.16x / 1.25x. Damage multiplier is additive to base damage mods such as Serration and Galvanized Aptitude.
- Shots have a 45% critical chance, 3x critical multiplier, 10% status chance, 1.0 fire rate, 2.5 meter punch through, and a magazine size of 12 with a reload speed of 2.25 seconds.
- On weak point hits, the bullet ricochets up to 4 times within 10 meters of the target, with a damage loss of 20% per ricochet.
- Enemies affected by Blood Altar or Well of Life will not cause ricochets when their weakpoints are hit.

- Three zoom modes:
- First zoom has 2.5x zoom distance and +40% critical damage multiplier.
- Second zoom has 4.0x zoom distance and +60% critical damage multiplier.
- Third zoom has 8.0x zoom distance and +80% critical damage multiplier.

- Alternate Fire launches a grenade that inflicts 10 Cold stacks and leaves a lingering field that lasts 5 seconds.
- Consumes 20 energy and 3 ammo per shot.
- Base damage is 20 pure Cold, with a damage multiplier of 1x / 1.08x / 1.16x / 1.25x.
- Shots have a 30% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, 0% status chance, and explodes in a 10 meter radius with 60% damage falloff.

- Neutralize can be equipped with primary mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Can equip the sniper-exclusive Target Acquired, Harkonar Scope, and Sharpshooter mods.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Neutralizer.

- Because Neutralizer is counted as a primary and sniper, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Dead Eye and Arcane Rage.

Weapon

Main article: Neutralizer Neutralizer is Cyte-09's Exalted Sniper Rifle, summoned by activating the ability Neutralize. Primary fire shoots sniper shots that ricochet on weak points, while its Alternate Fire lobs a Cold grenade.
- This weapon deals primarily Puncture damage on its primary fire and pure Cold on its Alternate Fire.
- Shots ricochet on weak point hits up to 4 times within 10 meters of the target, with a damage loss of 20% per ricochet.
- Three zoom modes:
- First zoom has 2.5x zoom distance and +40% critical damage multiplier.
- Second zoom has 4.0x zoom distance and +60% critical damage multiplier.
- Third zoom has 8.0x zoom distance and +80% critical damage multiplier.

- Alternate Fire launches a grenade that explodes in a 10 meter radius, inflicting 10 Cold stacks and leaves a lingering field for 5 seconds.
- Can use the sniper-exclusive Target Acquired, Harkonar Scope, and Sharpshooter mods.
- Innate and polarities.

Kit Interactions

- Kills from Neutralize's primary fire ricochet counts as a separate kill for Cyte-09's 1% Critical Chance from weak point kills passive.
- Seek highlights weak points for Neutralize's primary fire ricochet.
- Neutralizer is a sniper rifle that receives the doubled damage bonus from Resupply's ammo packs.

Tips & Tricks

- Equip Neutralizer with Sharpshooter to generate energy on headshot kills. The ricochet mechanic can also activate it under specific conditions.
- Equip Neutralizer with Cold damage (e.g. Rime Rounds, or Resupply) and Cyte-09 with Archon Flow to occasionally generate a Large Energy Orb.
