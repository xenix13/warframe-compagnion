Info

- Gara expends 25 Energy to thrust her glass longsword with 7 / 8 / 10 / 12 meters range toward the aiming reticle, dealing 250 / 300 / 350 / 400 Puncture damage and knocking back all enemies within 1.75 meters radius from the blade. Holding the ability key instead sweeps the glass longsword from left to right in a 225° horizontal arc in front of Gara, dealing 250 / 300 / 350 / 400 Slash damage to all enemies within reach and bashing them away in a Ragdoll state in the direction of the sweep.
- Shattered Lash has innate punch through, bypassing entities, walls, and obstacles in the environment.
- Sweep is not perfectly aligned in a straight line and is similar to a wave-like pattern, allowing Shattered Lash to hit enemies on a slightly higher terrain elevation than Gara past ~90°.
- Ability Strength is additive and multiplicative to base damage (e.g., Pressure Point, Arcane Fury).
- Shattered Lash has 20% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, and 30% status chance.
- Shattered Lash has a moddable Pseudo-Exalted Weapon, Shattered Lash, which does not provide an actual equippable weapon but can be modded and slotted with Arcane Enhancements to boost the ability's performance.
- Cannot perform blocking or heavy attacks and as such cannot equip related mods (e.g., Parry, Reflex Coil, Mentor's Legacy, Melee Animosity) and does not have an Exilus Slot.
- Cannot benefit from Carnis Mandible's heavy attack set effect.

- Speed is scaled by Cast Speed, rather than Attack Speed, and as such cannot equip Fury.
- This means that Spoiled Strike provides no downside.

- Cannot perform Finishers and as such cannot equip Finishing Touch and Melee Crescendo.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Shattered Lash.

- Shattered Lash can be recast within a 5 second window to perform a repeating combo chain, granting 20 Ability Combo Counter points. Shattered Lash gains 1x additional damage per combo multiplier, up to 12x damage at 12x combo.
- Ability Combo is functionally equivalent to Melee Combo and as such benefits from Combo Duration (e.g., Body Count, Primary Dexterity), Additional Combo Count Chance (e.g., Quickening, True Punishment, Relentless Combination) and Initial Combo (e.g., Corrupt Charge, Galvanized Reflex). It is also affected by Combo Decay from Naramon's Power Spike.

- Because Shattered Lash is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.

- Enemies crystallized by Mass Vitrify are not affected by Shattered Lash's ragdoll and knockback effects.
- Shattered Lash's tap cast is a one-handed action that allows Gara to fire and reload weapons, as well as move and perform maneuvers during cast.
- Hold cast is a two-handed animation that stops Gara's movement and other actions during cast.

- Can be cast while in midair.
- While ability is silent, it does not activate stealth multiplier.
- The glass longsword is visually summoned from Gara's left palm and does not possess a grip. Glass shards will constantly break away and fall from the blade as a visual effect, while the longsword fades into and out of existence for the duration of the thrust or sweep.
- The longsword and its particle effects are affected by Gara's chosen Warframe energy color.

- Shattered Lash can damage enemies across the Rift Plane.
- Shattered Lash is affected by and can trigger Warframe Arcanes.

Weapon

Main article: Shattered Lash (Weapon)
Shattered Lash is Gara and Gara Prime's Exalted Weapon empowering her Shattered Lash ability.

- This weapon deals solely Puncture damage on tap-cast and Slash damage on hold-cast.
- Utilizes the Exalted/Ability version of the Melee Combo system, receiving a damage multiplier of 1.0x per Combo Counter up to the cap of 12.0x.
- Default Combo Duration is 5 seconds, with duration resetting on any combo generation.
- Duration is affected by mods (e.g., Body Count) and arcanes (e.g., Primary Dexterity).
- Duration is not affected by Amalgam Furax Body Count.

- Hitting at least one enemy on ability cast will increase combo count by 20 hits (+1.0x) up to a maximum counter of 220 (12.0x).
- Combo count is affected by Additional Combo Count Chance mods (e.g., Quickening, True Punishment, Relentless Combination), but each proc of the effect only awards one additional hit alongside the initial 20. Stacking mods to reach above 100% chance can award additional hits.
- Combo count is not affected by the Rauta.

- Initial Combo is affected by mods (e.g., Corrupt Charge, Galvanized Reflex).
- Even if the combo UI is not shown, Initial Combo does provide the corresponding damage multiplier.
- Melee Crescendo has no effect and cannot be equipped.

- Combo Decay is reduced by the Power Spike passive from the Naramon focus school.

- Cannot perform blocking or heavy attacks and as such cannot equip related mods (e.g. Parry, Reflex Coil, Mentor's Legacy, Melee Animosity) and does not have an Exilus Slot.
- Cannot benefit from Carnis Mandible's heavy attack set effect.

- Does not benefit from Attack Speed and as such cannot equip Fury.
- This means that Spoiled Strike provides no downside.

- Cannot perform Finishers and as such cannot equip Finishing Touch and Melee Crescendo.

Kit Interactions

- Gara's Passive allows casting abilities to have a 15% chance to Blind enemies within 12 meters for 10 seconds, exposing them to Melee Finisher attacks. Blind chance increases by 20% until the blind triggers.
- Casting Shattered Lash on Gara's own Mass Vitrify glass barrier causes it to break and explode outward, dealing damage to enemies outside the barrier.
- Damage type of the glass barrier explosion is determined by Shattered Lash's thrust or sweep used to shatter the barrier.
- Explosion damage is affected by base damage mods (e.g. Pressure Point, Spoiled Strike) and elemental damage mods (e.g. North Wind, Melee Exposure) and combo multiplier on Shattered Lash. Explosion damage is not affected by critical chance or status chance mods as it cannot crit or proc status effects.
- If Splinter Storm is active and is within the explosion radius of the barrier, 50% of the barrier explosion damage is permanently added to Splinter Storm's damage per second until its duration expires.
- Shattered Lash also bypasses Gara's Mass Vitrify barrier, dealing damage from both the sword and the explosion to enemies struck.

Augment

Main article: Shattered Storm
Shattered Storm is a Warframe Augment Mod for Gara's Shattered Lash that, upon shattering Mass Vitrify, inflicts Splinter Storm to impacted enemies equal to a percentage of modded Ability Strength.

Tips & Tricks

- Tap 1 for quick cast to thrust the glass sword where you aim, puncturing through all enemies in a line to inflict Puncture damage, knockdown and knockback. Thrust allows Gara to keep on the move, use Maneuvers, as well as fire and reload weapons.
- Hold the button for hold cast to sweep the sword, slashing all enemies in an arc to inflict Slash damage, body ragdoll and knockback. However, sweep locks Gara in place during cast.
- Sweep is animation-locked and cannot be aimed upward or downward. However, because the sweep is in a wave-like pattern, it can hit enemies on a lower or higher terrain elevation than Gara depending on the troughs and crests of the wave. You can familiarize yourself with this pattern in Captura mode using the advance time function.
- If enemies are standing on a higher elevation, use Aim Glide (default hold RMB while in midair) and time your sweep to hit them with your blade.

- Use Splinter Storm on an enemy target to amplify Shattered Lash's damage on it.
- Cast Spectrorage on a cluster of enemies or in the enemy's path to cause them to gather inside the carousel, then use Shattered Lash to thrust at specific targets in a line or sweep the entire group away.
- Crystallize enemies using Mass Vitrify then attack them with Shattered Lash for increased damage.
- Damage multipliers from Mass Vitrify and Splinter Storm can stack to produce even higher damage output for Shattered Lash.

- Create a Mass Vitrify barrier and strike it down with Shattered Lash, destroying it in an explosion of glass fragments that damage enemies on the outside of the barrier. Thrust or sweep determines the physical damage type the explosion damage will adopt, allowing you to adjust its damage potential against different factions.
