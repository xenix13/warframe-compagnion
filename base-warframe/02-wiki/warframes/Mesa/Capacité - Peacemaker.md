Info

- Mesa expends 25 energy to stand her ground, drawing her Regulators pistols as her secondary Exalted Weapon. While Peacemaker is active, a focus ring appears in the form of a blurred reticle which can be aimed at enemies. Holding the fire button will cause Mesa to auto-target random enemies inside the focus ring up to 50 meters away. Each gunshot will reduce the size of the focus ring until its field of vision is reduced to 15°.
- In order to achieve target acquisition with Peacemaker, enemies have to be within range and positioned inside the focus ring, and the player's camera must have line of sight with the enemy.
- The formula used to calculate the field of view angle is given by the following:
Final FoV=arctan⁡(Range×tan⁡15∘)

- Unlike other Exalted Weapons, Mesa's Regulators do not scale directly with Ability Strength. Instead, they have 50 fixed base damage, with an innate damage bonus of +100% / +115% / +133% / +150%.
- Innate damage bonus stacks additively with base damage damage mods such as Hornet Strike.

- Peacemaker fires in bursts of 2 shots, ramping up in both damage and fire rate for each burst fired.
- As Peacemaker ramps up, it receives a unique damage bonus of +7.5% per burst fired, up to a fixed maximum of +150%.
- The ramp up damage bonus is multiplicative with all other damage bonuses, acting as a total damage multiplier of 2.5x when fully ramped up.
- The ramp up bonus increases linearly with every burst, with the 20th and all subsequent bursts having the maximum bonus.
- The first burst receives a damage bonus of +7.5% (rather than starting at +0%).
- The bonus resets to its initial value when Peacemaker expires or is deactivated.
- The bonus decays over time if Mesa's Waltz is equipped.

- A fractional multiplier applies to Fire Rate, ramping up from 25% to 100% over 3.5 seconds when firing. Fire Rate will reset if the fire button is released or if two seconds pass without firing.
- This effect is multiplied against other modifiers to fire rate.

- Peacemaker's damage formula is given by the following:
Total Damage=Base Damage⋅(1+150%⋅min(Burst Count,20)20)⋅(1+1.5⋅Ability Strength+Damage Mods)⋅(1+Elemental Mods)
- For example, using maxed Intensify, Hornet Strike, Convulsion, and on the 8th burst a rank-3 Peacemaker will deal:

50 * (1 + 150% * 8 / 20) * (1 + 1.5 * (1 + 30%) + 220%) * (1 + 90%) = 782.8 damage.
- Peacemaker snapshots at time of casting, meaning that bonuses like Growing Power will only apply if they were active at time of casting, and stacking effects like Arcane Impetus or Molt Augmented will not dynamically update.
- Damage bonuses, such as those granted by Cascadia Flare and Arcane Precision, are dynamically updated.

- Base damage distribution is 50% Impact, 25% Puncture, and 25% Slash.
- Regulators have 25% critical chance, 3x critical multiplier, and 10% status chance.
- Regulators are coded as a secondary weapon, and are affected by Warframe arcanes and auras that affect secondary weapons, like Arcane Velocity, Arcane Precision and Pistol Amp.
- Other effects that apply to secondary weapons, like Amalgam Furax Body Count and buffs created by Crimson and Topaz Archon Shards will apply to Regulators.

- Regulators can be equipped with secondary Arcane Enhancements and weapon mods.
- Regulators will maintain individual weapon buffs, such as those created by Galvanized Diffusion, Galvanized Shot, Secondary Outburst, even if Peacemaker is deactivated and recast.
- If Regulators and a regular secondary weapon are both equipped with Cascadia Flare or Conjunction Voltage, the arcane will apply to both weapons at the same value, rather than each weapon having to build individual stacks.
- Secondary Merciless and Secondary Deadhead do not share their effects.

- Regulators can be equipped with most secondary Exilus Mods (except Eject Magazine), but most effects are not applicable and/or requirements cannot be met (except Suppress, which does reduce the Noise Level stat).
- Riven Mods are not generated for Regulators.

- Peacemaker consumes 15 energy per second while active and will remain active until Mesa's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Mesa cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, and/or Wellspring while Peacemaker is active.
- Mesa can replenish energy from Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Rage, Hunter Adrenaline, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes, and Brightbonnet mushrooms while Peacemaker is active.
- Peacemaker's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- After a very brief "draw" period, Mesa enters the Peacemaker state, during which the only weapon she can use are the Regulators, the only other ability she can cast is Ballistic Battery, and movements are disallowed.
- Augment Mesa's Waltz allows Mesa to walk during Peacemaker at 50% movement speed and perform Dodges, but no other maneuvers.
- Although her lack of mobility renders her vulnerable to enemy attack, she will resist crowd-controlling status effects and knockdowns.
- Inertia at the time of casting will carry through to the Peacemaker state. If Peacemaker is cast while in the air, such as just after a Bullet Jump, she will complete the trajectory of that jump while in Peacemaker.
- If Peacemaker is cast while above a Brightbonnet mushroom, the mushroom's upward propulsion will repeatedly bounce Mesa into the air during Peacemaker, allowing her to remain a moving target while enjoying a wider view over the battlefield.

- Drawing the Regulators is affected by Casting Speed.
- Holstering the Regulators upon deactivation, however, is not affected.

- Peacemaker cannot target Capture Targets, Revenant's Enthrall, Arbitration Shield Drone and enemies within 10 meters from them, enemies inside a Nullifier Bubble, Deimos Genetrix, Infested Spawn Pods, Kyta Raknoids, Condrix, The Fragmented, Archon, nor the Teralyst, Gantulyst, or Hydrolyst.
- Peacemaker does not target invulnerable enemies.
- Peacemaker does not trigger "On Equip" mods like Vigorous Swap on either activation or deactivation.
- Peacemaker's shots can damage enemies across the Rift Plane.
- When activated the scenery around Mesa will be colored in a red hue. This effect is only noticable by Mesa's player.

Weapon

Main article: Regulators
The Regulators are Mesa and Mesa Prime's signature Exalted Weapon, summoned by activating the ability Peacemaker. Unlike other ranged weapons, the rapid-firing Regulators will automatically target and shoot at enemies within a large aiming circle directed by the player, removing the need for precise aim.

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Innate two and one polarities.
- Fires in 2-round bursts. Damage and fire rate ramp up for each burst fired.
- Ramp up damage bonus increments from +7.5% on the first two-round burst to a maximum of +150% after 20 bursts.
- This bonus is multiplicative to final damage, and resets when exiting Peacemaker.

- A fractional multiplier is applied to fire rate, with a minimum value of 0.25x, ramping up to 1.0x after 20 bursts.
- This multiplier is reset to its minimum value upon exiting Peacemaker, releasing the fire button, and when Peacemaker goes 2 seconds without firing.
- This effect is multiplied against all other modifiers to fire rate.

- Innate damage bonus from Mesa's Ability Strength.
- Value of the damage bonus is 150%, and stacks additively with damage mods like Hornet Strike.
- With the bonus from Shooting Gallery the Regulators can be expected to receive a sum 175% additive damage bonus from Mesa's abilities.

- See Peacemaker for more details.

Advantages

- Very high critical chance and critical multiplier.
- Very high fire rate of 14.80.
- This figure indicates the fire rate at maximum ramp up, which is attained after 20 two-round bursts have been fired.

- Pinpoint accuracy.
- Lowering the accuracy of Regulators via Magnum Force will not affect the placement of shots.

- Has infinite ammunition and does not need to reload.

Disadvantages

- Low status chance.
- Limited range of 50 meters.
- Before ramp up, has only a modest base fire rate of 3.70.
- Restricted method of aiming.
- User cannot move while active unless equipped with Mesa's Waltz.
- Most Exilus Mods effects are not applicable and/or their requirements cannot be met (except Suppress, which does reduce the Noise Level stat).
- Reflex Draw, ( Primed) Steady Hands, Strafing Slide: Regulators have no recoil and perfect accuracy.
- ( Primed) Pistol Ammo Mutation, Trick Mag: Regulators don't use ammo.
- Lethal Momentum: Regulators are hitscan weapons and don't fire projectiles.
- Energizing Shot: Regulators cannot shoot Energy Orbs.
- Air Recon, Hawk Eye, Spry Sights, Targeting Subsystem: Regulators cannot zoom.

- Since Regulators cannot zoom, they also cannot benefit from Embedded Catalyzer, Hydraulic Crosshairs, Pressurized Magazine, or Sharpened Bullets.
- Requires energy to activate and keep active.

Kit Interactions

- Peacemaker's Regulators can contribute to Ballistic Battery's charge counter, and Ballistic Battery can be deactivated while Peacemaker is active. Stored damage can be applied to a single Regulator shot.
- Peacemaker's Regulators are affected by Shooting Gallery's damage bonus.

Tips & Tricks

- Casting Shatter Shield before activating Peacemaker is recommended, due to Mesa becoming vulnerable to attacks while Peacemaker is active.
- While you cannot initiate movement while channeling Peacemaker, it can be cast while falling or immediately after initiating a bullet-jump, thus allowing you to start shooting before you actually arrive at your intended location. Channeling Peacemaker also makes you immune to the stagger effect you would normally receive upon hitting the ground after a long fall.
- You can also be moved around by certain entities/enemies, including the Domestik Drone.

- Limbo makes a great complement to Mesa when using this ability, as using Banish on Mesa allows her to become immune to enemy damage while also allowing her to damage enemies outside of the Rift Plane with her Peacemaker. If Cataclysm is cast anywhere with enemies inside Mesa may take damage if they fire at her, even she is not inside the Cataclysm but still under the effect of Banish.
- Similar to most Warframe abilities, Peacemaker targets the enemy's torso to inflict damage. It is particularly effective against Corrupted Vor as all Regulator pistol shots will always hit Vor's energy core, the only body part where he is vulnerable to damage.
- Slotting Crimson Archon Shards or Topaz Archon Shards for Secondary Critical Chance instead of Ability Strength may improve Peacemaker's overall damage output, as maximizing Ability Strength for base damage increases may give diminishing returns compared with improving crit rate.

Bugs

- Innate damage bonus is applied twice in the Simulacrum.
- Occasionally the draw animation for the Regulator pistols may not occur.
- Peacemaker's fire rate is severely hindered when the user's FPS is lower than 30, with lesser FPS resulting in lesser fire rate.
- Peacemaker can be utilized by Specters and still allow them to move to a certain degree of minimal slowdown.
- Regulators can remain active after energy is fully depleted if the warframe is occupied with another animation
