# Event Horizon (capacité de Orion & Sirius) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Event_Horizon

| 3 50 | Event Horizon Orion forms a drifting black hole, trapping enemies within its gravity. Hitting the black hole with Gravitic Slash or Coronal Ejection will extend its duration and change its trajectory: Gravitic Slash will direct it away from Orion, and Coronal Ejection will pull it towards Sirius. (HOLD) Swap to Sirius and use Light's Sanctuary. Introduced in Update 43.0 (2026-06-17) | Strength:300 / 450 / 600 / 750 ( Heat damage on collapse) 

Duration:10 / 12 / 16 / 18 s (duration) 5s (bonus duration) 

Range:5 / 5 / 8 / 8 m (black hole radius) 8 / 10 / 11 / 12 m (explosion radius) 

Misc: Ragdoll and pull 1 (black hole limit) ∞ (Punch Through vs enemies) 

Info

- Orion expends 50 energy to conjure a miniature black hole that drifts toward the reticle. Event Horizon's black hole lasts 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 seconds, spans a 5 / 5 / 8 / 8 meter radius that Ragdoll all enemies and pulls them into its umbral core. Upon expiry or recast, the black hole collapses explosively to inflict 300 / 450 / 600 / 750 Heat damage in a 8 / 10 / 11 / 12 meter radius. Only 1 Event Horizon may be active at once.
- Event Horizon has infinite Punch Through against entities and rebounds away from surfaces on contact. Melee weapon hits do not alter the black hole's trajectory.
- Holding this ability changes control to Sirius who executes Light's Sanctuary instead.
- When cast while stationary, moving sideways or backwards, Event Horizon drastically slows its travel speed. When cast while moving forward (default W ), Event Horizon accelerates toward the reticle and slows down after a few seconds.
- If the black hole phases into terrain or objects and cannot exit, it will prematurely collapse.
- Enemies dragged along can collide with objects and terrain, getting left behind if stuck on the obstacles.
- Event Horizon can group enemies together which allows Orion or Sirius to gain massive amount of Combo.

- Can be recast while active to instantly collapse the old black hole and conjure a new one.

Kit Interactions

- Hitting Event Horizon with Gravitic Slash causes it to be pushed away toward the reticle, while extending Event Horizon's duration by 5 seconds.
- Duration extention per cast is capped at 5 seconds, achievable with 100% Ability Duration. As such only values below 100% will have effect.
- Uncontrolled Orion's Gravitic Slash does not push Event Horizon.

- Hitting Event Horizon with Coronal Ejection causes it to be quickly pulled toward Sirius, while extending Event Horizon's duration by 5 seconds.
- Duration extention per cast is capped at 5 seconds, achievable with 100% Ability Duration. As such only values below 100% will have effect.
- Extention is based on Orion's Ability Duration, not Sirius's.
- Uncontrolled Sirius' Coronal Ejection does not pull Event Horizon.

- The black hole collapse triggers Jade Stars to attack enemies.
