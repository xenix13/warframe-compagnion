# Astral Shell (capacité de Orion & Sirius) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Astral_Shell

Duration:20 / 25 / 30 / 35 s (buff duration) 5 s (clone duration) 

Range:5 m (explosion radius) 

Misc: Damage negation on hit Decoy invulnerability 30 m (taunt radius) Stagger on explosion 5 s (clone cooldown) 

Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Orion expends 50 energy to surround himself and Sirius in an Astral Shell for 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 seconds. When hit, the incoming attack damage is negated and Orion projects a high Threat Level, Invulnerable decoy where he stands, taunting enemies within 30 meters to attack it and lasting for 5 seconds. Upon expiry, the decoy explodes dealing 50 / 100 / 150 / 200 Blast damage and big Stagger in a 5 meter radius. Decoy creation has a cooldown of 5 seconds.
- Holding this ability changes control to Sirius who executes Jade Stars instead.
- Tenno Self Stagger can trigger Astral Shell decoy creation.

- Can be recast while active to refresh duration and instantly bypass decoy creation cooldown.

Kit Interactions
Astral Shell's decoy detontation triggers Jade Stars to attack enemies.

Helminth

- Subsuming Sirius & Orion to the Helminth will offer Astral Shell, Jade Stars, and their augments to be used by other Warframes.
- However, Subsumed Astral Shell / Jade Stars cannot be used to swap Warframes.
