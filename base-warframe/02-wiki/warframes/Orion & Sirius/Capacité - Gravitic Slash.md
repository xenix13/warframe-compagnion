# Gravitic Slash (capacité de Orion & Sirius) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gravitic_Slash

Introduced in Update 43.0 (2026-06-17) | Strength:1,250 / 1,500 / 1,750 / 2,000 ( Slash damage) 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% (defense reduction) 100% ( Slash Status Chance) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:6 / 7 / 7 / 8 m 

Misc: 67.5° (angle) Stagger and Knockback ∞ (defense reduction duration) 

Info

- Orion expends 15 energy to swing Wrath in a wide frontal slash, cleaving enemies in a 67.5 degree angle and 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 meter radius, inflicting 1,250 / 1,500 / 1,750 / 2,000 Slash damage with 100% Status Chance, Stagger, Knockback, and permanently reducing their Armor and Shields by 35% / 40% / 45% / 50%.
- Enemy armor and shields are fully removed at 200% Ability Strength. With one Corrosive Projection, armor will be fully stripped at 164% Ability Strength.
- Defense strip only occurs once per ability cast.
- Shield strip removes a percentage of the current maximum value, as such it has diminishing returns on subsequent casts against the same enemies. Armor strip, however, is a percentage of an enemy's total, requiring only two casts at base strength to fully strip.
- Enemy shields can still regenerate with natural shield regeneration (such as Armis Ulta, Kuva Lich, or Sisters of Parvos) or while affected by regeneration buffs (such as Shield Ospreys).

Kit Interactions

- Holding this ability changes control to Sirius who executes Coronal Ejection instead.
- Gravitic Slash can trigger Jade Stars to attack enemies.
- Hitting Event Horizon with Gravitic Slash causes it to be pushed away toward the reticle, while extending Event Horizon's duration by 5 seconds.
- Duration extention per cast is capped at 5 seconds, achievable with 100% Ability Duration. As such only values below 100% will have effect.
- Uncontrolled Orion's Gravitic Slash does not push Event Horizon.
