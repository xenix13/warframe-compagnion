The crystalline citadel ascends. Citrine Prime captivates with refined radiance, her brilliant gems set with the finest gold filigree.

Release Date: September 23rd, 2026

Citrine Prime is the Primed variant of Citrine, featuring enhanced Armor and Energy, as well as an additional polarity. Citrine Prime was released alongside Steflos Prime and Corufell Prime.

## Notes

- Citrine Prime, compared to Citrine:
- Higher Armor (325 vs. 265)
- Higher Energy (150/200 vs. 130/180)
- Higher Starting Energy (75 vs. 50)
- Additional Polarity ( vs. )

- As with any other Prime Warframe, moving close to the location of an Orokin Void Death Orb will restore a pulse of 250 Energy to nearby allies, even if that Death Orb is inactive, destroyed, or non-present. This effect can only occur once per orb.
- Citrine Prime possesses Auxiliary Appearance Attachments titled Citrine Prime Adornments, featuring accessories over her shoulders, calves, and lower back.
