# Nourish (capacité de Grendel) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish

| 2 50 | Nourish Regenerate health as Grendel absorbs nourishment from enemies in his gut. While he digests, enemies that attack or are attacked by Grendel suffer Viral Damage and energy sources provide more energy. These buffs extend to squad mates. Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Grendel expends 50 energy to deal damage to the first digested victim equal to 20% of their total shield and health points, heal 600 / 700 / 800 / 1000 Health, and receive a buff that affects all allies within 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 meters for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds. This buff has three separate effects:
- All energy sources provide 1.5x / 1.65x / 1.8x / 2.0x more energy.
- Taking health or shield damage (but not overguard) causes an explosion with a 12 meter radius that deals 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Viral damage with 10 Viral stacks and Stagger to enemies within line of sight. This explosion has a 2 second cooldown.
- Adds +40% / +50% / +60% / +75% Viral damage to all weapons and certain abilities. See Universal Weapon Bonuses for more information.
- Note that this bonus Viral damage is additive with existing elemental and IPS mods. Think of it like adding a +75% Viral mod to your weapons (which is buffed by Ability Strength).

- Retaliation waves damage and break destructible Objects such as crates and resource caches.
- Can be recast while active, inflicting damage to the eaten enemy, healing Grendel, buffing new allies in range, and refreshing the buff's duration.

Kit Interactions

- Nourish requires at least 1 enemy swallowed by Feast to be alive in Grendel's gut to cast.

Helminth

- Subsuming Grendel to the Helminth will offer Nourish and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- Other Warframes are omitted from the swallowed enemy requirement, due to their inability to cast Feast.
- However, Subsumed Nourish does not provide healing, the explosion only applies 1 Viral stack, the energy multiplier is reduced to 1.3 / 1.39 / 1.48 / 1.6x, and Viral weapon bonus damage is reduced to +24% / +30% / +36% / +45%.
- Subsumed energy bonus is calculated with the following formula: 1 + ((0.3 / 0.39 / 0.48 / 0.6) × Ability Strength)

Augment

Main article: Hearty Nourishment
Hearty Nourishment is a Warframe Augment Mod for Grendel that allows Nourish to remove Status Effects and give him immunity based on the number of enemies in his stomach.
