# Feast (capacité de Grendel) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Feast

Duration:N/A 

Range:25 m (swallow range)

Info

- Grendel expends 25 energy to open his maw agape for 1.5 seconds, creating a powerful vacuum that disintegrates enemies near the aiming reticle within 25 meters and swallowing them whole to trap them inside Grendel's stomach. For each enemy swallowed by Feast, Grendel's maw stays open for an extra 1 second. Enemies within Grendel's stomach are slowly digested at a rate 1% of their total shields and health per second. A maximum of 5 enemies can be held within Grendel's stomach.
- Grendel can swallow multiple enemies by moving the aiming reticle to mark new targets with Feast, while his maw is still open.
- The five eaten enemies' profile icons and their remaining hitpoints are displayed as circular gauges, aligned in a cross-shape UI indicator above Grendel's ability icons.
- Feast also vacuums Pickups and thrown Hand Grenades near the aiming reticle for Grendel. Swallowed grenades will detonate inside Grendel's stomach and only damage nearby enemies.
- Enemy auras such as Ancient Healer or Guardian Eximus are disabled while within Grendel's stomach.
- Casting Speed appears to affect only the opening and closing animations, but not the 1.5 seconds during which the maw itself remains open, thus hardly making a difference to the overall casting speed of the ability.

- Hold down the ability key (default 1 ) to violently vomit all stored enemies out for no energy cost, inflicting 250 / 300 / 400 / 500 Toxin scaling damage with a 100% Toxin status chance to vomitted enemies and other enemies within 8 meters in a cone in front of Grendel.
- Toxin damage on vomit is affected by enemy level and amount of enemies stored.
- Feast's Toxin damage on vomit benefits from sum of enemies levels stored using the following expression:
Total Toxin Damage = Toxin Base Damage × ((ΣEnemy Level - 1) / 15 + 1)
- With 5 eaten enemies at level 101, Grendel will deal 500 × ((101 × 5 - 1) / 15 + 1) = 17,300 Toxin damage on vomit. Resulting damage is rounded down to the nearest whole number.

- Grendel cannot Feast on enemies grabbed by certain Warframe abilities, including:
- Nidus' Larva
- Vauban's Bastille in Vortex mode and Tether Coil from Minelayer
- Khora's Strangledome
- Hydroid's Tentacle Swarm
- Zephyr's Tornado and Airburst in suck mode
- Gauss' Mach Rush with Mach Crash
- Xaku's Gaze from The Lost
- Garuda's Blood Altar
- Trinity's Well of Life and Energy Vampire
- Sevagoth's Embrace by Sevagoth's Shadow from Exalted Shadow
- Titania's Spellbind and Lantern
- Nezha's Divine Spears
- Caliban's Sentient Wrath
- Follie's Plein Air
- Enemies in-flight from Grendel's Regurgitate,
- Enemies held or in-flight by Bonewidow's Meathook.
- Feasting on enemies affected by Nidus' Parasitic Link will erase them completely.

- Bosses cannot be Feasted.
- If Grendel crosses a Nullifier or falls Out of Bounds, enemies in his stomach are automatically expelled.
- Feasted enemies are considered removed from play, removing them from Exterminate waypoints and allowing Defense waves to advance, even if they are still alive.
- The Circuit's Corrupted Jackal still requires Feasted enemies to be vomited and killed to progress the fight.

Kit Interactions

- Grendel's passive grants him 250 armor per enemy alive in his stomach.
- Grendel must have at least 1 enemy alive in his stomach to cast Nourish and Regurgitate.
- Pulverize drains 0 energy if he has enemies in his stomach. The sphere also enlarges with engorged enemies, increasing the collision and ground slam's damage and radius.

Augment

Main article: Gourmand
Gourmand is a Warframe Augment Mod for Grendel's Feast to consume health instead of energy and increases armor for enemies in his gut.

Tips & Tricks

- Volatile Runners swallowed by Grendel’s Feast have a tendency to explode from within, causing Grendel to stagger.
- Increasing the Ability Range of Grendel’s Feast increases the effective Vacuum Range, this is especially useful for survival since feast can be used to Vacuum Life Support pickups from a fair distance without having to leave the current room.
