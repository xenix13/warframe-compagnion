Info

- Uriel expends 75 energy to split the souls of his currently living Passive Demons, draining 10% of their max Health, then hurling 1 soul per demon forward as homing projectiles with a lifespan of 5 seconds. Demonic souls seek enemies as they travel, bouncing between targets and exploding on contact with each enemy in a 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 meter radius, dealing 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Heat damage and applying 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% Damage Vulnerability to all enemies hit by the explosion, lasting indefinitely until death.
- On cast, split souls manifest 5m in front of Uriel for a moment before flying forward at 12m/s. During manifestation, Uriel can change his reticle direction to aim the split souls.
- If cast while on the ground, the souls will always be sent horizontally in the direction of the reticle. However if cast while airborne, the angle can be freely adjusted up and down.

- Each soul can directly hit up to 5 targets before it is destroyed.
- Souls travel toward the reticle direction, have infinite Punch Through against terrain, and home into nearby enemies in their flight path.
- Explosions require Line of Sight to damage enemies.
- Can be recast while active to launch more split souls.
- Demonium's health drain alone cannot kill Uriel's Demons.
- Cannot be cast without at least one living Demon.

- When casting this ability, Uriel says "KANUMMU!" (Voidtongue).

Kit Interactions

- Passive - Demonium unlocks his passive demon Vythelas. He approaches individual corpses to perform a ritual every 12 seconds, converting them into a floating collectible Demonium Rune that increases Fire Rate by 30% and grants an extra hit of 30% Heat damage, lasting for 10 seconds. Up to 3 Demonium Runes may be active at once. Uriel's allies may collect Demonium Runes to gain their buffs.
- Rune buff is shown as a Demonium buff icon beside Uriel's hitpoints indicator.
- Runes are tracked on the minimap using a unique runic icon.
- The strength of the buff is determined by the current when picking up the rune, including conditional buffs like Archon Intensify.
- Enemies killed with Blast or affected by Molecular Prime will not produce Demonium Rune.
- Replacing Demonium through the Helminth will not remove Vythelas. However, if Demonium is replaced, Vythelas's Rune buffs will no longer be affected by modded Ability Strength or Ability Duration, instead being locked to the default values.

- Infernalis - While active, souls projectiles from Demonium are set ablaze, emitting a ring of fire that inflicts Heat damage and status effect per second to nearby enemies.
- Brimstone - Picking up Vythelas' runes gives 5% charge. Each direct hit by a Demonium soul gives 0.75% charge, granting up to 3 * 0.75% * 5 = 11.25% charge per cast if all 3 souls hit 5 enemies each.
- If a soul directly hits less than 5 enemies and thus survives until the end of Demonium's duration, the soul will explode at the end of its duration, providing an additional 0.75% charge as if it had hit an additional enemy.
- Runes will grant charge regardless of which Uriel in the squad generated it.

Tips & Tricks

- While Infernalis is active, the demons will have a Heat aura around them, and their souls will apply that aura to enemies.
- Approaching affected enemies and Arbitration Shield Drones themselves will burn, exposing their location through walls.

- The extra instance of heat damage functions similarly to warframes such as Oraxia, Saryn, and Cyte-09, allowing for Uriel to make effective use of mods like Acid Shells or Combustion Beam to chain kill enemies with a single kill.
- Striking enemies with Demonium while using Infernalis will cause a Heat proc, resulting in Heat Inherit ruining the chain reaction. However, you can still use Demonium without Infernalis to obtain the damage vulnerability debuff and make the chaining more effective since Demonium by itself cannot cause a heat proc.

Bugs

- The explosion damage of Demonium does not scale with strength despite the tooltip showing so, leaving its explosion damage locked at 250. The damage vulnerability functions normally.
- Explosion damage can be calculated by the following formula: Explosion Damage = 125 * Ability Strength + 250

- The souls will despawn after flying about 100 meters without a target, causing them to no longer hit enemies or explode at the end of the ability's duration. This also stops them from generating Brimstone charge at the end of the ability's duration.
- The souls can hit body multipliers aside heads. On most units this will reflect as a critical hit, though the damage cannot be boosted with any critical damage bonuses. On Corpus (including Amalgam) units the souls can still hit body multipliers aside heads but it will not be shown as a critical hit.
- Demonium can target and debuff Arbitration Shield Drones (dealing 0 damage hits).
- While normally incarnons only receive the fire rate bonus of the rune, if at least two incarnons of any type (primary, secondary, or melee) are equipped and have charge for activation, activating one incarnon and then swapping to a second incarnon and activating it will apply the extra heat hit from the rune as well onto the incarnon swapped to. While melees can be used to trigger the effect on primaries or secondaries, they naturally receive no benefit from the rune.
