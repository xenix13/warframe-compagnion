Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Uriel expends 50 energy to channel demonic power from Hell to become Invulnerable during cast, cleansing himself of Status Effects while restoring his Health by 35% / 40% / 45% / 50%, with excess healing overflowing into Shields and then Overshields.
- The in-game ability stats are slightly misleading. The ability does not restore both 50% of max Health and 50% of max Shields. It only restores Health, with excess healing turning into Shields once Health is full.
- Restore amount is based on max Health value.
- Invulnerability duration scales with cast time, being shortened with increased casting speed.

- When casting this ability, Uriel says "TARAT!" (Voidtongue).

Kit Interactions

- - Remedium unlocks his Passive demon Gulphagor. He spirals in mid-air to lunge at an enemy target within 12 meters, latching his tentacled maw onto the victim's face for 10 seconds. Once latched, Gulphagor inflicts Stagger and 750 damage per tick at 4 ticks per second that bypasses shields but not armor or overguard, with a demonic mark shown above the enemy. Kill the marked enemy for 300% Health Orb and 100% Energy Orb drop chances, while leaving behind a circle of pain spanning a 4 meter radius lasting for 10 seconds; enemies within are burned for 200 Heat damage per 0.5 seconds with 500% Status Chance per hit. Gulphagor targets an enemy once every 4 seconds.
- Circle of pain damage is affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses applied to Uriel himself (not Gulphagor), such as Arcane Arachne, Nourish, and weapon swap mods like Holster Amp.
- Is incapable of critical hits even when buffed with flat critical bonuses like Arcane Avenger.

- Health and Energy Orb drop chances above 100% guarantee an orb while granting a chance for extra orb(s).
- Gulphagor is untargetable while latched onto an enemy.
- Only Gulphagor's latch damage bypasses shields, the circle of pain when killing a marked enemy does not. Furthermore, only the latch damage is affected by Summoner's Wrath.
- Replacing Remedium through the Helminth will not remove Gulphagor. However, if Remedium is replaced, Gulphagor will no longer be affected by modded ability stats and will use the default values.

- Living Demons from his Passive are healed by Remedium, while deceased Demons are fully resurrected.

- Remedium can trigger Archon Intensify by healing the Demons, even if Uriel himself is not injured.
- Remedium visually washes Void energy over Uriel and his Demons, as Uriel clasps his claws to unleash the revitalizing power.

Helminth
Subsuming Uriel to the Helminth will offer Remedium and its augments to be used by other Warframes.

- However, Subsumed Remedium's healing is reduced to 24% / 28% / 31% / 35% and does not summon Uriel's Demons.

Tips & Tricks

- Inputting Remedium slightly before equipping something with a restricted animation like an archgun will allow Uriel to remain invincible for most of the equip animation.

Bugs

- Gulphagor's latch DoT does not inflict status when he is buffed with a status buff such as Nourish despite Gulphagor visibly receiving the buff and his circle of pain applying status buffs properly.
- Gulphagor's latch damage over time does half the damage listed in the tooltip (e.g. 375 at base instead of 750). It can also hit body multipliers aside heads.
- Archon Vitality only adds 1 heat proc to Gulphagor's circle of pain despite the ability adding 5, leading to a total of 6 heat procs per hit rather than the expected 10.
- If Uriel dies while Remedium is casting, he will enter bleedout then instantly stand back up.
