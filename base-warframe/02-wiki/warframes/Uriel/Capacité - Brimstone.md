Info

- Once the Passive Demons' Fury gauge is full, Uriel can expend 75 energy and deplete the gauge to expand a domain of hellfire-burnt sulfur, turning Uriel Invulnerable during the casting animation, then setting his surroundings ablaze across a 10 / 12 / 14 / 15 meter area for 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 seconds. Each of Uriel's active demons will also cast their own field with identical stats and behavior to Uriel's, resulting in up to 4 overlapping fields. Brimstone initially inflicts 600 / 650 / 700 / 750 Heat damage with 100% Status Chance once every 0.25 seconds; every 0.5 seconds, the field's damage increases by 100% for each active field, up to a 10x damage multiplier cap.
- If cast in midair, Uriel will instantly drop to the ground to perform this ability.
- Brimstone visually expands a ring of updraft Void wind to indicate its borders, while surging stones and debris rise from within. The owner Uriel also sees a burning region of hellfire and molten sulfur in the ring that spreads across all surfaces.
- Brimstone cannot be dispelled by nullification or magnetic status and the remaining duration will count down normally when nullified as well rather than accelerating like Limbo's Cataclysm.
- When casting this ability, Uriel says "RAZUL-TARAT KANUMMU!" (Voidtongue).

Kit Interactions

- The Passive gauge is filled by the following actions:
- Infernum fireballs grant 1% charge on direct hits.
- Killing enemies chained by Catenach gives ?% charge.
- Killing enemies latched on by Gulphagor gives 15% charge.
- Picking up Vythelas' runes gives 5% charge.
- Works regardless of which Uriel in the squad generated the rune.

- Each direct hit by a Demonium soul gives 0.75% charge, granting up to 3 * 0.75% * 5 = 11.25% charge per cast if all 3 souls hit 5 enemies each.
- If a soul directly hits less than 5 enemies and thus survives until the end of Demonium's duration, the soul will explode at the end of its duration, providing an additional 0.75% charge as if it had hit an additional enemy.

Tips & Tricks

- Brimstone deals damage to Object Health, which allows it to temporarily break down Nullifier Crewman bubbles.
- The duplicate Brimstone fields produced by Uriel's summons can be spread out to cover far more area than normal, at the cost of some damage.
- This can be done with any movement technique that is sufficiently fast enough to move Uriel faster than his summons move or by allowing his summons to perform their actions and moving away from them (e.g. Catenach's chain, Gulphagor's latch, and Vythelas's sacrifice).
- One easy way to do it is as follows: pick a starting point for the first Brimstone field. From this point, if you move up and laterally utilizing any movement technique (e.g. bullet jump or Infernalis) and crash down to the ground with Brimstone, this should produce a Brimstone field roughly around the starting point and at the ending point that you land at after crashing to the ground with Brimstone, granted you did not touch the ground while moving between the two points.
- This will distort the shape of the field given enough distance between where Uriel was and where his summons were.
- Consequently, with enough distance, this can cause a gaps in between the multiple Brimstone fields.

- Avoid hitting the height limit of the map or slamming into a pitfall with Brimstone as this will often place the AoE too far away to hit enemies, resulting in a wasted charge.
- Due to the height limit and general short roofing of most tilesets, open world maps allow for easier and more effective usage of this technique.
