## Passive

This section is transcluded from Uriel/Abilities/Passive. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.

Uriel commands the Legion comprised of three fiendish Demons: Catenach, Gulphagor, and Vythelas. Demons are flying Health-based intangible Summons unlocked by Uriel's abilities gaining Rank, spawning with Uriel into the mission lasting indefinitely until killed, after which they automatically resurrect in 60 seconds. Demons draw enemy fire with their notable Threat Level whilst following Uriel wherever he roams, and perform specific devilish deeds to assist and amplify Uriel.

Uriel's Legion possesses various unique and shared attributes highlighted in the tabs below:

- Demons' maximum Health scales with enemy levels in the mission.
- Catenach and Gulphagor will deal many attacks when executing their moves, rapidly maxing out Arcane Camisado. Vythelas does not attack.
- Demons' alive or dead status, health bars and Brimstone gauge are displayed as a stylized triangular UI above Uriel's ability icons.
  - Unlike regular allies and enemy units, Demons' hitpoints bar and names are not shown above them when on the reticle.
- When outside of combat, Demons dynamically orient and position themselves toward Uriel's reticle direction, while floating near his sides to avoid obstructing his sight. When engaged with enemies, they fly toward their targets.
  - Demons position in a triad formation in front of Uriel. Catenach on the left and Vythelas on the right, 3 meters outward, whilst Gulphagor hovers in the air to the right, 4 meters outward.
  - Demons will remain near Uriel during Transference.
  - For the purposes of theorem arcanes (e.g. Theorem Contagion) Uriel's demons are classified as Uriel, allowing them to stack the arcanes for Uriel even when he isn't standing in the zone, provided they are.
- If Demons are more than 30 meters away from Uriel, they teleport near him in a burst of hellfire when he is on the ground. Catenach's chain channeling and Gulphagor's latching are interrupted if Uriel moves out of range, whilst Vythelas will finish his rune ritual before he teleports.
- Demons will disappear when entering Archwing.
  - When exiting Archwing in Landscapes, Demons will reappear at ground level.
- When Uriel is flying during Infernalis or otherwise airborne, Demons attempt to follow him from the ground below his current location, or teleport to him if too far away.
- Replacing an ability with Helminth will not remove any respective Demon. However, if replaced, the Demon will no longer be affected by modded ability stats and will use their default values.
  - Gloom can heal the Demons, however only Gulphagor can be meaningfully healed.
  - Vazarin's Protective Sling can heal the Demons even when Remedium is replaced.
- Demons do not appear in Base of Operations and Hubs. They are also absent from missions where Uriel's passive is disabled, such as Dog Days.
- During any Emote by Uriel, Demons remain stationary to perform their own unique Emotes:
  - Catenach curls his spinal cord and shivers, then flicks his cord forward.
  - Gulphagor growls and angrily opens his maw.
  - Vythelas practices his ritual, raising his arms high then bows down.

- Catenach the skeletal demon unlocks with Infernalis (Rank 0) and his stats display in its ability tooltip. His status icon is at the top of the triangle UI element.
- When Catenach is within 6 meters of an enemy target, and at least 2 enemies are 15 meters from each other, he flicks a cursed chain attached to his spinal cord to shackle them together, enchaining total 5 enemies for 10 seconds. Enchained enemies receive Stagger on chain contact, then 100 damage per second and 50%Slow. Any damage dealt to an enchained enemy is spread to other enchained enemies at 100% of the initial damage instance.
  - Slow is capped at 95%, achievable with 190% Ability Strength.
  - Catenach hovers in place several meters above his primary target while enchaining them. Catenach is still targetable by enemies and vulnerable to damage while enchained. While enchained, if teleported to Uriel from far away, Catenach is freed to move about and follow him.
  - Catenach will not target already enchained enemies. Enchain must end before Catenach casts it on a new group of enemies.
  - Status Effects applied to enchained enemies will not transfer, however they will share any ongoing Status Damage.
  - Summoner's Wrath will not increase transferred damage.

- Gulphagor the avian demon unlocks with Remedium (Rank 3) and his stats display in its ability tooltip. His status icon is at the left of the triangle UI element.
- He spirals in mid-air to lunge at an enemy target within 12 meters, latching his tentacled maw onto the victim's face for 10 seconds. Once latched, Gulphagor inflicts Stagger and 750 damage per tick at 4 ticks per second, with a demonic mark shown above the enemy. Kill the marked enemy for 300% Health Orb and 100% Energy Orb drop chances, while leaving behind a circle of pain spanning a 4 meter radius lasting for 10 seconds; enemies within are burned for 200 Heat damage per 0.5 seconds with 500% Status Chance per hit. Gulphagor targets an enemy once every 4 seconds.
  - Circle of pain damage is affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses applied to Uriel himself (not Gulphagor), such as Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
    - Is incapable of critical hits even when buffed with flat critical bonuses like Arcane Avenger.
  - Health and Energy Orb drop chances above 100% guarantee an orb while granting a chance for extra orb(s).
  - Gulphagor is untargetable while latched onto an enemy.
  - Gulphagor prioritizes specialist enemies such as Eximus, and can latch onto Overguard-protected enemies.

- Vythelas the impish demon unlocks with Demonium (Rank 5) and his stats display in its ability tooltip. His status icon is at the right of the triangle UI element.
- He approaches individual corpses to perform a ritual every 12 seconds, converting them into a floating collectible Demonium Rune that increases Fire Rate by 30% and grants an Extra Hit of 30% Heat damage with a guaranteed Heat status effect on every hit, lasting for 10 seconds. Up to 3 Demonium Runes may be active at once. Uriel's allies may collect Demonium Runes to gain their buffs.
  - Rune buff is shown as a Demonium buff icon beside Uriel's hitpoints indicator.
  - Runes are tracked on the minimap using a unique runic icon.
  - The Heat damage is applied as an Extra Hit.
    - This Extra Hit is exclusive to primary and secondary weapons. Weapons in Incarnon Form only receive the fire rate buff. Melee weapons, archguns and archmelees are unaffected.
    - Archon Vitality will not increase the number of Heat status effects applied by Demonium.
    - Most weapon hits will trigger an Extra Hit, including Acid Shells and Concealed Explosives.
  - If Demonium is replaced with another ability via Helminth, Vythelas will still spawn Demonium Runes as normal. However, the Rune buff will no longer scale with your Ability Strength or Ability Duration, instead staying at its base 30% damage increase and 10s duration.
  - Elemental bonus does not override the Toxin damage bonus from Oraxia's Silken Stride.
  - Elemental bonus can be overwritten by the Heat damage bonus from Cyte-09's Resupply, and vice versa.
    - However, upon reloading to clear Resupply and Demonium is still active, Demonium's Heat bonus will be reapplied.

- Ability Synergy:
  - Infernalis also ignites the Demons and their Demonium split souls, surrounding them in a sphere of flames.
  - Remedium heals or revives all of Uriel's Demons. Health Orbs cannot heal Demons.
  - Demonium channels the souls of Uriel's Demons, splitting them by draining 10% health from each Demon, then hurls the split souls at enemies. Demonium's health drain alone cannot kill Uriel's Demons. Number of split souls directly correspond to each living Demon. Cannot be cast without at least one living Demon.
  - Brimstone requires the full fury of Uriel's Demons to activate, tracked by the Passive's gauge below the Demons' status icons. Build the Demons' fury by killing enemies chained by Catenach and latched by Gulphagor, picking up Vythelas' runes, and striking enemies with Demonium's split souls.
- Bugs:
  - When returning back to the Drifter after using the limited transference power in Duviri, Uriel's summons will remain and become permanently doubled. All the summons will remain where Uriel was last before returning back to the Drifter and will be unable to perform any actions aside the Gulphagors who can still latch and spawn circles of pain. The summons are fully functional in the Undercroft however and will follow Uriel.
  - In some missions, such as the railjack mission Scoria's Angel on Uranus or the Granum Void on Corpus tilesets, Uriel's summons will freeze and become stacked over one another resulting in Uriel's passive being almost entirely disabled (the demons will still draw aggro however). This can be fixed by letting the affected demons die and respawning them with Remedium.

Info

- Uriel expends 25 energy to light himself ablaze with hellfire for 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 seconds, inflicting 1,500 Heat damage on cast and then 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Heat damage with 100% Status Chance per second to enemies within 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2 meters. While Infernalis is active, rolling airborne will cause Uriel to take as a flying fiery comet, moving at 16 m/s and gaining Knockdown and Stagger immunity with flight-based movement as he continuously spirals. Hold down the ability button to end Infernalis early.
  - Firing ranged weapons, flying into a surface, meleeing (including ground slams to forcibly land), double jumping, rolling, or aim gliding will stop Uriel's flight.
    - Reload, weapon switching, and Alternate Fire functions do not interrupt flight, neither firing using the mouse scroll wheel.
    - Double jump and Aim Glide duration are refreshed when entering flight mode.
  - While in flight mode, Uriel activates "while airborne" conditions (e.g. Aerodynamic, Agility Drift), but not "aim glide" conditions (e.g. Aero Set).
  - Abilities cannot be cast while in flight mode.
  - Mission Items will not be dropped during flight, and Uriel can interact with touch-based pickups (e.g. Convergence Orb).
  - Flight speed is affected by Parkour Velocity and Dodge Speed, which stack multiplicatively with each other.
    - Temporary buffs such as Arcane Consequence, Infested Mobility, and Proton Pulse will be snapshotted at the start of Uriel's flight and be maintained until he cancels it, even if the buff expires in the meantime.
    - Not affected by Movement Speed bonuses such as Molt and Speed.
  - While activated by rolling, flying does not count as actively rolling, and will not constantly re-trigger bonuses such as Arcane Circumvent.
    - However, the initiation of flight does count as a roll so if the roll button is quickly double-tapped it will maintain flight and trigger roll effects at will.
- When casting this ability, Uriel says "RAZUL!" (Voidtongue).

- Infernalis unlocks his Passive demon Catenach. When Catenach is within 6 meters of an enemy target, and at least 2 enemies are 15 meters from each other, he flicks a cursed chain attached to his spinal cord to shackle them together, enchaining total 5 enemies for 10 seconds. Enchained enemies receive 100 damage per second and 50%Slow. Any damage dealt to an enchained enemy is spread to other enchained enemies at 100% of the initial damage instance.
  - Slow is capped at 95%, achievable with 190% Ability Strength.
  - Catenach hovers in place several meters above his primary target while enchaining them. Catenach is still targetable by enemies and vulnerable to damage while enchained. While enchained, if teleported to Uriel from far away, Catenach is freed to move about and follow him.
  - Catenach will not target already enchained enemies. Enchain must end before Catenach casts it on a new group of enemies.
  - Replacing Infernalis through the Helminth will not remove Catenach. However, if Infernalis is replaced, Catenach will no longer be affected by modded ability stats and will use the default values.
- Demons from his Passive and Demonium are also set ablaze and apply a Heat aura to enemies.

Augment

- Main article: Infernum

Infernum is a Warframe Augment Mod for Uriel's Infernalis that causes volleys of fireballs to be released during Infernalis's flight, with the volleys growing larger for a short time after collecting a Demonium Rune.

Tips & Tricks

- Use this ability to quickly move between pickups and objectives (e.g. Power Cells for Excavators).
- The constant Heat aura from Infernalis can break crates.
- With enough Ability Range, the aura will break Sensor Bars and vents before Uriel walks or flies through them.
- Increase Casting Speed or Aim Glide duration for easier shooting of enemies and casting of Demonium in the air.
- Equip "while airbone" damage reduction mods (e.g. Aviator) to increase survivability.
- The Coda Catabolyst has particularly good synergy with Infernalis due to a bug with the reload grenades that allows them to be spammed when the reload animation is cancelled by a roll.

 | 3 75 | Demonium Uriel rips out the souls of his demons, draining their health, and sends them in search of new victims. Souls explode on contact with an enemy, rendering it vulnerable to Damage. Introduced in Update 41.0 (2025-12-10) | Strength:100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Heat damage 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% Damage Vulnerability 30% Vythelas Rune Fire Rate 30% Vythelas Rune Bonus Heat Damage

Info

- Uriel expends 75 energy to split the souls of his currently living Passive Demons, draining 10% of their max Health, then hurling 1 soul per demon forward as homing projectiles with a lifespan of 5 seconds. Demonic souls seek enemies as they travel, bouncing between targets and exploding on contact with each enemy in a 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 meter radius, dealing 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Heat damage and applying 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% Damage Vulnerability to all enemies hit by the explosion, lasting indefinitely until death.
  - On cast, split souls manifest 5m in front of Uriel for a moment before flying forward at 12m/s. During manifestation, Uriel can change his reticle direction to aim the split souls.
    - If cast while on the ground, the souls will always be sent horizontally in the direction of the reticle. However if cast while airborne, the angle can be freely adjusted up and down.
  - Each soul can directly hit up to 5 targets before it is destroyed.
  - Souls travel toward the reticle direction, have infinite Punch Through against terrain, and home into nearby enemies in their flight path.
  - Explosions require Line of Sight to damage enemies.
  - Can be recast while active to launch more split souls.
  - Demonium's health drain alone cannot kill Uriel's Demons.

Kit Interactions

- Passive - Demonium unlocks his passive demon Vythelas. He approaches individual corpses to perform a ritual every 12 seconds, converting them into a floating collectible Demonium Rune that increases Fire Rate by 30% and grants an extra hit of 30% Heat damage, lasting for 10 seconds. Up to 3 Demonium Runes may be active at once. Uriel's allies may collect Demonium Runes to gain their buffs.
  - Rune buff is shown as a Demonium buff icon beside Uriel's hitpoints indicator.
  - Runes are tracked on the minimap using a unique runic icon.
  - The strength of the buff is determined by the current when picking up the rune, including conditional buffs like Archon Intensify.
  - Enemies killed with Blast or affected by Molecular Prime will not produce Demonium Rune.
  - Replacing Demonium through the Helminth will not remove Vythelas. However, if Demonium is replaced, Vythelas's Rune buffs will no longer be affected by modded Ability Strength or Ability Duration, instead being locked to the default values.
- Infernalis - While active, souls projectiles from Demonium are set ablaze, emitting a ring of fire that inflicts Heat damage and status effect per second to nearby enemies.
- Brimstone - Picking up Vythelas' runes gives 5% charge. Each direct hit by a Demonium soul gives 0.75% charge, granting up to 3 * 0.75% * 5 = 11.25% charge per cast if all 3 souls hit 5 enemies each.
  - If a soul directly hits less than 5 enemies and thus survives until the end of Demonium's duration, the soul will explode at the end of its duration, providing an additional 0.75% charge as if it had hit an additional enemy.
  - Runes will grant charge regardless of which Uriel in the squad generated it.

Tips & Tricks

- While Infernalis is active, the demons will have a Heat aura around them, and their souls will apply that aura to enemies.
  - Approaching affected enemies and Arbitration Shield Drones themselves will burn, exposing their location through walls.
- The extra instance of heat damage functions similarly to warframes such as Oraxia, Saryn, and Cyte-09, allowing for Uriel to make effective use of mods like Acid Shells or Combustion Beam to chain kill enemies with a single kill.
  - Striking enemies with Demonium while using Infernalis will cause a Heat proc, resulting in Heat Inherit ruining the chain reaction. However, you can still use Demonium without Infernalis to obtain the damage vulnerability debuff and make the chaining more effective since Demonium by itself cannot cause a heat proc.

Maximization

Demonium

Bugs

- The explosion damage of Demonium does not scale with strength despite the tooltip showing so, leaving its explosion damage locked at 250. The damage vulnerability functions normally.
  - Explosion damage can be calculated by the following formula: Explosion Damage = 125 * Ability Strength + 250
- The souls will despawn after flying about 100 meters without a target, causing them to no longer hit enemies or explode at the end of the ability's duration. This also stops them from generating Brimstone charge at the end of the ability's duration.
- The souls can hit body multipliers aside heads. On most units this will reflect as a critical hit, though the damage cannot be boosted with any critical damage bonuses. On Corpus (including Amalgam) units the souls can still hit body multipliers aside heads but it will not be shown as a critical hit.
- Demonium can target and debuff Arbitration Shield Drones (dealing 0 damage hits).
- While normally incarnons only receive the fire rate bonus of the rune, if at least two incarnons of any type (primary, secondary, or melee) are equipped and have charge for activation, activating one incarnon and then swapping to a second incarnon and activating it will apply the extra heat hit from the rune as well onto the incarnon swapped to. While melees can be used to trigger the effect on primaries or secondaries, they naturally receive no benefit from the rune.

  - Picking up Vythelas' runes gives 5% charge.
    - Works regardless of which Uriel in the squad generated the rune.
  - Each direct hit by a Demonium soul gives 0.75% charge, granting up to 3 * 0.75% * 5 = 11.25% charge per cast if all 3 souls hit 5 enemies each.
    - If a soul directly hits less than 5 enemies and thus survives until the end of Demonium's duration, the soul will explode at the end of its duration, providing an additional 0.75% charge as if it had hit an additional enemy.

Tips & Tricks

- Brimstone deals damage to Object Health, which allows it to temporarily break down Nullifier Crewman bubbles.
- The duplicate Brimstone fields produced by Uriel's summons can be spread out to cover far more area than normal, at the cost of some damage.
  - This can be done with any movement technique that is sufficiently fast enough to move Uriel faster than his summons move or by allowing his summons to perform their actions and moving away from them (e.g. Catenach's chain, Gulphagor's latch, and Vythelas's sacrifice).
  - One easy way to do it is as follows: pick a starting point for the first Brimstone field. From this point, if you move up and laterally utilizing any movement technique (e.g. bullet jump or Infernalis) and crash down to the ground with Brimstone, this should produce a Brimstone field roughly around the starting point and at the ending point that you land at after crashing to the ground with Brimstone, granted you did not touch the ground while moving between the two points.
  - This will distort the shape of the field given enough distance between where Uriel was and where his summons were.
    - Consequently, with enough distance, this can cause a gaps in between the multiple Brimstone fields.
  - Avoid hitting the height limit of the map or slamming into a pitfall with Brimstone as this will often place the AoE too far away to hit enemies, resulting in a wasted charge.
    - Due to the height limit and general short roofing of most tilesets, open world maps allow for easier and more effective usage of this technique.
