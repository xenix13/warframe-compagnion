# Nokko/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nokko/Abilities

## Passive

Upon receiving fatal damage, if Nokko has at least one Stinkbrain or Brightbonnet placed, he will trigger Vital Decay to transform into his Invulnerable Sprodling form instead of entering Bleedout. While in this form, Nokko will revive if he can reach one of his active mushrooms within 15 seconds; failing to reach a mushroom in time will result in immediate death. Reviving has a 3-second animation, during which Nokko is invulnerable and gains 100% Health and Shields. Once reborn, Nokko remains invulnerable for an additional 1 second.

- If Nokko does not have any active mushrooms upon receiving fatal damage, Vital Decay will not activate and he will enter Bleedout normally.
- During Vital Decay, active mushrooms will glow and their durations are paused.
- Successfully reviving will immediately destroy all mushrooms.
- If all mushrooms are destroyed (for example, by a Nullifier Bubble), Vital Decay will end and Nokko will die.
- Nullifier Bubbles have no effect on Nokko's Sprodling form during Vital Decay.

- During Vital Decay, holding the Melee input will prematurely end Vital Decay, killing Nokko.

- While Nokko is a Sprodling, Fungal Spores will spawn nearby that grant a boost of Movement Speed, similar to Reroot.
- Fungal Spores spawned during Vital Decay do not restore health, unlike Reroot.

- Squadmates cannot directly interact with Nokko's Sprodling form to revive him.
- Jade can revive Nokko if she aims Ophanim Eyes where Nokko died, not where the Sprodling currently is.
- Nokko's Revival Progress is not communicated to Jade, and she will only know the location of his body via the minimap - it is not visible, and there is no waypoint in the HUD.
- Nokko will revive at the location where he died, and not the location of his Sprodling.

- A Sentinel with Sacrifice equipped can still trigger during Vital Decay, but this does not pause Vital Decay's duration and Sacrifice's revival progress is not communicated to Nokko.

- Vital Decay's duration is not affected by Bleedout Duration mods.
- Vital Decay does not function in Arbitrations; Nokko does not enter the Sprodling form at all and immediately dies.
- Vital Decay's revive time is drastically shortened with each revive in Archon Hunt, Netracells, Deep Archimedea, Temporal Archimedea, and any Hardmode Boss, going down to 3 seconds after the first revive and then 1 second after the second.
- Unairu's Last Gasp will not prompt on Vital Decay.
- The in-game functionality differs from official explanations:
- The patch notes and the official description states that if Nokko fails to reach a mushroom in time, he will automatically use a self-revive; however, in the actual game the player must manually choose to expend a self-revive.

Info

- Nokko expends 25 energy to throw an arcing bouncy spore that sprouts a poisonous mushroom on contact with a horizontal surface. The Stinkbrain is an intangible invulnerable object that lasts for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds, instantly releasing a pulse on creation, then periodically pulsing every 3 seconds over a range of 5 meters. Stinkbrain's pulse inflicts 175 / 200 / 225 / 250 Viral damage with 100% Viral Status Chance to enemies in range and Line of Sight, while lulling them to Sleep for 3 seconds. Enemies affected by Stinkbrain sleep are opened to Melee Finishers and receive 100% Damage Vulnerability to Finisher damage. A maximum of 4 Stinkbrains can be placed.
- Number of active Stinkbrains are tracked on the ability icon.
- If Stinkbrain cannot be created at the spore landing zone, energy cost is fully refunded to Nokko.
- Stinkbrain spores will bounce off walls and other mushrooms.
- Stinkbrain appears on the minimap using its unique mushroom icon. This icon is not distinct from Brightbonnet mushrooms.
- Nokko and allied players can jump on Stinkbrain Mushrooms to be propelled into the air, gaining bonus Double Jump velocity and immunity to Hard Landing until they next touch the ground.
- Each bounce also refreshes the player's ability to Double Jump or Bullet Jump, and allows the player to start a fresh aim glide.
- Jumping on a mushroom will force a pulse and place the mushroom on cooldown, which will cause subsequent players to pass through the mushroom instead of being propelled. The cooldown is visually indicated by the mushroom's brief glow and jiggle.

- Stinkbrain will despawn early if it is not rooted on a horizontal surface. Some Tilesets utilize shifting platforms and elevators, and mushrooms grown by Nokko do not move from their landing zone.

Kit Interactions

- Stinkbrains will become Invigorated when hit by Sporespring or Arbucep projectiles.
- An Invigorated Stinkbrain has its pulse interval reduced to 1.5 seconds.
- A Stinkbrain will remain Invigorated until it is hopped on, at which point it will return to normal and revert to its non-Invigorated pulse interval.
- Each time a Stinkbrain transitions from normal to Invigorated, its duration is reset to the full value.
- The first time a Stinkbrain becomes Invigorated, it will permanently receive a glowing rim around its silhouette. This glow will remain even if the Stinkbrain loses its Invigoration.
- Each time a Stinkbrain is Invigorated, it will visibly emit glowing spores until ⅔ of its duration has passed.
- While Arbucep is Nokko's Signature Weapon, this interaction is not unique to Nokko. Arbuceps held by any squadmate will Invigorate the Stinkbrains of any other squadmate.

- In addition to jumping, Sporespring and Arbucep projectiles will force a pulse on each impact.
- Reroot's Sprodling form can bounce off of Stinkbrains under lower ceilings than full-sized Warframes.
- Upon entering Bleedout with an active Stinkbrain, Nokko passively triggers Vital Decay to revert to his Sprodling form for 15 seconds and can revive himself by touching a previously placed Stinkbrain.
- Stinkbrains have their durations paused during Vital Decay, and all Stinkbrains are removed upon revival.

Tips & Tricks

- Throw a Stinkbrain into a crowd of enemies to instantly inflict Viral damage, status, and Sleep to immobilize them where they stand.
- Sleeping enemies are easy targets for Weak Point hits such as headshots, and are exposed to melee Finisher attacks that often guarantee a kill.
- Enemies with Overguard only receive Viral damage and status, but will be forced to Sleep by subsequent pulses once the Overguard is destroyed.

- Throw Stinkbrain in front of you and jump to bounce into the air, then Double Jump to gain height advantage for casting other abilities, aerial gunfire, or transition into a melee/heavy ground slam to increase its slam radius drastically.
- Jump on Stinkbrain to repeated bounce into the air, reducing enemy accuracy against Nokko.
- Create a Stinkbrain under a low ceiling, such as Tilesets corridors and hallways, then bounce on it to force multiple rapid pulses that quickly stack up Viral status with increasing damage to enemy Health.
- Reroot Sprodling form is ideal for this interaction, since its small size allows Nokko to fit into small spaces that normal Warframes cannot.
- Keep in mind Stinkbrains require Line of Sight and Sprodling form is untargetable by enemies. Place the Stinkbrain behind a doorway or in an open chokepoint where enemies must travel through.

- Create up to four Stinkbrains in separate areas for area denial, or cluster them close together to stack Viral damage and statuses quickly.
- Note that each stack of Viral has its own 6-second timer and adding new Viral stacks will not refresh the timers of older ones. So if you want to get a lot of Viral stacks on enemies, you can stack multiple Stinkbrains, bounce on them to increase their pulse rate, or use Sporespring to increase pulses directly via bounces and permanently via invigoration.
- To maximize the Viral status output and Sleep crowd control, newly created Stinkbrains should be hit by a Sporespring as soon as possible to permanently halve the pulse interval for that mushroom.
- Stinkbrains allow Sporespring to bounce to them to extend its enemy detection range. Position the mushrooms spread across a room for Sporesprings to trampoline off them for maximum destructive chaos!
- Sporesprings in turn trigger Stinkbrains to pulse on each bounce, freeing Nokko to do other things or join in the bouncy fun.

Info

- Nokko expends 50 energy to throw an arcing bouncy spore that sprouts a rejuvenating mushroom on contact with a horizontal surface, inflicting 200 damage and Stagger to enemies within 6 meters of the sprouting location. The Brightbonnet is an intangible invulnerable object that lasts for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds, instantly releasing a pulse on creation, then periodically pulsing every 3 seconds over a range of 15 meters to restore 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 Energy points to Nokko and allies, while increasing their Ability Strength by 15% / 20% / 25% / 30% for 5 seconds. A maximum of 2 Brightbonnets can be placed.
- Number of active Brightbonnets are tracked on the ability icon.
- If a Brightbonnet Mushroom cannot be created where the spore lands, the energy cost is refunded.
- Brightbonnet spores will bounce off walls and other mushrooms.
- Brightbonnet appears on the minimap using its unique mushroom icon. This icon is not distinct from Stinkbrain mushrooms.
- The Ability Strength bonus is capped at 150%, which is doubled to 300% while Invigorated by the Sporespring or Arbucep synergy interaction.
- The Ability Strength bonus will refresh on all allies if any ally is hit by subsequent pulses.
- Players will keep the single strongest Brightbonnet Strength buff that has been applied to them, and pulses from weaker Brightbonnets will refresh the Ability Strength bonus from stronger Brightbonnets.

- The Ability Strength bonus from Brightbonnets will not increase the Ability Strength bonus of Brightbonnets, but it will increase the amount of energy generated by any new Brightbonnets cast while affected by the bonus.
- Brightbonnet's energy restoration will affect Warframes (even with channeled abilities active), Archwings, and Railjacks, but not Necramechs.
- Nokko and allied players can jump on Brightbonnet Mushrooms to be propelled into the air, gaining bonus Double Jump velocity and immunity to Hard Landing until they next touch the ground.
- Each bounce also refreshes the player's ability to Double Jump or Bullet Jump, and allows the player to start a fresh aim glide.
- Jumping on a mushroom will force a pulse and place the mushroom on cooldown, which will cause subsequent players to pass through the mushroom instead of being propelled. The cooldown is visually indicated by the mushroom's brief glow and jiggle.

- Brightbonnet will despawn early if it is not rooted on a horizontal surface. Some Tilesets utilize shifting platforms and elevators, and mushrooms grown by Nokko do not move from their landing zone.

Kit Interactions

- Brightbonnets will become Invigorated when hit by Sporespring or Arbucep projectiles.
- An Invigorated Brightbonnet has its pulse interval reduced to 1.5 seconds and provides twice the Ability Strength buff.
- A Brightbonnet will remain Invigorated until an allied Warframe bounces upon it, at which point it will return to normal and revert to its non-Invigorated pulse interval and Ability Strength bonus.
- As long as a player does not lose their Ability Strength bonus, pulses from the de-Invigorated Brightbonnet will continue to refresh the Invigorated Ability Strength bonus.

- Each time a Brightbonnet transitions from normal to Invigorated, its duration is reset to the full value.
- The first time a Brightbonnet becomes Invigorated, it will permanently receive a glowing rim around its silhouette. This glow will remain even if the Brightbonnet loses its Invigoration.
- Each time a Brightbonnet is Invigorated, it will visibly emit glowing spores until ⅔ of its duration has passed.
- While Arbucep is Nokko's Signature Weapon, this interaction is not unique to Nokko. Arbuceps held by any squadmate will Invigorate the Brightbonnets of any other squadmate.

- In addition to jumping, Sporespring and Arbucep projectiles will also force a pulse on each impact.
- Reroot's Sprodling form can bounce off of Brightbonnets under lower ceilings than full-sized Warframes.
- Upon entering Bleedout with an active Brightbonnet, Nokko passively triggers Vital Decay to revert to his Sprodling form for 15 seconds and can revive himself by touching a previously placed Brightbonnet.
- Brightbonnets have their durations paused during Vital Decay, and all Brightbonnets are removed upon a successful self-revival.

Helminth

- Subsuming Nokko to the Helminth will offer a Brightbonnet and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- The Ability Strength bonus is diminished to 10% / 13% / 17% / 20% and energy restore is reduced to 6.67 / 8 / 8.67 / 10.
- The Ability Strength bonus cap is diminished to 100%, which is still doubled if Invigorated by Arbucep.

- Throw Brightbonnet in front of you and jump to bounce into the air, then Double Jump to gain height advantage for casting other abilities, aerial gunfire, or transition into a melee/heavy ground slam to increase its slam radius drastically.

- Reroot Sprodling form is ideal for this interaction, since its small size allows Nokko to fit into small spaces that normal Warframes cannot.

- Create two Brightbonnets side-by-side and bounce off of them in the middle, forcing both mushrooms to pulse.
- To maximize the Ability Strength bonus and energy production, newly created Brightbonnets should be hit by a Sporespring as soon as possible to permanently double the bonus and halve the pulse interval for that mushroom.
- Brightbonnets allow Sporespring to bounce to them to extend its enemy detection range. Position the mushrooms spread across a room for Sporesprings to trampoline off them for maximum destructive chaos!
- Sporesprings in turn trigger Brightbonnets to pulse on each bounce, freeing Nokko to do other things or join in the bouncy fun.

Info

- Nokko expends 50 energy to burrow into the ground leaving his mushroom cap above the surface, then uproot as a small Sprodling for 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 seconds. As a Sprodling, Nokko moves at 4.8 m/s, becomes Invulnerable, Invisible, intangible, immune to Status Effects and hard landings, while he restores 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 Health and Shields per second which can flow into Overshields. Press the ability button again, press Quick Melee (default E ), or on duration end to regrow into Warframe form.
- While in Sprodling form, Nokko's weapons are disabled and he cannot cast other abilities (including Transference).
- His Companion becomes invulnerable and removed from play, though Mod effects such as Vacuum/ Fetch continue to function.
- Reroot's duration timer is shown on the ability icon and as a buff icon beside Nokko's hitpoints indicator.
- Sprodling form can perform limited parkour Maneuvers, such as jump, double jump, triple jump, wall jump and dodge. Rolling (default L Shift ) grants a short burst of movement speed.
- Rolling does not activate "on roll" conditions (e.g. Melee Careen, Arcane Circumvent).

- Sprodling form cannot Sprint and is thus unaffected by Sprint Speed mods.
- Sprodling form is unable to accomplish any action that requires the Use key (default X ) while Reroot is active, thus cannot interact with consoles, pick up carriables such as datamasses, or revive allies from bleedout.
- Invulnerability immediately comes into effect when the casting animation begins, and lasts until the deactivation animation finishes.
- Sprodling form does not trigger Sensor Bars, Laser Barriers, and Security Cameras, and can pass through Spy defenses without triggering alarms.
- As Sprodling form counts as an invisibility state, Arcane Crepuscular will activate. The Final Critical Multiplier part of the effect is applied at the time critical damage is dealt, meaning that a previously cast Sporespring will benefit from the increased Final Crit Multiplier effect as long as Nokko is in Sprodling form at the time damage is dealt. However, because Ability Strength gets snapshotted at the time an ability is cast, and because Nokko cannot cast abilities during Reroot, there is no way to take advantage of the Ability Strength increase.
- There is currently a bug where the icon for Crepuscular will not show up on the HUD during Reroot.

- During Reroot, Nokko spawns 3 Fungal Spores around him within 10 meters. Fungal Spore is a glowing orb Pickup highlighted to Nokko through walls. Once collected, Nokko heals by 50 / 60 / 70 / 80 health and shield points, while granting him a 100% Movement Speed bonus for 3 seconds. When Nokko collects a Fungal Spore, another spawns nearby; when the spores are outside his spawn radius as Nokko moves away, they respawn near him. Once Reroot ends, all Fungal Spores will despawn.
- Fungal Spores can spawn on the ground or spawn floating in midair. At least one spore attempts to spawn near ground level and in the direction Nokko is facing when casting Reroot or when respawning nearby as he moves.
- Fungal Spores are not affected by Vacuum/ Fetch nor count for Equilibrium conversion.
- Fungal Spores do not grant Nokko's companion extra health and shields.
- Fungal Spores emit a rotating chattering sound when nearby.

- If cast in midair, Nokko will instantly drop to the ground to perform this ability.

Kit Interactions

- - Nokko's passive Vital Decay reverts to his Sprodling form and spawns Fungal Spores that grant the speed boost only.
- Nokko's Sprodling form can jump on Stinkbrain and Brightbonnet to trampoline high into the air, while forcing a pulse from the mushrooms per bounce. Reroot Sprodling can still receive energy from Brightbonnets.
- Reroot Sprodling's tiny size enables continuous bouncing on mushrooms in low ceiling areas.

Augment

Main article: Reroot Rampage

Tips & Tricks

- When push comes to shove, activate Reroot to effectively become untouchable, then waddle to a safe place to turn back into Warframe form.
- When in the air, such as while bouncing off of Nokko's mushrooms, cast Reroot to quickly descend back to the ground.
- Nokko has limited maneuvers in Sprodling form. Use Dodge or Roll, and pick up Fungal Spores often to speed boost. Combine dodge and spore collection to move even faster.
- Sprodling dodge carries some forward momentum when Nokko is in the air, or after wall jumping, allowing him to move faster than dodging on the ground.
- Sprodling form can use Triple Jump for extra air-time.

- Though Sprodling Nokko cannot attack or cast other abilities, he can repeatedly bounce off of Stinkbrain and Brightbonnet to force them to pulse. His tiny size lets him fit into small space his Warframe form cannot.
- Although Nokko and his Companion are unable to deal damage or activate abilities in Reroot, other deployables such as Duplex Bond clones and active Sporespring mushrooms remain effective on the field.

Info

- Nokko expends 75 energy to hurl a homing bouncing mushroom, inflicting 1,750 / 2,000 / 2,250 / 2,500 Toxin damage in a 3 meter radius on impact, with a starting 75% Critical Chance, 2x Critical Damage and a forced Toxin Status Effect. Sporespring bounces up to 10 times to nearby enemies, terrain, or Nokko's mushrooms within 8 meters, with each bounce increasing its damage by 1.5x and its Critical Chance by a flat 25%. A maximum of 3 Sporespring mushrooms can be active at once.
- Bounce damage can be calculated by Base Damage x 1.5 ^ Bounce Count.
- For example, the second bounce, on a yellow crit, deals 2,500 × 1.5 ^ 2 × 2 = 11,250 Toxin damage.

- Number of active Sporesprings are tracked on the ability icon.
- Sporespring prioritizes targets in the following order: non-Invigorated mushrooms, high threat enemies such as Eximus and Bosses, other enemies, and finally Invigorated mushrooms.
- However, Sporespring will never impact the same target twice in a row, not without bouncing off something else first.

- Once homed into a target, Sporespring gains infinite Punch Through to reach its target.
- Sporespring will activate Arcane Sculptor
- Sporespring is affected by the following:
- The flat critical multiplier buff of Arcane Crepuscular.
- The Status Damage multiplying buff from Emerald Archon Shard on Toxin Damage.
- Ability damage-boosting shard effects on foes afflicted by Corrosive ( Emerald Archon Shard), Radiation ( Topaz Archon Shard), and Electricity ( Violet Archon Shard).
- Can self prime for bonus Ability Damage from Emerald Archon Shard by inflicting Corrosive via Archon Continuity.

- Sporespring is not affected by the following:
- The critical chance buffs of Arcane Avenger, Puncture procs, and Arcane Hot Shot.
- The critical multiplier buffs of Tenacious Bond and Cold procs.
- The Viral damage buff of Nourish.
- Universal weapon damage boosts such as Lethal Levitation and Arcane Arachne.
- The projectile speed buff from Zephyr's Jet Stream augment.

Kit Interactions

- Each time a Stinkbrain or Brightbonnet is bounced on by a Sporespring, the impacted mushroom will pulse its effect.
- When Sporespring impacts a Stinkbrain or a Brightbonnet, that mushroom will become Invigorated.
- An Invigorated mushroom has its pulse interval reduced to 1.5 seconds, Brightbonnet's original Ability Strength buff is doubled.
- A mushroom will remain Invigorated until it is hopped on, at which point it will return to normal and revert to its normal pulse interval and Strength buff.
- However, as long as a player doesn't lose their Strength buff, pulses from the normal Strength buff will refresh the stronger version rather than replacing it.

- Each time a mushroom transitions from normal to Invigorated, its duration is reset.
- An Invigorated mushroom will visibly emit glowing spores until 2/3 of its duration has elapsed, which can be used to tell when it will disappear if not Invigorated again.
- The first time a mushroom becomes Invigorated, it will permanently receive a glowing rim around its silhouette. This glow will remain even if the mushroom loses its Invigoration.
