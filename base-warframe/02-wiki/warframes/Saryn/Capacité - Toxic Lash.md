Info

- Saryn expends 50 energy to imbue all her weapons with potent toxins for 20 / 25 / 35 / 45 seconds, adding an Extra Hit for 20% / 24% / 26% / 30% of the damage of each hit as Toxin damage (doubled for melee weapons), which has a guaranteed Toxin proc on every strike.
- Toxic Lash adds an extra Toxin attack that scales with weapon damage, before health and armor resistances.
- This damage buff is applied as an Extra Hit.
- For example, with an unmodded weapon whose damage sheet says it hits for 200 damage, a Rank 3 Toxic Lash, and a Rank 5 Intensify, Toxic Lash will deal: 200 × 0.3 × 1.3 = 78 direct Toxin damage, and always trigger a Toxin proc that ticks for 78 × 0.5 = 39 Toxin damage per second to an unarmored enemy with no toxin resistance.
- If the weapon has multishot, Toxic Lash adds these Extra Hits with guaranteed Toxin procs to every individual multishot hit.
- This means that shotguns are guaranteed to stack potentially dozens of Toxin procs per shot.

- All Toxin mods in the weapon will apply their toxin multiplier to Toxic Lash's Toxin status ticks, even if they are combined into other elements.
- The extra Toxin damage from Toxic Lash does not combine with weapon elements.
- Faction Damage Mods, after first applying to the weapon's base damage, will multiply Toxic Lash's Extra Hit a second time, and then multiply its Toxin ticks a third time, providing multiplicative triple-dipping.
- Elementalist Mods and the increased Toxin status effect from Emerald Archon Shard stack additively to affect the damage of the Toxin proc.
- Status duration mods and Saryn's own passive affect the duration of the Toxin proc.
- Most weapon hits will trigger a Toxic Lash attack, including Acid Shells and Concealed Explosives, but not Bursting Mass or Pathocyst's maggots.
- Damage is affected by Ancient Disruptor auras and other enemies that reduce ability damage, unlike other weapon buffing skills.
- Toxic Lash's damage instance does not charge Incarnon transformations.

- Cast animation of ~1 second is affected by Casting Speed.
- Saryn is immune to crowd control during the cast animation.

- Casting Toxic Lash is a one-handed action that can be done while performing many actions without interrupting them, including reloading, charging, shooting, maneuvering, and while on a zipline.
- Can be recast to refresh its duration.

Kit Interactions

- - Toxic Lash makes Spores burst with every attack, even without directly hitting the spore.
- A Spore-afflicted enemy will take TWO Extra Hits from Toxic Lash, though the target will receive only one stack of Toxin status.

Augment

Main article: Contagion Cloud
Contagion Cloud is a Warframe Augment Mod for Saryn that causes kills made with Toxic Lash, and damage over time effects generated by it, to leave behind a poisonous cloud that deals Toxin damage over time to enemies within it. Melee kills double the damage dealt by the cloud.

Tips & Tricks

- All mods that boost weapon damage, such as base damage mods, elemental mods, and faction mods, will boost the damage of Toxic Lash and its Toxin Status.
- The Venom Dose Augment for Spores applies similarly to an elemental damage mod for Corrosive. Nourish applies similarly to an elemental mod for Viral.

- Faction Mods and Roar will also apply to Toxic Lash damage on top of the base damage boost.
- Elemental Mods for Toxin will boost the damage of the Toxin Status an additional time due to how Toxin Status damage is calculated.
- Faction Mods and Roar will apply an additional, third, time to the Toxin status effect.
- For status focused builds, Toxin mods will provide the most benefit, followed by Faction mods, then your main damage mods and lastly your other element mods. Primed mods will have stronger effects.
- Weapon mods and features that cause additional hits will create additional instances of Toxic Lash Damage. This includes the radial damage of slam attacks, explode on death mods such as Acid Shells, and mods like Concealed Explosives and Nightwatch Napalm. This makes it possible for them to deal vastly more damage than normal through Toxic Lash, which is especially useful for Arca Titron, Tenet Exec, (Kuva) Sobek, and (Kuva) Ogris.
- Base damage bonuses from mods (e.g. Serration) and abilities (e.g. Eclipse) do not scale Toxic Lash directly on this kind of extra weapon hit, but they do scale the damage of mods like Nightwatch Napalm, which in turn scales the Toxic Lash hits triggered by the mod.

Bugs

- While playing in a squad as a client (not as host), the use of Toxic Lash significantly reduces Critical Hit chance. As long as Toxic Lash is active, one will see only occasional yellow crits even with 400% crit chance (which should guarantee red crits).
