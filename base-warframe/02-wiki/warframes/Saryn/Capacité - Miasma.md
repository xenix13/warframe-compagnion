# Miasma (capacité de Saryn) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Miasma

| 4 75 | Miasma Release a poisonous miasma that deals Viral Damage to enemies in range. Foes afflicted by spores are more susceptible to the mist. Introduced in Update 7.0 (2013-03-18) | Strength:110 / 125 / 135 / 150 ( Viral damage) 

Duration:6 s 

Range:10 / 12 / 16 / 20 m 

Misc: 100% ( Viral status chance) 4.0x (Spores damage multiplier) 

Info

- Saryn expends 75 energy to pollutes the environment with a viral mist, briefly Stunning enemies within 10 / 12 / 16 / 20 meters and sickening them for 6 seconds, dealing 110 / 125 / 135 / 150 Viral damage per second, with 100% Viral status chance on cast.
- Cast animation of ~1.5 seconds is affected by Casting Speed.
- Saryn is immune to crowd control during the cast animation.

- Miasma cannot stun enemies again if they are still being affected by a previous cast, but will refresh the sickness' duration.

- Casting Miasma can be done while sliding and in mid-air, but will interrupt any other action and will force Saryn in place for the entire animation.
- Cannot be used while on a zipline.

Kit Interactions
Miasma deals 400% damage to enemies affected by Spores, and Spored enemies that die while sickened by Miasma will spread Spores.

Tips & Tricks

- Use Spores first to start shredding armor (if any), then attack with Toxic Lash to inflict Toxin procs on enemies, and follow up with Miasma to deal quadruple damage and a guaranteed Viral Status Effect proc to increase damage dealt to enemies' health, so Toxin damage can eat their health up quicker.
- Miasma will not interrupt aim glide. Left/right directional input will be stopped, but the aim glide itself will continue straight as normal.
