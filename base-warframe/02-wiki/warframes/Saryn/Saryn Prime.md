Saryn
A golden blossom conceals deadly nectar. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization.

Release Date: February 16th, 2016

Saryn Prime is the Primed variant of Saryn, possessing higher armor, energy capacity, and sprint speed, as well as an additional polarity. Saryn Prime was released alongside Spira Prime and Nikana Prime.

## Notes

- Saryn Prime, compared to Saryn:
- Higher Armor (315 vs. 240)
- Higher Energy (200/300 vs. 175/225)
- Higher Starting Energy (100 vs. 50)
- Higher Sprint Speed (1.0 vs 0.95)
- Additional Polarity ( vs. )

- As with any other Prime Warframe, moving close to the location of an Orokin Void Death Orb will restore a pulse of 250 Energy to nearby allies, even if that Death Orb is inactive, destroyed, or non-present. This effect can only occur once per orb.
- Saryn Prime, along with Volt Prime, Chroma Prime, Ivara Prime, Baruuk ( Prime), Wisp ( Prime), and Yareli, possesses the third highest max-rank energy pool of all Warframes at 300 at rank 30, behind Yareli Prime's 315 and Garuda Prime's 320.
- This can be increased to 600 with a maxed Flow, 850 with Primed Flow, and 880 with Primed Flow and Endurance Drift.
