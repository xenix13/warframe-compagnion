Info

- Hildryn expends 100 shields or overshields to activate her jet thrusters as she lifts off from the ground. While active, her shield matrix drains 25 shield points per second to continuously generate a energy field with a range of 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 meters. Enemies inside the energy field and within Hildryn's line of sight drain 25 shield points per second per enemy, causing a fiery energy geyser to erupt from beneath them; enemies burned by energy geysers are suspended in midair, receive 100 / 125 / 150 / 200 Radiation damage per second, and drop an Energy Orb every 8-18 seconds. Deactivating Aegis Storm (default 4 ) causes Hildryn to become Invulnerable as she slams into the ground, knocking down and dealing 100 / 300 / 400 / 500 Impact damage against all enemies inside the energy field.
- Energy field range and damage is scaled by Hildryn's altitude.
- At the lowest elevation the energy field has 100% of the listed radius, and deals 50% of the listed damage.
- At the highest elevation the energy field has 30% of the listed radius, and deals 300% of the listed damage.

- Hildryn is unable to accomplish any action that requires the Use key (default X ) while Aegis Storm is active (e.g. interacting with consoles, picking up Mission Items, reviving allies, performing Finishers).
- The Operator can still be used while in Aegis Storm to do these actions without deactivating the ability.

- It's not possible to perform Finishers on suspended enemies.
- Hildryn becomes immune to Stagger, Knockdown, and Ragdoll and is instead moved around by these effects. She can be pulled by Kuva Scorpion or strongly pushed by Arson Eximus.
- The slam upon ability deactivation will move in the direction of the aiming reticle.

- Flight mechanics:
- Some Helminth abilities can be cast in flight.
- Hildryn moves at 7.6 m/s and gains vertical and horizontal flight capabilities using the movement (W A S D ), crouch (Ctrl ), and jump (Spacebar ) keys, similar to Archwing Maneuvers.
- Move Speed is affected by Movement Speed buffs, but not Sprint Speed buffs.

- Hildryn can perform dodging while Aegis Storm is active, at the cost of using 50 per dodge.
- Can activate Arcane Circumvent, granting extra protection and crowd control during Aegis Storm.
- Dodge distance is increased by Parkour Velocity bonuses, as well as by Amalgam Barrel Diffusion (stacks if equipped on both the Balefire Charger and the Secondary).

- Hildryn in Aegis Storm cannot ascend higher than 10m above the horizontal surface underneath her.
- If Hildryn is above this threshold when activating the ability or due to changes in elevation, she will float downward.
- If Hildryn moves onto terrain that has a higher elevation, she will rise to match the terrain.

- While Aegis Storm is active large shield pads appear above each of Hildryn's shoulders. These pads appear above the pads that are briefly visible when Hildryn casts Pillage.
- Unlike the pads that appear for Pillage and Haven (which appear to be generated by small spheres) the pads visible when Aegis Storm is active appear to be generated by two Balefire Chargers, Hildryn's Exalted Weapon.
- The Balefire Charger visible in Hildryn's hand when she casts Balefire appears to have five energy streams coming out of it. These are not visible in the Balefire Chargers generating her shield pads when Aegis Storm is active.

- Hildryn's Balefire Charger is the only weapon usable while this ability is active. Primary, Secondary, and Melee weapons cannot be used with Aegis Storm.
- Hildryn cannot aim with her Balefire Charger during Aegis Storm.
- While Hildryn holds two Balefire Chargers instead of one while Aegis Storm is active, this is a purely cosmetic feature and has no effect on the weapon's effectiveness.
- If a player uses the Helminth to replace Balefire, Hildryn will not have access to any weapons or ability to deal damage.

Kit Interactions

- Activating Aegis Storm will immediately equip Balefire at no shield cost.
- When Balefire is activated this way, Precision Intensify will apply its strength bonus to Balefire.
- Similarly, single use buffs like Energy Conversion and Empower will buff both abilities during one cast of Aegis Storm.

Tips & Tricks

- Balefire Charger equipped with Secondary Fortifier can grant extra protection and crowd control during Aegis Storm.
- Additionally equip Balefire Surge to replenish shields and provide crowd control against Nullifiers.

- Aegis Storm generates a huge amount of Energy Orbs, which easily triggers "On Energy Pickup" conditions (e.g. Energy Conversion, Arcane Eruption).
- While channeling Aegis Storm, Hildryn counts as being airborne for effects like Agility Drift and Aviator.
- Even though they can't be used, weapons with Weapon Augments can still trigger their Syndicate Radial Effects, as long as they were the equipped weapon when activating Aegis Storm.
- This is useful with the Perrin Sequences' Sequence effect, which recovers 25% of the warframe's shield.
- Using Balefire Charger will prevent these effects, as that then becomes the equipped weapon.

- Take care not to die in Operator mode, as Transference Static is particularly crippling for Hildryn.
- While channeling, Hildryn's shields can be restored by Volt's Capacitance, Trinity's Blessing, Night Equinox's Mend & Maim, Mag's Polarize, and Raksa Kubrow's Protect.
- Trinity's Vampire Leech will not restore shields.

- As you are unable to use any weapons except Balefire Charger, consider equipping a Furax with Amalgam Furax Body Count as it improves fire rate even when it cannot be used.
- Keep in mind that this will also increase shield drain.

- Aegis Storm snapshots Efficiency when using Arcane Sculptor.
