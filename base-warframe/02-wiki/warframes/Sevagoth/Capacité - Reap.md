Info

- Sevagoth expends 25 energy to sever apart from his Shadow and expel it toward the direction of his aiming reticle. Reap's Shadow is an invulnerable flying ally? that lasts for 2.5 / 3 / 6 / 6 seconds, with a variable flight speed of 10 meters per second if cast by tapping the ability key, or 20 meters per second if held down. The Shadow's harrowing wail weakens all enemies within 2 / 3 / 8 / 8 meters of it, dealing 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Radiation damage and inflicting 10% / 20% / 30% / 50% Damage Vulnerability debuff that lasts for 5 / 7 / 10 / 10 seconds.
- Only 1 Shadow from Reap may be active at once per player.
- Recasting the ability will remove the current Shadow and send out a new one.
- Reap's Shadow will expire prematurely upon colliding with a null bubble from enemies such as Nullifier Crewman or Isolator Bursa.
- Reap's Shadow is tracked on the minimap as an allied blue dot.
- Reap's Shadow is unaffected by mods, arcanes or ally buffs.
- Reap's Shadow does not require Line of Sight to affect enemies.

- The damage vulnerability's duration and strength cannot be refreshed by extra Reap casts; the debuff has to fully expire before applying new values.
- The damage and debuff only apply to each individual enemy once per instance of Reap.

- Aiming at a location (default RMB ) causes the Shadow to fly to the intended area at 15 meters per second, or 30 meters per second if Reap was held down when cast.
- The Shadow will audibly respond to Sevagoth's aim to indicate it is rerouting.
- If the Shadow reaches its intended location, it will spin around until Sevagoth stops aiming.
- Reap's Shadow flies forward on the horizontal plane if not being guided.
- Reap's Shadow still experiences collision with objects and terrain, and will automatically attempt to maneuver around them.
- Manually blocking with a melee weapon or using Sevagoth's Shadow will not redirect Reap's Shadow.

- Casting Reap is an upper-body animation that allows movement and Maneuvers while restricting other actions.
- Sevagoth visually raises his arms to open a portal for the Shadow to fly through. As the Shadow flies, it constantly emits an eerie wail with shadows trailing in its wake. Affected enemies emit shadow tendrils flailing from their torso. Upon expiration, the Shadow collapses into a fading blackhole to return to Sevagoth.
- Reap's Shadow uses Sevagoth's Shadow's Appearance customizations, except for energy color which uses Sevagoth's Appearance loadout selection.

Kit Interactions

- Reap's Radiation damage can benefit from the debuff inflicted by a previous instance of Reap or Death's Harvest.
- Sown enemies hit by Reap will detonate in a bright explosion, dealing damage in a 4-step process.
- 1) Enemies are dealt 25% of their current health as True damage.
- 2) Enemies are dealt Reap's 250 Radiation damage.
- 3) Reap's Damage Vulnerability is applied onto the enemies.
- 4) Enemies detonate and deal 25% of their current health (before the True damage) to other enemies in a 4 meter radius as Blast damage.
- While neither the True nor Blast damage scale with ability strength, both can be boosted by damage amplification sources, such as Reap's damage vulnerability.
- Note that Reap's damage vulnerability is applied before the Blast damage, resulting in it often being higher than 25%.

- While Gloom is active, enemies damaged by Reap's Shadow will heal Sevagoth.
- Each enemy killed while debuffed by Reap and/or marked by Sow fills 5% of the Death Well for the Exalted Shadow.
- Kills via Reap's 250 Radiation damage or with Sow's interaction of 25% current health True damage (if boosted to 100%) fills the Death Well by an additional 5%, for a total of 10% per kill.

- Reap cannot be used in conjunction with Death's Harvest and vice-versa, as they are considered the same debuff. Either debuff has to fully expire before applying new values.

Augment

Main article: Shadow Haze
Shadow Haze is a Warframe Augment Mod for Sevagoth that increases the Critical Chance from any source against enemies affected by Reap or Death's Harvest. When affected enemies die, a new Reap Shadow spawns from their corpse and seeks other enemies, up to a maximum of 3 additional active Shadows at all mod ranks.

Tips & Tricks

- Reap's Shadow lingers after casting the Exalted Shadow, which can be used as an alternative for Death's Harvest's damage vulnerability.
- Holding Reap while casted will significantly increase the shadow's speed, which can be useful to cover more area on larger tilesets.
- Using Sow and Reap on Eximus can significantly reduce their health low enough for a Mercy kill, benefitting from gear such as Power Drain and Arcane Escapist.
- Sow and Reap's interaction of 25% current health True damage can be combined with Reap's damage vulnerability and another damage multiplier (such as Viral statuses, Roar and/or Archon Shards' ability damage bonus), to achieve a full 100% True damage drain.
- Note that since Reap's damage vulnerability is applied after the True damage, a second Reap cast is often needed; or the usage of Shadow Haze may be advised, as it can trigger the Sow mark again when an enemy dies from the first explosion's Blast damage.
- Using 1 Viral status requires 200% strength to achieve a full drain.
- Using Roar requires 255% strength to achieve a full drain.

- Sow and Reap's interaction of 25% current Health Blast damage can also benefit from other damage amplifications, but grouping and armor strips (such as Embrace and Pillage) are more effective.
- A Shade or Huras Kubrow can be used to keep stealth while casting abilities if not using weapons or the Shadow Claws.
