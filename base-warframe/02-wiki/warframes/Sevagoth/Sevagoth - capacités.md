## Passive

This section is transcluded from Sevagoth/Abilities/Passive. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.

Intertwined forever in life and death, Sevagoth and his Shadow exhibit numerous unique properties and interactions, highlighted in the tabs below:

On fatal injury and entering Bleedout, Sevagoth's body petrifies into a broken tombstone as his Shadow manifests itself. Control the Shadow to collect the souls of 5 enemies by casting Consume via the ability, quick melee, or fire weapon buttons with a targeted enemy on the aiming reticle (default 2 , E and LMB respectively), reviving Sevagoth upon a full harvest. Alternatively, allies may manually revive Sevagoth by interacting with his tombstone.

- Targeted enemy must be within 14m to track
- While the passive is activated, a soul counter appears above Sevagoth's ability icons indicating the number of remaining enemies required to revive Sevagoth, as well as a bleedout gauge that counts down toward his death. If Exalted Shadow is unlocked, the Death Well is replaced by the soul counter until Sevagoth revives.
- Consume cannot target enemies with Overguard, you must first remove it by any means (e.g. using Shadow Claws, or a Companion with Assassin Posture).
  - Enemy health bars are not visible during this passive, making Ancient Protectors a high priority target.
- Enemies killed via Shadow Claws will not count towards reviving Sevagoth.
- Sevagoth's Shadow can perform any type heavy attacks (default MMB ) during this state.
  - Life Strike may be advised to increase survivability.
- Sevagoth's Shadow can perform combo attacks if melee (default E ) is held before and after Consume's animation ends.
- The Shadow Claws can still be used to block incoming frontal damage (default hold RMB ).
- Consume is altered to cost 0 Energy to cast and inflicts armor and shield-bypassing damage equal to the target's total hitpoints, instantly killing non-boss enemies.
- Sevagoth's Shadow summoned by the passive is still vulnerable to damage and status effects except for Knockdown. It utilizes its equipped Mods and Arcanes, but cannot cast its other abilities. In Solo play, dying as the Shadow will end Bleedout and kill Sevagoth early. In Co-op, his Shadow simply vanishes, and his tombstone will remain for the rest of his bleedout timer, still available to be revived by his allies.
- The player can still reach extraction while bleeding out and controlling Sevagoth's Shadow.
- Undying Will and Renewal will still function normally on Sevagoth's Tombstone.
  - Undying Will has no effect when equipped on the Shadow.
  - Undying Will increases the bleed-out timer for the passive from 20 seconds up to 34.4 seconds.
- Unairu's Last Gasp will not prompt on Sevagoth's Tombstone.
- Tombstone does not prevent Sevagoth from dying in Arbitrations; he will still enter the Tombstone animation before immediately dying.

The Shadow's harrowing wail weakens all enemies within 2 / 3 / 8 / 8 meters of it, dealing 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Radiation damage and inflicting 10% / 20% / 30% / 50% Damage Vulnerability debuff that lasts for 5 / 7 / 10 / 10 seconds.
  - Only 1 Shadow from Reap may be active at once per player.
    - Recasting the ability will remove the current Shadow and send out a new one.

  - The damage vulnerability's duration and strength cannot be refreshed by extra Reap casts; the debuff has to fully expire before applying new values.
    - The damage and debuff only apply to each individual enemy once per instance of Reap.
  - Aiming at a location (default RMB ) causes the Shadow to fly to the intended area at 15 meters per second, or 30 meters per second if Reap was held down when cast.
    - The Shadow will audibly respond to Sevagoth's aim to indicate it is rerouting.
    - If the Shadow reaches its intended location, it will spin around until Sevagoth stops aiming.
    - Reap's Shadow flies forward on the horizontal plane if not being guided.
    - Reap's Shadow still experiences collision with objects and terrain, and will automatically attempt to maneuver around them.
    - Manually blocking with a melee weapon or using Sevagoth's Shadow will not redirect Reap's Shadow.

Affected enemies emit shadow tendrils flailing from their torso. Upon expiration, the Shadow collapses into a fading blackhole to return to Sevagoth.
  - Reap's Shadow uses Sevagoth's Shadow's Appearance customizations, except for energy color which uses Sevagoth's Appearance loadout selection.

Kit Interactions

- Reap's Radiation damage can benefit from the debuff inflicted by a previous instance of Reap or Death's Harvest.
- Sown enemies hit by Reap will detonate in a bright explosion, dealing damage in a 4-step process.
  - 1) Enemies are dealt 25% of their current health as True damage.
  - 2) Enemies are dealt Reap's 250 Radiation damage.
  - 3) Reap's Damage Vulnerability is applied onto the enemies.
  - 4) Enemies detonate and deal 25% of their current health (before the True damage) to other enemies in a 4 meter radius as Blast damage.
    - While neither the True nor Blast damage scale with ability strength, both can be boosted by damage amplification sources, such as Reap's damage vulnerability.
    - Note that Reap's damage vulnerability is applied before the Blast damage, resulting in it often being higher than 25%.
- While Gloom is active, enemies damaged by Reap's Shadow will heal Sevagoth.
- Each enemy killed while debuffed by Reap and/or marked by Sow fills 5% of the Death Well for the Exalted Shadow.
  - Kills via Reap's 250 Radiation damage or with Sow's interaction of 25% current health True damage (if boosted to 100%) fills the Death Well by an additional 5%, for a total of 10% per kill.
- Reap cannot be used in conjunction with Death's Harvest and vice-versa, as they are considered the same debuff. Either debuff has to fully expire before applying new values.

Augment

- Main article: Shadow Haze

Shadow Haze is a Warframe Augment Mod for Sevagoth that increases the Critical Chance from any source against enemies affected by Reap or Death's Harvest. When affected enemies die, a new Reap Shadow spawns from their corpse and seeks other enemies, up to a maximum of 3 additional active Shadows at all mod ranks.

Tips & Tricks

- Reap's Shadow lingers after casting the Exalted Shadow, which can be used as an alternative for Death's Harvest's damage vulnerability.
- Holding Reap while casted will significantly increase the shadow's speed, which can be useful to cover more area on larger tilesets.
- Using Sow and Reap on Eximus can significantly reduce their health low enough for a Mercy kill, benefitting from gear such as Power Drain and Arcane Escapist.
- Sow and Reap's interaction of 25% current health True damage can be combined with Reap's damage vulnerability and another damage multiplier (such as Viral statuses, Roar and/or Archon Shards' ability damage bonus), to achieve a full 100% True damage drain.
  - Note that since Reap's damage vulnerability is applied after the True damage, a second Reap cast is often needed; or the usage of Shadow Haze may be advised, as it can trigger the Sow mark again when an enemy dies from the first explosion's Blast damage.
  - Using 1 Viral status requires 200% strength to achieve a full drain.
  - Using Roar requires 255% strength to achieve a full drain.
- Sow and Reap's interaction of 25% current Health Blast damage can also benefit from other damage amplifications, but grouping and armor strips (such as Embrace and Pillage) are more effective.
- A Shade or Huras Kubrow can be used to keep stealth while casting abilities if not using weapons or the Shadow Claws.

  - the Stalker and his upgraded forms will deactivate Gloom if it enters its radius;
  - some enemies in the Zariman Ten Zero such as Void Angels;
  - Thrax Legatus and Thrax Centurion's spectral forms; and,
  - Narmer Archons.
  - Do note that Sevagoth and his allies can still heal themselves while attacking these slow-immune enemies as long as everyone is within his aura.
- Casting Gloom is a full-body animation that restricts grounded movement and other actions. Deactivating Gloom is instantaneous and does not play an animation.
- Sevagoth visually conjures a shadow wave then sustains the ring of flowing darkness that indicates the aura radius, visible only to himself. Both he and his allies affected by Gloom visually emit a spiraling shadow torrent around their legs. Affected enemies visually appear hindered by three shadowy clouds around their head, torso, and thigh regions.

Kit Interactions

- Each enemy alive within Gloom's radius fills 0.1% per second of the Death Well for Exalted Shadow.
- While Gloom is active, damage dealt by Reap's Shadow and Sow heal Sevagoth. Damage dealt by Consume further heals the Shadow's health with lifesteal while the Shadow remains in range.
- Gloom remains activated and centered on Sevagoth's body during Exalted Shadow.

Helminth

- Subsuming Sevagoth to the Helminth will offer Gloom and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
  - When infused into Lavos, Gloom is altered to cost 0 energy to cast and sustain, lasts for 12 seconds that triggers a 10 second cooldown after deactivation.

Tips & Tricks

- Temporary and/or conditional Ability Strength bonuses from various sources can assist in reaching higher slow effect of Gloom and, in some cases, free mod slots.
  - For example, these include Molt Augmented, Sling Strength, Velox Conclusion, Vome Invocation, Power Drain, Helminth's Empower, and several Warframes' Passives, if Gloom is infused via the Helminth.
  - Protea trivializes the Ability Strength requirements to reach the Slow effect cap, as her additive 100% Ability Strength bonus every 4th ability cast means that she only has to mod for the remaining 72% Ability Strength, then reach the bonus by using her other abilities. Dispensary also lets her manage the Energy costs easily.
  - If Gloom is infused onto a given Warframe's fourth ability, Precision Intensify can used to reach the slow cap, leaving only the 82% remaining Ability Strength to be reached with other sources.
- Acolytes are not resistant to Gloom's slow, unlike other abilities with crowd-control effects.
  - Note that Gloom can sometimes prolong the invulnerability phases of enemies, such as Violence's dash, Misery's summoning or Torment's surge, so be mindful of either moving away from them or disable Gloom to let them finish their invulnerability mode.
- When extracted, Gloom is a very versatile ability to infuse into Warframes that otherwise have no means to heal in their ability kits (i.e. Mesa can heal herself with Peacemaker as she shoots down enemies within range of her infused Gloom}.
  - Garuda can exchange Blood Altar for Gloom, becoming a mobile offensive healer and crowd controller that is sustained extremely well by Bloodletting. This comes at the cost of Blood Altar's disabling ability and its passive healing, as she must actively attack to heal herself.
  - Ember can easily reach 95% Slow effect with her passive, in combination with several Ability Strength mods.
- Gloom can affect fighters flying too close to a Railjack, allowing the aura user to heal themselves while using a turret, be it via direct fire or offensive abilities such as Shatter Burst.
- Gloom is an ideal skill to utilize in Defection, Excavation, Kuva Survival, the Arbitrations' version of Defense, and Void Armageddon missions, as well as Isolation Vault bounties in the Cambion Drift, as it can help in managing swarms of enemies attacking moving or shifting targets that players have to protect.
  - Kavor Defectors and Arbitration Defense targets can heal themselves by shooting enemies while under the effects of Gloom.
  - Hostile Necramechs are also slowed by Gloom, making aiming at their exposed weakpoints far easier. Also, Loid and Otak's survivability is increased as long as the Gloom user stays near them to keep the Infested at bay.
  - Inaros Prime can opt to infuse himself with Gloom to help Baro Ki'Teer survive better in Void Raider missions, not only to help manage incoming Nightwatch enemies but also, as with the above Defectors, help him heal himself when he attacks.
  - Gloom will also affect Demo units in Disruption missions should they wander into range, but their nullifying pulses will briefly remove the slowing effect allowing for short "hops" towards the conduit. Coming too close to the Demo units while using the nullifying pulse can also dispel Gloom, so players should be mindful of reactivating it as soon as possible.
