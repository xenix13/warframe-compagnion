Info

- Sevagoth expends 50 energy to damn the souls of the wicked and implant death seeds into all enemies within a radius of 10 / 12 / 14 / 16 meters. Seeded enemies decay toward death taking 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 True damage per second over 5 / 8 / 11 / 10 seconds.
- Does not require Line of Sight to inflict damage.
- True damage ignores armor and shields, dealing damage directly to health.
- DoT Has Damage Falloff starting from half range to a maximum of 25% at max range.
- Recasting Sow on an already affected enemy will only overwrite their current Sow debuff if the new cast has a higher duration than the remaining duration on the current debuff. This is regardless of whether the new cast has higher ability strength or less damage falloff.
- For example, casting Sow with Arcane Concentration active will give the ability a 16s duration DoT on enemies. Recasting Sow 3 seconds later (once Concentration has expired) will cause the new cast to have a duration of 10s while the original cast still has 13s remaining, so the original cast will not be overwritten.

- Casting Sow is an upper-body animation that allows movement and Maneuvers while restricting other actions.
- Sevagoth visually conjures rising darkness around him that briefly remains visible to indicate the effective range, as he spreads his arms to sow the death seeds into his foes. Affected enemies are shrouded in a cloak of darkness with a tint of Sevagoth's chosen energy colors.

Kit Interactions

- Sown enemies hit by Reap will detonate in a bright explosion, dealing damage in a 4-step process.
- 1) Enemies are dealt 25% of their current health as True damage.
- 2) Enemies are dealt Reap's 250 Radiation damage.
- 3) Reap's Damage Vulnerability is applied onto the enemies.
- 4) Enemies detonate and deal 25% of their current health (before the True damage) to other enemies in a 4 meter radius as Blast damage.
- While neither the True nor Blast damage scale with ability strength, both can be boosted by damage amplification sources, such as Reap's damage vulnerability.
- Note that Reap's damage vulnerability is applied before the Blast damage, resulting in it often being higher than 25%.

- Sow's True damage per second can be increased with Reap's damage vulnerability.
- While Gloom is active, Sown enemies heal Sevagoth as they take damage per second.
- Each enemy killed while marked by Sow and/or debuffed by Reap fills 5% of the Death Well for the Exalted Shadow.
- Kills via Reap's 250 Radiation damage or with Sow's interaction of 25% current health True damage (if boosted to 100%) fills the Death Well by an additional 5%, for a total of 10% per kill.

Augment

Main article: Dark Propagation
Dark Propagation is a Warframe Augment Mod for Sevagoth's Sow that spreads its effects when affected enemies die. Additionally, Death's Harvest gains the ability to inflict Sow.

Tips & Tricks

- Using Sow and Reap on Eximus can significantly reduce their health low enough for a Mercy kill, benefitting from gear such as Power Drain and Arcane Escapist.
- Sow and Reap's interaction of 25% current health True damage can be combined with Reap's damage vulnerability and another damage multiplier (such as Viral statuses, Roar and/or Archon Shards' ability damage bonus), to achieve a full 100% True damage drain.
- Note that since Reap's damage vulnerability is applied after the True damage, a second Reap cast is often needed; or the usage of Shadow Haze may be advised, as it can trigger the Sow mark again when an enemy dies from the first explosion's Blast damage.
- Using 1 Viral status requires 200% strength to achieve a full drain.
- Using Roar requires 255% strength to achieve a full drain.

- Sow and Reap's interaction of 25% current Health Blast damage can also benefit from other damage amplifications, but grouping and armor strips (such as Embrace and Pillage) are more effective.
- Sow's True damage per second counts as Miscellaneous Damage while controlling Sevagoth's Shadow.
- A Shade or Huras Kubrow can be used to keep stealth while casting abilities if not using weapons or the Shadow Claws.
