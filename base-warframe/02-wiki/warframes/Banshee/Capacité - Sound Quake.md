# Sound Quake (capacité de Banshee) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sound_Quake

Duration:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 s 

Range:12 / 15 / 18 / 20 m 

Misc: 1 - 5 (blast procs per hit) Stagger per hit 

Info

- Banshee expends 75 energy to plant her hands on the ground, briefly becoming Invulnerable as she creates a zone of ultrasonic reverberation in a 12 / 15 / 18 / 20 meter area around the point of cast, lasting for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds. Enemies within the zone take 125 / 150 / 175 / 200 Blast damage per second, staggering them and proccing 1 - 5 Blast statuses per hit (scaling with distance to the epicenter).
- Damage does not diminish with distance and does not require line of sight.
- Number of Blast procs per hit diminishes as enemies get further from the zone's center, applying 5 procs per hit within roughly the first 20% of the ability's max range, 4 per hit within 20% - 40% of the range, etc. down to 1 per hit at 80% - 100% of the range.
- Enemies inside the area of effect are staggered for the duration of the skill. However, it's possible for an enemy to stumble outside of Sound Quake's range if they are too close the edge of the effective area.
- Does not stagger enemies with Overguard, innate immunity, or flying enemies (e.g. Oxium Osprey).

- Destroys all Objects in the ability's radius.
- If cast in midair, Banshee will instantly drop to the ground to perform this ability.
- Has a cast delay of ? seconds and post-cast delay of ? seconds.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies within 20 meters of Banshee when she casts Sound Quake passively receive 5 Puncture Status Effects.
- The 10-stack and on-death explosions caused by Detonate can hit Sonar spots on enemies surrounding the original target.
- Sound Quake's damage per second cannot be increased by Sonar.

- Gas Cloud from Gaseous Quake can hit Sonar spots on both the original target and the nearby enemies.

Augment

Main article: Gaseous Quake
Gaseous Quake is a Warframe Augment Mod for Banshee that gives Gas Damage and Status Effect to Sound Quake with a damage multiplier that increases every second on enemies in sight.

Tips & Tricks

- Sound Quake provides good crowd control against most enemies, aside from Eximus units, whose Overguard renders them immune to the stagger.
- Gaseous Quake in combination with Sonar can quickly get rid of Overguard.

- Sound Quake deals damage to objects, which allows it to temporarily break down Nullifier Crewman bubbles.
