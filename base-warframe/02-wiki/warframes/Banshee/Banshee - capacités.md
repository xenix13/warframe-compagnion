# Banshee/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Banshee/Abilities

## Passive

All of Banshee's abilities inflict 5 Puncture Status Effects to all enemies within 20 meters of her.

Additionally, all of her equipped weapons, including Gunblades such as Redeemer, and weapons equipped on Sentinels should they be present, are treated as silent.

## Abilities

| 1 25 | Sonic Boom Banshee emits a sonic shockwave that pushes targets in range with enough force to incapacitate attackers and reduce their Armor. Introduced in Update 7.0 (2013-03-18) | Strength:175 / 200 / 225 / 250 ( Impact damage) 70% (Armor Reduction) 5 (Push Force) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:10 / 12 / 13 / 15 m 

Misc: 180° (cone angle) Stagger on hit 

Info

- Banshee expends 25 energy to emit a sonic shockwave that propagates forward in a 180° cone in front of her, hitting enemies up to 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 meters away. Enemies caught by the wave receive 175 / 200 / 225 / 250 Impact damage with a guaranteed Impact proc, have 70% of their Armor permanently removed, and are pushed away from Banshee with a Push Force of 5, leaving them momentarily staggered afterwards.
- Sonic Boom is a one-handed action, allowing Banshee to move, shoot, reload, and perform most other actions while casting.
- Requires Line of Sight to the target.

- Can be used to break Storage Containers and other breakable decorations in the environment.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies within 20 meters of Banshee when she casts Sonic Boom passively receive 5 Puncture Status Effects.

| 2 50 | Sonar Using acoustic location, Banshee's Sonar power finds and tracks enemies, and exposes critical weak points to everyone in your squad. Introduced in Update 7.0 (2013-03-18) | Strength:2x / 3x / 4x / 5x 

Duration:10 / 15 / 20 / 30 s 

Range:20 / 25 / 30 / 35 m 

Misc: 20 m/s (propagation speed) 

Info

- Banshee expends 50 energy to emit a sound wave that travels at 20 meters per second and reveals the location of every enemy within 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 meters. Tracked enemies will be visible on every player's mini-map, and each enemy will have a body part highlighted. These effects last for 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 seconds. Hitting the highlighted body part causes the inflicted damage to be multiplied by 2x / 3x / 4x / 5x and triggers weak point related effects.
- Sonar spots are considered Weak Points for the purpose of triggering effects like Acuity Mods and charging Incarnon weapons.
- Highlighting an enemy's natural weak point will cause hits to gain both the body part multiplier and Sonar's multiplier, stacking multiplicatively with each other.
- Critical hits on Sonar spots will not become headcrits.
- While Sonar spots trigger weak point effects, they do not add the enemy's natural weak point damage multiplier to normal body parts. For example, a Butcher's highlighted leg will not gain the 3x damage multiplier associated with the Butcher's natural weak point (its head).
- Weak point damage bonuses, such as from Acuity Mods and Riven Mods, will still apply to highlighted body parts with no innate body part multiplier. For example, Acuity mods can increase the body part multiplier from 1.0x to 4.5x, in addition to Sonar's damage multiplier.

- Damage buffs which inflict an Extra Hit of damage (such as Xata's Whisper and Toxic Lash) will have the weak spot bonus applied once to the initial hit, and again to the extra hit, stacking multiplicatively.

- Sonar's effect propagates outward from the caster's position, traveling through obstacles in the environment and moving at a fixed speed of 20 meters per second, similar to the mechanics of Hildryn's Pillage or Nova's Molecular Prime. Enemies are only debuffed and highlighted once the wave reaches them.
- A sonar spot on an enemy's torso will increase the damage of many AoE damage sources, including many abilities, as they are often coded to target the enemy's center.
- DoTs ( Slash, Heat, Toxin, Electricity, Gas, and Blast) will have their damage calculated on the resulting damage if proc'd from a hit that hits a Sonar weakspot, increasing their lethality.
- AoE DoTs ( Electricity, Gas, and the 10-stack / on-death portion of Blast) are able to double-dip the Sonar effect, as their base damage will be increased by the resulting proc, and then further increased by hitting a body part affected by Sonar.

- Has a cast delay of about 0.8 seconds and a recast time of about 2.4 seconds.

- Highlighting Mechanics:
- With each cast of Sonar, one random body part is highlighted per enemy. All hits to that body part will gain the Sonar damage multiplier.
- Most basic enemies have 6 different body parts that can be highlighted: head, torso, arms (left and right), and legs (left and right).
- Highlights may visually stretch to nearby body parts, but the multiplier will only apply to the marked body part. Conversely, a highlight may not visually cover an entire body part, but the multiplier will still apply to the whole body part.

- Each body part has a limited number of nodes where a highlight can appear. For example, a Butcher's left arm has nodes on its shoulder and elbow, giving it only two potential spots where a Sonar highlight may appear.
- Each highlighted node applies the Sonar damage multiplier to the body part, with multiple highlighted nodes on the same body part stacking multiplicatively with each other.
- A single node cannot be highlighted multiple times at once; if the same node is targeted by Sonar again, the duration will be refreshed instead of stacking the effect.
- Most body parts typically have 2 nodes. Heads typically have 1.
- Certain non-humanoid enemies such as The Anatomizer and Techrot Babau have much more than 2 nodes on a single body part, allowing Sonar to stack with itself many times for extremely high damage multipliers.

- On repeat casts, Sonar will always choose a body part with no highlights if one is available. Once all body parts have 1 node highlighted, further Sonar casts may highlight any node on any body part. This includes nodes that are already highlighted, in which case the highlight duration will simply be refreshed.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies within 20 meters of Banshee when she casts Sonar passively receive 5 Puncture Status Effects.
- Sonar weakpoints will boost the damage of Detonate procs from Sound Quake or Gas Cloud procs from Gaseous Quake, if multiple enemies stand close together.

| 3 75 | Silence Using Silence surrounds Banshee in an aura that stuns enemies, limits their perception and tactical response to gunfire and Warframe attacks, and renders them unable to activate their abilities. Introduced in Update 7.0 (2013-03-18) | Strength:N/A 

Duration:10 / 15 / 20 / 30 s 

Range:10 / 13 / 15 / 20 m 

Misc: 2 s (stun duration) Enemy abilities disabled 

Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Banshee expends 75 energy to emit a sound dampening aura within 10 / 13 / 15 / 20 meters around her for 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 seconds. Enemies entering the aura will initially experience a sonic disturbance, becoming momentarily Stunned for 2 seconds, then becoming Silenced for as long as they are within range; affected enemies are deafened to sounds which alert them to the direction of nearby combat, as well as becoming unable to perform special abilities while within Silence's influence.
- Unaware enemies will not become alerted when affected by the aura and will resume normal behavior after recovering from the stun.
- The ability can be recast to reset the duration and reapply the stun.
- Enemies exiting then reentering the aura can repeatedly experience the stun again.
- Silence will also deafen the caster, muffling all gunfire from friend, foe, and self alike.
- Enemies immune to crowd control, such as Overguard or bosses, will not experience the stun effect, but will be affected by the Silence status effect.

- Enemies under the effect of the aura will not respond to sounds such as gunfire, alarms and death screams, and generally become unable to detect players and other enemies by means other than sight or touch.
- Unaware enemies will still become alerted when sighting players or taking damage.
- Taking damage will not inform enemies to the location of the attacking player, but they are still likely to investigate in the direction the attack came from.
- Enemies that are alert before entering Silence's aura will remain alerted, but may still lose track of players if line of sight is broken.
- After a brief delay, enemies that do not have line of sight with a player will become susceptible to Stealth Damage Multipliers from melee attacks as long as line of sight remains broken and the player does not come into direct physical contact with the enemy.
- The ability does not change a weapon's Noise Level from "Alarming" to "Silent".

- The Silence status effect is applied to all enemies in range, which renders them unable to use abilities, special attacks, or Eximus effects.
- Enemies with Overguard are still affected by the Silence debuff.
- Affected enemies will glow with Banshee's energy color.

- For interactions with specific enemies, see Silence (Status Effect).
- Has a cast delay of about 0.4 seconds.
- Subsuming Banshee to the Helminth will offer Silence and its augments to be used by other Warframes.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies within 20 meters of Banshee when she casts Silence passively receive 5 Puncture Status Effects.

Tips & Tricks

- Silence can be used as a form of crowd control due to the momentary stun dealt to enemies entering its field. This is most useful for Banshees engaging in melee combat, as they can stun ranged enemies simply by approaching them, giving them time to close the distance and engage.
- Silence can also be used to keep Infested enemies at bay, giving more time to attack them from range.
- With a maximized range build, Silence can be used to clear a room of enemies using firearms without alerting enemies in adjacent rooms. Keep in mind however, that enemies will still react if they see you, or if they see one of their own killed.
- Despite the excellent utility provided, Silence can also render Banshee deaf, preventing her from anticipating nearby enemies or enemies from behind while attacking enemies.
- Too much range may reduce the potency of this ability as a crowd control tool, as enemies are only stunned when they initially enter the aura and may be out of attack range (behind walls, etc.), allowing them to recover.
- Enemies are more likely to target the player's Sentinel when Silence is active, which can prove to be harmful for weaker Sentinels.
- Blinded enemies under the effect of Silence will not react to most things aside from direct contact and wander around aimlessly.
- Staying on the move and frequently breaking line of sight with affected enemies can result in taking much fewer hits as they cannot use the sound of gunfire to find players or other enemies that are in combat.
