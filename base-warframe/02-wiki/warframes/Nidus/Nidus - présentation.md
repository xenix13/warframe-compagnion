CODEX
Nidus draws strength from the Infestation. It bolsters him with survivability as he deals high damage and provides crowd control. Plague your enemies.

Release Date: December 22nd, 2016

Epitome of the endlessly evolving Infestation, Nidus bends the vile corruption to his will, mutates his genetic potency to adapt and assimilates his victims with an insatiable parasitic hunger. Nidus and his strain developed in Update 19.5 (2016-12-22).

Nidus is a Warframe that harnesses the Infestation to mutate his deadly and crowd controlling powers. His Passive grants Mutation Stacks from abilities to enhance them while allowing him to negate death. Virulence stomps the ground to release a trail of damaging fungal growth. Larva creates an Infested pod that latches onto enemies to group them. Parasitic Link tethers a link that grants weapon damage and Ability Strength on allies or Damage Redirection on enemies. Ravenous summons a field of Infested growth that provides Health restore over time while hatching Maggots that latch onto enemies and explode.

## Notes

Nidus with max Mutation Stacks
- Nidus has unique interactions due to his lack of Shields :
- The shield reduction from the Decaying Dragon Key has no effect, allowing Nidus to carry it without issue.
- The No Shields Nightmare Mode condition has no effect.
- Cryogenic Leakage has no effect on his nonexistent shields. His friction is still affected however.
- The increased shield damage from Magnetic has no effect. Energy drain and HUD disruption still occurs however.
- The use of Rage and Hunter Adrenaline is facilitated as the damages Nidus receives , directly affect his Health.
- The Hijack objective will drain his health instead in order to move.
- He cannot receive overshields, or gain shields from allied Shield Ospreys, Orokin Drones, Hildryn's Haven, or Protea's Grenade Fan.
- His lack of shields prevents him from receiving shield gating.

- Nidus can take advantage of his high Base Health and Armor by using Arcane Persistence , substantially increasing his resiliency.
- Using Parasitic Vitality in conjunction of Arcane Persistence grant him survival capacity that are beyond reach for most Warframes.
- Ravenous cleanse and Parasitic Link status immunity will protect him from Corrosive, Heat or Magnetic that can deactivate Arcane Persistence .

- Nidus can uniquely access the Orbiter's Helminth Infirmary without the Helminth Segment prerequisite. If he sits in the infirmary's chair, he will be injected with the "Helminth Virus", forming an Infested cyst on the left side of his neck after 24 hours and grows to full size after 7 days, while simultaneously "awakening" the Helminth which will begin to communicate to the Tenno. Nidus can also spread this virus to other players he comes into contact with in combat and in a Clan Dojo or Orbiter, causing the cyst to grow on their Warframe, and those Warframes can also further propagate the virus.
- The injection will only occur once, and will not happen again to additionally built Nidus. However, Nidus can still receive the cyst from another infected player.
- This is not to be confused with the Technocyte Coda cyst.

- When entering the Helminth room, Nidus will automatically switch to his fully infested form (as he appears with 10 Mutation Stacks). As he leaves the room, he will revert to his normal appearance.
- Nidus's appearance changes as he accumulates Mutations Stack:
- At 3 stacks, tendrils will protrude from his neck and upper spine, while the carapace around his ribs will open.
- At 5 and 7 stacks respectively, the carapace around his thighs and arms will open to reveal more infested flesh and tendrils beneath.
- At 10 stacks, the sides of his head extend outward to form a pair of horns, also exposing many more tendrils.
