Info

- Nidus expends 25 energy to eject an infested larva at high speed toward the targeted location over unrestricted range. Upon impact, the larva rapidly matures into a floating mass of infested tentacles that sprouts tendrils to grab all enemies within Line of Sight and a radius of 8 / 9 / 10 / 12 meters, forcefully Ragdolling and pulling them into the mass, becoming unable to move or attack while grotesquely meshing together within the writhing whirlpool of flesh. Larva will wither away when it has no enemies to grip, is cast when no enemies are in range, or after 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 seconds have elapsed, dropping enemies to the ground upon expiration.
- Each enemy killed from any source while being held by Larva has a 50% chance to generate 1/5th of a Mutation stack, capped at a 100% chance at 200% Ability Strength.
- Enemies gripped by tendrils will float helplessly and rotate around the Larva, similar to the Vortex portion of Bastille and Sandstorm. Dealing damage or killing enemies in this mass can cause other affected enemies to bounce outward, swing wildly in an orbit, then be pulled back in.
- Larva will attach to enemies affected by Bastille; however, it will not pull them.
- If enemies cannot be ragdolled by Larva after being attached to tendrils, they will be released after 3 seconds.
- Has a cast time of 0.6 seconds.
- Can be recast, destroying the previous instance of Larva.
- Larva is a One-Handed Action that allows movement and doesn't interrupt reload, but restricts other actions.
- Enemies grabbed by Larva are alerted to the player's presence.
- Enemies latched onto by its tendrils visually appear to be covered in infestation, while robotic targets appear encrusted in rust.

Kit Interactions

- Larva can be used to gather enemies with Ravenous's Maggots latched on them to inflict their detonation damage to all enemies and increase Nidus's Mutation Stacks .
- Maggots seem to prioritize enemies that are trapped in Larva.
- Can be used to gain a large amount of mutation and Energy by maximizing the numbers of enemies affected by Virulence.
- Will not tether to an enemy currently affected by Parasitic Link.

Helminth

- Subsuming Nidus to the Helminth will offer Larva and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- However, Subsumed Larva's range is reduced to 5.33 / 6 / 6.67 / 8 meters.
- When Larva is injected onto a Prime Warframe, it will gain the appearance of Nidus Prime's Larva.

Augment

Main article: Larva Burst
Larva Burst is a Warframe Augment Mod for Nidus's Larva that allows the player to detonate Larva by recasting the ability, dealing Toxin damage proportional to the enemies trapped in Larva beforehand in a small area of effect.

Tips & Tricks

- It is highly recommended to build for range to allow Larva to catch as many enemies as possible.
- Weapons and abilities with splash damage and/or Punch Through can be used to quickly dispatch enemies stuck in Larva.
- When Larva expires, enemies will drop to the ground and begin to recover from their ragdoll state. Recast Larva to immediately disable them again.
- Enemies with Overguard are immune to the ragdoll and pull effects and are not disabled, but will remain attached to tendrils showing their positions.
- Scyotid's alt-fire can replace the grouping functionality provided by Larva, which comes with both benefits and drawbacks:
- Benefits of replacing Larva with Scyotid:
- No cast time.
- Scyotid's alt-fire can group enemies normally unaffected by Larva.
- This include enemies affected by Parasitic Link letting Nidus reposition them at will .

- The pull speed of the alt-fire is faster and more predictable, as it moves and stuns the enemy as opposed to ragdolling them.
- This can enable easier headshots and Incarnon charging.

- Reduced need for Ability Range or Ability Duration, although these stats are still valuable for Nidus's other abilities.
- As the alt-fire does not require energy, a smaller energy pool or low Ability Efficiency will not impact it.
- Can be used when other abilities are locked.
- Can be modded to deal on it's own against Nullifier Crewman' bubbles and strip enemies off their Overguard to maximize potential targets for alt-fire.
- Allows for subsuming other abilities onto Nidus, expanding build variety.

- Drawbacks of replacing Larva with Scyotid:
- Scyotid's alt-fire does not grant 1/5th Mutation when a snared enemy is killed.
- Without Larva, the augment Larva Burst cannot be used.
- At base stats, the Scyotid's alt-fire affects 8 valid enemies within range (increasing with higher Multishot values, potentially as many as 20+ enemies), while Larva will affect all valid enemies within range and Line of Sight.
- The alt-fire has a static area of effect of 15 metres. Larva's area of effect can equal this at only 125% range, with greater investment able to even double this range, allowing for many more enemies to be ensnared at once.
- This is of particular benefit in more open tilesets , but has diminished usefulness in more cramped, corridor-heavy tilesets.

- Like Larva Scyotid's alt fire has no effect on enemies with Overguard.
- One difference between the two that is also worth noting is that Larva pulls enemies into itself, while Scyotid's alt-fire will pull enemies horizontally to the nearest point on the floor to where the alt-fire landed.
