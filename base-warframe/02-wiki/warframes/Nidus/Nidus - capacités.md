## Passive

This section is transcluded from Nidus/Abilities/Passive. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.

The Infestation dwelling within Nidus mutates rapidly when used against his foes, fueling his abilities and yielding various symbiotic benefits.

Mutation Stacks are a resource unique to Nidus, indicated by the Mutation counter and gauge displayed in the lower-right on the HUD, above the ability icons.

- The gauge is divided into 5 sections which represent the number of hits dealt to enemies via Nidus's abilities, while the counter at the center of the gauge keeps track of the number of Mutation stacks up to a maximum of 200.
  - When generating Mutation, the sections light up clockwise. Once all 5 sections have lit up, the gauge completes a cycle and the sections become unlit again, while the counter increases by 1 Mutation stack. The following abilities generate 1/5th of a Mutation stack:
    - Each enemy hit with Virulence, from direct hits and damage over time.
    - Each enemy killed from any source while held by Larva at a 50% chance. A stack is guaranteed at 200% Ability Strength.
    - Each enemy hit by Ravenous's maggot explosion.
    - Abilities are not required to deal damage to enemies, only ability contact is needed to increase Mutation stacks.
    - When gaining or losing large numbers of stacks, the gauge only changes one at a time, making it difficult to estimate the actual count.
      - This can be bypassed by briefly switching to the Operator, as this will instantly refresh to the current amount of stacks.
  - Mutation stacks are stored until used by the player. Stacks will not disappear in the event of a host migration.
  - Ability-nullifying enemies such as Nullifier Crewman's Bubble will drain 1 Mutation stack every 2 seconds, ramping up the drain rate the longer Nidus remains in their radius.
  - Each Mutation stack causes the gauge to gradually appear more infested, and upon reaching maximum stacks the counter is hidden and the gauge is overlapped by Virulence's ability icon colored in red and black.
- The amount of Mutation stacks stored benefits Nidus' abilities:
  - Increases the damage of Virulence and Ravenous' Maggot explosion.
  - Ravenous costs 3 stacks to activate instead of using Energy.
- Nidus' appearance changes as more stacks are collected:
  - With the base skin:
    - At 3 stacks, tendrils will protrude from his neck and upper spine, while the carapace around his ribs will open.
    - At 5 and 7 stacks respectively, the carapace around his thighs and arms will open to reveal more infested flesh and tendrils beneath.
    - At 10 stacks, certain sections of his currently-equipped helmet will open up.
      - With the base helmet, the sides of his head extend outward to form a pair of horns, also exposing many more tendrils.
      - With the Nidus Prion Helmet, the "mouth" and the ligature open up, revealing Void-like energy underneath.
      - With the Nidus Myxini Helmet, the helmet opens up down the middle to reveal a monstrous mouth-like face.
  - With the Nidus Phryke Skin:
    - At 3 stacks, he gains protective armor on his thighs, and his shoulder pads are covered in a cage-like armor with a protruding spike on the top.
    - At 5 stacks, a large curved blade appears on each of the shoulder pad spikes.
    - At 7 stacks, his forearms are lined with a curved blade that extends two large spikes.
    - At 10 stacks, his face becomes covered with a crown-like visor.
  - With Nidus Prime:
    - At 3 stacks, dark blue tendrils form on the left side of his face, as well as his entire left arm.
    - At 5 stacks, the tendrils extend to cover his chest and back.
    - At 7 stacks, the right side of his body becomes covered in a dark blue mass as the golden metallic spikes on his right arm protrude outward, while tendrils form on his lower body.
    - At 10 stacks, the tendrils extend to cover his entire head and thighs.
  - When stacks fall below the required triggers, Nidus's appearance will revert to the appropriate stage.

If Nidus suffers fatal damage with at least 15 stacks of Mutation, Nidus will consume the 15 stacks to avoid bleedout while receiving 5 seconds of invulnerability and restoring 50% of his health.

- A UI banner displaying "Undying" and an invulnerability timer will appear at the top-center of the screen while this passive is activated. The required amount of Mutation stacks will be visibly subtracted from the counter and gauge.
- If Nidus has fewer than 15 Mutation stacks, all stacks will be consumed and he will not Death Gate.
- If Nidus has 15 or more stacks while Quick Thinking is equipped, Undying will be given a higher priority, triggering before any energy is drained by Quick Thinking.

 | 1 40 | Virulence Rupture the ground with a damaging fungal growth that steals energy from each enemy it strikes. For every five enemies hit, the Infestation mutates, multiplying its destructive force. Introduced in Update 19.5 (2016-12-22) | Strength:150 / 160 / 175 / 200 Puncture Damage (Initial Hit Damage) 75 / 80 / 87.5 / 100 Viral Damage (Lingering Field Damage)
Duration:5 s (Lingering Field Duration)
Range:10 / 11 / 13 / 16 m
Misc: 4 m (fungal growth width) 10 (energy cost refund per hit)

Info

- Nidus expends 40 energy to rupture the ground to unleash a linear growth of fungal infestation 10 / 11 / 13 / 16 meters long and 4 meters wide toward the targeted location. Enemies struck by fungal growth as it extends refund 10 energy, are Staggered, and dealt 150 / 160 / 175 / 200 Puncture damage, amplified by the number of Mutation stacks. The fungal growth lingers for 5 seconds, dealing 75 / 80 / 87.5 / 100 Viral Damage per second, amplified by the number of Mutation stacks, to all enemies in its range.
  - Virulence's total damage uses the following expression when accounting for Ability Strength and Mutation stacks: Amplified Damage = Base Damage × (1 + Ability Strength rank bonus + Ability Strength) × (1 + Number of Stacks)
    - With a maxed Intensify, 15% Ability Strength rank bonus, and 100 stacks, rank-3 Virulence's total damage will be increased to: 200 × (1 + 0.15 + 0.3) × (1 + 100) = 29,290 damage (decimals are rounded down to the nearest whole number during calculations).
  - Virulence Damages use a snapshot of both Mutation stacks and Ability Strength.
  - Energy refund scales inversely with Ability Efficiency, meaning less Efficiency increases the amount of energy refunded.
    - Energy refund value is equal to 1/4th of the ability's energy cost, meaning hitting at least 4 enemies minimum will restore the energy used to cast Virulence.
    - Energy refund value is based on cost, not energy spent. Energy cost being removed by abilities such as Zenurik's Inner Might will not reduce energy refund.
  - Fungal growth will only travel along terrain that can be simply walked upon, such as slopes.
- Holding the ability key displays a rectangular range indicator on the horizontal plane in front of Nidus. Releasing the key casts Virulence. Range indicator color is affected by the chosen energy color.
  - Player can move the aiming reticle while holding down the button to aim Virulence at the desired direction.
  - While held, the player can still perform other actions such as moving, jumping, firing weapons, and casting other abilities. Releasing the key while in midair will exit range indicator mode without casting Virulence.
- Virulence will cast toward the targeted location regardless of Nidus' orientation.
- If cast in midair, Nidus will instantly drop to the ground to perform this ability.
- Cannot be recast until the fungal growth's maximum length is reached or until it cannot spawn any further due to obstruction. Since the maximum length is affected by Ability Range, it also impacts how long before Virulence can be recast.
- Casting Speed only affects the speed of Nidus' stomping animation and not the speed at which it grows to its maximum length.
- Virulence does not spawn up or down high terrain elevations, but does flow up and down stairs and ramps.
- Virulence will not detonate maggots spawned by the Strain Mod Set, the Pathocyst, and Volatile Parasite.

Kit Interactions

- Virulence increases Nidus Mutation stacks by 1/5th per enemy hit, from both direct hits and damage over time.
- While Nidus is connected to a target via Parasitic Link, using Virulence will spawn a second instance from the linked target that grows toward the targeted location, converging with to the destination of the first instance from Nidus.
  - Enemies hit by the second instance also increase Mutation stacks.
    - Enemies hit by the second instance also reward energy to Nidus at the same rate.
  - While connected via Parasitic Link, a second range indicator visible to Nidus only will also appear around the linked target if Nidus hold the 1 ability's key.
    - The color of the indicator is tied to the warframe energy color.

  - It will also mark Maggot from Ravenous on the Nidus' player HUD, allowing increased visibility for selective detonation using Virulence.

- Maggots normally
- Maggots when 1 ability key is held down.

- Maggots spawned from Ravenous will immediately explode when hit by Virulence, stacking with Ravenous' Mutation gain.

Tips & Tricks

- Can stagger enemies to help keep them in place for recasts.
- Can completely refund its cost when it hits at least 4 targets. 5 or more targets allow Nidus to earn extra energy.
  - Due to the refund per hit always being kept at 1/4th the ability's cost, negative efficiency can be used to gain more energy over 4 hits at the cost of a higher initial cast cost.
- Collect Mutation stacks to amplify Virulence's damage drastically over the course of a mission.
- Hold the ability key to line up Virulence on groups of enemies before releasing to cast. This is an efficient method to gather extra energy and collect hits for stacks.
- Use Larva to gather enemies in one spot, then unleash Virulence to optimize damage and stack collection.
- Casting Virulence while Parasitic Link is active allows both Nidus and the linked target to create a line of fungal growths from your positions toward where you aim. This allows Nidus to effectively extend his reach on Virulence, and collect double hits for stacks if both lines hit the same enemies. Hold the ability key to help determine where you and your linked targets' lines will spawn.
- Can detonate Maggots spawned from Ravenous by you and any Nidus players in your squad. Hits from both the fungal growth and maggot explosion each count toward the Mutation gauge. Blow up anyone's Maggots to help you and your brethren gather more stacks quickly!
- If the fungal growth cannot spawn any further because of obstruction, you can immediately recast Virulence.
  - Since the speed of fungal growth increases with length, having a high Ability Range means the growth will hit the obstruction faster, allowing for much faser recasting.
- Virulence can bypass frost eximus' globes but will stop when entering in contact of nullifiers bubbles .
- Work well in conjonction with slowing/stunning abilities or effects (ex:Gloom or Cold) , as ennemies staying in the virulence path will generate stacks .
  - The energy refund will help sustaining expensive abilities such as Gloom.
    - Gloom's Lifesteal will also make Virulence grant Health on initial impact and lingering damages .

 | 2 25 | Larva Spawn an Infested pod that erupts with tendrils, latches onto nearby enemies and pulls them in. Enemies killed while held have a chance of generating a Mutation stack. Introduced in Update 19.5 (2016-12-22) | Strength:50 % (Mutation stack chance)
Duration:4 / 5 / 6 / 7 s
Range:8 / 9 / 10 / 12 m (grab radius) 5.33 / 6 / 6.67 / 8 m (grab radius for subsumed version)
Misc: 3 s (release delay for unaffected targets)
Subsumable to Helminth

Info

- Nidus expends 25 energy to eject an infested larva at high speed toward the targeted location over unrestricted range. Upon impact, the larva rapidly matures into a floating mass of infested tentacles that sprouts tendrils to grab all enemies within Line of Sight and a radius of 8 / 9 / 10 / 12 meters, forcefully Ragdolling and pulling them into the mass, becoming unable to move or attack while grotesquely meshing together within the writhing whirlpool of flesh. Larva will wither away when it has no enemies to grip, is cast when no enemies are in range, or after 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 seconds have elapsed, dropping enemies to the ground upon expiration.
- Each enemy killed from any source while being held by Larva has a 50% chance to generate 1/5th of a Mutation stack, capped at a 100% chance at 200% Ability Strength.
- Enemies gripped by tendrils will float helplessly and rotate around the Larva, similar to the Vortex portion of Bastille and Sandstorm. Dealing damage or killing enemies in this mass can cause other affected enemies to bounce outward, swing wildly in an orbit, then be pulled back in.
- Larva will attach to enemies affected by Bastille; however, it will not pull them.
- If enemies cannot be ragdolled by Larva after being attached to tendrils, they will be released after 3 seconds.
- Has a cast time of 0.6 seconds.
- Can be recast, destroying the previous instance of Larva.
- Larva is a One-Handed Action that allows movement and doesn't interrupt reload, but restricts other actions.
- Enemies grabbed by Larva are alerted to the player's presence.
- Enemies latched onto by its tendrils visually appear to be covered in infestation, while robotic targets appear encrusted in rust.

Kit Interactions

- Larva can be used to gather enemies with Ravenous's Maggots latched on them to inflict their detonation damage to all enemies and increase Nidus's Mutation Stacks .
- Maggots seem to prioritize enemies that are trapped in Larva.
- Can be used to gain a large amount of mutation and Energy by maximizing the numbers of enemies affected by Virulence.
- Will not tether to an enemy currently affected by Parasitic Link.

Helminth

- Subsuming Nidus to the Helminth will offer Larva and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
  - However, Subsumed Larva's range is reduced to 5.33 / 6 / 6.67 / 8 meters.
  - When Larva is injected onto a Prime Warframe, it will gain the appearance of Nidus Prime's Larva.

Tips & Tricks

- It is highly recommended to build for range to allow Larva to catch as many enemies as possible.
- Weapons and abilities with splash damage and/or Punch Through can be used to quickly dispatch enemies stuck in Larva.
- When Larva expires, enemies will drop to the ground and begin to recover from their ragdoll state. Recast Larva to immediately disable them again.
- Enemies with Overguard are immune to the ragdoll and pull effects and are not disabled, but will remain attached to tendrils showing their positions.
- Scyotid's alt-fire can replace the grouping functionality provided by Larva, which comes with both benefits and drawbacks:
  - Benefits of replacing Larva with Scyotid:
    - No cast time.
    - Scyotid's alt-fire can group enemies normally unaffected by Larva.
      - This include enemies affected by Parasitic Link letting Nidus reposition them at will .
    - The pull speed of the alt-fire is faster and more predictable, as it moves and stuns the enemy as opposed to ragdolling them.
      - This can enable easier headshots and Incarnon charging.
    - Reduced need for Ability Range or Ability Duration, although these stats are still valuable for Nidus's other abilities.
    - As the alt-fire does not require energy, a smaller energy pool or low Ability Efficiency will not impact it.
    - Can be used when other abilities are locked.
    - Can be modded to deal on it's own against Nullifier Crewman' bubbles and strip enemies off their Overguard to maximize potential targets for alt-fire.
    - Allows for subsuming other abilities onto Nidus, expanding build variety.
  - Drawbacks of replacing Larva with Scyotid:
    - Scyotid's alt-fire does not grant 1/5th Mutation when a snared enemy is killed.
    - Without Larva, the augment Larva Burst cannot be used.
    - At base stats, the Scyotid's alt-fire affects 8 valid enemies within range (increasing with higher Multishot values, potentially as many as 20+ enemies), while Larva will affect all valid enemies within range and Line of Sight.
    - The alt-fire has a static area of effect of 15 metres. Larva's area of effect can equal this at only 125% range, with greater investment able to even double this range, allowing for many more enemies to be ensnared at once.
      - This is of particular benefit in more open tilesets , but has diminished usefulness in more cramped, corridor-heavy tilesets.
  - Like Larva Scyotid's alt fire has no effect on enemies with Overguard.
  - One difference between the two that is also worth noting is that Larva pulls enemies into itself, while Scyotid's alt-fire will pull enemies horizontally to the nearest point on the floor to where the alt-fire landed.

 | 3 25 | Parasitic Link Bind to a target with parasitic link. When cast on an ally, both the host and Nidus have their Ability Strength and Weapon Damage increased. Linked enemies take some of the damage inflicted on Nidus. Introduced in Update 19.5 (2016-12-22) | Strength:10% / 15% / 20% / 25% (strength bonus) 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% (damage bonus) 20% / 30% / 40% / 50% (damage redirection)
Duration:30 / 35 / 45 / 60 s
Range:24 / 28 / 34 / 40 m (ally link) 10 / 13 / 16 / 20 m (enemy link)
Misc: 4 s (link break grace period) = 6 s ( post link brake effect grace period)

Info

- Nidus expends 25 energy to perform symbiosis with a targeted ally Warframe or Companion (excluding Sentinels) within 24 / 28 / 34 / 40 meters or parasitically link with a targeted enemy within 10 / 13 / 16 / 20 meters. The link persists as long as Nidus and his target remain in range of each other, lasting for 30 / 35 / 45 / 60 seconds or when manually deactivated by holding the ability key.
- Holding the ability key 3 will make Parasitic Link target the nearest ally from Nidus (Targeting in priority ally that is the closest from Nidus' crosshair while using a relaxed line of sight check ignoring obstacles and allowing tether to targets outside field of view).
  - When linked with an ally Warframe or Companion, Nidus and his target both receive a 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% Ability Strength bonus and a 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% Weapon Damage bonus.
    - Parasitic Link's Ability Strength bonus affects Ability Strength multiplicatively using the following expressions:
    - Modified Strength Bonus = Strength Bonus × (1 + Ability Strength)
      - With a maxed Intensify and the 15% Ability Strength rank bonus, Nidus will have 145% Ability Strength, resulting in 0.25 × (1 + 0.3 + 0.15) = 36.25% Modified Strength Bonus displayed on the UI.
      - Modified Ability Strength = (1 + Ability Strength) × (1 + Modified Strength Bonus)
      - Modified Strength Bonus then increases Ability Strength multiplicatively, granting 1.45 × (1 + 0.3625) = 197.5625% Modified Ability Strength that affects Nidus' other abilities while a rank-3 Parasitic Link is active.
      - The Modified Strength Bonus is the same percentage for the linked ally. However, Modified Ability Strength's calculation will depend on the linked ally's own Ability Strength.
    - The damage bonus is multiplicative to other damage bonus sources such as Serration and is a Universal Weapon Bonus.
      - Damage bonus does not affect the damage gained from additive Condition Overload sources.
        - For example at 200% ability strength, Parasitic Link will increase weapon damage by 50%. Using a melee weapon with 100 base damage and Condition Overload, attacking an enemy affected by 1 status will cause them to take (100 * 1.5) + (100 * 0.8) = 230 damage. If Parasitic Link's damage bonus affected the CO damage bonus, they would instead take 100 * 1.5 * 1.8 = 270 damage.
  - When linked with an enemy, the target is incapacitated for the duration while Nidus gains 20% / 30% / 40% / 50% Damage Redirection toward the linked target.
    - All negative Status Effects, including Knockdown and Stagger, are also redirected.
    - Damage Redirection is capped at 90%, achievable with 180% Ability Strength.
    - The enemy target is invulnerable to outside sources of damage and immune to external crowd control effects while linked, but is not immune to damage and crowd control effects transferred from Nidus. All damage prevented by the external invulnerability is stored and dealt in full when the ability ends.
    - While Parasitic Link can be used on enemies with Overguard, the target will only be incapacitated and made invulnerable after Overguard is removed.
  - Parasitic Link does not require Line of Sight to maintain connection between Nidus and his target.
  - Casting Parasitic Link is a One-Handed Action and can be done while in midair.
  - Can be recast to move the link to the new target.
    - Recasting on the same target will refresh Parasitic Link's duration.
  - If an enemy target dies during cast before the link is attached, the energy used for Parasitic Link will be refunded.
  - If the target moves out of range, dies or is nullified, there is a 3 second grace period before the link breaks.
    - If the target moved back into range or leaves the nullification field before the grace period ends, the link will not be broken.
    - If Nidus is the one nullified, the link will be broken immediately.
  - When Parasitic Link ends, there is a 6 second grace period before previously-affected players lose any bonuses gained, including Health granted by Parasitic Vitality.
- Nidus and the target are visibly covered in infestation and connected via a tether of biochemical energy, which is affected by the chosen Warframe energy color.
- Cannot link to a target already affected by Parasitic Link. Multiple Nidus players cannot link to the same target, nor to each other while their own Parasitic Link ability is active.
- When connected to another Nidus player, both players will use the Ability Strength bonus from the casting player; the link will break if the partner activates Parasitic Link on a different target.
- Cannot link to enemies affected by Mind Control.

Kit Interactions

- While linked to a target, using Virulence will spawn a second instance from the linked target that grows towards the targeted location, converging with the first instance from Nidus.
  - The second instance grants Mutation stacks independently of the first, allowing for multiple Mutation stacks to be gained from the same enemies.
- Ability Strength increase from linking with an ally or Companion will affect all of Nidus's abilities.

Tips & Tricks

- Link with a nearby teammate/companion or enemy for Virulence to create two lines from Nidus and the linked target. This effectively extends Virulence's reach, doubles its damage output, staggers enemies in its paths, can hit more enemies for mutation stacks, and earns extra energy for the cost of one cast.
  - Virulence's range indicator, activated by holding 1 before releasing to cast the ability, will show the directions both Nidus and his linked target will cast towards.
- Bringing a free moving Companion is strongly advised, as it allows Nidus to use Parasitic Link to gain additional Ability Strength and Weapon Damage.

  - Panzer Vulpaphyla synergize well with Nidus. Using Viral Quills and Hunter Adrenaline offer a consistent source of healing. Be mindful that Panzer Vulpaphyla can feel very vulnerable on high level content. Nidus is unable to apply Parasitic Link to any Vulpaphyla while they are in their larval states, as they are considered sentinels rather than free moving companions in this state.

  - Hound may be preferred for such content, with the precept mod Reflex Denial and Null Audit the hound will offer Nidus a strong and reliable anchor point for Parasitic Link while granting him and his teammates all sorts of beneficial effects depending on the copied Eximus power.

- Link Fiber and Link Vitality can greatly increase your Companion's survivability, especially when using Parasitic Vitality. Pack Leader/Primed Pack Leader can additionally help sustain its health.

- Using Arcane Bellicose and Parasitic Vitality will let you gain additional Ability Strength, increasing Parasitic Vitality's effectiveness.

- Enemies with high health and armor values such as Heavy Gunners can absorb a large amount of damage when linked to.
- Linked enemies with Shield will see their Shield restored overtime after some time passes without taking damage.
- As long as Nidus and his target are in range of each other, Parasitic Link's biochemical tether will help you visually track your positions.
- Before reviving a downed teammate, link with a nearby enemy to protect yourself from taking too much damage.
- A linked enemy, despite being immune to damage, will still count as being hit for effects that require doing so, such as gaining melee combo, charging Incarnon gauge, and triggering relevant Arcane effects.
  - Coda Hema can let player heal from headshots on the linked target.
  - Sancti Magistar will partially proc its Lifesteal effect, attackers will not be granted any Health Regen BUT nearby allies and objective will be healed.
  - Coda Hirudo will not proc its Lifesteal effects on enemies affected by Parasitic Link.
- Maggots from Ravenous will ignore your linked enemy target. However, Maggot's explosions will still count as hits for the Mutation gauge even when they cannot damage the target. When you cast Parasitic Link on an enemy with maggots attached, the maggots will detach once the link is established.
  - They are also not valid targets for parasitic link.
- Parasitic Link can be cast on Juggernauts, and though it will not immobilize the Juggernaut it will still transfer damage to it, bypassing the Juggernaut's normally-impenetrable armor.
  - This also applies to other Boss enemies such as Acolytes, Kuva Lich, and Sisters of Parvos.
- Can be used to immobilize Synthesis targets, instead of deploying Kinetic Siphon Trap. This also prevents the target from being killed, provided Nidus himself is not under heavy fire.
- Parasitic Link can transfer negative Conduit Effects given in Disruption. For example, Energy Drain will drop from 5 energy per second to 1. This reduces, but does not negate, the threat of negative effects from conduits, should one fail.
- Teammates with Warframes that possess high ability damage and Ability Strength-based effects are prime candidates for Parasitic Link.
  - Allies such as Wisp,Dante or Revenant before they cast Reservoirs, Light Verse or Mesmer Skin can provide tremendously improved squad wide effects.
- Linking to the last enemies of a Defense mission wave allows you to stall the wave, which lets Ravenous heal the Defense Objective without risking the health of the defense objective.

Tips & Tricks

- Ravenous snapshots ability stat bonuses (e.g. Energy Conversion, Growing Power) and will keep the same stats if recast before expiring.

- Ravenous is a formidable tool to protect objectives and control the map due to the Maggots forcing enemies to attack them instead of players or objectives .

- The passive heal and status clear make it valuable when fighting Technocyte Coda in their arena .

  - Placing Ravenous on the edge of the arena will allow the Maggots to spawn outside of arena's bounds and reach enemies that are not yet in .

  - Using Virulence while touching the arena's edge will allow you to detonate them .

- Constantly detonating Maggots using Virulence helps maximize damage output and Mutation gained as Maggots will continuously respawn throughout the duration of Ravenous.
