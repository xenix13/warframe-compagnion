Info

- Nidus expends 3 Mutation stacks into ravenous Infestation, which spreads outward from Nidus and across the surrounding landscape to create spawning grounds with a diameter of 8 meters, lingering for 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 seconds. When inside or above the spawning grounds, Nidus and his allies will regenerate / 55 / ? / 75 health per second and are cleansed of Status Effects.
- Ravenous will NOT prevent Stagger nor Knockdown.
- The amount of Mutation stacks used is NOT affected by Ability Efficiency .
- Only 1 spawning grounds can be active at a time.
- Recasting Ravenous will make all maggots explode granting stacks if they touched enemies neaby hosts.

- Has a casting time of 1.5 seconds.
- Nidus become Invulnerable during it .

- Health regeneration area is an invisible cylinder . A buff icon beside the shield and health indicators on the HUD indicates when the player is affected by it, and what the health regeneration rate is.
- Affected allies includes all Warframes, Companions, Eidolon Lures, summoned allied units such as Specters, allied Invasion units, Hostages, Kavor Defectors, Sortie and Arbitrations Defense Operatives, and Defense Objects.
- Defense Objects will be healed only if their passive hp regen kicks in , them taking damage to health will prevent in heal .
- Healing is applied at 100% of its strength on any eligible entity.
- Cannot heal Player's Necramechs

- Health regeneration per second is calculated when Ravenous is cast, and is not be recalculated if any changes in Ability Strength occur while Ravenous is active.

- Spawning grounds are created at the location where Nidus casts the ability.
- Spawning grounds' infestation does not expand up or down high terrain elevations, but does flow up and down stairs and ramps.
- Within the spawning grounds, various unique models will spawn for visual representation only. Players and AI can path through them unhindered. Textures on the ground are blended with an infestation mesh.
- Numerous patches of infested flora resembling tall grass encompass the ground, rustling when brushed past by players and AI while glowing in the chosen energy color. Several infested branches sprout from the infestation to attach to nearby walls and objects in vertical and diagonal axis. Branches glow in the chosen energy color.

- Three infested nests erupt on the edges of the infestation, positioned in triangular vertices. Each nest spawns 3 Maggots, for a maximum of 9 active Maggots.
- Nests serve to maintain the Infestation. If a Nullifier's Bubble comes in contact with a nest, it will dissipate; when all three infested nests are dissipated, Ravenous will expire automatically.
- Maggots use a custom model to differentiate them from an enemy Maggot.
- Maggots possess a base health of 1000, scaled by their level and draw enemy aggro.
- Maggots have their own rank equal to Nidus' rank (e.g., a rank-25 Nidus' Maggots will be level 25).
- On death, a new Maggot will spawn from a nest throughout the duration of Ravenous, up to the maximum number of 9 active Maggots.

- After casting Ravenous, Maggots will immediately start to spawn. Once a Maggot detect an enemy, they will move with increased movement speed and leap toward their prey to latch onto them. While attached to an enemy, each Maggot will continuously stun its target and inflict 10 Toxin damage per second until the target dies or Maggot expires.
- Maggots can latch onto enemies with Overguard they will damage it but their stun effects will not apply until the Overguard is removed .

- If the Maggot or it's host dies , any enemies in explosion range will count towards Mutation.
- Maggots explosion increases Nidus's Mutation stacks by 1/5th per explosion per enemy hit (excluding host).

- - If Nidus is far from ravenous nest , all maggots that were not latched on enemies will be teleported to his position and attack enemies nearby him .
- Latch damage is amplified by the number of Mutation stacks accumulated using the following formula: Toxin Damage × (1 + Number of Stacks)
- Due to the total damage calculation accounting for Mutation stacks after the cost of Ravenous, it is not possible to reach amplified damage results above 197 Mutation stacks (497 with Abundant Mutation).

- Multiple Maggots cannot attach or attack the same enemy target . They will wait until the death of the currently latched Maggot before trying to latch again on this target .
- If a Maggot cannot attach to an enemy, it will instead bite or seek other enemies to latch on to , if no more enemies are present maggots will idle inside ravenous or teleport to Nidus if he is too far .

- When a Maggot dies, either from being hit by Virulence, its health being depleted, its host dying, or Ravenous's duration expiring, it explodes, dealing 100 / 110 / 125 / 150 Blast damage to all enemies within a 4 meter radius.
- Except when they are nullified , visiually an explostion will happen but no stack will be granted .
- Explosion total damage uses the following expression when accounting for Ability Strength and Mutation stacks:
Base Explosion Damage × (1 + Ability Strength rank bonus + Strength Mods) × (1 + Number of Stacks) =
- With a maxed Intensify, 15% Ability Strength rank bonus, and 100 stacks, rank-3 Ravenous's total damage will be increased to:
150 × (1 + 0.15 + 0.3) × (1 + 100) = 21,967.5
- Due to the total damage calculation accounting for Mutation stacks after the cost of Ravenous, it is not possible to reach amplified damage results above 197 Mutation stacks (497 with Abundant Mutation).

- Maggots are counted as Summons and are affected by Summoner's Wrath, Arcane Camisado, and Theorem Infection.
- The Maggots' death burst is not considered summon damage, and is not affected by the above. However, their melee attacks are.
- Their on-death explosions count as Nidus' ability damage. (See #Bugs).
- Maggots affected by Nourish will burst with Viral damage and status, which lets them both prime the enemies and quickly stack Arcane Camisado.

- Maggots will not attach to small enemies, such as a Brood Mother's Maggots and Kuva Jesters.
- Maggot Nests glow, pulsate, and emit spore particles, which are affected by the chosen energy color.

- If another Nidus use Ravenous both healing effects will stack .

Kit Interactions

- Maggots hit by Virulence will immediately explode.
- At the end of Ravenous, all maggots will expire and explode, granting stacks to Nidus if they hit enemies.

Tips & Tricks

- Ravenous snapshots ability stat bonuses (e.g. Energy Conversion, Growing Power) and will keep the same stats if recast before expiring.

- Ravenous is a formidable tool to protect objectives and control the map due to the Maggots forcing enemies to attack them instead of players or objectives .

- The passive heal and status clear make it valuable when fighting Technocyte Coda in their arena .

- - Placing Ravenous on the edge of the arena will allow the Maggots to spawn outside of arena's bounds and reach enemies that are not yet in .

- - Using Virulence while touching the arena's edge will allow you to detonate them .

- Constantly detonating Maggots using Virulence helps maximize damage output and Mutation gained as Maggots will continuously respawn throughout the duration of Ravenous.
- Maggots can pick up motes from Wisp's Reservoirs ability. They are also affected by Mesmer Skin.
- During an ETA with enought range it can be used to heal flare affected by vamp rock by castîng it at around ~11 meters and preventing any damages to his health for few seconds .
