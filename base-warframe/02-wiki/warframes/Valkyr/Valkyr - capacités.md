# Valkyr/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Valkyr/Abilities

## Passive

Driven by fury, Valkyr builds Rage from meleeing and killing enemies, gaining up to 300% additional melee damage bonus. Additionally, if Valkyr receives a fatal hit while at or above 150% Rage, all of it is consumed to prevent death, grant her 5 seconds of invulnerability and regenerate 100% health. This ability has no cooldown.
- Valkyr gains Rage from:
- Melee attack: 3% per hit
- Melee kill or assisted kill: 12%
- Finisher or Parazon Mercy Kill: 27%

- After not building Rage for 5 seconds, the damage bonus decays at a non-linear rate dependent on the amount of Rage Valkyr has, with the following formula:
- λ=0.016n=ln⁡(1000λ)λd(t)={e−λt,if 0≤t≤ne−λn−0.001(t−n),if t>nBonus(t)=⌊300⋅d(t)⌋
- Above 18%, the meter decays by half over 43 seconds. Below 18%, the meter decays by 0.3% every second.

- Bonus damage is additive with mods like Pressure Point and Condition Overload.
- Damage bonus is displayed above the ability bars as a stylized UI, along with a gauge beneath it to represent the death prevention meter.
- Rage's value persists when inside Nullifier bubbles and when recovering from falling Out of Bounds.
- Works in Archwing missions.
- Hits from Melee Duplicate do not generate Rage. Likewise, damage from status effects will not contribute to Rage, although killing enemies with either source still counts as a melee kill, granting the usual 12% Rage. The same goes for Melee Influence.
- Hitting invulnerable enemies that can take hits (e.g. Arbitration Shield Drone) will generate Rage.

Valkyr is immune to Hard Landings, landing with no touchdown delay, without the need to roll, slide, or aim-glide beforehand.

- Valkyr's immunity to hard landings prevents her from triggering Heavy Impact.

| 1 25 | Rip Line Valkyr hurls forth a hook and pulls herself to whatever it hits. If it hits an enemy, nearby foes are pulled in as she unleashes a coordinated Melee attack. Introduced in Update 11.0 (2013-11-20) | Strength:300 / 400 / 500 / 600 ( Slash damage 

Duration:1s combo window 

Range:25 / 40 / 60 / 75 m 9m pull radius 

Misc: 35 m/s (terrain pull speed) 150 m/s (enemy pull speed) 0.4 s (cooldown) 

Info

- Valkyr expends 25 energy to fire a grappling hook that covers a range of 25 / 40 / 60 / 75 meters. If the hook hits terrain, Valkyr will be pulled towards the hook's location. If the hook hits an enemy, all enemies within 9 meters around the hooked enemy are pulled toward it and are dealt 300 / 400 / 500 / 600 Slash damage, before Valkyr lunges at the target. Has a 0.4 second cooldown.
- Pulled enemies that were not killed immediately are laid prone, briefly setting them up for Ground Finishers.
- If the hook hits terrain, Valkyr pulls herself towards the target at a speed of 35 m/s. If the hook hits an enemy, Valkyr pulls herself towards the target at a speed of 150 m/s.
- Hitting a corpse counts as hitting an enemy and will pull nearby enemies.

- If the hook hits an enemy and the target is alive when Valkyr reaches them, she automatically performs a melee attack.
- Casting Rip Line refreshes Valkyr's Double Jump.
- Jumping, meleeing, or casting another ability during Rip Line will cancel it while preserving forward momentum.
- Pressing backward movement key during cancel will stop all momentum.

- Can be recast, which briefly halts momentum before Valkyr pulls herself to the new location.

- If Rip Line is successfully cast within a window of 1 second after the previous cast, its damage will be multiplied by 200%, and its energy cost will be reduced by 50%. Any successful cast thereafter will multiply Rip Line's damage by 400% and reduce its energy cost by 75%.
- Energy cost reduction is applied to Rip Line's modified activation cost. As an example, with a maxed Streamline the next successful cast will consume 25 × 0.7 × 0.5 = 8.75 energy, and any successful cast thereafter will consume 25 × 0.7 × 0.25 = 4.375 energy.
- Combo window cannot be decreased below 0.5 seconds.
- The duration of the combo window and the respective damage multiplier for the next successful cast are displayed underneath the HUD's targeting reticle.
- If no casts are performed within the combo window, the damage and energy cost will reset.

- Rip Line's pull does not affect bosses with the exception of the Zanuka Hunter, Ambulas, and The Grustrag Three.
- Cannot be cast on openable doors.
- Rip Line is a one-handed ability when cast onto terrain and can be used while performing various maneuvers and actions without interruption. However, when casting on enemies (while grounded) will cause interruption.
- Will not disrupt Shade's Ghost or a Huras Kubrow's Stalk.
- Rip Line when used targeting Nullifiers will pass through the shield. When moving through the shield, momentum is preserved and Rip Line will not be cancelled.

Tips & Tricks

- This is another alternative ability for players to take advantage of heights quickly, similarly to Wormhole or Tail Wind, pulling Valkyr to elevated areas which are not accessible with traditional movement.
- As Rip Line is silent, it can be used as a mean of disposing enemies without alerting nearby units during stealth playthroughs.
- Rip Line will consistently mutilate enemies killed by the hook, as with heavily Slash-focused weapons. The pieces of their dismembered corpse will also be flung in Valkyr's general direction.
- Can be very helpful when used in tandem with a Desecrating Nekros, as each corpse will yield its own loot.
- Can potentially impede stealth play, as enemies may become alerted at the sight of either corpse. Targeting selectively and thinking ahead should help avoid such situations.

- It is possible to Rip Line onto a rocket fired from an Ogris.
- Very effective during Capture missions as it can bring the target near Valkyr or her teammates.
- Sprinting and Maglev make no noticeable impact on speed or distance, however.

- Very effective in PvP, as it allows the user to either quickly travel around an arena or pull and knock-down the opponent, rendering them an easy target.
- Will trigger the robotic mod Odomedic.

Info

- Valkyr expends 75 energy to let out a rallying cry, generating an aura within Affinity Range that strengthens Valkyr and her allies with 15% / 20% / 25% / 50% Attack Speed and 25% / 35% / 45% / 50% Armor for 10 / 14 / 17 / 20 seconds.
- If allies lose Warcry such as from death or nullifiers, they can regain it upon re-entering Valkyr's Affinity Range. However, if Valkyr herself enters a nullifier field or falls out-of-bounds, Warcry will be dispelled from the entire squad.
- Attack Speed bonus is additive to mods (e.g., Fury).
- A maxed Fury and Intensify with a rank-3 Warcry will increase Valkyr's attack speed by:
Attack Speed Mods + Warcry Modifier × (1 + Strength Mods) =
0.3 + 0.5 × (1 + 0.3) = 95%

- The armor buff is based off of the affected Warframe's base armor value, and therefore stacks additively with other armor bonuses.
- For example, a Valkyr with a max rank Steel Fiber, 130% Ability Strength, and while using Warcry increases her armor to:
Base Armor × (1 + Armor Mods + Warcry Modifier × (1 + Strength Mods)) =
855 × (1 + 1 + 0.5 × (1 + 0.3)) = 2,265.75

- Warcry affects other Warframes, Companions, Hostages, Defense objectives, Shadows, and Specters.

Kit Interactions

- Warcry's armor bonus is multiplied by 3x when used while Hysteria is active.
- Warcry's melee attack speed bonus allows Valkyr to charge her Rage passive faster, making it easier to reach the death prevention threshold while she is still invulnerable after a fatal hit.

Info

- Overcome with rage, Valkyr expends 25 energy to unleash a pair of energy talons, the Valkyr Talons, as her melee Exalted Weapon, while also becoming immune to Status Effects. Normal attacks deal 100 / 125 / 200 / 250 damage within 2.15 meters and all of Valkyr's attacks restore 70 / 80 / 90 / 100 Health per hit.
- Enemies that are immune to damage but can take hits (e.g. Arbitration Shield Drone) will also restore Valkyr's health.
- Exclusively uses the Hysteria Stance.
- Damage is distributed between 25% Puncture and 75% Slash.
- Wall attacks and slide attacks inflict 300 / 375 / 600 / 750 damage for each strike.
- Aerial attacks inflict 200 / 250 / 400 / 500 damage.
- Slam attacks inflict 300 damage within 5 meters.
- Attacks have a 2x critical multiplier, 50% critical chance, and 10% status chance.
- Valkyr Talons can be equipped with melee mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Valkyr Talons.

- Because attacks with Valkyr Talons are counted as melee strikes, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Hysteria consumes 5 energy per second while active and will remain active until either Valkyr's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Valkyr cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, and/or Wellspring while Hysteria is active.
- Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, and Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes can still replenish Valkyr's energy during Hysteria.
- Hysteria's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- While Hysteria is active, Valkyr can cast other abilities and use primary and secondary weapons. However, switching weapons will disable Hysteria's effects, including the status immunity, health restore, and Warcry's tripled armor, while continuing to drain energy over time.
- Cast time of 1 second is affected by Casting Speed.
- Hysteria's attacks can damage enemies across the Rift Plane.
- Movement-boosting passives from Incarnon melee weapons typically continue to function while Hysteria is active. A few examples include:
- Drifting Grace (+20% Sprint Speed, +20% Slide)
- Evolved Ascension (+30% Parkour Velocity)
- Striking Swiftness (+30% Sprint Speed)
- Swordsman's Celerity (+30% Movement Speed)

- Mods that boost Wind Up Speed equipped on your regular melee weapon benefit heavy attacks done with Valkyr Talons.
- This stacks with Wind Up Speed mods on Valkyr Talons itself.

Weapon

Main article: Valkyr Talons
Valkyr Talons are Valkyr and Valkyr Prime's signature Exalted Weapon, summoned by activating the ability Hysteria. The weapon exclusively uses the Hysteria stance.

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Innate and two polarities.

Advantages:

- Highest critical chance of all melee weapons.
- Innate 100 lifesteal on every hit.
- Very high attack speed.

Disadvantages:

- Low status chance.
- Short range.
- Requires energy to activate and keep active.

Tips & Tricks

- The health generation and high armor stat synergize greatly with Arcane Persistence, you just need to add max health.
- There may be a dark red hue for the duration of Hysteria, making it difficult to see enemies. This is especially problematic if Infested Parasites are nearby.
- Turning 'Color Correction' off in Options > Display can alleviate this issue.

- Considered alone, Steel Charge adds more damage to Hysteria than Growing Power, but this changes when using Condition Overload.
- Having enough status effects on the target causes CO's damage boost to vastly reduce Steel Charge's overall benefit, since they stack additively, while Growing Power's boost stacks with CO multiplicatively.
- Steel Charge does grant an extra 4 mod capacity to Valkyr herself, though.
- Even with Steel Charge equipped, using Power Drift will still add more damage than Coaction Drift.

- Heavy Attacks in Hysteria open enemies to Finishers, allowing you to reliably use the damage boost without spending energy on her Paralysis ability.
- This finisher also knocks enemies down, allowing for a successive ground finisher for a quick burst of high damage.

- The opening heavy attack in Hysteria's two-attack heavy combo is considered a slam, and thus benefits from Seismic Wave.
