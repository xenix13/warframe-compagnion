# Paralysis (capacité de Valkyr) (wiki)

Info

- Valkyr expends 25 energy to release a paralyzing wave within 5 / 7 / 8 / 10 meters around her, inflicting enemies within direct Line of Sight with 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 Impact damage, 15% / 20% / 25% / 30% Slow, and 20% / 30% / 40% / 50% Melee Damage Vulnerability for 15 seconds.
- Slow is capped at 75%.
- Enemies struck are stunned for 3 seconds (unaffected by Ability Duration), which leaves them open to Finisher attacks.
- If an enemy is already affected by a similar stun (e.g. Heat panic effect, Electricity's stun, being Frozen), this effect will not trigger, and the target will not be vulnerable to Finisher attacks.
- If the enemy cannot be attacked with a Finisher (either due to positioning or the enemy type), it will be susceptible to Stealth Damage Multipliers from melee attacks for the duration of the stun. As with other stealth attacks, coming into physical contact with the enemy while attacking will temporarily remove the damage bonus.

- Line of sight checks come from camera position, rather than Valkyr's position.
- Can destroy Objects, which does not require line of sight.
- Enemies with Overguard are affected by the Melee Vulnerability, but not by the slow.

Kit Interactions

- Paralysis enables Finisher attacks, which provides the biggest increase to Rage.

Augment

Main article: Prolonged Paralysis
Prolonged Paralysis is a Warframe Augment Mod for Valkyr that causes Paralysis to pull affected enemies towards her and inflict Knockdown.

Tips & Tricks

- Paralysis deals a blast with tremendous knockback, doing a full 360 angle of damage with half of the original range.
- A single use of Paralysis is typically sufficient to destroy all the fuel injectors in a Sabotage mission.
- Paralysis's ability to enable reliable Finishers synergizes well with Arcane Trickery, allowing Valkyr to benefit from stealth attacks and Arcane Crepuscular.

Bugs

- When Paralysis is used repeatably, Valkyr's accompanying scream may sometimes skip over itself and not play. This is merely an auditory bug.
- Enemies don't get opened to Finishers if they are animation-locked (e.g. under Heat's panic effect, Frozen, Electricuted).
- Paralysis can slow enemies that have Overguard.
