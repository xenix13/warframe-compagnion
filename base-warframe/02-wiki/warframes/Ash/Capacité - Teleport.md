# Teleport (capacité de Ash) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Teleport

| 3 25 | Teleport Teleport to a target and perform a Finisher. If the finisher kills the target, Ash regains a portion of the energy spent. Introduced in Vanilla (2012-10-25) | Strength:125% / 150% / 175% / 200% (finisher damage) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:20 / 45 / 45 / 60 m 

Misc: 50% (energy refund on finisher kill) 100 % (energy discount on Blade Storm target) 

Info

- Ash expends 25 Energy to perform a somersault as he teleports to a target within 20 / 45 / 45 / 60 meters on his aiming reticle. If the target is an enemy, Ash performs a melee Finisher or Mercy with a 125% / 150% / 175% / 200% damage bonus. Additionally, if the target is killed by the finisher, 50% of Teleport's energy cost is refunded.
- Eligible teleport targets include: enemies, allies, hostages, Cryopods, Excavators, and destructible objects.
- Requires direct Line of Sight to a small part of the target to cast. It should teleport through solid windows.

- Ash will perform a front finisher or back finisher depending on how the enemy is positioned relative to him. If the enemy is under the health threshold for Mercy, Ash will always perform a Mercy kill.
- When targeting certain strangely shaped enemies (e.g. Deimos Jugulus, Shuffling Fragment), Ash will use a generic "universal" finisher animation instead.
- If the enemy cannot be attacked with a melee finisher (due to positioning or enemy type), it will be susceptible to Stealth Damage Bonus for the duration of the stagger.
- As with Smoke Screen, coming into physical contact with the enemy while attacking will temporarily remove the damage bonus.

- Bonus finisher damage stacks additively with Finishing Touch and Covert Lethality, and multiplicatively with Finisher Damage Vulnerability effects, such as Savage Silence.
- Note that using Teleport on enemies will alert them to your presence, preventing you from receiving the Stealth Affinity Bonus.
- Wardens additionally lose the massive stealth attack bonus they receive from normal stealth attacks.

Kit Interactions

- Using Teleport on an enemy during Blade Storm's Shadow Clones assault phase will cause Ash to join the clones in a cinematic cutscene sequence.
- Each clone and Ash target different enemies, so adding Ash will speed up the assault.
- Ash will use the Shadow Clones weapon instead of his equipped melee weapon.
- While Blade Storm is an ability, using Teleport tchnically counts as attacking and will dispel conditional cloaking (e.g. Stalk).

- Equip Teleport Rush to extend the duration of Smoke Screen.

Augment

Main article: Teleport Rush
Teleport Rush is a Warframe Augment Mod for Ash's Teleport that increases Parkour Velocity for a time and extends Smoke Screen's duration upon executing a Finisher. The duration bonuses are refreshed upon performing a Teleport Mercy Finisher.

Rank | Parkour Velocity Bonus | Parkour Velocity Duration | Smoke Screen Duration Increase | Cost

1 | 20% | 12s | 5s | 7

3 | 30% | 12s | 5s | 9 

Tips & Tricks

- Use a Rapier with Mecha Set to spread Bleed procs in a wide area and multiply them.
- Use a Hammer for the highest Finisher Damage Multiplier.
- This ability can easily trigger Finisher and Mercy effects such as Innodem Evolution V, Arcane Trickery, Arcane Ultimatum, Arcane Escapist, Malicious Code, Power Drain, etc.
- Teleport allows for rapid movement through a tileset, especially with Teleport Rush.
- Held charge type weapons held when using this ability will stay charged until the finisher begins to execute. The target will be immune during the finisher animation, but AoE weapons can still hit nearby enemies.
- Untested, but this likely also works with weapons like the Opticor with the added requirement of timing the shot.

- The energy refund on kill can be boosted by Nourish. When subsumed via Helminth, 167% Strength is necessary to fully refund the ability cast; strength values exceeding this allow Ash to generate more energy than was spent casting this ability.
