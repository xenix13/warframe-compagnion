# Follie/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Follie/Abilities

## Passive

Follie's abilities splatter thick viscous Inkblot onto enemies, applying a 50% Slow for 10 seconds. While coated in ink, slain foes have a 20% chance to congeal an ink balloon that pops, dropping a random mixture of 3 Health Orbs and Energy Orbs.

- Affects Overguard protected enemies.
- Is not dismissed by Nullifier fields.
- Ability Synergy:
- Self Portrait and Plein Air apply Inkblot and pause its duration on affected enemies, until they leave the ink effigy's ink pool or detach from the ink balloon.
- Follie's signature Enkaus splatter an Inkblot glob on its alternate fire mode and can siphon back ammo from Inkblot inflicted by Follie's abilities.

| 2 25 | Shadowgraph Bring shadowgraphs from Follie's sketchbook to life as items that can be used for tactical advantage. Shadowgraphs splash Inkblot onto nearby enemies when created. HOLD to open the Shadowgraph gear wheel. Introduced in Update 42.0 (2026-03-25) | Strength:250 Corrosive damage on creation and expiry 

Duration:20 s object lifetime 

Range:4 m splash radius 

Misc: 50 m object creation range Stagger on creation and expiry 14 object types 16 Brush Stroke layers 3 object limit (Explosive Barrel, Arc Trap, Amphors, Launch Pad) 1 object limit (Corralizer Armament, Lohk Surge, Lost Spirit) Object creation cooldowns (see tabs below) Inkblot Passive 

Info

- Follie expends 25 Energy to sketch the selected Shadowgraph in the air above the reticle location within 50 meters, summoning an inky Shadowgraph object for 20 seconds. Shadowgraph object creation and expiry inflict 250 Corrosive damage and Stagger to enemies in a 4 meter radius.
- Hold down the ability button to open Follie's Sketchbook to select one of 14 Shadowgraphs via a Gear wheel menu, then release the ability button while on the desired Shadowgraph icon to cast the ability. The selected Object is saved for subsequent casts, with its enlarged Shadowgraph icon shown above Follie's ability icons.
- Each Shadowgraph performs the original function of its corresponding object with some alterations.
- All shadowgraphs can ride on vertically moving platforms.
- Shadowgraphs are visually distinct from their original, often the same size or smaller, while coated in Follie's ink tinted in her primary energy color and allies see them with a dithering translucency effect.
- Most objects must undergo a cooldown before Follie can summon them again, shown in the UI element above Follie's ability icons as the corresponding Shadowgraph icon, and in the center of the sketchbook gear wheel. The most recent Shadowgraph selections and their cooldown timers are tracked at the bottom right as an icon/timer list.
- Damaging Shadowgraph objects inflict percent health damage (Explosive Barrel, Arc Trap, Amphors, Death Orb, Corralizer), with the exception of the Thermian RPG. While their damage does not scale with Ability Strength, they are tagged as abilities, allowing them to benefit from effects that impact ability damage (such as Roar) as well as trigger effects that require ability damage being dealt (such as certain Archon Shard effects).
- Explosive barrels, arc traps, amphors, and corralizers all have their damage scaled up to counteract the damage reduction on armored units, resulting in a fixed amount of damage regardless of enemy armor. This fixed damage effect is different from simply ignoring armor, as removing an enemy's armor after the initial hit from one of these objects will cause their resulting DoT to increase in damage accordingly. For example, if an enemy with 10,000 health and 2700 armor (90% DR) is hit by a Gas explosive barrel (12.5% HP damage), it would initially take 1250 damage, then 625 damage per second from the resulting gas proc. If the enemy's armor is then removed, the DoT will increase to 6250 damage per second, which would not happen if armor was just being ignored the whole time.

- None of Shadowgraph objects can damage enemies immune to ability damage (e.g. Arbitration Shield Drone), even Thermian RPG which looks like a weapon.
- Shadowgraph objects exhibit unique properties highlighted in the tabs below:

Explosive Barrel

- Creates a random type of Explosive Barrel, either Blast, Cold, or Gas. When detonated, explosive barrels deal fixed Health point damage in a radius of 12 meters, and applies their respective Status Effect (10 stacks for Cold, or 1 for Blast and Gas).
- Blast and Cold barrels deal 65% enemy max health worth of damage, regardless of armor.
- Gas barrels deal 12.5% enemy max health worth of damage.
- Damage and Range is not affected by Ability Strength and Ability Range.

- Limited to 3 Explosive Barrels active.
- Has collision with players and other objects, subjected to object physics and gravity.
- Has no cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 0. Default selected object when Follie joins a mission.
- Ability Synergy:
- Forced Perspective, Self Portrait, and Plein Air's Corrosive damage detonate explosive barrels.

Arc Trap

- Creates an invulnerable Arc Trap that inflicts Electricity damage and status effect in a 10 meter radius, ending after 10 hits on the same target. Disappears when all hits are expended, or the Shadowgraph object timer ends, whichever is earlier.
- Deals 250 base damage plus 2.5% of enemy max health, regardless of armor.
- Damage and Range is not affected by Ability Strength and Ability Range.

- Limited to 3 Arc Traps active at once.
- Has no collision with players, subjected to object physics and gravity.
- Has no cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 0.
- Violet Archon Shard's effect to increase ability damage on enemies affected by Electricity Status by +10% (+15%) does affect objects created by Shadowgraph, including Arc Trap itself.
- Ability Synergy:
- Arc Traps scale their Electricity damage based on enemy Health and Armor, calculated only on the first tick of damage and used for all subsequent ticks. Cast Plein Air on armored enemies zapped at least once by Arc Traps to increase damage dealt by up to 10x (depending on the amount of armor stripped) on the remaining ticks.

Thermian RPG

- Creates a Thermian RPG, with an item Waypoint to alert all players. Disappears when all three rockets are fired, or the Shadowgraph object timer ends, whichever is earlier.
- Damage and Range is not affected by Ability Strength and Ability Range.
- Can benefit from Buffing Fields.

- Has a 30 second cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 1.
- Ability Synergy:
- Thermian RPG explosions pop Plein Air balloons.

- Ammo cannot be refilled by Universal Ammo or other ammo packs.

Amphors

- Creates 3 Amphors of random elemental affinity ( Heat, Cold, Toxin, or Electricity) to use as grenades or for Alchemy. Pick up the Amphor using the interact key, then hold down weapon alt-fire key to throw the Amphor toward the reticle in an arcing trajectory. On impact, the Amphor explodes in a 10 meter radius, splashing its respective elemental damage and applying 2 Status Effects to all enemies in range.
- Deals 90% of enemy max health, regardless of armor.
- AoE has 100% damage falloff, resulting in minimal damage at the edge of the AoE.
- Damage and Range are not affected by Ability Strength and Ability Range.
- Can benefit from Buffing Fields.

- Has a 30 second cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 1.
- Ability Synergy:
- Amphor explosions pop Plein Air balloons.
- Amphors scale their damage based on enemy Health and Armor, calculated only on the amphor explosion and used for all subsequent ticks of damaging Status Effects. Cast Plein Air on armored enemies hit by amphors to increase damage dealt by up to 10x (depending on the amount of armor stripped) on the remaining ticks of damaging Status Effects.

Death Orb

- Creates an active Orokin Death Orb, which fires 4 rotating horizontal lasers, and 1 vertical laser.
- Lasers deal 500 damage plus 15% of enemy health and shields (respects armor) as Radiation damage with 100% status chance.
- Prime Warframes will trigger its one-time energy pulse to refill 250 Energy.
- Damage and Range is not affected by Ability Strength and Ability Range.

- The orb lasers have a range of 1km.
- Has no collision with players and other objects.
- Can only be summoned on a horizontal surface.
- Has a 30 second cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 1.
- Topaz Archon Shard's effect to increase ability damage on enemies affected by Radiation status by +10% (+15%) does affect objects created by Shadowgraph, including Death Orb itself.

Corralizer Armament

- Creates a Corralizer Armament that fires Cold beams up to 6 enemies within 15 meters.
- Beams deal 1% enemy max health, regardless of armor, as Cold damage and have 60% status chance.
- Damage and Range is not affected by Ability Strength and Ability Range.

- Limited to 1 Corralizer Armanent active at at a time.
- Has no collision with players and other objects.
- Can only be summoned on a horizontal surface.
- Has no cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 1.
- Can trigger Arcane Ice Storm.

Lohk Surge

- Creates a single use Lohk Surge pedestal with one of the Lohk Surge buffs. Press the interact key on the pedestal to gain the surge buff. Beware, a random enemy Eximus is also summoned when the buff is activated.
- Infinite Ammo and Fire Rate Lohk Surge buff is not available.

- Has no collision with players and other objects.
- Can only be summoned on a horizontal surface.
- Has a 7 minute cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 2.
- Will not count towards Zariman bounties that require Lohk Surges to be activated as a side objective.

Universal Ammo

- Creates a Universal Ammo dispenser that dispenses Ammo every 5 seconds.
- Has no collision with players and other objects.
- Can only be summoned on a horizontal surface.
- Has a 30 second cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 2.

Launch Pad

- Creates a launch pad that provides additional upward mobility. As a player, stand on the pad and press jump to gain upward momentum and a double jump boost. Against enemies, when walking into the pad or the pad is created beneath them, they are bounced into the air in a diagonal upward direction and inflicted with Ragdoll.
- Limited to 3 Launch Pads active at once.
- Has no collision with players and other objects.
- Can only be summoned on a horizontal surface.
- Very useful in Ascension.
- Has no cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 2.

Lost Spirit

- Creates a Lost Spirit from the Zariman. The Lost Spirit will track and sprint to the nearest hidden treasure item, leading the Tenno to it.
- The Lost Spirit will show on the map as a blue dot (allied entity) until it finds the treasure. The radar range to see the dot is unlimited, but it will not mark the treasure on your map. Once the Lost Spirit finds a treasure, it will mark the treasure for any players within 50 meters of it.
- The Lost Spirit is capable of finding Ayatan Treasures, Syndicate Medallions (including Voidplumes, Voca, and Hex Treasures), Rare/Reinforced Storage Containers ( Grineer (both normal and Forgotten), Corpus, Orokin only), Sentient Cores, Orokin Defixios, Zarium Accolades, Techrot Safe biocodes, Security Bypasses, unscanned Kurias, unscanned Cephalon Fragments, unscanned Frame Fighter Fragments, and unscanned Somachord Fragments.
- The Lost Spirit can be sped up with Speed.

- Detection Range is a 300 meter radius and is not affected by Ability Range.
- The center of the Detection Range is at player's location instead of the Shadowgraph's location.
- Detection is based on three-dimensional straight-line distance between player and a treasure, ignoring any walls and obstacles. Player may need to traverse much more than 300m distance or to run across the whole map to reach the detected treasure. As a result, player may need backtracking to detect the other items that were once nearer on foot.
- If an item was previously detected by Lost Spirit or Orokin Eye, it will still be detected even when it is out of Detection Range, provided that no other items are nearer to player. However, Golden Instinct has no such synergy.

- Limited to 1 Lost Spirit active.
- Has a 30 second cooldown.
- If no treasure is within the detection range, the Lost Spirit will immediately disappear and reduce the cooldown to 5 seconds.
- In rare cases, if the detected treasure is located at a location unreachable by the Lost Spirit, the Lost Spirit will linger in the same place for few seconds before disappearing. Difference from the 'no treasure' case can be noticed by normal 30 second cooldown. The detected item is still marked and only visible to any players within 50 meters of it as usual.

- Golden Instinct with high Ability Range may be helpful by providing direction to traverse when Lost Spirit cannot detect the remaining treasures or the mark is not yet visible, as Golden Instinct's detection range can be over 500 meters.
- Unlocked at ability rank 2.

Life Support Capsule

- Creates a Life Support Capsule. Press the interact key to deplete the capsule to refill 30% life support in Survival missions and produce a single Health Orb.
- Cannot be converted into a Kuva Harvester in Kuva Survival missions.

- Has a 3 minute cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 3.

Power Cell

- Creates a standard Excavator Power Cell.
- It is functionally identical to the power cells found in Excavation missions.

- Has a 60 second cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 3.

Defense Capsule

- Creates a Defense cryopod capsule.
- The cryopod has high Threat Level to distract enemies and absorb their attacks.

- Has no collision with players and other objects.
- Can only be summoned on a horizontal surface.
- Has a 60 second cooldown.
- Unlocked at ability rank 3.

Conduit Key

- Creates a semi-random Disruption conduit key.
- Multiple Follies in the same squad will create keys in the same semi-random order.
- Has a 3 minute cooldown
- Unlocked at ability rank 3.

Art Tool
In the Arsenal screen with Follie equipped, the player can select the Shadowgraph slot to open Follie's Sketchbook. Select an object to open the Shadowgraph customization screen.

- Akin to Photoshop, the Shadowgraph customization screen lets the player draw their own unique Shadowgraph object icons using Brush Strokes shapes and color them using Color Palettes, with a max layering of 16 Brush Strokes. The custom icons will appear in the Shadowgraph gear wheel, above Follie's ability icons, and appear briefly when cast to summon a Shadowgraph in mission.
- Follie's Sketchbook user manual:

- Each of Follie’s Shadowgraphs can be customized as many times as your heart desires from the Arsenal! Your creations are projected upon casting Shadowgraph.
- How to Access Sketchbook: Equip Follie, select Shadowgraph in the Arsenal menu and select which Shadowgraph item art you’d like to customize. Reminder that you must rank up Follie to unlock her Shadowgraphs.
- Her sketchbook will open up the canvas where you can customize the selected Shadowgraph from simply changing the color to completely reworking the piece from scratch.

1. Prepare your canvas:

- If you wish to start from scratch, select the “Clear Canvas” button – This will remove all existing layers from the canvas.
- If you wish to use the existing layers, continue on to the next steps!
- Select your preferred canvas state using the two options below the Brush Strokes window: Canvas Light Mode or Canvas Dark Mode

2. Select Brush Strokes

- The sketchbook comes with a set of Brush Strokes for you to create your masterpiece.

- Select and drag Brush Strokes of choice onto the canvas, doing so will create a new Layer.
- You can create a maximum of 16 layers per Shadowgraph in the sketchbook.

3. Adjust Layers

- In order to move and/or change a layer, it must first be selected from either the canvas or the Layers list.
- To move a layer around on the canvas, select it from the Layers list and then grab and drag it around to the desired spot.
- Make adjustments to a selected layer using the following tools:
- Transformation Box: Change the scale, width, height and rotation of a layer using the blue transform box around it.
- Reset Scale: Scale can be reset to default.
- Mirror Horizontally/Vertically: Layers can be mirrored horizontally and vertically.
- Change layer order: Select and drag a layer from the layers list to change the order of how they appear on the canvas.

- If you’d like a layer to appear behind another, drag it below said layer. And for it to appear in front of a layer, drag it on top.
- Change color of layer: Select the color box of a layer you wish to change from the Layers list. Choose a new color for your layer from your owned color palettes.

- Use the dropper icon to copy a layer’s color onto the currently selected layer.

- Copy Selected Layer: Create a duplicate of the selected layer in the Layers list.
- Delete layer: Delete a selected layer.
- Undo/Redo: Take a step back or forward on a selected layer.
- If you wish to restart the following options are always available to you:
- Clear Canvas: Clear all layers from canvas, blank slate!
- Note: There is no way to undo clearing a canvas, so be absolutely sure you want to clear it before committing!

- Reset Canvas: Reset all layers to their default state.

4. Preview

- At any time you can preview your creation using the “Preview” button at the bottom of the screen. From here you also have the option to change the background color for some great screenshots!
5. Save and Exit
- Once you are happy with your creation, select the “Save and Exit” button to save changes before exiting back to the Arsenal.
- Attempting to save when there are no layers in the canvas will reset to the default.

- Shadowgraphs must abide by the Community Guidelines: Any art created in Follie’s sketchbook must abide by the Community Guidelines. Be respectful to other Tenno with your creations. If you see sketchbook art from other players that violate the Community Guidelines, report the player by selecting their name in chat > “Report Player” > "Offensive Image” > write why you are reporting them in the text box. The sketchbook image will be reviewed by the team.
- Note: If the Sketchbook is being widely misused with sketches that violate the Community Guidelines, we can adjust and/or remove access to it however we feel is appropriate. So please abide by the Community Guidelines so that everyone can continue enjoying this incredibly fun tool!

- Shadowgraph Settings: Shadowgraph visibility can be changed using the following:
- “Creator Mode” toggle in the Social options. Toggling this mode ON will disable custom Shadowgraphs (yours and others players) and revert them to their defaults.
- Adjusting the “Reduce Teammate Visual Effects” slider in the Video options

Kit Interactions

- Shadowgraph object creation and expiry splash Follie's Passive Inkblot onto nearby enemies.

Info

- Follie expends 50 Energy to congeal an inky effigy of herself, centered around the location on the aiming reticle up to 50 meters away. The ink effigy lasts 20 / 24 / 26 / 30 seconds, sustaining an ink pool with a starting radius of 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 meters; each enemy killed inside the ink pool increases its duration and range, up to a maximum of 40 / 48 / 52 / 60 seconds and 15 / 17.5 / 17.5 / 20 meters. On cast and on ability expiration, the effigy deals 250 / 350 / 450 / 550 Corrosive damage to enemies within its starting radius and applies Inkblot to them, then continuously applies Inkblot to all enemies that enter the ink pool for the entire duration. While Follie and allies are in the Self Portrait's ink pool, they are granted 10% Damage Reduction, with each enemy killed inside the ink pool increasing the Damage Reduction up to 35% / 40% / 45% / 50%.
- Damage Reduction applies to Warframes, Companions, summoned allied units such as Specters, allied Invasion units, Hostages, Kavor Defectors, Sortie and Arbitration Defense Operatives etc.
- Killing an enemy inside the ink pool does the following:
- Increases its duration by 3 seconds.
- Increases the Damage Reduction by Max Damage Reduction − Min Damage Reduction15.
- Increases its radius by 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.7 / 0.8 meters.

- Maximum Damage Reduction is capped at 90%, achievable with 180% Ability Strength.
- Damaging effect requires Line of Sight to the target, while inking does not.
- On cast damage instance has 100% damage falloff over its radius, while the ability expiration damage instance has 0% falloff.
- Follie can recast Self Portrait at any time to move it a new location, while maintaining its current Damage Reduction, range, and duration.
- If the remaining duration is lower than the starting duration, recasting Self Portrait will increase its duration back to the starting duration.

Helminth

- Subsuming Follie to the Helminth will offer Self Portrait and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- However, Subsumed Self Portrait's maximum Damage Reduction caps at 75% and does not apply Inkblot.

- Balloons will lift their targets up until a certain height or until the balloon touches a ceiling.

- If a player jumps on a balloon, the balloon will pop and the player will bounce upwards and have their double jump/bullet jump refreshed.
- Enemies that are immune to crowd control, such as bosses or enemies with Overguard, will not ascend but still receive the reduction to their defenses on cast. Additionally, they are tied to an indestructible balloon, which pops shortly thereafter. However, the balloon's only effect is dealing 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Corrosive damage when it pops. This damage is only dealt to the target and has no Area of Effect.
