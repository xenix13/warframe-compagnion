Info

- Follie expends 15 Energy to dive into an inky portrait frame below, cleansing Status Effects and becoming Invulnerable for 3.5 seconds, while inflicting 300 / 450 / 600 / 750 Corrosive damage only and Stagger to enemies around her in a 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 meter radius. After diving, another inky portrait frame opens on the targeted surface on the reticle within a range of 100 meters. When Follie emerges, she inflicts a second instance of Corrosive damage and Stagger.
- Follie can cast this ability on vertical and horizontal surfaces, altering the portrait frame's alignment and her emergence animation.
- Arctic eximus and Nullifier Crewman globes are valid teleportation surfaces, you can also teleport out of eximus globes.

- Hold down the ability button to center a teleport visual indicator on the reticle, then release the button to cast this ability.
- Ability will not cast if there is insufficient space for Follie at the destination, showing "Target Obstructed" below the ability icons.

- Invulnerability timer is tracked on the ability icon.

Kit Interactions

- Forced Perspective splashes Follie's Passive Inkblot on enemies, once on the dive and emergence.
- Shadowgraph objects interact with Follie's teleportation:
- Explosive Barrel detonates from Corrosive damage.
- Launch Pad bounces Follie upward when she teleports directly atop the pad.
- Death Orb, Corralizer Armament, Universal Ammo Dispenser, Life Support Capsule, and Defense Capsule are eligible surfaces for teleportation. Only Life Support Capsule has collision with players for standing atop.

- Plein Air balloons are not eligible surfaces for teleportation and do not pop from Forced Perspective's Corrosive damage.
