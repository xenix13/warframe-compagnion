# Titania/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Titania/Abilities

## Passive

Frolicking and fluttering about, Titania passively possesses +25% Bullet Jump and Rolling distance. Casting her abilities rejuvenates Titania and her allies within 15 meters, granting them the Upsurge buff which regenerates 4 Health points per second for 20 seconds.

- Upsurge is granted at the start of Titania's cast animations.
- The duration of the heal is refreshed on successive ability casts.

- Upsurge buff icon is displayed with a duration timer next to the shield and health indicators, at the top-right of the HUD.
- An icon also appears next to each allied player affected by Upsurge.

- Affected allies includes all Warframes, Companions, Eidolon Lures, summoned allied units such as Specters, allied Invasion units, Hostages, Kavor Defectors, Sortie and Arbitrations Defense Operatives, and Defense Objects.

Range:N/A 

Misc: 50% (evasion bonus) 10 m (vacuum radius) 6 (razorfly drones) ∞ (drones duration) 100% (drone damage vulnerability) 4 (max extra razorflies from Tribute) 

Info

- Titania expends 25 energy to shrink down to a quarter of her original size, becoming a flying pixie. In this form, through the use of her built-in Archwing, Titania permanently remains in mid-air, gaining vertical and horizontal flight capabilities using the movement (W A S D ), crouch (Ctrl ), jump (Spacebar ), and Archwing Blink (Shift ) hotkeys, similar to Archwing Maneuvers but without terrain collision damage. Additionally, Titania passively gains 50% evasion and a 10 meter Vacuum-like pickup radius. In Razorwing, Titania flies at 10 m/s and sprints at 20 m/s.
- Sprinting flight speed is affected by Sprint Speed (e.g., Rush).
- Terrain collision animation is considered a Knockdown effect, and can be prevented as such.
- Railjack Intrinsic bonuses do not affect Razorwing, with the exception of the Rank 8 Tactical Intrinsic "Archwing Tactical Blink", which reduces Razorwing's Archwing Blink cooldown by 25%.
- Razorwing mode counts as being airborne in regards to the effects of Aerodynamic, Agility Drift and Aviator/ Ironclad Flight.
- Despite being functionally an Archwing, Razorwing cannot be modded with Archwing mods.
- Cast animation of 1.4 seconds and deactivation of 1.0 second are affected by Casting Speed.
- If cast while airborne, the activation time is greatly reduced.

- Razorwing consumes 5 per second while active and will remain active until Titania's energy is depleted, or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Titania cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, and/or Wellspring while Razorwing is active.
- Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Rage, Hunter Adrenaline, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes and Brightbonnet can still replenish Titania's energy while in Razorwing.
- Razorwing's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- Instead of all her normal weapons, Titania wields two proprietary Razorwing Exalted Weapons: the Dex Pixia dual machine pistols and the Diwata heavy sword.
- Both weapons can damage enemies across the Rift Plane, although Diwata's auto-targeting doesn't work across planes.
- Unaffected by Amalgam Serration or Amalgam Furax Body Count while in Razorwing form.
- However, Incarnon melee weapons apply a buff that is carried over into Razorwing.

- Swapping between Dex Pixia and Diwata does not trigger On Equip effects like Vigorous Swap.

- Dex Pixia is an Exalted Weapon with its own unique statistics.
- Base damage is 50 / 80 / 120 / 160 per hit.
- Because Ability Strength modifies the base damage properties of the weapon, damage bonuses like Hornet Strike and are fully multiplicative with the effects of Ability Strength mods.

- Base damage distribution is 10% Impact, 10% Puncture, and 80% Slash.
- Has a Fire Rate of 5.83 shots per second with a 60 round magazine. When not firing, the clip will automatically regenerate 50 shots per second after a 0.3 second delay.
- Dex Pixia has 2.0x critical multiplier, 10% critical chance, and 25% status chance.
- Dex Pixia can be equipped with secondary mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Dex Pixia.

- Because Dex Pixia is counted as a secondary, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Pistol Amp and Arcane Precision.
- Dex Pixia, despite being a secondary Exalted Weapon, functions much like the Dual Decurion Archgun.

- Diwata is an Exalted Weapon with its own unique statistics.
- Exclusively uses the Razorwing Stance.
- Base damage is 150 / 165 / 180 / 200 with a 2.5 meter range.
- Because Ability Strength modifies the base damage properties of the weapon, damage bonuses like Pressure Point and are fully multiplicative with the effects of Ability Strength mods.

- Base damage distribution is 15% Impact, 75% Puncture, and 10% Slash.
- Attack Speed of 1.08 times per second.
- When attacking with the Diwata, Titania will dash a short distance to her target.
- Diwata has 2.0x critical multiplier, 20% critical chance, and 10% status chance.
- Diwata can be equipped with melee mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Diwata.

- Because Diwata is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.

- While Razorwing is active, Titania will also command 6 Razorfly drones to attack nearby enemies. Razorflies last indefinitely and are Invulnerable with increased Threat Level. They deal 10 / 24 / 48 / 80 damage each, and inflict 100% Damage Vulnerability permanently.
- Damage distribution is 10% Impact, 10% Puncture, and 80% Slash.
- Attack Speed of 2.0 times per second.
- 25% Critical Chance, 2.0x Critical Multiplier, and 10% Status Chance.
- Razorflies will attack the first enemy in their Line of Sight within 250m of them. Once aggroed onto an enemy, they will not change targets or return to Titania until the enemy is dead.
- Razorflies have a variable flight speed when attempting to return to Titania or engage an enemy. This flight speed depends on the distance to their target (Titania or an enemy) and is determined as follows:
- The distance (in meters) from the target is clamped within a range of 5 - 20.
- Using this value, a proportionate value is derived within the scalar range of 0.25 - 2.00.
- The distance (not clamped) is then divided by the scalar to get the Razorfly's speed.
- For example, if the target is 10m away, this is 1/3 of the way between the range of 5 - 20, which will give a scalar 1/3 of the way between 0.25 - 2, so 0.833. The distance of 10m is then divided by the scalar of 0.833 to get a speed of 12 m/s.

- The speed value can therefore be expressed as S = D / ((7/60)d - (1/3)), where S is speed, D is distance from the target, and d is the distance clamped between 5 and 20.
- Between 0m - 5m, their speed equals the distance to the target multiplied by 4.
- Between 5m - 20m, their speed scales between 20m/s and 10m/s.
- Greater than 20m, their speed equals the distance to the target divided by 2.

- Flight speed is not affected by Razorwing Blitz or Arcane Camisado.

- Razorflies are counted as Summons and are affected by Summoner's Wrath, Arcane Camisado, and Theorem Infection.
- Razorflies do not gain the speed bonus from Arcane Camisado but they do stack the strength bonus.

- Razorflies have a chance to land headshots, triggering the effects of Arcane Consequence, Arcane Precision, and Arcane Rage.
- Razorflies cannot damage enemies across the Rift Plane.

- Ability Synergy:
- Spellbind's status immunity negates the stagger from "crashing" into surfaces.
- Razorwing spawns an extra Razorfly for each Tribute collected, up to 4 extra Razorflies up to a maximum of 10.
- While Titania remains in Razorwing, these extra Razorflies are permanent and do not expire when the corresponding buff does.

- Razorflies will fly to and pick up Tribute offerings for Titania.

- While Razorwing is active, Titania can move around freely while casting her other abilities. She can also use most items from her gear, and equipped gear items' sizes will also be shrunk to her scale. Deployed gear items will still retain their original sizes.
- Kills done by Titania with Razorwing active count towards the "From on High" (Kill 100 enemies in Archwing form on the Plains of Eidolon) challenge.
- On the other hand, Razorwing is not considered an Archwing for Riven Mod challenges.

- Titania is unable to accomplish any action that requires the Use key (default X ) while Razorwing is active, thus cannot interact with consoles, pick up carriables such as datamasses, or revive allies from bleedout.
- Titania can pick up carriables before activating Razorwing, but is not able to use them until she deactivates the ability.
- The Operator can still be used while in Razorwing, and be able to do these actions without deactivating the ability. However, energy drain and countdowns on buffs like Razorwing Blitz will still continue while in Operator mode.
- Despite most interactions being normally blocked while Razorwing is active, certain actions, such as interacting with and hacking consoles, opening Friendship Door, reviving allies from bleedout, capturing Capture targets, collecting Ayatan Treasures, calling and starting elevators, executing Secret Mercy and Requiem Mercy, calling remote pickup for tranquilized creatures, depositing Antiserum Charges into Vaporizers on Infested Salvage and other Use key interactions can still be performed if Titania is holding a Tranq Rifle while Razorwing is active.

- Subsumed abilities injected into Titania via the Helminth system can be cast while in Razorwing mode.
- Companions disappear when activating Razorwing and will come back once the ability is deactivated.
- Animal Instinct/ Primed Animal Instinct will still apply, similar to Archwing,
- Duplex Bond will continue to create clones.
- Reinforced Bond and Tenacious Bond's buffs will continue, if the conditions are met on the companion.
- Retriever Mods can still double resources/credits.
- Companions will not regenerate shields while Titania is in Razorwing.
- If Titania enters Razorwing while a companion is downed, the companion recovery timer will continue to count up, and effects that shorten the companion recovery timer such as Aerial Bond will work as normal. The recovery timer will pause at 100%, and the companion will not revive until Titania exits Razorwing.

The Dex Pixia are Titania's and Titania Prime's signature dual pistols, summoned by activating the ability Razorwing.

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Innate two and one polarities.
- Does not use ammo pickups; ammo regenerates over time.
- Has a 0.3-second delay after the weapon stops firing before regenerating ammo.
- Regenerates 50 ammo per second; takes 1.2 seconds to regenerate a fully depleted magazine.

- Very high reload speed (0.30 s)
- Above average fire rate (5.83 attacks/sec)
- Above average status chance (25.00%)
- High damage per shot (176)
- Above average crit multiplier (2.00x)

- Requires energy to activate and keep active.
- Normal Attack (wiki attack index 1)

Tips & Tricks

- Razorwing greatly improves Titania's survivability:
- Titania presents a smaller target profile with Razorwing active, making it more difficult for enemies to hit her.
- Razorwing's innate evasion allows Titania to avoid shots.
- Razorflies have increased threat rating, drawing aggro away from Titania.
- The fact that Titania is airborne with Razorwing also allows her to fully avoid taking damage from certain enemies, such as melee enemies and Nox's toxin cloud.
- Equipping Agility Drift and Aviator can provide even higher survivability, especially against high level enemies.

- Dex Pixia has a high rate of fire which allows Titania to deal a lot of damage in a short amount of time. They are also fully capable of triggering Arcane Pistoleer, frequently allowing for up to 12 second bursts of unlimited fire.
- Casting the ability while in the air will shave off about 0.3 seconds of the animation, the part where she lifts off of the ground, since she is already in the air.
- When trying to decide between equipping Growing Power or Pistol Amp, Growing Power increases Dex Pixia's base damage before secondary weapon mods are considered, and Pistol Amp increases Dex Pixia's base damage as if it was a secondary weapon mod (e.g., Hornet Strike). This means that Growing Power's 25% ability strength increase is the better choice accompanying ability strength bonuses less than 300% and stronger secondary weapon damage bonuses, while Pistol Amp's 27% damage increase is the better choice with ability strength bonuses greater than 300% and weaker secondary weapon damage bonuses.
- When trying to decide between equipping Growing Power or Steel Charge, Growing Power increases Diwata's base damage before melee weapon mods, and Steel Charge increases Diwata's base damage as if it was a melee weapon mod. Despite this advantage for Growing Power, its 25% ability strength increase is inferior to Steel Charge's 60% damage increase in most situations. It will, however, depend if you are using a subsume or not, as Roar, for instance, would make the ability strength the superior option again.
- Razorflies can consistently trigger the effect of Growing Power thanks to how frequently 10 Razorwings (the max number of summonable Razorwings) can proc status effects.
- Titania will drop a datamass or lantern when razorwing is invoked.
