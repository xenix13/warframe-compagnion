# Oraxia/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Oraxia/Abilities

## Passive

An apex predator patiently prowls, hidden in plain sight. Perform Wall Latch (default hold RMB near a wall) to activate Predator’s Lurk, rendering Oraxia and her Companion Invisible for 8 seconds.

- Passive timer is shown as a Buff icon beside Oraxia's hitpoints indicator.
- Predator's Lurk can have its duration refreshed when Oraxia releases then reuses wall latch again.
- Invisibility does not break when Oraxia stops wall latching or attacks with her weapons and abilities.
- When used on a vertical surface or ceiling, Silken Stride's Silken Thread web-sling allows Oraxia to automatically wall latch for an infinite duration and trigger Predator's Lurk for its normal invisibility duration.

Info

- Oraxia expends 25 Energy to become Invulnerable while pouncing at 150 m/s at an enemy target within 40 meters and a ? degrees cone on the reticle. On cast, the victim is exposed to a melee Finisher prompt for 3 seconds. When Oraxia strikes her victim, it receives 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 Toxin damage and Stagger, or instantly killed if its health was at or below 50% or Mercy kill threshold when Mercy's Kiss was cast. An enemy that dies during Mercy's Kiss has a 200% chance to drop a Health Orb and a 50% chance to drop an Energy Orb.
- Enemies in the instant kill threshold are marked by an overhead spider claw icon indefinitely, tracking their movement even behind walls.
- The nearest claw-marked enemy in the reticle cone is visually highlighted and glows in Oraxia's energy color.

- Orb drop chances above 100% will guarantee an orb with a chance to drop another orb.
- Once Mercy's Kiss is cast, orb drop chances will roll even if the victim dies while Oraxia is mid-flight.
- When cast on a Mercy prompted enemy, Mercy's Kiss can trigger Oraxia's equipped Parazon mods with on-kill conditions.
- Can trigger finisher-related mods and arcanes, such as Arcane Trickery, but only when the victim is under the Mercy Kill threshold.
- It cannot however, activate Arcane Ultimatum unless the enemy is by default vulnerable to Parazon finishers. (ie normal units will never activate the arcane despite counting as a finisher).

Kit Interactions

- Webbed Embrace's Damage Vulnerability increases Mercy's Kiss's damage.
- Widow's Brood's Scuttlers inflict guaranteed Impact status effects that raise the Parazon Mercy threshold on specialist enemies, weakening them for Mercy's Kiss' instant kill attack.
- During Silken Stride, using melee (default E ) will cast Mercy's Kiss and can trigger the Toxin damage explosion on kill.
- If Mercy's Kiss is subsumed over with another ability, casting Mercy's Kiss this way is impossible, and melee weapons are still inaccessible in this form.

Tips & Tricks

- The increased Orb drop rate allows Oraxia to effectively make use of Health Conversion, Energy Conversion, Equilibrium, Arcane Energize, Arcane Eruption, Arcane Pulse, Arcane Blessing, and Violet Archon Shard (health/energy pickup conversion).
- Impact status effect increases the minimum health needed to do a finisher/trigger Mercy's Kiss, thus weapons with high impact status chance or guarantee impact procs are valid picks for oraxia's loadout, such as Acceltra and Epitaph (both give force impact proc's on direct hits)
- Enemies marked by Mercy's Kiss' instant kill threshold can be tracked behind walls, as the overhead icon becomes permanently visible when Oraxia is in range.

Info

- Oraxia expends 50 Energy to hurl an arcing cocoon sac that splatters on impact with any surface, then spreads an expanding web over 5 / 7 / 8 / 10 meters, lasting for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds. Upon entering the web, enemies take 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Toxin damage with 100% Status Chance. While expanding for ? seconds, the web instantly traps enemies in cocoons; once the web is fully expanded, enemies walking into its radius with Line of Sight to the web's center, Slow down for ? seconds then become cocooned. Cocooned enemies cannot move and become debuffed with 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% Damage Vulnerability. Up to a maximum of 3 webs can be created.
- Number of active webs are shown on the ability icon.
- The web visually expands over the horizontal plane, encircled by a radial Void energy aura.
- Webbed Embrace's ability effects occur inside an invisible sphere centered on the web, allowing Oraxia to create her webs on walls and still affect enemies below.

- Nullifiers will not destroy Webbed Embrace, but instead render enemies immune to its effects while inside the Nullifier Bubble.

Kit Interactions

- Damage Vulnerability enhances the damage of Mercy's Kiss, Widow's Brood darts and Scuttler bites, and Silken Stride's bonus Toxin damage on ranged weapons and damage explosion on kills.

Helminth

- Subsuming Oraxia to the Helminth will offer Webbed Embrace and its augments to be used by other Warframes, however, subsumed Webbed Embrace's range is reduced to 6.67 meters.

Tips & Tricks

- Throw the cocoon sac directly on groups of enemies to immediately immobilize them.
- Cast on an open area to increase the line of sight of the web, to catch more enemies walking into its path.
- Create up to three webs in high traffic chokepoints to crowd control multiple fronts.
- Cocooned enemies receive more damage from all sources, and make for easy targets for enemy weakpoint shots, Mercy's Kiss and Widow's Brood Scuttlers.

| 3 50 | Widow's Brood Oraxia launches a barrage of stinging darts in front of her that applies Toxin Status Effect. Poisoned enemies spawn Scuttlers when killed. Introduced in Update 39.0 (2025-06-25) | Strength:300 / 450 / 600 / 750 Toxin damage per dart

Range:16 / 19 / 22 / 25 m 

Misc: 10 dart projectiles 35° cone angle 4 forced Toxin status effects Stagger on hit 50 Toxin damage (Scuttler damage) Toxin and Impact statuses on Scuttler hits 10 max Scuttlers +6s Scuttler status duration bonus 60 m recall radius 

Info

- Oraxia expends 50 Energy to thrust her spider legs forward, hurling 1 venom-laced egg dart toward each enemy within 16 / 19 / 22 / 25 meters in a 35 degree frontal angle. Each dart inflicts 300 / 450 / 600 / 750 Toxin damage with 4 forced Toxin status effects, Staggers the target, and marks them with gaseous poison and dripping venom for 10 / 12 / 14 / 15 seconds.
- Each dart homes into a single enemy in the targeting cone and in direct Line of Sight. Darts can strike multiple enemies if a non-targeted enemy steps into a dart's flight path.
- If less than 10 enemies are in the targeting cone, 10 darts will be fired: 1 at each enemy, then the remaining darts will fly towards the aiming reticle.
- If more than 10 enemies are in the targeting cone, the number of darts fired will increase so that each enemy will have 1 dart fired at them. The number of darts is only limited by the number of enemies in range.

- Darts are affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
- Darts have 0% critical chance, 1.0x critical multiplier, and 0% status chance.

- When enemies marked by Widow's Brood are killed by any source other than the direct damage of the darts, a Scuttler hatches from their body. Scuttlers are spiderlings that scale to the killed enemy's level, with increased Threat Level. They crawl quickly toward nearby enemies to pounce at them, then rapidly bite and claw them. Scuttlers deal 50 Toxin damage per hit (scaling with their level), with 100% Status Chance and a forced Impact status effect. Each Scuttler lasts for 30 / 35 / 40 / 45 seconds (slowly dying from health drain), with a maximum of 10 Scuttlers simultaneously spawned.
- Number of active Scuttlers are shown on the ability icon.
- Scuttler attack damage is scaled by their level but will have a minimum level of 30. This results in a minimum damage of 188.6 Toxin.
- Scuttler damage is given by this formula:

Damage=50×[1+0.015×(Current Level−1)1.55]
- - - Scuttler Toxin procs are capable of interacting with Archon Continuity.
- Scuttler Toxin status damage is increased twice by Roar, since it increases both the initial attack that the proc is based on, and then again on each proc. However, Roar is incompatible with Silken Stride.
- Capable of inflicting other status effects when buffs like Nourish or Shock Trooper are applied, and does not combine with added elements (i.e., attacks with Shock Trooper will apply both Toxin and Electricity, not Corrosive).
- Not affected by Emerald Archon Shard's buff to Ability Damage on enemies afflicted by Corrosive, or to Toxin status damage.

- Status effects inflicted by Scuttlers have their duration increased by 6 seconds.
- Scuttlers count as summons, and are thus affected by Summoner's Wrath, Arcane Camisado, and Theorem Infection.
- When Oraxia is over 60 meters away, whether she is on the ground or wall-latching, Scuttlers will teleport close to her location.
- Scuttlers are tracked on the mini-map as allied blue dots, highlighted through walls for Oraxia only, and are visually translucent to allied players.
- Scuttlers will not be rendered invisible by Predator's Lurk, meaning that when enemies fire their weapons towards them, they might indirectly hit an invisible Oraxia.
- This is especially dangerous with Arson Eximus units, whose large AOEs will still hit Oraxia if the player is in range. Shock Eximus orbs can also track invisible players and deal high DPS to them.

Kit Interactions

- Webbed Embrace's Damage Vulnerability increases Widow's Brood's and Scuttlers' damage.
- Scuttlers' forced Impact status raises the Parazon Mercy threshold on specialist enemies, weakening them for Mercy's Kiss' instant kill attack.

Killing enemies causes them to burst with Toxin. Silken Thread: Tap Dodge to leap to a targeted surface. Duration:N/A

Info

- Oraxia expends 25 Energy to unfurl her spider legs and go on the prowl. In this form, Oraxia becomes immune to Status Effects and hard landings, increases her maximum Health by 1.25x / 1.5x / 1.75x / 2x, and gains +30% Movement Speed, +25% Parkour Velocity, and +100% Jump Height.
- The health bonus is coded as a multiplier on max health, following the formula:
- Silken Health=(Base Health at Rank×(1 + Health Mods) + Health Bonuses)×(1+Ability Strength)
- For example, (675 × (1 + 100%) + 1200) × (1 + (100% + 30%)) = 5,865

- Ability Strength is snapshotted on ability cast
- Silken Stride's effects are multiplied against max health from all sources, including abilities, shards, and arcanes.
- Oraxia is healed to her new maximum health points when Silken Stride is cast.

- She is able to perform most parkour Maneuvers, except rolling, side strafe, and backflipping. Wall latching happens automatically when Oraxia jumps against a vertical surface.
- The player camera is positioned further back, to expand one's field of view.
- Oraxia's spider legs visually disappear when aiming down sights with ranged weapons.
- Oraxia's character model is the center of her foothold on horizontal surfaces, even though her spider legs appear to stretch out.
- In spider form, Oraxia's upper body continues to play her chosen Idle Animations set, while her legs dangle in mid-air.

- While active, Oraxia laces her primary and secondary weapons' magazine ammo with potent venom, causing each weapon hit to inflict an Extra Hit of 10% / 20% / 30% / 40% Toxin damage (20% / 40% / 60% / 80% while Wall Latching) with 100% status chance; however, Oraxia is unable to equip her melee weapon. Enemies killed by Oraxia's guns and abilities (and assisted kills by allies) explode, dealing 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Toxin damage with 100% Status Chance and Stagger to enemies within a 5 meter radius.
- Damage bonus is calculated on ability cast, and doesn't change with Ability Strength changes, like Arcane Bellicose.
- Toxin ranged weapon damage bonus is shown as a buff icon beside Oraxia's hitpoints indicator, and beside the primary or secondary firearm magazine counter, similar to Cyte-09's Resupply buff.
- The buff will override the elemental damage bonus of Cyte-09's Resupply, and the Heat damage bonus of the Reconifex and Uriel's Demonium.

- Explosion radius is capped at 10 meters, achievable with 200% Ability Range.

- Silken Stride consumes 5 energy per second while active, and will remain active until Oraxia's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated manually.
- Silken Stride's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.
- Oraxia cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, Archon Stretch, or Wellspring while Silken Stride is active.
- Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Rage, Hunter Adrenaline, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, and Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes can still replenish Oraxia's energy while Silken Stride is active.
- Silken Stride counts as a "Channeled Ability" for certain Incarnon evolutions.
- Though be aware that the toxin damage bonus will not apply while a weapon is in Incarnon Form, since Incarnon Forms do not have a magazine to buff.

- Oraxia's melee weapon will still be equipped, just not accessible. This means that passive effects (like Amalgam Furax Body Count) will function as normal, as will effects that involve checking and updating the melee weapon's state (like Secondary Outburst and Ceramic Dagger's Gun and Blade perk).

- Rolling (default Shift ) causes Oraxia to shoot from her hand a Silken Thread, up to 75 meters long toward the surface the reticle is pointing at, which then immediately pulls her at a speed of 70 meters per second to her destination. When cast on a vertical surface or the ceiling, Oraxia wall-latches onto it upon arrival for an infinite duration.
- Silken Thread can be canceled mid-sling by pressing the jump key (default Spacebar ), allowing Oraxia to carry the forward momentum and perform other actions.
- The speed of the Silken Thread pull is not affected by mods to parkour velocity, dodge speed, or sprint speed.

- While wall-latched after using Silken Thread, Oraxia is considered constantly airborne, which will trigger aerial prerequisite Mods and Arcanes.
- When wall-latched, Oraxia's spider legs visually animate to cling to the surrounding surfaces. Her main body rotates and orients with the reticle direction and movement keys (default W A S D ).
- Riven challenges that require wall-latching will not be counted during Silken Stride, but Arcane Arachne and Proton Set mods will still trigger their effects as normal.
- While the wall-latch duration is infinite, the duration of Predator's Lurk remains capped at 8 seconds.
- Wall-latching is permitted even when an Archgun is equipped.

Kit Interactions

- Silken Thread can quickly wall latch to activate Predator's Lurk.
- Oraxia may cast her other abilities during Silken Stride and while wall-latched.
- Performing a melee attack (default E ) during Silken Stride will instead perform Mercy's Kiss on an enemy in range, which is able to trigger Silken Stride's Toxin damage explosion on kill.
- If Mercy's Kiss is subsumed over with another ability, casting Mercy's Kiss this way is impossible, and melee weapons are still inaccessible in this form.

- Webbed Embrace's Damage Vulnerability increases Silken Stride's explosion damage.
- Widow's Brood's Scuttlers are able to trigger Silken Stride's Toxin damage explosion on kill.

Tips & Tricks

- Unlike normal wall latching, Silken Stride enables airborne damage reduction via mods such as Aerodynamic, Aviator, and Boreal's Set while latched onto a wall or ceiling.
- While melee weapons are unusable during Silken Stride, their passive effects still apply, such as set bonuses from equipped mods or passive Incarnon evolutions.
- Only the on-kill explosions will apply Archon Continuity.
- Casting Silken Stride will instantly heal Oraxia to the modified max health. By flicking the ability on and off you can heal indefinitely as long as you can afford the initial energy cost.
- Rage and Hunter Adrenaline can be used alongside her Mercy's Kiss orbs to provide Oraxia a consistent way to regenerate energy. Combined with Arcane Persistence and at least 700 armor from other sources, the only danger to Oraxia is not restoring enough health during her 4, as the two mods will provide 200/225 energy respectively per each 500 health damage taken.
