CODEX
Harness antimatter. Nova uses antiparticles to provide crowd control and amplify damage output. She obliterates enemies by destabilizing matter itself.

Release Date: July 13th, 2013

Traverse across the universe at lightspeed by rematerializing through spatial portals. Destabilize molecular bonds with potent antimatter, triggering an explosive cascade of atomic chain reactions. Simulate the nuclear destruction of dying stars, and harness the astral phenomena as the advanced prototype, codename: Nova. Nova materialized in Update 9.0 (2013-07-13).

Nova is a Warframe with antimatter and molecular crowd controlling powers. Her Passive has increased Health Orb drop chance on slain enemies that are Slowed, or Energy Orb drop chance on slain enemies that are sped-up. Null Star surrounds her with particles that provide Damage Reduction and seek out nearby enemies. Antimatter Drop condenses a particle of antimatter that absorbs all damage which is accumulated into an explosion upon collision. Wormhole creates a portal for instantaneous travel on its exit point. Molecular Prime releases a wave of antimatter that slows or speeds up enemies while applying Damage Vulnerability and exploding upon death.
