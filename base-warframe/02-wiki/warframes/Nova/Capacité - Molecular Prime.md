# Molecular Prime (capacité de Nova) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molecular_Prime

| 4 100 | Molecular Prime Primes all enemies in an expanding radius with volatile antimatter. Press the ability to slow down enemy movement, hold the ability to speed them up. Introduced in Update 9.0 (2013-07-13) | Strength:150 / 300 / 500 / 800 (explosion Blast damage) 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% (slow/speed-up) 

Duration:2 / 3 / 4 / 6 s (wave duration) 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 s (prime duration) 

Range:6 / 7 / 8 / 10 m (explosion radius) 

Misc: 5 m (starting wave radius) 5 m/s (wave speed) 20% ( Blast status chance) +100% (Damage Vulnerability) 75% (slow/speed cap) 

Info

- Nova expends 100 energy to unleash a radial wave of antimatter particles. The wave starts 5 meters from Nova and propagates at a speed of 5 meters per second for 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 seconds before dissipating. All enemies in range are coated in antimatter that destabilizes their molecular structure for 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 seconds.
- Wave Radius is calculated as:
Wave Radius = 5 + (5 × (Wave Duration × Ability Duration))
- For example, Molecular Prime at rank 3 with 150% Ability Duration will have wave radius of:
5 + (5 × (6 × 150%)) = 50 meters

- If Nova dies or leaves the mission, enemies will immediately lose their primed state.
- A maximum of 5 Molecular Prime waves can exist at a time. Recasting the ability will remove the oldest wave.

- Tap the ability key to unleash a Slow wave. Hold the ability key to unleash a Speed-Up wave. Primed enemies have their action speed increased or decreased by 35% / 40% / 45% / 50%, receive +100% Damage Vulnerability, and detonate upon death dealing 150 / 300 / 500 / 800 Blast damage with 20% Status Chance to enemies within 6 / 7 / 8 / 10 meters.
- Slow/speed-up debuff is capped at 75%, achievable with 150% Ability Strength.
- Explosion damage has 60% linear Damage Falloff.
- Each explosion can cause the subsequent death and detonation of nearby enemies, resulting in a chain reaction.

- Molecular Prime does not stack from multiple Novas.
Unique Interactions With Explosions

- Due to Molecular Prime explosions counting as a Weapon Hit, it can cause the ability to inherit certain traits from the Weapon/Ability that dealt the killing blow to the primed enemy which can all be propagated through chain reactions of explosions. Some such effects are:
- Anything that provides an Extra Hit will apply to Molecular Prime, these are as follows:
- Weapons:
- Reconifex's passive, giving a Heat Magazine on perfect reload.

- Warframes:
- Uriel's Demonium Runes
- Grendel's Gastroparesis Augment.
- Cyte-09's Resupply
- Narin's Sangodae
- Garuda's Seeking Talons
- Oraxia's Silken Stride
- Saryn's Toxic Lash
- Xaku's Xata's Whisper

- Molecular Prime explosions from Melee Kills will be able to Stealth Hit, thus benefitting from the Stealth Damage Bonus (+700%)
- Killing a primed enemy can cause the explosion to trigger equipped Weapon Arcanes.
- On melee kill, the explosion benefits from Melee Influence while it is active, and Melee Careen while the enemy is Frozen.
- On secondary kill, the explosion can deal increased Overguard Damage if Secondary Fortifier is equipped.
- On Molecular Prime hit that causes Status with Secondary Encumber equipped, you have a 24% chance to proc another random Status Effect.
- The explosion from killing a primed enemy with a your Primary/Secondary/Melee will cause Molecular Prime kills to stack Merciless Arcanes ( Primary/ Secondary) as well as Dexterity Arcanes( Primary/ Secondary) Respectively.
- Kills from your Primary/Secondary will cause Molecular Prime Headshot Kills to stack Deadhead Arcanes ( Primary/ Secondary) Respectively.

- The explosions can also benefit from select Weapon Mods. Some such mods that apply are:
- Faction Damage Bonuses from the killing Weapon will apply to explosion damage.
- Elementalist Mods will apply to the Molecular Prime explosions Blast procs.
- Combustion Beam will trigger on Molecular Prime kills.
- Energizing/ Mending Shot will trigger on Molecular Prime hitting the respective orb type.

Tips & Tricks

- Enemies that spawn after the initial cast but are within the expanding wave are immediately primed.
- Speed-Up (hold cast) is particularly useful in Defense missions to force enemy waves to come out faster.
- Recasting the ability while it is still active will allow a wider average coverage, as by the time the first cast collapses, the recast will have already spread some distance.
- Speed-Up works on Eximus and other units protected by Overguard, although the slow does not.
- Molecular Prime works incredibly well in conjunction with Antimatter Drop, weakening a boss, or killing entire legions of enemies by triggering an explosion.
- It should be noted that the slow effect for Molecular Prime stacks with other abilities that slow. When used alongside Frost's Snow Globe, or other effects such as Cold elemental mods, this ability is also capable of bringing enemies and bosses to a grinding halt, dramatically slowing them and easing the capability of landing headshots for the whole team.
- Synergizes well with Nyx's Chaos as units will attack each other dealing double damage, weakening every individual until someone dies and causes a series of chain explosions that can kill the entire group.
- Synergizes well with Saryn's Spores Corrosive proc by first spreading and weakening every individual armored unit, then causing a series of chain explosions when a single one dies.
- Synergizes well with Loki's augment mod Irradiating Disarm for his fourth ability Radial Disarm which creates an AoE disarm and Radiation proc as units will attack each other dealing double damage, weakening every individual until someone dies and causes a series of chain explosions that can kill the entire group.
- Primed enemies' bodies visibly dissolve after death by detonation, but they are actually hidden and not yet removed, thus allowing Nekros to cast Desecrate.
- Speed alteration does not affect the interval between tick damage of status effect nor their duration.
