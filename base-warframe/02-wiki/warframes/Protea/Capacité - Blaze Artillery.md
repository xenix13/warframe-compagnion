# Blaze Artillery (capacité de Protea) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blaze_Artillery

| 2 50 | Blaze Artillery Deploys an artillery unit to blast plasma charges at enemies it faces. Each enemy hit increases the power of subsequent plasma attacks. Introduced in Update 28.0 (2020-06-11) | Strength:100 / 150 / 200 / 500 ( Heat damage per shot) 

Duration:3 s 

Range:30 m (targeting range) 

Misc: 3/s (shots per second) 130° (firing arc) 3 (limit of active turrets) 100% ( Heat status chance) 2 m (area of effect) +100% (damage bonus per hit) 

Info

- Protea expends 50 energy to deploy a plasma turret attached to her utility belt, facing toward the aiming reticle. Once deployed, Blaze Artillery hovers in place for 3 seconds, repeatedly acquiring enemy targets within 30 meters in a 130 degree frontal cone and within Line of Sight. It fires plasma charges upon those targets with a fire rate of 3 shots per second. Protea may deploy up to 3 plasma turrets at once.
- Deploying animation speed is affected by Casting Speed, from mods such as Natural Talent as well as Amber Archon Shard.
- Recasting the ability while 3 turrets already exist will remove the oldest turret.
- Turret duration is shown on the ability icon, and displayed as a holographic wheel at the rear of the turret that spins down until the turret despawns.

- When a target is acquired, the turret fires a stream of scorching plasma with infinite body Punch Through to inflict 100 / 150 / 200 / 500 Heat damage with a 100% status chance within a 2 meter radius around its target. Blaze Artillery's overall damage increases by 100% for each successful hit against all enemies caught in a plasma stream, stacking for subsequent attacks.
- The current damage multiplier (counting enemies hit by all previous shots) is shown on the rear of the turret.
- The turret will attempt to fire at a different target that's within range for each subsequent shot.
- Enemies within coverage of a Nullifier Crewman's bubble will not be targeted by the turret.

- Ability Synergy:
- Blaze Artillery contributes damage to Temporal Anchor's recorded damage pool for the implosion on rewind.

- Casting Blaze Artillery is an upper-body animation that allows movement.
- Blaze Artillery is an invulnerable object that hovers in front of and above Protea as she deploys it. It counts as an "object" for Arcane Sculptor.
- Once deployed, the compact state of the plasma turret disappears from Protea's utility belt.

- Blaze Artillery's turret uses a custom model that resembles a floating flak turret with dual cannons. Cannons visually recoil when firing plasma charges, as particle effects pour out of the firing cannon's barrel followed by the plasma stream surging through the air at high speed.
- On expiration, the turret collapses in on itself in a warping effect; if the expiring turret is the last active instance, it will instantly reappear on Protea's utility belt, in the compact state.

Augment

Tips & Tricks

- Face toward a tight crowd of enemies before placing a turret, to best make use of the stacking damage bonus.
- Because the turret cannot turn (without the augment), face it in a direction where enemies already exist, or are likely to soon appear.
- When heading to extraction, a turret deployed facing ahead will both fire at enemies in front, as well as those who come up from behind.
- Since the damage multiplier is based on the number of enemies hit by each plasma stream, Blaze Artillery's damage rises very quickly when used in crowded hallways and tight corridors, where plasma streams can pierce through multiple enemies aligned with the shot.
- Scyotid's alt-fire, Nidus's Larva (subsumable), Nautilus's Cordon, and slide attacks with Telos Boltace can force enemies into tighter groups, to help maximize Blaze Artillery's hits per shot.
- One can also arrange parallel lines of Shrapnel Vortexes with Grenade Fan, creating a funnel to push enemies into a single lane.

- Artillery turrets prefer to target the closest enemy first, then switch between targets that are within range. Use this behavior to your advantage to spread the damage around and apply Heat status effects to stun multiple enemies.
- Due to the plasma streams only punching infinitely through bodies, it's better to deploy Blaze Artillery near the ground on the same level as the enemies it faces. If placed high up, the shots will impact the ground prematurely.
- However, if there are obstacles to contend with, deploying a turret from higher up can give it a greater field of view.

- Activate Temporal Anchor and place multiple turrets. Direct damage from plasma turrets, including the damage over time from Heat status effects, are recorded to fuel the temporal implosion.
- Energy spent on the turrets is returned once Protea rewinds.
- Alternatively, use the temporal implosion to pull enemies toward a central location, then deploy turrets to increase their damage multipliers quickly.

- The ever-increasing damage multiplier has several interesting properties:
- For instance, it causes Ability Strength to have diminishing returns on damage. At 100% Ability Strength, Blaze Artillery will kill an enemy with 5000 health in four hits: 500, then 1000, then 1500, then 2000, for 5000 total damage (ignoring the heat status effect for simplicity). Increasing Ability Strength to 167% will kill the same enemy in three shots (835, then 1670, then 2505), which is a DPS increase of only 33%.
- By the same token, placing three Blaze Artillery turrets in the same direction does NOT triple your average DPS compared to a single one. Using an example target with 15000 health, the three turrets will kill it in 4 shots each (1500, then 3000, then 4500, then 6000), while the singular turret will need 8 shots (i.e. 3 turrets together kill only twice as fast as a lone turret). Taking the heat status effect into account makes this effect even more pronounced.
- This applies to every "damage increasing effect" in one way or another, such as using Viral status, Roar, shredding armor, Heat Inherit, etc. They remain valuable, but not as much.
- This also applies the other way around: an enemy having twice as much health won't take twice as much time to kill. It also means that high-armor targets like Grineer won't have a disproportionately higher time-to-kill compared to other factions, even without Temporal Erosion.
- In general, this makes Ability Duration more valuable than Ability Strength for scaling the damage of individual turrets. It also means that it's usually better to cover non-overlapping areas when deploying multiple turrets, rather than stacking them in the same place.
