# Temporal Anchor (capacité de Protea) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Temporal_Anchor

| 4 100 | Temporal Anchor Drops a Temporal Anchor which, after a short duration, Protea rewinds to trigger a temporal implosion. Implosion damage increases based on damage dealt between anchor drop and rewind. Everything lost or expended in that time is returned. Dying while Anchor is active rewinds Protea to the anchor, saving her. Protea is knocked down and the anchor vanishes. Introduced in Update 28.0 (2020-06-11) | Strength:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 % (recorded damage conversion) 

Duration:5 / 6 / 7 / 8 s (anchor duration) 

Range:8 / 10 / 12 / 15 m (implosion range) 

Misc: 3 s (initial invulnerability phase) 100 % (enemy knockdown chance on implosion) 100 % (status cleanse on rewind) 1.5 s (rewind countdown) 100 % (self knockdown chance on lethal hit) 5 % (health restore on lethal hit) 

Info

- Protea expends 100 Energy to snapshot her past self where she stands, deploying a temporal anchor that saves her position and state at the time of ability activation for 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 seconds. Protea becomes Invulnerable for 3s, and her actions are recorded as a trail of afterimages. Simultaneously, 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% of each damage instance dealt by Protea's weapons and abilities while anchored is recorded beneath the aiming reticle. Hold down the ability key (default 4 ) while active to destroy the anchor, cancelling all effects of the ability to keep Protea in her present position and state.

- Anchor Mechanics:
- When the anchor expires or is deactivated by tapping the ability key (default 4 ), Protea unleashes all recorded damage as Blast damage to all enemies within Line of Sight in a 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 meter radius around her.
- Enemies suffer a Knockdown while they are violently pulled inward.
- Implosion will not occur on rewind if there is no recorded damage.

- Protea is cleansed of all negative Status Effects and thrust back into the past, becoming Invulnerable while causing her to physically rewind at accelerated speed through her afterimages to her anchor.
- Once the rewind is complete, all afterimages and the anchor disappear, while Protea's Ammo, Energy, Shield, Health, and Combo Counter are restored to the amounts saved by her anchor.
- Activation energy cost, and Incarnon charge is not restored.

- Any Mission Items held by Protea while anchored will not be dropped.
- When there is 1.5s remaining of the ability's duration, a ticking sound effect occurs to warn the player before Protea rewinds.

- Death Gating:
- If Protea receives fatal damage while anchored, all recorded damage is lost as she immediately rewinds to her anchor, suffering a Ragdoll with her Health restored to 5% of max. All other stats are restored as usual.
- Protea can cast Temporal Anchor again right after the rewind, while still Ragdolled. The ability will protect her from fatal damage immediately, and 3s Invulnerability allows her to get up.
- Note that no mod, arcane, or ability seems to prevent Ragdoll, not even abilities which broadly state "Immune to status effects".

- Rewind:
- Protea can cast all other abilities while she is rewinding.
- Temporal Anchor can be dispelled if Protea herself enters a Nullifier Crewman bubble, in which case there will not be a rewind.
- Nullification will not interrupt an ongoing rewind.

- Temporal Anchor uses a translucent model of Protea that mimics her pose on the ability cast. The anchor's pose changes throughout the duration of the ability. Afterimages capture the poses of Protea depending on the action she takes within that second of time.
- Stats are reset by the afterimages - if somehow the rewind is halted early, stats may not be fully restored.
- Both the anchor and the afterimages will disappear from view after some time, though this is purely a visual effect as Protea will still rewind through her entire journey.
- The player's camera view is locked to the afterimages while rewinding. All animations and sounds related to Protea's actions (including sounds from her environment) are recorded then played in reverse when she rewinds.
- Any Melee attacks and Archgun Deployer mid-animation (as the rewind occurs) will continue the animations, and hit once the rewind completes.

- Rewind speed varies depending on the number of afterimages produced by Protea. If Protea has not moved far from her anchor, rewind speed is nearly instantaneous.
- Activation and rewind end animation speeds are affected by Casting Speed.

- If Protea initiates Transference while anchored, the Operator will be forced to rejoin the Warframe when the rewind begins.
- When performing an Emote, the rewind will play it backwards and it will continue after the rewind ends. Shawzin will continue to show notes and even auto play.
- Casting Temporal Anchor is a full-body animation that allows movement and gliding.

Kit Interactions

- Shrapnel Vortexes and Blaze Artilleries contribute damage to Temporal Anchor's recorded damage pool for the implosion on rewind.
- Shield Satellites attached to Protea and her companion will follow them throughout the rewind.
- While Shield will be reset to anchored state, the satellite protects Protea from any unforeseen damage after she arrives at the anchor point.

Tips & Tricks

- Use the rewind to explore branching paths or shorten the travel between pickups and objectives (e.g. Power Cells for Excavators).
- Tap the ability key to rewind early, or hold down the ability key to abort the rewind.
- Using Transference after rewinding will allow the Operator to move freely, while the rewinding audio still plays. Once the rewind stops, the Operator can initiate Transference again to teleport Protea to their location, restoring all of the saved values without returning to the anchored location.
- Record at least 1 damage point to trigger a temporal implosion on rewind.
- The ability does not inflict Blast procs, strip enemy armor for better damage output.
- Activate and engage enemies with your entire arsenal of weapons and abilities! All stats are fully restored to the anchored state once Protea rewinds.
- Deploy many Shrapnel Vortexes and Blaze Artilleries to help record damage for the temporal implosion.
- Temporal Erosion and Temporal Artillery can be used to massively increase first the recorded and then the implosion damage.

- Protea returns to her anchor once the ability ends, be mindful of any enemies near it before you rewind as the rewind end animation will lock Protea in place briefly.
- You may rewind into or past a Nullifier Crewman bubble.
- Deploy Shield Satellites and Dispensary at the anchor location to ensure a safer rewind end.

- Reach a high melee combo count before activating this ability, allowing your heavy attack to deal substantial damage, then rewind the combo to the anchored amount.
- Use in tandem with the Glaive (Weapon Type) to perform charged throw heavy attacks.

Bugs

- The game has forced ability deactivation points (e.g. mission extraction, Simulacrum arsenal), this is different from dispelling abilities and will trigger rewind. This can lead to bugs in Boss arenas, and other rooms that close permanently.
- Known examples: H-09 Efervon Tank arena, Perita Rebellion arena.

- If an active Atmospheric Archgun runs out of ammo reserves while Protea is anchored, the Archgun will briefly disappear then reappear with the stored ammo count once Protea rewinds.
- The implosion will not pull but Knockdown some enemies in place (e.g. Drekar Manic Bombard).
- The implosion will not pull body blocked enemies, cast it in the air for better results.
