Info

- Koumei expends 75 Energy to surround herself in sacred Omamori charms, with the amount of charms summoned equal to the total dice roll number from her passive. While active, Koumei has a 50% chance to negate each instance of incoming enemy damage. Negating a hit consumes a charm and heals Koumei for 0.5 / 0.65 / 0.8 / 1 times the hits damage, and puts Omamori on a 3 seconds cooldown, during which it cannot heal or consume a charm; however, the chance to negate damage remains active during this cooldown. Each charm lasts indefinitely until consumed.
- Heal amount is based on damage after all sources of Damage Reduction.
- Heal amount is calculated before Arcane Persistence 500 HP/s cap.

- If Koumei's health is full when healing from a negated hit, Omamori will give Shields that can overflow into Overshields.
- Cooldown timer is shown beside the hitpoints indicator.
- Omamori's cooldown is paused during Shield Gate invulnerability.

- Charms float and orbit around Koumei. When consumed, a charm floats away toward the right while indicating the number of healing or shielding gained.
- Charms visually dissolve away when Koumei is aiming her weapons.

- Can be recast while active when 5 charms or less are remaining. Audible sound cues play when at five or no charms left.

Kit Interactions

- - When Koumei's Passive Five Fates dice roll score triple sixes, Omamori charms glisten in silver and Koumei becomes Invulnerable by negating damage until all charms from the same cast are consumed.

Helminth

- Subsuming Koumei to the Helminth will offer Omamori and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- Subsumed Omamori creates between 10 to 20 charms, but cannot gain invulnerability.
