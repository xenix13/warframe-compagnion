Info

- Koumei expends 25 Energy to guide a Weaver Star toward the reticle over a distance of 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 meters. When the weaver star collides with a surface or enemy, it deals damage equal to 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 multiplied by the total dice roll number from Koumei's Passive (between 1 and 30) in a 3 meter AoE before bouncing off and continuing to travel. The AoE deals a random elemental damage type to each enemy hit and inflicts the corresponding Status Effect with a random amount of stacks between 3 and 5. As the star travels it also weaves 5 / 8 / 10 / 15 threads of destiny in the environment, each imbued with a random elemental damage type. Each thread spans a length of 15 meters and remains active for 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 seconds. On contact with enemies, Kumihimo threads inflict damage equal to 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 multiplied by the total dice roll number from Koumei's Passive, and apply 1 stack of the thread's elemental status effect;
- Eligible status effects inflicted by Kumihimo include:
- Primary Elemental ( Cold, Electricity, Heat, Toxin)
- Secondary Elemental ( Blast, Corrosive, Gas, Magnetic, Radiation, Viral)

- Kumihimo cannot apply the following:
- Impact, Puncture, Slash, Void, Tau, Knockdown, Stagger, Ragdoll, Lifted, and Microwave

- Weaver Star flies toward the reticle direction on cast until reaching its maximum range, allowing threads to form in open air. If cast toward the ground, the weaver star floats along the horizontal surface and accounts for terrain elevation difference.
- Weaver Star's bounce AoE deals the same damage type as the Status Effect it applied to each hit enemy.
- The damage type is randomized for each enemy hit, even if multiple enemies are hit by the same bounce.

- Threads attempt to adhere to the walls and floors with a distance of 2 meters between each thread, but will try to catch enemies in their path as the threads form when possible.
- Threads are positioned diagonally, with each subsequent thread flipped opposite to the previous thread. Threads do not have collision and can phase through walls.
- Upon reaching a wall the star will stop adhering to the floor and ricochet.

- Kumihimo thread's contact damage is evenly split between Impact, Puncture, and Slash, regardless of the elemental status it inflicts.
- Each thread deals its damage and status only once per physical contact. To reapply the damage the enemy must first move away from the thread then touch it again.
- Ragdolled enemies will rapidly take damage as long as they are touching the thread potentially causing hundreds of damage instances within a few seconds.
- Threads constantly emit different primary elemental particle visual effects and become briefly invisible when players aim their weapons.

- Number of maximum threads is capped at 30, achievable with multiple casts or from a single cast with 600% / 375% / 300% / 200% Ability Range. Creating additional threads will remove the oldest thread active, even if it was created within the same cast.

- Ability Synergy:
- When Koumei's Passive Five Fates dice roll score triple sixes, Kumihimo's Weaver Star bounce AoE will deal drastically increased damage; a total of ? / ? / ? / ~2750 multiplied by the total dice roll number from Koumei's Passive, evenly split between each of the 10 elemental damage types and inflicting 1 stack of each corresponding status effect. The resulting threads of destiny become silver threads that inflict 1 stack of all 10 types of elemental status effects to enemies on contact.
- Weaver Star triple sixes bounce damage is more accurately equal to 2781.25 multiplied by the total dice roll, or 111.25x the normal bounce damage.

Augment

Main article: Kumihimo Loading
Kumihimo is a Warframe Augment Mod for Koumei's Kumihimo that gives a loaded die after killing 6 enemies with Koumei's passive. This loaded die will always roll a 6 and empowers Kumihimo.

Rank | Kills Required | Cost

1 | 10 | 7 

2 | 8 | 8 

3 | 6 | 9 

Tips & Tricks

- Due to the hard to control ricochet and thread limit, builds with high range can potentially cause threads to form very far from the player and/or at high elevations(such as n the ceilings of tall rooms) where enemies will not be caught by them.
