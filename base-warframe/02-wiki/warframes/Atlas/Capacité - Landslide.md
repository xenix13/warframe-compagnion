Info

- Atlas expends 25 energy to charge forward at 40 m/s to punch a target enemy up to 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 meters away. The target and enemies within 3 / 3.2 / 3.4 / 4 meters are dealt 100 / 200 / 300 / 350 Impact damage as a melee strike.
- Ability Strength is additive and multiplicative to base damage (e.g., Pressure Point, Arcane Fury).
- Landslide has a 35% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, and 15% status chance.
- Landslide has a moddable Pseudo-Exalted Weapon, Landslide Fists, which does not provide an actual equippable weapon but can be modded and slotted with Arcane Enhancements to boost the ability's performance.
- Cannot perform blocking or heavy attacks and as such cannot equip related mods (e.g., Parry, Reflex Coil, Mentor's Legacy, Melee Animosity) and does not have an Exilus Slot.
- Cannot benefit from Carnis Mandible's heavy attack set effect.

- Does not benefit from Attack Speed and as such cannot equip Fury.
- This means that Spoiled Strike provides no downside.

- Cannot perform Finishers and as such cannot equip Finishing Touch and Melee Crescendo.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Landslide.

- Landslide can be recast within a 5 second window (Affected by Combo Duration Mods) to perform a repeating combo chain, granting 20 Ability Combo Counter points. Landslide gains 1x additional damage per combo multiplier, up to 12x damage at 12x combo.
- Additionally, recasting the ability during this duration will perform consecutive punches that increases the move's range and reduces energy cost. On the 2nd punch, the impact radius is increased to ? / ? / ? / 6 meters and halves the energy cost, and the 3rd punch increases the impact radius to ? / ? / ? / 8 and quarters the energy cost. As long as the combo counter is active, the next punch in the sequence will not reset, following a 1-2-3-1-2-3... pattern for the impact range, but keeping the energy discount. Running out of combo window resets to first punch.
- This reduction in energy cost modifies Landslide's activation cost and is afterward affected by Ability Efficiency as normal.

- Ability Combo is functionally equivalent to Melee Combo and as such benefits from Combo Duration (e.g., Body Count, Primary Dexterity), Additional Combo Count Chance (e.g., Quickening, True Punishment, Relentless Combination) and Initial Combo (e.g., Corrupt Charge, Galvanized Reflex). It is also affected by Combo Decay from Naramon's Power Spike.

- Because Landslide is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Atlas' is Invulnerable to damage while dashing.
- Each enemy hit increases the Melee Combo Counter by 1 point.
- AoE damage bypasses obstacles in the environment and does not diminish with distance.
- If the target dies before Atlas lands his punch, energy used for Landslide will be refunded.
- The third hit of the combo will knock down enemies, and activate Melee Afflictions

- Can be cast while in mid-air.
- Landslide attacks can damage enemies across the Rift Plane.

Weapon

Main article: Landslide Fists
Landslide Fists are Atlas and Atlas Prime's Pseudo-Exalted Weapon empowering his Landslide ability.

As a Pseudo-Exalted, the ability does not summon an actual useable weapon but can equip melee mods to enhance the ability itself.

- This weapon deals primarily Impact damage.
- Utilizes the pseudo-exalted/Ability version of the Melee Combo system, receiving a damage multiplier of 1.0x per Combo Counter up to the cap of 12.0x.
- Default Combo Duration is 5 seconds, with duration resetting on any combo generation.
- Duration is affected by mods (e.g., Body Count) and arcanes (e.g., Primary Dexterity).
- Duration is not affected by Amalgam Furax Body Count.

- Hitting at least one enemy on ability cast will increase combo count by 20 hits (+1.0x) up to a maximum counter of 220 (12.0x).
- Enemy corpses count as enemies for combo generation on ability cast.
- Combo count is affected by Additional Combo Count Chance mods (e.g., Quickening, True Punishment), but each proc of the effect only awards one additional hit alongside the initial 20. Stacking mods to reach above 100% chance can award additional hits.
- Combo count is not affected by the Rauta.

- Initial Combo is affected by mods (e.g., Corrupt Charge, Galvanized Reflex).
- Even if the combo UI is not shown, Initial Combo does provide the corresponding damage multiplier.
- Melee Crescendo has no effect and cannot be equipped.

- Combo Decay is reduced by the Power Spike passive from the Naramon focus school.

- Cannot perform blocking or heavy attacks and as such cannot equip related mods (e.g. Parry, Reflex Coil, Mentor's Legacy, Melee Animosity) and does not have an Exilus Slot.
- Cannot benefit from Carnis Mandible's heavy attack set effect.

- Does not benefit from Attack Speed and as such cannot equip Fury.
- This means that Spoiled Strike provides no downside.

- Cannot perform Finishers and as such cannot equip Finishing Touch and Melee Crescendo.
- The third hit of the combo will knock down enemies, and activate Melee Afflictions

Kit Interactions

- Using Landslide to kill petrified enemies frozen by Path of Statues, Petrify, or by casting Rumblers grants 50% more health and armor points from the enemies' dropped Rubble.
- Rubble Heap removes the energy cost, doubles the punch distance, and adds 100% Ability Strength to Landslide.

Tips & Tricks

- Landslide excels at rampaging between spread out clusters of enemies.
- Take advantage of the dash's health invulnerability to charge through devastating attacks
- Modifiers for increasing melee combo duration (such as Primary Dexterity at Rank 5) as well as decreasing melee combo loss (such as Naramon's Power Spike) can be used to minimize the combo loss if you are unable to hit a target for a period of time (such as while traveling between objectives).
- Performing a Bullet Jump between casts allows players to extend Atlas' reach.
- Rubble Heap Doubles the dash range of Landslide
- Note that Atlas' knockdown immunity will not apply while he is not grounded.

- Landslide benefits from elemental damage granted by certain augments, including:
- Ember's Fireball Frenzy
- Frost's Freeze Force
- Oberon's Smite Infusion
- Saryn's Venom Dose
- Volt's Shock Trooper
