## Notes

- Excalibur expends 25 Energy to draw an ethereal Skana, the Exalted Blade, and wields it as his melee Exalted Weapon. Normal attacks inflict 100 / 125 / 200 / 250 damage within 2.5 meters.
- Exclusively uses the Exalted Blade Stance.
- Damage is distributed between 15% Impact, 15% Puncture, and 70% Slash.
- Normal, slide, and aerial attacks will emit a 3 meter-wide energy wave in the direction of aim. Energy waves have the same base damage as the attacks that release them, inflict Stagger, and fly at a constant speed of 30 m/s until they dissipate after 2.5s, which corresponds to a maximum distance of 75 meters.
- The energy waves and Exalted Blade itself are considered separate entities and will both deal damage separately.
- Energy waves can increase Melee Combo Counter and trigger Tennokai.
- Energy waves will punch through and hit enemies and terrain regardless of thickness up to their maximum range.
- Energy waves lose 40% of their damage per second; Wave damage falls off linearly with distance from 100% to 0% between 0 and 70m.
- Energy waves lose 20% of their damage each time they hit an enemy; this decreases the projectile lifetime by 0.5s (range by 15m) for every enemy hit from the max lifetime of 2.5s (max range of 70m).

- Performing a slide attack will also drain 25 energy to cast a Judged Severance, a diminished version of Radial Blind that Blinds and Stuns enemies within 5m for 6 seconds, as well as disabling security cameras.
- This will occur even if Radial Blind is replaced via the Helminth system, which affords Excalibur access to 5 active abilities if you choose to replace his 2nd ability (2 ). The visual and sound effects are removed when this is done on Excalibur and Excalibur Prime but not on Excalibur Umbra.
- Enemies who cannot be stunned instead have their weapons and abilities disabled, this includes but is not limited to Acolyte, Eximus with overguard,Stalker, Legacyte.
- Stealth Damage Bonus is also applied.

- This effect does not trigger Brief Respite or the Augur Set bonus.

- Heavy attacks will emit 2 energy waves in a cross pattern in the direction of aim.
- Waves are 10 meters wide, have the same base damage as the attacks that release them, inflict Lifted, and fly at a constant speed of 30 m/s until they dissipate after 2.5s, which corresponds to a maximum distance of 75 meters.
- Energy waves lose 40% of their damage per second; Wave damage falls off linearly with distance from 100% to 0% between 0 and 70m.
- Energy waves from the heavy attack do not lose 20% of their damage each time they hit an enemy, unlike normal waves.
- Energy wave's damage does not scale off combo counter.

- Attacks and energy waves have a 15% critical chance, 2.0x critical multiplier, and 15% status chance.
- Exalted Blade can be equipped with melee mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Exalted Blade.

- Because Exalted Blade is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Radial damage from slam attacks diminishes with distance, does not have a critical chance, is not affected by the Melee Combo Counter, and will cause enemies within range to suffer a Knockdown.
- Ground finishers inflict 400% of the total modified damage from normal attacks. Prompted Finishers inflict 3200% of the total modified damage from normal attacks as Finisher damage.
- Exalted Blade consumes 2.5 energy per second while active and will remain active until Excalibur's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Excalibur cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, Archon Stretch and/or Wellspring while Exalted Blade is active.
- Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Rage, Hunter Adrenaline, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, and Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes can still replenish Excalibur's energy while Exalted Blade is active.
- Exalted Blade's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- Can be cast while moving or sprinting without interruption.

Weapon

Main article: Exalted Blade (Weapon)
Exalted Blade is Excalibur, Excalibur Prime, and Excalibur Umbra's signature Exalted Weapon, summoned by activating the Exalted Blade. The weapon exclusively uses the Exalted Blade stance.

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Chromatic Blade augment changes the damage from physical to a primary elemental one based on Excalibur's Emissive Color.

- Slide attacks trigger a Radial Blind that stuns enemies within 5 meters for 6 seconds.
- This stun does go through overguard and appears to disable enemy abilities similar to Silence on even Acolytes.

- Innate two and one polarities.
- Excalibur Umbra's Exalted Umbra Blade instead has innate two polarities

Advantages

- Can reach over 100% Status Chance with a maxed Chromatic Blade and at least 189% Ability Strength.
- Attacks release waves with unlimited punch through up to 40 meters.
- Waves can increase Melee Combo Counter and trigger Tennokai.

For a full breakdown, see Exalted Blade.

Disadvantages

- Low wave flight speed.
- Requires energy to activate and keep active.
- Slide attack blinds do not open affected enemies to Finisher Attacks.
For a full breakdown, see Exalted Blade.

Stance

Main article: Exalted Blade (Stance)
Exalted Blade is a unique Stance exclusive to Exalted Blade that can only be acquired and used by activating Excalibur's fourth ability, Exalted Blade. This Stance overrides the Stance on any currently equipped melee weapon for as long as Exalted Blade remains active.

Kit Interactions

- - Being a Sword, Exalted Blade will always gain the 10% damage and attack speed bonuses of Excalibur's Passive.
- Slash Dash is affected by mods equipped on Exalted Blade, and each of its hits will release an energy wave when Exalted Blade is active, while adding hits to the Melee Combo Counter.

Tips & Tricks

- Due to Exalted Blade's waves being able to punch through up to its maximum range, Exalted Blade is well suited to taking out hordes of enemies, especially if they are huddled together.
- Furthermore, Exalted Blade can be used to deal with enemies while in cover without requiring punch through mods.

- The innate slide attack blind provides a cheaper alternative to Radial Blind, albeit with greatly reduced duration and range. (Note that the Radial Blind power is cheaper both per meter of range and per second of effect, despite having a higher cost per cast.)
- Allows players to blind enemies and quickly deal with them without being delayed by finisher animations.

- Combined with Mag's Magnetize or a Void status effect, the sword waves can be redirected to hit the enemies inside multiple times until its maximum range can be reached.
- Crouching will allow the use of all Exalted Blade attacks while remaining entirely stationary; this can be extremely useful during boss fights, defense missions, survival, etc.
- Melee Afflictions synergies:
- Slash Dash will trigger because of guaranteed Knockdown, even while Frozen, which will trigger this arcane.
- Normal slams will trigger because of guaranteed Ragdoll which will also work on Frozen enemies.
- Heavy slams will trigger because of guaranteed Lifted.
- Heavy attack waves will trigger because of guaranteed Lifted.
- Neutral and Block Neutral can also trigger, but it is more efficient to rely on Tennokai and heavy attack waves.
- Status effects stacked by Melee Afflictions can fully stack Arcane Impetus.
- Void can be reapplied from enemies afflicted by Void status (does not stack).
- Tau can be applied then stacked with sources like Haalvu and Venato Prime.

Bugs

- Using this ability during an "Unarmed" bug will result in Exalted Blade having the same stats as your Melee Weapon, and sometimes will perform your Melee Weapon's Unsheathed attacks without the Waves.
- If Excalibur enters Bleedout state while Exalted Blade is active, Excalibur will use his melee and sometimes primary weapon rather than his secondary.
- Does not benefit from Violet Archon Shard Ability Damage.
- Slams and heavy slams do not benefit from Chromatic Blade.
- This can be used to get non-combinational elemental bonuses. An example being a Toxin only modded Exalted Blade with Heat emissive for only Gas, allowing slams to trigger toxin as well as corrosive with Archon Continuity. This is especially powerful with Arcane Impetus since slams will trigger this effect, stacking each +6.
