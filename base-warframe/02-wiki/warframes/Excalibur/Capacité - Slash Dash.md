# Slash Dash (capacité de Excalibur) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Slash_Dash

Duration:N/A 

Range:20 m (initial target range) 7 m (chain range) 36 m/s (initial dash velocity) 

Misc: 0.87 s (non-locked on dash time) 75 m/s (locked on dash speed) 

Info

- Excalibur expends 25 Energy to dash forward, gaining an boost of 36 m/s to his current velocity, and becoming Invulnerable during the dash. If an enemy is within 20 meters of Excalibur and 10° of the reticle, then Excalibur instead will lock on to them, dash at 75 m/s, and strike them with Exalted Blade, then automatically chain to and strike additional enemies within 7 meters. Each strike deals Exalted Blade's damage, along with a forced Slash Status Effect and Knockdown.
- Every instance of damage increases the Melee Combo Counter by 1 point.
- Only benefits the active melee weapon combo counter.

- Chained Slash Dash can be cancelled by melee, jumping or rolling.
- When used on the ground, Excalibur's momentum is stopped at the end of the cast. When used in the air, Excalibur's momentum is returned to what it was before casting Slash Dash at the end of the cast.
- If Slash Dash is cancelled with a melee attack in a tight window right before connecting with a target, the momentum from Slash Dash is maintained.

- Slash Dash is affected by Aim Glide and momentum, but will not change momentum outside of its animation. Thus, whichever way you were going before you cast Slash Dash you will continue to go unless stopped, by other means, at same speed. This will affect how far your Slash Dash will go when not targeted.

- Dash time and recovery time at the end of the cast affected by Casting Speed. Casting Speed reduces the dash time, which reduces distance traveled.
- Slash Dash inherits the stats, melee mods, and Arcane Enhancements equipped on Exalted Blade.
- Base Damage is determined by the Ability Strength of Exalted Blade when Slash Dash is casted.
- Allows Precision Intensify to increase Slash Dash's damage.
- Snapshotted Ability Strength to an active Exalted Blade for Slash Dash's direct damage is not applied.

- Slash Dash has Exalted Blade's 15% Critical Chance, 2x Critical Damage, and 15% Status Chance, affected by mods equipped on it.
- Slash Dash is affected by other effects that boost melee damage such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Does not benefit from Condition Overload.
- This includes when Exalted Blade is drawn, however, waves from connecting with a target are affected as usual.

- Slash Dash gains 25% additional damage based on the current combo counter, up to a 2.75x damage multiplier at 12x combo, or 3.0x with Venka Prime equipped at 13x combo.
- Does not apply to initial combo on Exalted Blade and is still based on the active melee weapon combo counter.

- Slash Dash cannot benefit from combo counter mods such as Blood Rush unless Exalted Blade is active or by using initial combo from mods such as Galvanized Reflex or Melee Crescendo equipped on Exalted Blade.
- Slash Dash hits satisfy "On Melee Hit" conditions such as Primed Pack Leader and Royal Wrath, and on kills with it will trigger "On Melee Kill" effects such as Primary Dexterity.
- Melee Range does not effect on the range of Slash Dash's strike.
- If Exalted Blade is removed via the Helminth system, Slash Dash loses all related damage bonuses, including its Critical Chance.

- Slash Dash can damage enemies across the Rift Plane.

Weapon

Main article: Exalted Blade (Weapon)
Exalted Blade is Excalibur, Excalibur Prime, and Excalibur Umbra's signature Exalted Weapon, summoned by activating the Exalted Blade. The weapon exclusively uses the Exalted Blade stance.

Kit Interactions

- - When Exalted Blade is active, Slash Dash gains the following benefits:
- Benefits of Combo Counter based mods such as Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds without requiring initial combo.
- Slash Dash releases an energy wave identical to the normal waves of Exalted Blade.

Tips & Tricks

- Slash Dash will hit through a Grineer Shield Lancer's Shield.
- If there are no enemies within its targeting area, Slash Dash will make Excalibur dash forward in the direction of aim, allowing it to be used as a mobility aid when reaching high places.
- Slash Dash will launch the player in most of the crosshair directions, except for straight upward. Instead it will launch the player in a 60% degree angle forward.
- When changing movement direction with Slash Dash, the current direction of momentum will affect Slash Dash, requiring overcompensation. Example, if traveling forward at a high velocity and wanting to change direction by 90 degrees, the player will have to angle >90 degrees relative to the forward velocity before casting Slash Dash to travel in the desired direction.
- Useful traversal tool that can launch player quite high in the air and greatly increase the aerial momentum, as long as the player is holding the Aim Glide button while casting and hasn't ran out of Air Glide Time.
- With high Ability Range and not aiming at enemies, players can launch themselves very high into the air and travel with very high velocity and long distance.
- Can be used to pass through Corpus laser doors mostly unharmed.
- Perfect for medium-distance traveling and quickly closing in on distant enemies. Consecutive use of the ability allows the user to quickly traverse the maps, similar to Tail Wind or Mach Rush.
- With the proper mods this is one of the most damage-efficient AoE abilities in the game, doing massive damage to multiple targets for very little cost.
- Provides Excalibur with full immunity, including Overguard, shield, and health immunity during the animation.
- Invulnerability allows players to deal with enemies by repeatedly casting Slash Dash without fear of damage from enemies or status procs during the animation. However, being that there is no invulnerability overlap between the end of the first cast and the beginning of the second cast, there is a small window for Excalibur to receive damage.
- Slash Dash can be interrupted with a roll, which allows synergy with Arcane Circumvent in building and maintaining overguard.

- Slash Dash damage can be increased significantly by using Melee Afflictions which is triggered by its Knockdown effect.
- Can knockdown Frozen targets.
- This enables Slash Dash to fully stack Arcane Impetus, and can trigger Archon mod secondary effects such as Archon Stretch if a target knocked down was affected by electric when struck.

- Benefits from Wrathful Advance, which also snapshots to the entire chain when cast, similar to Furious Javelin.
- - Does not snapshot into the waves of Exalted Blade if Wrathful Advance expires while in a Slash Dash chain.

- Casting Slash Dash while Exalted Blade is active will allow players to increase the amount of damage they deal and hit a larger amount of enemies without having to build for ability range due to the wave it releases.
- Pressing the melee button or jump button at any time during the targeted Slash Dash cast will cancel the rest of ability's targets and summon your melee weapon instantly.
- Effective for canceling out of the recovery animation for heavy and slam attacks. The ability to then cancel out of the Slash Dash with another attack can lead to large speed increases in combat.

Bugs

- It is possible to get stuck on an enemy during the duration of the ability, and in most cases the enemy will not take damage.
- Melee Combo Counter gain is affected by Hate Incarnon's Swordsman's Flourish evolution, including the combo gain from Surging Dash.
- Does not receive the status chance nor full IPS conversion from Chromatic Blade in actual missions, but does in Simulacrum.
- Some amount of the emissive element is applied and combines as expected in actual missions.
