Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Excalibur/Abilities

## Passive

Excalibur and Excalibur Prime has improved mastery in the Tenno art of the blade, receiving +10% Attack Speed and +10% damage bonuses for melee attacks while wielding the following weapons:

- Swords, such as the Skana.
- Dual Swords, such as the Dual Skana.
- Nikana (Weapon Type), such as the Dragon Nikana.
- Rapiers, such as the Destreza.
- Exalted Blade
- Bonuses are additive to mods such as Pressure Point and Fury.

Excalibur Umbra exhibits sentience, allowing him to remain active in combat even when a player uses Operator form, acting similarly to a Specter.

- Umbra can receive damage and healing in his specter form. Health and shields lost in his specter form will not be restored upon returning to Umbra unless all of Umbra's health is depleted.
- Once all of Umbra's health is depleted, he will stop moving or attacking.
- The player does not die or lose a revive, however, if Umbra's health is depleted.

- Umbra can use any equipped weapon (with the exception of Archguns) and most abilities, which will take into account any mods installed.
- When enemies come within 3 meters of Umbra, he will automatically switch to the equipped melee weapon.
- If no melee weapon is equipped, Umbra will not attack enemies who approach him, but will still cast abilities.

- Umbra's weapons will still expend ammo reserves normally. Umbra is capable of looting ammo pickups, and will actively search for them should his ammo reserves be depleted.
- Depleting all ammo reserves displays a red ammunition icon above Umbra's head.
- Squad Ammo Restores will replenish ammo but only if the player is in range, even if Umbra himself is in range.

- If Umbra falls down a bottomless pit while in Specter mode, he will not be teleported back and will remain missing until players return to Warframe mode.
Umbra's passive can be disabled with Warrior's Rest. Radial Blind is also replaced with Radial Howl:

- Enemies affected by the ability are stunned for the entire duration.
- Radial Howl is slightly faster by ~100ms in casting its effect on enemies, but is also ~100ms slower in recovery compared to Radial Blind.
- Bosses are still able to move, attack, or use abilities while affected by the ability.
- Bosses will attack the player's last known location and will follow the sound of gunfire.
- Successive casts will reduce the duration of the ability's effect on bosses.

- Sentient fighters and Shadow Stalker will have their resistances removed if they are affected by Radial Howl.
- Security cameras will deactivate their laser barriers for the duration.
- Affected enemies have smoke diffusing from their eyes or similar body part for the effect's duration.

| 1 25 | Slash Dash Slash and dash through enemies alongside a radial specter. The powerful Exalted Blade's slashes inflict Slash Status. Introduced in Vanilla (2012-10-25) | Strength:N/A 

Duration:N/A 

Range:20 m (initial target range) 7 m (chain range) 36 m/s (initial dash velocity) 

Misc: 0.87 s (non-locked on dash time) 75 m/s (locked on dash speed) 

Info

- Excalibur expends 25 Energy to dash forward, gaining an boost of 36 m/s to his current velocity, and becoming Invulnerable during the dash. If an enemy is within 20 meters of Excalibur and 10° of the reticle, then Excalibur instead will lock on to them, dash at 75 m/s, and strike them with Exalted Blade, then automatically chain to and strike additional enemies within 7 meters. Each strike deals Exalted Blade's damage, along with a forced Slash Status Effect and Knockdown.
- Every instance of damage increases the Melee Combo Counter by 1 point.
- Only benefits the active melee weapon combo counter.

- Chained Slash Dash can be cancelled by melee, jumping or rolling.
- When used on the ground, Excalibur's momentum is stopped at the end of the cast. When used in the air, Excalibur's momentum is returned to what it was before casting Slash Dash at the end of the cast.
- If Slash Dash is cancelled with a melee attack in a tight window right before connecting with a target, the momentum from Slash Dash is maintained.

- Slash Dash is affected by Aim Glide and momentum, but will not change momentum outside of its animation. Thus, whichever way you were going before you cast Slash Dash you will continue to go unless stopped, by other means, at same speed. This will affect how far your Slash Dash will go when not targeted.

- Dash time and recovery time at the end of the cast affected by Casting Speed. Casting Speed reduces the dash time, which reduces distance traveled.
- Slash Dash inherits the stats, melee mods, and Arcane Enhancements equipped on Exalted Blade.
- Base Damage is determined by the Ability Strength of Exalted Blade when Slash Dash is casted.
- Allows Precision Intensify to increase Slash Dash's damage.
- Snapshotted Ability Strength to an active Exalted Blade for Slash Dash's direct damage is not applied.

- Slash Dash has Exalted Blade's 15% Critical Chance, 2x Critical Damage, and 15% Status Chance, affected by mods equipped on it.
- Slash Dash is affected by other effects that boost melee damage such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Does not benefit from Condition Overload.
- This includes when Exalted Blade is drawn, however, waves from connecting with a target are affected as usual.

- Slash Dash gains 25% additional damage based on the current combo counter, up to a 2.75x damage multiplier at 12x combo, or 3.0x with Venka Prime equipped at 13x combo.
- Does not apply to initial combo on Exalted Blade and is still based on the active melee weapon combo counter.

- Slash Dash cannot benefit from combo counter mods such as Blood Rush unless Exalted Blade is active or by using initial combo from mods such as Galvanized Reflex or Melee Crescendo equipped on Exalted Blade.
- Slash Dash hits satisfy "On Melee Hit" conditions such as Primed Pack Leader and Royal Wrath, and on kills with it will trigger "On Melee Kill" effects such as Primary Dexterity.
- Melee Range does not effect on the range of Slash Dash's strike.
- If Exalted Blade is removed via the Helminth system, Slash Dash loses all related damage bonuses, including its Critical Chance.

- Slash Dash can damage enemies across the Rift Plane.

Tips & Tricks

- Slash Dash will hit through a Grineer Shield Lancer's Shield.
- If there are no enemies within its targeting area, Slash Dash will make Excalibur dash forward in the direction of aim, allowing it to be used as a mobility aid when reaching high places.
- Slash Dash will launch the player in most of the crosshair directions, except for straight upward. Instead it will launch the player in a 60% degree angle forward.
- When changing movement direction with Slash Dash, the current direction of momentum will affect Slash Dash, requiring overcompensation. Example, if traveling forward at a high velocity and wanting to change direction by 90 degrees, the player will have to angle >90 degrees relative to the forward velocity before casting Slash Dash to travel in the desired direction.
- Useful traversal tool that can launch player quite high in the air and greatly increase the aerial momentum, as long as the player is holding the Aim Glide button while casting and hasn't ran out of Air Glide Time.
- With high Ability Range and not aiming at enemies, players can launch themselves very high into the air and travel with very high velocity and long distance.
- Can be used to pass through Corpus laser doors mostly unharmed.
- Perfect for medium-distance traveling and quickly closing in on distant enemies. Consecutive use of the ability allows the user to quickly traverse the maps, similar to Tail Wind or Mach Rush.
- With the proper mods this is one of the most damage-efficient AoE abilities in the game, doing massive damage to multiple targets for very little cost.
- Provides Excalibur with full immunity, including Overguard, shield, and health immunity during the animation.
- Invulnerability allows players to deal with enemies by repeatedly casting Slash Dash without fear of damage from enemies or status procs during the animation. However, being that there is no invulnerability overlap between the end of the first cast and the beginning of the second cast, there is a small window for Excalibur to receive damage.
- Slash Dash can be interrupted with a roll, which allows synergy with Arcane Circumvent in building and maintaining overguard.

- Slash Dash damage can be increased significantly by using Melee Afflictions which is triggered by its Knockdown effect.
- Can knockdown Frozen targets.
- This enables Slash Dash to fully stack Arcane Impetus, and can trigger Archon mod secondary effects such as Archon Stretch if a target knocked down was affected by electric when struck.

- Benefits from Wrathful Advance, which also snapshots to the entire chain when cast, similar to Furious Javelin.
- - Does not snapshot into the waves of Exalted Blade if Wrathful Advance expires while in a Slash Dash chain.

- Casting Slash Dash while Exalted Blade is active will allow players to increase the amount of damage they deal and hit a larger amount of enemies without having to build for ability range due to the wave it releases.
- Pressing the melee button or jump button at any time during the targeted Slash Dash cast will cancel the rest of ability's targets and summon your melee weapon instantly.
- Effective for canceling out of the recovery animation for heavy and slam attacks. The ability to then cancel out of the Slash Dash with another attack can lead to large speed increases in combat.

## Notes

- Excalibur expends 25 Energy to draw an ethereal Skana, the Exalted Blade, and wields it as his melee Exalted Weapon. Normal attacks inflict 100 / 125 / 200 / 250 damage within 2.5 meters.
- Exclusively uses the Exalted Blade Stance.
- Damage is distributed between 15% Impact, 15% Puncture, and 70% Slash.
- Normal, slide, and aerial attacks will emit a 3 meter-wide energy wave in the direction of aim. Energy waves have the same base damage as the attacks that release them, inflict Stagger, and fly at a constant speed of 30 m/s until they dissipate after 2.5s, which corresponds to a maximum distance of 75 meters.
- The energy waves and Exalted Blade itself are considered separate entities and will both deal damage separately.
- Energy waves can increase Melee Combo Counter and trigger Tennokai.
- Energy waves will punch through and hit enemies and terrain regardless of thickness up to their maximum range.
- Energy waves lose 40% of their damage per second; Wave damage falls off linearly with distance from 100% to 0% between 0 and 70m.
- Energy waves lose 20% of their damage each time they hit an enemy; this decreases the projectile lifetime by 0.5s (range by 15m) for every enemy hit from the max lifetime of 2.5s (max range of 70m).

- Performing a slide attack will also drain 25 energy to cast a Judged Severance, a diminished version of Radial Blind that Blinds and Stuns enemies within 5m for 6 seconds, as well as disabling security cameras.
- This will occur even if Radial Blind is replaced via the Helminth system, which affords Excalibur access to 5 active abilities if you choose to replace his 2nd ability (2 ). The visual and sound effects are removed when this is done on Excalibur and Excalibur Prime but not on Excalibur Umbra.
- Enemies who cannot be stunned instead have their weapons and abilities disabled, this includes but is not limited to Acolyte, Eximus with overguard,Stalker, Legacyte.
- Stealth Damage Bonus is also applied.

- This effect does not trigger Brief Respite or the Augur Set bonus.

- Heavy attacks will emit 2 energy waves in a cross pattern in the direction of aim.
- Waves are 10 meters wide, have the same base damage as the attacks that release them, inflict Lifted, and fly at a constant speed of 30 m/s until they dissipate after 2.5s, which corresponds to a maximum distance of 75 meters.
- Energy waves lose 40% of their damage per second; Wave damage falls off linearly with distance from 100% to 0% between 0 and 70m.
- Energy waves from the heavy attack do not lose 20% of their damage each time they hit an enemy, unlike normal waves.
- Energy wave's damage does not scale off combo counter.

- Attacks and energy waves have a 15% critical chance, 2.0x critical multiplier, and 15% status chance.
- Exalted Blade can be equipped with melee mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Exalted Blade.

- Because Exalted Blade is counted as a melee strike, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Steel Charge and Arcane Fury.
- Radial damage from slam attacks diminishes with distance, does not have a critical chance, is not affected by the Melee Combo Counter, and will cause enemies within range to suffer a Knockdown.
- Ground finishers inflict 400% of the total modified damage from normal attacks. Prompted Finishers inflict 3200% of the total modified damage from normal attacks as Finisher damage.
- Exalted Blade consumes 2.5 energy per second while active and will remain active until Excalibur's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Excalibur cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, Archon Stretch and/or Wellspring while Exalted Blade is active.
- Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Rage, Hunter Adrenaline, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, and Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes can still replenish Excalibur's energy while Exalted Blade is active.
- Exalted Blade's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- Can be cast while moving or sprinting without interruption.

Weapon

Main article: Exalted Blade (Weapon)
Exalted Blade is Excalibur, Excalibur Prime, and Excalibur Umbra's signature Exalted Weapon, summoned by activating the Exalted Blade. The weapon exclusively uses the Exalted Blade stance.

- This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
- Chromatic Blade augment changes the damage from physical to a primary elemental one based on Excalibur's Emissive Color.

- Slide attacks trigger a Radial Blind that stuns enemies within 5 meters for 6 seconds.
- This stun does go through overguard and appears to disable enemy abilities similar to Silence on even Acolytes.

- Innate two and one polarities.
- Excalibur Umbra's Exalted Umbra Blade instead has innate two polarities

Advantages

- Can reach over 100% Status Chance with a maxed Chromatic Blade and at least 189% Ability Strength.
- Attacks release waves with unlimited punch through up to 40 meters.
- Waves can increase Melee Combo Counter and trigger Tennokai.

For a full breakdown, see Exalted Blade.

Disadvantages

- Low wave flight speed.
- Requires energy to activate and keep active.
- Slide attack blinds do not open affected enemies to Finisher Attacks.
For a full breakdown, see Exalted Blade.

Augment

Main article: Chromatic Blade
Chromatic Blade is a Warframe Augment Mod for Excalibur's Exalted Blade that increases Exalted Blade's status chance while changing its damage from physical damage to a primary elemental damage depending on Excalibur's chosen primary emission color.

Tips & Tricks

- Due to Exalted Blade's waves being able to punch through up to its maximum range, Exalted Blade is well suited to taking out hordes of enemies, especially if they are huddled together.
- Furthermore, Exalted Blade can be used to deal with enemies while in cover without requiring punch through mods.

- The innate slide attack blind provides a cheaper alternative to Radial Blind, albeit with greatly reduced duration and range. (Note that the Radial Blind power is cheaper both per meter of range and per second of effect, despite having a higher cost per cast.)
- Allows players to blind enemies and quickly deal with them without being delayed by finisher animations.

- Combined with Mag's Magnetize or a Void status effect, the sword waves can be redirected to hit the enemies inside multiple times until its maximum range can be reached.
- Crouching will allow the use of all Exalted Blade attacks while remaining entirely stationary; this can be extremely useful during boss fights, defense missions, survival, etc.
- Melee Afflictions synergies:
- Slash Dash will trigger because of guaranteed Knockdown, even while Frozen, which will trigger this arcane.
- Normal slams will trigger because of guaranteed Ragdoll which will also work on Frozen enemies.
- Heavy slams will trigger because of guaranteed Lifted.
- Heavy attack waves will trigger because of guaranteed Lifted.
- Neutral and Block Neutral can also trigger, but it is more efficient to rely on Tennokai and heavy attack waves.
- Status effects stacked by Melee Afflictions can fully stack Arcane Impetus.
- Void can be reapplied from enemies afflicted by Void status (does not stack).
- Tau can be applied then stacked with sources like Haalvu and Venato Prime.

Bugs

- Using this ability during an "Unarmed" bug will result in Exalted Blade having the same stats as your Melee Weapon, and sometimes will perform your Melee Weapon's Unsheathed attacks without the Waves.
- If Excalibur enters Bleedout state while Exalted Blade is active, Excalibur will use his melee and sometimes primary weapon rather than his secondary.
- Does not benefit from Violet Archon Shard Ability Damage.
- Slams and heavy slams do not benefit from Chromatic Blade.
- This can be used to get non-combinational elemental bonuses. An example being a Toxin only modded Exalted Blade with Heat emissive for only Gas, allowing slams to trigger toxin as well as corrosive with Archon Continuity. This is especially powerful with Arcane Impetus since slams will trigger this effect, stacking each +6.
