# Radial Javelin (capacité de Excalibur) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Radial_Javelin

| 3 75 | Radial Javelin Radial javelins impale nearby enemies, inflicting Slash Status. Introduced in Vanilla (2012-10-25) | Strength:500 / 650 / 800 / 1000 (damage) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:15 / 18 / 22 / 25 m 

Info

- Excalibur expends 75 Energy to summon a floating javelin next to each enemy in Line of Sight within a radius of 15 / 18 / 22 / 25 meters. Excalibur then drives his weapon into the ground, commanding the javelins to strike their targets. Each javelin inflicts 500 / 650 / 800 / 1000 damage evenly distributed between Impact, Puncture, and Slash, with 10% status chance, a forced Slash Status Effect, and Stagger.
- Radial Javelin will not target enemies behind obstacles in the environment unless Excalibur has line of sight, or unless the enemy is aware of Excalibur within a short period of time (e.g., an enemy that spots Excalibur and runs out of view can still be targeted by Radial Javelin if the ability is used within a short time frame).
- During the casting animation, javelins may visually spawn inside walls if enemies are close enough to one, but the javelins will ignore collision and always strike their target. Likewise, the javelin may initially spawn inside another nearby enemy or even strike through them during the striking animation, but the javelin will only ever damage its initial target.

- The javelins benefit from Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Arachne, Vigorous Swap, and Nourish.
- Has a base 0% Critical Chance and 1x Critical Damage, which can be increased by universal critical bonuses like Arcane Avenger and Tenacious Bond.

- Each javelin visually appears as an ethereal Skana. Excalibur Prime's and Excalibur Umbra's javelins appear as ethereal Skana Primes and "Umbra" Javelins, respectively.

Augment

Main article: Furious Javelin
Furious Javelin is a Warframe Augment Mod that temporarily increases Excalibur's melee damage for every enemy hit by Radial Javelin.

Rank | Damage Bonus per hit | Duration | Cost 

0 | 8% | 9s | 6 

1 | 10% | 11s | 7 

2 | 12% | 13s | 8 

3 | 15% | 16s | 9 

Tips & Tricks

- Useful for clearing out large swarms of enemies if one is surrounded.
- Allows players to deal both damage and crowd control at the same time.
