Info

- Zephyr launches a rapidly traveling slice of dense air towards her aiming reticle, expending 50 energy while grounded or 25 energy while airborne. Upon colliding with a surface or travelling its maximum distance of 100 meters, the slice explodes in a 4 / 5 / 7 / 8 meters radius, inflicting 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 damage distributed between Impact, Puncture, and Slash damage with a 50% Status Chance, as well as Knockdown and Ragdoll. Each enemy hit directly by the slice or by the subsequent explosion radius will increase Airburst's damage by +35% for subsequent hits.
- Tap the ability key (default 2 ) to pull enemies towards the center of the blast. Hold down the ability key to push enemies away instead.
- Airburst's pull force lingers for 2 seconds on the surface it collides with, during which the affected enemies remain ragdolled and gradually slide toward the center.

- The 35% damage increase is additive per hit, for a total damage increase of +35% after 1 hit, +70% after 2 hits, etc.
- The bonus accumulated from direct hits will carry over to the explosion.
- The explosion will hit all enemies within its range at the same time, but the hits will be registered one at a time (in order of the enemies' distance from the explosion). This allows the damage bonus to stack up for each enemy hit. For example, hitting 3 enemies with just the explosion will cause the nearest enemy to take 500 damage, the next closest to take 500 * 1.35 = 675, and the furthest to take 500 * 1.70 = 850.

- Damage distribution is 20% Impact, 10% Puncture, and 70% Slash.
- Airburst projectiles have innate punch through and can bypass certain objects in the environment (e.g. closed doors).

- The explosion can damage objects and enemies through walls and other cover, bypassing Line of Sight.
- Casting Airburst is a One-Handed Action, meaning it can be used while performing many actions without interrupting them, including reloading, charging, shooting, and moving (either sprinting or sliding).
- Direct hits from the projectile benefit from Vigorous Swap and other modifiers that affect abilities that deal "weapon damage", including damage boosting abilities such as Eclipse.

Helminth

- Subsuming Zephyr to the Helminth will offer Airburst and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- Subsumed Airburst includes the tap-cast for wind vacuum and hold-cast for wind explosion.

Augment

Main article: Airburst Rounds
Airburst Rounds is a Warframe Augment Mod for Zephyr that provides a damage bonus for secondaries for every enemy hit with Airburst.

Tips & Tricks

- Airburst is an effective way to deal with large swarms of enemies, especially melee, and when they are huddled together.
- Airburst allows players to apply crowd-control effects on enemies in the distance.
- Synergizes well with Zephyr's Tail Wind as the latter allows Zephyr to stay airborne longer, halving the casting cost of the ability.
