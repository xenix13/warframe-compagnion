Info

- Temple expends 50 energy and rocks out where they stand for 3 seconds, becoming Invulnerable and restoring 50% of their Health and Shields per second that overflow into Overshields. After rocking out, Temple imbues themselves with 30% / 45% / 60% / 75% weapon Heat damage for 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 seconds, and shares the damage bonus with allies inside Affinity Range. When the ability ends, the weapon damage bonus decays at a rate of ? per second.
- Damage bonus is additive with mods.
- Damage bonus does not combine with a weapon's existing elements to create secondary elements such as Gas or Blast.
- Casting animation time counts down on the ability icon, then switches to damage bonus duration timer.
- Ripper's Wail damage bonus percentage is shown as a buff icon beside Temple and their allies' hitpoints indicator.

Kit Interactions

- Time the Backbeat to grant Ripper's Wail a 50% Ability Efficiency bonus, fuel up 55 ammo for Lizzie, and increase Temple's invulnerability duration by 4 seconds.
- Time the Backbeat and strike enemies with Pyrotechnics or Overdrive, or recast Ripper's Wail on the Backbeat, to increase Ripper's Wail weapon Heat damage bonus by 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% up to a maximum of 750% Heat damage and extend its duration by 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 seconds up to a maximum of 60 seconds.

Tips & Tricks

- While increasing casting speed does decrease total healing received as well as the initial invulnerability duration, it doesn't decrease the extra invulnerability duration received from being timed to the Backbeat.
- Shields restored and overshields generated still scales off of total Health, not Shields, allowing an extra degree of synergy by using Vitality or Archon Vitality.
