# Temple/Abilities (wiki)

## Passive

Temple tracks their rhythmic mayhem by keeping in sync with the Backbeat metronome, shown below their aiming reticle. The metronome's pendulum swings from left to right and vice-versa at a pace of 78 BPM, while passing through a central zone that doubles as a fuel gauge for Lizzie, the living Techrot guitar wielded during Exalted Solo.

When in the zone, the pendulum's bulb above its needle lights up. Cast an ability while the Backbeat is in the zone to amplify Temple's ability with a 50% Ability Efficiency, fuel up the gauge by 55 ammunition, and earn an extra effect specific to each ability:

- Pyrotechnics: 2x additional flame pillars.
- Overdrive: 2x Critical Chance vulnerability.
- Ripper's Wail: 4 second Invulnerability duration.
- Additionally striking enemies with Pyrotechnics or Overdrive, or recasting Ripper's Wail, increases its weapon Heat damage bonus by 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% up to a maximum of 750% Heat damage and extend its duration by 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 seconds up to a maximum of 60 seconds.

- Exalted Solo: Energy cost reduced to 0.
- Sonic sigils become larger and inflict 3 guaranteed Viral (1 ), Magnetic (2 ), Cold (3 ), or Corrosive (4 ) status effects.

- Successful Backbeat timings play a crowd cheering sound.

Infused abilities can trigger Backbeat.

Enabling the "Metronome Audio" option in Temple's ability screen will have timed backbeats casts play up to seven ticking sounds when the pendulum crosses the central zone.

Main article: Rhythm Guard

Info

- Temple expends 25 energy and shreds their guitar causing 3 / 3 / 4 / 5 pillars to erupt from below enemies inside a 20 meter radius around the aiming reticle, from up to 25 meters away. Each pillar inflicts 550 / 700 / 850 / 1,000 Physical damage evenly split between Impact, Puncture, and Slash damage, with 100% Heat Status Chance.
- Due to damage quantization, the base damage is actually 1031.25 in game (343.75 for each of the three damage types). The guaranteed heat proc will scale with the full 1000 base damage however.
- Damage is treated as weapon damage and is therefore affected by universal buffs like Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
- Pyrotechnics has 0% critical chance which, unlike other weapon damage abilities, cannot be buffed even with flat bonuses like Arcane Avenger.

- Pyrotechnics auto-targets enemies in the reticle radius and a frontal cone before Temple, by spawning one fire pillar under each enemy in sight. Otherwise, the pillars randomly erupt at the reticle location. When targeting the ground under an enemy, multiple pillars can hit the same enemy to inflict multiple Heat damage and statuses.

Kit Interactions

- Time the Backbeat to grant Pyrotechnics a 50% Ability Efficiency bonus, fuel up 55 ammo for Lizzie, and create 2x more fire pillars.
- Time the Backbeat and strike enemies to increase Ripper's Wail weapon Heat damage bonus by 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% up to a maximum of 750% Heat damage and extend its duration by 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 seconds up to a maximum of 60 seconds.

Helminth

- Subsuming Temple to the Helminth will offer Pyrotechnics to be used by other Warframes.

Info

- Temple expends 50 energy and rocks out where they stand for 3 seconds, becoming Invulnerable and restoring 50% of their Health and Shields per second that overflow into Overshields. - Ripper's Wail damage bonus percentage is shown as a buff icon beside Temple and their allies' hitpoints indicator.

Tips & Tricks

- While increasing casting speed does decrease total healing received as well as the initial invulnerability duration, it doesn't decrease the extra invulnerability duration received from being timed to the Backbeat.
- Shields restored and overshields generated still scales off of total Health, not Shields, allowing an extra degree of synergy by using Vitality or Archon Vitality.

Info

- Temple expends 75 energy to holster away their primary weapon to summon the Exalted Weapon flamethrower Lizzie. Press the ability button again to deactivate the ability and return Temple's primary weapon for use.
- Lizzie has its own unique statistics:
- Primary fire shoots a continuous stream of fire.
- Base damage is 85 pure Heat, with a damage multiplier of 1x / 1.08x / 1.15x / 1.25x. Damage multiplier is additive to base damage mods such as Serration and Galvanized Aptitude.
- Shots have a 35% critical chance, 2.3x critical multiplier, 35% status chance, 8.0 fire rate, 10 meter beam length, and a magazine size of 275.

- While aiming Lizzie (default hold RMB ), Temple's four abilities change to four Elemental sonic sigils: Viral, Magnetic, Cold, and Corrosive. Press the respective ability buttons (default 1 , 2 , 3 , and 4 ) to expend 2 Backbeat ammo to launch the sonic sigil.
- All sonic sigils have a base damage of 100 pure Viral/ Magnetic/ Cold/ Corrosive, with a damage multiplier of 1x / 1.08x / 1.15x / 1.25x.
- All sonic sigils have a 15% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, 25% status chance, 5.33 fire rate, and 1.4 meter punch through.
- All sonic sigils inflict a guaranteed status proc of their respective element and Impact.
- Sonic sigils do not have cooldowns between casts and can be launched consecutively like playing notes on a guitar.

- Lizzie can be equipped with primary mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Sonic sigils will combine their damage with modded elements. For example, the Cold sigil can become Viral if modded with Toxin. However it will still inflict forced Cold and Impact procs.
- Can equip Sinister Reach.
- Riven Mods are not generated for Lizzie.
- Because Lizzie is counted as a primary, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Rifle Amp and Arcane Rage.

Weapon

Main article: Lizzie Lizzie is Temple's Exalted Flamethrower, summoned by activating the ability Exalted Solo. Her primary fire shoots streams of Heat, while aiming down sights and casting abilities fire Viral, Magnetic, Cold, or Corrosive projectiles.
- This weapon deals pure Heat damage.
- Primary fire shoots a continuous stream of Heat.
- The zone of impact or maximum beam range has a spherical blast radius of 4 meters, allowing it to hit multiple enemies at once.
- Can benefit from Firestorm ( Primed).
- Area of effect does not inflict self-stagger and has no Damage Falloff.
- Multishot has no effect on the radial damage like other AoE Continuous Weapons.

- While aiming, casting abilities fires a Viral (1 ), Magnetic (2 ), Cold (3 ), or Corrosive (4 ) sonic projectile, with a guaranteed status effect of their respective element and Impact.
- Cost 2 ammo to fire.
- All sonic projectiles have innate 1.4 meter punch through.
- Time the Backbeat to apply 3 guaranteed status effects.

Advantages:

- Pinpoint accuracy.
- Heavy Caliber's accuracy penalty is minimal on her.

Disadvantages:

- Requires timed Backbeat ability casts to charge 55 ammo. Exalted Solo cannot be used when the fuel gauge is empty.
- Limited range of 10 meters.
- Can be increased with Sinister Reach.

Kit Interactions

- Time the Backbeat when casting Pyrotechnics, Overdrive, and Ripper's Wail to fill 55 ammo charges for Lizzie. 5 Backbeat casts is needed to fully charge the fuel gauge.
- Exalted Solo cannot be used if the fuel gauge is empty.

- Time the Backbeat to Exalted Solo to reduce its energy cost to 0.
- Time the Backbeat to Exalted Solo's sonic sigils to inflict 3 status procs of their respective element and Impact.
