Info

- Temple expends 25 energy to summon twin flying Techrot speakers set to insane volumes to blast enemies in a 100° frontal cone over a range of 14 / 16 / 18 / 20 meters. Enemies wince in ear pain for 2 seconds and receive 300 / 450 / 600 / 750 Heat damage, then carry surround sound wavelengths that apply 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% final Critical Chance Vulnerability for 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 seconds.

Kit Interactions

- Time the Backbeat to grant Overdrive a 50% Ability Efficiency bonus, fuel up 55 ammo for Lizzie, and apply double the critical chance vulnerability.
- Time the Backbeat and strike enemies to increase Ripper's Wail weapon Heat damage bonus by 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% up to a maximum of 750% Heat damage and extend its duration by 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 seconds up to a maximum of 60 seconds.
