CODEX
Achieve redemption through vengeance. The monastic Harrow increases damage to support allies. Defy enemies and ascend.

Release Date: June 28th, 2017

Dauntless and devout, the dark priest Harrow bestows upon his coven three virtues: salvation through sacrifice, redemption through vengeance, and ascension through defiance. Nemeses beware, for only death awaits. Enchain the tainted, silence their heresy, and deliver their tormented souls unto the Void's embrace. Harrow was absolved in Update 21.0 (2017-06-29).

Harrow is a monastic Warframe that provides damage and support. His Passive doubles the amount of Overshields he can gain and enters missions with full Energy. Condemn releases chains that binds enemies and restores his Shields. Penance increases Fire Rate and Reload Speed and grants Harrow lifesteal for himself and allies. Thurible channels a ritual to recover energy on kill, with bonus energy from headshots. Covenant protects Harrow and his allies with brief Invulnerability, after which absorbed damage is converted into increased Critical Chance, further amplified for headshots.

## Notes

- If Harrow wields his signature Knell/ Knell Prime, the weapon's magazine size is increased by 1 round, for a total of 2 rounds.
- Currently, Scourge/ Scourge Prime lacks a Signature Weapon bonus with Harrow.

- Due to his passive doubling overshield capacity, Harrow possesses the fourth highest base total shield value of all Warframes (2,955), behind Hildryn, Hildryn Prime and Harrow Prime.

## Tips

- Harrow's ability kit allows him to make better use of Critical-focused weapons, especially ones that benefit from headshots: Condemn binds enemies and exposes their heads, Penance increases weapon reload speed and fire rate, and Covenant greatly increases Critical Chance especially on headshots.
- The self-synergizing nature of Harrow's kit makes using Helminth more difficult than other frames and overall less necessary. Generally, he can slot in an alternate shield-restoring or damaging ability over Condemn or Penance respectively.
- Pillage can be used to strip armor.
- Thermal Sunder can restore energy in tandem with Thurible, making it highly spammable.
