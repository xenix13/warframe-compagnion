## Passive

This section is transcluded from Sirius & Orion/Abilities/Passive. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.

As one and the same from different alternate timelines, Sirius & Orion exhibit numerous unique properties and interactions, highlighted in the tabs below:

## The Duo Sons

- One of the two Warframes appear as the Primary Son. The Primary Son depends on which name was chosen at the end of Jade Shadows, but can be freely changed after completing Jade Shadows: Constellations via Pontis Tower. If Jade Shadows is not completed and the Warframe is acquired through other means (such as being purchased with Platinum), Sirius becomes the default Primary Son.
  - The Primary Son is the default Warframe the player controls.
  - The Secondary Son is the default uncontrolled Warframe.
    - Only the Primary Son will be shown on the login screen, cutscenes (such as mission entry and exit), the end of mission screen, and Teshin's Cave selection pedestal if they are generated for Duviri. The Secondary Son will be present in the Base of Operations and in Hubs, but absent from any event that disables their Passive (such as Dog Days).

## The Unchosen

- The uncontrolled Warframe becomes an Invulnerable AI-controlled ally similar to a Specter with their own visible Health, Shield and Energy bars.
  - The uncontrolled Warframe attacks nearby enemies with his equipped weapon and frequently casts all his abilities except Celestial Clash. However, the uncontrolled Warframe has a 0.25x damage multiplier applied to all their weapons, reducing their damage.
    - The uncontrolled Warframe cannot use the same weapon as the controlled Warframe, and will swap as necessary. It prefers a ranged weapon over a Melee weapon, and will be unarmed if there is only one weapon in the loadout.
    - When in close range to enemies, the uncontrolled Warframe will switch to a melee weapon if one is in the loadout and not currently in use by the controlled Warframe.
  - If the controlled Warframe enters Bleedout, both Sirius and Orion will be downed.
  - In Archwing missions, the uncontrolled Warframe will be entirely absent.
  - If an Archwing Launcher, K-Drive Launcher, Necramech Summon, Kaithe Summon, or Atomicycle Summon are used, the uncontrolled Warframe will not enter a vehicle of their own and attempt to follow on foot.
    - Additionally, hold-casting the vehicle's abilities does not swap to the uncontrolled Warframe.
  - If Transference is used, the uncontrolled Warframe will still continue to fight.
  - Squad Restores will only affect the controlled Warframe.
  - When using Emotes, the uncontrolled Warframe will perform the same emote. The sons can also perform Handshakes with each other.

## Switching

- Holding down the ability inputs (default 1 , 2 , 3 ) causes the player to become briefly Invulnerable as the uncontrolled Warframe kicks aside the controlled Warframe, switches player control to the other Warframe, and casts their own version of the ability instead. For example, when controlling Sirius, his first ability will tap-cast Coronal Ejection, while hold-casting will switch to Orion and cast Gravitic Slash.
  - Carriable objects (e.g. Mobile Defense datamass, Excavation power cells, Disruption keys) stay with the original Warframe when swapping, but will be dropped by the uncontrolled Warframe if it switches to a Primary weapon. This can be prevented either with a loadout that lacks a Primary weapon, or by ensuring the controlled Warframe always has the Primary weapon active whenever enemies are nearby.
    - The negative effects of a Defixio will apply to whichever Warframe is holding it.
  - If an Archgun Deployer is used and then switches control, the Arch-gun will still be equipped on the Warframe that used the deployer.
    - Of note, this does not bar you from redeploying an Archgun, though aggression on the uncontrolled warframe may temporarily stop until swapped to and from that warframe.
  - Switching Warframes carries the same energy cost as the ability used to switch. (e.g. switching from Orion to Sirius by holding 1 will cause Orion to lose 15 energy, as if he had casted Gravitic Slash, and for Sirius to lose no energy)
- Switching between Sirius and Orion provides a +45% Ability Efficiency bonus for the next two abilities, lasting until used.
  - Switching while the Efficiency bonus is active will refill the charges to 2 max.
  - Switch during Celestial Clash does not activate this Passive.
- When the controlled Warframe falls below 50 energy, it automatically steals energy from the uncontrolled Warframe as much as possible to get back up to the aforementioned threshold.
  - Energy stealing occurs when falling below the threshold after (e.g.:) casting an ability, or getting hit by an Energy Leech Eximus.
  - Energy stealing cannot happen if the uncontrolled Warframe has no energy
  - The uncontrolled Warframe's energy pool flashes brightly when the stealing occurs.
- Switching applies a refresh to all Maneuver limits, including bullet jumps, double jumps, rolls, and the air-glide timer.
  - This allows for potentially endless flight, only limited by energy consumption spent on casting abilities to switch.
  - Airborne maneuverability can help maximize the effectiveness of Event Horizon.

- Sirius & Orion utilizes separate Upgrade loadouts from each other, allowing the player to slot in a different Aura, Arcane Enhancements, and Mods for customization according to their own attributes.
  - Both Sons can receive Forma, Omni Forma, Umbra Forma, and Exilus Warframe Adapter.
    - Sirius can install a Focus Lens, but Orion can not regardless of the player's chosen Primary Son.
    - Orion comes with a pre-installed Orokin Reactor.
  - Equipping two of the same Aura on both Warframes do not stack.
  - Conditional effects (e.g. Arcane Fury) cannot be triggered by the uncontrolled Warframe, nor can he trigger conditional Arcanes for himself.
  - Passive effects (e.g. Arcane Battery) or already built-up stacks (e.g. Molt Augmented) are retained by the uncontrolled Warframe.
  - Power Donation affects both Warframes; the Warframe with it equipped will lose Ability Strength, while the other gains it.
  - The effect of Coaction Drift functions as though it was two separate players.
    - This means if both brothers has it installed, both will have a resulting effect of 1.3225, or 1.15 due to both being effected by the 1.15 Aura Strength and 1.15 Aura Effectiveness.
    - This also means if only one brother has Coaction Drift installed, the aura from the brother without Coaction Drift will only get the 1.15 Aura Effectiveness on the brother with it installed, and will not effect the aura on that brother itself. Likewise, the aura from the brother with Coaction Drift installed will have the 1.15 Aura Strength applied to the other brother without Coaction Drift, and the brother with it installed will get their own aura at 1.3225 from both buffs, just like a normal frame.
  - Rolling Guard tracks separate cooldown timers if installed on both Sirius and Orion.
  - Helminth abilities can only be subsumed on the Primary Son.
    - If the Primary Son is swapped, any Helminth ability present will be replaced with the default ability.
      - Swapping back will restore the infused ability.
    - Note that subsuming over Celestial Clash will disable Celestial Clash for both Sons.
    - If the subsumed ability has a hold/tap mechanic, the hold to swap mechanic of the replaced ability will be overwritten. For example, if Eclipse is placed over Orion's Gravitic Slash, Eclipse's damage buff and reduction can be used, but the first ability can no longer switch to Sirius and cast Coronal Ejection.
  - Archon Shards and Decrees are shared between Sirius and Orion.
- Both Warframes gain Affinity simultaneously.
  - The Secondary Son does not award Mastery Rank Points.
- When Sirius & Orion is the player's equipped Warframe, the Secondary Son is available for customization with its own Upgrade, Appearance, and Abilities tabs in the Arsenal loadout screen.
- Primary, Secondary, and Melee Weapons and their Upgrades and Arcanes are shared between Sirius and Orion.
  - Weapon Appearance tabs (A, B, C, etc) select two adjacent slots, one for Sirius and one for Orion, indicated by their names above the tabs. The player can also copy the Primary Son's colors, or the current Son's colors, to that weapon.
- Regardless of the weapon's Accuracy stat, the unchosen frame's weapon fire will be very inaccurate.
- Weapon Arcanes that proc from applying status effects can sometimes activate on weapon fire from the uncontrolled Warframe. Cascadia Flare, Conjunction Voltage, and Primary Frostbite can be activated, because they do not have a weapon slot-specific requirement to activate.
  - Secondary Encumber can also be activated.
  - However, Arcanes that only activate on primary or secondary fire, like Primary Blight, Primary Exhilarate and Secondary Cryogenic, cannot.
- Weapon damage from the uncontrolled Warframe is not considered ability damage, and cannot trigger Archon Mods or Arcane Hot Shot.

When casting abilities, Sirius & Orion ignite Constellation Stars displayed above the ability icons. Casting a Sirius ability ignites a green round star, while casting an Orion ability ignites a red diamond star; only 2 of the same-colored stars can be created consecutively and up to 7 stars can be created in total. Stars may be spent using Celestial Clash, where matching the ability inputs with the green star/Sirius (LMB ) or red star/Orion (RMB ) results in more damage and higher Critical Chance.

- Celestial Clash can only be cast with at least 1 Constellation Star.
- Once 7 Constellations Stars have been acquired, the crosses on the Constellation Stars and smaller stars will light up.
- Constellation Stars matching their Sons inherit their primary energy color.

Subsumable to Helminth

Info

- Sirius expends 50 energy to summon a constellation array of 7 Jade Light motes behind himself and Orion for 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 seconds. Using weapons and abilities, attack an enemy to launch a mote toward them, inflicting 350 / 400 / 450 / 500 Heat damage with 100% Status Chance in a 4 meter radius. Once launched, motes shuffle from the right to left of the array, and replenish one every 0.2 seconds.
  - Enemy targeting and explosion radius both require Line of Sight.
  - Can be recast while active to refresh duration and instantly replenish all motes.
  - Motes will not launch if the target is over 50 meters away.
  - Motes can directly miss and fly past the target. Missed motes can directly hit enemies behind the target, if they were close before the mote disappears.
  - Motes launched by Orion use the modded ability stats of Sirius.
    - However, Archon Vitality on Sirius does not add an extra instance of Heat status effect when Orion's motes proc Heat status effects (and vice versa).
      - Equipping Archon Vitality on Orion grants him an extra instance of Heat status effect on his motes.
  - Motes are affected by some Universal Weapon Bonuses:
    - Not affected by base damage bonuses like Arcane Arachne and Vigorous Swap, or damage multipliers like Eclipse.
    - Are affected by certain elemental damage bonuses, such as Nourish and Valence Formation (see Elemental Damage section for full list).
    - Base 0% critical chance can be increased by using flat bonuses, such as Arcane Avenger or Covenant.
    - Base 1.0x critical multiplier can be increased by using effects like Arcane Crepuscular, Tenacious Bond, or Grave Spirit.
- Damage from abilities and weapons will launch motes at struck enemies. Abilities can trigger multiple mote launches, while weapons will usually only launch one mote per attack.
  - Area-of-effect abilities that strike multiple enemies at once will cause multiple motes to fly out, up to the maximum 7.
    - Abilities that move enemies while dealing damage, like Nezha's Divine Spears, may cause some of the motes to miss their target.
  - AoE abilities that leave a lingering damaging field, like Ember's Inferno or Oberon's Hallowed Ground, will cause motes to launch at every enemy damaged by the field, on every damage tick, only limited by the mote spawn rate.
  - Abilities that rapidly deal damage ticks, like Xaku's Grasp of Lohk, will cause a mote to launch every time it deals damage to a target.
  - Weapon fire with Punch Through will usually only launch one mote.
    - Weapons with Infinite Body Punch Through projectiles, like the Arca Plasmor and Lex Incarnon, can trigger 2 motes to launch if about 0.2 seconds elapse between the projectile hitting targets.
  - Weapon fire with area-of-effect damage seem to only launch one mote.
    - This also applies to Exalted Weapons with area-of-effect damage, like Jade's Glory.
  - Weapon fire with Multishot will only launch one mote, even when striking multiple enemies.
  - Striking with a melee weapon will only launch one mote, even for strikes that hit multiple enemies, including Heavy and slam attacks.
    - This also applies to projectiles created by the Incarnon form of melee weapons which have AoE damage, like Ceramic Dagger Incarnon.
    - Lingering damaging fields created by melee weapons like the Furax Incarnon will usually launch a mote per damage tick dealt by the field, with a maximum of one per tick.
  - Motes are not launched by abilities and weapon fire from specters or clones, including the uncontrolled Warframe.
    - This causes reduced or no synergy with abilities that rely on specters, like Styanax's Final Stand or Mirage's Hall of Mirrors.
  - Motes will not be launched by most status effect damage.
    - Detonation of Blast status effects cause one mote to be launched.
  - Motes will not be launched when an attack is fatal.

Kit Interactions

- Coronal Ejection, Gravitic Slash, Astral Shell's decoy detonation, Event Horizon's black hole collapse, and Celestial Clash trigger Jade Stars to attack enemies.
- Holding this ability changes control to Orion who executes Astral Shell instead.

Info

- Orion expends 50 energy to conjure a miniature black hole that drifts toward the reticle. Event Horizon's black hole lasts 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 seconds, spans a 5 / 5 / 8 / 8 meter radius that Ragdoll all enemies and pulls them into its umbral core. Upon expiry or recast, the black hole collapses explosively to inflict 300 / 450 / 600 / 750 Heat damage in a 8 / 10 / 11 / 12 meter radius. Only 1 Event Horizon may be active at once.
  - Event Horizon has infinite Punch Through against entities and rebounds away from surfaces on contact. Melee weapon hits do not alter the black hole's trajectory.
  - Holding this ability changes control to Sirius who executes Light's Sanctuary instead.
  - When cast while stationary, moving sideways or backwards, Event Horizon drastically slows its travel speed. When cast while moving forward (default W ), Event Horizon accelerates toward the reticle and slows down after a few seconds.
  - If the black hole phases into terrain or objects and cannot exit, it will prematurely collapse.
  - Enemies dragged along can collide with objects and terrain, getting left behind if stuck on the obstacles.
  - Event Horizon can group enemies together which allows Orion or Sirius to gain massive amount of Combo.
- Can be recast while active to instantly collapse the old black hole and conjure a new one.

Kit Interactions

- Hitting Event Horizon with Gravitic Slash causes it to be pushed away toward the reticle, while extending Event Horizon's duration by 5 seconds.
  - Duration extention per cast is capped at 5 seconds, achievable with 100% Ability Duration. As such only values below 100% will have effect.
  - Uncontrolled Orion's Gravitic Slash does not push Event Horizon.
- Hitting Event Horizon with Coronal Ejection causes it to be quickly pulled toward Sirius, while extending Event Horizon's duration by 5 seconds.
  - Duration extention per cast is capped at 5 seconds, achievable with 100% Ability Duration. As such only values below 100% will have effect.
  - Extention is based on Orion's Ability Duration, not Sirius's.
  - Uncontrolled Sirius' Coronal Ejection does not pull Event Horizon.
- The black hole collapse triggers Jade Stars to attack enemies.

 | 4 100 | Celestial Clash Sirius & Orion take to the skies in a cosmic duel. Each attack consumes a Constellation Star to inflict colossal collateral Heat or Slash damage. Match the star's color to the Warframes color to gain increased Critical Chance. |LEFT_ATTACK| Sirius attacks |RIGHT_ATTACK| Orion attacks Generate Constellation Stars by using abilities. No more than two stars of the same color can be added in a row. Introduced in Update 43.0 (2026-06-17) | Strength:7,000 / 8,000 / 9,000 / 10,000 ( Heat or Slash Damage)
Duration:N/A
Range:20 / 22 / 24 / 26 m
Misc: 50% (critical chance / matched hit) +1.5x (damage bonus per matched hit) 100% (Sirius Heat status chance) 100% (Orion Slash status chance)

Info

- Sirius or Orion expends 100 energy to gain aerial movement, preparing to clash with each strike dealing 7,000 / 8,000 / 9,000 / 10,000 Heat or Slash damage in a 20 / 22 / 24 / 26 meter radius to enemies in Line of Sight. The reticle changes to a green semicircle and a red triangle that lights up in response to the Passive Constellation Stars: press the fire button (default LMB ) to have Sirius attack with Heat damage with 100% Status Chance and press the aiming button (default RMB ) to have Orion attack with Slash damage with 100% Status Chance, both dashing toward the location on the reticle while becoming Invulnerable as they collide. Press the ability button again (default 4 ) to cancel Celestial Clash without expending Constellation Stars.
  - Celestial Clash can only be cast with at least one Constellation Star, which is filled by casting other abilities. No more than 2 stars of the same color can be added in a row and up to a maximum of 7 stars can be created.
    - If an eighth Constellation Star would be created, the oldest one is discarded.
    - Constellation Stars are expended in the inverse order they are created.
  - If either Warframe is unable to cast Celestial Clash or has their abilities nullified, Celestial Clash will be interrupted.
    - If an ability is infused over either Warframe's instance of Celestial Clash, neither Warframe will be able to cast it.
  - Attacks move the controlled Warframe up to 19 meters in the direction of the reticle, teleporting the uncontrolled Warframe to the end position. Then, the uncontrolled Warframe is sent up to 19 meters in the direction of the reticle.
    - These distances are not affected by any stats.
  - Attacking with the uncontrolled Warframe will switch control to them.
  - Celestial Clash has a base damage multiplier of 1.0x, base Critical Chance of 0% and base Critical Multiplier of 2.0x. Matching the Constellation Stars to the attacker has the attack gain an additional stacking +50% Critical Chance and +1.5x damage multiplier. Matching the first hit therefore applies a 2.5x damage multiplier, then 4.0x on the second hit and so on up to 11.5x on the final hit.
    - These are not affected by any Ability stats.
    - If all 7 Constellation Stars are matched, the ability will end with both sons clasping their scythes then punching each other for a final damaging explosion. This is otherwise functionally identical to any other attack.
    - If a Constellation Star is not matched, the smaller stars on the Passive HUD will dim.
    - Critical Damage is only affected by Arcane Crepuscular.
    - Critical Chance cannot be modified, even with external Buffs or Status Effects.
  - Energy is only consumed for the Warframe that cast Celestial Clash.
    - If the Ability ends before any attacks are performed, the casting cost is refunded.
  - The stats of Celestial Clash are taken from the Son performing each individual hit, regardless of who initiated the ability.
    - Both Warframes snapshot their personal Ability Strength when initiating Celestial Clash.
  - Animations for starting Celestial Clash, attacking, and ending the ability are affected by Casting Speed.
    - For attacks, the Casting Speed of the current Warframe is used, instead of the one attacking.
- While active, the controlled Warframe gains vertical and horizontal flight capabilities using the movement (W A S D ), crouch (Ctrl ), and jump (Spacebar ) keys, similar to Archwing Maneuvers. Since the player stays at one height unless manually ascending or descending, it is almost identical to the movement within Jade's Glory on High. They can also teleport (Shift ) a short distance toward the reticle while carrying momentum. The uncontrolled Warframe remains in place.
  - Flight speed is 12 meters per second, affected by the controlled Warframe's Movement Speed. Flight is not considered to be sprinting and is unaffected by Sprint Speed.
  - Teleportation distance is 14 meters, affected by the controlled Warframe's Parkour Velocity. Teleportation speed is affected by the controlled Warframe's Dodge Speed.
  - Teleportation is treated as a Roll and can be done directionally by holding a movement input.
- While active, Sirius and Orion cannot cast other abilities, use weapons, or perform Context Actions. Pickups can still be collected.
  - Transference can still be used during Celestial Clash.
- Clash explosions destroy Storage Containers, ignoring Line of Sight.

Kit Interactions

- Celestial Clash can trigger Jade Stars to attack enemies.
