# Coronal Ejection (capacité de Sirius & Orion) (wiki)

Info

- Sirius expends 15 energy to hurl his Pride scythe infused with Jade Light, inflicting 750 / 1,000 / 1,250 / 1,500 Heat damage with 100% Status Chance to all enemies directly hit by the scythe. Pride travels up to ? meters or until it strikes a surface, then boomerangs back toward Sirius; pickups within a 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 meter radius from Pride are vacuumed along then collected by Sirius upon his scythe's return.
- Enemies can be struck multiple times by Coronal Ejection, once when the scythe is thrown and again when the scythe boomerangs back along their path.
- Pride always travels back to Sirius, even after switching with Orion.
- When cast by uncontrolled Sirius, pickups are deposited at Sirius' feet. Only the controlled Warframe can collect pickups.

- Can be recast while active to throw another scythe, but all in-flight scythes disappear when the first returns to Sirius.

Kit Interactions

- Holding this ability changes control to Orion who executes Gravitic Slash instead.
- Coronal Ejection can trigger Jade Stars to attack enemies.
- Hitting Event Horizon with Coronal Ejection causes it to be quickly pulled toward Sirius, while extending Event Horizon's duration by 5 seconds.
- Duration extention per cast is capped at 5 seconds, achievable with 100% Ability Duration. As such only values below 100% will have effect.
- Extension is based on Orion's Ability Duration, not Sirius's.
- Uncontrolled Sirius' Coronal Ejection does not pull Event Horizon.
