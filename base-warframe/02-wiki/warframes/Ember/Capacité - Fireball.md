# Fireball (capacité de Ember) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fireball

| 1 25 | Fireball Release a fiery projectile that ignites enemies on contact. Introduced in Vanilla (2012-10-25) | Strength:300 / 500 / 600 / 800 ( Heat damage on impact) 100 / 200 / 250 / 300 ( Heat area damage) 

Duration:1.5 s (combo window) 

Range:3 m (radius) 

Misc: 100% ( Heat status chance) 8.0x (combo damage multiplier cap) +1%/s (heat generation rate bonus for Immolation) 200% (damage increase) at max Immolation. 

Info

- Ember expends 25 Energy to launch a fiery projectile that inflicts 300 / 500 / 600 / 800 Heat damage on direct hit. Upon impact, the projectile explodes within a 3 meter radius to inflict 100 / 200 / 250 / 300 Heat damage with 100% status chance.
- Direct hit can perform Headshots and get the respective damage bonus. The direct hit cannot proc Heat status.
- AOE effect cannot perform Headshots. Can proc Heat status.

- Fireball can be recast within a 1.5 second window to perform a repeating combo chain, increasing the damage of successive casts.
- The game treats the ability's base damage as 400 for the direct hit and 150 for the AoE. These are the base values that are multiplied by the combo multiplier.
- The combo multiplier represents the additional damage being added onto the base damage, not the actual damage multiplier that will be seen.
- For example, a 4x combo multiplier will add 400% extra damage, for an actual damage multiplier of 5x. This will result in 5 x 400 = 2000 direct damage and 5 x 150 = 750 AoE damage.

- Before any Fireballs have been cast, the combo multiplier starts at 1x, meaning the ability will actually do 200% base damage, for a total of 800 direct damage and 300 AoE.
- Each successive cast in the chain increases the current combo multiplier by 1x, then 2x, then 4x. The resulting combo multipliers are 1x (no combo), 2x (with 1 previous cast), 4x (2 casts), and 8x (3+ casts). The resulting damage multipliers would therefore be 2x / 3x / 5x / 9x.
- Fireball base damage at max combo is equal to 9 * 400 = 3600 direct hit damage and 9 * 150 = 1350 AoE damage.

- The Immolation Heat meter adds up to 4x to the base combo multiplier. This combo bonus is based on the current Heat level, so for example 75% Heat would only add 3x to the starting combo multiplier.
- With maxed Immolation Heat, Fireball's combo multiplier will therefore be 5x / 6x / 8x / 12x, for a total damage multiplier of 6x / 7x / 9x / 13x.
- Fireball base damage at max combo and Heat is equal to 13 * 400 = 5200 direct hit damage and 13 * 150 = 1950 AoE damage.

- Fireball damage can be calculated as follows:
- Direct Damage = 400 * (combo multiplier + 1)
- AoE Damage = 150 * (combo multiplier + 1)
- On first cast, the combo multiplier is hidden but is equal to 1 + (4 * Immolation Heat percentage), resulting in 1x combo at 0% Heat and 5x combo at 100% Heat.

- Projectile has flight time and will continue to travel until it hits a solid object or enemy.
- Direct hit and AoE damage can benefit from Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
- Will not disrupt Shade's cloak.
- Casting Fireball is a One-Handed Action that allows the player to perform attacks, movement and parkour Maneuvers.
- Has a delay of 0.5 seconds between uses.

Kit Interactions

- Each cast of Fireball increases Immolation's heat generation by 1% per second.
- Immolation adds up to 4x (based on Heat level) to Fireball's combo multiplier.
- At max Heat, this increases Fireball's minimum damage from 800 to 2400 on direct hit and 300 to 900 on AOE.
- At max Heat and 3+ consecutive casts, this increases Fireball's maximum damage from 3600 to 5200 on direct hit and 1350 to 1950 on AOE.

Augment

Main article: Fireball Frenzy
Fireball Frenzy is a Warframe Augment Mod for Ember that allows Fireball to, instead of charging, be held on cast, creating a wave of energy traveling outward from the user that temporarily grants the caster and nearby allies additional Heat damage to all attacks.

Tips & Tricks

- Does not need directly hit the enemy to build combo.
- Reaches the maximum combo at the 3rd cast.
- Fireball can be affected by Arcane Arachne and Vigorous Swap as final multipliers.
- Can be used as a cheap form of crowd control.
- Modding for high efficiency through the use of Fleeting Expertise can allow for this ability to be spammed, quickly building up the combo multiplier and damage over time.
