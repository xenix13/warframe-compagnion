# Vauban/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vauban/Abilities

## Passive

An inventor by nature, the process of creation necessitates demolition and deconstruction. Vauban inflicts 25% bonus damage with his weapons and abilities against enemies that are incapacitated.

- Enemies are considered incapacitated from:
- Electricity status effect's stun, including from an attached Tesla Nervos
- Heat status effect's panicking
- Cold status effect's frozen on 10 stacks
- Note that enemies must be in the crowd control animation, simply having the status effect does not count.

- Minelayer's Tether-Flechette Orb coil
- Bastille's lifting prison
- Bastille's vortex gravity pull

- Knockdown and Stagger do not count as incapacitation.
- Damage bonus stacks with Minelayer's Overdriver buff for weapon damage only.
- Damage bonus applies multiplicatively to total damage, according to this formula: Damage × (1 + 0.25)
- Damage bonus applies a second time on status damage, if the damaging status effect is applied while an enemy is incapacitated.
- For example, with a weapon that inflicts 100 damage per shot, that procs Slash, Vauban's passive will increase the damage per tick from the slash to: 0.35 × 100 × (1 + 0.25) × (1 + 0.25) = 54.6875.
- The damage bonus is multiplicative to Faction Damage Bonus and to Minelayer's Overdriver buff. For example, with a Vauban modded with maxed Intensify, and a weapon that inflicts 100 damage per shot, modded with maxed Serration, maxed Bane of Orokin, that procs a slash status, on an incapacitated orokin enemy, Vauban's passive will increase the damage per tick from the slash to: 0.35 × 100 × (1 + 1.65) × (1 + 0.3) × (1 + 0.3) × (1 + 0.25 × (1 + 0.3)) × (1 + 0.25) × (1 + 0.25) = 324.5163.
- If a damaging status effect is applied before the enemy hit is incapacitated, the damage bonus will apply only once. Similarly, the damage bonus for damaging status effects on an enemy is lost should the enemy no longer be incapacitated.

Info

- Vauban expends 25 energy to throw a capsule in an arcing trajectory toward the aiming reticle, releasing a Tesla Nervos on impact with a surface. Upon directly striking an enemy, the capsule inflicts 15 Blast damage with ~10% status chance. The Tesla Nervos is an invulnerable Latcher Specter that follows Vauban and seeks to latch onto a nearby enemy, shocking the target and stunning it in place as long as the Tesla Nervos remains attached. The stunned target receives 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 Electricity damage per second, while discharging an electric burst every 1 second to inflict 80 / 100 / 120 / 150 Electricity damage with 50% Status Chance to all enemies within a 4 / 4 / 5 / 6 meter radius; each Tesla Nervos holds 5 / 6 / 7 / 10 electrical charges that last until used.
- If the stunned target is killed before the Tesla Nervos uses up its charges, the Tesla Nervos will detach and look for another enemy.
- If the Tesla Nervos uses up all of its charges, it will dissipate and release its stunned target.
- Tesla Nervos will teleport to Vauban provided it is too far away and not currently attached to an enemy.
- Multiple Tesla Nervos can attach to the same enemy target.

- Holding down the ability key (default 1 ) causes Vauban to juggle up to 4 Tesla Nervos capsules once fully charged. Release the hotkey to throw and scatter them toward the direction of the aiming reticle, deploying multiple drones in one cast for no extra energy cost.
- Deploying new Tesla Nervos past the drone limit causes older drones to dissipate, even when they are still attached to enemies.
- Number of active drones is displayed on the ability icon.

- Can be recast while active to deploy new drones with full charges.
- While Tesla Nervos are moving, they emit a mechanical clicking sound to indicate their presence.
- Tesla Nervos do not show minimap icons or waypoints to track their position.
- Tesla Nervos features a unique model apart from the Grineer Roller and Latcher. Vauban's Appearance colors affect the Tesla Nervos' appearance as well.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies stunned by Tesla Nervos passively receive 25% increased damage from Vauban's weapons and abilities.
- Tesla Nervos is affected by Minelayer's Vector-Overdive Pad and will be propelled toward the pad's direction on contact.
- Tesla Nervos can attach to enemies already affected by Minelayer's Tether-Flechette Orb and Bastille.

Helminth

- Subsuming Vauban to the Helminth will offer Tesla Nervos and its augments to be used by other Warframes.

Info

- Vauban expends 25 Energy to throw a specialized mine. Tap the ability key to throw a Tether-Flechette Orb. Hold the ability key to throw a Vector-Overdrive Pad. Upon striking an enemy, the mine inflicts 15 Blast damage with a ~10% status chance; the mine lasts for 25 seconds once deployed.
- Mine selection can be inverted in the Options menu.

Tether-Flechette Orb

- Tether-Flechette Orb deploys upon contact with an ally, enemy or surface, sticking onto the first entity or object it touches. Once stuck, the coil does the following:
- Extends energy wires that inflict 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 Slash damage, Ragdoll and pull in up to 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 enemies within sight and 15 / 16 / 18 / 20 meters from the coil.
- If tethered enemies are killed, the Tether-Flechette Orb will tether to new targets throughout its duration.
- Tether-Flechette Orb does not target enemies already pulled by other Tether-Flechette Orbs.

- Rapidly fires high velocity nails randomly in all directions, inflicting 200 / 250 / 300 Puncture scaling damage with a 50% critical chance, 2.0x critical multiplier, ~5% status chance, and a forced Puncture effect on enemies hit; enemies coming within 10 meters are prioritized by the turret with high accuracy. In the last second before expiring, the Tether-Flechette Orb will release a barrage of nails in all directions.
- Tether-Flechette Orb's nail damage scales with enemy level using the following expression:
Total Puncture Damage = Puncture Base Damage × (1 + Ability Strength) × ceil(Enemy Level ÷ 10)
- Multiplier from enemy level only increases after 1 level past the per-10-levels mark (e.g. levels 11, 21, 31, 41, etc).
- With a maxed Intensify, and an enemy at level 101, a Tether-Flechette Orb nail will deal 300 × (1 + 0.3) × ceil(101 ÷ 10) = 4,290 Puncture damage.

- Nails are affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses (which includes Vauban's passive and Vector-Overdrive Pad) and Buffing Fields.
- Flechette orbs can hit weakpoints. This is can be done by having the orbs placed in such a way that the nail's trajectory hits weakpoints when it aims for center mass, E.g. placing the orbs on a higher surface than the one a humanoid target is standing. The alt fire of Scourge and Scourge Prime can almost guarantee weakpoint hits.
- Enemies killed by nails from Tether-Flechette Orbs have their bodies pinned to walls and objects where the nail lands.

- Vauban can deploy up to 4 Tether-Flechette Orbs on the battlefield.

Vector-Overdrive Pad

- Vector-Overdrive Pad deploys upon contact with an enemy or surface, falling onto the ground and illuminating arrows toward the throw direction. Allies crossing the pad while moving in the arrow direction are propelled forward at a speed of 25 meters per second and gains 25% Movement Speed and 1.25x Weapon damage for 25 seconds.
- Weapon Damage bonus stacks multiplicatively with other damage buffs.
- Weapon Damage bonus also affects your and allies' Companion.
- Movement Speed bonus stacks additively with other sources of Movement Speed.
- Even if allies are sliding or rolling across the pad, they need to be inputting a movement in the pad's direction to get boosted. Correspondingly, players can get boosted by the pad even if maneuvering in a different direction by inputting the appropriate movement.
- As such, players can roll or slide without holding forward during the maneuver if they want to cross the pad in it's direction without getting boosted.

- Allies can reduce Vector-Overdrive Pad's momentum by shifting their movement away from the launch direction during and after launch.
- Can be used in conjunction with different parkour Maneuvers, such as Bullet Jump, jump, double jump, dodge roll and slide.

- Enemies crossing the pad are propelled away toward the arrow direction at the same speed, regardless of the direction they were moving.
- Enemies repelled away by Vector-Overdrive Pad can often be launched in an upward arcing trajectory.

- Vauban can deploy up to 4 Vector-Overdrive Pads on the battlefield.
- Casting Minelayer is a two-handed animation that allows free movement but interrupts certain actions such as reloading.
- Can be recast while active. If there are already four of a given orb type active, the oldest will be replaced by the recast.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies pulled by Tether-Flechette Orb passively receive 25% increased damage from Vauban's weapons and abilities.
- Tether-Flechette Orb can bring enemies into range of a Bastille containment field for suspension or vortex suction, while releasing the coil's wires to seek new targets.
- Vector-Overdriver Pad can launch enemies into range of Tether-Flechette Orbs, other Vector-Overdriver Pads, and Bastilles.
- Vector-Overdriver Pad's damage bonus applies Tether-Flechette Orb's nails.

Info

- Vauban expends 50 energy to throw a targeting beacon in an arcing trajectory toward the aiming reticle. Upon striking an enemy, the beacon inflicts 15 Blast damage with a ~10% status chance. Upon contact with a surface or 1.5 seconds has elapsed, an orbital cannon fires a powerful energy laser that penetrates through solid matter to strike the beacon, inflicting 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 / 2,500 Blast scaling damage with a 100% status chance and Ragdoll to all enemies within a ? / ? / ? / 7 meter radius. Enemies with Overguard are dealt 2x damage by Photon Strike.
- Photon Strike's explosion damage benefits from Ability Strength, Vauban's passive, and enemy level using the following expression:
Total Explosion Damage = Blast Damage × (1 + Ability Strength) × (1 + Passive Damage Bonus) × Enemy Level Multiplier

- If multiple vortexes are in range, Photon Strike's beacon will be pulled to the first vortex created.

| 4 100 | Bastille Erect a containment field to capture enemies and suspend them in stasis, stripping their armor and granting it to Vauban and any allies within Bastille's field. HOLD to collapse all Bastilles into a single damaging vortex. Introduced in Update 7.11 (2013-05-17) | Strength:15 ( Blast damage on device hit) 2.5 / 5 / 7.5 / 10 % (armor removal per second) 20 armor per second (bastille armor buff rate) 50 ( Magnetic damage per second in vortex) 40% (vortex status chance) 

Duration:8 / 10 / 12 / 15 s (bastille duration) 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 s (vortex duration) 10 s (armor buff duration) 3 s (auto collapse vortex duration) 

Range:5 / 7 / 8 / 10 m (capture radius) 

Misc: ?% ( Blast status chance) 2x (armor buff rate from armored enemies) ∞ (armor buff duration in field) 1,500 (armor bonus cap) 4 bastille cap 4 vortex cap 

Info

- Vauban expends 100 energy to throw a modular device in an arcing trajectory toward the aiming reticle. Upon striking an enemy, the device inflicts 15 Blast damage with a ~10% status chance. Upon contact with a surface, the device erects a Bastille energy containment field that suspends enemies into the air within a 5 / 7 / 8 / 10 meter radius. Armored enemies inside the Bastille's area permanently lose 2.5% / 5% / 7.5% / 10% of their total Armor points per second, while unarmored enemies are simply held captive. Enemies do not have to be suspended by the Bastille to lose armor. Simultaneously, Vauban and allies standing inside the containment field gain 4 armor every 0.2 seconds for each enemy within the containment field, with armored enemies granting 2x more armor. The armor bonus stacks up to 1,500 armor points. Bastille's armor bonus lasts indefinitely while inside the field and remains active for 10 seconds once outside.
- Bastille armor bonus duration is reset to full when moving into any Bastille containment fields.
- Suspended enemies are lifted into the air and completely immobilized, allowing headshots to hit their mark without resistance.

- After 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 seconds have elapsed, the containment field automatically collapses into a Vortex singularity for 3 seconds. The Vortex constantly Ragdolls and draws in all enemies within the same radius toward the device, inflicting 12.5 Magnetic damage every 0.25 seconds with 40% status chance per hit.
- Hold down the ability key (default 4 ) without any containment fields active to throw a new device set to Vortex mode. Hold down the ability key to collapse all active Bastille containment fields into Vortexes. Both methods of manually creating Vortexes resets the new and existing devices to last for 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 seconds.
- If Bastille fields are in range of each other, manually collapsing them into vortexes causes them to converge on the location of the first active Bastille, based on the order of creation. Enemies and pickups captured by the vortexes will be dragged along toward the new destination.
- Vortex also draws in Pickups within its radius for convenient collection.

- A maximum of 4 Bastilles and 4 Vortexes can be simultaneously active.
- Casting a 5th Bastille or Vortex will replace the oldest one of the same type.

- Can be used while performing many actions without interrupting them, including reloading.
- Can be cast multiple times while active.
- If cast while in midair, the casting animation changes with Vauban hovering briefly in the air as he throws the device.
- Bastille is a one-handed action. As such, it can be used while performing various maneuvers and actions without interruption.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies suspended by Bastille or pulled by Vortex passively receive 25% increased damage from Vauban's weapons and abilities.
- Minelayer's Flechette-Tether Orb and Vector-Overdriver Pad can bring enemies into range of Bastille. Enemies tied to Flechette-Tether orbs are released into Bastille's containment field for suspension, freeing the tether to seek new targets.
- Tethers are not freed if enemies tied to Flechette-Tether Orb are pulled into Bastille's vortex mode, however.

- Photon Strike's targeting beacon is attracted to Bastille's vortex mode, causing the beacon to home in on the device with the laser striking all enemies within range.

Tips & Tricks

- Bastille spawns from a ball that Vauban throws in an arc, so it can be deployed where aimed.
- Unlike many area-affecting abilities, neither Bastille nor Vortex require line of sight to affect enemies. This is disadvantageous for Vortex, as it frequently causes enemies to become stuck in corners or behind doorframes instead of reaching the device, forcing players to go looking for them. To avoid this, Vortex should be placed in open areas.
