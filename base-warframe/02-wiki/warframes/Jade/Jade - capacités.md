# Jade/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Jade/Abilities

## Passive

Where sacred light shines the song of life begins, as the shadow of time marches evermore toward deathly silence. As The Anointed, Jade possesses two passives:

- Jade possesses 2 Aura mod slots in her Arsenal loadouts to assist herself and her squadmates.
- This also includes increased mod capacity from both Auras (+28 from two maxed Auras, or +32 / +36 with Steel Charge and/or Power Donation), allowing Jade more modding space with less need for Polarization, as well as granting her more potential Starting Energy.

- Enemies that enter Light's Judgment well and Glory on High's primary fire at a 10% chance afflict enemies with the Judgements debuff as an overhead icon visual, applying 50% Damage Vulnerability lasting for 10 seconds.
- Enemies killed while debuffed by Judgments extends Symphony of Mercy's duration by 2 seconds.
- Glory on High's Alternate Fire can detonate Judgments, with increased damage and range.
- Detonated Judgments have an increased 1.5x explosion radius while inside Light's Judgment.

## Abilities

| 1 25 | Light's Judgment Create a well of light that heals allies and hurts enemies. Those who enter the well will be highlighted by Judgments. Introduced in Update 36.0 (2024-06-18) | Strength:200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Heat damage per half second 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 % health restore per second 

Duration:4 / 6 / 8 / 10 s well and buff timer 

Range:6 m 

Misc: ∞ buff time inside well 5 wells limit 

Info

- Jade expends 25 energy to throw one of her plumes in an arcing trajectory toward the reticle, creating a light well upon the first solid surface, covering a radius of 6 meters lasting for 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 seconds. The well burns enemies in direct Line of Sight for 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Heat damage per half second, while healing allies for 5% / 6% / 7% / 8% of their maximum Health per second.
- Jade also receives the healing buff the moment she throws the well.
- The healing buff lasts 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 seconds and is constantly refreshed to its maximum duration while inside the well, which is displayed as ∞ duration.
- The buff's duration will only be refreshed if the well gives more duration than the buff's remaining timer, otherwise it will continue counting down despite the buff display showing otherwise. For instance, a well that gives 10 seconds will refresh any buff that has less than 10 seconds remaining, but will not refresh any buffs that have more than 10 seconds remaining.
- Refreshing the buff's duration has no effect on its healing.

- Affected allies include all Warframes, Companions, Eidolon Lures, summoned allied units such as Specters, allied Invasion units, Hostages, Kavor Defectors, Sortie and Arbitration Defense Operatives, and Defense Objects.
- On Defense Objects, Light's Judgment restores 75 health per second.

- Ability Strength and Ability Duration for a well are snapshotted at the moment it touches a surface.
- Arcane Concentration affects both the buff that Jade gives to herself when throwing the well and the well's duration when it lands (provided that the well lands while Concentration is active), even if Concentration was not active before the ability was cast.

- A maximum of 5 wells can be created. Creating new wells will remove the oldest one.
- Enemies in the radius of multiple wells will receive damage from every well.
- Players who are affected by multiple wells will retain the healing from the well with highest Ability Strength that they entered.

- Wells are considered objects for Arcane Sculptor. The object is considered "created" when it leaves Jade's hand.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies in the well are debuffed by Judgments, receiving 50% Damage Vulnerability for 10 seconds.
- Enemies killed while debuffed by Judgments extends Symphony of Mercy's duration by 2 seconds.
- Judgments can be detonated by Glory on High's Alternate Fire.
- Detonated Judgments have an increased 1.5x explosion radius while inside Light's Judgment.

- Symphony of Mercy's Power of The Seven Ability Strength bonus enhances Light's Judgment's Heat damage and health restore per second.

Spirit of Resilience increases Shield Effectiveness. Extend the duration of each song by killing enemies surrounded by Judgments. Duration:15 / 20 / 25 / 30 s 

Range:N/A 

Misc: 10m (pushback range) 5m ( Stagger range) 1 s invulnerable during cast 50 m Affinity Range auras 150% Power of The Seven Ability Strength cap -80% Spirit of Resilience recharge delay cap 2 s Judgments extra aura duration on kill 

Info

- Jade expends 50 energy by holding down the ability button (default 2 ) to sing with triumphant praise. Enemies within 10 meters are pushed backwards while those within 5 meters are also Staggered. Jade becomes Invulnerable for 1 second while blessing herself and allies with one of three holy hymns: Power of The Seven, Deathbringer, or Spirit of Resilience, as an aura spanning her Affinity Range for 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 seconds:
- Power of The Seven: Adds 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% Ability Strength
- Ability Strength increase is capped at 150%, achievable with 600% Ability Strength.
- Light green colored icon on cycle wheel.

- Deathbringer: Adds 40% / 60% / 80% / 100% Damage to weapons.
- The damage bonus is additive to the damage increase from mods such as Serration.
- Light beige colored icon on cycle wheel.

- Spirit of Resilience: Restores Shields by 4% / 6% / 8% / 10% per second, which can flow into Overshields, and reduces Shield Recharge Delay by 10%.
- Shield regen bonus stacks with native shield regeneration, and does not apply during the Shield Recharge Delay period.
- Shield recharge speed and delay greatly benefit the Arbitration Operative, because he does not posess a Shield Gate mechanic.
- Shield Recharge Delay reduction is capped at -80%, achievable with 800% Ability Strength.
- Sky blue colored icon on cycle wheel.

- Buffs are shown above Jade's ability icons as a custom UI element including a cycle-selection wheel, duration timer, hymn icons, names and effect descriptions.
- Buffs can be cycled before cast or while the ability is active at no energy cost by tapping the ability button.
- The active aura is indicated by a buff icon beside the hitpoints indicator, an audio cue on cycle selection, and the musical wavelength visual effect surrounding Jade.

- Can be recast with hold-cast to refresh duration to full.

Kit Interactions

- Killing an enemy debuffed with Judgments from Light's Judgment or Glory on High extends Symphony of Mercy's duration by 2 seconds.
- The ability duration is capped at twice the modded full duration.
- Recasting while the remaining duration is greater than the ability's full duration (due to increases from killing enemies debuffed with Judgments) will lower the current duration to the modded full duration.

- Power of The Seven enhances Light's Judgment, Ophanim Eyes, and Glory on High's Ability Strength. Deathbringer enhances Glory's damage.

Tips & Tricks

- Each cycling of the buff aura by tapping the ability key counts as a fresh ability cast. This allows Jade to take advantage of external ability strength buffs after casting without needing to spend any more energy. This also does mean, however, that the strength buff can be lost if the buff aura is cycled after the strength buff expires.
- Symphony of Mercy works on Operator/Drifter even though the abilities don't scale with Ability Strength

Introduced in Update 36.0 (2024-06-18) | Strength:20 / 30 / 40 / 50 Heat damage 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 % armor and shield strip per second 

Duration:15 / 20 / 25 / 30 s 

Range:20 m 

Misc: 70° cone angle 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 % slow per second 90% slow cap 0.5s damage ticks Heat status after defense strip Ally bleedout revival at range

Info

- Jade expends 50 energy to assemble her beloved Ophanim orbitar choir, accusing all who stand within her Line of Sight in a 20 meter conical gaze with an angle of 70° for 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 seconds. Gazing upon enemies inflicts 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 Heat damage every 0.5 seconds, 6% / 9% / 12%/ 15% Slow per second stacking up to a cap of 90%, and permanently lose 4% / 6% / 8% / 10% of their Armor and Shields per second. Once defenses have been removed, the enemy receives a Heat Status Effect. Gazing upon fallen allies in Bleedout state will halt their bleedout timer and gradually gain revive progress until full revival.
- Ophanim Eyes hits every 0.5 seconds, with the slow and defense strip applying for half of their value every hit.
- Holding the ability button (default 3 ) while active will cancel the ability. Tap-cast while active to refresh duration to full.
- Each Ophanim Eye projects a light streak toward the reticle to indicate Jade's conical gaze direction.
- The slow effect does not affect enemies with Overguard, but the defense strip and Heat damage and status do.
- While not in Ophanim Eye's area of effect, slowed enemies gradually regain speed.
- Though the Heat status is applied only once upon fully removing defenses, enemies with Shields will regain shields after full depletion, allowing Ophanim Eyes to apply another Heat status.
- Since the slow effect extends enemy shield recharge delay, fully slowed enemies will only regain shields once every 30 seconds.

- When in Operator mode, Ophanim Eyes remain active and stares at the last direction before Transference from the Warframe's location.

Kit Interactions

- Symphony of Mercy's Power of The Seven Ability Strength bonus enhances Ophanim Eyes' Heat damage and defense strip per second.
- Ophanim Eyes can revive allies during Glory on High, which disables any Use key (default X ) interactions.

Helminth

- Subsuming Jade to the Helminth will offer Ophanim Eyes and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- However, Subsumed Ophanim Eyes will not have the ability to revive allies from Bleedout.

| 4 25 +5/s +25/alt fire | Glory on High Soar with destructive power. Use alternate-fire to detonate Judgments, causing an explosion of Jade Light. Enemies inside the Light's Judgment radius empower the explosion. Introduced in Update 36.0 (2024-06-18) | Strength:75 / 100 / 125 / 150 Heat damage 750 / 1000 / 1,250 / 1,500 Alternate Fire Heat damage 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 % damage reduction 

Duration:N/A 

Range:2 m primary fire explosion 4 m alternate fire explosion 

Misc: 50% damage reduction cap 10% Judgment chance on Glory primary fire 1.5x Light's Judgment empowered range multiplier 7.2 ms (base movement speed) 100% (innate movement speed bonus). 

Info

- Ascending the stairway to heaven, Jade expends 25 energy to soar into the sky with her seraphic wings unfurled. In this form, Jade permanently remains in mid-air, moving at 7.2 m/s with an innate +100% Movement Speed along with gaining vertical and horizontal flight capabilities using the movement (W A S D ), descend (Ctrl ), ascend (Spacebar ), and dodge (Shift ) hotkeys, similar to Archwing Maneuvers. Additionally, Jade gains 20% / 25% / 30% / 35% Damage Reduction while in flight and sheathes her weapons to equip her Exalted Weapon secondary orbitars Glory.
- Damage Reduction is capped at 50%, achievable with 143% Ability Strength.
- Movement speed bonus is additive with other sources of movement speed.
- Jade cannot sprint during Glory on High.
- Jade counts as being airborne in regards to the effects of Aerodynamic, Agility Drift and Aviator.

- Glory has its own unique statistics:
- Primary fire shoots light orb projectiles.
- Base damage is 75 / 100 / 125 / 150 pure Heat.
- Shots explode in a 2 meter radius on impact with a surface or enemy.
- Affected by Fulmination ( Primed).
- Bonus only scales with the base explosion radius, stacking additively with the bonus from ability range.

- Has 100% damage falloff over its radius.
- Requires Line of Sight to deal damage.
- Line of Sight is affected by Punch Through; Sufficient amounts of Punch Through allows the explosion to deal damage to enemies not within line of sight.

- Alternate Fire detonates all enemies affected by Judgments, consuming their marks in the process.
- Base damage is 750 / 1,000 / 1,250 / 1,500 pure Heat.
- The detonated enemy receives the damage before the Judgments mark is removed, increasing its damage taken by 50%. Nearby enemies will only receive the increased damage if they themselves are currently marked by Judgments.
- Multiple marked enemies will all be detonated on the same game tick, but internally they are detonated one at a time. If multiple detonated enemies are within each others' explosion radii, some of those enemies will take fewer hits boosted by Judgments than others. The damage they take depends on how early into the detonation chain their Judgments mark was removed.

- Detonated enemies explode in a 4 meter radius.
- Not affected by Fulmination ( Primed).

- Consumes 25 energy.
- Requires Line of Sight to at least 1 enemy afflicted by a Judgment to be triggered, however all enemies marked with Judgments will be detonated, regardless of distance or if they can be seen.
- Has a fixed casting cooldown of 1.5 seconds, unaffected by fire rate, reload speed, or casting speed.
- Glory's Alternate Fire is not affected by:
- Base Damage ( Hornet Strike, Secondary Merciless, Deathbringer's weapon damage bonus)
- Multishot ( Barrel Diffusion)

- Shots have a 15% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, 20% status chance, 1.67 fire rate, infinite ammunition, and does not need to reload.
- Glory can be equipped with secondary mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- Galvanized Shot's damage per status type has no effect on Glory's primary or alternate fire.
- Riven Mods are not generated for the Glory.

- Because Glory is counted as a secondary, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Pistol Amp and Arcane Precision.
- Headshot effects only trigger if it procs a status effect that can headshot, like Electricity or Gas, or must be activated beforehand.

- Glory on High consumes 5 energy per second while active and will remain active until Jade's energy is depleted, or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Jade cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rally Point, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, and/or Wellspring while Glory on High is active.
- Energy Orbs, Orokin Void Death Orb's energy restores, Rage, Hunter Adrenaline, Spellbound Harvest, Arcane Energize, and Emergence Dissipate's Energy Motes can still replenish Jade's energy while in Glory on High.
- Glory on High's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- Jade is unable to accomplish any action that requires the Use key (default X ) while Glory on High is active, thus cannot interact with consoles, pick up carriables such as datamasses, or directly revive allies from bleedout.
- Jade can pick up carriables before activating Glory on High, but is not able to use them until she deactivates the ability.
- The Operator can still be used while in Glory on High, and be able to do these actions without deactivating the ability. However, energy drain will still continue while in Operator mode.

- Subsumed abilities injected into Jade via the Helminth system can be cast while in Glory on High.

Weapon

Main article: Glory
Glory is Jade's Exalted Orbitars, summoned by activating the ability Glory on High. Primary fire shoots explosive Heat beams, while its Alternate Fire detonates Judgments in a disintegration blast.

- This weapon deals pure Heat damage.
- Innate two polarities.
- Primary fire shoots explosive projectiles.
- Shots explode in a 2 meter radius on impact with a surface or enemy, with 100% damage falloff from the center.
- Affected by Fulmination ( Primed).
- Bonus only scales with the base explosion radius, stacking additively with the bonus from ability range.

- Requires Line of Sight to deal damage.
- Line of Sight is affected by Punch Through; Sufficient amounts of Punch Through allows the explosion to deal damage to enemies not within line of sight.

- Shots have a 10% chance to debuff enemies with Judgments, applying 50% Damage Vulnerability for 10 seconds.
- Debuff chance is unaffected by Status Chance, or any Ability Stats.

- Alternate Fire detonates Judgments.
- Enemies explode in a 4 meter radius.
- Not affected by Fulmination ( Primed).
- Enemies standing within Light's Judgment have their explosion radius increased by 1.5x.
- Is affected by:
- Critical Chance and Critical Damage
- Elemental Damage
- Faction Damage
- Status Chance, Status Damage, and Status Duration
- Secondary Enervate, Secondary Encumber, Secondary Fortifier, and Secondary Outburst

- Is not affected by:
- Base Damage ( Hornet Strike, Secondary Merciless, Deathbringer's weapon damage bonus)
- Multishot
- Fire Rate
- Reload Speed
- Cascadia Empowered, Secondary Irradiate, Secondary Shiver, and Secondary Surge

Advantages:

- Has infinite ammo and does not need to reload.

Disadvantages:

- Primary fire has 100% damage falloff, dealing 0 damage at the edge of its explosion radius.
- Requires energy to activate and keep active. Alternate Fire uses additional energy to activate.
- Galvanized Shot's damage per status type has no effect on the explosion.

Kit Interactions

- Primary fire shots have a 10% chance to inflict Judgments, inflicting 50% Damage Vulnerability for 10 seconds.
- Debuff chance is unaffected by Status Chance, or any Ability Stats.

- Light's Judgment can apply Judgments. Alternate Fire gains an increased 1.5x explosion radius against enemies inside Light's Judgment.
- Enemies killed while debuffed by Judgments extends Symphony of Mercy's duration by 2 seconds.
- Symphony of Mercy's Power of The Seven Ability Strength bonus and Deathbringer weapon damage bonus enhance Glory on High and its Glory exalted secondary weapon.
- Ophanim Eyes can revive allies during Glory on High.
