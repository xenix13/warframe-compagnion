Info

- Jade expends 25 energy to throw one of her plumes in an arcing trajectory toward the reticle, creating a light well upon the first solid surface, covering a radius of 6 meters lasting for 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 seconds. The well burns enemies in direct Line of Sight for 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Heat damage per half second, while healing allies for 5% / 6% / 7% / 8% of their maximum Health per second.
- Jade also receives the healing buff the moment she throws the well.
- The healing buff lasts 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 seconds and is constantly refreshed to its maximum duration while inside the well, which is displayed as ∞ duration.
- The buff's duration will only be refreshed if the well gives more duration than the buff's remaining timer, otherwise it will continue counting down despite the buff display showing otherwise. For instance, a well that gives 10 seconds will refresh any buff that has less than 10 seconds remaining, but will not refresh any buffs that have more than 10 seconds remaining.
- Refreshing the buff's duration has no effect on its healing.

- Affected allies include all Warframes, Companions, Eidolon Lures, summoned allied units such as Specters, allied Invasion units, Hostages, Kavor Defectors, Sortie and Arbitration Defense Operatives, and Defense Objects.
- On Defense Objects, Light's Judgment restores 75 health per second.

- Ability Strength and Ability Duration for a well are snapshotted at the moment it touches a surface.
- Arcane Concentration affects both the buff that Jade gives to herself when throwing the well and the well's duration when it lands (provided that the well lands while Concentration is active), even if Concentration was not active before the ability was cast.

- A maximum of 5 wells can be created. Creating new wells will remove the oldest one.
- Enemies in the radius of multiple wells will receive damage from every well.
- Players who are affected by multiple wells will retain the healing from the well with highest Ability Strength that they entered.

- Wells are considered objects for Arcane Sculptor. The object is considered "created" when it leaves Jade's hand.

Kit Interactions

- Enemies in the well are debuffed by Judgments, receiving 50% Damage Vulnerability for 10 seconds.
- Enemies killed while debuffed by Judgments extends Symphony of Mercy's duration by 2 seconds.
- Judgments can be detonated by Glory on High's Alternate Fire.
- Detonated Judgments have an increased 1.5x explosion radius while inside Light's Judgment.

- Symphony of Mercy's Power of The Seven Ability Strength bonus enhances Light's Judgment's Heat damage and health restore per second.

Augment

Main article: Jade's Judgment
Jade's Judgment is a Warframe Augment Mod for Jade's Light's Judgment that has a chance to spawn a Jade Light above enemies damaged by its wells.

Bugs

- Light's Judgment may fail to heal an Eidolon Lure.
- If Casting Speed is above the default 100%, Jade is vulnerable to Stagger and Knockdown effects shortly after casting (presumably until the original casting duration timer runs out), regardless of the presence or absence of knockdown immunity buffs (e.g. Primed Sure Footed).
