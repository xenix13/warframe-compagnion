# Dagath/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dagath/Abilities

## Passive

Dagath feeds from the souls of the deceased, granting her a 35% chance to activate Abundant Abyss when picking up Health Orbs or Energy Orbs to increase their effective yield by +300%. The passive has no cooldown and can activate on each orb collected.

- This applies to:
- Health Orbs — 50 health becomes 200 health
- Dispensary's Empowered Health Orbs — 100 health becomes 400 health
- Small Energy Orbs — 25 energy becomes 100 energy
- Large Energy Orbs — 50 energy becomes 200 energy
- Universal Orbs — 25 health and 25 energy becomes 100 health and 100 energy
- Note that when picking up a Universal Orb, Abundant Abyss performs two separate rolls; the first roll determines whether the health is increased, and the second roll determines whether the energy is increased. It is possible for one to increase and not the other.

- Does not affect Equilibrium conversion.
- Effect is multiplicative with Energy/Health Orb Effectiveness increases from Amber Archon Shard, Energizing Shot, Mending Shot, and Restorative Bond
- For example, with two normal Amber Archon Shards slotted for Energy Orb Effectiveness (+100%), a small Energy Orb will normally provide 50 energy, and Abundant Abyss's activation will increase the amount to 200 energy.

- When her passive activates, swirling Void and mist particle effects surround Dagath for a moment.
- Works in Archwing missions.

- Dagath expends 25 Energy to whirl a macabre disc of 7 spectral sickles that rapidly expand from a radius of 1 to 5 meters around her, inflicting 350 / 400 / 450 / 500 Viral damage and Status Effect per sickle. Sliced enemies are Slowed by 20% / 25% / 30% / 35% reduced action speed for a duration of 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 seconds.
- Slow is capped to 95% achievable with 272% Ability Strength.
- Sickles rotate around Dagath at high speed to strike enemies multiple times. Since each sickle inflicts Viral status, subsequent damage from other sickles against enemy Health is increased per status stack applied.
- Spinning Wyrd Scythes damage and break destructible Objects such as crates and resource caches.

- After Wyrd Scythes spin for 1 second, Dagath throws the sickles outward as 7 fast homing projectiles after a 0.5 second throw delay, striking nearby enemies to inflict 700 / 800 / 900 / 1,000 Viral damage, status, and slow, with infinite Punch Through to slice other foes behind the target while the sickles fly off into the distance.

- Sickles will not auto-target enemies obscured by obstacles, or that are positioned behind Dagath (regardless of line of sight being blocked or not).

- With no enemies to target, Dagath will launch them all toward her aiming reticle.
- Sickles travel at 40m/s for 3 seconds before disappearing, traveling a total of 120 meters.
- Spin time is shown on Wyrd Scythe's ability icon as a recast cooldown timer.

- Cannot be recast during spin phase. Flying spectral sickle projectiles are not launched if recast during the throw delay.

Kit Interactions

- Strike Doomed foes with Wyrd Scythes to trigger Phantom Wrath, while refreshing Doom on the targets hit and spreading the curse to other enemies within a 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 meter radius around the Doomed foes.

Helminth

- Subsuming Dagath to the Helminth will offer Wyrd Scythes and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- However, Subsumed Wyrd Scythe's slow is reduced to 15 / 18 / 22 / 26%.
- Slow is capped to 71% achievable with 272% Ability Strength.

| 2 50 | Doom Condemn nearby enemies to their doom. A portion of the damage Dagath deals is revisited upon them by a Wyrd Scythe. They also suffer Viral Damage. Introduced in Update 34.0 (2023-10-18) | Strength:350 / 400 / 450 / 500 Viral damage 35% Phantom Wrath damage stored per damage instance 

Duration:7 / 10 / 12 / 15 s 

Range:10 / 12 / 13 / 15 m cone range 

Misc: 40° cone angle Stagger inside cone 100% damage redirection per damage instance cap 2 s Phantom Wrath damage storage time limit 1 s recast cooldown 

Info

- Dagath lets loose a morbid scream, expending 50 Energy to conjure a 40 degree cone of cursed energy spanning 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 meters from her faceless head, inflicting 350 / 400 / 450 / 500 Viral damage and Stagger to all enemies within the cone and line-of-sight. Doomed foes remain cursed for 7 / 10 / 12 / 15 seconds.
- Doomed foes are visually death-marked by two large crosses of eerie light.

- Inflict damage to a Doomed foe with Dagath's weapons and abilities to trigger Phantom Wrath, which summons a floating spectral sickle above that individual target and stores 35% of every damage instance dealt by Dagath herself within a time limit of 2 seconds. After the time limit elapses, or when the stored damage amount becomes lethal to its target, the spectral sickle strikes its victim to inflict the stored damage as True damage. Phantom Wrath can be triggered again repeatedly on Doomed foes.
- Damage redirection per damage instance is capped at 100%, achievable with 286% Ability Strength.
- Stored damage is based on damage dealt after all sources of enemy Damage Reduction are applied.
- Phantom Wrath is also applied on Doom's initial cast to store a percentage of the ability's Viral damage.
- Each Doomed foe may only have one sickle floating above them at a time.
- Phantom Wrath's stored damage is not shared between Doomed enemies, only to the single individual enemy.
- Phantom Wrath's damage, being it's own instance, is also multiplied by Viral status effects.
- Lethal damage is calculated including Viral status effect but not damage bonuses such as Roar, damage vulnerability debuffs such as Sentient Wrath or damage reductions, despite being affected by them.

- Can be recast after a 1 second cooldown. Recast on Doomed enemies to renew duration and trigger Phantom Wrath.
- Recast cooldown is shown on Doom's ability icon.

- Doom can steal kills from Dagath's weapons, impeding the activation of weapon arcanes and galvanized mods.
- This also will prevent companions from making use of Contagious Bond.

Kit Interactions

- Strike Doomed foes with Wyrd Scythes to trigger Phantom Wrath, while refreshing Doom on the targets hit and spreading the curse to other enemies within a 10 / 12 / 13 / 15 meter radius around the Doomed foes.
- Strike Doomed foes while Grave Spirit is active to inflict 70% / 80% / 90% / 100% weapon critical damage bonus and double the damage stored by Phantom Wrath.
- Stampede Doomed foes with Rakhali's Cavalry to trigger Phantom Wrath, and induce 20% / 25% / 30% / 35% permanent Armor and Shield reduction. Defense reduction on Doomed foes occurs on every Viral damage instance from individual Kaithes.
- Defenses are fully removed at 286% Ability Strength from a single hit. However, because the Cavalry deals multiple instances of damage, far less Strength is necessary, with 143% Ability Strength fully removing defenses after two hits.

| 3 75 | Grave Spirit Supercharge Dagath's weapons with extra Critical Damage. The effects are doubled on Doomed enemies. Escape fatal blows by briefly assuming a spectral form. Introduced in Update 34.0 (2023-10-18) | Strength:35 / 40 / 45 / 50 % critical damage bonus 70 / 80 / 90 / 100 % Doom critical damage bonus 

Duration:7 / 8 / 9 / 10 s invulnerable time 

Range:N/A 

Misc: ∞ duration (not deactivatable) 10 m Knockdown radius 100% dodge chance 100% Health Orb drop per kill in spectral form 25 s cooldown 

Info

- Dagath expends 75 Energy to attune her soul with the spectral realm, gaining a 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% Critical Damage bonus on all her equipped Weapons while the ability is toggled on. Once cast, Grave Spirit remains active indefinitely until Dagath suffers a lethal hit, she falls into an Out of Bounds zone, or when she enters an ability nullification zone such as Nullifier Crewman bubbles.
- Critical damage bonus stacks additively with critical damage mods such as Vital Sense.
- Critical damage bonus percentage appears beside Dagath's hitpoints indicator as a Grave Spirit buff icon.
- While active, spirit particles emit from Dagath.

- While active, Dagath escapes death from the next lethal hit by phasing into an Invulnerable spectral form for 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 seconds, initially causing Knockdown to enemies within 10 meters. During spectral form, Dagath may continue her killing spree, aided by a 100% Dodge chance for all enemy attacks to phase through her, and a 100% Health Orb drop chance from slain foes. Once she returns to physical form, Grave Spirit deactivates and enters a cooldown of 25 seconds before it can be recast.
- Spectral form timer appears beside Dagath's hitpoints indicator.
- Dagath's body and the player's screen borders are enveloped in ghostly mist during spectral form.
- Shield regeneration does not self-start during spectral form. Recharge is possible when kick-started by external means, such as Brief Respite and Augur set bonus.
- Dagath is not truly intangible, she can suffer from Eximus ability procs. Despite not dealing any damage, something like Magnetic effect will drain her Energy.

- Casting Grave Spirit is a One-Handed Action. As such, it can be used while performing various maneuvers and actions without interruption.
- Cannot be recast while active.

Kit Interactions

- Strike Doomed foes while Grave Spirit is active to inflict 70% / 80% / 90% / 100% weapon critical damage bonus and double the damage stored by Phantom Wrath.

Augment

Main article: Spectral Spirit
Spectral Spirit is a Warframe Augment Mod for Dagath's Grave Spirit that immediately enters the spectral form when used and gives all weapons and abilities a chance to inflict Doom for the duration. When outside the spectral form, kills will reduce Grave Spirit's cooldown by 1 second for every kill.

Rank | Doom Chance | Cost 

0 | 25% | 6 

1 | 50% | 7 

2 | 75% | 8 

3 | 100% | 9 

Tips & Tricks

- The guaranteed Health Orb drops during the spectral form allows Dagath to make use of Health Conversion, Equilibrium, Arcane Pulse, and Arcane Blessing.
- When using Arcane Escapist, Grave Spirit will trigger first on receiving fatal damage if active.
- With 9 stacks of rank 4 Arcane Escapist it is possible to stay completely invulnerable for the duration of the cooldown.

Info

- Dagath expends 100 Energy to summon 5 spectral remnants of Rakhali's soul in a cavalry charge formation 5 meters away. During the casting time of 1.5 seconds, Dagath becomes Invulnerable then commands the cavalry of phantom Kaithes to continuously charge forward at 15m/s for 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 seconds. Each spectral Kaithe inflicts 15,000 / 20,000 / 25,000 / 30,000 Viral damage and Stagger per 0.15 seconds to all enemies in its path; damage is dealt in full on each interval and continues for as long as enemies remain in physical contact with the phantom Kaithes.
- All effects apply to enemies in contact with Kaithes during the summoning animation.
- Enemies touched by Kaithes emit a burst of ghostly light on every hit.

- Dagath's invulnerability while casting Rakhali's Cavalry scales directly with its casting time. Reducing casting time through Natural Talent or an Amber Archon Shard will reduce this invulnerability by the same amount.
- Phantom Kaithes charge in a V formation, with the leading Kaithe in the center. Each Kaithe has a sizable hitbox to strike multiple enemies, aided by the stagger effect.
- When cast while Dagath is on the ground, phantom Kaithes' formation is aligned with the leading Kaithe summoned on the nearest available horizontal surface, regardless of Dagath's own elevation.
- When cast from the air, the Kaithes align toward the exact direction of Dagath's aiming reticle and can fly through the air. If they touch the ground, they charge forward wherever their head is facing and run along the surface, adhering to any changes in the terrain.
- Phantom Kaithes will phase through vertical terrain and obstacles as they travel, by visually passing through a ghostly portal aligned with the wall.

- Phantom Kaithes damage and break destructible Objects such as crates and resource caches.

- Can be recast while active to summon all Kaithes toward a new direction. Previous cavalry charge is removed on recast.

Kit Interactions

- Stampede Doomed foes with Rakhali's Cavalry to trigger Phantom Wrath, and induce 20% / 25% / 30% / 35% permanent Shields and Armor reduction. Defense reduction on Doomed foes occurs on every Viral damage instance from individual Kaithes.
- Defenses are fully removed at 286% Ability Strength from a single hit. However, because the Cavalry deals multiple instances of damage, far less Strength is necessary, with 143% Ability Strength fully removing defenses after two hits.
