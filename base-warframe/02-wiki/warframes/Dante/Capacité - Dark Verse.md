Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Dante expends 25 energy to scribble a crossword with twin strokes from his quills, inflicting 2 instances of 500 / 750 / 1,000 / 1,250 Slash damage with guaranteed status and light Stagger to enemies in front of him, within 15 / 17 / 18 / 20 meters and Line of Sight in a 50 degree cone.
- Dark Verse damages and breaks destructible Objects such as crates and props in its area of influence, bypassing line-of-sight restriction.
- Line-of-sight detection begins from the crossword visual effect several meters in front of Dante himself.

- Can be recast immediately once the second stroke appears on the crossword.
- Casting Dark Verse is a full-body animation that allows uninterrupted movement and parkour Maneuvers.

Helminth

- Subsuming Dante to the Helminth will offer Dark Verse and its augments to be used by other Warframes.

Tips & Tricks
The second stroke can be used in a different direction by aiming elsewhere right after the first stroke, either to target additional enemies or break more destructibles. The time between strokes is affected by Casting Speed, so increasing it can make aiming the second stroke more difficult.
