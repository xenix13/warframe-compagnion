# Dante/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dante/Abilities

## Passive

Enemies slain by Noctua's primary fire, page fragments, and alt-fire are scanned into the Codex upon death, with each enemy death recording 1 scan entry.
Enemy types with completed Codex research become permanently debuffed with Chronicler's Mark, which grants Dante's equipped weapons, including the Noctua itself, and Final Verse's Wordwarden Noctua copies an extra 50% Status Chance versus these foes.

- Status chance bonus is multiplicative to a weapon's total status chance after mods and other bonuses are applied.
- For example, Noctua with Scorch equipped will have 40% status chance, or 40% * 1.5 = 60% against fully researched enemies.

- Status chance bonus is also multiplicative to Pageflight's status increase.
- Works in Archwing missions.

Info

- Dante expends 5 energy to equip his Exalted Weapon tome sidearm, the Noctua, replacing his equipped secondary weapon. Press the ability key again (default 1 ) to deactivate and return his secondary weapon to the swap weapon cycle (default F ).
- Noctua has its own unique statistics:
- Primary fire shoots fully automatic arcane energies.
- Consumes 2 energy per shot.
- Base damage is 175 / 200 / 225 / 250 pure Slash.
- Shots have a 30% critical chance, 2x critical multiplier, 25% status chance, 1.5 fire rate, infinite ammunition, and does not need to reload.
- On impact with an enemy, the shot splits into 4 fragments that seek enemies within 20 meters at a 60 degree angle behind the struck enemy. Homing fragments have the same damage, critical stats, and status chance as the main projectile, and charge the alt-fire meter when striking enemies.

- Alternate Fire hurls a wide projectile.
- Also consumes 2 energy per shot.
- Requires a fully filled meter beneath the reticle in order to fire. The meter takes 60 seconds to completely recharge. Hitting enemies with the primary fire or page fragments reduces recharge time by 1 second per hit. Picking up secondary or universal ammo reduces recharge time by 10 seconds.
- Base damage is 2,000 / 2,250 / 2,500 / 2,750 distributed between 40% Impact and 60% Radiation.
- Shots have a 45% critical chance, 3x critical multiplier, 45% status chance, 1.0 fire rate, infinite Punch Through against bodies, and 1.4 meter Punch Through against surfaces.

- Noctua can be equipped with secondary mods and Arcane Enhancements.
- The Noctua's unique trait bypasses the restriction of one Canticle Mod ( Fass Canticle, Jahu Canticle, Khra Canticle, and Lohk Canticle) and one Invocation mod ( Xata Invocation, Netra Invocation, Ris Invocation, and Vome Invocation), allowing it to equip all Tome Mods simultaneously, if desired.
- However, note that Noctua will not be affected by the effects of Vome Invocation or Netra Invocation unless they were active at the time of casting the ability, and must be recast to update ability stats.

- Multishot will increase the number of primary fire projectiles, and each additional projectile will also produce 4 fragments on hit. This means that 2 projectiles will produce 8 fragments, etc.
- Each additional projectile and fragment that strikes an enemy counts towards replenishing the Alt-Fire meter.
- Alt-Fire projectile is unaffected by multishot bonuses.

- Galvanized Shot's damage bonus does not scale correctly. On the primary fire and its homing fragments, it acts like the base damage is 200 (rather than 250), regardless of ability strength. On the alt-fire, it acts like the base damage is 2500 (rather than 2750), regardless of ability strength.
- These values also apply to Wordwarden's Noctua, despite it dealing 50% of Noctua's normal damage.

- Can be equipped with Amalgam Barrel Diffusion, granting its bonus dodge speed even when not summoned. Also applies if the ability is replaced.
- Will correctly stack if also equipped on Dante's secondary weapon.

- Riven Mods are not generated for the Noctua.

- Because Noctua is counted as a secondary, it is affected by mods and Warframe Arcanes such as Pistol Amp and Arcane Precision.

- Casting and deactivating Noctua are full-body animations that allow uninterrupted movement and parkour Maneuvers.
- Noctua is compatible with passive energy regeneration while it is active and while firing, unlike most Exalted Weapons. This means that energy sources such as Energy Nexus and Wellspring are viable ways to replenish Dante's energy while attacking with Noctua.
- Noctua can also act as a source of energy regeneration. When inflicting Electricity status effects, Archon Stretch will be triggered. Alt-Fire attacks can trigger energy regeneration from Xata Invocation and Noctua Swarm, with the latter restoring energy to allies in range as well.

Weapon

Main article: Noctua (Weapon) Noctua is Dante's Exalted Tome, summoned by activating the ability Noctua. Arcane energies from its primary fire split and seek nearby enemies, while its Alternate Fire throws a large wave.
- This weapon deals pure Slash damage on its primary fire and primarily Radiation damage on its Alternate Fire.
- Primary fire hurls fully automatic arcane energies.
- On impact with an enemy, the shot splits into 4 fragments that seek enemies within 20 meters at a 60 degree angle. Multiple fragments may target the same enemy if fewer than 4 enemies are within seeking range.
- One set of 4 fragments will be generated for each main hit caused by Multishot, but number of fragments per set is not affected by multishot; e.g. with a multishot of 2, a total of 8 fragments will be generated per shot, not 16. Additional fragment sets generated due to multishot will generally target the same enemies; e.g., the 8 fragments in the above example will usually target 4 enemies, hitting each with 2 fragments.
- Fragment damage and other stats appear to be identical to the main projectile, and appear to inherit mod effects (including modded elements) and inflict status effects and critical hits as usual.

- Despite visually being a medium-sized energy ball, actual hitbox of the projectile is just the tiny center point itself traveling at 80m/s straight.

- Alternate Fire hurls a wide projectile.
- Requires a fully filled meter beneath the reticle in order to fire. The meter takes 60 seconds to completely recharge. Hitting enemies with the primary fire reduces recharge time by 1 second per hit. Picking up secondary or universal ammo reduces recharge time by 10 seconds.
- Multishot will count as an additional hit.
- Ammo from Pistol Ammo Mutation will not reduce recharge time.

- Has Infinite Body Punch Through and 1.4 meter Punch Through against surfaces.

Augment

Main article: Noctua Swarm
Noctua Swarm is a Warframe Augment Mod for Dante that creates a crowd-controlling zone that Silences and chain- Stuns when Noctua's alternate fire mode hits a surface. Spectral Paragrimms lacerate enemies with small Bleed procs every second, and restore energy to Dante and his allies for each enemy under their assault.

Tips & Tricks

- Because the four page fragments each deal as much damage as the primary projectile, Noctua's greatest damage potential is in hitting enemies behind the first target struck.
- Noctua never interrupts energy regen, even while firing. Xata Invocation, Archon Stretch, Energy Nexus, Noctua Swarm, Wellspring etc., are capable of replenishing the energy consumed by Noctua, even with negative efficiency.
- Noctua has unlimited ammo, but a fairly slow base fire rate, so increasing the rate of fire is key to increasing overall damage output.
- Lohk Canticle buffs Dante and his allies from the Noctua's Exilus slot, ** Arcane Velocity offers a large fire rate buff, and Amalgam Furax Body Count and Reinforced Bond increase fire rate without consuming a slot on the weapon or the frame.
- However, bear in mind that Noctua's per-shot energy consumption scales with fire rate, so Dante's passive energy regeneration may need to be increased to match.

- Because damage stats are multiplicative with increases in Noctua's base damage from Ability Strength, it is a good idea to acquire large amounts of both strength and weapon damage when building Noctua for damage.
- Cascadia Flare and Arcane Precision can give secondary weapons a large damage bonus. However, Galvanized Shot does not properly scale with Ability Strength.

- Dante may benefit from carrying a Grimoire with one of Netra Invocation or Vome Invocation, allowing him to buff up before casting Noctua. Buffs to Ability Strength and Efficiency last for as long as the cast of Noctua is active, and loading it on a Grimoire frees up a mod slot on Noctua itself.
- However, if Noctua needs to be recast, for example after falling out of bounds, the buff will have to be acquired again.

- Noctua's primary attack has a damage distribution that is pure Slash. Physical mods that add Slash like Maim apply at full value, and are a practical choice for increasing primary fire damage. However, they will not have any effect on Alt-Fire.
- Though Noctua strikes multiple enemies per shot and has explosion-like visuals, the weapon has no AoE component, so Fulmination should be avoided.

Info

- Cast Light Verse and/or Dark Verse to fill in 2 pages that complete the Final Verse. Cannot be cast without two pages. Once the story is written, Dante fulfills the narrative by expending 50 energy on cast, which grants him 1 second of Invulnerability during casting and materializes one of four stories based on page order: Triumph, Tragedy, Wordwarden, or Pageflight, while removing both pages from the storybook display above the ability icons. Each story lasts for 30 / 35 / 40 / 45 seconds with a range of 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 meters.
- The specific story ability icon appears above the storybook display when two pages are filled, with the pages visually glowing.
- Replace filled pages from the right to left by recasting Light or Dark Verses to create the desired story combination.
- Stories with active duration appear as ability buff timers beside Dante's hitpoints indicator.

- All stories can be recast while active.
- Casting Final Verse is a full-body animation that allows uninterrupted movement and parkour Maneuvers.
- Dante's four favored chapters exhibit diverse effects involving protection, damage, summoning, and debuffing:
Triumph

- Written with two Light pages (default 2 twice). Triumph bestows 2,250 / 2,500 / 2,750 / 3,000 Overguard to Dante and his allies instantly in story range. While story duration is active, each kill or assisted kill made by Dante or his allies' weapons and abilities will regenerate their personal Overguard pools by 85 / 90 / 95 / 100 points over 2 seconds. Overguard granted by Triumph is capped to 7,500 / 10,000 / 12,500 / 15,000 total points.
- Ability Synergy: Overguard generated by Light Verse and Triumph are combined into a single pool with two separate maximum values affected by Ability Strength.
- Overguard from Triumph does not apply to rescued hostage in Rescue missions.

Tragedy

- Written with two Dark pages (default 3 twice). Tragedy inflicts 5,000 / 5,500 / 6,000 / 6,500 Slash damage and Stagger to all enemies within story range and in Line of Sight. Enemies hit by Tragedy while debuffed with active Slash, Heat, or Toxin Status Effect will instantly receive the full damage from all damage ticks of the procs, multiplied by 2.25x / 2.5x / 2.75x / 3x; status effects are removed after inflicting damage.
- Damage is capped at 1 billion.
- Line-of-sight extends from Dante's model and from the player camera perspective.
- Enemies directly on screen, ran behind cover moments before the check, those already behind cover with partial body exposure, and standing on different height elevations are all eligible targets.
- Enemies to the sides and back of the camera view are detected via raycast lines to their head, torso and feet.
- Line-of-sight check is ignored for enemies within 5 meters of Dante.

Wordwarden

- Written with first Light and last Dark pages (default 2 then 3 ). Wordwarden summons 1 spectral Noctua companion that floats beside Dante and his allied Warframes in story range on cast for its story duration, attacking enemies struck by its warded player's weapons for 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% of the original Noctua's damage amplified by its modded stats, including Status Chance for its equipped Physical and Elemental damage types.
- Wordwarden fires a homing Noctua energy projectile toward the warded player's target. The projectile has travel time and can be blocked by obstacles in the environment, unless modded with Punch Through stat.
- Projectile is not affected by Multishot.

- Targets the enemy last struck by its warded player, firing a maximum of once every 0.333 seconds.
- Fire rate of 3 is not affected by fire rate bonuses.
- Wordwarden targets the enemy's head hitbox if the player's attack was a headshot, and otherwise targets the center of the enemy hitbox.

- Will not use the Alternate Fire and thus cannot trigger Invocation Mods. Will also not trigger Canticle Mods.
- Can trigger mods and Arcanes such as Archon Stretch (provided the Exalted Noctua has Electricity modded onto it) or Secondary Encumber.
- Galvanized Shot's damage bonus does not scale correctly. On direct hits and homing fragments, it acts like the base damage is 200 (rather than 125), regardless of ability strength.

- Ability Synergy:
- Kills from Wordwarden Noctua passively scan enemies into the Codex. Wordwarden Noctua also gains 50% Status Chance against enemies that have been fully scanned.
- Wordwarden inherits Mods placed on Noctua and splits page fragments on hit that charge the held Noctua's alt-fire.
- Inherits Noctua's stats, including the effects of Ability Strength on Noctua's base damage.

- Notes:
- Damage dealt by the extra hit of Xata's Whisper or a Blast status effect can cause Wordwarden to attack.
- If a Helminth ability is subsumed over Noctua, Wordwarden can still be cast. Ability strength and mods installed on Noctua will still function as normal.

Pageflight

- Written with first Dark and last Light pages (default 3 then 2 ). Pageflight summons 3 spectral Paragrimms with high Threat Level that fly around for the story duration and attack enemies within 30 meters, dealing 150 / 200 / 250 / 300 Slash damage, status, and Stagger. Enemies hit by Paragrimms also receive 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% Status Chance vulnerability and 35% / 40% / 45% / 50% increased Status Damage for 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 seconds.
- Status chance vulnerability is multiplicative to other sources of status chance, increasing a weapon's total status chance after mods and other bonuses are applied. It does not increase Pageflight's Slash statuses per Paragrimm hit.
- Does not affect the number of statuses from forced status procs, such as those in many melee combos, Epitaph's cold proc on uncharged shot AoE, or Daikyu's impact proc on hit.

- Status damage increase is multiplicative to other sources of status damage.
- Enemies debuffed by the Paragrimms are visually surrounded by an upsurging double helix.

- Pageflight Paragrimms are Summons with diverse properties and behaviors:
- Invulnerable and intangible, able to phase through enemy attacks, solid entities and obstacles in the environment.
- Are affected by Summoner's Wrath, Arcane Camisado, and Theorem Infection.
- Slash status effects from the Paragrimms do not double dip on the Summoner's Wrath bonus, despite it being a faction damage bonus (bug).

- Fly above Dante when no enemies are detected in range, and return to him when farther away than range.
- Detect enemies in range without direct line-of-sight.
- Prefer striking different targets to apply status vulnerability, while able to focus their combined attacks on a lone enemy. Once all enemies in range are debuffed, the Paragrimms prioritize attacking enemies closest to Dante himself.
- Strike each target by flying through them, then encircling back along their flight path.

- Ability Synergy: Pageflight's status chance vulnerability increases Dark Verse's Slash status stacks per hit.
