# Shadows Of The Dead (capacité de Nekros) (wiki)

Info

- Nekros expends 100 energy to summon 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 shadow copies of his most recently slain enemies, prioritizing the strongest enemy groups among those killed to summon. The copies retain the level and stats of their original selves but will gain 25% / 50% / 75% / 150% damage, 15% / 35% / 65% / 100% shields and 15% / 35% / 65% / 100% health, with each copy losing 3% of their health per second while active.
- Shadows spawn around Nekros in a clockwise fashion, within a 10 meter radius.
- High priority enemies will spawn first, enemies of equal priority will appear in order of deaths.
- Spawn order starts from the direction where the player's camera is looking, but the Shadows themselves face the same direction as Nekros.

- Shadows of the Dead's damage, shield and health bonuses multiply a Shadow's total damage, shield and health. For example, +30% Ability Strength will increase the damage and health bonuses to 1.5 × 1.3 = 195% and 1 × 1.3 = 130% respectively at max rank. A copy whose original had 200 total health and dealt 100 damage per attack will have 200 × (1 + 1.3) = 460 health when summoned and will inflict 100 × (1 + 1.95) = 295 damage per attack.
- There is currently no way to prematurely kill shadows in order to refresh them, either the shadows or Nekros must die in order for this to happen.
- The number of active Shadows is displayed on the ability icon.
- Shadows of enemies that summon additional units, like Drahk Masters, can summon Shadows of their respective minions. These can exceed the number of Shadows Nekros can normally summon, and will be reflected in the number on the ability icon. They also contribute extra damage reduction when Shield of Shadows is equipped.

- Shadows can be healed with some Healing mods, abilities, arcanes, weapons, and allied Shadows' abilities (e.g. Aerolyst canisters/sparks, Leech Eximus damage zone, etc.). Some shadows also cannot die.
- This can become a problem later on when the Nekros player needs to refresh their shadows to get higher level versions, but cannot do so until they die.
- Eximus Shadows cannot be healed and protected by Protective Sling, probably because of their Overguard.

- Has a very long cast animation, during which Shadows usually will not attack. Nekros is not immune to knockdown and takes full damage during this time.
- Casting the ability again (default 4 ) while there are Shadows still active performs additional functions:
- Active Shadows that are more than 30 meters away from Nekros will teleport into Nekros' spawn radius.
- Fully replenishes active Shadows' shields and health on each recast. If the number of active Shadows are at maximum capacity, Nekros will perform a shortened animation.
- Shadows are not healed before the completion of the animation; as such, if recasting at maximum Shadows, some can die before getting healed, and more will not be spawned to compensate.

- When the number of active Shadows falls below the maximum, Nekros performs the full summoning animation to create additional Shadows up to the maximum capacity.
- This function can only be performed when there are slain enemies in Nekros's "recently-killed" list (see #Summoning Priority).

- Dying or experiencing a host migration will destroy any Shadows you currently had stored.

This is a list of general properties common for all Shadows. See also: #Shadow Quirks.

- Shadows can benefit from Buffing Fields, Decrees, and all teammate abilities that apply to summons (e.g. Righteous Negation).
- As the shadows appear, each one inflicts a small area knockdown which can only affect each enemy once.
- Nekros and his allies are able to walk through Shadows without being obstructed.
- Shadows have raised Threat Level, causing enemies to prioritize attacking them over players.
- Shadows are able to use their original abilities, gear, and tactics (including Eximus).
- Shadows will take cover and organize, while occasionally seeking out to follow the player or the host around.
- Shadows will interact with the environment, for example using Ramparts, or hacking into disabled Ambulas or Bursa - turning them into allies just as if the player had hacked them.
- Shadows of enemies that leave explosions upon death will damage allies.
- Shadows of units such as Ancient Protector will not provide Overguard.
- Other buffing Shadows (e.g. Shield Osprey, Ancient Healer, Aerolyst) may affect Defense Objects, Excavators, Companions, players, and allied NPCs.
- They will prioritize following your Shadows, or enemies if not in range of Shadows.

- Shadows with globes (e.g. Nullifier Crewman, Arctic Eximus, Entropic Eximus) will block both enemy and friendly fire from the outside.

- Shadows are counted as summons and are affected by Summoner's Wrath, Arcane Camisado, and Theorem Infection.
- They are able to damage enemies immune to ability damage (e.g. Arbitration Shield Drone).
- Shadow eximus abilities may receive elemental damage bonuses and may grant buffs to the player. For example:
- Leech Eximus will create a damage zone which heals allies, it also deals Cold damage and status procs when using Theorem Infection with Residual Malodor.
- Shock Eximus will trigger Archon Stretch. Other elemental eximus may trigger other archon mods. Leech Eximus will not trigger Archon Intensify.

- Eximus Shadows possess Overguard, which isn't considered as a part of their health and will not be drained, making them far more resilient than their normal variants.
- Enemies that spawn additional enemies (i.e. Drahk Master) can add to the number of Shadows, even above the max Shadow count.
- Kills by a player's Shadows grant that player 50% of the kill experience to their Warframe.
- Shadows are black, and have a mist effect that is colored with Nekros' energy color. A pure black energy color will remove the mist effect and leave simply blackened forms.
- If a Shadow enters inside the sphere of a Nullifier Crewman or Corrupted Nullifier, or gets in contact with the disrupting aura of a Comba or Scrambus, the shadow will be drained of health while remaining inside the bubble or aura. If Nekros is the one making contact with one of the mentioned, his Shadows will not die.

- Shadow copies are taken from a list of the last 20 enemies killed by either Nekros or his Shadows; teammate, Companion, and Operator mode kills do not count. Once a specific enemy has been copied, it is removed from the list.
- The game does not remember any kills past the 20th, for example: if you kill 30 enemies and cast this ability 4 times without killing any more enemies, this ability will create 7, 7, 6, and 0 Shadows.
- When using weapons with Syndicate mods, or weapons with built-in syndicate effects, the radial blast will count as the player's kills, which will overwrite any current ones.

- Soul Punch is the highest "priority" in a way, because it (or its Mark) immediately summons a Shadow.
- Some Shadows can be created only via Soul Punch instakill. Some enemies cannot be copied.

- Shadows of the Dead summoning priority is such: 1) the highest level enemies in the list; 2) the order of appearance in the list (first to die); 3) Eximus, otherwise the highest priority group.
- Eximus variants will ignore the priority group and will only respect enemy levels and the order of appearance in the list. Eximus enemies spawn with higher levels than the rest, which means they are almost always prioritized.
- If you stop killing for a while, enemies in the kill list will have lower levels compared to currently spawning enemies.

- The ? priority means the unit can definitely be copied, but it's hard to test the priority of it.

This is a list of strange properties unique to specific Shadows. See also: #Shadow Properties.

- Some Shadows have a very slow decay rate compared to others (potentially via passive regeneration): Deimos Therid, Manic Bombard, and Ghoul Rictus Alpha.
- Some Shadows can spawn allied non-Shadow units, these units collide with players, do not increase the Shadow count, do not contribute to Shield of Shadows, and do not decay. See #Bugs for more details.
- Some Shadows will not use their abilities or gear. See #Bugs for more details.
- Entropic Eximus were designed against Tenno, as as result their Shadows are extremely tanky against enemies, they retain their damage reduction and literally nothing can break their globe.
- A Drahk Master will not disarm enemies.
- Amalgam Heqet, Amalgam Arca Heqet, and Amalgam Osprey will not create a Spectralyst.
- A Spectralyst will not retain the loadout it copied, the Shadow will use the base loadout.
- A dying Thrax Legatus or Thrax Centurion will not become incorporeal.
- A decayed or killed Eidolon Vomvalyst will become incorporeal instead of dying.
- If an Eidolon Lure is tethered to such Shadow - it will be charged, and the Shadow will be consumed.

- A Tusk Dargyn/ Tusk Shield Dargyn Shadow spawns complete with a Dargyn Pilot model, can fly like normal, and counts as 1 Shadow.
- Upon death it will spawn a Dargyn Pilot enemy.
- Killing an empty or unmarked Tusk Dargyn/ Tusk Shield Dargyn will not create a Shadow.

- A Mimic will spawn disguised and will reveal itself only when approached by a player.
- It will occasionally try to move away from players and into another room, to disguise itself.

- Kosma Gokstad Officer, Anarch Grineer Trapper, and Summulyst will deploy allied drones, which are not Shadows and will not die when their master dies.
- Same thing happens with Techs, Vapos Tech Ranger, Corrupted MOA, and Fusion MOA except their drones will die.

- A dying Mitosid will split into allied Basal Diploid and Feral Diploid, which are not Shadows.
- A Mutalist Osprey will spawn complete with a Crawler Shadow level 1 and drop it.
- It will not pick up Crawlers.

- A Deimos Tendril Drone will not face-hug enemies.
- A dying Deimos Undying Flyer will deploy a pod, which is immediately destroyed (probably by the Shadow decay).
- An Anarch Grineer Lancer will occasionally become a suicide bomber, even with no enemy presence (probably because of the Shadow decay).
- An Anarch Tenebra will open enemies up to Ground Finishers with its lurk attack.
- A Scaldra Barbican's shield can break with enough damage.

Kit Interactions

- Soul Punch makes the target 'Marked for Harvest' for 3 seconds (before dealing damage), creating their Shadow if killed while marked.
- If the max Shadows count is reached, all active Shadows are healed to full health instead.
- 'Marked for Harvest' can create multiple copies of the same enemy.
- Enemies with Overguard cannot be marked.
- If Shadows of the Dead was subsumed over, the Shadows do not receive bonus stats.
- Some shadows can be created only via Soul Punch.
- Soul Punch helps pick and choose desirable targets (e.g. Eximus), though the instakill mechanic may waste a unit if it has Overguard.

Augment

Main article: Shield of Shadows
Shield of Shadows is a Warframe Augment Mod that allows Nekros to redirect a percentage of damage he receives to any active Shadows of the Dead minions within 50 meters.

Tips & Tricks

- Entropic Eximus can be summoned only if their globe was broken before they were killed.
- Generally Enemy Damage Scaling outpaces Enemy Health Scaling, thus Shadows become better killers at higher levels.
- 600% Ability Strength is required for really good KPM at base Steel Path, or less strength but more buffs for the Shadows. See #Info -> Shadow Properties.

- Shadows can be scanned, allowing for faster enemy research and granting some amount of Standing or Affinity.
- If you cast Shadows of the Dead right before entering Bleedout, all Shadows will spawn anyway.
- Shadows can soften up enemies, making it easier to revive with Last Gasp.

- Shadows draw enemy attention, they are effective at defending objectives or areas of a map.
- Shadows are not reliable defenders when there are more than 7 enemies, or enemies are using explosives.

- Actively target and focus your attacks on enemies you want to clone prior to casting the ability.
- Because you still take full damage during the long summoning animation, it might be useful to cast Terrify just before using this skill.
- Shield buffing Shadows (i.e. Orokin Drone) can be used as a temporary survival measure in Survival when the oxygen runs out, as disconnecting/connecting to them will instantly regenerate your shield.
- Shadows of the Dead pairs extremely well with a subsumed Blood Altar, as that ability's healing aura greatly outpaces the passive health decay that Shadows suffer, as long as they stay within its radius. With attentive use, Altars are capable of keeping Shadows alive indefinitely, even under heavy fire and the use of Shield of Shadows.
- Sancti Magistar's Lifesteal can easily outheal the Health decay of summons on top of healing Nekros.

Bugs

- Theorem Infection buffs have infinite duration on the Shadows.
- Anarch Libritor will not call an orbital strike.
- Anarch Capsarii will not cast a shield, it will generally stay away from enemies and rarely cast a push-back wave.
- Non-shadow units summoned by a Shadow will block Defense progress, the wave will not complete until they die.
- A Roller Shadow will often roll around aimlessly and get stuck.
- All Shadows block players' bullets, or even break them.
- Some embedded projectiles will disappear when striking a Shadow (e.g. Proboscis Cernos, both fire modes of Afentis).
- Speargun alt-fire will disappear when striking a Shadow, except for Scourge which normally cannot be embedded into enemies.

- A Deimos Carnis Rex Shadow collides with players.
- Specifically a Tusk Dargyn Shadow provides 5% less damage protection than all other Shadows when using Shield of Shadows.
- Shadows of these enemies do not deploy their respective drones: Seeker, Corpus Tech (but not its variants), and Tusk Grineer.
- Shadows of these enemies do not deploy their respective drones only in Simulacrum: Corrupted MOA, and Fusion MOA.
- Amalgam Phase MOA Shadow's main fire knocks down players when exploding.
- Thrax Legatus and Thrax Centurion Shadows will ignore all their former allies (your enemies) except enemy Thrax units.
- Sometimes Shadows can look like normal enemies, once they perform an action which temporatily changes their appearance.
- Derivator Crewman Shadow's shield ability will collide with the players and will damage them.
- Kuva Trokarian Shadow will still throw Kuva Trokar and force Transference, though they will be aiming at enemies.
- Rarely, a shadow or two will not die when the duration ends, and will continue to exist until they die.
- A Sapping Osprey shadow's sapping field will not have Nekros' energy colour, and the orb can be destroyed by allies. The field will not damage allies, however.
- If this ability is used to revive a Grineer Prosecutor, the Lotus will prompt you that she is detecting an enemy one. Respectively, she will pronounce that they have been killed once they decay, or the ability ends.
