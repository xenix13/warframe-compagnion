# Soul Punch (capacité de Nekros) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Soul_Punch

| 1 25 | Soul Punch A blow so powerful, it turns the enemy's very soul into a deadly projectile, damaging all in its path. Enemies that survive the blow are Marked for Harvest and become one of Nekros' Shadows of the Dead if killed while marked. Introduced in Update 10.0 (2013-09-13) | Strength:100 / 200 / 350 / 500 (initial Impact damage) 

Duration:N/A 

Range:20 / 30 / 40 / 50 m (cast range) 

Misc: Knockback on hit 25 % (instant kill threshold) 50 (projectile Impact damage) 100 (area Impact damage) ~5 m (explosion radius) 3 s (marked for harvest duration) 

Info

- Nekros expends 25 energy to strike a single targeted enemy within 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 meters with telekinetic force, dealing 100 / 200 / 350 / 500 Impact damage and Knockback. If the target is at or below 25% health, it is instantly killed.
- The target's soul is violently ejected from its body, becoming a high-velocity Ragdoll projectile. Enemies that make contact with the projectile are dealt 50 Impact damage. When the projectile makes contact with a hard surface, 200 Impact damage is inflicted in a ~5 meter area of effect.
- The direct hit damage is doubled for each enemy directly hit by the projectile, exponentially increasing the damage with each hit. The AoE damage gains the same damage multiplier as the projectile had at the time of impact.
- Direct hit and AoE damage are affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
- AoE damage has no falloff over its radius.
- Affected enemies are knocked back, and both damage sources can be increased with headshots.

- Despite having a noticeable visual and audio effect, the skill is perfectly silent and can be used as a means of disposing enemies without alerting nearby units during stealth playthrough.
- However, if an enemy is not killed by the ability, the enemies will be alerted.

- Can be used while performing many actions without interrupting them, including reloading, charging, and shooting.
- Cannot instakill: Bosses, Necramech (Enemy), Raknoids, Anyo Corp, Demolishers, Gruzzling, The Severed Warden, Corpus Trencher Target and Gyre Hyena, Kuva Guardian, Eidolon Lure, and Arbitration Shield Drone.

Kit Interactions

- Soul Punch makes the target 'Marked for Harvest' for 3 seconds (before dealing damage), creating their Shadow if killed while marked.
- If the max Shadows count is reached, all active Shadows are healed to full health instead.
- 'Marked for Harvest' can create multiple copies of the same enemy.
- Enemies with Overguard cannot be marked.
- If Shadows of the Dead was subsumed over, the Shadows do not receive bonus stats.
- Some shadows can be created only via Soul Punch. See Summoning Priority.

Augment

Main article: Soul Survivor
Soul Survivor is a Warframe Augment Mod that allows Nekros to use Soul Punch on allies in bleedout to revive them with partial health.

Tips & Tricks
See also: Shadows of the Dead#Shadow Properties.

- Entropic Eximus can be marked or instakilled only if their globe is broken.
- Entropic Eximus were designed against Tenno, as as result their Shadows are extremely tanky against enemies, they retain their damage reduction and literally nothing can break their globe.

- Weapons and abilities with tethered pull (e.g. Harpak, Larva, Proboscis, Proboscis Cernos) can cancel out the knockback effect.
- Shadows can be scanned, allowing for faster enemy research and granting some amount of Standing or Affinity.
- The ability can instakill Turrets, but will not create a Shadow.
- The ability can instakill Mitosid and Mitosid Rex.
- This is the only way to make a Shadow of that enemy, since normal damage splits it into 2 other enemies.

- The ability can instakill a Spectralyst, though the Shadow will not be a copy of your Warframe - it will use the base loadout.
- This is the only way to make a Shadow of this unit, since it does not appear in the "recently-killed" list.

- The ability can instakill flying Tusk Dargyn and Tusk Shield Dargyn, kicking out the Dargyn Pilot (usually alive).
- The ability can instakill corporeal Thrax Centurion, Thrax Legatus, and Eidolon Vomvalyst - allowing you to avoid the need to build a strong Amp or upgrade a Focus school.
- This is the only way to make Shadows of those enemies, since normal damage makes them incorporeal.

- The ability can apply multiple Marks on the same enemy. Quickly kill that enemy to create multiple copies of it at once (within max Shadow count).
- The ability will not damage other Tenno in the Conclave, however the knockback effect will still occur.
- The ability can launch ragdolls up with great speed, send surrounding enemies flying, or throw enemies Out of Bounds when:
- Enemies are grouped tightly together.
- There is an object right behind the enemy.
- Ability projectile can hit the ground near the target.

- The ability can be used to force Ground Finishers upon enemies.
- Reviving allies using Soul Survivor augment mod also counts toward Challenges that count Revives.
