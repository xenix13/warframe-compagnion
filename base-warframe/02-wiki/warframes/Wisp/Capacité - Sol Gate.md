Info

- Wisp expends 25 energy to tear open and sustains a portal to the sun to harness its destructive power. While sustaining the portal, Wisp focuses the solar plasma into a single beam with curving flares over a length of 40 meters; the beam possesses infinite Punch Through against enemy bodies, and inflicts 500 / 550 / 625 / 750 damage alternating between Heat and Radiation every half second with a 100% status chance of the respective element type. Each enemy that receives damage from the beam becomes individually more vulnerable to the respective element dealt by the beam, with beam damage increasing by 100% per hit of an element up to a maximum of 1000%, meaning it takes up to 10 seconds of sustained damage to each enemy to maximize the damage they receive. Press the ability key (default 4 ) while active to deactivate this ability.
- Damage Ramp is a per-damge-type, per-enemy debuff that persists until the ability is deactivated.
- Wisp recoils backward when the ability deactivates.
- Holding the fire button (default LMB ) doubles the beam's base damage to 1,000 / 1,100 / 1,250 / 1,500 per hit. Wisp's movement speed is slowed by 50% while boosting Sol Gate.
- It can be more energy efficient to debuff targets before activating this level of the ability, as the damage ramp is unaffected by it.

- Damage is unaffected by mod damage bonuses, but is affected by body part multipliers such as headshots.
- Sol Gate consumes 12 energy per second (24 energy per second during the boosted version) while active and will remain active until Wisp's energy is depleted or the ability is deactivated by pressing the ability key again.
- Activation energy cost is affected by Ability Efficiency, and the energy drain is affected by Ability Efficiency and Ability Duration.
- Wisp cannot replenish energy using Energy Vampire, Rift Plane's innate energy regeneration, Squad Energy Restores, Energy Siphon, Energy Nexus, Dreamer's Bond, and/or Wellspring while Sol Gate is active.
- Energy can be replenished by Energy Orbs, Arcane Energize, Orokin Void Death Orb energy restores, Rage, and/or Hunter Adrenaline while the ability is active.
- Sol Gate's energy drain is not converted into shields by Augur Mods.

- Wisp cannot cast Reservoirs or Breach Surge, perform parkour Maneuvers or sprinting except dodge rolls, or other general interactions while channeling this ability.
- Wisp is immune to knockdown while channeling this ability.
- Energy beams, energy particles, and energy surge color tinting are affected by Wisp's chosen Warframe energy color.

Kit Interactions

- Currently obtained Motes provide additional properties to Sol Gate.
- Vitality: Provides a 25% damage increase.
- Haste: Allows Sol Gate to apply a Corrosive status effect on every damage instance.
- Does not grant Corrosive damage.

- Shock: Provides a 25% damage increase.
- Mote Damage bonuses add together to 50%, a multiplicative bonus to Sol Gate's final damage.

- Reservoir Motes orbiting Wisp will instead orbit around the portal. When Sol Gate is deactivated, Motes will return to Wisp again.

- Wil-O-Wisp can be cast during Sol Gate to cloak, spawn a clone and teleport to it when desired.
- Has a 100% chance to trigger Breach Surge's surge spark.

Augment

Main article: Cataclysmic Gate
Cataclysmic Gate is an Warframe Augment Mod for Wisp's Sol Gate that causes her and two spectral images of herself to launch a single Sol Gate that leaves behind a field that inflicts Heat and Radiation damage with guaranteed status effects over time.
