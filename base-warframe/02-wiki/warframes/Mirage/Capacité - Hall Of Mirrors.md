Info

- Mirage expends 25 energy to project 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 holographic mirror images of herself that follow and mimic her actions for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds. Each hologram is invulnerable to damage, draws enemy attention, and deals 5% / 10% / 15% / 20% of the active weapon's damage.
- For performance reasons, only a maximum of 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 holograms are able to fire primary and secondary weapons. As such, each hologram that fires actually deals 5% / 20% / 22.5% / 40% of the active primary or secondary weapon's damage.
- Hall of Mirrors' damage multiplier also reduces critical/status chance by the same amount as damage, unless the multiplier is pushed to 100% or more via having 250%+ Ability Strength.

- All holograms will use melee attacks, but each only deals 5% / 10% / 15% / 20% of the melee weapon's damage.
- Hall of Mirrors' damage multiplier also reduces critical/status chance by the same amount as damage, unless the multiplier is pushed to 100% or more via having 500%+ Ability Strength.
- Holograms do not benefit from the Melee Combo Counter.
- Holograms can throw their glaives, but not detonate them.

- The stats of the holograms' weapon are inherited from the modded stats of the active weapon.
- Holograms do not benefit from Arcane Enhancements or Galvanized Mods' stacking buffs.
- There are some exceptions to this, in cases where the Arcanes are applied as infinite-duration, simple buffs. Melee Retaliation and Melee Doughty will function based on the holograms' stats.
- Secondary Fortifier will make the holograms deal increased damage to Overguard, but will not grant overguard to Mirage.

- Holograms do not benefit from the ability damage bonus granted by Emerald Archon Shard, Topaz Archon Shard, or Violet Archon Shard.
- Melee weapons, including those with ranged capabilities such as Glaives, Gunblades, Zenistar, Sigma & Octantis, and others, will deal damage from all holograms.
- All holograms deal Bullet Jump damage, modified by Rending Turn and other Bullet Jump mods.
- Casting delay of 1 second, affected by Casting Speed.

- Once spawned, the holograms remain fixed in position around Mirage.
- Holograms partially fade from view when the HUD's targeting reticle is near them.
- Holograms will mimic Mirage's aim, which can shift away from the HUD's targeting reticle depending on their position.
- Holograms do not path along terrain and are intangible, allowing them to phase through obstacles and walk on air.
- Kills by holograms count as ability kills, and give all Affinity to Mirage instead of the weapon used.
- Incarnon weapons that gain incarnon charge from weapon hits do not gain incarnon charge from shots fired by holograms.

- Holograms cannot damage Blunts with melee attacks.
- Holograms will not retain any equipped accessories.
- Holograms will be dispelled once they come into contact with Nullifier Crewman bubbles. When all the holograms have been dispelled, Hall of Mirrors ends and it can be recast immediately.
- Holograms can benefit from most buffs that affect "allies", including Nourish and Total Eclipse, however some such buffs are not included, such as Thermal Transfer.

Kit Interactions

- While Hall of Mirrors is active, casting Sleight of Hand will cause the holograms to create additional smaller jewels with reduced effectiveness.
- Holograms do not gain Eclipse's Solar Eclipse damage bonus unless Total Eclipse is equipped, as they count as 'allies' rather than part of Mirage herself.

Tips & Tricks

- The damage from Thunderbolt is unaffected by Hall of Mirrors' damage multiplier.
- When low on health, using Hall of Mirrors will give you a better chance to survive because enemies will be more likely to target your holograms than you.
- Drawing enemy fire while the holograms are fairly close to Mirage can prove to be disadvantageous in some instances, especially when dealing with Napalms or Bombards, as their explosive AoE weapons will hit the holograms and be close enough to still damage you.
- Hall of Mirrors can be extremely useful when reviving a downed teammate.
- Hall of Mirrors is great for large bosses and groups of enemies, as the slight spread from each hologram allows you to cover more area near your crosshair.
- This is particularly effective when using a primary weapon equipped with Primary Compression, as the holograms' shots will not have their areas of effect reduced, due to them being unaffected by Arcanes.

- When used in conjunction with the Paracyst's Alternate Fire, each hologram will fire its own hook capable of pulling an enemy.
- When Mirage is inside Limbo's Rift, the holograms are still able to damage enemies outside the Rift, as long as they are outside of it themselves.
- This makes it possible to destroy Nullifier Crewman shields without exposing yourself. (May only work as host, needs confirmation)

- Bullet Jumps performed by holograms will still do damage and break containers, which can be useful when looting.
- Melee weapon synergy: many melee weapons have special/unique effects on heavy/jump/slide/slam attacks. Below is a list of melee weapons whose unique effects will be duplicated by the holograms:
- Zenistar creating its disc with heavy attack.
- Tenet Grigori creating discs on slide heavy/second heavy attack.

Bugs

- Arsenal lists the damage multiplier as 20%, but for ranged weapons it is actually 40% (20% is correct for melee weapons).
- This bug likely exists due to the performance patch from 2015 which cut the number of weapon-firing Holograms in half, but doubled their ranged weapon damage to compensate.

- In some circumstances, a group of holograms can get "stuck" in one place. This bug only affects clients, and is only visible to the bugged player.
- While holograms cannot benefit from the buffs provided by Arcane Enhancements, they can apparently trigger the conditions for any Arcane in your loadout. This includes Melee Influence, Pax Seeker, etc.
- After Syam's continuous Slam Attack concludes, the holograms immediately perform an additional Slam Attack. The base damage of this extra attack is one-fifth of their normal damage.
