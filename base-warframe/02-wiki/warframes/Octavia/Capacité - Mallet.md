# Mallet (capacité de Octavia) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mallet

| 1 25 | Mallet Rhythmically beats damage into nearby enemies and draws their fire. Damage inflicted on the Mallet increases its lethality. Introduced in Update 20.0 (2017-03-24) | Strength:1x / 1.5x / 2x / 2.5x (damage multiplier) 

Duration:8 / 12 / 16 / 20 s 

Range:5 / 6 / 8 / 10 m 

Misc: 1 (limit of mallets) 

Info

- Octavia expends 25 energy to throw a spherical music device in an arc toward the direction she aims. Upon impact, the device deploys into the Mallet which hovers in place for 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 seconds. The Mallet is an invulnerable hovering orb that continuously plays percussive music, attracting enemies in sight to attack it while absorbing and accumulating all enemy damage hitting its energy mass. Stored damage is converted into Blast damage, multiplied by 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 2.5x, and rhythmically dealt to all enemies within a radius of 5 / 6 / 8 / 10 meters and Line of Sight from the Mallet.
- Mallet does not have increased Threat Level; enemies will be likely to shoot the Tenno if they are in line of sight.
- Mallet's music is in tune with the Percussion section of the Mandachord, drawing from the preset musical notes to determine the percussive aura's damage strength and intervals.
- Each beat that has at least one percussive note will emit a pulse of damage, dealing 100% of the Mallet's currently stored damage (equal to 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 2.5x the damage received by the Mallet). Having multiple percussive notes on the same beat will not increase the damage dealt.
- Before dealing damage on a beat, the total stored damage decays by: 1 divided by the number of percussive beats in the whole song. For example, if all 64 beats in the song have at least one percussive note, the stored damage will be reduced by 1/64th (1.5625%) of its current amount before each damaging beat.
- The placement of the beats does not matter, only the number of beats. Having 32 percussive beats will cause the absorbed damage to decay by 1/32nd per beat, regardless of all 32 beats being grouped together or evenly spaced throughout the song.
- Damage decay only occurs on beats with at least one percussive note. Rest beats (beats with no percussive notes) will not incur any damage decay.
- Having only one percussive beat in the entire song will cause the Mallet to deal 0 damage regardless of the amount of damage absorbed, as the damage will decay by 100% before each damaging beat.

- In addition to absorbed damage being multiplied by 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 2.5x, rest beats will also further multiply absorbed damage from a hit. Each rest beat between the time the Mallet receives a hit from an enemy and before the Mallet's next percussive beat will add a separate +100% damage multiplier to that individual hit's damage.
- Take a song with a repeating pattern of 1 percussive beat followed by 7 rest beats. If the Mallet absorbs a hit right after a percussive beat, there will be 7 rest beats between the absorbed hit and the next percussive beat, meaning the hit will gain +700% damage before being added to Mallet's stored damage, therefore multiplying the hit's damage by 8x. A separate hit dealt only 2 beats before the percussive note will only have 2 rest beats to increase its damage, therefore only gaining a 3x damage multiplier. These values are then further multiplied by 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 2.5x before being stored into Mallet.
- The rest beat multiplier is only applied once for each individual hit received by the Mallet; after the Mallet emits a damage pulse on a percussive beat, hits from before that beat will not accumulate more damage on subsequent rest beats.

- Up to 1 Mallet can be deployed at a time. Deploying a new Mallet will remove the current one.
- Casting Mallet is not a One-Handed Action and interrupts other actions, but allows movement.
- Throwing animation speed is affected by Casting Speed.
- Mallet will visually emit sound bars from its core to beat-match Percussion notes. Sound bars enlarge and shrink based on Percussion, Bass, and Melody music in the environment.
- Percussion bars appear from a horizontal ring around the Mallet core, while Bass and Melody bars appear from diagonal rings that layer on top the Mallet when Resonator and Metronome are active and nearby.

- Mallet's core and percussive aura borders are affected by the chosen Warframe energy color. The percussive aura is briefly visible to allies when Mallet is cast before fading away, and is constantly visible to Octavia herself.

Kit Interactions

- When Mallet and Resonator are both active, Resonator will reroute toward the Mallet and carry it around.
- Enemies charmed by Resonator are no longer pacified and will attack a carried Mallet to fuel its damage.
- Resonator increases Mallet's Threat Level, making enemies outside of Resonator's charm radius more likely to shoot the Mallet instead of the Tenno.
- If Mallet is placed where Resonator is unable to reach, Resonator will ignore Mallet.
- Mallet will be dropped to the ground when Resonator expires.

- When Mallet is hovering over an Amp field, Mallet's range doubled.
- Damage is not doubled, contrary to the ability description.

Augment

Tips & Tricks

- Enemies who have both ranged and melee weapons will prefer to use melee.
- Octavia can position behind Mallet to minimize her chance of taking enemy fire.
- Be careful of explosive effects drawn to Mallet's center of origin.

- Mallet's localized control effect is particularly potent against groups caught in narrow choke points.
- Mallet can damage nullifier bubbles, preventing nulifiers from removing abilities centered within the mallet.
