# Rift Surge (capacité de Limbo) (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rift_Surge

| 3 50 | Rift Surge Surges nearby Rift-bound enemies with Rift energy. When killed the Rift Surge is transferred to a nearby enemy outside the rift. Surged enemies that leave the Rift perform a radial Banish. Introduced in Update 15.0 (2014-10-24) | Strength:N/A 

Duration:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 s (surge duration) 6 / 10 / 14 / 18 s (banish duration) 

Range:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 m (surge & transfer range) 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 m (banish radius) 

Misc: 300 (Rift transitional Impact damage) 

Info

- Limbo expends 50 energy to apply the Rift Surge status to all enemies in the Rift plane within a radius of 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 meters around him for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds.
- The Rift Surge status can activate two different effects: "Radial banish" and "Transfer"
- Radial banish: Enemies with Rift Surge that leave the Rift will activate Radial Banish and banish all enemies within 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 meters, including themselves, back into the Rift for 6 / 10 / 14 / 18 seconds.
- After activation the Rift Surge status is removed.

- Transfer: Killing an enemy with Rift Surge in the Rift Plane will transfer its Rift Surge status to a random nearby enemy unit within 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 meters in the material plane.
- This will not move them to the Rift plane. The radial banish only activates if they enter and then leave the Rift.
- The duration does not reset on transfer.
- Killing an enemy with Rift Surge in the material plane will do nothing and the status simply dies with them.
- Enemies with Rift Surge in the material plane count towards Rift Torrent

- Limbo can cast this from any plane and it will only target enemies in the Rift.
- Cannot be recast unless an unaffected enemy enters the Rift and comes within cast range.
- Cast animation of 1 second, affected by Casting Speed.
- Unaffected by Overguard

- For more information on Rift-related attributes, see Rift Plane.

Kit Interactions

- Banish and Cataclysm can put enemies in the Rift, as well as trigger the radial banish by sending enemies back into the material plane.
- Dispel for Banish functions on enemy targets affected by Rift Surge's radial banish. Can be cast in succession to keep pulling more enemies into the Rift(1, 3, Hold 1, 3, Hold 1, etc..)
- Dispelling or the shrinking of cataclysm can make enemies leave the Rift

- The Rift Surge status on enemies in the material plane is most often activated in this way:
- Enemy with Rift Surge in the material plane walks into Cataclysm with Stasis active.
- The enemy is frozen on the border of Cataclysm.
- Cataclysm shrinks and the enemy is released from the Rift.
- Radial banish activates sending them and everyone around them back into the Rift.

Augment

Main article: Rift Torrent
Rift Torrent is a Warframe Augment Mod for Limbo's Rift Surge that increases Limbo's damage while in the Rift Plane for each enemy affected by Rift Surge.

Tips & Tricks

- Casting speed is highly recommended to make full use of this ability.
- Rift Surge is designed to keep enemies inside the Rift by extending the banish duration, as well as bringing new enemies into the Rift without having to leave the rift.
- The following techniques and occurrences can trigger Rift Surge's banish effect on charged enemies:
- Recast or dispel Banish on banished enemies, causing them to momentarily leave the Rift plane.
- The duration of Banish or Rift Surge's radial banish ends on affected enemies.
- Detonating Cataclysm to bring enemies inside it to the material plane (unless affected by Banish or Rift Surge radial banish).

- Due to the nature of how Rift Torrent works the damage buff can become very big. Each enemy affected by Rift Surge gives an extra 30% at base. Banishing just 30 enemies will give a damage buff of 900%.
- The combination of Banish and Rift Surge (with high range or grouping) can substitute some of the utilities of cataclysm, freeing up an ability slot.
- Good abilities to subsume onto Limbo include Breach Surge, Null Star, Silence and Roar/ Eclipse
- The low radial banish range of Rift Surge can be mitigated using a grouping ability or weapon. Examples include Airburst, Scyotid, Void Snare or Magus Anomaly
