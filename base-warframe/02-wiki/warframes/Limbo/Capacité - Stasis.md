Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stasis

Info

- Limbo expends 50 energy to freeze all enemies in the Rift for 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 seconds. Additionally, all enemy projectiles are frozen for the duration with a capacity of 300 projectiles.
- Exceeding the projectile cap will end the ability prematurely.
- Stasis has no range limit.
- Cast and deactivation animations of 1 second, affected by Casting Speed.
- Does not freeze certain enemies, including but not limited to: Acolytes and units with Overguard.
- Overguard or any other cc immunity does not stop their bullets from being frozen.
- Stasis will freeze enemies once the overguard is removed.

- When stasis ends, all enemies in the Rift will resume their actions prior to Stasis and all frozen projectiles will start moving depending on priority:
- All hit-scan bullets are released simultaneously.
- Projectiles are released one by one, by order of creation.
- Manual deactivation is a one handed action.

- Prevents the use of Melee Afflictions and Melee Vortex on enemies affected by stasis.
- Enemies affected by stasis will cause additional enemies to spawn in certain mission types. The enemies spawned are equal to the number of enemies currently affected and up to double the original amount possible in a mission..
- Missions with this interaction are: Survival, Mobile Defense, Void Cascade and Extermination.
- Defense, Disruption and Excavation are unaffected

- When used repeatedly against Sentient enemies, Stasis' freeze effect will gradually diminish to a fraction of its total duration for the affected enemies. Sentients exposed to this ability repeatedly will be able to move and attack after being frozen for a short duration, eventually becoming immune to Stasis altogether.
- Despite being able to move and attack, the ranged attacks of Sentients are still frozen for as long as Stasis remains active.
- If the Sentients exit and re-enter the Rift plane. Stasis' freeze effect will renew with shortened duration.

- For more information on Rift-related attributes, see Rift Plane.

Kit Interactions

- Stasis continuously freezes all enemies in the rift, including those Riftbound by Banish, Rift Surge, or Cataclysm.
- If there are multiple Limbo players in a squad, Stasis will not deactivate until either the weapon fire object limit is reached or the latest Stasis instance ends.

Tips & Tricks

- Activating Stasis before banishing enemies is an effective way of protecting yourself whilst in the Rift.
- Even though enemies with Overguard are able to move, the survivability Stasis brings is largely unaffected. Bullets shot by overguarded units will still be frozen.
- While in the Rift with Stasis active, outside of Eximus abilities and edge cases, melee Overguarded units are the only enemy type that are able to hit Limbo reliably.

- Having Stasis active while being in Cataclysm, will prevent you from taking damage from enemies that enter Cataclysm's radius.
- Synthesis targets and Feral Kavats can be put in Stasis, making it easier to scan them.
- If an enemy is affected by Petrify before being frozen with stasis, the Petrify status effect will last for the entire duration of stasis.
- Vampiric Liminus can be put into the rift and be frozen by stasis. Effectively rendering them harmless.
- Stasis pauses enemy AI, because of this unalerted enemies frozen by stasis will never raise to a higher alert level. Likewise, the stealth damage bonus will never be removed and stay active for the entire duration of Stasis. However the player must use blind, Silence or be cloaked(through Evade, Shade or Huras Kubrow) before the enemy is frozen by stasis for this to take affect.
- Stasis prevents the use of many grouping abilities and weapons. This is a non-exhaustive list of the ones that DO work:
- Weapons:
- Gammacor Incarnon
- Tenet Plinx

- Subsumes:
- Coil Horizon
- Airburst

- Other:
- Magus Anomaly
- Cordon
