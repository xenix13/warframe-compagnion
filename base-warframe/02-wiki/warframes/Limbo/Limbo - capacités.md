# Limbo/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Limbo/Abilities

## Passive

Main article: Rift Plane
Limbo's abilities allow access to the Rift plane ("Rift", for short), an alternate dimension to the normal environment, or material plane. Interaction between the Rift and material planes is highly limited. Generally, for both players and enemies, weapons will not function across planes, but Abilities will. Anyone in the Rift can be forcibly removed from it by entering a Nullifier's bubble. Any player can leave the Rift voluntarily by performing a Roll, Sidespring, or Backspring.

Enemies that transition between planes will receive Knockdown and 300 Impact damage, unaffected by any modifiers. Enemies in the Rift that are killed by Limbo will grant him 10 Energy (regardless of whether Limbo is himself in the Rift).

Warframes in the Rift gain 2 Energy per second, except when using most Channeled Abilities. Companions will enter and exit the Rift alongside their owner Warframe. Players in the Rift cannot collect Pickups, except when inside Cataclysm. They also cannot initiate most Context Actions, with the notable exception of revival. Operators cannot enter the Rift under any circumstance, meaning they cannot damage enemies in the Rift, but are not impeded from interacting with the material plane.

While a player is in the Rift, there will be an unique ambient sound, certain things like enemies and allies will appear to have thick black outlines, and wispy black waves will appear beneath the player. If Color Correction is enabled, the player's vision will be tinted yellow while they are in the Rift; otherwise, their vision will be tinted white. Allies in the Rift gain a white moving outline. Enemies in the Rift can be identified by a gray flame near their feet and a "shimmering" effect on their body; these are affected by the Visual Effects Intensity Setting. None of these are affected by Limbo's Appearance.

Certain weapons, Arcanes and Precepts can interact across planes; see here for more information.

Limbo's Roll, Sidespring and Backspring are modified to a slide which transitions him between the Rift and material planes, commonly referred to as Rift Walk.

When Rift Walking in the material plane, Limbo enters the Rift and leaves behind a portal for 5 seconds. Limbo remains in the Rift until he Rift Walks back to the material plane or is otherwise removed from the Rift. Any allies or enemies that touch the portal will be Banished to the Rift for 15 seconds. Limbo himself will always be able to use any portal to enter the Rift as if he had Rift Walked, but he does not gain the 15 seconds of Banish. The duration of the portal and the Banish effect are unaffected by Limbo's Ability stats.

| 1 25 | Banish Casts a wave of Rift energy that damages hostiles while pushing enemies and allies out of Limbo's current plane of existence. Introduced in Update 15.0 (2014-10-24) | Strength:150 / 200 / 225 / 250 ( Impact damage) 

Duration:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 s 

Range:20 / 25 / 30 / 35 m (wave distance) 

Misc: 5 m (cone initial radius) 15° (spread angle) ∞ (dispel range) 2 e/s (Rift energy regen per second) 300 (Rift transitional Impact damage) 

Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Limbo expends 25 energy to banish enemies or allies in front of him out of Limbo's current plane of existence into either the Rift or the material plane.
- The area of affect is a truncated cone of 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 meters long, with a base radius of 5 meters and a 15° spread angle.
- The Area extends a little bit behind Limbo and is aimed around the reticle.

- Enemies or allies banished to the Rift stay there for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds;
- When cast in the material plane only targets in the material plane will be send to the Rift; When cast in the Rift only targets in the Rift will be send to the material plane.

- Does not bypass obstacles and requires line of sight with targets.

- Casting Banish is a One-Handed Action.
- Cast animation of 0.7 seconds, affected by Casting Speed.

- Holding down the ability key (default: 1 ) removes all targets from the rift at no additional energy cost.
- Banish has different effects when cast on an ally or enemy:

- Targets in different planes can still have collision.
- Weapon attacks are only effective when both parties are in the same plane. Otherwise attacks will phase through their target. See Rift Plane for exceptions.
- Abilities can hit their targets no matter the plane they are on.
- This includes Exalted Weapon attacks.

- Players affected by Banish can leave the Rift plane by rolling (default Shift ).
- Warframes regenerate 2 energy per second. This energy recharge is constant and cannot be interrupted, except by some abilities or by exiting the Rift.
- Banished players can still be hit by most bosses' special abilities, as well as ability nullifying effects from Nullifier Crewman, Comba, Scrambus, Datalyst, Isolator Bursa, Disruptor Drone and the Stalker.
- Players in the Rift cannot prompt the interact function (default X ) on most interactive objects in the environment.
- Players in the Rift can still revive and be revived by other players outside the Rift.
- Portable items (such as datamasses, power cores and void keys) can only be picked up inside Cataclysm or outside the Rift.

- Banished players cannot collect pickups unless they are in Cataclysm. However, a Companion's Vacuum or Fetch precepts can gather pickups in midair until the player exits the Rift plane.
- Players in the Rift Plane are given an icon next to their name to indicate they are in the Rift plane.
- Banish can't target Companions and static Defense Objects (including Temple).

- Enemies affected by Banish receive Knockdown and are dealt 150 / 200 / 225 / 250 Impact damage in addition to the transitional damage.
- Rift transitional damage is not affected by mods.
- If an enemy banished to the Rift is killed outright by Banish's Impact damage, Limbo still receives 10 energy from his passive.

- Enemies are visually coated in rippling energy and monochrome flames while in the Rift plane.
- It can affect some bosses, such as Jackal with diminishing effectiveness on duration, as well as Sortie Tenno operatives, Rescue targets, and Assassins (e.g. Zanuka and The Grustrag Three).

- Cannot be cast without at least 1 target on the same plane as Limbo, and within the area of effect.
- Can only be dispelled when at least 1 ally or enemy is affected by Banish or Rift Surge.
- Enemies with Overguard are affected by this ability.
- Subsuming Limbo to the Helminth will offer Banish and its augments to be used by other Warframes.
- Subsumed Banish includes the hold-cast to remove all targets from the Rift.

- For more information on Rift-related attributes, see the Rift Plane article.

Kit Interactions

- All enemies banished into the Rift are frozen in place by Stasis.
- Dispel Banish on enemies in the Rift affected by Rift Surge to make them leave the rift and thus immediately trigger the radial banish
- Dispel Banish works on enemies banished by Rift Surge's radial banish.

- Cast Banish on enemies inside Cataclysm to briefly blink them out and back into the Rift, dealing damage and knockdown.

Tips & Tricks

- Before casting Banish, remind yourself which plane you are currently in, either the material plane or the Rift plane. Banish only works on eligible targets that exist in the same plane as you, and pushes them into the opposite plane.
- Enemies will be damaged and knocked down by Banish, allowing you and teammates to dispatch them with melee ground finisher attacks.
- Ally Warframes will be pushed alongside nearby enemies into the opposite plane if caught in Banish's area of effect. This enables ally players to immediately use weapon attacks on banished enemies that need to first recover from knockdown.
- To avoid confusion, give a friendly reminder to fellow players that they can perform a dodge (default Shift ) to end their Banish status and exit the Rift at will.
- Alternatively, hold down the ability key to phase them back to the material plane.

- Use Limbo's passive (dodging, default Shift ) after casting Banish to enter the opposite plane, allowing you to attack banished enemies with your weapons.
- Toggle on Stasis before casting Banish. Enemies banished into the Rift plane while Stasis is active will immediately freeze in suspended animation, as they are receiving Banish's knockdown effect which will resume animation once Stasis ends.
- Use this method to add a delayed crowd control effect to Rift-bound enemies, buying you time to recast Stasis or kill them before they fully recover from the knockdown.

- Cast Rift Surge on Rift-bound enemies, then hold down Banish to make surged enemies discharge, banishing themselves and nearby material-bound enemies back into the Rift, without expending energy. Repeat this ability combo to continuously draw enemies into the Rift while you stay safe inside.
- This can be a good alternative to casting Cataclysm, as it can be unwelcomed by other players, and also to avoid accidentally banishing unwilling teammates.

- While you are in the Rift plane, cast Banish on enemies inside a Cataclysm to damage and knockdown your targets for additional crowd control. Those enemies will remain Rift-bound due to Cataclysm, allowing you to immediately strike them down with your weapons.
- Banish a Rescue target to protect them from harm while you head towards extraction.
- Likewise, Banish the operative in Defense missions in Sorties and Arbitrations to keep them safe from enemy fire.

- Banish can also be used on Hijack objectives .
- Can be used on Loki's Decoy, Saryn's Molt or Chroma's Effigy to make them immune to damage from enemies that are not in the Rift plane.
- Can also be cast on Shield Osprey specters, which is very useful considering that Shield Ospreys often will simply wander into groups of enemies and die rather quickly, and because the shield link works across planes.

| 2 50 | Stasis Freezes Rift-bound enemies. While active, enemy projectiles are arrested in mid-air, resuming its trajectory when stasis ends. Introduced in Update 20.0 (2017-03-24) | Strength:N/A 

Duration:8 / 10 / 12 / 15 s 

Range:N/A 

Misc: ∞ (affected targets) ∞ (effect range) 300 (gunfire objects limit) 

Info

- Limbo expends 50 energy to freeze all enemies in the Rift for 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 seconds. Additionally, all enemy projectiles are frozen for the duration with a capacity of 300 projectiles.
- Exceeding the projectile cap will end the ability prematurely.
- Stasis has no range limit.
- Cast and deactivation animations of 1 second, affected by Casting Speed.
- Does not freeze certain enemies, including but not limited to: Acolytes and units with Overguard.
- Overguard or any other cc immunity does not stop their bullets from being frozen.
- Stasis will freeze enemies once the overguard is removed.

- When stasis ends, all enemies in the Rift will resume their actions prior to Stasis and all frozen projectiles will start moving depending on priority:
- All hit-scan bullets are released simultaneously.
- Projectiles are released one by one, by order of creation.
- Manual deactivation is a one handed action.

- Prevents the use of Melee Afflictions and Melee Vortex on enemies affected by stasis.
- Enemies affected by stasis will cause additional enemies to spawn in certain mission types. The enemies spawned are equal to the number of enemies currently affected and up to double the original amount possible in a mission..
- Missions with this interaction are: Survival, Mobile Defense, Void Cascade and Extermination.
- Defense, Disruption and Excavation are unaffected

- When used repeatedly against Sentient enemies, Stasis' freeze effect will gradually diminish to a fraction of its total duration for the affected enemies. Sentients exposed to this ability repeatedly will be able to move and attack after being frozen for a short duration, eventually becoming immune to Stasis altogether.
- Despite being able to move and attack, the ranged attacks of Sentients are still frozen for as long as Stasis remains active.
- If the Sentients exit and re-enter the Rift plane. Stasis' freeze effect will renew with shortened duration.

- For more information on Rift-related attributes, see Rift Plane.

Kit Interactions

- Stasis continuously freezes all enemies in the rift, including those Riftbound by Banish, Rift Surge, or Cataclysm.
- If there are multiple Limbo players in a squad, Stasis will not deactivate until either the weapon fire object limit is reached or the latest Stasis instance ends.

Tips & Tricks

- Activating Stasis before banishing enemies is an effective way of protecting yourself whilst in the Rift.
- Even though enemies with Overguard are able to move, the survivability Stasis brings is largely unaffected. Bullets shot by overguarded units will still be frozen.
- While in the Rift with Stasis active, outside of Eximus abilities and edge cases, melee Overguarded units are the only enemy type that are able to hit Limbo reliably.

- Having Stasis active while being in Cataclysm, will prevent you from taking damage from enemies that enter Cataclysm's radius.
- Synthesis targets and Feral Kavats can be put in Stasis, making it easier to scan them.
- If an enemy is affected by Petrify before being frozen with stasis, the Petrify status effect will last for the entire duration of stasis.
- Vampiric Liminus can be put into the rift and be frozen by stasis. Effectively rendering them harmless.
- Stasis pauses enemy AI, because of this unalerted enemies frozen by stasis will never raise to a higher alert level. Likewise, the stealth damage bonus will never be removed and stay active for the entire duration of Stasis. However the player must use blind, Silence or be cloaked(through Evade, Shade or Huras Kubrow) before the enemy is frozen by stasis for this to take affect.
- Stasis prevents the use of many grouping abilities and weapons. This is a non-exhaustive list of the ones that DO work:
- Weapons:
- Gammacor Incarnon
- Tenet Plinx

- Subsumes:
- Coil Horizon
- Airburst

- Other:
- Magus Anomaly
- Cordon

| 3 50 | Rift Surge Surges nearby Rift-bound enemies with Rift energy. When killed the Rift Surge is transferred to a nearby enemy outside the rift. Surged enemies that leave the Rift perform a radial Banish. Introduced in Update 15.0 (2014-10-24) | Strength:N/A 

Duration:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 s (surge duration) 6 / 10 / 14 / 18 s (banish duration) 

Range:10 / 15 / 20 / 25 m (surge & transfer range) 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 m (banish radius) 

Misc: 300 (Rift transitional Impact damage) 

Info

- Limbo expends 50 energy to apply the Rift Surge status to all enemies in the Rift plane within a radius of 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 meters around him for 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 seconds.
- The Rift Surge status can activate two different effects: "Radial banish" and "Transfer"
- Radial banish: Enemies with Rift Surge that leave the Rift will activate Radial Banish and banish all enemies within 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 meters, including themselves, back into the Rift for 6 / 10 / 14 / 18 seconds.
- After activation the Rift Surge status is removed.

- Transfer: Killing an enemy with Rift Surge in the Rift Plane will transfer its Rift Surge status to a random nearby enemy unit within 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 meters in the material plane.
- This will not move them to the Rift plane. The radial banish only activates if they enter and then leave the Rift.
- The duration does not reset on transfer.
- Killing an enemy with Rift Surge in the material plane will do nothing and the status simply dies with them.
- Enemies with Rift Surge in the material plane count towards Rift Torrent

- Limbo can cast this from any plane and it will only target enemies in the Rift.
- Cannot be recast unless an unaffected enemy enters the Rift and comes within cast range.
- Cast animation of 1 second, affected by Casting Speed.
- Unaffected by Overguard

- For more information on Rift-related attributes, see Rift Plane.

Kit Interactions

- Banish and Cataclysm can put enemies in the Rift, as well as trigger the radial banish by sending enemies back into the material plane.
- Dispel for Banish functions on enemy targets affected by Rift Surge's radial banish. Can be cast in succession to keep pulling more enemies into the Rift(1, 3, Hold 1, 3, Hold 1, etc..)
- Dispelling or the shrinking of cataclysm can make enemies leave the Rift

- The Rift Surge status on enemies in the material plane is most often activated in this way:
- Enemy with Rift Surge in the material plane walks into Cataclysm with Stasis active.
- The enemy is frozen on the border of Cataclysm.
- Cataclysm shrinks and the enemy is released from the Rift.
- Radial banish activates sending them and everyone around them back into the Rift.

Tips & Tricks

- Casting speed is highly recommended to make full use of this ability.
- Rift Surge is designed to keep enemies inside the Rift by extending the banish duration, as well as bringing new enemies into the Rift without having to leave the rift.
- The following techniques and occurrences can trigger Rift Surge's banish effect on charged enemies:
- Recast or dispel Banish on banished enemies, causing them to momentarily leave the Rift plane.
- The duration of Banish or Rift Surge's radial banish ends on affected enemies.
- Detonating Cataclysm to bring enemies inside it to the material plane (unless affected by Banish or Rift Surge radial banish).

- Due to the nature of how Rift Torrent works the damage buff can become very big. Each enemy affected by Rift Surge gives an extra 30% at base. Banishing just 30 enemies will give a damage buff of 900%.
- The combination of Banish and Rift Surge (with high range or grouping) can substitute some of the utilities of cataclysm, freeing up an ability slot.
- Good abilities to subsume onto Limbo include Breach Surge, Null Star, Silence and Roar/ Eclipse
- The low radial banish range of Rift Surge can be mitigated using a grouping ability or weapon. Examples include Airburst, Scyotid, Void Snare or Magus Anomaly

| 4 100 | Cataclysm A violent blast of Void energy tears open a pocket of rift plane which can sustain itself for a short period before collapsing in another lethal blast. Introduced in Update 15.0 (2014-10-24) | Strength:200 / 300 / 400 / 500 ( Blast damage) 

Duration:15 / 20 / 25 / 30 s 

Range:10 / 12 / 14 / 16 m (initial radius) 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 m (final radius) 

Misc: ∞ (cast range) 2 e/s (Rift energy regen per second) 100 % (formation and collapse stagger chance) 300 (Rift transitional Impact damage) 10 % (collapse damage bonus) 25-100 % (damage bonus radius scaling) 

Info

- Limbo expends 100 energy to bring forth a dimensional nexus into existence that bridges the Material and Rift planes, forming a sphere of swirling void energy with an initial radius of 10 / 12 / 14 / 16 meters centered around the location on the aiming reticle over unrestricted range. The sphere continuously shrinks over a duration of 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 seconds to a final radius of 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 meters before collapsing on itself.
- Radius reduction per second uses the following formula:
Reduction = 2 × Base Radius × (1 + Range Mods) ÷ (3 × Base Duration × (1 + Duration Mods))
- Cast animation of 2 seconds, affected by Casting Speed.
- The sphere will be created wherever the reticle is aiming when the casting animation finishes, allowing time before then to fine-tune the sphere's placement.

- Cataclysm will collapse when deactivated manually by pressing the ability key again (default 4 ) or when its duration expires.
- Cataclysm exhibits various effects on allies, enemies, and objects within its influence.

- Allies can freely move between the material and Rift planes by entering and exiting the sphere.
- Warframes regenerate 2 energy per second. This energy recharge is constant and cannot be interrupted, except by some abilities or by exiting the Rift.
- Weapon attacks only apply when allies and their targets are both present in the Rift. Otherwise, weapon damage, Critical Hits, and Status Effects have no effect on both parties.
- Warframe abilities can affect targets across the different dimensional planes.
- Players can still revive teammates, but cannot interact with consoles or activate Life Support capsules.
- Players are visually coated in a layer of white energy while in the Rift plane.

- Enemies can freely move between the material and Rift planes by entering and exiting the sphere, however if the enemy has a Rift Surge active upon exiting, they are pulled back into the Rift Plane.
- Cataclysm's formation and collapse each deal 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Blast damage and Stagger all enemies caught within the area of effect; Additionally, collapsing deals bonus damage based on the average health and shields of all enemies, NPCs, and players caught in the explosion:
- Bonus Damage = 0.1 × ((Sum of Units' Current Health + Sum of Units' Current Shields) ÷ Number of Unts) when an un-modded Cataclysm expires after its full duration.
- Bonus damage further scales with Cataclysm's current range/duration, dealing 25% of its total when at its maximum and ranking up to 100% when at its minimum.
- Damage Bonus's through Archon Shards and warframe abilities can increase the explosion damage. Most notably Banshee's Sonar which can instantly kill certain enemies with enough power strength.
- Damage does not diminish with distance and bypasses obstacles in the environment.

- Weapon attacks only apply when enemies and their targets are both present in the Rift. Otherwise, weapon damage, Critical Hits, and Status Effects have no effect on both parties.
- Warframe abilities can affect targets across the different dimensional planes.
- Rift Walking causes Limbo to enter the Rift plane, allowing him to damage enemies within Cataclysm.
- Cataclysm affects most bosses and objectives that are otherwise not affected by Banish, causing them to enter the Rift plane while within its influence.
- Enemies are visually coated in rippling energy especially around their feet when within the Rift.

- Cataclysm will pull Cryopods and Excavators into the Rift plane. These objects will remain in the Rift as long as the bottom center part of their in-game model is within the Cataclysm sphere.
- Cataclysm's forming explosion can break Reinforced Glass, while the collapsing explosion can break the former as well as Storage Containers.
- Portable items like datamasses, power cores and void keys within the Cataclysm can be picked up and carried, and will not be dropped by transitioning between planes.
- Pickups within Cataclysm can be collected by players.

- Cataclysm's sphere void energy is affected by Limbo's chosen energy color.
- Nullifier Bubble will not immediately destroy Cataclysm. Instead, its duration expires 4 times faster while in contact.
- For more information on Rift-related attributes, see Rift Plane.

Kit Interactions

- Banish enemies inside Cataclysm to briefly blink them out and back into the Rift, dealing damage and knockdown.
- All enemies within Cataclysm are frozen in place by Stasis.
- Collapse the Cataclysm to force enemies affected by Rift Surge back into the material plane, discharging their surge to result in a radial banish.

Tips & Tricks

- Because Cataclysm prevents enemy fire outside the sphere from damaging objects inside it, this ability can be used as an alternative to Snow Globe in protecting Defense, Mobile Defense and Excavation objectives.
- Power Cores can be picked up and delivered to Excavators safely, all within the Cataclysm.

- Kuva Guardians will remain in the material plane, even inside a Cataclysm, while retaining their Keshegs.
- This can help in Kuva Siphon missions to ignore them completely, or disarm them through the Rift since Operator powers work across planes.

- While Cataclysm can be used to protect an objective or team members from harm directly, it is also possible to manage ranged threats by surrounding them with the skill and firing into the sphere (while Limbo is in the Rift on the exterior) to kill said enemies like a Riot Shield, instead of encasing the players. Or eliminating them individually as they are revealed by the shrinking sphere.
- Grouping tools can be fired within the Cataclysm and will drag rifted and non rifted foes through the Void Membrane. Allowing Limbo a means of getting enemies into the Rift without relying purely on Banish and/or the Banish Rift Surge radial explosion combo.
- Generally, this is disadvantageous when facing high concentrations of melee enemies, as it effectively provides them cover from the team until outside the sphere (unless utilizing the strategies mentioned above).
- Allies can be Banished so they can attack enemies within Cataclysm without them needing to go inside it.

- Using Cataclysm in this way is most effective when enemies are in open spaces or stationary. In particular, Corpus MOAs and the heavy units from several factions exhibit the tendency to stand in the open and fire while relatively stationary, leaving them vulnerable to this technique.
- Stasis can also be activated to keep enemies inside the Cataclysm.

- This method offers an alternative means of managing scattered groups of enemies without denying the use of datamasses in Mobile Defense, since players in the Rift cannot interact with consoles, even if they are carrying a datamass.

- When debating whether to emphasize Duration or Range mods in a Cataclysm-oriented build, consider that the Range will often dictate the "effective duration" of the ability, since it can often become smaller than is useful in the latter stages of its duration.
- Narrow Minded is a mod to consider carefully before equipping at high level, since it will not only decrease the maximum size of the sphere, but also the minimum size. This is especially important since Narrow Minded will also serve to prolong the skill's later stages in which it has shrunken considerably and may be less useful.
- This mod can also be highly advantageous when size is of less importance than duration, such as screening Defense, Mobile Defense, or Excavation missions objectives from harm.

- Since Range mods improve both the initial AND final range of the skill, it is possible to create a Cataclysm which never becomes too small to fight inside before it ends naturally, never requiring an early recast.

- The darkest black energy color from the Smoke color palette can greatly increase visibility within the sphere but on the downside makes it harder to see the Cataclysm when the players is not inside.
- Cataclysm can break loot containers over a large area, and building for range and efficiency is very effective for activities such as farming resources in the Plains of Eidolon.
- Melee companions can be used to break nullifier bubbles while within cataclysm
