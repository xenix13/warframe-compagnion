# Narin/Abilities (wiki)
Source : https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Narin/Abilities

## Passive

Enemies killed by any source, while afflicted with Cold Status Effects from Narin, have a 1% chance per Cold status effect (10% chance at 10 Cold status) to drop a Sangodae Cold Ammo rune. Ammo runes remain indefinitely until picked up; up to 5 ammo runes may be active at once, with the newest spawned removing the oldest.

- Sangodae ammo runes are client-side pickups, which can be collected separately by Narin herself and allied Warframes.
- Ammo runes are represented as floating runic globes containing a crane and bullets symbol.
- Narin's Companion-inflicted Cold status effects do not activate Sangodae drop chance.

Sangodae grants elemental Magazine ammo to the affected Warframe's primary and secondary Weapons, providing an Extra Hit of 50% bonus Cold damage with a guaranteed Cold status effect on every hit, lasting for 15 seconds. Reloading doesn't clear the effect, it only temporarily removes the icon during the reload. On Narin, she also gains 25% bonus Cold damage for her abilities.

- The Extra Hit deals 50% of the triggering hit's damage (all damage types, after mods and critical hits), calculated before the enemy's damage type modifiers and vulnerabilities. It is dealt as a separate Cold damage instance, so it is affected by Cold Damage Vulnerability (such as Hakchum), Armor and Viral status effects like any other Cold damage.
- The Extra Hit inherits the triggering hit's critical damage but is always displayed as a white number.
- Each Multishot projectile triggers its own Extra Hit.
- The Extra Hit lands after the triggering hit's own status effects, so a Viral status inflicted by that projectile already increases it.

- The ability bonus is a separate multiplier (×1.25) on Nurinarim's frost aura and icy slashes and on Hakchum's ice storm damage, multiplying with Hakchum's Cold Damage Vulnerability.
- Hakchum's ice storm snapshots the bonus when cast: a storm cast while Sangodae is active keeps it for its whole duration, even after Sangodae expires; a storm cast without Sangodae does not gain it.

- Sangodae lasts a fixed 15 seconds; Ability Duration does not extend it.
- While Sangodae is active, the Warframe is visually encircled by a flying crane made of energy.

Kit Interactions

- Neote stacks 10 Cold status effects rapidly to maximize the chance to spawn Sangodae ammo globes.
- Casts of Neote that hit an enemy grant 7% Ice for Nurinarim, plus an additional 0.5% Ice per hit.
- Example: Hitting 2 enemies with all 5 hits of Neote will grant 7% + 0.5% × 2 × 5 = 12% Ice

- The Cold status effects from Neote allow Naraemagi to activate and generate Overguard.

Helminth

- Subsuming Narin to the Helminth will offer Neote and its augments to be used by other Warframes .

Tips & Tricks

- Neote is excellent at enemy damage prevention and reduction, since max stacks of Cold status freeze them solid for 3 seconds, then after thawing they are slowed by 3 Cold statuses up to 60% slow, while max stacks of Puncture status reduce their attack damage by 80%.
- Enemies that survive Neote's blizzard are tagged with max stacks of both Cold and Puncture, which raise Narin and her teammates' weapon Critical Chance and final Critical Damage Multiplier. Take full advantage of both bonuses by firing guns at frozen weakened enemies within 3 seconds, as they will thaw leaving only 3 Cold statuses.

- Combine repeated casts of Neote and Naraemagi to rapidly build Ice for Nurinarim.
- Hakchum dive into crowds then use Neote to prime them with max stacks of Cold status, drastically increasing the chance of Songodae ammo drops, using Cold Damage Vulnerability to boost Neote's damage, and gradually refreeze surviving enemies solid using Hakchum's Cold status per second.

- Narin expends 75 Energy to leap 3 meters into the air, gaining Invulnerability and flight movement controls for a maximum of 3 seconds; during flight, Narin's reticle indicates her landing location with an area-of-effect visual indicator. Recasting the ability, pressing the fire button or melee button, or waiting for the flight mode to time out will cause Narin to dive towards the targeted location, dealing 400 / 600 / 800 / 1,000 Cold damage and inflicting 5 Cold status effects to enemies in a 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 meter radius and Line of Sight, while creating a lingering ice storm in the area.

- Vulnerability is instantly removed if the enemy leaves the ice storm or if the storm expires.

- Only uncombined Cold is affected; combined elements containing Cold (i.e. Blast, Magnetic, and Viral) are not boosted.
- Sangodae's 25% Cold ability damage bonus applies to the storm only if Sangodae is active when Hakchum is cast; the bonus will be kept for the storms entire duration, regardless of Sangodae expiring.

- Hakchum's initial 6 Cold statuses and 1 Cold status per second raise Sangodae ammo drop chance on multiple enemies.

- Hakchum's ice storm and Nurinarim's frost aura rapidly stack Cold statuses to 10, enabling icy slash explosions against frozen enemies.
- Nurinarim's frost aura continues to apply 2 Cold status per second during Hakchum's flight.
- Hakchum's vulnerability boosts the Cold Extra Hit from Sangodae on every weapon hit, regardless of the weapon's own elements.

Tips & Tricks

- When Narin's Overguard and Shields break, with no nearby Cold status to absorb, cast Hakchum as an emergency escape backup plan to become invulnerable and reposition.
- During Nurinarim, only Hakchum can be cast, so Narin will often find herself exposed with no way to rebuild Overguard and Shields.

- During the sword dance, Narin projects a frost aura within 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 meter radius around her, inflicting 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Cold damage per second with 200% status chance, applying 2 Cold statuses per hit.
- The radial damage is affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
- As the ability has 200% status chance, adding Viral damage through Nourish will change the elemental weighting and allow it to sometimes inflict Viral status effects instead of Cold.

- The ice field does not require Line of Sight.

- Press the fire button (default LMB ) to launch an icy slash. Before dealing its damage, each slash hit removes 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% of the enemy's current Shields and max Armor per Cold status effect on that enemy; the armor reduction from multiple slashes stacks additively. Each slash then inflicts 12,500 / 15,000 / 17,500 / 20,000 Cold damage with a guaranteed Cold status effect to every enemy in its path. If an enemy is frozen (10 Cold Status Effects) when hit, it will be hit twice by the slash and then explode to inflict 6,250 / 7,500 / 8,750 / 10,000 Cold damage in a 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 meter radius with a guaranteed Cold status effect, hitting itself and every enemy in range, with no Damage Falloff.
- The frozen AoE explosion requires Line of Sight between Narin and enemies caught in the explosion radius. However, line of sight is not required between the initial frozen target and enemies caught in the AoE.
- Slashes and explosions have no Damage Falloff over their range.
- Explosions also remove Shields and Armor from the enemies they hit, using each enemy's own Cold status count.
- Enemies capped at 4 stacks of status (such as bosses or enemies with Overguard) cannot be fully frozen, so they can neither have their slash hit doubled nor cause an explosion.
- The Shields and Armor reduction works on Overguarded enemies, using their (at most 4) Cold status count.

- Slashes travel 230 meters at 50 m/s and have infinite Punch Through against terrain and enemies.
- Slashes can hit enemy weakpoints.

- Immediately on cast and every 0.667 seconds during the sword dance, 1 enemy within 50 meters and line of sight of Narin will be marked by a targeting ring for 3 seconds. Firing a slash at a marked enemy consumes no Ice and instantly consumes the mark, while causing the slash to deal 2x damage to every enemy it passes through (marked or not) as well as doubling the damage of any explosions caused on frozen enemies.
- A slash fired at an unmarked enemy deals 1x damage, even to marked enemies in its path.
- When the crosshair is aiming at a targeted enemy, it will turn blue, and the enemy's targeting ring will spin.
- Line of sight is only required for the enemy to be targeted with a ring. A slash can be fired if the crosshair is aimed at a target that is no longer in line of sight.
- Marks are prioritized on enemies close to the targeting reticle.

- While Nurinarim is active, Narin can only move, jump, roll and cast Hakchum and Transference.
- Narin becomes translucent when rolling and jumping, though this is only a visual effect; she is still visible to enemies.
- Narin has limited influence over her aerial momentum during Nurinarim.
- Narin's ground speed during the sword dance is the same as her Sprint Speed.
- The roll is affected by Parkour Velocity and Dodge Speed via Amalgam Barrel Diffusion.

- Nullifier bubbles do not end the sword dance, but Narin cannot fire slashes while inside a bubble. Enemies inside a bubble cannot be marked and take no damage from slashes aimed at them; a slash aimed at a marked enemy outside or behind the bubble works normally.
- Recasting Nurinarim ends the sword dance, retaining the Ice that was not spent while it was active.

Kit Interactions

- Nurinarim requires full Ice to cast. Ice is generated by her other abilities:
- Casts of Neote that hit an enemy grant 7% Ice, plus an additional 0.5% Ice per hit.
- Naraemagi generates 5% Ice for on cast, plus an additional 1% Ice for each enemy hit by the ability.
- Each enemy hit by Hakchum's initial cast generates 1% Ice.

- Hakchum's Cold Damage Vulnerability and Sangodae's ×1.25 ability bonus multiply each other.
- Naraemagi absorbing Cold status effects from a frozen enemy unfreezes it immediately, so it will not explode until refrozen.
