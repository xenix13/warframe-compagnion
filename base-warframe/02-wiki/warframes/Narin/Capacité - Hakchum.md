Info

- Narin expends 75 Energy to leap 3 meters into the air, gaining Invulnerability and flight movement controls for a maximum of 3 seconds; during flight, Narin's reticle indicates her landing location with an area-of-effect visual indicator. Recasting the ability, pressing the fire button or melee button, or waiting for the flight mode to time out will cause Narin to dive towards the targeted location, dealing 400 / 600 / 800 / 1,000 Cold damage and inflicting 5 Cold status effects to enemies in a 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 meter radius and Line of Sight, while creating a lingering ice storm in the area.
- Ascend (default LShift ) or descend (default LCtrl ) during Narin's flight by holding down the corresponding buttons.
- The dive has a limited horizontal range of about 30 meters regardless of how high up Narin is when casting the ability.
- Narin's Companion follows her as she flies, but is not invulnerable.

- The ice storm lasts for 15 seconds, dealing 100 Cold damage every second to enemies inside and inflicting 1 Cold status per hit. Enemies currently inside the storm receive +120% / +130% / +140% / +150% Cold Damage Vulnerability. Only 1 ice storm can be active at once, with the previous storm immediately expiring if the ability is cast again.
- Ice storm does not require line of sight to damage enemies, inflict status, or apply damage vulnerability.
- The vulnerability multiplies all Cold damage dealt to enemies from any source, including the storm's own damage, all of Narin's other damaging abilities, and weapons with Cold damage.
- Vulnerability is instantly removed if the enemy leaves the ice storm or if the storm expires.

- Only uncombined Cold is affected; combined elements containing Cold (i.e. Blast, Magnetic, and Viral) are not boosted.
- Sangodae's 25% Cold ability damage bonus applies to the storm only if Sangodae is active when Hakchum is cast; the bonus will be kept for the storms entire duration, regardless of Sangodae expiring.

Kit Interactions

- Hakchum's initial 6 Cold statuses and 1 Cold status per second raise Sangodae ammo drop chance on multiple enemies.
- Creates a constant supply of Cold statuses for Naraemagi to absorb.

- Each enemy hit by Hakchum's initial cast generates 1% Ice for Nurinarim.
- Hakchum's ice storm and Nurinarim's frost aura rapidly stack Cold statuses to 10, enabling icy slash explosions against frozen enemies.
- Nurinarim's frost aura continues to apply 2 Cold status per second during Hakchum's flight.
- Hakchum's vulnerability boosts the Cold Extra Hit from Sangodae on every weapon hit, regardless of the weapon's own elements.

Tips & Tricks

- When Narin's Overguard and Shields break, with no nearby Cold status to absorb, cast Hakchum as an emergency escape backup plan to become invulnerable and reposition.
- During Nurinarim, only Hakchum can be cast, so Narin will often find herself exposed with no way to rebuild Overguard and Shields.

Bugs

- Hakchum's initial cast will generate 1% Ice per enemy body part when hitting corpses.
- Can become bugged and not apply damage vulnerability nor damage over time for duration of a mission.
- Weapons can occasionally fail to reload when empty after casting Hakchum, requiring the player to swap weapons before a reload can occur.
