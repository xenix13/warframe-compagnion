CODEX
Narin descends in swirling eddies, her blade keen and deadly as an icy wind. She wields ice to heal and defend as deftly as her swift rapier strikes.

Release Date: September 23rd, 2026

North winds pierce the frigid winter calm, as the crane's descent churns a mystical iceborne breeze. Sheathed in hibernation atop Yuvan Peak, the frozen sword awaits her mistress' fated embrace. Dancing elegantly amidst flurrying snow, Narin foretells the chilling legacy of a blizzard's fury. Majestic wings aflutter and icicle-sharp beak poised, she freezes the fleeting moment when mortal shell shatters to shed the soul. Narin seized the Iceblade in Update 44.0 (2026-09-23): Iceblade of Narin.

Narin is an offensive Warframe that attacks with swift blades and biting Cold. Her passive causes enemies affected by Cold statuses to drop Cold ammo pickups when slain. Neote thrusts to rapidly stack Cold and Puncture statuses, while Naraemagi siphons Cold from enemies to restore Shields and Overguard. Hakchum has her take flight and divebomb, creating a frost dome that applies Cold Damage Vulnerability. Nurinarim engages a sword dance, creating an aura of Cold while hurling powerful Armor and Shield-shattering energy waves.

## Notes

- Currently, Narin's Signature Weapons - Nunchasa and Aksondol - lack any specific bonuses with her.
- However, both weapons offer sources of Cold damage and status procs, which synergize with Narin's ability kit.

- Cephalon Melica has unique dialogue when spoken to as Narin.
