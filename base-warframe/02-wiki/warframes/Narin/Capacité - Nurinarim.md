Info

- Once Narin has completely filled the Ice meter, she can expend 25 Energy to enter a sword dance. During the sword dance, Narin gracefully skates across the battlefield, projecting an aura of frost around her and gaining the ability to launch icy slashes to cleave through enemies. Her Ice meter drains steadily over 20 seconds and whenever she fires a slash, with the ability ending once her Ice has fully depleted.
- Slashes fired at marked targets consume no Ice.
- Each slash fired at an unmarked target consumes 2% Ice, effectively reducing Nurinarim's duration by 0.4 seconds.
- Ability Efficiency does not reduce the Ice drain over time or per slash.
- As Nurinarim lasts 20 seconds, it drains 5% Ice per second. Increased Ability Duration will increase the maximum duration by decreasing the drain per second.

- Sword dance duration = 20s minus 0.4s per unmarked slash;
- A full Ice meter therefore holds at most 50 unmarked slashes, although this is lower in practice due to the Ice drain over time.

- Ice is displayed as a light-blue orb carried by a crane, placed above the ability icons. When fully charged, a low humming sound will play and the orb will begin to shimmer.
- Narin cannot gain Ice during the sword dance (including via Hakchum).

- During the sword dance, Narin projects a frost aura within 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 meter radius around her, inflicting 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 Cold damage per second with 200% status chance, applying 2 Cold statuses per hit.
- The radial damage is affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Arachne and Nourish.
- As the ability has 200% status chance, adding Viral damage through Nourish will change the elemental weighting and allow it to sometimes inflict Viral status effects instead of Cold.

- The ice field does not require Line of Sight.

- Press the fire button (default LMB ) to launch an icy slash. Before dealing its damage, each slash hit removes 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% of the enemy's current Shields and max Armor per Cold status effect on that enemy; the armor reduction from multiple slashes stacks additively. Each slash then inflicts 12,500 / 15,000 / 17,500 / 20,000 Cold damage with a guaranteed Cold status effect to every enemy in its path. If an enemy is frozen (10 Cold Status Effects) when hit, it will be hit twice by the slash and then explode to inflict 6,250 / 7,500 / 8,750 / 10,000 Cold damage in a 7 / 8 / 9 / 10 meter radius with a guaranteed Cold status effect, hitting itself and every enemy in range, with no Damage Falloff.
- The frozen AoE explosion requires Line of Sight between Narin and enemies caught in the explosion radius. However, line of sight is not required between the initial frozen target and enemies caught in the AoE.
- Slashes and explosions have no Damage Falloff over their range.
- Explosions also remove Shields and Armor from the enemies they hit, using each enemy's own Cold status count.
- Enemies capped at 4 stacks of status (such as bosses or enemies with Overguard) cannot be fully frozen, so they can neither have their slash hit doubled nor cause an explosion.
- The Shields and Armor reduction works on Overguarded enemies, using their (at most 4) Cold status count.

- Slashes travel 230 meters at 50 m/s and have infinite Punch Through against terrain and enemies.
- Slashes can hit enemy weakpoints.

- Immediately on cast and every 0.667 seconds during the sword dance, 1 enemy within 50 meters and line of sight of Narin will be marked by a targeting ring for 3 seconds. Firing a slash at a marked enemy consumes no Ice and instantly consumes the mark, while causing the slash to deal 2x damage to every enemy it passes through (marked or not) as well as doubling the damage of any explosions caused on frozen enemies.
- A slash fired at an unmarked enemy deals 1x damage, even to marked enemies in its path.
- When the crosshair is aiming at a targeted enemy, it will turn blue, and the enemy's targeting ring will spin.
- Line of sight is only required for the enemy to be targeted with a ring. A slash can be fired if the crosshair is aimed at a target that is no longer in line of sight.
- Marks are prioritized on enemies close to the targeting reticle.

- While Nurinarim is active, Narin can only move, jump, roll and cast Hakchum and Transference.
- Narin becomes translucent when rolling and jumping, though this is only a visual effect; she is still visible to enemies.
- Narin has limited influence over her aerial momentum during Nurinarim.
- Narin's ground speed during the sword dance is the same as her Sprint Speed.
- The roll is affected by Parkour Velocity and Dodge Speed via Amalgam Barrel Diffusion.

- Nullifier bubbles do not end the sword dance, but Narin cannot fire slashes while inside a bubble. Enemies inside a bubble cannot be marked and take no damage from slashes aimed at them; a slash aimed at a marked enemy outside or behind the bubble works normally.
- Recasting Nurinarim ends the sword dance, retaining the Ice that was not spent while it was active.

Kit Interactions

- Nurinarim requires full Ice to cast. Ice is generated by her other abilities:
- Casts of Neote that hit an enemy grant 7% Ice, plus an additional 0.5% Ice per hit.
- Naraemagi generates 5% Ice for on cast, plus an additional 1% Ice for each enemy hit by the ability.
- Each enemy hit by Hakchum's initial cast generates 1% Ice.

- Hakchum's Cold Damage Vulnerability and Sangodae's ×1.25 ability bonus multiply each other.
- Naraemagi absorbing Cold status effects from a frozen enemy unfreezes it immediately, so it will not explode until refrozen.

Bugs

- On Warframe Mobile, pressing the crouch button just once will cause Narin to stay slowed for the duration of Nurinarim.
- Ability fails to properly deactivate when under effect of Enemy Silence from Acolyte Violence, removing players ability to attack for the duration, forcing player to flee in order to try deactivate Nurinarim to regain ability to attack Violence.
