Subsumable to Helminth 

Info

- Narin expends 25 Energy to lunge 4 meters forward and thrust her Iceblade, Neote, toward the reticle, unleashing a blizzard of spectral blades to rapidly strike all enemies inside a ? degrees cone and Line of Sight, spanning a distance of 9 / 10 / 11 / 12 meters. Each targeted enemy will be struck 5 times in rapid succession, with each strike inflicting 250 / 300 / 350 / 400 Cold damage, 2 Cold status, and 1 Puncture status, for a total of 1,250 / 1,500 / 1,750 / 2,000 Cold damage plus 10 Cold and 5 Puncture status effects, along with staggering enemies hit.

Kit Interactions

- Neote stacks 10 Cold status effects rapidly to maximize the chance to spawn Sangodae ammo globes.
- Casts of Neote that hit an enemy grant 7% Ice for Nurinarim, plus an additional 0.5% Ice per hit.
- Example: Hitting 2 enemies with all 5 hits of Neote will grant 7% + 0.5% × 2 × 5 = 12% Ice

- The Cold status effects from Neote allow Naraemagi to activate and generate Overguard.

Helminth

- Subsuming Narin to the Helminth will offer Neote and its augments to be used by other Warframes .

Tips & Tricks

- Neote is excellent at enemy damage prevention and reduction, since max stacks of Cold status freeze them solid for 3 seconds, then after thawing they are slowed by 3 Cold statuses up to 60% slow, while max stacks of Puncture status reduce their attack damage by 80%.
- Enemies that survive Neote's blizzard are tagged with max stacks of both Cold and Puncture, which raise Narin and her teammates' weapon Critical Chance and final Critical Damage Multiplier. Take full advantage of both bonuses by firing guns at frozen weakened enemies within 3 seconds, as they will thaw leaving only 3 Cold statuses.

- Combine repeated casts of Neote and Naraemagi to rapidly build Ice for Nurinarim.
- Hakchum dive into crowds then use Neote to prime them with max stacks of Cold status, drastically increasing the chance of Songodae ammo drops, using Cold Damage Vulnerability to boost Neote's damage, and gradually refreeze surviving enemies solid using Hakchum's Cold status per second.
