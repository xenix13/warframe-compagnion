Excalibur Umbra
From the shadow of the long night emerges a new Excalibur.

Release Date: June 15th, 2018

Excalibur Umbra is the Umbra variant of Excalibur, sporting higher armor and energy, as well as three polarities while losing a . Unique to Umbra is his display of sentience, allowing him to fight on his own when not piloted by the Operator.

He was first released in the Chinese build of WARFRAME as Excalibur Umbra Prime and was later added to the Global build in Update 23.0 (2018-06-15) as a non-prime. He can be acquired through the The Sacrifice quest.

## Notes

- Excalibur Umbra, compared to Excalibur:
- Higher Armor (315 vs. 240)
- Higher Energy (175/225 vs. 100/150)
- Higher Starting Energy (100 vs. 50)
- Different Polarities ( vs. )
- Different Aura Polarity ( vs. None)

- Excalibur Umbra, compared to Excalibur Prime:
- Different Polarities ( vs. )

- Umbra, along with the Skiajati, will be obtained by the player already fully ranked. All the Mastery Rank points that would normally be granted from ranking up Warframe and weapon will be automatically received upon acquiring them.
- Due to their unique nature both are also unable to be sold from the inventory.

- Excalibur Umbra comes with three mod slots polarized with the unique Umbra polarity. He also comes with 3 Umbral Mods: Umbral Intensify, Umbral Fiber, and Umbral Vitality fusioned to rank 5 out of 10. His Skiajati Nikana and Exalted Umbra Blade each have two polarity slots and come with Sacrificial Pressure and Sacrificial Steel equipped.
- In place of Radial Blind, Umbra has Radial Howl, which not only blinds enemies, but also removes any active damage resistances from Sentient units and Shadow Stalker within range.
- Unlike other Warframes, Excalibur Umbra's sentience allows him to remain active after using Transference, supporting the Operator with abilities and equipped weapons. However, Umbra can be damaged, and will stop moving once all of his health is depleted.
- This can be disabled through use of Warrior's Rest.
- Excalibur Umbra is incapable of using Archguns when in autonomous mode, as transferring out with the Operator will make him switch back to using his primary weapon.
- Excalibur Umbra Sentience specter benefits from starting energy sources such as Preparation and Amber Archon Shards.
- Abilities casted by the specter consume the specter’s energy, which does affect whether Exalted Blade stays active or not on the Warframe version of Umbra.
- Excalibur Umbra’s Sentience specter is considered a ‘Warframe’ for some effects like Magus Repair to enable the Operator to heal him.
- When Excalibur Umbra's Sentience is "Killed" and enters its kneeling state, all his HP is automatically refilled when Transfering back in if using Arcane Persistence

- Umbra is able to enter the Warframe-disabling bubble of an Exolizer (found in Void Cascade) if he uses Slash Dash, teleports in or follows the operator when no enemies are nearby. He will kneel and enter his rest state but he won't be pushed out of the bubble. Once the Exolizer is purged, Umbra Sentience will restart and operate as normal. His Exalted Blade will not be decast in this state.

## Known Bugs

Due to Umbra's sentient passive, the following bugs can be observed:

- Most subsumed duration, charge, and channeling abilities are removed when transferring out of Umbra when sentience is enabled. The only way to avoid this is by the use of Warrior's Rest.
- This does not include Exalted Blade, but if the Umbra Sentience specter runs out of energy when Exalted Blade is active, it will be lost as expected.
- A strange exception to this is when using Remedium where Umbra's sentience will immediately decast Exalted Blade like he used to.
- This effect can be mitigated by using starting energy like from Amber Archon Shards or Preparation. A lot of energy sources do not apply to the specter, but effects like Rage do work to prevent Exalted Blade’s removal if the specter is taking sufficient damage.
- If going into Operator form while Exalted Blade is cast and Umbra Sentience runs out of energy, Exalted Blade will be cast by the sentience if enough energy is recovered throughout Rage or Hunter Adrenaline (Currently Broken as Jade's Shadow Constellations Update - Since it wasn't announce on the patch notes it is likely it is an unintended change)

- There’s a small chance that when transferring back into Umbra, both the 1st and 2nd ability will be permanently disabled for the rest of the mission. This may be induced by transferring back into Umbra too quickly.
- Occasionally, maximum health will be reduced down to the base 370 when transferring back into Umbra. The workaround for this is to transfer out and back in.
- Once this bug is triggered in a mission, it will reoccur for the rest of the mission at a moderate chance.

- If the Umbra specter is downed as you go to transfer in, there’s a small chance that the player will be stuck kneeling like what Umbra’s specter does after lethal damage. The current workaround is to get downed by enemies.
- If Mania applies the Lifted status effect on Umbra while Exalted Blade is active and the player switches to Operator and back into Umbra, he will be unable to use both Exalted Blade and the equipped melee weapon. Only the first few frames of the animation will play out, before the attack is canceled.
- If the Combo Counter is built with Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush or the Gladiator Set bonus and Power Spike is used to maintain it, once the player switches to Exalted Blade and hits at least one enemy then let its combo duration reset, either on its own over time or by using a Heavy Attack, he will lose the Gladiator set bonus applying to Exalted Blade in addition to Weeping Wounds, Blood Rush and the Gladiator set bonus applying to the equipped melee weapon, even if combo remains on it. This seems to be the case for all exalted weapons.
- In order for those mods to begin working properly again, the player must either use a Heavy Attack on the equipped melee weapon to lose all combo or wait for Power Spike to drop the combo to its previous multiplier. Another solution is to use negative combo duration mods so that combo is never built up on the Exalted weapon.

- If the Operator goes through a Conduit in Sanctuary Onslaught but Umbra does not, switching back to Umbra after the Conduit has closed will make Umbra invisible and unable to use his abilities or Transference, weapons are unaffected.
- Furious Javelin bonus isn't carried over if Umbra Sentience uses Radial Javelin while the augment is equipped. Radiant Finish and Purging Slash and their effects still work
