The Descendia is a Mission type where the Tenno descend a tower of Hell to conquer its numerous foes and trials, where Roathe's memories are sealed within.

Access to this mission requires completion of The Old Peace quest. It is accessed through the Dark Refractory sub-section on the Navigation console.

The Descendia's challenges, blessings, progress, and rewards reset weekly at Monday 00:00 UTC. Players can still partake in The Descendia once they have exhausted all rewards.

## Mission Preview

WARNING: You will encounter story spoilers, and face extremely difficult gameplay intended for experienced players with fully modded, max rank weapons and Warframes.

Vendors will only be unlocked by completing The Old Peace quest and its prerequisites.

Players can bypass The Old Peace quest requirement to receive early access to this mission. Preview instead requires completion of The Teacher quest.

Players who use this option cannot publicly matchmake until Mastery Rank 10, certain spoiler content is hidden from view, and they do not have access to rewards and content granted directly from the quest, such as vendors.

## Mechanics

The Tower of Hell has 21 floors, called Infernums. Each Infernum's challenge must be completed before proceeding to the next.

The 7th, 14th, and 21st Infernums are checkpoints that allow players to exit The Descendia without losing any rewards, and can return later from any of those points.

### Restrictions

NO SELF REVIVE Can only be revived by other Tenno.—In-Game Description

- Self-revive is not allowed. A player that dies is out of commission until the next floor is reached. A dead player may also lose their eligibility for rewards once the 1-minute Inactivity Penalty triggers.

### Challenges

As the challenges are randomly chosen each weekly reset, it is possible for The Descendia to contain at least 2 of the same type of missions.

- Alchemy: Fill a Crucible using two elemental Amphors dropped by enemies. The Conversion Process is skipped.
- Battle Kaithes: Destroy a specific number of floating hologlobes aboard a Kaithe.
  - Dismounting, most abilities, and primary/melee weapons are disabled.
  - If no secondary weapon is equipped, players are automatically given a Lato.
- Defense: Defend Marie, Lyon, or a Duviri Golden Cradle from a single wave of enemies.
- Destroy Hologlobes: Destroy a specific number of floating globes.
  - The hologlobes are vulnerable to container breaking (commonly called "box breaking") abilities.
- Excavation: Protect two Excavators and keep them powered with Power Cells dropped by Power Carriers. Each Excavator awards 25 Ignia, and only two Power Cells are required to power the Excavators to Full before they can be used to restore shields.
- Extermination: Kill a specific number of enemies.
- Gather: Collect a specific number (30 on normal) of Vitoplast.
- Hive: Destroy 2 Infested Tumors to eliminate their Hives. All Tumor Nodes (The total scales with the number of players in the squad) must be destroyed first before both Tumors become vulnerable.
- Legacyte Capture: Protect Kalymos to spawn a Legacyte boss. Pheroglands do not spawn, and the Legacyte does not escape.
  - The challenge is not considered complete until the Legacyte is captured via interacting.
- Mobile Interception: Follow a moving Astrolabe to fill its Decode Progress meter.
- Pressure Cooker: Fill a Conversion Progress gauge. The gauge fills faster when the meter is within the white brackets, which can be balanced by destroying Distressed Pipes. The brackets will shift every 25% of the gauge filled.
- Purify: Cleanse 3 nodes of Infestation to fill a gauge, using Antiserum to power a Vaporizer.
- Shrine Defense: Keep enemies out of a singular point until it creates offerings. Deposit two offerings to spawn the Infested Oni and kill it.
  - This version of the Infested Oni only has a single health bar.
- Targeted Elimination: Kill a specific number of marked Necramites. Enemies will periodically spawn in the area. Once a group of Necramites are defeated, there will be a slight delay before the next group spawns.
- Time Tribulation: Race through a number of gates that appear in order within a time limit. Each gate adds 5 seconds to the timer.

#### Plunder

A reward room that gives players limited time to loot and reap Ignia and Maphica.

- Plunder: Players have 30 seconds to loot storage containers.
- Plunder Roulette: Players have 30 seconds to loot storage lockers. Beware that some containers may be Sentient Mimics.
  - Mimicked lockers have a distorted effect before they are approached and are marked on the minimap with the standard collectable icon if the player has Loot Radar equipped.
- Gruzzling Plunder: Kill a total of 6 Gruzzlings (may include Eximus variants). If not dealt with quickly enough, they will eventually escape with the rewards they are carrying.

#### Assassination

Defeat the boss to proceed.

- Archons: Defeat Archon Amar, Archon Nira, or Archon Boreal.
  - The Archon is not considered defeated until the Shard has been removed from it via interacting.
- Corrupted Vor: Defeat a certain number of enemies in addition to Corrupted Vor, who will spawn once half the enemies have been defeated.
- H-09 Efervon Tank: Defeat the tank, in either the Scaldra Tank form (Phase 1), or Techrot infested form (Phase 2).
  - Only has a single health bar in Phase 1 and will immediately disappear upon defeat with no extra interaction.
  - Thermian RPGs from this floor can be taken to following floors.
- Hyena Pack: Defeat a certain number of enemies in addition to the Hyena Pack, who will spawn once half the enemies have been defeated.
- Kuva Liches: Defeat a certain number of enemies in addition to the Kuva Lich, who will spawn once half the enemies have been defeated.
  - The Lich spawns alongside an Arbitration Drone.
- Oraxia: Defeat Oraxia.
  - This version of Oraxia only possesses two health bars.
- Phorid: Defeat a certain number of enemies in addition to Phorid, who will spawn once half the enemies have been defeated.
- Raptors: Defeat 3 Raptors and their Gravity Conveyors.
- Sisters of Parvos: Defeat a certain number of enemies in addition to the Sister of Parvos, who will spawn once half the enemies have been defeated.
  - The Sister spawns alongside its Hound, and an Arbitration Drone which periodically respawns.
- Technocyte Coda: Defeat a randomized Solo, Duet or an entire team of Technocyte Coda, along with a specific number of enemies.
- The Fragmented: Defeat one of the three variants of The Fragmented. Each Fragmented varies between Normal and Steel Path modes.
  - This version of The Fragmented can either come with The Fragmented Tide or immediately start at phase 2 with a single health bar.
- The Prodmen: Defeat a certain number of enemies in addition to John Prodman, who will spawn once half the enemies have been defeated.
- Untamed Guilt: Defeat Kullervo.
  - The Prosecutor Liminus at his third phase immediately die when spawned, instantly triggering his laser attack.
- Roathe's Oblivion: The 21st Infernum features a boss fight against Roathe.

### Penance

Each Infernum has a Penance that modifies enemy encounters. Some Infernum may (rarely) lack Penances entirely.

- Arbitration Drones: Enemies are protected by Arbitration Shield Drones.
- Bomb Defusal: Players glow with Heat, exploding after 15 seconds dealing massive damage and staggering them. Delay the explosion by killing enemies.
- Bubble Blasters: All enemies are ranged units and enemy projectiles are replaced with large, slow moving spheres.
- Chains of Fire: Enemies are connected by flaming beams, burning players on contact.
- Chemical Warfare: Poison gas is dispersed around the arena, dealing Toxin damage to Tenno and their allies. Enemies drop safety zones which can be used to avoid damage.
- Eximus Cabal: Greatly increased chance of enemies spawning as an Eximus unit. Each Eximus Cabal is one of 9 types, which determines which Eximus types are allowed to spawn:
  - Fire & Ice: Arctic, Arson, or Blitz Eximus.
  - Frozen Guardians: Arctic, Energy Leech, or Guardian Eximus.
  - Frozen Light: Arctic, Energy Leech, or Jade Light Eximus.
  - Jade Guardian: Guardian, Jade Light, or Shock Eximus.
  - Leech Blitz: Blitz, Guardian, or Leech Eximus.
  - Power Hungry: Arson, Blitz, or Jade Light Eximus.
  - Shocking Leech: Leech, Shock, or Venomous Eximus.
  - Toxic Flames: Arson, Shock, or Venomous Eximus.
  - Toxic Leech: Energy Leech, Leech, or Venomous Eximus.
- Falling Debris: Debris is constantly falling from the ceiling, dealing damage to any caught underneath it.
- Fire Trails: Enemies leave behind a trail of fire which burns Tenno and their allies. The fire trail can clear status effects from enemies akin to Nezha's Fire Walker ability.
- Fireball Rollers: All enemies are Rollers that leave behind a trail of fire and burn players who cross it. If the Infernum is a Targeted Elimination, Legacyte Capture or Assassination with Penances, the targets will also leave fiery trails.
- Flaming Juggernauts: All enemies are Juggernaut Demolishers complete with the extreme damage resistance and ability-purging pulse.
- Foes Vulnerable to Amphors: Enemies are heavily resistant to all damage not dealt by Amphors. Amphors will regularly spawn in the arena.
- Foes Vulnerable to Archguns: Enemies are heavily resistant to all damage not dealt by Archguns. Imperators will spawn in the area.
  - Summoned Archguns from Archgun Deployer will benefit from this penance.
- Foes Vulnerable to Rockets: Enemies are heavily resistant to all damage not dealt by Rockets. Thermian RPGs will regularly spawn in the arena, and can be brought to further Infernums.
- Only Weak Points Are Vulnerable: Enemies are heavily resistant to damage applied outside of weak spots.
  - May also roll alongside all enemies being Rogue Necramechs (Voidrig or Bonewidow).
- Frictionless: Friction is greatly reduced for Tenno.
- Gigantism: Enemies are larger and slower than normal.
- Glassmaker Cephalites: Void tears turn enemies to glass, rendering them immortal until glass shards on their body are destroyed.
- Harbingers: All enemies are Scaldra Harbingers.
- Head Stompers: Enemies are smaller than normal and take massively increased damage from being jumped on. Bosses from Assassination with penances are not affected by the stomping damage, but can still be bounced on.
- Hidden Threats: Enemies are invisible and do not take damage until they first attack or approached.
- Juggernauts: Enemies are replaced by a small number of Juggernauts, Deimos Juggernauts, or Demolisher Juggernauts.
- Laser Limbo: A laser security grid identical to the one used by The Jackal periodically spawns, rotating around its base. Players caught by the lasers take damage.
- Manic Mania: All enemies will be Manics.
- Minefield: Stasis Mines are scattered throughout the arena.
- Narmer Deacons: Narmer Deacons are present, slowing down players in their field of view. The Deacons lack health and cannot be killed, but are marked on the minimap with a unique icon. The lights are turned off during this penance.
- Necramechs: All enemies are Rogue Necramechs (Voidrig or Bonewidow).
- Sneaky Retreats: Upon reaching half health, enemies become invulnerable for a short duration and teleport away.
- Sol Banished: The lights have been turned off in this Infernum, causing darkness.
- Sol's Fury: Enemies emit a damaging light aura, which damages Tenno and allies who enter it.
- Tau's Revenge: All enemies (except Power Carriers if applicable) will be Sentients.
- Total Nullification: All enemies will be Nullifiers and Scrambus.
- Vampyric Liminus: Slow moving, immortal Liminus drain 150 Health (750 to Overguard) and 25 Energy per second from allies who get within 5 meters. The lights are turned off during this penance.
- Vilcor's Revenge: The player is forced to use their Necramech, and all enemies are Rogue Necramechs (Voidrig or Bonewidow).
- Weakpoint Horde: Enemies are heavily resistant to damage applied outside of weak spots, and will be more likely to swarm around you with melee attacks.

### Blessings

On the 7th and 14th Infernums are rest points, hosting Marie's Sanctuary and Lyon's Sanctuary respectively. They each offer one of two selectable buffs, with Lyon's having an additional debuff for each buff he provides. The buffs cannot be changed and will persist in subsequent replays until the weekly reset.

When replaying The Descendia, Blessings are only available beyond the Inferums they are received on. Normal and The Steel Path Blessings are independent of each other.

Marie:

- Vol Sacré: Aim Glides last longer and have reduced gravity.
- Angelic Balm: Regenerate 10 Energy and 50 Health per second.
- Shroud of Invisibility: While airborne, become invisible.
- Renewed Faith: Upon death, restore Health to full with 5 seconds of invulnerability. Can be used once per floor.
- Sol's Shield: While Shields are active, gain Armor that stacks over time. Upon taking health damage, lose 25% of the accumulated Armor.
  - Maximum armor gained is 1000
- Surging Vulnerability: Hitting Weak Points causes enemies to release Surge Sparks.
- Motes of Prayer: Gain a permanent set of Motes from Wisp's Reservoirs ability.
- Holy Roller: Rolling restores 200 Shields.
- Avian Grace: Gain +3 jumps. While airborne, incoming damage is reduced by 50%.
- Righteous Celerity: Gain +50% Parkour Velocity and Ability Casting Speed.
- Congregation's Protection: Enough kills and assists by Companions and Summons grant 5 seconds of invulnerability. Gain an additional 2 seconds per additional kill while active.
- Triumphant Bounty: Killed Eximus units grant your companions their aura for 30 seconds.
- Fervent Gunner: Gain +25% damage for a weapon upon picking up its ammo type; can stack up to 10 times.
- Hidden Benevolence: Upon entering a new Infernum, gain +1000 Overguard and invisibility that lasts until you attack or are damaged.

Lyon:

- Ascendant Penance: Abilities cost no Energy but consume 200% of their Energy cost in Health.
  - This blessing also renders you immune to most sources of healing.
- Restrained Fury: Sacrifice 100% Movement Speed while firing to double your Fire Rate and Magazine Capacity.
- Faithful's Wager: Melee Damage is reduced to x0.5 but you gain 25% chance to strike 4 times.
- Mutual Suffering: Apply Cold Status to nearby enemies. Standing still has a 10% chance of giving yourself Cold Status.
- Leaded Discipline: Increase Magazine Capacity by 400% but reduce damage by 90%. The last bullet in the chamber deals +100% damage multiplied by magazine capacity.
- Sacred Annihilation: Ranged hits have a 5% chance to instantly kill the target, expending all remaining ammo in the magazine.
  - Infernum fireballs will receive this buff as well and function properly.
  - Incarnon ammunition will not be consumed, but still benefits from this buff.
  - This blessing will effectively disable Mesa's Regulators for the rest of the mission. Peacemaker can still be activated and will drain energy as usual, but shots cannot be fired. It is currently unknown whether this is intended or a bug.
- Measured Force: Deal 3x more damage for 5seconds after a reload, but reloading now takes twice as long.
- Ferocious Condemnation: Heavy Attacks trigger Harrow Condemn ability, but consume 50 Shields for each enemy hit.
- Lua's Blessing: Deal +200% damage as the Drifter and gain 50% Damage Resistance. Warframes take +100% damage.
- Nullified Nullifiers: Gain usage of Abilities within Nullifier bubbles, but receive Heat Status within their range.
- Ascetic Munitions: Archguns gain unlimited ammo, but drain Energy instead of ammo upon reloading.
- Sharpshooter's Benediction: Sacrifice 50% damage to gain +25% damage for every consecutive hit to an enemy Weak Point with a ranged weapon. Damage bonus resets upon missing a Weak Point.
- Hollow Decimation: Eximus units no longer drop orbs, but take +100% damage.
- Sinner's Tithe: Deal -50% less damage to Overguard, but steal enemy Overguard upon damaging them.
- Flowing Slayer: Gain +75% Ammo Efficiency while Sliding or Crouching, but receive -25% Ammo Efficiency otherwise.
- Agony's Enlightenment: Gain +75% Heat Damage resistance, but all damage taken has a 10% chance of inflicting Heat Status.
- Rejuvenating Temperance: Gain +200% Energy Orb Effectiveness, but orb pickups now have a 10 second cooldown.
- Haunted Strength: Increase Status Damage over time by 200%, but gain 50% Status Vulnerability.
- Steadfast Adherence: Gain 99% Damage Reduction while Wall Latching, but lose 2% of your Health per second.

## Notes

- The Somachords for Decrypted, Roses from the Abyss, and The Cardinal Calls can be found within The Descendia.
  - The Cardinal Calls: Infernum 7, on a desk to the right of Marie (when facing her).
  - Decrypted: One of the potential arenas for the Assassination Infernum. Note that this room is not guaranteed to spawn in the rotation for a given week.
  - Roses from the Abyss: Entrance of Infernum 21, on one of the high stone pillars to either side of the room.
- Ayatan Sculptures can also spawn near the Somachord on the 7th Infernum, or on top of the Somachord on the 21st Infernum.
- Uriel's Prex can be found on the 21st Infernum, on the floor in front of the chained statue opposite of the door.
- To receive The Steel Path decoration and emote for clearing The Descendia, players are required to complete floors 1-21, and then an additional completion of floor 21 on its own.
- Specters, On Call Crew, and Air Support Charges cannot be used.
- Due to story reasons, control of the Drifter is forced and thus the Operator cannot appear in The Descendia.
- All known enemy Factions introduced as of Update 41.0 (2025-12-10) have a chance to spawn in each Infernum, and crossfire does not occur between factions. However, these enemies (and their associated bosses) will not drop their exclusive resources or mods (e.g.: a Battalyst does not drop any Intact Sentient Core), though they will still yield Credits, Endo, Riven Sliver, Health Orbs, Energy Orbs, and Ammo Pickups.
  - For example, Scaldra will fight alongside Techrot when spawned together.
  - Wild/Unaffiliated enemies do not spawn, but Arbitration Shield Drones, Duviri Dax and Hollow Thrax enemies do spawn.
  - Sentients currently only spawn either during the "Tau's Revenge" penance or alongside Narmer forces, possibly due to their adaptation which makes them a harder faction to beat as a group.
  - Enemies originally exclusive to other game modes such as The Unseeing Herald can also spawn, allowing the player to more easily scan for Codex entries.
  - Enemy spawns appears to be either randomized, based off groups found in certain missions (e.g. Duviri Dax + Corrupted from The Circuit/Undercroft), or from a single type of similar enemies (e.g. consisting only Runner and Galliflex-type enemies, or only Anarch Grineer)
  - With the exception of Prime Grineer, quest-exclusive enemies do not appear in the Descendia.
  - Assassins, Syndicate Death Squads and Acolytes also do not spawn.
  - All Eximus variants except Entropic Eximus can spawn in the Descendia, as Entropic Eximus are exclusive to the Zariman "for narrative reasons".
- If the boss present is an Archon, the player still needs to pull out their Archon Shard to complete the fight, however they will not obtain the shard.
- If the player stalls on the current cleared floor for too long without going to the next, the room will slowly burn starting from the top, procing Heat with each tick dealing more damage than the last until they die or move onto the next floor.
- All enemies in the current Infernum automatically die without giving out Mastery Points once the associated challenge is completed. All storage containers and lockers will despawn after their Plunder challenges instead, as do Hologlobes if applicable.

## Tips

- Survivability is essential. A fallen player can be revived in bleedout, but not once they have "fully" died.
  - Avoid straying too far from the squad to ensure they can reach a player in bleedout in time.
  - Use of the Vazarin Tauron Strike can easily revive all dead squadmates.
- Plunder's containers can easily be broken with Cataclysm, Dark Verse, Razor Gyre, Rotorswell, and The Vast Untime; among other Object breaking abilities and explosive weapons.
- Plunder Roulette's Mimic can be recognized by using Loot Detector mods, as (counter to expectations) they show up on Loot Radar where the real lockers never do, even on non-mimic floors.
- The checkpoint system can be utilized for farming The Descendia resources Maphica and Ignia. Warning: should the squad fail to reach the checkpoint or defeat Roathe in the end, the resources will be lost.

## Trivia

- The "Vilcor's Revenge" penance will always be named as such regardless players have reached Rank 2 - Acquaintance of Entrati or not.

## Bugs

- The electric arc effect from mines can become permanently stuck on a warframe until exiting the mission.
- This game mode does not contribute to Clan Weekly Initiatives.
- Completing The Descendia in The Steel Path for the first time will continually reward Dark Refractory trophies and 25 Steel Essence each time Roathe is fought. This persists until the Dark Refractory screen is closed, after which no more trophies and steel essence will be given.
- Picking up a Thermian RPG on the same floor as a Kaithe Race will instantly kill the player.
