Infernum is a Warframe Augment Mod for Uriel's Infernalis that causes volleys of fireballs to be released during Infernalis's flight, with the volleys growing larger for a short time after collecting a Demonium Rune.

## Stats

2 | 1350 | 5s | 8

## Acquisition

- This mod can be acquired by attaining the rank of General under Steel Meridian, or the rank of Partner under The Perrin Sequence, and spending 25,000 Standing 25,000 to purchase.

## Notes

- While flying with Infernalis, Uriel releases 4 fireballs per second which home onto targets in Line of Sight within 50 meters. Fireballs deal 1500 Heat direct hit damage, plus an additional 1500 Heat AoE damage in a 5 meter radius with a forced Heat proc.
  - The AoE damage has 100% damage falloff over its radius.
  - If there are no targets, fireballs will shoot in the direction of the aiming reticle with a ~45° spread to either side.
  - The fireballs will not target Nullifier Crewman-type enemies.
    - You can fly at the ground near a nullifier to aim the fireballs and damage the globe with explosions.
    - If another enemy is under a nullifier globe, the fireballs will target it, swirl around the orb unable to penetrate it, then eventually explode and damage the globe.
- Picking up a rune from Demonium will increase Uriel's output to 12 fireballs per second for 5 seconds.
  - The rune does not need to be collected during flight to trigger the effect, despite the mod description. It may be picked up on the ground and the bonus will apply during any time Uriel is in flight over the next 5 seconds.
  - Recasting Infernalis during the buff will prematurely reset the fireball rate back to normal.
  - Duration is calculated at the time of pickup, allowing the use of Arcane Concentration.
- Fireball direct hit and AoE damage are both affected by Universal Weapon Bonuses such as Arcane Avenger, Arcane Arachne, and Nourish.
  - Fireballs have 0% critical chance and 1.0x critical damage, requiring additive bonuses in order to gain any effect.
  - Both the direct hit and AoE damage have 0% status chance (although the AoE has a forced heat proc), so Nourish will only increase the direct hit and AoE damage. It will not allow for Viral procs nor will it increase the resulting heat status damage.
    - For example, at 100% ability strength a subsumed Nourish will increase the total damage of a single fireball from 6750 damage (1500 direct hit + 1500 AoE + [5s * 750 heat status damage per tick] = 6750) to 8100 damage (1.45 Nourish damage multiplier * [1500 direct hit + 1500 AoE] + [5s * 750 heat status damage per tick] = 8100).
  - Even though Uriel can swap weapons during flight, he will only equip the weapon upon landing or when interrupting the flight with an aim glide. Vigorous Swap consequently cannot be refreshed during flight.
  - Using flat critical chance buffs like Arcane Avenger, the fireballs may trigger Arcane Acceleration, Arcane Fury, Arcane Momentum, Arcane Tempo, and Arcane Velocity.
- Each fireball direct hit grants 1% Brimstone charge.
- The duration of the fireball buff when picking up runes is still affected by Uriel's modded Ability Duration even if Demonium has been replaced via Helminth, despite the rune's weapon buff being locked to 100% duration in this case.
- Uriel's Infernalis flight speed does not affect the cast rate of fireballs.
- Fireballs can hit weak points, allowing them to activate arcanes such as Arcane Consequence, Arcane Precision, and Arcane Rage.
  - The parkour velocity from Arcane Consequence will not be applied during flight however, requiring the flight to be stopped then restarted to gain the bonus.
  - "On headshot kill" and "on weakpoint kill" conditions cannot be triggered by the fireballs.

## Tips

- For quick completion of low level missions, combine Arcane Arachne and Proton Pulse to gain a large fireball damage boost and massive Infernalis flight speed boost, both from a simple wall latch + wall dash maneuver.
  - Conversely, if one desires more Brimstone charge it is worth considering removing sources of parkour velocity so that more fireballs will be launched towards groups Uriel passes by.
- Archon Vitality can be used to increase the number of heat procs to two per fireball.
- If one intends to spend an extensive time flying, it is advisable to avoid free-roam companions such as Kubrows, Kavats, Predasites, or Vulpaphylas as they will spend much of their time attempting to catch up, unlike a sentinel.
  - Furthermore, usage of aerial mods such as Agility Drift, Aerial Bond, Aviator, Aerodynamic and so forth may be advisable in these conditions.
    - Note that kills from fireballs will not count for Aerial Bond's recovery timer reduction but the cold aura works and accumulates properly.
- Sacred Annihilation from The Descendia applies to the fireballs, allowing them to potentially instant kill targets with each hit.
- The runes from other Uriel's Demonium will also trigger the increased fireball rate.
