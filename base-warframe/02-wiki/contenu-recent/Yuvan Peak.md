Yuvan Peak

Yuvan Peak is a player hub located on Earth, available upon completing Angels of the Zariman quest and Whispers in the Walls quest.

## Lore

### Yuvan Theater

Yuvan Peak is the same mountain summit visited during The War Within, where the Operator passes through an abandoned Yuvan Theater. According to Teshin, the "young and exotic" were once paraded through the mountain pass and displayed before the Orokin, who bartered over them while preparing for Continuity. These prospective hosts, known as Yuvans, were selected as replacement bodies for Orokin undergoing the Yuvan Ceremony.

### Tethyan Parchment

There are eight distinct Tethyan Parchment Tatters which can be found in Melica's Lab Room or in the Chrysalith's hidden credits room aboard the Zariman. When pieced together, the parchment forms a poem written in the Orokin Language concerning Yuvan Peak and the 'dancer of the bloodstained ice'. Each player can only find a single parchment tatter, selected at random; once it has been acquired, they cannot find more. The remaining seven tatters can be purchased from Cephalon Melica.

The poem reads as follows:

“Unto the mountain bleak and lean Where golden lords made sacrifice. She will walk on timeline's path The dancer of the bloodstained ice.

Can constructs die can burdens be A silent honor above her crown? But burdens four, will be the key The dancer of the bloodstained ice.

To dance for whom, a form unknown. A warding clasp, five azure lights. Bale and bind her life and limb To hold at bay the raging night.

She paid no heed to wounds nor pain No words to spare or send. Until her like might come again Waiting for they of void legend.”

## Cephalon Melica

Cephalon Melica offers the following services:

- Trades Entropic Kuva found on the Zariman Ten Zero for Narin, Nunchasa, and Aksondol blueprints, as well as a variety of cosmetic items.
- Initiate travel to the Zariman.
- Initiate The Icebind 6-player special mission. This requires Cryobells that grow next to her; three are generated every Weekly Reset and the player can hold up to six.

## The Iceblade

Students, I need a volunteer to free Narin's Iceblade. Who can help me?—Cephalon Melica

The Iceblade is trapped in ice on Yuvan Peak. It can be interacted with (default: X ) to attempt to "Pull" it, which can have different outcomes.

Attempting to "Pull" the Iceblade presents the player with a button-mash quick-time event (melee button, default E ). Unless specific conditions are met, the Iceblade will fail to be pulled and the message "Prophecy Unfulfilled" will appear at the top of the screen.

Successfully pulling the Iceblade requires the following conditions to be met when loading into the hub:

▾The Iceblade (Spoiler)▾

- Being at least Legendary Rank 1
- Narin must be equipped
  - Can be unranked
- Five Archon Shards must be equipped on Narin
  - They do not need to be Tauforged
  - Four Azure Shards as a minimum
- All four Dragon Keys must be equipped on the Gear wheel
- "On Wings of Ice" Honoria must be equipped
- You do not need to have picked up the Tethyan Parchment Tatters

Successfully pulling the Iceblade awards:

- Neote Rapier Skin
- "The Legend of the Void" Honoria

WARFRAME player "The_Madman_of_Chaos" was the first to pull the Iceblade. After it was pulled for the first time, the Iceblade's second functionality as a True Master's Font was permanently unlocked for all players.

If the player is Mastery Rank 30 or above, then after their pull attempt, they will be given the option to select a blessing like a True Master's Font, which grants the blessing buff to all players within the current hub instance. The same blessings as a typical True Master's Font may be selected, and additionally, a unique "Iceblade of Narin" blessing is available for selection as well.

### The Iceblade of Narin Blessing

- Provides the player with a version of Neote that floats around them, which allows melee weapons to break Entropic Eximus orbs on contact, either by hitting the orb itself or the Entropic Eximus unit.
- Adds 3 extra instances to each melee hit that deal no damage, but instead apply 1 Cold status effect each.
  - This effect applies to each Melee Influence hit when it spreads status effects.
  - Melee Duplicate will not work properly with this blessing applied. Instead of copying the damaging hit it will give an additional Cold stack and no extra damage.
  - Frozen enemies will prevent Melee Afflictions from working with Knockdown and Lifted, however Ragdoll will still work.
  - Enemies frozen from this will trigger Arcane Ice Storm.
  - With Xata's Whisper it will apply on the Xata's Whisper hit and give 3 additional Cold status effects.
  - Each Blast detonation will apply an additional 3 Cold status effects.
- Also applies to companion melee attacks.

## Notes

- Mastery Rank 30+ players may use the Iceblade like a True Master's Font even if the pull attempt failed, and even if they have never successfully pulled it before.
