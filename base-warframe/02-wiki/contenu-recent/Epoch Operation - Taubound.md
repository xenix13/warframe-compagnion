Epoch Operation: Taubound, sometimes also referred to as simply Operation: Taubound, is a limited-time event where the Tenno will open a path to the Tau System with help from the Sentient Mothership, Praghasa.

The event is scheduled to last from October 15, 2026 to November 17, 2026 on all platforms, and will not return following its conclusion.

## Gameplay Teaser

## Event Synopsis

With Arturos and Zioa having lain dormant, the time to assume control of the Tenno's Vessel draws close. The possibility of reaching new worlds beyond the Origin System is closer than ever, the Tenno must prepare for their journey to the Tau System. For this, Lotus enlists the aid of an unlikely ally; Praghasa. Long thought dormant since her arrival during The New War, the newly-awakened Praghasa requires resources to create a path to Tau, and Lotus decides to sacrifice Eris to slowly but surely feed Praghasa the necessary raw materials. The Tenno are thus tasked to gather Moonflesh from the planet's surface.

## How to Start

After completing the Vor's Prize quest, Mission nodes will be available on Eris. The Operation will appear on the World State window.

## What You Need To Know

Players must have completed the Vor's Prize Quest to have access to Eris. Star Chart and Junction requirements will be lifted during the event. Praghasa will have daily login and interactive narrative updates for the duration of the event.

## What You Need To Do

While Epoch Operation: Taubound is live, players can collect Moonflesh to provide fuel for Praghasa, in Praghasa (Node), Eris, by doing one of the following:

- Kill Infested enemies on Eris.
  - Killed Infested of Eris (Maggots not included) have a fixed ~2% chance to drop 2 Moonflesh per enemy (3 per enemy on Steel Path).
- Operation Alerts.
  - Each Alert offers 50 Moonflesh (75 on Steel Path) as a completion reward. Infested enemies in these Alerts will also have a chance to drop Moonflesh.
  - Up to three Alerts can be active at a time, with new Alerts appearing every half hour.
  - Alerts are exclusive to Eris, and will only select Exterminate, Mobile Defense, Survival, and Hive missions.
    - Hive missions do not contain Resource Caches.
    - Mission extraction is forced even if only part of the squad is waiting in the extraction zone. Random squad (automatic matchmaking) is automatically disbanded after extraction.
    - Eximus units have a higher chance of spawning compared to standard missions.

## Community Participation

This event calls for the collective participation of Tenno from across the entire community. By slaying Infested enemies on Eris, Tenno can unlock the Contegorus Greaves at 30%, Contegorus Pauldrons at 60%, and the Contegorus Chest Plate at 90% progress.

## Notes

- For the duration of the event, all Daily Tribute transmissions are replaced with Praghasa, with a different voiceline for each day of the event.
  - Only the visuals/audio are changed. As such, rewards are unaffected.
  - It is possible to fall 'out of sync' if the player does not log in every day, as transmissions for missed days are not skipped.
