  - Passive: Frozen enemies drop Cold Ammo Packs when killed, bestowing Cold Damage on Primary and Secondary weapons for a short time.

Press Attack on the marked targets to launch shattering icy slashes that deplete Armor and Shields. Hitting Frozen enemies causes them to explode, dealing massive radial Cold Damage.
- New Warframe: Citrine Prime
- Warframe Rework: Banshee
  - Passive:
    - Banshee’s abilities pierce the ears of nearby enemies. Casting abilities applies maximum 5 Puncture Status on foes within 10m.
    - Banshee’s weapon noises are hushed.
  - Sonic Boom:
    - Sonic Boom is now cast one-handed and no longer roots Banshee in place.
    - Removed Ragdoll from affected enemies — now pushes them back and stuns them.
      - The ragdoll did not synergize well with Sonar as it made it harder to target the weakpoints.
    - Increased Impact damage from 50 to 250.
    - Sonic Boom now reduces 70% of Armor, scaling with Ability Strength up to 100%.
      - This mechanic was originally tied to Sonic Fracture, but is now part of her base kit.
    - Sonic Boom now breaks resource containers and breakable decorations.
    - Sonic Boom now deals a guaranteed Impact Status Effect.
    - Renamed the Strength stat to say “Push Force” stat, as it affects how strongly Sonic Boom will push enemies.
    - Sonic Fracture Augment:
      - Renamed to “Sonic Siphon”.
      - Banshee gains Armor for 20s if Armor is stripped by Sonic Siphon, up to a cap of 1500 Armor.
      - Recasting Sonic Siphon will refresh the Bonus Armor duration if Enemy Armor is reduced during the cast.
  - Sonar:
    - Sonar’s Weak Spots are now considered Weak Points, giving them synergy with a variety of Mods, Arcanes, and more*.
    - Sonar now prioritizes unmarked parts of the body for its Weak Spots to help with Weak Spot legibility.
      - The reticle now turns red when shooting at the Weak Spots, just like other Weak Points.
    - Casting Sonar no longer roots Banshee in place.
    - Updated SFX when hitting a Weak Spot.
  - Silence:
    - Silence can now be recast to reset its duration and reapply the Stun.
    - Casting Silence no longer roots Banshee in place.
    - Improved VFX on enemies to make it clearer they are affected by Silence.
  - Sound Quake:
    - Sound Quake is no longer channelled. Casting the ability creates an area of effect with a duration of 25 seconds.
      - Resonating Quake fans may be confused by this change — we are swapping the mechanics of the Augment and the base Ability to make Sound Quake synergize better with the rest of Banshee’s kit.
    - Banshee is briefly invulnerable when casting Sound Quake.
    - Enemies receive guaranteed Blast Status Effects while in Sound Quake’s area of effect, which reduces based on distance from the center.
    - Increased casting animation speed to make it more fluid.
    - Sound Quake now breaks containers within its entire area of effect, not just the 5 metres around the epicenter.
    - Resonating Quake Augment:
      - Renamed to “Gaseous Quake”.
      - Gaseous Quake is now a channelled Ability, which takes on the mechanic of Banshee’s old Sound Quake.
        - Banshee is rooted in place to channel Gaseous Quake, which deals additional Gas Damage and Status Effects to enemies in the area of effect.
      - Gaseous Quake’s damage is multiplied every second during cast, up to a cap.
        - Energy drain also increases every second during casting.
      - Gaseous Quake's area of effect now persists for a short duration after channelling ends.
      - Improved Line of Sight now applies to Gaseous Quake.
      - Updated VFX and SFX to reflect this new gassy styling of the Ability.

Ammo Efficiency Changes

Our recent introduction of Ammo Efficiency traits to Riven mods via Riven Splicers has made this stat more accessible to players, and led to much discussion internally on how the availability of infinite ammo builds affects Warframe’s game balance.

Warframe is about the power fantasy, and infinite ammo is the epitome of that. Shooting endlessly into hordes of enemies can feel incredible, and we want players to be able to experience that — sometimes. The recent introduction of Riven Splicers has changed that “sometimes” for infinite ammo into a much more guaranteed thing, which has brought around this the following changes:

- Ammo Efficiency is now capped at 75%.
  - For those unfamiliar with the Ammo Efficiency mechanic, this means only 1 in 4 shots will consume ammo.
  - This applies to Ammo Efficiency increases from multiple sources — they will only increase the stat up to this cap.
  - The Ammo Efficiency cap applies to all equipment that uses Ammo, including Necramechs and Amps.
- Sources that intentionally gave 100% Ammo Efficiency (ex: Void Relic cracking buffs) have now been rephrased to indicate they give Infinite Ammo.
- There are other Mods, Arcanes, and Incarnon Evolutions that come with their own edge-cases, which we’ll explain in detail below.

Recursive “Explode on Death” Changes

Acid Shells has been a hot topic of late — for those unaware, it’s a Sobek Augment that causes enemies to explode on death and deals radial damage that scales with enemy health. Alone, it’s powerful, but it was made even more so via a niche interaction with Elemental Ammo, most commonly used with Cyte-09 (but also seen with Grendel, Oraxia, and Saryn, among others). These Elemental Ammo upgrades could cause Acid Shells to recursively trigger — meaning the one-time death explosion from a shot could instead be triggered infinitely if there were enough enemies to sustain it.

This build type has been on our radar for a while, but we didn’t feel it was the right time to address it. As developers, we’ve been pretty transparent about our threshold to “nerf” content; the Cyte-09 and Acid Shells build never felt dominant or disruptive enough to warrant a closer look, but over the past few weeks that has changed. Arbitrations, discussions about Ammo Efficiency, and the introduction of the Epoch Operation: Taubound have rapidly increased the popularity of this build, to the point where we feel we need to address it.

For the sake of consistency, we have reviewed all death detonation effects as part of the following changes, since this mechanic is not unique to Acid Shells:

- Fixed status effects caused by Elemental Ammo on “enemies explode on death” effects being able to retrigger the death explosion effects for the following Mods:
  - Note: these Mods will still benefit from increased damage and status effects gained from Elemental Ammo — they just won’t retrigger the death effects infinitely.
  - Acid Shells
  - Amalgam Furax Body Count
  - Vulcan Blitz
  - Explosive Demise
  - Combustion Beam
  - Thermagnetic Shells
  - Entropy Detonation
  - Reduced Acid Shells’ explosion radius from 15m to 10m.

Riven Mod Changes

First introduced alongside the launch of Rivens in 2016, Riven Dispositions were internal multipliers that guided how strong Rivens would be on individual weapons — ranging from 0.5 on popular weapons, up to 1.55 on those lesser-used.

The intent of these Dispositions was to incentivize players to use less powerful weapons, but ultimately we saw that investment was still focused on making powerful weapons more powerful. Incarnons were a more successful attempt at breathing life into older outdated weapons, and the overlapping of these systems only multiplied their power further.

With all that in mind, we are making the following change:

- Removed Riven Dispositions.
  - All Rivens now use the same multiplier for their possible value ranges, the equivalent of 1.2 Disposition in the old system.
  - For more information on the history behind this decision, please review our Dev Workshop.
- Restored the ability to trade Riven Mods.

NEW GAMEMODE: THE ICEBIND

The Icebind is manifested through the Cryobell ceremony. High upon Yuvan Peak, Cryobells have formed, drawn out by Narin’s influence. Each bell's tone leads to a time and place where a dark influence has taken hold. Six warriors must gather to ring the bell. Together they must overcome the Binds of a force not of this plane.

General Overview for The Icebind

Players can ring a Cryobell to take them to The Icebind, a new mission type with these key characteristics:

- Six player squads, where there can be no more than one of each Warframe.
  - (i.e. no six man Pink Rhino Squads this time, sorry!)
- Approximately 10-15 minute long missions.
- Level 999+ Enemies.
- Each Cryobell (mission key) comes with a randomized mission type, enemy, location, and modifiers — the squad votes on which one to play!
- Various mission restrictions, including No Self Revives, Gear Cooldown Increase, Empowered Enemies, and more.
- Guaranteed Riven Splicer upon completion, plus an additional reward from the reward pool.
- Bonus rewards for each Squad Side Objective completed (up to 3 per mission).
- Players can complete these missions three times a week.*
  - *players can stockpile up to six mission keys if they do not use them all each week.

To access The Icebind, players must have completed the following:

- Completed both the Angels of the Zariman and Whispers in the Walls quests
- Unlocked (but not necessarily completed) either Elite Temporal Archimedea or Elite Deep Archimedea

The Core Icebind Loop

The Icebind is different each time a player steps into it, beholden to the randomized traits of the Cryobell. Before we explain the various Binds (i.e. modifiers) introduced by the Cryobells, it’s important to understand the core gameplay loop. This section explains the overarching structure of playing The Icebind, and the section following will outline the various Binds that can affect it.

Starting The Icebind

This new mission can be started from Yuvan Peak, where players need to perform two important steps:

- First, claim their Cryobells from the ice pillar atop Yuvan Peak.
  - Three Cryobells form each week, following weekly reset at Midnight UTC on Sundays.
  - Players can stockpile up to six Cryobells if they did not use them all in one week.
- Second, with Cryobells in-hand, speak to Cephalon Melica and select “Ring a Cyrobell”

Doing so will open The Icebind party creation screen, where you can invite players to form your squad. The host can only form a six player squad using the “Ring the Cryobell” screen. With its launch, The Icebind does not have public matchmaking as these missions require more cooperation than traditional Warframe missions (which we’ll cover a little more in the Squad Side Objective section).

SELECTING A CRYOBELL

Players who are invited to a Squad will be summoned to Yuvan Peak, where they can then gather their Cryobells and proceed to Cephalon Melica. All players must speak to Cephalon Melica and select “Ring a Cryobell” to start the mission.

In this screen, players can edit their loadout and select which Cryobell they wish to ring. Your choice of Cryobell matters, because it determines your Personal Bind (which is a guaranteed personal modifier for the mission, regardless of which Cryobell is chosen by the squad) and which version of The Icebind players will vote on (including the mission type, enemy type, and modifiers).

VOTING ON A CRYOBELL

Once all players have selected a Cryobell, a 60 second voting countdown begins. Players can review all of the available mission options available to them, and vote on which one to play! If no votes are cast, a random Cryobell is chosen; in the case of a tie, the Host’s vote is the deciding choice.

Playing The Icebind

Entering the Icebind, you’ll be starting a mission as you normally would — as normal as it can be, considering randomized Binds of a Cryobell. Players will be presented with either a Survival, Mobile Defense, Excavation, or Disruption mission depending on the chosen Cryobell. At the beginning, you’ll be prompted to start your objective and aim to complete it successfully, with bonus Squad Side Objectives spawning in at regular intervals to keep things interesting. We’ll get to those in a bit!

While many elements of The Icebind are random, there are guaranteed elements you can expect each time you play.

STANDARD MISSION TRAITS

- Enemy Level: 999+
- Guaranteed Reward: Riven Splicer (plus Kuva and an additional reward from the Reward Pool after opening a Crested Frozen Chest)
- No Duplicate Warframes: Only one of each Warframe can enter the mission.
- Mission Restrictions:
  - Empowered Enemies: Enemy Health and Shields and Armor increased by 200% and an additional 100% for each squad member, stacking to a maximum of 800%. Increased chance of Eximus.

At least 3 surviving Tenno must sacrifice part of their health temporarily to bring the Tenno back into the action. HOW TO START AN OPTIONAL SQUAD SIDE OBJECTIVE

Depending on the objective, a certain number of Squad members are required to be nearby before it can be even started. This is important, because failing a Squad Side Objective will fail your mission and require you to start again. Choosing to do Side Squad Objectives is a matter of risk vs reward — do you trust your squad to succeed, and in return earn a Frozen Key to unlock more rewards? Or will you decide to not start the Squad Side Objectives and just earn the guaranteed Riven Splicer and completion reward upon mission success?

To ensure these are not started until players are ready, the Side Objective must be activated via a “Start” prompt, which then updates the objective description to indicate how many players need to be nearby. Once enough players are in the area, the objective will begin! Through teamwork, you all must work together to complete your task within the allotted time.

With The Icebind’s launch, there are 5 Squad Side Objectives to master:

- Lockbox: collect Cyphers from fallen enemies and bring them to the Lockbox to unlock it.
- Cryothermia: neutralize a Cryo Core by holding it while it deals escalating damage to player health.
- Indomitable Ice: charge ice crystals with concentrated fire.
- Entropy Orb: gather around the orb and avoid its damaging pulses.
- Signal Chain: connect a beam of energy between one point to another using your Warframes.

Successfully completing a Squad Side Objective rewards all squad members with a Frozen Key, which can be used to unlock rewards in a special treasure room upon mission completion.

Since players can try to tackle 3 of these objectives in one mission, time management is another factor to master! Rest assured, if you are rushing to complete your final Squad Side Objective before the mission ends, the mission will not extract players if there is an active Squad Side Objective*. Once that is complete, you’ll be whisked away to the end of mission screen (whether that’s a mission fail or mission success is up to your performance).

* Specifically, if the Squad Side Objective has had the "Start" input pressed — not just the waypoint appearing on the map.

- Dull Blades: -50% melee combo chance.

- Firewalker: For each meter travelled on foot, take increasing heat damage over time. Jumping or being airborne reduces it.
- Flankable: Max shield capacity is reduced by 75%, but an impenetrable Volt shield is permanently in front of you.
- Fractured Armor: Casting an ability reduces armor by 10% for 10s.
- Framecurse Syndrome: Activating an Ability inflicts 50 health and 150 shield damage upon you.

- Performance Anxiety: A spotlight chases you. Killing enemies while illuminated inflicts damage on you that increases with each kill.

- Secondary Wounds: Gain 1 Puncture Status Effect every time you take damage.

- Undersupplied: Max ammo on all weapons reduced by 75%
- Untreatable: Pickups do not heal, and Health Orbs cannot be picked up.

Mission Bind

A Mission Bind is a complication that alters the entire theme of the mission. These introduce gameplay elements to work around, and can affect what type of units spawn.

- Entropic Overload: Using abilities spawns Entropic Crystals on you and triggers Entropic Overload, causing enemies to self-revive as Leech Eximus and hostile Entropic Wisps appear. Getting hit by a Wisp causes stacked crystals to explode. Destroying Entropic Crystals and Wisps will decrease saturation.
- Stone Soldiers: Enemies are hard as stone and hit even harder. Taking them by surprise is the best way to exploit weak points in their stony flesh. In addition, you will face hostile Tomb Guardians.
- Miasmic Militia: Enemies slowly take damage, but are immune to Toxin, Gas, Viral, and Corrosive status effects. On death, a Gas cloud is released.
- Thin Boundary: Void Tears rip into reality, growing with nearby ability use. Tears inflict Void Shackles, reducing ability stats and weapon combo durations, while draining energy, health, and Incarnon weapon energy.
- Inferno: Heat Status present on players or enemies will feed the Inferno, drawing in Arson Eximus, Heat empowered enemies, and damaging anyone near active sources of Heat. Avoid inflicting Heat Status and killing hostile Heat sources to starve the Inferno.

Tactical Bind

A Tactical Bind is an additional complication that may affect your loadout or gameplay decisions.

- Antimatter Foes: Enemies killed by abilities spawn a hostile Antimatter Drop that tracks you.
- Archaic Armor: Enemy armor is greatly increased and cannot be stripped normally. Each hit they take reduces their maximum armor by 1%.
- Bruised Shields: Shields can only be restored via melee attacks.
- Bolstered Belligerents: All enemies have Overguard equal to 50% of their max health.
- Clumsy Munitions: When taking damage, a grenade may be dropped at your feet that explodes after a few seconds.
- Cumulonimbus: Being airborne triggers cumulative electrical damage, which dissipates over time once on the ground.
- Entanglement: Allies within 4m of slain enemies endure reduced Movement Speed and Parkour Velocity.
- Heavy Warfare: Enemies take -90% less damage from non-heavy weapons. Enemies will drop heavy ammo packs and heavy weapon recall time reduced to 5 sec.
- Jade Spirits: Jade Wisps haunt the region. If approached, they chase down the player responsible and transform into Jade Light beams.
- Kela's Revenge: Missiles strike from above periodically.
- Myopic Munitions: Enemies will only take damage if a player is within 15m of them.
- Parasitic Light: The squad is given two parasitic lanterns which drain energy of those that carry them. Enemies outside the lanterns’ light have massive damage reduction.
- Postmortal Surges: Slain enemies explode with Void energy.
- Toasty Toes: Grounded movement inflicts Heat damage over time. Airborne movement reduces it.

How you choose to counteract these Binds is up to you, as The Icebind does not come with specific loadout restrictions.

RIVEN EXPANSION: RIVEN SPLICING

The second half of our Riven Expansion arrives: Riven Splicing!

With the use of Riven Splicers, obtained exclusively from The Icebind gamemode, specific traits can be combined to create all-new spliced traits! When selecting a Riven Mod, a new Riven Splicing menu will appear. Opening this menu will display all owned Rivens, detailing which Rivens have valid trait combinations.

Select any lit up Riven Mod to see which traits can be spliced!

Image Description: A view of the new Riven Splicing menu. On the left side of the screen is a list of Rivens, with incompatible Rivens grayed out with a “No Compatible Traits” label on top of them. The right side of the screen shows the possible Splicing options for the Selected Riven, in this case a Hikou Riven. The screen offers the choice to merge +88.5% room and -222.6% Damage traits into a +285.1% Weakpoint Trait and a ??? Trait.

Spliced traits require a combination of two specific traits to splice together, with different combinations creating different outcomes! Combinations are pre-selected and confirmation is required before applying a spliced trait to your Riven. We have listed all combinations at the bottom of this section, and you can view the combinations possible on a specific Riven with the “Show All Possible Splices” option in the Riven Splicing menu. This means you’ll always know what spliced trait you’re about to receive before you make a decision.

If multiple spliced traits are available from a single Riven Mod, a “Change Selected Trait” option appears, allowing you to choose which spliced trait you want!

As an example: A Riven Mod with +Heat Damage, +Cold Damage, and +Toxin damage will have 3 spliced traits to choose from: +Blast Damage, +Gas Damage, and +Viral Damage. Which spliced trait goes on the Riven Mod is your choice!

Image Description: A pop-up for deciding which Trait to Splice. The options provided are “Weak Point Damage” or “Weak Point Critical Chance”

When traits are spliced, the two required traits are consumed, with the spliced trait AND a second random trait taking their place. The random trait is one you may receive from cycling normally, including one just used in making the spliced trait, with one change. If one of the consumed traits was a negative bonus, the random trait will also be a negative; if both consumed traits were positive, it will be a positive. This ensures your Riven keeps the same layout of positive and negative traits it has before combining!

After splicing, the total number of positive and negative traits will no longer change on the Riven Mod, no matter how many times it is cycled after the fact. This can be undone by removing the spliced trait from the Riven.

Image: The end-result of a successful Riven Splicing. A Hikou Riven Mod is in the center of the screen, featuring the new +274.1% Weak Point Damage trait, as well as the random -188.3% Damage trait.

Once a spliced trait is on your Riven, it’s there to stay! It is locked, but is not considered a locked trait — meaning it will not double your Kuva cost. Should you ever decide to remove the trait, the lock can be removed and the spliced trait can be cycled off, allowing a new trait to be spliced on! Utilize both a spliced trait and trait locking to lock two traits at once, making cycling for the last trait or two you want even easier.

As an example: Let’s say you had a Riven Mod with 3 Positive Traits and 0 Negative traits, and chose a + Viral spliced trait. No matter how many times the Riven is cycled, it retains +Viral Damage and 2 other positive traits, with 0 negative traits. You could even lock one of the other traits, guaranteeing two traits and cycling for the perfect final trait!

The Mod has two locked traits: one of them is the Spliced Trait (+274.1% Weak Point Damage), and another is a locked trait via the Trait Locking system (in this case, +164.3% Critical Chance). The cost to cycle this Riven is 7,000 Kuva, which is doubled as a result of the locked Critical Chance trait.

A second new feature will join Glacial Defiance: Riven Splicing. We’ll share a little information about Riven Splicing below, for a more detailed preview of the system you can check out our Dev Workshop.

TRAIT LOCKING (LIVE)

One of the primary requests we’ve received for Riven Mods is to allow players to lock a stat to prevent it from being Cycled. Now, you can do so with a system called Trait Locking!

One trait (stat) can be locked at a time while cycling a Riven, freezing it until the lock is removed or the Cycling menu is closed.

There is no cost or item required to lock a trait. Locks can be removed and re-added for free! However, when cycling a Riven Mod with a locked trait, the Kuva cost is doubled. Locking a trait is entirely optional, and Rivens can still be cycled as usual without a lock.

How to Lock a Riven Trait:

1. Go to the Mod Segment in your Base of Operations

2. Select the Riven Mod you’d like to cycle and select the “Cycle Riven” option

3. Select the “Lock Trait” button

Image Description: Screenshot of the Riven Mod UI showing the Soma Argicron Mod. The options for Lock Trait and Cycle for 3,500 Kuva are shown and the cursor is hovering on Lock Trait.

4. Select the trait you wish to lock. The locked trait will appear greyed out with a lock icon on the Mod and will also be listed above the “Lock Trait” button.

Image Description: Screenshot of the Choose A Trait To Lock UI screen. Two traits are displayed as options, the first is a 1.5 times Damage to Grineer, and the second is an increased Critical Chance by 167.2%.

Image Description: Screenshot of the Riven Mod UI screen showing the Soma Argircon Mod with a locked trait denoted by a lock icon. Above the Lock Trait option is the currently locked trait of increased Critical Chance by 167.2% and a remove icon next to it.

5. Begin cycling your Riven with the locked trait! Notice that once locked, it will not change while cycling.

Image Description: Screenshot of the Riven Mod Cycling UI at the Cycle preview screen. We can see that the Riven Mod has a locked trait and the resulting cycled Mod retains the locked trait.

It is important to note that a trait needs to be re-locked each time you open this menu, as they are not maintained once the menu has been closed.

Cycling a Riven with a locked trait prevents the number of traits on the Mod from changing. This means a Mod with 3 positive traits and 1 negative trait will always cycle into another version with 3 positive traits and 1 negative trait if one of the traits is locked. To cycle into a different number of traits, unlock all traits and cycle again.

RIVEN SPLICING (LAUNCHING AT A LATER DATE WITH GLACIAL DEFIANCE)

Riven Splicing combines two traits on a Riven together to form a new spliced trait which is then locked on the Riven (without consuming a Trait Lock). Riven Splicing will require Riven Splicers, which will be rewarded from Glacial Defiance when it releases sometime before or on October 15 (release window PSA for more info). For more details about Riven Splicing, refer to our Dev Workshop.

BANSHEE REWORK

Banshee may not be at the top of your Loadout list, but those who have mastered her kit are able to deal a significant amount of damage in-mission. While not the flashiest pick, she can turn any weapon into something truly lethal — our goal is to maintain that lethality, while adding a few quality of life lifts to her Abilities. The following changes aim to make her kit feel fluid and impactful, while staying true to her identity as a sound-based Warframe.

Passive

Banshee’s Passive is helpful to a sect of players who value stealth, but largely has little effect in most missions. We are keeping her hush mechanic, but adding a little extra value that feels thematically appropriate for our sound-based Warframe.

- Banshee’s abilities pierce the ears of nearby enemies. Casting abilities applies maximum Puncture Status on foes within 10m.
- Banshee’s weapon noises are hushed.

First Ability - Sonic Boom

Sonic Boom is an excellent survival tool for Banshee, but its ragdoll mechanic does not synergize well with other elements of her kit. The following changes help improve its synergy, and improve Banshee’s overall survivability.

- Sonic Boom is now cast one-handed and no longer roots Banshee in place.
- Removed Ragdoll from affected enemies — now pushes them back and stuns them.
  - The ragdoll did not synergize well with Sonar as it made it harder to target the weakpoints.
- Increased Impact damage from 50 to 250.
- Sonic Boom now reduces 70% of Armor, scaling with Ability Strength up to 100%.
  - This mechanic was originally tied to Sonic Fracture, but is now part of her base kit.
- Sonic Boom now breaks resource containers and breakable decorations.
- Sonic Boom now deals a guaranteed Impact Status Effect.
- Renamed the “Strength” stat to “Push Force”, as it affects how strongly Sonic Boom will push enemies.

Sonic Fracture Augment

- Renamed to “Sonic Siphon”.
- Banshee gains Armor for 20s if an enemy is hit by Sonic Siphon, up to a cap of 1500 Armor.
  - Armor does not need to be stripped for this bonus Armor to apply!
- Recasting Sonic Siphon will refresh the Bonus Armor duration if an enemy is struck by the cast.

Second Ability - Sonar

Sonar turns many weapons into a powerhouse in the right hands, but requires a lot of spam casting to reach its full potential. In addition, it offers little synergy with existing Weak Point enhancements as Sonar’s Weak Spots are considered a separate mechanic. These changes address common player feedback, while also giving the ability a light quality of life lift.

- Sonar’s Weak Spots are now considered Weak Points, giving them synergy with a variety of Mods, Arcanes, and more.
- Sonar now prioritizes unmarked parts of the body for its Weak Spots to help with Weak Spot legibility.
  - The reticle now turns red when shooting at the Weak Spots, just like other Weak Points.
- Casting Sonar no longer roots Banshee in place.
- Updated SFX when hitting a Weak Spot.

Resonance Augment

- Unchanged.

Third Ability - Silence

Silence is a powerful Ability, but its impact on the battlefield is not always clear to players. The following changes help improve the visibility of its effects, with a few quality of life touches!

- Silence can now be recast to reset its duration and reapply the Stun.
- Casting Silence no longer roots Banshee in place.
- Improved VFX on enemies to make it clearer they are affected by Silence.

Savage Silence Augment

- Unchanged.

Fourth Ability - Sound Quake

Sound Quake is an iconic part of Banshee’s kit, but it struggles to synergize with her other Abilities. Our goal with the following changes is to make Sound Quake fit more fluidly into Banshee’s playstyle, while retaining the nostalgic elements that are emblematic of her kit.

- Sound Quake is no longer channelled. Casting the ability creates an area of effect with a duration of 25 seconds.
- Resonating Quake fans may be confused by this change — we are swapping the mechanics of the Augment and the base Ability to make Sound Quake synergize better with the rest of Banshee’s kit.
- Banshee is briefly invulnerable when casting Sound Quake.
- Enemies receive guaranteed Blast Status Effects while in Sound Quake’s area of effect, which reduces based on distance from the center.
- Increased casting animation speed to make it more fluid.

S*ound Quake now breaks containers within its entire area of effect, not just the 5 metres around the epicenter.

Resonating Quake Augment

- Renamed to “Gaseous Quake”.
- Gaseous Quake is now a channelled Ability, which takes on the mechanic of Banshee’s old Sound Quake.
- Banshee is rooted in place to channel Gaseous Quake, which deals additional Gas Damage and Status Effects to enemies in the area of effect.
- Gaseous Quake’s damage is multiplied every second during cast, up to a cap.
  - Energy drain also increases every second during casting.
- Gaseous Quake's area of effect now persists for a short duration after channelling ends.
- Improved Line of Sight now applies to Gaseous Quake.
- Updated VFX and SFX to reflect this new gassy styling of the Ability.
