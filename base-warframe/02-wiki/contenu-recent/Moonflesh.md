Moonflesh is a resource introduced during Epoch Operation: Taubound. During the Operation, it can be exchanged with Praghasa in a special Relay on Eris for various rewards.

## Acquisition

While Epoch Operation: Taubound is live, players can collect Moonflesh to provide fuel for Praghasa by doing any of the following:

- Killing enemies on Eris.
  - Defeat Infested in missions on Eris (Maggots not included) for a 2% chance for them to drop 2 Moonflesh (3 on Steel Path).
    - Resource drop chance boosters and looting abilities of any type (e.g. Ore Gaze, Pilfering Strangledome, Desecrate) do not affect the drop chance or drop rate.
    - Resource Booster and Resourceful Retriever do not multiply the resource amount.
    - Moonflesh is distributed to the entire squad on pickup. If one member picks up a Moonflesh drop, all players will receive the drop regardless of whether they are near the resource or not.
- Completing Operation Alerts on Eris.
  - Each Alert offers 50 Moonflesh (75 on Steel Path) as a completion reward. Infested enemies in these Alerts will also have a chance to drop Moonflesh.
    - Alerts occur every 30 minutes and last for 90 minutes. They can only be completed once while active, either on Normal or Steel Path.
  - Only Exterminate, Hive, Mobile Defense, and Survival are generated for Alerts.

## Farming Tips

Your thirst for knowledge is as insatiable as mine.

These farming locations and tips are based on opinions and may not be 100% true. These should be viewed as advice and not critical facts, and results may vary from player to player.

- As Moonflesh is not affected by any type of boosters or loot retrieval abilities, farming speed is directly tied to the squad's KPM (kills per minute). When farming for Moonflesh, it is therefore recommended to play game modes with infinitely spawning enemies and high spawn rates, such as Survival.
  - Steel Path increases the spawn rate of enemies while also increasing the Moonflesh amount per drop from 2 to 3, massively increasing gains.
- The Dark Sector Survival mission Zabala is the most popular farming spot, as while the +30% resource drop chance bonus has no effect on Moonflesh drop chance, it does let players farm additional resources such as Plastids and Mutagen Samples while collecting Moonflesh. It also rewards a guaranteed Neo Void Relic on rotation B and has a 87.5% chance to reward an Axi relic on rotation C.
  - Farming speeds will vary based on kill rate, but a medium KPM of 100 - 150 will yield about 6 - 9 Moonflesh per minute.
- Completing Operation Alerts for Moonflesh is highly effective, with the Steel Path version yielding 75 Moonflesh for missions that can typically be completed in 5 minutes or less.
  - Acquiring all event-exclusive items will cost the player 4,175 Moonflesh, equivalent to 56 Steel Path alerts (not counting any Moonflesh dropped by enemies in those alerts).

## Lore

Moonflesh is a viscous substance harvested from Eris and used as fuel by Praghasa, the Sentient Mothership, to generate the enormous amount of energy needed to open a path to the Tau System.

## Trivia

- Moonflesh drops are not actually exclusive to Infested enemies, but instead can potentially drop from any enemy so long as the mission takes place around Eris. This even includes enemies such as Errant Specters in the Granum Void, Kuva Grineer troops, Corrupted from Void Fissure missions on Eris and even Kuva Clouds in Kuva Siphon missions.
- Sometimes when picking up Moonflesh, Praghasa can be heard whispering.
