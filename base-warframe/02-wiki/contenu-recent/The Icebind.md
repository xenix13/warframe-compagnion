The Icebind (formerly known as Glacial Defiance) is a game mode that consists of six-player squads voting on and engaging in randomized missions with additional restrictions.

Access to the Icebind requires completion of Angels of the Zariman and Whispers in the Walls, along with access to either Elite Deep Archimedea or Elite Temporal Archimedea. It can be accessed by speaking to Cephalon Melica on Yuvan Peak, Earth.

## Mechanics

### Restrictions

All Icebind missions incur the following restrictions, regardless of individual Binds:

- No Duplicate Warframes:
  - The Squad may not have more than one of each Warframe.
    - This also includes Prime variants (e.g. players cannot use a Mag and a Mag Prime together).
      - This excludes Excalibur Umbra from Excalibur and Excalibur Prime.
- Empowered Enemies:
  - Enemy Health, Shields and Armor increased by 200%, plus 100% per squad member, up to a maximum of 800%.
  - Increased chance of Eximus.
- Restricted Respawns:
  - Self-revive is not allowed.
  - Last Gasp's drain begins 2 seconds earlier with each use.
  - Bleedout timer reduces with each revive by -5 seconds, down to a minimum of 5 seconds.
    - Nokko's Vital Decay will only activate 2 times after which he will enter bleedout on subsequent downs.
  - After a Tenno has bled-out, a Tombstone will appear where they died. Reviving that Tenno requires at least 3 surviving Tenno to temporarily sacrifice their maximum Health.
    - If fewer than 3 Tenno are alive, all surviving players must sacrifice.
- Frozen Keys:
  - After being dead for 3 minutes, you will not obtain any Frozen Keys that the squad acquires from completing objectives while you are dead.
- Restricted Consumables:
  - Team Bonus Consumables have a 3-minute cooldown.
- Fatigued Companions:
  - Companion Recovery timers are increased by 100%.
- Stunted Shields:
  - Invulnerability duration from a full Shield Gate is reduced by 50%.

### Cryobells

Players can collect three Cryobells per Weekly Reset on Yuvan Peak, being able to hold up to six. Each Cryobell represents a Mission, containing a random mode, a random Planet, a random Faction, a Mission Bind, a Tactical Bind, and a Personal Bind.

A squad of six players can be formed on Yuvan Peak, where each player will select one of their Cryobells. Once the Cryobells have been selected, a mission vote will start, and a minute-long timer will start counting down once at least three players have voted (the Host can start the timer early, if they'd like). The Host's vote breaks any ties, and a random one is chosen if none are voted for. Once the mission is successfully completed, all selected Cryobells will be consumed.

Public matchmaking and playing with fewer than six players is not currently available.

#### Game Modes

A Cryobell can contain one of the following game modes:

- Survival (10 minutes)
- Mobile Defense (3 Terminals, 3 minutes each)
- Excavation (600 Cryotic)
- Disruption (8 Conduits)

The mission will take place on any Planet in the Origin System. Duviri, The Dark Refractory and Höllvania are excluded from selection.

The mission will have a random Faction independent of its region, being one of the following:

- Grineer
- Corpus
- Infested
- Orokin
- The Murmur

### Side Objectives

Icebind missions contain up to three Side Objectives, marked by a purple waypoint. They will appear periodically through the mission's duration. In Excavation Icebinds, Side Objectives will instead appear based on total Cryotic excavated.

Each Side Objective requires a certain number of players to be nearby before starting, and if the squad chooses to start one, it becomes mandatory in order to complete the mission; failing it will fail the entire mission, and players will not be allowed to extract until it is completed. Succeeding in the Side Objective will reward all players with a Frozen Key (however, if a player has been dead for 3 or more minutes, they will not receive one).

Once initiated, the squad has 1 minute and 30 seconds (1 minute for Cryothermia) to complete the side objective. This timer is paused if progress is being made to the objective (except for Indomitable Ice). If the mission's main objective completes while a Side Objective is in progress, extraction will be delayed until the current side objective has finished.

## Notes

- The Icebind can only be initiated by talking to Cephalon Melica on the Yuvan Peak, not the Zariman Ten Zero.
- In the Glacial Laboratory reward room, eight audio logs can be found, detailing Melica's pre-Cephalon life as Mel Cantor and her encounter with Nuri and The Indifference. One audio log is unlocked per completion of the Icebind.
- Security Bypasses do not appear in Survival.
- Voidplumes and Voca can spawn and be collected in their designated tilesets.
- The Murmur spawns periodically in Albrecht's Laboratories tilesets even if the assigned faction is different.
- Ice Speaker plays during all Icebind missions.
- It is theoretically possible to claim rewards 9 times in a single week, provided the player has 6 Cryobells saved up from previous weeks and claims the 3 Cryobells of the current week. (Needs to be tested.)

## General Tips

- Survivability is extremely important. A fallen player can be revived in bleedout, but not once they have "fully" died.
  - Consider bringing the following Warframes:
    - Support (Dante, Trinity, Oberon, Octavia Wisp, Citrine, Garuda, Follie, Frost,Harrow, Styanax, Jade, Equinox, or Vazarin)
    - High survivability (Rhino,Nidus,Nezha, Inaros, Chroma, Gara, Nyx, Revenant, Valkyr, or Hildryn)
    - Specialized crowd controllers (Banshee's Sound Quake, Saryn's Spores, Sevagoth's Gloom (Helminth subsumable), Titania's Lantern, Mag's Pull and Targeted Magnetize, Khora's Ensnare and Strangledome, or Vauban's Vortex)
  - Equip survival mods (Adaptation, Quick Thinking, and/or Rolling Guard).
  - Avoid straying too far from the squad to ensure they can reach a player in bleedout in time.
    - Vazarin's Mending Soul is especially helpful at ensuring an ally gets up as quickly as possible and does not miss out on rewards by dying.
    - Vazarin's Nidri Tauron Strike can instantly revive dead squad members.
- There are no Gear restrictions (apart from the 3 minute cooldown for Team Bonus Consumables), allowing the use of Specters.
- Unlike Deep Archimedea and Temporal Archimedea, there are no loadout restrictions. Take whichever Warframes and weapons necessary to counteract the Binds.
- If planning to bring Titania or Yareli, be aware that Razorwing or riding on Merulina, respectively, prohibit the player from carrying items for Side Objectives.
  - Though this can be circumvented by use of Transference, this leaves the player much more vulnerable.

## Bugs

- If any players leave the squad during a mission, secondary objectives will become impossible as six players are required to complete them.
- Some secondary objectives can spawn in unintended and possibly unreachable areas, depending on the tileset used for the mission.
  - Lua tiles can be particularly problematic as side objectives may spawn in the Halls of Ascension rooms despite these trials being impossible to activate.
