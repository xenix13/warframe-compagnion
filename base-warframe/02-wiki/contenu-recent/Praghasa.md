Praghasa is the Sentient Mothership. She first appeared in the teaser trailer for The New War during TennoCon 2018. She makes a full appearance during The New War, used as the base of operations for Ballas and Erra.

She later reappears during Epoch Operation: Taubound and serves as both a limited-time relay and the event vendor, trading various goods for Moonflesh.

## Lore

### Erra and The Maker

Aboard the Sentient Mothership, Natah discusses with Erra, as the latter holds Ballas on a leash, about their plans for war with the Origin System. Erra orders Natah to rally the Sentients.

Erra reveals that their mother is dead, but Natah has her fire and can finish the war. Natah, however, recalls when Erra was apparently destroyed by the Tenno in the Old War. Erra replies that she remembers wrongly and will need time to heal from what the Makers had done to her. As Natah points out that Ballas is a Maker, Erra suddenly grabs and throws Natah onto a pedestal which traps her in an energy field. Erra orders Natah to finish the war as he drops Ballas's leash, allowing him to stand as they both silently stare at Natah.

### The New War

Lotus: "But not the one ship Ballas now controls. The great Sentient weapon of the Old War. Do you know what she was called? Praghasa. The Devourer." Ordis: "Yes, we know it. The ship that feeds on debris and derelicts to rebuild itself."—Lotus and Ordis on Praghasa

In the events of the actual quest, Praghasa is shown to be rather a gargantuan Sentient battleship. The Tenno- Grineer- Corpus alliance engage the Sentient Murex fleet in the Veil Proxima, allowing the Railjack to get close enough for Teshin to board her so he can disable the battleship's Orphix fields. Despite their efforts, Ballas banishes the Lotus and the Tenno into a Void portal, allowing him to take over the Origin System and brainwash the masses under Narmer.

After the Drifter partially restores the Lotus and returns the Tenno, an enraged Ballas decides to use Praghasa's intended purpose, which the Lotus reveals to be a forced eradication tool of the entire Sol System by consuming the Sun, removing all sources of natural heat, energy, and over the course of time would result in the Sol System becoming devoid of life. Ordis adds that Praghasa consumes derelicts and debris to grow and recover from damage. Praghasa also shows that it has the ability to, in some extent, create access to the Void as a method of crossing between the Sol and Tau systems; this particular power seems to have had backlash, as Sentients were weak and harmed by the Void as well as The Man in the Wall seeming to interfere with Praghasa's process of consuming the sun. This power was also what allowed the Lotus to repel her mother away from Sol, ultimately causing her severe internal damage.

The Lotus and the Tenno board Praghasa separately. Erra, realizing Ballas's plan to sacrifice the Sol System and his Narmer empire to escape to Tau means dooming the Sentients, defects and guides the Tenno's Necramech when they board. He leads them to the final Archon in hopes of fully restoring the Lotus, before being seemingly crushed by debris. With the Archon's shard, the Lotus and Tenno confront Ballas, and after a long battle they finally subdue and end his threat for good. The victory is cut short as The Man in the Wall emerges from a Void portal and leaves behind cryptic words before mysteriously vanishing.

### The Old Peace

While reminiscing on his past and the sacrifices others did for his sake, Roathe mentions a time in the early Old War in which an ally of his, Traivon, sacrificed himself along with his Railjack and crew in a battle with her. Traivon rammed his ship into Praghasa's maw, which she consumed, something that distracted her long enough for Roathe to escape with his life.

### Prologue to Tau / Epoch Operation: Taubound

I... AWAKEN.—Praghasa

Praghasa's long-dormant mind has reawakened in response to her daughter's call, and has chosen to help the children of her daughter to reach Tau. To make the jump, Moonflesh from Eris is used as fuel (in lieu of using Sol as Ballas originally intended). Presumably thanks to Moonflesh or through other means, she has largely recovered from her previous damage, although what used to be Ballas' throne and Narmer's All-Seeing Eye remains destroyed and floating in front of a swarm of blue energy, where her consciousness presumably resides.

During the Operation, Praghasa sent daily messages through the Daily Tribute to the player, about the following:

- She awoke to the calls of her daughter, Natah, and subsequently called for both her and Erra, only to describe them as "in ruin(s)".
- She denounced Pazuul as a false prophet, and that she should have let him die instead.
- She claimed to not hear the lords of Tau and only whispers, describing it as "The chattering masses swarm, and fill your mind with madness".
- She claimed that Tau is "not for you" (though she does not specify whether she means the Tenno, the Lotus, or even Pazuul), claiming their return will cause harm.
- She called for Hunhow, before going on a rumination on how many are gone and broken now, including a passing remark on the Golden Makers being no more, and how she rejoiced at their fall.
- She also remarks the Sentients as a whole "made the world a reflection of a shallow self" and just like the Orokin, they chose "gilded hatred". This matches Roathe's remark in one of his KIM chats that the Sentients knew the kind of cruelty the Orokin would unleash on the terraformed worlds, and they instead wanted to be on top of it, explaining why even during the time of The Old Peace there were Sentients wanting to restart the war; this is offhandedly mentioned by Erra during The New War as well.

## Trivia

- Praghasa's first appearance was in a teaser trailer for The New War, which revealed her to be a gargantuan Sentient outpost or ship, located far outside the Origin System and containing a massive army of Sentients within a chamber shaped similarly to a ribcage. Referred to by Natah as "mother", Praghasa urged Natah to "take her fire back" from the Tenno who had "seduced" and "used" her. However, this sequence of events is likely no longer canon, as the quest displays much different circumstances for Praghasa and her presence in the story.
- During the Art Panel of Tennocon 2017, it was hinted that the original Sentients sent from the Origin System were in fact one singular, massive individual, which fragmented into pieces along the way. One of these pieces was stated to be the 'Sentient Outpost', a label frequently used for Praghasa during concept and development of the quest.
  - Whether or not this concept is still canon is unknown; dialogue from The Old Peace, as well as Brysko's comments regarding the Sentient children he encounters in Fornax indicates that Sentients may not in fact share a single common precursor.
- Praghasa's voice lines during The New War Teaser appear to be identical to the recordings in Stolen Dreams and The New Strange, implying that she may have been the speaker, and that the Arcane Machine is connected to her in some manner. However, during Epoch Operation: Taubound, Praghasa's voice is very different and she speaks much more slowly. As Hunhow, a fellow Sentient of similar size and power, speaks relatively normally (even in his current condition), this may be a sign that Praghasa is still weak and recovering from her previous condition.
  - The cause of said condition is unknown, as is the exact cause for her recovery. However, given that Natah stated during The New War that Praghasa was capable of self-repair using debris and derelicts she consumed, this is a likely explanation, as Natah was last seen surrounded by a field of derelict Narmer and Sentient ships.
- While Praghasa is referred to by Natah as "the great Sentient weapon of the Old War", she was most likely rendered inactive (whether deceased, dormant, or braindead) at some point before the War actually ended, as Erra, when attacking the Reservoir as seen during the Erra quest's flashback, refers to himself as "the only family [Natah's] got left".
  - During The Maker, Erra simply says Praghasa is "gone", with her body being the only thing left of her. This indicates even he may not have been entirely sure whether Praghasa was actually dead.
- During the Ropalolyst boss fight, Natah states that her mother was "a carpenter". Assuming this is a metaphor, it could possibly allude to her original intended purpose as a terraforming tool for the Tau System.
  - Another interpretation of this dialogue is that Natah is recalling memories of her Margulis identity and that the carpenter is Margulis' mother, as she later reveals that the first story she shared was a fabrication and that she is, in fact, a sentient mimic who merely played the role of the Tenno's mother.
    - Later revelations give credence to the possibility that Natah/the Lotus was telling the truth about her origins.
- Praghasa is referred to as "Sentient Mothership" in the art panel during TennoCon 2021. The art was called "Sentient Outpost". This would later be how she is used, as both a major battlecraft and mothership intended to swallow the Sun during The New War.
- Praghasa in Sanskrit means "voracious" or "gluttonous", befitting her role as a star/planet-devouring Weapon of Mass Destruction.
- Despite being much larger than the Murex, she shares much of the tilesets with little to no alterations except the skybox. The only unique tilesets for Praghasa are the base during Erra and The Maker, as well as Ballas' Narmer throne (which may actually be the same room), as the other room with spectral Ropalolysts forming from the center (see Gallery below) is shared with Pazuul's Murex during Veilbreaker and Break Narmer's Prison Break.
