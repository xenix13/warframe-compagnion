# Patch note : Jade Shadows: Constellations: Hotfix 43.0.1
Date : 2026-06-17
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1510149-jade-shadows-constellations-hotfix-4301/

A Note About Build Stability!
Jade Shadows: Constellations is an ambitious update on many fronts: our most complicated Warframe, earned from a complicated mission type. The release wasn’t to our usual standards, and we want to thank you all for your patience and understanding as we work to improve it.

As a result, the team is working on a script to give additional Crimson and Emerald Talents to our Day 1 “early adopter” Tenno who weathered the Railjack turbulence with us. Details to come on that tomorrow!

Expect more hotfixes to come! In the meantime, we hope you enjoy all the goodness that shipped with Jade Shadows: Constellations.

Speaking of goodness: you may have heard that additional Garuda lore was released with Jade Shadows: Constellations. It is true! But you may have been looking in the wrong spot. We advise you to move your searches to Pontis Tower on Uranus, for there you shall find what you seek about our murderous maiden.

Changes:

- Increased the Stug's Blob Embed Status Chance to 10%.

Top Fixes:

- Fixed Clients being forced into Archwing and losing function during a transition in The Kuva Wytch mission.
- Fixed not being extracted properly from Uranus Proxima missions.
- Now, you should be returned to your Base of Operations or the Pontis Tower upon mission completion.

- Fixed loss of function for Sirius and Orion Fixed after installing a Forma or swapping polarities.
- Also fixed this visually duplicating Warframes in the Arsenal.
- Fixed Clients stuck in an infinite loading screen when joining a Uranus Proxima mission from the Base of Operations, while the Host is in the Pontis Tower.
- Fixed a case of becoming permanently invincible as Sirius & Orion.
- Fixed a crash when entering the Personal Quarters in a specific segment of the Jade Shadows quest

Uranus Proxima Fixes:

- Fixed Ryoku's shadow clones looking black on low settings.
- Fixed various flickering environment elements in The Kuva Wytch mission.
- Fixed ship names in Uranus Proxima missions using the Host's language.
- Fixed being able to sell a special trinket.
- If you sold the trinket in question before this hotfix, please contact support to have it returned to you!

- Fixed transmissions and SFX incorrectly playing during a lockdown segment in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed an incorrect spawn position in the Jade Shadows: Constellations quest.
- Fixed a Ramsled in the new Uranus Proxima missions displaying a filepath instead of their name.
- Fixed being able to access a certain character's ability screen in the Jade Shadows: Constellations Quest.
- Fixed script errors related to Ramsleds and Ryoku's shadow clones in the Jade Shadows: Constellations quest.
