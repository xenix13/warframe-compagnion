# Patch note : Iceblade of Narin: Hotfix 44.0.3
Date : 2026-09-30
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1526292-iceblade-of-narin-hotfix-4403/

Narin Changes & Fixes:

- Increased the duration of Cold Ammo Packs dropped by Narin from 10s to 15s.
- Nurinarim Mark Changes & Fixes:
- Narin can now fire projectiles during Nurinarim at targets regardless of whether they are Marked or not.
- Attacks on Marked targets cost no Ice and do increased damage.

- Increased Nurinarim's duration from 15s to 20s.
- Narin can now use Transference during Nurinarim.
- Parkour Velocity and Dodge stats now apply to Narin during Nurinarim.
- Mark Changes:
- The initial Mark is now created immediately upon Nurinarim activation instead of having a delay.
- Reduced the time between Marks being generated.
- Increased the likelihood that targets closer to where you are currently aiming will be Marked during Nurinarim.
- Marks are now placed based on the target’s height to improve readability.
- Added a pulse VFX to Narin’s reticle when successfully hitting a Marked target during Nurinarim.

- Fixed Nurinarim’s projectiles not targeting targets beyond 50m even if the target was already Marked.
- Fixed Nurinarim not dealing damage to The Severed Warden's arms.
- Fixed Nurinarim being able to target neutral units, like Conservation targets.

- Fixed Client Narins not gaining Ice when damaging enemies with Hakchum.

Changes:

- Reduced the Blitz Eximus' shockwave attack's base damage from 250 to 100.
- This attack was a common mission killer at higher levels if it targeted an objective like a Defense Target or Mobile Defense Terminal. Now their attacks should be less likely to one-shot a defensible objective.

- The Iceblade of Narin blessing's VFX now only appears when players are in mission with a Melee weapon equipped.
- Removed the ability for Entropic Eximus to spawn as Nullifier units.
- Reverted the Research Point changes introduced to Archimedea missions with the Iceblade of Narin.
- Using a Warframe that matches those in the Individual Parameters rewards 1 Research Point once more.
- Restored the Research Point reward track to provide the Legendary Reward at 34 Research Points, and Vosfor or Pix Chips at 37 Research Points.
- This change had a mixed reception with players, and we ultimately decided to revert it so players didn’t feel “forced” to match their Warframe due to the increased Research Points they offered.
- Note: the other changes to Archimedea missions introduced with Iceblade of Narin remain untouched.

- Players affected by the No Pets Allowed Personal Modifier in Archimedea missions now have an increased innate Vacuum range.
- The "Hide Owned" toggle now hides Blueprints of crafted cosmetics in the Nightwave Cred Offerings since players can only own one copy of a cosmetic.
- Removed muffled SFX when players are dead and spectating squadmates.
- Restored Entropic Eximus bubble hit SFX as it no longer triggers squad-wide.
- Reduced Grendel's Nourish SFX distance for squadmates, and slightly reduced its volume.
- Rescue Target downed transmissions are no longer affected by the Mission Tutorial Transmissions toggle.

Top Fixes:

- Fixes towards players losing function when swapping between Sirius & Orion.
- There were quite a few different cases causing this, and we hope to have addressed as many as we could find. We’ll be keeping an eye out for reports if this issue persists after this hotfix!

- Fixed Ash Teleporting to Join the Fray resulting in future casts of Teleport being broken.
- Fixed being unable to access Operator or Focus menus on Yuvan Peak.
- Fixed the Energy tint channel not applying to the Opula Armor Set.
- Fixed Hildryn's Balefire being able to bypass Entropic Eximus bubbles.
- Fixed Valkyr's Rage VFX flashing on-screen every time a player Transferences back to Valkyr.
- Fixed various offset issues on Styanax Prime.
- Fixed Sevagoth's Shadow missing in the Arsenal when attempting to upgrade or customize him.

Fixes:

- Fixed Temple's Exalted Solo breaking their ability to Wall Jump/Latch.
- Fixed the Jade Aureolt Helmet not opening when toggling on Glory in the Arsenal.
- Fixed the metallic elements on the Harrow Profitas Skin no longer appearing metallic.
- Fixed overlapping Arcane and Exilus Slots when linking a Mod Config for an Exalted Primary or Secondary weapon.
- Fixed various cases of Entropic Kuva pickup SFX not playing.
- Fixed additional edge cases of players being able to Emote (and therefore fall out of the map) when attempting to pull Narin's Iceblade on Yuvan Peak.
- Fixed Cephalon Melica playing a transmission about Narin's Iceblade now that players can successfully pull it from the ice.
- Fixed suggested Polarity marker bleeding across Mod categories in the Railjack Plexus.
- Fixed the Pause Menu dissolving if open while going through a Sanctuary Onslaught portal.
- Fixed Mods popping into view if they are partially offscreen while scrolling through Mods in the Mod Bench.
- Fixed performance issues caused by chatlinking a Riven if a chat filter is applied.
- Fixed a filepath in the Headcracker Honoria description.

Script Errors:
Script errors mean that something went wrong when the game client was executing a script, which can have various outcomes. Usually it means the related content didn’t function properly under specific circumstances!

- Fixed a script error in The Descendia.
- Fixed a script error related to Narin's Neote.
- Fixed a rare script error related to Duviri Decree generation.
- Fixed a script error related to Koumei's Omamori.
- Fixed a script error related to Oraxia's Scuttlers.
- Fixed a script error related to Cephalon Simaris' ability restrictions in Sanctuary Onslaught.
- Fixes towards clipping issues with Narin's skirt during animations.
- Fixed a script error related to Sirius' Jade Stars.
- Fixed a script error related to Cyte-09's Resupply.
