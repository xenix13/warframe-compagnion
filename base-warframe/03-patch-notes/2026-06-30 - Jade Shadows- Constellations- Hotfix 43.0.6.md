# Patch note : Jade Shadows: Constellations: Hotfix 43.0.6
Date : 2026-06-30
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1514654-jade-shadows-constellations-hotfix-4306/

Steel Essence Acquisition in Steel Path Railjack

- Steel Path Railjack missions now reward 2 Steel Essence upon mission completion.
- Removed Steel Essence from Eximus drop tables in Steel Path Railjack missions.
- With the introduction of Steel Path Railjack, we added Steel Essence to Eximus drop tables since Acolytes cannot reliably spawn in Railjack missions. This system unfortunately did not meet our goals: space-only “casual” Railjack missions saw little in terms of Steel Essence acquisition, while savvy players were able to acquire significant amounts of Steel Essence in endless Railjack missions with the right amplified setup. Our aim is to serve all Railjack players, so we’re changing our approach with this hotfix!

Top Fixes:

- Fixed Sirius & Orion's attacks no longer triggering Jade Stars.
- Also reduced the SFX of Sirius' Jade Stars.

- Fixed Combat Discipline not triggering when equipped on Sirius.
- Fixed Sirius's Coronal Ejection only dealing one Heat Status effect regardless of its modded Status Chance.
- Fixed the Obex Incarnon "Rapid Conclusion" Evolution treating Mercy and Finisher kills separately, causing stacks to reset from eligible kills.
- Fixed enemies flying out of the map in Archwing Interception missions, causing players to not be able to complete the mission.
- Hotdropped: Fixed players receiving an incorrect amount (too low) of Talents at the end of Uranus Proxima missions.

PC Exclusive Changes and Fixes:

- Improved handling of shader corruption.
- Improved defenses against drawing with broken shaders.
- Fixed the launcher's mute button not saving preferences on future game launches.

Fixes:

- Fixed the Mawbound Director not dealing any damage in The Kuva Wytch mission.
- Fixed cases of borrowed Railjacks not being at full health at the start of Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed the Scoria's Angel mission being progression stopped if players enter the Ramsled with a dead squadmate.
- Fixed Clients rejoining into space if a host migration occurred right after boarding the Scoria Angel in its Uranus Proxima mission.
- Fixed a script error in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed a script error in the Arsenal.
- Fixed a script error related to the KIM system.
