Archimedea Personal Modifier Groups
This is a new system in Personal Modifier generation for Temporal and Deep Archimedea that was missed in our original patch notes. There are some weeks that can almost feel excessively punishing depending on which Personal Modifiers you receive — to avoid cases of this happening in the future, Personal Modifiers have been separated into four different Groups.

Each week on reset, one Modifier from each Group will be randomly selected to help avoid overly difficult combinations. Due to Archimedea Modifiers being selected at the beginning of the week and Iceblade of Narin launching on a Wednesday, this is not currently live. That said, you will see the first installment of this updated system apply to Personal Modifiers after this upcoming weekly reset: September 28, 2026 at Midnight UTC.

Entropic Eximus Clarifications
With the introduction of our new Entropic Eximus units, we kept our initial patch note description intentionally vague to encourage players to discover the best ways to take them down. An unintended result was player confusion about some of the mechanics and we apologize for any frustration this decision caused.

To help you in your hunt for Entropic Eximus (and their Entropic Kuva), we’re offering more information on how these enemies work so you can understand a) how to kill them and b) what behaviours are intended. We are also adding this information to the original Update Notes.

Entropic Eximus have a resistance to non-melee attacks, forcing you to get in close and use your melee weapon. Breaking the bubble will enable abilities to target the Eximus, but will not change the Eximus’s damage resistance.

- Damage from non-melee sources is reduced by 90%.
- For Operators, damage is instead reduced by 75%.

- Companions do not target Entropic Eximus

Entropic Eximus bubbles prevent projectiles from entering, and prevent Warframe abilities from dealing damage to the Entropic Eximus, from both inside and outside the bubble! The bubble can be broken two ways:

- Dealing melee damage with the Iceblade of Narin buff from Neote on Yuvan Peak will break the bubble if either the bubble or the Entropic Eximus is struck.
- Tauron strikes will break the bubble upon contact.

We hope this clears up confusion around how to best approach taking on Entropic Eximus. If you’re hunting them down, don’t forget to check Void Angels for a few guaranteed spawns! To provide us feedback on Entropic Eximus, please head over to our Update 44 Zariman Updates Feedback Forum.
