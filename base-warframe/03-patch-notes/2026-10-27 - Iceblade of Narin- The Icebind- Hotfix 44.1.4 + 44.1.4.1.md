# Patch note : Iceblade of Narin: The Icebind: Hotfix 44.1.4 + 44.1.4.1
Date : 2026-10-27
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1530579-iceblade-of-narin-the-icebind-hotfix-4414-44141/

Iceblade of Narin: The Icebind: Hotfix 44.1.4.1

Changes & Fixes:

- Removed the Dismantle Demolishers modifier from Cavia Bounties, as it was causing Rogue Necramechs to be permanently invulnerable.
- Fixed broken textures on the Saryn Velanosa Helmet.

Iceblade of Narin: The Icebind: Hotfix 44.1.4

Changes

- Reduced the Nami Skyla Prime Blade's Ducat value from 100 to 65 as a result of its varied rarity from Void Relics.
- The Skull Shots Augment (Viper) can now retrigger its buff while it is currently active.
- Added the option "Make Spawn Room" to the following Dojo rooms (at room console)
- Duviri Cave
- Earth Forest Chamber
- Galleon Chamber
- Infested Chamber
- Kuva Asteroid
- Orokin Tower Chamber
- Ostron Cove
- Starlight Market
- Uranus Chamber

Fixes

- Fixed cases of The Icebind Mobile Defense missions not properly extracting due to a specific tile in the Grineer Asteroid tileset.
- Fixed players who have not unlocked Eris being unable to see or play the Epoch Operation: Taubound Alerts.
- Fixed Amanata Pressure not applying to Heavy Slam Attacks.
- Also fixed Amanata Pressure only affecting one enemy per Heavy Attack.

- Fixed an Incarnon's reticle turning blue before its transformation into an Incarnon is complete, sometimes leading players to interrupt transformation prematurely.
- Fixed the Vasto Incarnon's "Reaper's Plenty" evolution missing its hud buff icon when triggered.
- Fixed a case of Sirius & Orion losing function if you swapped between a Son at a specific moment when travelling through a Sanctuary Onslaught portal.
- Fixed extracting from Zariman missions during Last Gasp potentially breaking active abilities in future missions started from Chrysalith
- Fixed loud SFX playing if players used Front Artillery while the Railjack was boosting with the "Fire Manual Trigger Weapons Continuously" toggled on.
- Fixed pickups being able to spawn in an inaccessible area in a specific Lua tile.
- Fixed various shoulder armor offset issues for Caliban Prime.
- Fixed offset issues for the Spritsail Prime Shoulder Guards on Citrine Prime.
- Fixed a crash related to Voice Chat.
- Fixed a missing "+" in Energized Munition's description.
- Fixed Rusalka's hair appearing too brown in The Hex quest.
- Fixed a rare case of Nidus' Parasitic Vitality being able to kill Nidus or crash the game.
- Fixed Teshin missing animations during a specific segment of The New War quest.
- Fixed Valkyr's Warcry hud buff icon remaining active after being Valkyr is Nullified (via a Nullifier bubble, etc.).

Script Error Fixes:
Script errors mean that something went wrong when the game client was executing a script, which can have various outcomes. Usually it means the related content didn’t function properly under specific circumstances — often we don’t actually know what the error outcome was, so we list it as a Script Error fix to indicate it was addressing an unintended behaviour.

- Fixed a script error related to transmissions, notably those from Vena.
- Fixed a script error in the input binding menu.
- Fixed a script error related to the Dark Refractory screen.
- Fixed a script error related to "explode on kill" effects.

For a list of known issues that are on our radar, visit our dedicated thread: https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1523875-known-issues-iceblade-of-narin/
