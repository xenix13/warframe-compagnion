# Patch note : Jade Shadows: Constellations: Hotfix 43.0.3
Date : 2026-06-19
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1511623-jade-shadows-constellations-hotfix-4303/

Happy Friday, Tenno! A rare hotfix sight from us, but we wanted to address some of the more egregious issues before the weekend.

We’ll continue to investigate and do our best to address the top priorities in our Known Issues List next week. Thanks again for your patience as we continue to work towards stability.

Fixes

- Fixes towards players receiving the same Blueprint rewards upon subsequent Uranus Proxima missions.
- This is our hail mary attempt at a fix for the weekend, there may still be some related issues but we’ll be watching closely for follow up next week.

- Fixed Mission Failed Screens appearing at the end of Uranus Proxima missions after aborting a mission and then replaying it again.
- As shared in our original PSA, this was a UI only issue and players were still receiving their rewards. But we wanted to get this fix out before the weekend as it was understandably deceiving!

- Fixed Personal Railjacks in Uranus Proxima missions using the "loaner" version's Armaments and Crew.
- Known Issue: Ryoku or Vena's crew names appear in the Squad List, but the actual Crew are your own.

- Fixed additional issues caused by Sirius & Orion when passing through Sanctuary Onslaught portals.
- Fixed rare case of being unable to purchase and/or claim new skins (Nightwave rewards).
- Players who were in this state should now be able to purchase/receive skins once again.

- Fixed SFX being far too loud for those using 7.1 headsets.
- Fixed filepaths appearing in Protoframe Crew member subtitles.
- Fixed a crash that could happen if you had Arthur in your Backroom.

For list of known issues that are on our radar, visit our dedicated thread: https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1509550-known-issues-jade-shadows-constellations/
