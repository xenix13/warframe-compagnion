# Patch note : Amir’s Shockwave: Hotfix 43.5.2
Date : 2026-08-13
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1520019-amir%E2%80%99s-shockwave-hotfix-4352/

Changes:

- Removed the blocking volume around the Fables & Frontiers table in the Dominion of Höllvandeim Scene.

Top Fixes:

- Fixed various Amps with Alt-Fire sometimes not responding to Alt-Fire inputs or to the Fire Manual Trigger Weapons Continuously toggle.
- Fixed more Surge Eximus units causing Clients to crash, notably in missions in the Albrecht's Laboratories tileset.
- Fixed damage of multiple Electric Ammo Packs from Cyte-09's Resupply stacking with another.
- Fixed the Raptor fight not progressing after dropping an explosive Power Cell into a Gravity Conveyor while in Transference.
- Fixed Clients not hearing the Surge Eximus' ability SFX.

Fixes:

- Fixed DJ Shockwave's Radio transmissions in the Orbiter not being randomized.
- Fixed the POM-2 not displaying an exclamation mark if there are new Fables & Frontiers KIM messages.
- Fixed another case of being able to equip the Vinquibus in only one weapon slot, or equip disallowed weapons on Companions.
- Fixed duplicate Höllvandeims appearing in the Fast Travel wheel after entering and exiting the Protoframes category.
- Fixed a script error when interacting with Eidolon Lures in the Plains of Eidolon if the mission was joined in progress.
- Fixed a rare script error in The Descendia that resulted in The Shrine Defense mode not working properly.
- Fixed a crash related to Moa Companions.

Performance Changes & Fixes:

- Further improved handling of corrupt shader caches in DirectX 11.
- Made systemic micro-optimizations to DirectX 11 rendering.
- Fixed spotloading when using a Kubrow or Kavat in Höllvania missions.

For list of known issues that are on our radar, visit our dedicated thread: https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1519687-known-issues-amirs-shockwave/
