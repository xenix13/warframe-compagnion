# Patch note : Jade Shadows: Constellations: Hotfix 43.0.2
Date : 2026-06-18
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1510970-jade-shadows-constellations-hotfix-4302/

Future Hotfix Priorities
This is our second hotfix following Jade Shadows: Constellations’ release, and it’s a chunky one at that. While we have been able to tackle quite a few issues in these first few patches, we’re not done yet!

We’re continuing to investigate issues and do our best to ensure overall build stability. The top of our current priority list includes end of mission UI bugs (mission failed or missing rewards) and progression stops, and more. As a reminder, we have a public Known Issues List that tracks priorities on our radar.

Thank you again for sticking with us, for sending us your bug reports and feedback, and for weathering the proverbial storm, Tenno!

Scripts
Two scripts were run today, each offering goodies to different groups of players.

Jade Shadows: Constellations Early Adopters Script
As a thank you (and apology) to those who weathered the storm on Day 1, 100 Crimson Talents and 100 Emerald Talents are being delivered via Inbox to anyone who completed the Jade Shadows: Constellations Quest within the window of June 17th at 11 a.m. ET - June 18th at 12 a.m. ET. This script is still in progress, and we will update the official PSA when it has concluded.

Nora’s Mix Time Tempests Cred Script
Players who already owned the Naimore Armor Bundle prior to this recent Nightwave Series did not receive 50 Nora’s Mix Time Tempest Creds — a script was run earlier today, and the missing Creds have been added to their accounts!

We fixed this issue with Jade Shadows: Constellations, so future players will not come across it.

Changes

- Players now auto-extract after completing The Kuva Wytch and Scoria's Angel missions.
- This prevents cases of players being stuck in limbo when other squadmates do not move towards the extraction point.
- Known Issue: Auto-extracting results in Hosts only seeing Credits in their End of Mission screen — this is a UI-issue only!

- Added the Neo V12 Relic to droptables.
- This is a replacement to the Neo V11 Relic, which is now vaulted as it contains the Epitaph Prime Barrel.

Top Fixes

- Fixed cases of not enough Golden Maws spawning in The Kuva Wytch mission.
- Fixed Sirius & Orion being able to scale Ability Strength infinitely via an interaction with Diriga.
- Fixed Sirius & Orion being able to use Gear via Hotkeys in Sanctuary Onslaught.
- Fixed allied Maggots standing around and not attacking anything (except for those spawned by Nidus).
- Fixed Drifter facial animation issues when using the Vena and Ryoku Gemini Emotes.
- Known Issue: the Drifter's right arm now turns invisible when using these Emotes. Fixing nightmare fuel apparently comes with a cost.

- Fixed Last Gasp teleporting players outside of the boss room in the Scoria's Angel mission.
- Fixed Railjack Crew sometimes not firing their weapons when assigned as Defender.
- Fixed Companions affected by Nidus' Parasitic Link permanently sleeping after entering a Nullifier Bubble.
- Fixed players becoming progression stopped during a section of The New War quest if they started the quest with Sirius & Orion as their equipped Warframe.

Sirius & Orion Fixes

- Fixed a loss of function when attempting to install a Helminth Invigoration on Orion and then attempting to view that Invigoration in the Helminth Chair.
- Folks in this state should have their accounts fixed upon login.

- Fixed being unable to progress in The Teacher quest after installing Vitality on Sirius & Orion.
- Fixed high latency causing Sirius & Orion to be yeeted when dodging during Celestial Clash.
- Fixed cases of the Primary Son not being teleported between Infernums when using Sirius & Orion in The Descendia missions.
- Fixed a loss of function when triggering a cutscene as the Secondary Son for Sirius & Orion in The War Within quest.
- Fixed the inactive Son being partially invisible during gameplay in The New War quest.
- Fixed various issues with SFX from Sirius & Orion's Celestial Clash.
- Fixed a script error when casting Subsumed abilities as Sirius & Orion.
- Fixed various script errors related to Sirius & Orion's Celestial Clash.
- Fixed a script error related to Sentinels when equipped on Sirius & Orion.
- Fixed a script error when swapping to Sirius & Orion in the Arsenal.

Uranus Proxima Fixes

- Fixed cases of extra loud SFX in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed an edge-case of players being able to enter Archwing in Uranus Proxima missions if they joined a mission in progress that was started from the Pontis Tower.
- Fixed cases of ship names in Uranus Proxima missions using the host's language.
- Fixed a script error when attempting to open the Railjack Tactical Screen immediately upon loading into a mission.
