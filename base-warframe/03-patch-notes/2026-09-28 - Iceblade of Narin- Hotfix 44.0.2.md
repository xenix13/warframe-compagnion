# Patch note : Iceblade of Narin: Hotfix 44.0.2
Date : 2026-09-28
Source : https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1525835-iceblade-of-narin-hotfix-4402/

Additions:

- Citrine Prime’s Adornments can now be unequipped as a new Auxiliary Attachment.
- With Iceblade of Narin’s release, these pieces would be removed if Citrine Prime equipped a TennoGen or the Aphrodita helmet. We fixed that bug, but we have made their functionality permanent with this new Auxiliary!

- Added various Day of the Dead cosmetics from the Nightwave: Dreams of the Dead series to the Nightwave Offerings. These are permanent additions that will rotate in as the store refreshes each week!
- Note: We will also be adding these items to Daughter’s Naberus wares when that event launches in October.
- Okina Day of the Dead Skin
- Atomos Day of the Dead Skin
- Taxon Day of the Dead Skin
- Skaut Day of the Dead Skin
- Kayota Day of the Dead Mask
- Monarcierro Day of the Dead Syandana
- Calaverita Sentinel Mask
- Calaverita Sentinel Tail
- Calaverita Sentinel Wing
- Nora Night Catrina Glyph

Changes:

- Shotgun Rivens can now roll Zoom stats in anticipation of the Riven Splicer system (which we hope to release soon).
- The Zoom stat is required to create specific Spliced Traits, as outlined in our Riven Splicer Dev Workshop.

- Adjusted the Deimos Genetrix's SFX to be quieter and to play at a shorter distance.

Top Fixes:

- Fixed the Entropic Eximus' damage reduction being applied twice.
- Fixed Entropic Eximus spawning in The Descendia missions.
- These specific Eximus only spawn in the Zariman for narrative reasons!
- Known Issue: Entropic Eximus can spawn in Sanctuary Onslaught.

- Fixed allied barriers (like Gara's Mass Vitrify) blocking line of sight checks for Narin's Neote.
- Fixed the Galvanized Scope's description incorrectly stating "On Weak Point Hit" (instead of the intended "On Weak Point Kill").
- More fixes towards golden decorations being too yellow.
- With our metal shader improvements in Iceblade of Narin we fixed the issue of "gray cast" on metals, which also applied to decorations and caused them to appear as a very saturated yellow instead of the familiar gold tone. The above fixes aim to return the gold closer to what it was before, while also taking advantage of the wider range of colors we now have at our disposal.

- Fixed Rivens with duplicate traits that were the result of a Riven Cycling bug immediately following Iceblade of Narin’s release.
- Removed negatives from Rivens that had both a positive and negative value of the same trait. (ex: +80% Reload Speed and -17% Reload Speed)
- Any remaining Rivens with numerous negative traits have had the extra negative traits removed.
- Kuva Refund Script: Players who cycled Rivens and were presented with a broken option will have their Kuva refunded.
- Update 3:30pm ET - the script is complete, affected players should see their Kuva refunded!

- Fixed Narin and Uriel’s passives granting permanent elemental buffs if applied without a weapon equipped.

Fixes:

- Fixed Zymos' unique trait not triggering consistently.
- Known Issue: The seeking spore projectiles from the Zymos’ unique trait are invisible.

- Fixed the Dual Toxocyst Incarnon's Neurotoxin evolution not adding Toxin damage.
- Fixed Entropic Eximus killed by Mirage's Hall of Mirrors not dropping Entropic Kuva.
- This also fixes Eximus not dropping Riven Slivers if killed by Mirage's clones.

- Fixed the Archimedea bonus for Ability Strength applying Ability Duration instead.
- Fixed various offsets for the Opula Shoulder Armor.
- Fixed offsets for the Spinele Prime Accessories on the Drifter to match the Operator.
- Fixed cases of the Kerulyst Sentinel Skin's attachments becoming detached when certain Sentinels perform attacks.
- Fixed Voruna’s growling SFX remaining after exiting Ulfrun’s Descent, causing it to stack loudly after multiple casts.
- Fixed placeholder text in the Sharpshooter Mod description.
- Fixed an issue when attempting to use a Zipline.
- Fixed a map hole in the mountain pass in The War Within quest.
- Fixed chat being hidden when viewing a Profile.
- Fixed being able to perform an Emote while pulling out Narin's Iceblade, causing players to fall through the map.
- Fixed a rare crash when starting Zariman Ten Zero missions from the Chrysalith, and a squadmate attempts to enter the Dormizone elevator.
- Fixed a rare in-game chat crash after toggling the Cross Platform Play setting.

Performance Fixes:

- Fixed performance issues caused by the Latron's Incarnon Form projectiles if modded for a high fire rate while using Mirage's Hall of Mirrors.
- Fixed performance issues caused by Banshee's Gaseous Quake.
- Fixed performance issues caused by Aksondol.

Script Errors Fixes:
Script errors mean that something went wrong when the game client was executing a script, which can have various outcomes. Usually it means the related content didn’t function properly under specific circumstances!

- Fixed script errors related to Sirius & Orion's Passive.
- Fixed a script error related to Mining Lasers.
- Fixed a script error related to Narin's Neote.
- Fixed a script error related to Zenurik's Tauron Strike ability.
- Fixed a script error related to Citrine's Crystalize.
- Fixed a script error related to Banshee's Sound Quake.
- Fixed a script error when chatlinking Mods.

For list of known issues that are on our radar, visit our dedicated thread: https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1523875-known-issues-iceblade-of-narin/
