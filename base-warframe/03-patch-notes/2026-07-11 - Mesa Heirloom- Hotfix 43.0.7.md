Top Fixes:

- Fixed cases of Clients desyncing during Sirius & Orion's Celestial Clash, which could cause Clients to be ineligible for Relic Rewards in Void Fissures, and other effects.
- Fixed cases of Clients teleporting to previous locations during Sirius & Orion’s Celestial Clash.
- Fixed Primary Compression not reducing explosive range and increasing damage for the Tenet Envoy while aiming down sights.
- Fixed dying as the Secondary Son resulting in Sirius & Orion having no Secondary Weapon after reviving.
- Fixed cases of players in a special state (Last Gasp, Protea's Temporal Anchor, Sevagoth's Shadow, etc.) not being teleported by objective countdown timers in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Known issue: This issue still occurs for Inaros in his special death state.

- Fixed instances of players getting stuck in mission load until restart.
- Improved handling of crashes caused by running out of memory.
- Fixed the Imperator Vandal receiving a duplicate entry in player Profiles.
- Fixed the Secondary Son of Sirius & Orion being able to bypass weapon restrictions in Sortie missions.

Sirius & Orion Fixes:

- Fixed the Mausolon not consuming ammo when equipped by the Secondary Son.
- Fixed the Secondary Son being able to enter a Warframe-restricted area, and therefore a cinematic in the Angels of the Zariman quest.
- Fixed swapping to Sirius & Orion from a Warframe with an Exalted weapon resulting in that Exalted Weapon's icon appearing next to the Secondary Son's name in the Arsenal.
- Fixed Sirius & Orion being tiny in the Jordan Golem Assassinate extraction cinematic if you extracted as the Secondary Son.
- Fixed cases of Sirius & Orion getting kicked out immediately after entering Points of Interest in Railjack missions.
- Fixed the Vehicles tab not properly displaying in the Base of Operation's Arsenal if it was swapped to from Orion.
- Known Issue: this still occurs when accessing the Arsenal in different Hubs.

- Fixed the Secondary Son appearing in a cinematic during The Old Peace quest.
- Fixed a certain cinematic fading out to a permanent black screen in The Old Peace quest if Sirius & Orion are equipped.
- Fixed minor diorama issues in Sirius & Orion's Ability screens.
- Fixed Sirius & Orion having the incorrect Scythes equipped during a segment in the Jade Shadows: Constellations quest.
- Fixed various chest armor offset issues on Sirius & Orion.
- Fixing a script error for Sirius & Orion when casting Well of Life.

Uranus Proxima Fixes:

- Fixed the Vena or Ryoku's fight not starting if a squadmate is dead upon entering the Boss Fight room in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed cases of fog being incredibly bright on low-spec machines in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed cases of the Vault's door remaining open during the Mawbound Director fight, resulting in players being able to get stuck outside the room.
- Fixed players being locked outside of the Sister fight in The Kuva Wytch missions if they died before it was triggered and revived after it had started.
- Fixed cases of the Megacoil powercell counter not updating in the second stage of the Ryoku fight.
- Fixed Necramechs not being disabled after Host Migration in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed Uranus Proxima missions not ending if players run out of revives or the Railjack suffers a catastrophic failure (that is not repaired in time).
- Fixed Vena missing a hacking animation in Scoria’s Angel missions.
- Fixed borrowed Railjack using a player's equipped Tactical Mods after a Host Migration.
- Fixed a case of Excalibur Umbra falling out of the map if they entered the Ramsled while in Transference during Scoria’s Angel missions.
- Fixed cases of waypoints not appearing for Clients in the "Destroy Captured Ice Blocks" objective in The Kuva Wytch mission.
- Fixed Ryoku missing his smoke VFX upon disappearing in The Kuva Wytch mission.
- Fixed a lighting issue in The Kuva Wytch.
- Fixed Sisters using Transmissions that were not context appropriate in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed performance issues caused by Bladeswarm enemies.
- Fixed a script error related to an enemy ability in Uranus Proxima missions.
- Fixed a crash for Clients at the end of the Ramsled section of the Scoria's Angel mission.
